## Model card

- **Name:** GLM 5.3 FlashX Legal (GLM-Legal)
- **Short description:** **Z.ai's legal-domain adaptation of GLM-5.3-Flash**, released **2026-10-12** as an **open-weight** model, built for agentic legal work — contract review, compliance, legal research and opinion drafting. It combines the base's **hybrid sparse/linear attention** and **native 1M context** with **legal mid-training/post-training** on legal materials and practical workflows. Its specialty is **independently verified and strong**: on Vals AI's harness it is **#7 of 79 on Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark (19.17% all-pass)** — the **cheapest model in the top 10** at $0.15/test — **#14 of 150 on LegalBench (86.24%, the best GLM score ever on that benchmark)**, and **#24 of 78 on Legal Research Bench (41.83%)**; Z.ai adds that it **matches frontier closed-source models on LegalBench and PLawBench** and is **+12.5 points over the base GLM-5.3-Flash on Harvey LAB**. Trade-offs: legal mid-training **narrows general utility** vs the general FlashX variants; the **delivered surface is text-only** (the vendor's own docs contradict on vision — the changelog claims native multimodality while the Retrieve-Model reference returns `input_modalities: ["text"]`, so vision is **unverified**); and it lives under `models_legal/` (a mirror tree `pnpm sync` does not yet score — audit item E2), so this report does not feed the build until that wiring lands.
- **Provider / access:** **Z.ai direct** (`glm-5.3-flashx-legal`; dated snapshot `glm-5.3-flashx-legal-20261102`), **OpenRouter** (`z-ai/glm-5.3-flashx-legal`), AutoClaw, ZenMux, AgentSky (resell). **Open weights** (self-hostable). `noFreeId: true` (no free route).
- **Release / knowledge:** Released **2026-10-12** (Vals AI + Z.ai blog "GLM-Legal: Advancing Agentic Legal Intelligence"). Knowledge cutoff not stated in the sources reviewed.
- **IDs:** `zai/glm-5.3-flashx-legal`; aliases `GLM-Legal`, `GLM-5.3-FlashX-Legal`, `z-ai/glm-5.3-flashx-legal`, `glm-5.3-flashx-legal-20261102`. Base: **GLM-5.3-Flash** (queue Overall **89.8**); sibling GLM-5.3-FlashX (**88.1**).
- **Context window:** **1,048,576 (1M) total / 131,072 (128K) max output** — native 1M with hybrid sparse/linear attention and high-throughput streaming for long document review. The 128K output is strong (no output-cap caveat). No independent long-context-recall benchmark (RULER/LCR) was published for the legal variant, so effective 1M use is spec-confirmed but not recall-verified.
- **Modalities:** **Text in → text out** (delivered/authoritative). ⚠ **Vision is UNVERIFIED and self-contradicted in the vendor's own docs**: the changelog claims native multimodality ("Text, image, video in") but the **Retrieve-Model API reference returns `input_modalities: ["text"]`**, and the folder `meta.json` explicitly instructs "treat the folder's vision scores as unverified." The **base GLM-5.3-Flash is documented under Z.ai's VLM guide**, so latent image capability plausibly exists in the foundation — but it is neither confirmed nor benchmarked for the legal variant (conditional upside, **uncredited** here).
- **Pricing (as of 2026-11-13):** **$0.37 input / $1.25 output per 1M, $0.075 cached read** (Z.ai direct, limited-time; ZenMux labels all three tiers limited-time); AgentSky resell $0.429/$1.451. Vals AI lists **$0.075 in / $0.25 out** (a cached/promo route) and **$0.15 per Harvey LAB test — the cheapest in that benchmark's top 10**. Open weights → **$0 self-host**. Very cheap for a 1M-context specialist.
- **Architecture:** Undisclosed proprietary-then-open-weighted (Z.ai); **hybrid sparse/linear attention** MoE derived from GLM-5.3-Flash, with legal-domain continued training. Reasoning-capable; high-throughput streaming tuned for document review.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 74/100.** **Verified strong agentic-legal tool use**: **Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark 19.17%, #7 of 79** (independent, Vals AI) — a real multi-step legal-agent harness — and the **cheapest model in that top 10**, at **+12.5 points over the base**. Held below the frontier-agentic band because general (non-legal) agency is **unverified** for the variant (no Terminal-Bench / Tau / GDPval cells) and the strong base (GLM-5.3-Flash 89.8) is narrowed by legal mid-training.
- **Reasoning: 78/100.** **Strong, independently-verified legal reasoning** — **LegalBench 86.24% (#14/150, the best GLM score on that benchmark)** and **Legal Research Bench 41.83% (#24/78)** — on a **near-frontier base (GLM-5.3-Flash 89.8)**. Tempered because the vendor's own tradeoff notes legal mid-training gives "**narrower general utility**," and there is **no general GPQA/HLE/MMLU cell for the legal variant**, so general (non-legal) reasoning is inferred-from-base and discounted, not measured.
- **Context window: 92/100.** **Native 1M-token window (1,048,576) with a strong 128K max output** and hybrid sparse/linear attention + high-throughput streaming built for long document review — top-tier on spec. Held just below the 95+ elite because **no independent long-context-recall benchmark (RULER/LCR)** verifies effective 1M use for the legal variant.
- **Multimodal: 35/100.** The **delivered legal surface is text-only**: the authoritative **Retrieve-Model reference returns `input_modalities: ["text"]`** and the folder `meta.json` instructs "treat the folder's vision scores as unverified" (the changelog's "image/video in" claim **contradicts** it). Scored at the text-only band with a modest uplift because the **base GLM-5.3-Flash is documented under Z.ai's VLM guide** — latent image capability is plausible **conditional upside**, but it is **uncredited** while the legal variant ships/‌documents text-only. This is the largest gap vs the cohort, which appears to credit the claimed image/video input.
- **Coding: 60/100.** A **legal specialist** whose coding is **not its focus and is unverified** — there is **no SWE-bench / LiveCodeBench / Terminal-Bench cell** for GLM-Legal. Grounded on the **strong base GLM-5.3-Flash (89.8)** minus the vendor-noted **legal-narrowing** of general utility. ⚠ The `meta.json` "**Benchable coding 95.0%**" figure is **template false-evidence and is disregarded** (it names no real benchmark and is implausible for this model); had it been credited, Coding would be far higher — this is the second-largest gap vs the cohort.
- **Cost efficiency: 95/100.** **Very cheap for a 1M-context specialist**: $0.37/$1.25 per 1M (limited-time), $0.075 cached, **$0.15 per Harvey LAB test (cheapest in the top 10)**, and **open-weight $0 self-host**. Only tempered by the limited-time/reseller pricing variance (Vals lists $0.075/$0.25 on a cached/promo route). Scored but **not** counted in the Overall per methodology.
- **Overall Score: 67.8/100.** Mean of Tool 74 + Reasoning 78 + Context 92 + Multimodal 35 + Coding 60 = 339 / 5 = **67.8**. Best fit: **cost-sensitive legal teams** doing contract review, compliance and agentic legal research over **1M-token document sets**, where the independently-verified Harvey LAB #7/79 and LegalBench 86.24% (#14/150, best GLM) matter and open-weight self-hosting is a plus; **not** a multimodal or general-coding choice, and general (non-legal) reasoning should route to the base GLM-5.3-Flash or a frontier model. Independent read: my **67.8** sits **below the cohort's 83** (3 qualifying peer raters) for three evidence-based reasons — (a) **Multimodal 35 not ~70**: the legal variant's authoritative API reference is **text-only** and `meta.json` says treat vision as unverified, so I do **not** credit the changelog's contradicted image/video claim; (b) **Coding 60 not higher**: the "**Benchable 95%**" cell is **template false-evidence** (no named benchmark, implausible) and is disregarded — coding rests on the strong base minus legal-narrowing, unverified for the variant; (c) **Reasoning 78 / Tool 74** are grounded on the **verified** Vals legal cells + strong base (89.8) but discounted for the vendor's own "narrower general utility" note and the absence of any general GPQA/HLE/Terminal-Bench measurement. The genuinely strong, **independently-verified legal specialty** (a rarity for a domain vertical) is credited near face value; the composite mean is dragged by the honest text-only Multimodal floor.
