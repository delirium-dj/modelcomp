Two-layer layout: thin CLI entry points under `scripts/` orchestrate filesystem work while pure, testable helpers live in `scripts/lib/`. `sync-data.mjs` is the main orchestrator — it walks `model/`, auto-quarantines evidence-free files, validates `meta.json`, recomputes `average.md` over a top-10 cohort gated by `RATER_GATE`, reconciles the `SourceKey` / `SOURCE_DEFS` registry in `src/data/sources.generated.ts`, and emits `scores.generated.ts` (numbers-only, ~1.7 MB prose kept out of the client bundle). Supporting CLIs (`debug-sync.mjs` filters FAIL lines from sync output; `find-fails.mjs` scans for missing `**Tool use:` markers) wrap or reuse the same lib. The lib modules are deliberately dependency-free and fully covered by co-located `.test.mjs` files: `parse.mjs` (score parsing, half-up rounding, rater gating, top-10 ranking), `validate.mjs` (path classification, filename hygiene, meta schema), `average.mjs` (average body/entry text assembly), `codegen.mjs` (registry text surgery and `scores.generated.ts` serializer), and `naming.mjs` (slug normalization, key derivation, label resolution). Dependency direction is strictly one-way: entries → lib helpers; no reverse imports. Git tripwire logic in sync consults `git ls-tree HEAD -- model` to forbid silent deletion of tracked research files unless `ALLOW_MODEL_DELETE=1`.
