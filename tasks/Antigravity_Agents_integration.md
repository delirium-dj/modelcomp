Here is a practical, real-world example of how you can use an AI agent with these specific rate limits (**60 Requests Per Minute**, **100K Tokens Per Minute**, and **100 Requests Per Day**).

---

### **Use Case: Automated Daily Competitive Intelligence & News Digest Agent**

In this scenario, **"Antigravity"** is an AI agent set up to monitor your industry competitors, gather the latest news or product updates, summarize them, and deliver a clean report to your inbox or Slack channel every morning.

#### **Why these limits fit this use case perfectly:**

1. **100 Requests Per Day (RPD):** This is your main bottleneck and daily budget. Running this agent **twice a day** (e.g., once in the morning and once in the afternoon) uses only 2 out of your 100 daily requests, leaving plenty of room for manual trigger tests or secondary runs.
2. **60 Requests Per Minute (RPM) & 100K Tokens Per Minute (TPM):** When the agent runs, it needs to fetch multiple web pages, parse articles, and write summaries in a short burst. A high burst limit like 60 RPM allows the agent to scrape and process 5 to 10 competitor websites concurrently without hitting rate-limit errors.

---

### **How the Agent Works (Workflow Example)**

1. **Trigger:** The agent activates automatically twice a day.
2. **Data Collection:** It visits predefined RSS feeds, news sites, or competitor blogs to fetch the latest text content (consuming a portion of your TPM burst capacity).
3. **Processing & Summarization:** The agent filters out irrelevant fluff, extracts key feature updates or pricing changes, and compiles them into a structured bulleted list.
4. **Delivery:** It sends the final report directly to your team's communication channel.
