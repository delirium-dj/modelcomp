# Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber — Independent Research Report

## Summary and evidence

Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber is Google's September 2026 defensive-security specialization of Gemini 3.8 Flash. Google describes it as its most capable cybersecurity model, with frontier-level vulnerability discovery and automated patching. It shares the 3.8 foundation's long-horizon coding, reasoning, and autonomous-agent improvements, but is restricted to trusted defenders through the Fairwind Program.

Source: [Google Gemini 3.8 Flash and Flash Cyber announcement](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/3-8-flash-and-3-8-flash-cyber/)

## Cost and limitations

Google does not publish ordinary self-service API pricing or complete specifications for the Cyber variant. Restricted access limits reproducibility and general deployment; its safeguards and evaluation focus are intentionally specialized for authorized defensive work.
