## Model card

- **Name:** Gemini 2.5 Pro. Proprietary, `base` variant. **Classified "Non-Reasoning" by the benchmark tracker** — worth flagging, because Google marketed this generation specifically on "thinking" capability; the tracker's classification reflects how the default serving configuration behaves rather than the marketing.
- **Short description:** Google's **June 2025 stable** prior-generation flagship, pre-Gemini-3. **This is the furthest-superseded Google model assessed in this research pass with a computable score**: a composite tracker places it **#94 of 882 at 49.47/100** on 25 of 613 benchmarks, and **ten newer Google models score above it** — Gemini 4 Argon (81.81), Gemini 3.8 Flash (73.06), 3.7 Flash (67.6), 3.1 Pro (64.66), 3.6 Flash (63.22), 3.5 Flash (62.48), 3 Pro (59.48), 3 Flash (54.52), 3.5 Flash-Lite (50.75) and even Gemma 4 26B A4B (45.97) is close behind. One capability remains genuinely strong and is the reason to care about it: **full omnimodal input — text, image, audio, video and PDF — with AA-MMMU-Pro at 74.9%.** Everything agentic has collapsed relative to current models: **GDPval-AA at 616 Elo and an AA Agentic Index of 3.5% are both the lowest figures found anywhere in this research pass, by wide margins.**
- **Provider / access:** Google AI Studio, Vertex AI, Gemini API. Google published launch benchmarks in its March 2025 "Gemini model thinking updates" post and on the DeepMind Gemini Pro model page.
- **Release / knowledge:** **Stable June 2025** (repository metadata); launch benchmarks published March 2025. **No verified knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `gemini-2.5-pro`. **No free-tier ID.**
- **Context window:** **1,048,576 tokens with 64K maximum output** (repository metadata; tracker records 1M). **AA-LCR at 69.0% is weak** for this nominal size — against DeepSeek V4.1 Flash's 84.0%, GPT-5.6 Luna's 83.7% and Qwen3.7 Max's 79.0% at the same 1M. **And the price doubles above a 200K prompt** (see Pricing), so the economically sane operating window is a fifth of nominal.
- **Modalities:** **Text, image, audio, video and PDF input → text output** (repository metadata). **This is the model's one enduring strength and the broadest input surface of any model assessed in this research pass** — Gemini has carried native audio and video input since this generation, which most competitors still lack. **AA-MMMU-Pro at 74.9%** is solid measured evidence.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03, two-tier by prompt length):**
  - **$1.25 / 1M input, $10.00 / 1M output for prompts at or under 200K**
  - **$2.50 / 1M input, $15.00 / 1M output for prompts above 200K**
  - The sub-200K rate undercuts the methodology's $3/$15 anchor on input but only modestly on output. **Above 200K it reaches the anchor exactly**, which means using the advertised million-token window costs frontier-flagship money for a model ranked #94.
- **Architecture:** Proprietary, closed weights. Parameter count and topology: not disclosed.

### Raw benchmarks found

> **25 of 613 tracked benchmark slots are populated** (tracker snapshot **2026-10-06**; the tracker has grown to 882 models, where earlier reports in this pass cited 784). **(G)** = Google's own March 2025 thinking-updates blog post or the DeepMind Gemini Pro model page; **(AA)** = Artificial Analysis; **(Vals)** = Vals AI; **(Epoch)** = Epoch AI; plus the independent Gert Labs rankings and OpenRouter. Composite: **49.47/100, #94 of 882**, flagged conservative on partial coverage.

Agent / tool use — **the weakest agentic profile found anywhere in this research pass**:

- **τ²-bench: 54.1% (AA)** — **narrowly clears the methodology's Tau-family ~50%+ reference**, and is the only creditable agentic figure here.
- **Gert Labs: 42.01%** (independent rankings)
- **GDPval-AA: 616 Elo (AA) / 0.0% normalized (AA)** — **the lowest GDPval figure found anywhere in this research pass, 1,134 Elo below the ~1750+ reference.** The next-lowest in this pass is Grok 4.3 at 1,018, and GLM-5.3-Flash reaches 1,773. A normalized score of **0.0%** means it is at the floor of the measured field.
- **AA Agentic Index: 3.5% (AA)** — **the lowest Agentic Index found anywhere in this research pass**, against Grok 4.3's 17.2% (the next-lowest), Muse Spark 1.1's 27.5% and GLM-5.3's 53.4%.
- **No Terminal-Bench figure at any version**, and no Claw-Eval, OSWorld, MCP-Atlas, BrowseComp, Toolathlon, CyberGym or AutomationBench. Eight agentic reference benchmarks unreported, including Claw-Eval, whose absence the methodology explicitly penalises.

Reasoning / knowledge:

- **AA-LCR: 69.0% (AA)** — the only respectable reasoning figure on the page.
- **GPQA: 83% (G)** and **AA-GPQA Diamond: 84.4% (AA)** — **both miss the 90%+ frontier reference**, by 7 and 5.6 points.
- **HLE: 18.8% (G)** and **AA-HLE: 22.5% (AA)** — **both far below the 40%+ reference**, by 21.2 and 17.5 points.
- **Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index: 16.1% (AA)** — **below the methodology's 20–35 mid band entirely**, and 43.9 points below the 60+ frontier marker.
- **AA-Omniscience: Index −16.3%, accuracy 39.1%, hallucination rate 90.9% (AA)** — a negative reliability index on a very high confabulation rate.
- **CritPt: 2.6% (AA)** — near-floor. **AA-IFBench: 48.7% (AA)** — weak instruction following.
- **Epoch AI's FrontierMath v2: Tiers 1–3 14.138%, Tier 4 4.167%** — near-floor on research mathematics, and identical to Claude Sonnet 4.5's Tier 4 figure.
- **ARC-AGI (any version) and MMLU-Pro: no verified public score found.**

Coding:

- **SWE-bench Verified: 63.8% (G)**, independently **SWE-bench: 54.4% (Vals)** — a **9.4-point vendor-to-independent gap**, and both figures are well below current frontier levels (DeepSeek V4 Pro reaches 96.4% on the Vals harness).
- **AA-SciCode: 46.3% (AA)** — misses the 55%+ reference by 8.7 points.
- **AA Coding Index: 33.3% (AA)** — **36.7 points below the methodology's 70%+ reference.**
- **Vibe Code Bench: 0.40% (Vals)** — **effectively zero, and the lowest coding figure recorded anywhere in this research pass.**
- **No LiveCodeBench, no DeepSWE, no Terminal-Bench, no SWE-bench Pro, no Codeforces, no CursorBench.**

Multimodal:

- **AA-MMMU-Pro: 74.9% (AA)** — **genuinely solid, and better than several far newer models in this pass**: above Claude Sonnet 4.6 (70.6%), MiMo-V2.6-Flash (73.1%) and Inkling-Small (74.0%), and close to GPT-5.6 Luna (78.6%).
- **Design Arena Website: 1,174 Elo** (OpenRouter) — generated design quality via code, **not** a multimodal input measure, and among the lowest in this pass.
- **No CharXiv, MMVU, OCR, document, video or audio benchmark exists**, so the audio and video input paths — the model's distinguishing feature — are entirely unmeasured.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 34/100. The weakest agentic assessment in this research pass, and the two composite measures agree emphatically.** **GDPval-AA at 616 Elo is 1,134 points below the ~1750+ reference and the lowest figure found anywhere in this pass** — its normalized form of **0.0%** places it at the floor of the measured field — and **the AA Agentic Index of 3.5% is also the lowest here**, roughly a fifth of the next-lowest model. These are not isolated weak rows; they are the two benchmarks designed to measure aggregate real-world agentic value, and both say the same thing. **No Terminal-Bench figure exists at any version**, and **Claw-Eval, OSWorld, MCP-Atlas, BrowseComp, Toolathlon, CyberGym and AutomationBench are all unreported** — eight agentic references absent. The score is not lower because **τ²-bench at 54.1% does narrowly clear the Tau-family reference**, which shows basic structured tool-calling works, and **Gert Labs at 42.01%** is a second independent datapoint. This model predates the agentic era and should not be deployed into it.
- **Reasoning: 38/100.** Weak on every measured axis, and the weakness is consistent rather than patchy. **The Intelligence Index of 16.1% falls below the methodology's 20–35 mid band entirely.** **Both HLE figures miss the 40%+ reference by 17.5 to 21.2 points** (18.8% vendor, 22.5% independent). **Both GPQA figures miss the 90%+ reference** (83% vendor, 84.4% independent). **CritPt at 2.6% is near-floor**, **AA-IFBench at 48.7% is weak**, and **FrontierMath v2 Tier 4 at 4.167% is near-floor** on research mathematics. **The AA-Omniscience Index is −16.3% on a 90.9% hallucination rate** with 39.1% accuracy — poor calibration on moderate knowledge. The only respectable figure is **AA-LCR at 69.0%**, and **ARC-AGI and MMLU-Pro are entirely unmeasured.** For context on how far the field has moved: this model's HLE of 18.8% sits against Muse Spark 1.1's tool-free 52.2%.
- **Context window: 86/100. Set well below the ≥1M tier floor of 95, on two independent grounds.** The nominal window qualifies — **1,048,576 tokens** — but **AA-LCR at 69.0% is weak for this size class**, 15 points below DeepSeek V4.1 Flash's 84.0% and 10 below Qwen3.7 Max's 79.0% at the same nominal length, so measured usable depth does not support the tier. More decisively, **Google's own price schedule doubles above a 200K prompt** — from $1.25/$10.00 to $2.50/$15.00 — which means the vendor has priced the advertised million as a premium product, and at the higher tier you pay the methodology's full frontier anchor for a model ranked #94. The **64K output ceiling** is also modest against DeepSeek's 384K or the 128–131K common across this pass, and **no MRCR, RULER, CorpusQA or GraphWalks figure exists**. The score reflects a window that is real, moderately usable, and economically discouraged beyond 200K.
- **Multimodal: 90/100. This is the model's one genuinely competitive dimension and the sole reason to still consider it.** Input spans **text, image, audio, video and PDF** — **the broadest input surface of any model assessed in this research pass**, matching MiMo V2.6's omnimodal reach and exceeding every GPT, Claude, Qwen, GLM and DeepSeek model assessed here, most of which remain text-and-image only. That places it at the floor of the methodology's top band ("+audio or non-text out = 90–100"), and the one available measurement supports it: **AA-MMMU-Pro at 74.9% is better than several far newer models** — above Claude Sonnet 4.6 (70.6%), MiMo-V2.6-Flash (73.1%) and Inkling-Small (74.0%). It is held at the band floor rather than higher because **the audio and video paths that justify the band are entirely unmeasured** — no MMVU, no audio benchmark, no video benchmark — and **no CharXiv, OCR or document figure exists either**, so chart reasoning and document extraction cannot be checked. **Design Arena Website at 1,174 Elo is excluded** as an output-quality measure.
- **Coding: 42/100.** Comprehensively below current standards, and one figure is remarkable in a bad way. **The AA Coding Index at 33.3% is 36.7 points below the methodology's 70%+ reference.** **Vibe Code Bench at 0.40% is effectively zero and the lowest coding figure recorded anywhere in this research pass** — a result that indicates the model simply cannot complete the kind of open-ended build task that benchmark poses. **SWE-bench Verified at 63.8% vendor drops to 54.4% independently at Vals AI**, a 9.4-point gap, and even the vendor figure sits roughly 32 points below what DeepSeek V4 Pro achieves on the Vals harness. **AA-SciCode at 46.3% misses its reference by 8.7 points.** And **LiveCodeBench, DeepSWE, Terminal-Bench, SWE-bench Pro, Codeforces and CursorBench are all unreported**, so six of the dimension's references cannot be checked. The score is not lower only because SWE-bench Verified at 63.8% shows real, if dated, repository-patching capability.
- **Cost efficiency: 24/100.** The sub-200K rate of **$1.25 / $10.00** undercuts the methodology's $3/$15 anchor on input but only modestly on output, and **above 200K the rate reaches $2.50 / $15.00 — the anchor almost exactly.** That is the core problem: **you pay frontier-flagship money for a model ranked #94 of 882.** The dominance inside Google's own lineup is comprehensive: **ten newer Google models score higher**, and the comparison that matters most is **Gemini 3.8 Flash at 73.06 composite against this model's 49.47** — a 23.6-point gap in favour of a *Flash*-tier model, which by Google's own tiering is the cheap one. Paying Pro-tier prices for sub-Flash-tier capability is indefensible on any workload. Two further deductions: **the price doubles above 200K**, penalising the one specification (1M context) that might justify the Pro tier; and **there is no free-tier ID**, so there is not even an evaluation path at zero cost. The score is not lower because the omnimodal input surface is genuinely valuable and not fully replicated by every cheaper sibling.
- **Overall Score: 58.0/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (34 + 38 + 86 + 90 + 42) / 5 = 58.0 — **a deeply superseded generalist with exactly one remaining reason to exist: its input surface.** The honest summary is that **the Multimodal score of 90 is carrying this model**, and the four other dimensions average 50. What still holds up: **text, image, audio, video and PDF input is the broadest input surface of any model assessed in this research pass**, with **AA-MMMU-Pro at 74.9% beating several far newer models**, and **AA-LCR at 69.0%** over a million-token window. If you specifically need audio or video input and your workload is document-heavy analysis inside 200K tokens, it remains functional. Four findings rule it out otherwise. **Agentic capability is the worst in this pass** — GDPval-AA at 616 Elo (1,134 below reference, 0.0% normalized) and an AA Agentic Index of 3.5%, with no Terminal-Bench figure at any version and eight agentic references unreported. **Coding is far below current standards** — an AA Coding Index of 33.3% and **Vibe Code Bench at 0.40%, the lowest coding figure anywhere in this pass**. **Reasoning is weak throughout** — an Intelligence Index of 16.1% below the mid band, both HLE figures missing 40% by 17 to 21 points, CritPt at 2.6%, and a −16.3% reliability index on a 90.9% hallucination rate. And **it is commercially indefensible**: ten newer Google models rank higher, **Gemini 3.8 Flash scores 23.6 points more as a cheap Flash tier**, and above 200K this model charges the full frontier anchor rate.
