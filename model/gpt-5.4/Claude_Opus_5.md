## Model card

- **Name:** GPT-5.4. Proprietary, Reasoning. Variant/alias flag: this is the **base** tier, with **GPT-5.4 Pro**, **GPT-5.4 mini** and **GPT-5.4 nano** as siblings. ARC Prize's ARC-AGI-1 entry for it is labelled **"(XHigh)"**, so the headline abstract-reasoning figures reflect a maximum-effort configuration rather than default settings.
- **Short description:** A composite tracker places it **#26 of 784 at 67.01/100** across a deep **55 of 649 benchmarks**. Its distinction in this research pass is abstract reasoning: **ARC Prize records ARC-AGI-2 at 74.0% and ARC-AGI-1 at 93.67%, and both are the highest figures of their kind found anywhere in this pass** — ahead of Claude Opus 4.8 (ARC-AGI-2 72.1%) and DeepSeek V4 Pro (ARC-AGI-1 90.00%). It also posts **τ²-bench at 98.9%** and **OSWorld-Verified at 75%**, both pass-leading, and **the broadest measured multimodal battery of any model assessed here**. Against that: **deep legacy** — OpenAI has shipped GPT-5.5, GPT-5.6 (Sol/Terra/Luna/Cyber), GPT-6 Sol/Luna/Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol since, with **GPT-6 Astra at 84.77 composite against this model's 67.01** — and **GDPval-AA at 1,307 Elo is 443 points below the methodology's reference**, so real-world economic task value is weak. **There is no SWE-bench figure of any variant for this model.**
- **Provider / access:** OpenAI API and ChatGPT. Launched with its own announcement post ("Introducing GPT-5.4") and a separate mini/nano post; subsequently used as the comparison baseline in OpenAI's "Introducing GPT-5.5" post, which is where several of its figures below originate.
- **Release / knowledge:** **No verified release date or knowledge cutoff found** for this specific ID.
- **IDs:** `gpt-5.4`; siblings `gpt-5.4-pro`, `gpt-5.4-mini`, `gpt-5.4-nano`. **No free-tier ID.**
- **Context window:** **1,050,000 tokens.** On GPT-5.x family precedent — verified for GPT-5.6 Sol and Terra earlier in this research session but **not separately confirmed for GPT-5.4** — this implies a 922,000-token input cap, a 128,000-token output limit and a **272K full-request repricing threshold**. **AA-LCR at 82.0%** is strong.
- **Modalities:** **Text and image input → text output.** Image input is confirmed by an unusually wide measured battery (MMMU-Pro, CharXiv, ScreenSpot Pro, ERQA, SimpleVQA, ZeroBench, MedXpertQA-MM). No audio or video path — OpenAI ships those as separate GPT Realtime / GPT Audio / Whisper models. **OfficeQA Pro at 53.2% suggests document handling, but PDF input was not separately verified for this ID.**
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **No verified rate card found for `gpt-5.4`.** No primary OpenAI pricing page for this ID was reached, and no aggregator figure was confirmed. **Cost efficiency below therefore rests on tier positioning and supersession, not on a rate card — treat it as directional and re-verify before budgeting.**
- **Architecture:** Proprietary, closed weights. Parameter count and topology: not disclosed.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 86/100.** Several pass-leading results, tempered by a weak economic-value showing. **τ²-bench at 98.9% is the highest in this research pass** and clears the Tau-family reference outright; **OSWorld-Verified at 75% is also the highest here**, corroborated by **ScreenSpot Pro at 85.4%** on GUI grounding; **MCP Atlas at 70.6%** is one of few MCP measurements available anywhere in this pass; **Claw-Eval at 60.3%** satisfies a benchmark the methodology explicitly penalises models for lacking; and **BrowseComp 82.7%, CyberGym 79.0% and Toolathlon 54.6%** fill out a broad agentic picture. Four deductions. **GDPval-AA at 1,307 Elo is 443 points below the ~1750+ reference** — against GLM-5.3's 1,769 — so benchmark tool-calling excellence does not translate into economically valuable work. **The entire long-horizon cluster collapses**: ExploitGym 6.0%, ApprenticeBench 11%, ResearchClawBench 15.3%, APEX-Agents 33.3%, JobBench 38.9%. **No Terminal-Bench beyond 2.0 exists**, and 75.1% there is well below the ~88% anchor. And the two strongest figures (τ² and OSWorld) are **vendor-reported with no independent corroboration**.
- **Reasoning: 92/100.** The strongest abstract-reasoning evidence in this research pass, independently verified. **ARC Prize records ARC-AGI-2 at 74.0% and ARC-AGI-1 at 93.67% — both the highest of their kind found anywhere here** — and ARC Prize is a third party running its own harness, which makes these the most trustworthy headline figures on the page. **GPQA clears the 90%+ reference under three measurements agreeing within 0.8 points (92.8% / 92.8% / 92.0%)**; **AA-HLE at 43.7% clears the 40%+ reference**; **CritPt at 23.4% is the highest in this pass**; **AA-LCR reaches 82.0%**; **AA-IFBench at 73.9% is second-highest here**; and **the AA-Omniscience Index is positive (+5.8%) on the highest raw accuracy recorded in this pass (50.8%)**. Five deductions keep it from the mid-90s: **the Intelligence Index of 39.0% is strikingly low for a #26-ranked model** and 21 points below the frontier marker; **OpenAI's own tool-free HLE figure of 39.8% misses the 40%+ reference**, so the 52.1% headline is tool-assisted; **FrontierMath v2 Tier 4 at 27.1% is less than half GPT-5.6 Luna's 58.5%**; **ARC-AGI-3 is 0.2%**, so the ARC strength does not generalise to the interactive variant; and **the 91.7% hallucination rate** means the strong knowledge base is not safely accessible without verification. The **"XHigh"** label on the ARC results is also a reminder that these are maximum-effort numbers.
- **Context window: 95/100.** **1,050,000 tokens** places it in the ≥1M tier (95–100), and **AA-LCR at 82.0% is strong**, among the better long-context reasoning figures in this pass. It sits at the tier floor rather than above it for four reasons: the 100 condition requires **≥98% retrieval at 512K+** and **no MRCR, RULER, CorpusQA or GraphWalks figure exists** (contrast DeepSeek V4 Pro, which publishes MRCR 1M at 83.5%); the GPT-5.x family's **922,000-token input cap** means the nominal million cannot be filled; the **272K full-request repricing threshold** should be assumed to apply; and **none of those three family parameters was separately verified for this ID** — they rest on GPT-5.6 Sol and Terra documentation read earlier in this research session.
- **Multimodal: 70/100.** **The broadest measured visual battery of any model assessed in this research pass** — eleven distinct multimodal rows — and it earns the **top of the methodology's "+image in = 60–70" band. **MMMU-Pro at 81.2% vendor is corroborated at 78.4% independently** (2.8-point agreement) and reaches 82.1% with Python; **ScreenSpot Pro at 85.4%** is strong GUI grounding that independently corroborates the pass-leading OSWorld-Verified result; **CharXiv at 82.8%** is strong chart reasoning; and **MedXpertQA (MM) at 77.1%** shows real domain transfer. It cannot exceed the band because the input surface is **text and image only** — no audio or video path, which OpenAI ships as separate Realtime/Audio/Whisper models — and because **ZeroBench at 41.0% and OfficeQA Pro at 53.2%** mark genuine ceilings on hard visual reasoning and office-document work. **OfficeQA Pro hints at document handling, but PDF input was not verified for this ID**, so the higher "+video/PDF = 75–90" band cannot be claimed. Note also that **six of the eleven multimodal rows come from a competitor's comparison chart** and are directional rather than confirmed.
- **Coding: 78/100.** Competent but the thinnest-evidenced dimension relative to the model's rank. **The AA Coding Index at 71.0% clears the methodology's 70%+ reference**, **React Native Evals at 85.3% is strong and independently measured**, **LiveCodeBench Pro at 87.5%** is high (though competitor-reported), and **Vibe Code Bench at 67.42% from Vals AI** is respectable. Four deductions, and the first is substantial. **No SWE-bench figure of any variant exists for this model** — not SWE-bench, not Verified, not Multilingual — so the single most-cited measure of repository engineering is simply unavailable, and standard LiveCodeBench, DeepSWE, AA-SciCode, CursorBench, Codeforces and NL2Repo are absent too. **Terminal-Bench 2.0 at 75.1% is 9.9 points below the 85%+ coding reference**, with no 2.1 or 3.0 figure to update it. **SWE-bench Pro at 57.7%** is mid-band. And **PostTrainBench v1.1 at 19.0%** is near-floor on ML engineering. The dimension is scored on four datapoints where most peers in this pass offer ten or more.
- **Cost efficiency: 48/100. This score carries an explicit caveat: no rate card was verified for `gpt-5.4` at all**, so it rests on supersession and tier positioning rather than on price. What is verifiable is unfavourable. **Four OpenAI generations have shipped since**: GPT-5.5 (67.74 composite), GPT-5.6 Sol (77.93), GPT-6 Sol (75.39), GPT-6.1 Sol (81.44) and **GPT-6 Astra (84.77 against this model's 67.01)**. More pointedly, **GPT-5.6 Luna carries a verified $1/$6 rate card and scores 66.03 — within one point of GPT-5.4's 67.01 — while also offering the same 1.05M context, a higher Intelligence Index (51.2% against 39.0%), FrontierMath Tier 4 at more than double (58.5% against 27.1%) and SWE-bench at 93.0% where GPT-5.4 has no SWE-bench figure at all.** Unless GPT-5.4 is priced below $1/$6 — implausible for an older reasoning-tier flagship — it is dominated by a cheaper, newer, better-measured sibling. The score is not lower because the model retains two genuine, independently verified advantages no sibling matches: **pass-leading ARC-AGI-1 and ARC-AGI-2**, and **the broadest multimodal battery in this pass**.
- **Overall Score: 84.2/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (86 + 92 + 95 + 70 + 78) / 5 = 84.2 — a strong model with a genuine, narrow, independently verified claim to excellence and a weak commercial case. What holds up and is third-party verified: **ARC-AGI-2 at 74.0% and ARC-AGI-1 at 93.67% from ARC Prize are the highest of their kind found anywhere in this research pass**; **GPQA clears 90% under three measurements within 0.8 points**; **AA-HLE 43.7% clears its reference**; **CritPt 23.4% leads this pass**; **AA-IFBench 73.9% is second-highest here**; **τ²-bench 98.9% and OSWorld-Verified 75% both lead this pass**; **Claw-Eval 60.3% and MCP Atlas 70.6% are present where most peers have nothing**; and **the eleven-row multimodal battery — MMMU-Pro 81.2%, ScreenSpot Pro 85.4%, CharXiv 82.8%, MedXpertQA-MM 77.1% — is the broadest measured here.** Choose it specifically for **abstract pattern reasoning, GUI and computer-use automation, and wide visual understanding**. Four findings argue against it otherwise. **Real-world economic value is weak** — GDPval-AA 1,307 Elo, 443 below reference, with ExploitGym at 6.0%, ApprenticeBench at 11% and ResearchClawBench at 15.3%. **Coding is under-evidenced**, with no SWE-bench figure of any variant and Terminal-Bench stuck at 2.0. **The Intelligence Index of 39.0% is low for the rank**, and the 91.7% hallucination rate means its pass-leading 50.8% knowledge accuracy needs verification to use. And **it is commercially dominated**: GPT-5.6 Luna matches its composite within a point at a verified $1/$6, with a higher Intelligence Index, double the FrontierMath Tier 4 and an actual SWE-bench result.
