## Model card

- **Name:** GPT-5.4, released **2026-03-05**, API model ID `gpt-5.4`, with `gpt-5.4-pro` for maximum performance on the most complex tasks. OpenAI's framing is one line long: **"Designed for professional work."** It rolled out gradually across ChatGPT and Codex, and **GPT-5.4 Thinking replaced GPT-5.2 Thinking** for ChatGPT Plus, Team and Pro users (with GPT-5.2 Thinking retained for three months).
- **Short description:** A professional-work generalist from the GPT-5.4 generation. **As of today it is roughly seven months old and has been superseded twice over** — by GPT-5.5 (April 2026), the GPT-5.6 family (July 2026) and GPT-6 Astra (August/September 2026). Its BenchLM profile remains the most complete of any model in this dataset at **37 tracked benchmarks**, which makes it the most honestly assessable of the older checkpoints, and its September 2026 price cut makes it considerably better value than it was at launch.
- **Provider / access:** OpenAI first-party, plus OpenRouter, Amazon Bedrock and Azure OpenAI.
- **Release / knowledge:** released 2026-03-05. No first-party knowledge cutoff was located for GPT-5.4 in the sources reviewed; the family sits behind GPT-5.6 Sol's documented **February 16, 2026** cutoff.
- **Context window:** **1,050,000 tokens (1.05M)**, confirmed by BenchLM, pricepertoken and BenchLM's comparison pages. Max output is not sourced in the material reviewed.
- **Reasoning:** explicit reasoning mode. BenchLM classifies it as Reasoning; GPT-5.3 Codex and GPT-5.4 are both reasoning models, and BenchLM's cross-model comparison shows **GPT-5.4 at 70.96/100 against GPT-5.3 Codex's 65.22**. DeepInfra benchmarks it at **xHigh** reasoning effort, scoring **GPQA Diamond 93.0**.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-09-28), per 1M — this model has been repriced, and it matters:**
  - **Current at OpenAI: $1.25 input / $7.50 output / $0.125 cached**
  - **Launch list price: $2.50 input / $15.00 output**, with a **272K-token breakpoint above which input is $5.00 and output $22.50**
  - **pricepertoken states the cheapest input price has fallen 50%, from $2.50 to $1.25, over roughly the past 90 days**
  - Resellers are all dearer: **OpenRouter $2.50/$15.00/$0.250, Amazon Bedrock $2.75/$16.50/$0.275, Azure $2.75/$16.50/$0.275**
  - Siblings: **gpt-5.4-mini $0.75/$4.50, gpt-5.4-nano $0.20/$1.25, gpt-5.4-pro $30.00/$60.00 input and $180.00/$270.00 output** (with the same 272K breakpoint)
- **Architecture:** undisclosed parameters, proprietary, closed weights.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 78/100.** Down 7 from 85. The positives are real: **OSWorld-Verified at 75%** is a genuinely good computer-use result, **Terminal-Bench 2.0 75.1%** is competitive, and **DeepSearchQA 73.6%** shows credible research-agent behaviour. Held at 78 on three grounds. First, **Terminal-Bench 2.0 is a superseded version** — for calibration, Claude Opus 4.8 scores 74.6% on TB 2.0 and then collapses to 21.1% on TB 3.0, so a TB 2.0 number tells you very little about current terminal ability, and **no TB 2.1, 3.0 or 4.0 figure exists for GPT-5.4 at all**. Second, **ResearchClawBench at 15.3% and ExploitGym at 6.0%** are poor, and the agentic lane sits at **#35 of 151**. Third, **freellm-style category coverage is absent** — no τ²-bench, MCP Atlas or BrowseComp.
- **Reasoning: 86/100.** Down 4 from 90. **GPQA 92.8% (93.0% at xHigh)**, **HLE 52.1%** — which is higher than Gemini 3.8 Flash's 47.8 and GPT-5.6 Sol's 49.5, and is the most interesting single number on this model, because it was measured in March 2026 and has not been overtaken on that specific benchmark. **FrontierMath v2 Tier 4 at 27.100%** and Tiers 1–3 at 47.600% are strong for the era, and **ARC-AGI-2 74.0%** was a frontier number in March. Held at 86 with one clear warning attached: **ARC-AGI-3 scores 0.2%** — effectively zero. Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview also scores near zero on ARC-AGI-3 (0.4%), so this is a generational pattern rather than a model-specific failure, but it does mean the abstract-reasoning gains measured on ARC-AGI-2 **do not transfer to the hardest available reasoning benchmark.**
- **Context window: 88/100.** Down 4 from 92. **1,050,000 tokens.** Held at 88, with a commercial caveat that belongs in this dimension: **there is a 272K-token pricing breakpoint**, above which input is $5.00 and output $22.50 against $1.25 and $7.50 below it — so the top three-quarters of the window bills at roughly double. And **no independent long-context retrieval measurement exists for GPT-5.4** — no AA-LCR, no MRCR — so 1.05M is a capacity claim.
- **Multimodal: 82/100.** Up 10 from 72 — the largest upgrade in this report's quality dims. **MMMU-Pro 81.2%** and **MMMU-Pro with Python 82.1%**, with a multimodal lane of **69.3 (#20 of 48)**, and text, image, video, audio and file input. Held at 82 rather than higher: **OfficeQA Pro at 53.2% is weak**, and there is **no Video-MMMU, MathVision, CharXiv or ScreenSpot figure**, so document and video performance is unmeasured.
- **Coding: 82/100.** Down 2 from 84. **LiveCodeBench Pro 87.5%** is the strongest number, **React Native Evals 85.3%** and **Vibe Code Bench 67.42%** are respectable, **SWE-bench Pro 57.7%** is adequate. Held at 82 on the combination of **no SWE-bench Verified figure**, a **coding lane of 54 at #49 of 183** — which is poor for a model of this class and generation — and the plain fact that three newer OpenAI generations exist.
- **Cost efficiency: 75/100.** Up 10 from 65 — the largest upgrade in this report, and the one that makes GPT-5.4 worth considering again. **$1.25 input / $7.50 output / $0.125 cached at OpenAI, after a 50% cut from the $2.50/$15.00 launch price**, puts it **3.2× cheaper on output than Claude Opus 4.8 or Opus 5 ($25)** and **4× cheaper than GPT-5.6 Sol ($30)**, while being roughly level with Kimi K3 on input and half its output. It remains dearer than **Gemini 3.8 Flash ($0.75/$3.75)** and than DeepSeek V4.1 Flash. Held at 75 rather than higher for three reasons: **the $1.25 rate is a recent cut and may be promotional rather than permanent**; **the 272K breakpoint** penalises exactly the long-context workloads a 1.05M window implies; and **every reseller is at or above the old list price** — OpenRouter at $2.50/$15.00 and Bedrock and Azure at $2.75/$16.50 are 2× first-party, so the cheap rate is available only from OpenAI directly.
- **Overall Score: 83.2/100.** Half-up mean of the five quality dims: (78 + 86 + 88 + 82 + 82) / 5 = 83.2. Best fit: **a well-measured, professionally-oriented generalist from March 2026 whose September price cut has made it a better deal than its benchmark profile alone would suggest — and which you should buy from OpenAI directly or not at all.** The case for it: **HLE 52.1%**, still ahead of two newer frontier models; **GPQA 92.8%**; **FrontierMath v2 Tier 4 at 27.1%**; **MMMU-Pro 81.2%**; **OSWorld-Verified 75%**; and **$1.25/$7.50 with a 1.05M window**. The case against: **ARC-AGI-3 at 0.2%**, a **coding lane ranked #49 of 183**, **ResearchClawBench 15.3% and ExploitGym 6.0%**, and the plain arithmetic that **$7.50/M output buys less than Gemini 3.8 Flash's $3.75 for comparable Flash-tier work**, while the reseller channel charges double for the same weights.
