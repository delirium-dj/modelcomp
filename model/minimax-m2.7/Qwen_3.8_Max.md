> **Verdict:** MiniMax M2.7 is the "self-evolving" open-weight agentic flagship of the M2 series — a 228.7B-total / ~10B-active MoE trained with agent-native RL (Forge), whose headline claim is an early operational form of self-evolution: during training the model autonomously triaged failed runs on MiniMax's own infrastructure and edited its own agent scaffold across 100+ optimization rounds. On paper it approaches frontier closed-weight systems at ~10B active params: AIME 2026 94.2, GPQA-Diamond 89.8, SWE-bench Pro 56.2 (reported to match GPT-5.3-Codex), SWE-Multilingual 76.5, Terminal-Bench 2.0 57.0, and an MLE-Bench Lite 66.6% medal rate ("second only to Opus-4.6 and GPT-5.4"). BUT every number is vendor-only — AA's Intelligence Index is N/A (the model is deprecated, superseded by M3), no independent re-run exists, and the community explicitly flagged the results as needing scrutiny. Add a NON-COMMERCIAL license (the key regression from M2.5's Modified MIT), only 3 API providers, and notably slow 40.4 tok/s. Scored Overall 72 (div +5.4 vs curated 66.6).

## Model card

- **Name:** MiniMax M2.7
- **Short description:** MiniMax's self-evolving open-weight agentic flagship (March 2026): a 228.7B-total / ~10B-active Mixture-of-Experts trained with the Forge agent-native RL system, notable for autonomously editing its own agent scaffold during training. Approaches frontier closed-weight performance on agentic coding and reasoning at ~10B active params, but is **deprecated** (M3 successor), **non-commercial** licensed, and slow.
- **Provider / access:** Open weights on **Hugging Face** (`MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-M2.7`) and ModelScope — self-hostable (8× H100, FP8 served) but under a **NON-COMMERCIAL LICENSE** (commercial use requires written authorization). API via **3 providers** (per AA), including MiniMax's own API; id `opencode/minimax-m2.7`. No free ID.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released **March 2026** (AA); reported **March 18, 2026** (aithinkerlab). **DEPRECATED** — "MiniMax has launched a newer release, MiniMax-M3. We suggest considering it instead." Technical paper: *The MiniMax-M2 Series: Mini Activations Unleashing Max…* (arXiv 2605.26494).
- **IDs:** id `opencode/minimax-m2.7`; HF `MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-M2.7`; AA `minimax-m2-7`.
- **Context window:** **204,800 (205K class)** total / **131K max output**. (AA spec says 205k, AA FAQ says 200k — minor inconsistency, resolved to 204,800.)
- **Modalities:** **Text in / text out only.** Reasoning: **Yes** (AA shows the reasoning version; a non-reasoning variant may exist). Not multimodal, no image input.
- **Pricing:** **$0.30 / 1M input, $1.20 / 1M output** (cached **$0.06**; 80% cache discount); blended (7:2:1) **$0.22 / 1M**. Better than the class median ($0.50 in / $2.10 out). No free ID.
- **Architecture:** Mixture-of-Experts, **228.7B total / ~10B active** params (AA rounds to 230B/10B; FlexAI lists "11B active" as an outlier — resolved to ~10B per AA + HF). Hybrid sliding-window attention (SWA) plus **3 MTP (multi-token-prediction) modules** for speculative throughput at identical output quality. Trained with **Forge** (agent-native RL admitting white-box and API-only black-box agents in one loop) and **Terminal-Gym** (automated terminal-task synthesis). Non-commercial license.

Built on the **OpenClaw** agent harness, it ran **100+ rounds of scaffold optimization** during training (~30% improvement reported) and handled **30–50% of routine ML-engineering work**. The within-series gains M2 → M2.5 → M2.7 on agentic benchmarks reflect this. (It participates in its own training loop; it does NOT rewrite its own architecture.)

  - **Terminal-Bench 2.0: 57.0** (vendor). TB 2.0 is the older/vendor version — NOT the harder Terminal-Bench 4.0 in AA's v5.0 basket; do not conflate.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 77/100.** A broad vendor agentic suite — Terminal-Bench 2.0 57.0, MM Claw 62.7, BrowseComp 77.8, GDPval-AA 50.0, Toolathlon 46.3 — plus the genuinely novel self-evolution capability (autonomous scaffold editing, failed-run triage, multi-round self-improvement). Tempered because every number is vendor-only, TB is the older 2.0 version (not TB 4.0), and Toolathlon/GDPval are mid.
- **Reasoning: 78/100.** Elite vendor reasoning — AIME 2026 94.2 and GPQA-Diamond 89.8, frontier-approaching for a ~10B-active model — but 100% vendor with no independent verification (AA Intelligence N/A), so scored just below independently-anchored peers.
- **Context window: 76/100.** 204,800 (205K class) with a generous 131K max output — solid but unexceptional, and no long-context retrieval benchmark is published.
- **Multimodal: 50/100.** Text in / text out only; not multimodal and no image input (per AA spec + FAQ).
- **Coding: 78/100.** A broad, strong agentic-coding profile — SWE-bench Pro 56.2 (reported to match GPT-5.3-Codex), SWE-Multilingual 76.5, Multi-SWE 52.7, and an MLE-Bench Lite 66.6% medal rate ("second only to Opus-4.6 and GPT-5.4") — but entirely vendor-reported with no independent re-run, and SWE-Pro 56.2 is mid in absolute terms.
- **Cost efficiency: 80/100.** Cheap API ($0.30/$1.20, blended $0.22, 80% cache — better than the $0.50/$2.10 median; one review pegs it at "90% of Opus quality at 7% the cost") and open weights for self-hosting — but the NON-COMMERCIAL license blocks free commercial self-hosting (written authorization required), there are only 3 API providers, and it is notably slow (40.4 tok/s, 1/4). (Scored but NOT counted toward Overall.)
- **Overall Score: 72/100.** Half-up mean of the five quality dims (77+78+76+50+78)/5 = 71.8 → 72. Divergence **+5.4** vs the curated queue value 66.6 — driven by the elite vendor reasoning (AIME 2026 94.2 / GPQA-Diamond 89.8), the broad agentic-coding suite, and the novel self-evolution capability, against the curated heavier discount for vendor-only evidence (AA Intelligence N/A, no independent verification, community-flagged for scrutiny), deprecated status, non-commercial license, and slow speed. Sits a couple of points above its M2.5 predecessor (b12, Overall 70), consistent with M2.7's stronger reasoning and broader agentic benchmark suite. Cost efficiency excluded from the mean per methodology.
