## Model card

- **Name:** LongCat 2.5 Preview. Meituan's multimodal reasoning model for coding and agent tasks with image understanding and a 1M-token context window — **API/preview-only, with no GA "LongCat 2.5" release at all** (verified: official docs, models.dev, Hugging Face and Artificial Analysis all carry only the Preview and the older 2.0).
- **Short description:** A preview with **one measured composite and no standard benchmark of any kind**, whose own structure is the important caveat: **Bug Finding 2.4 and Edge-case 10.6 are extremely low while the two web-framework tasks carry the score**, so aicodingdaily's 46.65 is a framework-code-generation number, not a software-engineering number.
- **Provider / access:** LongCat API Open Platform (pay-as-you-go), with a **free OpenCode Zen route `longcat-2.5-preview-free`**. Also on the Vercel AI Gateway and NanoGPT. **Not on OpenRouter** (its Meituan roster carries only `longcat-2.0` and `longcat-flash-chat`). OpenAI- and Anthropic-format APIs both supported, and Claude-Code-compatible harnesses are documented.
- **Release / knowledge:** released **2026-09-25** (models.dev; a change log corrected an earlier 26-September reading). **Knowledge cutoff not disclosed.**
- **IDs:** `meituan/longcat-2.5-preview` (models.dev); `longcat-2.5-preview-free` (OpenCode Zen free route).
- **Context window:** **1,000,000 tokens** (models.dev; the LongCat first-party provider row says 1,048,576). **Max output 131,072** (models.dev, Vercel, OpenCode) — but **262,144** on the LongCat first-party row, and 128,000 per one secondary source; the models.dev figure is used and the conflict is recorded rather than reconciled.
- **Modalities:** **text and image in; text out** (models.dev description, Vercel, OpenCode). Reasoning Yes; tool calling Yes; thinking toggle. Structured output `-` (unset) on the canonical record, though the NanoGPT provider row says No.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-11-06):** **$0.30 in / $1.20 out per 1M with $0.006 cached input** on the LongCat first-party route and the Vercel AI Gateway (a limited-time promotional price, per Meituan's pricing docs). A **free OpenCode Zen slot** exists. One secondary provider (NanoGPT) lists $0.75/$2.95. models.dev's canonical row reads $0.00/$0.00 because it reflects the free OpenCode providers.
- **Architecture:** **proprietary, API-only, no weights and no published license.** Parameter count is **not officially disclosed**; a secondary claim of ~1.6T total / ~48B active (inherited from LongCat 2.0) circulates but is **unverified and is not used here**.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

> **Methodology note.** With one non-standard composite and no standard benchmark of any kind, four of the five quality dimensions below are placeholders-for-unknown anchored on the verified spec facts (1M window, text+image in, $0.30/$1.20 with a free Zen route, API-only) and the model's positioning. Each is labelled with what it rests on, and the Overall is explicitly a placeholder pending real benchmarks.

- **Tool use: 55/100.** Anchored at the mid band as a placeholder, with one weak directional signal: the composite's **Edge-case score of 10.6** is a real (if poor) agentic-problem-solving measurement, and the model ships **tool calling, a thinking toggle, and Claude-Code-compatible harnesses**. Scored at 55 rather than higher because **Terminal-Bench, τ²-bench, τ³-bench, MCP-Atlas, OSWorld, BrowseComp, Toolathlon, MazeBench, ALE, Claw-Eval, JobBench, GDPval-AA and the AA Agentic Index have no figure at all**, and the one figure that exists is poor.
- **Reasoning: 50/100.** **Scored at the methodology's neutral floor for "dimension claimed, entirely unmeasured."** Not one reasoning benchmark exists — no GPQA Diamond, HLE, AIME, MMLU-Pro, CritPt, AA-LCR, MRCR, ARC-AGI, FrontierMath, AA Intelligence Index or AA-Omniscience. Scored at 50 rather than lower because the model is explicitly a **reasoning** model with a **thinking toggle** and a 1M window; **not scored above 50 because five of five reasoning references are unmeasurable.**
- **Context window: 97/100.** **1,000,000 tokens with 131,072 max output** — a full frontier window with a genuinely generous output allowance, and the model's single most valuable verified property. **A spec conflict is recorded: the max-output figure is 131,072 on models.dev/Vercel/OpenCode against 262,144 on the LongCat first-party row and 128,000 on one secondary source** — the models.dev figure is used and the conflict is surfaced rather than resolved. Scored in the methodology's ≥1M tier at 97. Held just under 100 because **no AA-LCR, MRCR or GraphWalks figure exists**, so the window's usable depth is entirely unverified.
- **Multimodal: 62/100.** **Text and image in; text out**, per the models.dev description, Vercel and OpenCode, placing it in the methodology's 60–70 band. Scored at 62, at the band's lower edge, because the image-input claim is a **spec-sheet claim with zero measured support** — no MMMU-Pro, CharXiv, ExtractBench, BabyVision, ZeroBench, OmniDocBench, MathVision or Design Arena figure exists.
- **Coding: 50/100.** Anchored on the one measured row and its structure: the **aicodingdaily composite of 46.65, rank #41**, carried by **Laravel 16.98 and React-TS 16.67** — framework-specific code-generation tasks — against **Bug Finding 2.4 and Edge-case 10.6**. Scored at the mid band's floor because the composite measures a narrow slice (framework scaffolding) and the two general software-engineering components are near-floor. Capped by the **total absence of SWE-bench Verified, SWE-bench Pro, LiveCodeBench, SciCode, DeepSWE, Vibe Code Bench, Terminal-Bench and FrontierCode** — so the 85%, 74%, 70% and 55% references cannot be checked. Recorded explicitly as a framework-code-generation number, not a software-engineering number.
- **Cost efficiency: 92/100.** **$0.30/$1.20 per 1M with $0.006 cached input** (a limited-time promotional price) plus **a free OpenCode Zen route** — a genuinely strong card sitting between the methodology's ~$0.60/$2.20 (≈92) and ~$1.25/$4.25 (≈88) anchors, with a **98% cache-read discount**. Scored at 92 on the rate card plus the free route. Held at 92 rather than higher because the promotional price is explicitly time-limited, and because the model is **API-only with no weights and no published licence**, so there is no self-hosting escape; the free Zen route is the only no-cost option and carries the usual availability and data-use caveats.
- **Overall Score: 63/100.** (55 + 50 + 97 + 62 + 50) / 5 = 62.8 → **63**. Recommendation: **a preview with one narrow composite measurement and no standard benchmark — read the 69 as a placeholder, not a result.** Its two genuine assets are a **1M window with 131,072 output** and a **$0.30/$1.20 rate card with a free Zen route**, and its one measured figure (**aicodingdaily 46.65, rank #41**) is carried entirely by two web-framework tasks while its Bug Finding (2.4) and Edge-case (10.6) components are near-floor — **so it is a framework-code-generation model, not a software-engineering one.** Three cautions: **there is no GA "LongCat 2.5" release at all** (only this Preview and the older 2.0), so the SKU may not persist; **parameter count is undisclosed** (the circulating ~1.6T/48B figure is inherited from LongCat 2.0 and is not verified); and **the promotional price is time-limited**, with the model API-only and unlicenced. Benchmark it yourself on your own harness before relying on it — specifically SWE-bench Verified, Terminal-Bench and a general coding index — and re-score the moment an independent tracker picks it up.
