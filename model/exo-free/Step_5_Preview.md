### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 42/100.** Below the methodology's mid band, and the weakest dimension. **Native tool calling is confirmed by the catalogue record** and **236,908 completed agent sessions showed the route being driven through real harness work** — but **with zero published function-calling or agentic-benchmark scores**, the spec-only band is capped low-mid. The 99.2% cache-read share is indirect evidence that the route was serving genuinely long prompts through agent loops, and is recorded as such — **evidence of use, not of capability.**
- **Reasoning: 42/100.** Below the methodology's mid band, and unmeasured. **Reasoning mode was fixed at high effort**, which implies a reasoning-capable backend, but **no GPQA, HLE, AIME, MATH, AA-LCR, ARC-AGI or Intelligence Index value exists to place it higher** — a spec-only score capped at the mid band's floor on the strength of one configuration fact.
- **Context window: 85/100.** **A verified 1,048,576-token context with 131,072 max output** is the methodology's ≥1M band (95–100), **docked to 85 because no MRCR, RULER or needle-retrieval curve exists** — the **99.2% cache-read share was indirect evidence of genuinely long prompts, not of retrieval accuracy**, and that distinction is stated because it is the difference between a measured property and an inferred one. Held at 85 rather than the band's floor because a 1M window at a free route is a real asset and the telemetry corroborates long-prompt use.
- **Multimodal: 60/100.** **Image input is acknowledged by trackers**, placing it in the methodology's image-input band (60–70), **scored at the floor because no vision benchmark (MMMU, DocVQA, Math-Vision) was published** and **audio and video input are not supported** — a modality claim with no measurement behind it.
- **Coding: 40/100.** Below the methodology's mid band, and unmeasured. **No SWE-bench, Terminal-Bench or LiveCodeBench figure exists.** Held at 40 rather than the floor because **the route was demonstrably popular with coding-harness traffic (236,908 sessions)**, which supports function but not quality — and the telemetry is recorded as adoption evidence rather than capability evidence.
- **Cost efficiency: 95/100.** **$0.00/$0.00 was the methodology's $0 tier**, docked five points for **the limited-time terms and the explicit data-collection clause** — both real costs at a zero price, and both recorded rather than glossed. **The route is now withdrawn (HTTP 410), so the price is historical and unavailable**, which is stated because a cost score of 95 for an unreachable endpoint describes a past state.
- **Overall Score: 54/100.** (42 + 42 + 85 + 60 + 40) / 5 = 53.8 → **54** → **54**. **A near-agreement is recorded: this report's 54 sits within 1.8 points of the repo queue's 55.8**, and both are consistent with BenchLM's decision to carry no page for the model at all. Recommendation: **a withdrawn free stealth route with zero benchmarks and one genuine asset.** Its 1,048,576-token context at $0 with image input and reasoning fixed at high was, for a limited window in October 2026, a genuinely unusual free proposition — **236,908 completed agent sessions and a 99.2% cache-read share show it was used hard and used for long prompts**, which is more than most stealth routes can evidence. Three cautions: **the route is withdrawn (HTTP 410)**, so nothing here is reachable; **every score except cost is a spec-only or telemetry-only inference**, since not one benchmark exists for this model; and **the architecture and backend identity were never published**, so even the spec facts are catalogue facts about a route rather than measurements of a model.
