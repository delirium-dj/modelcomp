## Model card

- **Name:** Solar Mini 4 (`solar-mini4`)
- **Short description:** Upstage's (Korea) **compact agentic model**, released **2026-09-22** — a **35B-total / 3B-active MoE**, pretrained from the ground up by Upstage and aimed squarely at **repetitive, high-volume agent workloads where cost per step dominates**. Its headline is a new **Pareto-optimal point on Intelligence Index vs. active parameters for sub-3B-active models**: it scores 24.1 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, the best of any model with 3B active parameters, while cutting per-token pricing by a third versus Upstage's own flagship line. Top use case: document parsing, structured information extraction, classification and multi-step rule-based workflows at volume. **Proprietary — weights are not released and no licence is published.** It is now **#2 of three models in Upstage's own Solar Pro / Solar Mini / Solar Open lineup**, behind Solar Pro 4 (AA Index 27).
- **Provider / access:** Upstage Console API (`solar-mini4`, alias `solar-mini4-260922`), Solar Chat, Playground, **OpenRouter** (`upstage/solar-mini4`), and **on-premises deployment**. OpenAI-compatible base URL `https://api.upstage.ai/v1`, Chat Completions. **No OpenCode Zen ID found.** No published rate limit for this model.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released **2026-09-22** (OpenRouter lists 2026-09-23; Artificial Analysis's release article is dated 2026-09-30). **Training data cut-off: February 2026.** It supersedes `solar-mini-250422` (10.7B), which Upstage deprecates **fully on 2026-10-30 (KST)** with an explicit instruction to migrate to the `solar-mini4` alias.
- **IDs:** `solar-mini4` (Upstage alias), `solar-mini4-260922` (dated snapshot), `upstage/solar-mini4` (OpenRouter)
- **Context window:** **524,288 tokens (512K)**, max output **128K–131,072**. Verified on Upstage Console's model page, Upstage's model-history page, and the console's model-comparison table — all three first-party surfaces say 512K / 128K. **Discrepancy still flagged and still unresolved:** Artificial Analysis's release article *and* its live model page both say **1M context / 262K max output**, contradicting every first-party and provider source (OpenRouter and LM Market Cap both say 524,288 / 131,072). **The first-party 512K / 128K is used here**; AA's figures are not credited.
- **Modalities:** **Text in → text out only.** Zeplik explicitly records "Vision (image input): No"; aimlapi lists `Text → Text`; Artificial Analysis: "supports text input, outputs text." Reasoning: **yes**, with a documented `reasoning_effort` parameter. Languages: **English, Korean (fluent) and Japanese**. Capabilities per Upstage Console: Chat, structured outputs, tool calling, reasoning, streaming, and **parallel tool calling**.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-17):** Upstage list **$0.10 in / $0.40 out / $0.01 cached** per 1M (excl. 10% VAT). **The launch promotion runs in two phases, which the first pass missed and this pass corrects:** phase 1 was **$0.03 / $0.003 / $0.12 (70% off) from 2026-09-22 to 2026-10-11**, phase 2 is **$0.05 / $0.005 / $0.20 (50% off) from 2026-10-11 to 2026-10-23 00:00 UTC**, after which list applies. **Conflict recorded:** the first pass and Upstage's own social copy say the discount ends "October 22"; Upstage's pricing-page structured data and a 2026-10-16 independent writeup both give **2026-10-23 00:00 UTC**, which is preferred here as the machine-readable first-party field. No general Free API tier; third-party resellers charge more (aimlapi: $0.13754/$0.55016).
- **Architecture:** **Proprietary — weights not released, and (checked 2026-10-12) Upstage publishes no licence and no open-weights statement for this model on either its model page or its model-history page.** **35B total / 3B active** MoE. Upstage's own wording: "the full 35B model weights must still be loaded into memory," and a quantized form runs on a **single 80 GB H100** on-premises. **The full 35B is therefore the memory floor, not the 3B.** Artificial Analysis notes that, as a proprietary model, "its size cannot be independently verified."
- **Privacy:** Upstage states **API input data is not collected, not used for model training, and not stored** unless required for service delivery and explicitly stated — a materially better data posture than most inference providers.
- **Downstream service built on this model (not a separate model):** `solar-decide` (beta), a **System One structured-decision endpoint** served on Solar Mini 4 at the same 2026-09-22 release date and the same 512K context, returning a choice/score/yes-no with a calibrated probability in a single forward pass, with **output tokens free**. Relevant to this project's `decision` category, but it is a service on top of Mini 4 and carries no separate weights or scores.

### Raw benchmarks found

> Independent figures are from Artificial Analysis (release article 2026-09-30 and its live model/provider tables) and from Plotly's own private data-analytics exam. **Correction to the first pass:** the SciCode and AA-LCR figures the first pass attributed to "Upstage's launch blog" are Artificial Analysis measurements — Upstage published **no benchmark table at all** for Solar Mini 4 (its changelog entry carries specs and no scores), so they move from vendor-reported to independent. Conversely, the **τ³-Banking 47.2** figure attributed to Upstage's launch blog **could not be re-verified anywhere this pass** and is treated below as unsourced.

Reasoning / knowledge:

- **Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index: 24.1 / 24.05** (Index v4.3.2) — **the highest score among all models with 3B active parameters**. For calibration: **+16 points over Upstage's own previous-generation flagship Solar Pro 3 (8)**; **6 points above Qwen3.6 35B-A3B (Reasoning)**, which has the same 3B active parameters; **1 point above Nemotron 3 Ultra**, which has 55B active; **below K2 Horizon MoVA 36B-A4B (25.3 with 4B active)**. Ranked **#143** on BenchmarkList's AA-Index import table (2026-10-03); "well above average among comparable models (median: 13–18)".
- **AA-LCR: 83.3%** (83.33, **96.4th percentile** — one of the model's strongest absolute numbers). Level with MiniMax-M3 and GPT-6 Luna (max); ahead of Gemini 3.8 Flash and GPT-6 Astra at 81%.
- **HLE: 25.8%** (25.76) — **new this pass**; the first pass recorded "no verified public score found". Independently measured by AA.
- **CritPt (research-level physics): 1.4%** (1.43) — **new this pass**. Bottom of a 210-model board: Solar Pro 4 scores 5.4%, Solar Pro 3 0.0%, Qwen3.5-35B-A3B 0.9%. AA flags SciCode and CritPt as "Under review" evaluations, which tempers how much weight either should carry.
- **AA-Omniscience: −10.8 / −11, with Accuracy of just 18.4%** — but a **non-hallucination rate of 64.2%** (hallucination rate 35.8%, 88.6th percentile), well ahead of Inkling (xhigh) at 32% and GPT-6 Luna (max) at 23%. AA's read: the model **abstains on about half of questions** rather than guessing. Domain Omniscience indices: Business −8.0, Humanities & Social Sciences −7.6, Science/Engineering/Math −6.1, Law −7.5, Health −20.1.
- Token verbosity: **88k output tokens per Intelligence Index task** (including ~72k internal reasoning tokens), averaging **7.1 minutes per task** — the slowest-task profile in its class. AA's live figure: **370M tokens generated** across the index against a 96M median.
- GPQA Diamond / MMLU-Pro: **no verified public score found** for this model (unchanged).

Agent / tool use:

- **Terminal-Bench 4.0: 1.0%** (Artificial Analysis, AA's own 66-task mini-swe-agent harness, pass@1 over three repeats) — AA states plainly: *"**Agentic coding is a relative weakness**"*
- **AutomationBench-AA: 22.3%** (Artificial Analysis) — low
- **GDPval-AA: 1072 Elo**; **AA-Briefcase: 872 Elo** (both Artificial Analysis) — both "close to Inkling (xhigh)"
- **τ³-Banking: 47.2** — **attributed by the first pass to Upstage's launch blog; NOT re-verifiable this pass and now treated as unsourced.** Upstage appears to have published no benchmark table for this model at all. Do not credit it.
- **Plotly data-analytics exam (independent, published 2026-10-17): 21 of 43 questions correct (49%)** for **$1.04** per full run — the first genuinely independent, task-level agentic evaluation of this model. Breakdown: analytics 10/20, **impossible requests 5/5** (it declined every unanswerable question), ambiguous 3/5, statistics traps 3/5, **data quality 0/3** (missed duplicated rows and a missing day even when told "make sure they're right"), **hidden needles 0/5** (found none of five planted patterns). **28 seconds median per question, ~28 model calls and ~2.6M tokens read per question.** Plotly's verdict: cheap per token, expensive per question, "not yet accurate enough for unsupervised analysis".
- Design Arena Elo (independent human preference, 2026-10): **Code categories 1158, Website 1159, Game development 1140**.
- Tau2-Bench other domains, OSWorld, Claw-Eval / ClawProBench: no verified public score found
- Tool calling and **parallel tool calling** are both supported — a genuine capability with no published benchmark attached

Coding:

- **SciCode: 47.6%** — **Artificial Analysis's own measurement** (first pass mis-attributed this to Upstage's blog). 48%, one point above MiniMax-M3 and Inkling (xhigh) at 47%.
- **Terminal-Bench 4.0: 1.0%** (see above)
- SWE-bench Verified / SWE-bench Pro / LiveCodeBench / DeepSWE / Vibe Code Bench: **no verified public score found for this model** (unchanged)
- Design Arena code-preference Elo 1158 (see above) — a preference signal, not a correctness signal
- Upstage's own positioning is code *review*, *test generation* and *mechanical migrations* across a mid-sized codebase (per aimlapi's use-case writeup) — not frontier code generation

Long context:

- **524,288 tokens native** with up to 128K output, per first-party docs (three separate Upstage surfaces agree).
- **AA-LCR at 83.3%** is real, independent long-context retrieval evidence and is one of the model's strongest measured numbers — notably *better* than its Intelligence Index, which is unusual and suggests the architecture handles retrieval better than it handles reasoning.
- No MRCR / RULER / GraphWalks numbers.

Speed / cost efficiency inputs (Artificial Analysis, plus one speed conflict worth recording):

- **Speed conflict — recorded, not smoothed.** Artificial Analysis's **launch article (2026-09-30)** reports **208 tokens/s** (alphasignal's transcription says 204), and the first pass used that figure. AA's **live provider/model tables now report a median of 80–81 tok/s** for Solar Mini 4 (with TTFT 1.82 s, total response 32.77 s, reasoning time 24.77 s), and a third-party tracker reports 95 tok/s. **Preferred here: AA's live median (80–81 tok/s)**, because it is a rolling multi-provider measurement rather than a single launch snapshot, and because the "fast but slow tasks" framing survives either way. The 208 figure is kept, labelled, as the launch-day reading.
- **Cost per Intelligence Index task: $0.37** (AA's live table; the 2026-09-30 article said $0.36) — versus roughly **$0.07 for GPT-6 Luna (max)**, i.e. still ~5× more per completed task despite broadly similar per-token prices. AA's release headline states this plainly.
- Root cause per AA: **88k output tokens per task** plus a **48% cache hit rate on repeated context**, versus 99% for GPT-6 Luna. Uncached input alone is about $0.30 of the total. Agentic tasks resend the growing conversation each turn, so cache behaviour dominates real cost. Plotly's independent run reproduces the shape exactly: ~28 calls and ~2.6M tokens read per question.
- **Blended price $0.07 per 1M tokens** is the **cheapest of any of Upstage's three models** (Solar Pro 4 $0.22, Solar Pro 3 $0.10) on AA's 7:2:1 cache/input/output blend.
- Upstage's own counter-comparison: 1,000 task sets cost **$1.25 with Solar Mini 4 vs $2.20 with MiMo-V2.5** (~43% cheaper) — but Upstage concedes Solar Mini 4 was called **directly through the Upstage API while MiMo-V2.5 went through a third-party router**, and that **latency was excluded**. Treat the 43% figure as workload- and harness-specific, not general.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 57/100** (was 58). **Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 1.0%, AutomationBench-AA at 22.3%, GDPval-AA 1072 and AA-Briefcase 872** describe a small agent, and **parallel tool calling** is genuinely supported. **Lowered by one point on evidence quality, not on new capability:** the τ³-Banking 47.2 figure the first pass leaned on is not traceable to any published Upstage table (two independent writeups state Upstage published no benchmark table for this model), so one of the four agentic pillars behind the old 58 is now unsourced. The one fresh *independent* agentic datapoint, Plotly's 2026-10-17 exam, is a genuine contribution to this dimension but cuts against it: 49% overall, **0 of 5 hidden needles**, **0 of 3 data-quality checks**, and ~28 calls / 2.6M tokens burned per question. Capped by the absence of Tau2-Bench other domains, OSWorld and Claw-Eval.
- **Reasoning: 68/100** (was 70). **AA Intelligence Index 24.1 remains the best in the 3B-active class** (+16 over Upstage's own prior flagship) and **AA-LCR 83.3%** is genuinely strong. **Lowered by two points on two new independent sub-scores that the first pass listed as "not found" and that are both weak: HLE at 25.8% and CritPt at 1.4%, the latter bottom-decile on a 210-model board.** Also held down by AA-Omniscience at −10.8 with only 18.4% accuracy, the 7.1-minute average task time, 88k output tokens per task, and the still-total absence of GPQA Diamond and MMLU-Pro data. The 64.2% non-hallucination rate and Plotly's 5/5 on impossible requests are real reliability pluses and are credited in this judgment, not as a capability score.
- **Context window: 88/100** (unchanged). **524,288 tokens native** (first-party, now confirmed on three separate Upstage surfaces) — upper-mid tier — with **AA-LCR 83.3%** at the 96.4th percentile as strong, independent retrieval evidence. Still not credited the 1M / 262K that AA's article and live model page claim, since every first-party and provider source says 512K / 128K and the conflict remains unresolved.
- **Multimodal: 15/100** (unchanged). **Text-only**, confirmed by three sources this pass (Zeplik "Vision: No", aimlapi `Text → Text`, AA "supports text input, outputs text"). Floor score by methodology. Upstage's vision-capable line is a separate model.
- **Coding: 55/100** (unchanged). **SciCode 47.6%** is respectable for the class — and this pass **upgrades its provenance from vendor-reported to independent** (it is AA's measurement), which improves confidence in the score without changing it, since no new coding number appeared. Upstage targets code review, test generation and mechanical migrations rather than frontier generation. Capped hard by **Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 1.0%**, AA's explicit "agentic coding is a relative weakness" verdict, and **no SWE-bench, SWE-bench Pro, LiveCodeBench or DeepSWE figure existing** for this model.
- **Cost efficiency: 93/100** (was 94). **$0.10 in / $0.40 out / $0.01 cached** is genuinely cheap — **the cheapest blended price ($0.07/M) of any Upstage model** — with **on-premises deployment down to a single quantized 80 GB H100**, free output tokens on the `solar-decide` System One endpoint, and Upstage's unusually strong privacy posture (no data collected, trained on, or stored). **Lowered by one point on three new facts:** the promo is **phased and has already stepped up once** (70% off ended 2026-10-11; the 50% round ends **2026-10-23**, six days after this pass), so anyone budgeting on $0.03 is already wrong and anyone on $0.05 has six days left; **per-task cost rose to $0.37**; and the **speed offset is weaker than the first pass assumed** — AA's live median is 80–81 tok/s, not the 208 tok/s of the launch snapshot. AA still finds it ~5× dearer per task than GPT-6 Luna: **88k output tokens per task** and a **48% cache hit rate** (versus 99%) remain the real cost drivers.
- **Overall Score: 57/100** (unchanged). Arithmetic: **(Tool 57 + Reasoning 68 + Context 88 + Multimodal 15 + Coding 55) / 5 = 283 / 5 = 56.6 → 57** (half-up, cost efficiency excluded per `RULES.md`). Best fit: **very high-volume, repetitive, latency-tolerant agentic work — document parsing, structured extraction, classification and rule-based validation loops — where Upstage's Korean fluency, 512K context, strong AA-LCR, single-GPU on-premises option and no-data-collection posture are decisive.** Not the pick for agentic coding (Terminal-Bench 4.0 = 1%), for factual recall (18.4% omniscience accuracy, by choice) or for unsupervised analysis (Plotly's 49% with 0/5 hidden needles). Verify the post-2026-10-23 price and measure real cache behaviour on your own workload before committing, because AA finds it ~5× dearer per task than the headline tariff suggests.
