> **Verdict:** Qwen3.5-9B is **Alibaba's current (released 2026-03-02) dense 9.65B open-weight multimodal reasoner** — the smallest of the "big" Qwen3.5 dense line and, per **Artificial Analysis, "the most intelligent model under 10B parameters."** It is a **unified vision-language** model (**text + image + video in → text out**, early-fusion, AA-confirmed video), **Apache 2.0**, **262K native context** (vendor-extensible to ~1M), 32K out, with the family's **hybrid thinking/non-thinking** reasoning. At release its **AA Intelligence Index was 32** (pre-v5 basket) — **~2× the next-closest sub-10B** (Falcon-H1R-7B 16, Nemotron Nano 9B V2 15) and **+15 over Qwen3 VL 8B (17)** — and it **leads all sub-15B models on MMMU-Pro (69.2%, AA-measured)**. Vendor cards add **tau2 86.8%** (telecom tool calls) and **LiveCodeBench v6 65.6%**. The catches are real: under the **new AA Intelligence Index v5.0 (launched 2026-10-27) its Index is N/A** ("no publicly available API" — only 2 providers, so AA can't run the 9 private-dataset evals); it is **extremely verbose** (~**260M** output tokens to run the Index, ~3× the 27B's 98M), **slow** (53.7 tok/s vs the 86.5 class median), and **weak on AA-Omniscience** (−56; **82% hallucination**, 14.7% accuracy). It is cheap ($0.14/$0.20 hosted; ~6 GB 4-bit self-host on consumer hardware). Net: **Overall 78 (divergence +6.0 vs the curated 72)** — the best small open multimodal reasoner of its generation, whose verified sub-10B intelligence lead, confirmed video modality and 262K context I credit above the curator's heavy small-model penalty, while its 9B ceiling, verbosity, hallucination and v5.0-N/A keep it out of the mid-tier.

- **Tool use: 77/100.** Strong vendor agentic signal — **tau2 86.8%** (telecom tool calls) and **native tool calling** (Together-confirmed) with hybrid reasoning — but **no independent agentic verification** (AA v5.0 tool/agentic evals are N/A, no public API), and the meta's own tradeoff holds: at 9B "*it assists, larger models architect.*"
- **Reasoning: 78/100.** The standout dimension for its size — **AA Intelligence Index 32 (pre-v5) = most-intelligent sub-10B**, ~2× Falcon-H1R-7B and **+15 over Qwen3 VL 8B**, with hybrid thinking/non-thinking. Capped by the **9B absolute ceiling** (Index 32 is low vs frontier 45+), the **v5.0 Index now N/A**, a **weak AA-Omniscience (−56, 82% hallucination)** and extreme verbosity.
- **Context window: 80/100.** **262,144 native / 32,768 out**, vendor-extensible to ~**1M** (rope scaling, family-standard, not independently benchmarked). Solid and matches the larger Qwen3.5 siblings; the ~1M claim is vendor-only, so it sits at the strong-262K tier rather than the proven-1M tier.
- **Multimodal: 82/100.** **Text + image + video in** (AA-confirmed, unified early-fusion VL that matches/exceeds Qwen3-VL) with an **independently-measured MMMU-Pro 69.2% — best of all sub-15B models**. Held below the omni tier by **text-only output**, **no audio**, and the 9B's absolute multimodal ceiling.
- **Coding: 75/100.** **LiveCodeBench v6 65.6%** is respectable for a 9B, but it is **vendor-only** (no SWE-bench/Terminal-Bench published; AA v5.0 coding evals N/A) and the small dense ceiling limits repo-scale work — present-but-modest.
- **Cost efficiency: 88/100.** *(Not counted in Overall.)* Excellent on paper — **Apache 2.0 open weights**, ~**6 GB at 4-bit** (runs on laptops/phones), **$0 self-host** and cheap hosted (**$0.14/$0.20** AA median). Capped below the low-90s by **extreme verbosity (~260M output tokens/task, ~3× the 27B)** and **slow 53.7 tok/s**, which inflate real-world per-task cost and wall-clock time.
- **Overall Score: 78/100.** Half-up mean of the five quality dims: (77 + 78 + 80 + 82 + 75) / 5 = 392 / 5 = **78.4 → 78**. **Divergence +6.0** vs the curated 72. Qwen3.5-9B is the **best small open multimodal reasoner of its generation** — AA-verified most-intelligent sub-10B, class-leading MMMU-Pro, confirmed video input, 262K/1M context, Apache weights and pocket-hardware cost — but a **9B absolute ceiling, ~260M-token verbosity, 82% hallucination, slow speed and a v5.0 Index that is now N/A** keep it out of the mid-tier. My **+6.0** over the curated 72 reflects crediting its **independently-verified sub-10B intelligence lead + confirmed video multimodality + open 262K context** more than the curator's apparently heavy small-model penalty, while still marking down the verbosity, faithfulness and absent current-basket Index.
