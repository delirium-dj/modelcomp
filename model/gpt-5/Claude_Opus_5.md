## Model card

- **Name:** GPT-5. Variant/alias flag: this entry covers the **reasoning (medium) effort configuration**, which is how benchmark trackers index the base `gpt-5` ID; separate **GPT-5 (high)**, **GPT-5 mini** and **GPT-5 nano** entries exist. **This is the oldest OpenAI model in this part of the queue and is comprehensively superseded** — a composite tracker places it **#107 of 783 at 47.07/100**, behind twenty later OpenAI models including every GPT-5.x and GPT-6.x release.
- **Short description:** The original GPT-5. Its most striking published figure is not a capability number but a reliability one: **an AA-Omniscience Index of −10.9%, the only negative score recorded in this entire research pass**, driven by an **83.2% hallucination rate** against 39.5% accuracy — on a benchmark that penalises confident wrong answers and applies no penalty for declining to answer, a negative index means its confabulations outweigh its correct answers. It also records **CritPt at 0.0%**, a literal zero on research-level physics reasoning.
- **Provider / access:** OpenAI API and ChatGPT; OpenRouter (`openai/gpt-5`). Reasoning: effort-configurable (this entry = medium).
- **Release / knowledge:** Released in the original GPT-5 cycle, before GPT-5.1. **No verified public release date or knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `gpt-5`; `openai/gpt-5` (OpenRouter). Siblings: `gpt-5-mini`, `gpt-5-nano`. **No free-tier ID.**
- **Context window:** **128,000 tokens** — the smallest in this part of the dataset by a wide margin, and a quarter of GPT-5.2's 400K or an eighth of the 1.05M that every later OpenAI flagship offers. Max output: no verified figure found (a later comparison records 32K for this generation). Long-context evidence: **AA-LCR at 76.0%**, respectable for the window size.
- **Modalities:** **Text and image input → text output.** No audio or video. A published AA-MMMU-Pro figure confirms the vision path.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **$2.50 / 1M input, $10.00 / 1M output** — taken from OpenAI's own comparison table in the GPT-5.2 launch analysis, which lists GPT-5 at those rates against GPT-5.2's $1.75/$14.00. **Re-verify against OpenAI's live pricing page**; no primary source was retrievable for this model.
- **Architecture:** Proprietary, closed weights. Parameter count and topology: not disclosed.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 68/100.** **τ²-bench at 86.5% clears the methodology's Tau-family ~50%+ frontier reference**, and it is the only agentic measurement in existence for this model. **All six of the methodology's other tool-use references are unreported** — no Terminal-Bench at any version, no Tau3-Banking, no GDPval-AA, no Claw-Eval, no OSWorld or AutomationBench, no MCP-Atlas or Toolathlon — which triggers the explicit missing-evidence penalty. 68 reflects one solid datapoint with nothing to corroborate or contradict it; it is not a demonstrated agentic capability. The **128K context** is also a practical constraint on agent loops, since multi-turn histories with replayed reasoning exhaust it far faster than the 1M windows peers now offer.
- **Reasoning: 62/100.** Two figures are creditable — **AA-IFBench at 70.6%** and **AA-LCR at 76.0%** — and MATH-500 at 99.1% is near-saturated, though that benchmark no longer discriminates. Everything else is below reference or actively bad. **GPQA Diamond at 84.2% misses the 90%+ reference by 5.8 points**; **HLE at 25.4% misses the 40%+ reference by ~15**; **the Intelligence Index of 22.9% is mid-band**, 37 points off the frontier marker; **CritPt is a literal 0.0%**; and most seriously, **the AA-Omniscience Index of −10.9% is the only negative score in this entire dataset** — with an 83.2% hallucination rate against 39.5% accuracy, the benchmark's own arithmetic says this model produces more confidently-wrong answers than correct ones. For any factual or analytical use that is a disqualifying property, not a rough edge.
- **Context window: 56/100.** **128,000 tokens** places it in the methodology's 100K–200K tier (50–64), toward the lower half since 128K is only modestly above the tier floor. **AA-LCR at 76.0%** is respectable for a window this size and lifts it within the band. Two things hold it down: the window is **a quarter of GPT-5.2's 400K and an eighth of the 1.05M that every later OpenAI flagship offers**, so it is not merely smaller but a different class; and there is **no MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks retrieval measurement**, with max output tokens also undocumented (a later comparison records 32K for this generation, which would be a quarter of the current 128K norm).
- **Multimodal: 66/100.** Input is **text and image** with text-only output and no audio or video, placing it in the methodology's "+image in = 60–70" band. **AA-MMMU-Pro at 74.3%** is the single measurement and is mid-field — roughly 12 points behind GPT-6.1 Sol's 86.0% and 10 behind Gemini 3.5 Flash. **Design Arena Website at 1,191 Elo is the lowest recorded in this research pass**, which is a real signal on visual-design output quality. **No CharXiv, document, OCR, video or audio benchmark exists**, so chart and document capability is entirely unmeasured. 66 reflects one mid-field datapoint plus one poor one.
- **Coding: 55/100.** **No coding benchmark of any kind exists for this model** — not SWE-bench Verified, not SWE-bench Pro, not LiveCodeBench, not SciCode, not DeepSWE, not Vibe Code Bench, not Terminal-Bench at any version, not even an AA Coding Index. **Every one of the methodology's coding references is unchecked.** This is the only model in this part of the dataset with zero coding evidence, and 55 reflects an absence of evidence rather than a measurement. One adjacent datapoint argues against inferring strength: its successor GPT-5.1, which is itself comprehensively superseded, measures an **AA Coding Index of 49.4%** and **Vibe Code Bench at 24.61%** — so the generational baseline here is low.
- **Cost efficiency: 62/100.** At **$2.50 / $10.00** it is marginally better than the $3/$15 anchor that maps to ~60, and that is essentially the whole case. Four deductions keep it from going higher. **The price is unconfirmed by any primary source** — it comes from a competitor-generation comparison table and must be re-verified. **The capability-per-dollar position is indefensible**: a composite tracker places it #107 of 783, behind twenty later OpenAI models, and **GPT-5.2 at $1.75/$14.00 offers a 400K window with a 90% cached-input discount** while **GPT-5.6 Terra at $2.00/$12.00 offers 1.05M context and vastly higher measured capability**. **No free tier** exists. And the **128K window** forces chunking on work that peers handle in a single call, adding orchestration cost the rate card does not capture.
- **Overall Score: 61.4/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (68 + 62 + 56 + 66 + 55) / 5 = 61.4 — a comprehensively superseded model whose published evidence contains one genuine red flag. The honest summary of what is known: **13 benchmarks, all from one independent evaluator, no OpenAI-published figures at all, and no coding evidence whatsoever.** Of what exists, τ²-bench (86.5%), AA-IFBench (70.6%), AA-LCR (76.0%) and MATH-500 (99.1%) are creditable; GPQA Diamond, HLE, the Intelligence Index, CritPt (0.0%), MMMU-Pro and Design Arena are all below reference or poor. The finding that should govern any decision is the **AA-Omniscience Index of −10.9% with an 83.2% hallucination rate** — the only negative reliability score in this dataset, meaning the model's confident errors outnumber its correct answers on a benchmark that explicitly rewards abstention. **Do not select this model for any new workload.** For factual or analytical work it is actively unsafe without a verification layer; for coding there is no evidence it is competent; for long-context work 128K is a different class from the 1M peers now offer. Within OpenAI's own range, **GPT-5.6 Terra at $2/$12 offers 1.05M context, six reasoning-effort levels including `none`, the full agentic tool surface and a composite score of 73.21** — a 26-point gain for a comparable price.
