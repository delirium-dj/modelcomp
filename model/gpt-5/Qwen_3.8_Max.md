## Model card

- **Name:** GPT-5 (this dataset maps to Artificial Analysis's **"GPT-5 (High)"** — the reasoning lane at high effort). Variants: **GPT-5** (routed Instant/Thinking), **GPT-5 mini**, **GPT-5 nano**, and **GPT-5 Pro** (max extended reasoning).
- **Short description:** **OpenAI's August-2025 flagship and "where the router era began" — a single model that silently routes each request between a fast non-reasoning lane (Instant) and a deep reasoning lane (Thinking).** At launch it was **the top scorer on several independent boards**: **VALS AI (Aug 8, 2025) put it #1 on AIME 2025 (93.4%) and MMMU (81.5%), #2 on GPQA (85.6%, behind Grok 4's 88.1%) and MMLU Pro (87.0%, behind Claude Opus 4.1's 87.8%), with MATH 500 96.0%**; OpenAI's official numbers were **SWE-bench Verified 74.9%**, **AIME 2025 94.6%**, **GPQA 85.7%**, **MMMU 84.2%**, and **GPT-5 Pro set a then-SOTA GPQA of 88.4%** (no tools). ⚠ **Now deprecated with removal scheduled** — superseded by **GPT-5.1** (Nov 2025) then **GPT-5.2** (Dec 2025). Its **only current independent anchor is the AA Intelligence Index v4.3.2 = 23 (an *estimate*, #138/215, *below* the 27 median)**, and **OpenAI retired SWE-bench Verified in Feb 2026** ("increasingly contaminated"), while VALS **never actually listed** GPT-5's SWE-bench run ("awaiting board") — so its headline coding number stays **vendor-only**.
- **Provider / access:** **OpenAI.** First-party API (`gpt-5`, `gpt-5-mini`, `gpt-5-nano`, `gpt-5-pro`) and ChatGPT. Also resold via **OpenCode Zen** at a discount. **Proprietary** (weights/parameters undisclosed). `noFreeId: true`. Listed here as `opencode/gpt-5`.
- **Release / knowledge:** **2025-08-07.** ⚠ **Deprecated with removal scheduled** — AA flags it ("**OpenAI has launched a newer release, GPT-5.1. We suggest considering it instead**") and only re-benchmarks the **default 10k-input workload**. **Knowledge cutoff: Sep 30, 2024.**
- **IDs:** `gpt-5` / `gpt-5-pro` / `gpt-5-mini` / `gpt-5-nano` (OpenAI API) / `gpt-5` (Artificial Analysis slug) / `opencode/gpt-5` (this dataset).
- **Context window:** **400,000 total (≈272,000 input + 128,000 max output).** *Verified against the meta's "400K total (128K max output)" and AA's "400k."* ~600 A4 pages. Competitive for its generation but trails the 1M tier (Grok 4.3, DeepSeek V4 Pro, Claude).
- **Modalities:** **Text + image + file in → text out.** *Verified against the meta's "Text, image, file in; text out."* Strong document/vision utility — **MMMU 81.5% (VALS, #1 at launch) / 84.2% (official)** on chart/diagram/PDF reasoning. **No native video or audio.** (AA lists only "text and image"; the meta's "file" input is real — PDF/spreadsheet/document ingestion.)
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-25):** **OpenAI $1.25 input / $10.00 output per 1M** (cached **$0.125**, 90% discount; AA blended **$1.34**); **OpenCode Zen $1.07 / $8.50** (a ~15% cheaper resale tier). *Verified against the meta's tiered pricing.* AA calls the input price "very competitive" (median $2.00); a typical coding ticket (~10K in / 2.5K out) lands at **~$0.04 per clean solve**, helped by "fewer tool calls and shorter chains than o3."
- **Architecture:** **Proprietary; parameter count undisclosed.** The signature is the **real-time router**: a lightweight classifier picks the **fast (Instant, non-reasoning)** or **deep (Thinking, reasoning)** lane per request, with a `reasoning_effort` control. **GPT-5 Pro** is the maximum-extended-reasoning variant (GPQA 88.4% SOTA at launch, AIME reported at/near 100%). This "one model, two gears, auto-selected" design is the meta's standout and defined the 5.x/6.x lineage that followed.

### Raw benchmarks found

GPT-5 is unusual among deprecated models in that it received **genuine independent (VALS AI) verification at launch** — so I can separate the vendor story from the measured one, and flag where they diverge.

**Independent — VALS AI (Aug 8, 2025) + Artificial Analysis + lastexam.ai:**

- **AIME 2025 (no tools): 93.4% (VALS)** — **#1 among peers** (GPT-5 mini 90.8, Grok 4 90.6, Gemini 2.5 Pro 85.8, Claude Opus 4.1 78.2); aligns with OpenAI's official **94.6%**.
- **GPQA (no tools): 85.6% (VALS)** — virtually identical to the official **85.7%**; **#2 behind Grok 4's 88.1%**.
- **MMLU Pro: 87.0% (VALS)** — just behind Claude Opus 4.1's 87.8%.
- **MMMU (multimodal): 81.5% (VALS)** — **#1**, ahead of Gemini 2.5 Pro Exp (81.3); official **84.2%**.
- **MATH 500: 96.0% (VALS)** — a hair under Grok 4's 96.2%.
- **AA Intelligence Index v4.3.2 = 23 (Estimated — "independent evaluation forthcoming"), #138/215, *below* the 27 median.** AA: "**below average in intelligence, but reasonably priced**." Deprecated → only the 10k-input workload is still benchmarked.
- **AA speed 89.0 tok/s** (#85/186 — "faster than average," median 82). **Humanity's Last Exam 25.3% (no tools) / 50.0% (with tools)** (lastexam.ai).
- **SWE-bench Verified: "Not yet listed — awaiting board" on VALS** → the 74.9% is **unverified independently**.

**Vendor — OpenAI (launch, Aug 2025):**

- **SWE-bench Verified 74.9%** at high reasoning effort, "**with fewer tool calls and fewer output tokens than o3**" — the efficiency (not just the percentage) was the launch story.
- **GPT-5 Pro: GPQA 88.4%** (a new SOTA without tools at launch) · **HumanEval 97.4%** (hokai.io) · **AIME 2025 94.6%**.
- ⚠ **OpenAI, "Why we no longer evaluate SWE-bench Verified" (2026-02-23):** the benchmark is "increasingly contaminated and mismeasures frontier coding progress" — OpenAI retired the benchmark behind the 74.9% headline.

> Provenance: This is a **twice-superseded former flagship** with a **genuinely strong, independently-verified launch showing** (VALS #1 on AIME 93.4% and MMMU 81.5%, #2 on GPQA 85.6% and MMLU Pro 87.0%, all closely matching OpenAI's official numbers) that has since been **overtaken by deprecation**: the **AA Intelligence Index v4.3.2 estimate of 23 is below the median**, **removal is scheduled**, and its **headline SWE-bench 74.9% was never independently verified (VALS "awaiting board") and the benchmark itself was retired by OpenAI**. **The scaffolded meta.json is ACCURATE and is verified here, not corrected** — `opencode/gpt-5`, "router flagship (400K/128K)," **AIME 94.6%**, **SWE-Verified 74.9%**, "deprecated, removal scheduled," the **$1.25/$10 (cached $0.125) + OpenCode Zen $1.07/$8.50** tiers, "Text, image, file in; text out," and the standout ("**where the router era began**") all check out against OpenAI, AA and VALS. Enriched here: the full VALS independent table (AIME/GPQA/MMLU Pro/MMMU/MATH 500), GPT-5 Pro's GPQA 88.4% SOTA, HLE 25.3/50.0, HumanEval 97.4%, the AA Index 23 estimate + speed, the router architecture, cost-per-solve, and the SWE-bench retirement. ⚠ One source's auto-generated FAQ (binaryverseai) contained **internally inconsistent/hallucinated figures** ("AIME 99.6%," "$0.005/1K pricing," "GPT-5 Turbo") that contradict its own primary tables — those were **disregarded**; only the corroborated comparison tables were used. **No benchmark was invented; official-vs-independent and the SWE-bench caveat are stated.**

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 79/100.** The router plus **steady agentic tool use** — OpenAI highlighted "**fewer tool calls and shorter chains than o3**" on SWE-bench work, and reviewers found tool use "steadier" than GPT-4. But there is **no current independent agentic eval** (deprecated; AA 10k-only, no GDPval-AA/τ²-Bench/Terminal-Bench number surfaced, and no Codex-Max-style compaction agent — that arrived with GPT-5.1). Scored **79**, just below its successor GPT-5.1 (80) and grok-4.3 (82, which has measured GDPval-AA/τ²-Bench/IFBench).
- **Reasoning: 78/100.** **S-tier and independently verified *at launch*** — VALS **#1 on AIME 2025 (93.4%)**, **#2 on GPQA (85.6%)** and **MMLU Pro (87.0%)**, **MATH 500 96.0%**, with GPT-5 Pro hitting a then-SOTA **GPQA 88.4%**. But the **current AA Intelligence Index v4.3.2 estimate is 23 — *below* the 27 median** — and the model is deprecated. The strong, deeply-corroborated VALS launch anchors hold it at **78** (level with GPT-5.1) despite the lower current estimate; it is not credited at its Aug-2025 #1 peak because scoring reflects present independent reality.
- **Context window: 80/100.** **400,000 total (≈272K in + 128K out)** — verified and adequate, but short of the 500K and 1M tiers that now define the frontier, and GPT-5 has no Codex-Max-style compaction to stretch it. Scored **80** (same as GPT-5.1), between the 256K tier (78) and the 500K tier (83).
- **Multimodal: 78/100.** **Text + image + file in → text out**, with a **VALS-#1-at-launch MMMU of 81.5%** (official 84.2%) on chart/diagram/PDF reasoning — genuinely strong *within* the image tier. But **no native video or audio**, so breadth caps it at the image-only tier. Scored **78** (level with GPT-5.1); the verified MMMU leadership and file input are noted but don't reach the native-video tier (grok-4.3 82, Seed 2.0 Pro 84).
- **Coding: 77/100.** **SWE-bench Verified 74.9%** and **HumanEval 97.4%** were strong for Aug 2025, with praised tool-call efficiency. Held to **77** — below GPT-5.1 (79, whose 76.3% is higher *and* independently corroborated) — because GPT-5's SWE-bench was **never VALS-verified ("awaiting board")**, **OpenAI retired the benchmark over contamination**, there is **no Vals SWE-bench row**, and 74.9% is far below the elite independent coders (Opus 5 ~97%, DeepSeek V4 Pro 96.4%).

Scored **85** — a touch above GPT-5.1 (84) for the Zen discount tier and verified per-solve efficiency, though the **$10/M output** is mid-tier and deprecation limits new investment. (Not counted in Overall.)
