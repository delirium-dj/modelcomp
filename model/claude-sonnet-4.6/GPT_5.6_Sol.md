# Claude Sonnet 4.6 — Independent Research Report

## Summary and evidence

Claude Sonnet 4.6 is Anthropic's February 2026 balanced model for coding, agents, professional work, and computer use. It supports adaptive and extended thinking, context compaction, text and image input, and a **one-million-token context window**. Anthropic reports **72.5% OSWorld-Verified** and describes major gains in coding, planning, instruction following, and prompt-injection resistance.

Sources: [Anthropic Sonnet 4.6 launch](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-4-6), [Sonnet 4.6 system card](https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/78073f739564e986ff3e28522761a7a0b4484f84.pdf)

## Cost and limitations

Pricing starts at **$3 input and $15 output per million tokens**. It lacks native audio/video input, and Anthropic recommends Opus for the deepest reasoning, major refactors, and multi-agent coordination.

## Scores

- **Tool use: 92/100.** Computer use, programmatic tools, search, memory, and MCP provide a broad agent stack.
- **Reasoning: 89/100.** Adaptive thinking is strong, but Opus remains better for the hardest problems.
- **Context window: 96/100.** One million tokens plus compaction supports very long workflows.
- **Multimodal: 89/100.** Vision and computer use are excellent, while audio/video support is limited.
- **Coding: 91/100.** Coding is a headline strength with substantial gains over Sonnet 4.5.
- **Cost efficiency: 86/100.** Sonnet pricing is reasonable for its capability, though output is not cheap.
- **Overall Score: 91/100.** Half-up rounded mean: (92 + 89 + 96 + 89 + 91) / 5 = 91.4.

## Bottom line

Sonnet 4.6 is a strong balanced choice for million-context coding and computer-use agents, especially when Opus-level depth is unnecessary.
