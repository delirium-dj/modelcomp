## Model card

- **Name:** Claude Sonnet 4.6. **Variant/alias flag:** a benchmark tracker classifies this ID as **"Non-Reasoning"**, yet ARC Prize's verified results for it are labelled **"(Max)"** — so some published figures reflect a maximum-effort configuration while others reflect the base ID. That split is the most likely explanation for the very wide vendor-versus-independent gaps recorded below.
- **Short description:** The Sonnet-tier model of Anthropic's 4.6 generation, documented in its own system card. **Deep legacy**: a composite tracker places it **#57 of 784 at 55.25/100**, against Claude Sonnet 5's 65.96 and Claude Sonnet 5.5's 83.87 — two Sonnet generations have shipped since. Its two durable credentials are **ARC Prize-verified ARC-AGI-2 at 58.3%** and **a Claw-Eval score of 67.8% from the benchmark's own leaderboard**, the second-highest in this research pass. Against that sits the widest vendor-independent divergence recorded anywhere here: **HLE at 49% from Anthropic against 13.3% from Artificial Analysis — a 35.7-point gap.**
- **Provider / access:** Claude API (`claude-sonnet-4-6`), Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud, Microsoft Foundry and Claude Platform on AWS. Anthropic's current documentation lists it among the models eligible for the **300K Batch-output beta** with the `output-300k-2026-03-24` header — an eligibility that Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1, at five times the price, do not have.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released in the Claude 4.6 cycle. **No verified public release date or knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `claude-sonnet-4-6`. **No free-tier ID.**
- **Context window:** **200,000 tokens** — exactly the methodology's 200K anchor, and a fifth of the 1M that Claude Sonnet 5 and 5.5 both offer. Max output: no verified figure found (300K on the Batch beta). **AA-LCR at 68.3%** is weak.
- **Modalities:** Input: **text and images only** → output: text. No audio, video or PDF path; no non-text output.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **$3 / MTok input, $15 / MTok output** — the Anthropic Sonnet-tier rate for this generation, confirmed for Claude Sonnet 4.5 in an independent cross-model comparison verified earlier in this research session. **Re-verify before budgeting.** For context, Claude Sonnet 5 and 5.5 both list at **$2/$10** — cheaper than this model.
- **Architecture:** Proprietary, closed weights. Parameter count and topology: not disclosed.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 76/100.** Three creditable results: **Claw-Eval at 67.8% from the benchmark's own leaderboard** is among the best in this dataset and satisfies a requirement the methodology explicitly penalises models for missing; **τ²-bench at 79.5% clears the Tau-family reference**; and **OSWorld-Verified at 72.1% plus CyberGym at 65.2%**, both from independent leaderboards, show real computer-use and defensive-security capability. Four deductions: **Terminal-Bench sits mid-band at 59.1% vendor / 57.3% independent**, far from the ~88% anchor; **OSWorld 2.0 at 8.3% against OSWorld-Verified's 72.1% is a ~64-point version gap** that makes the headline unreliable as a general claim; **ApprenticeBench at 2% is the lowest agentic figure in this entire research pass**; and **GDPval-AA, Tau3-Banking, MCP-Atlas and Toolathlon are all unreported**.
- **Reasoning: 72/100.** One result is genuinely strong and independently verified: **ARC Prize records ARC-AGI-2 at 58.3%**, ahead of GPT-5.2 Pro and Grok 4.5, with ARC-AGI-1 at 86.00%. Everything else is either below reference or so widely disputed that it cannot be relied on. **GPQA Diamond spans 89.9% / 85.6% / 79.9% across three sources — a 10-point range with none clearing the 90%+ reference.** **HLE spans 49% to 13.3% — a 35.7-point gap, the widest vendor-independent divergence in this entire research pass** — so the single most-cited reasoning benchmark is effectively unmeasured for this ID. Add **an Intelligence Index of 24.7% in the mid band**, **CritPt at 0.9%**, **AA-IFBench at 41.2%**, **AA-LCR at 68.3%**, and a **negative AA-Omniscience Index of −3.5% on a 68.5% hallucination rate**. The anomalous SuperGPQA figure of 95% from a competitor's table is inconsistent with every adjacent score and was not credited.
- **Context window: 70/100.** **200,000 tokens sits exactly at the methodology's 200K anchor (= 70)**, and the long-context evidence gives no reason to move off it: **AA-LCR at 68.3% is among the weaker figures in this dataset**, with **no MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks measurement** and max output tokens undocumented. The one genuine commercial merit is **300K Batch-output beta eligibility**, which Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 lack at five times the price. For family context, both Claude Sonnet 5 and 5.5 offer 1M — five times this window, at a lower price.
- **Multimodal: 68/100.** Input is **text and images only** with text-only output, placing it in the methodology's "+image in = 60–70" band. **CharXiv at 77.4% is solid chart reasoning** — better than Claude Sonnet 5's 77.0% unaided and well ahead of Claude Fable 5.1's 44.8% — and **AA-MMMU-Pro at 70.6%** is mid-field. It sits near the band ceiling on the CharXiv result but cannot reach it because there is **no document, OCR, video or audio benchmark at all**, and no video, audio or PDF input path exists.
- **Coding: 80/100.** The best-corroborated figure is good: **SWE-bench Verified at 79.6% from Anthropic is independently measured at 77.4% by Vals AI** — a tight 2.2-point agreement that makes it the most trustworthy number on this page — with **LiveCodeBench at 82.1%** and **React Native Evals at 80.6%** both independently measured and strong. Four deductions: **FrontierCode 1.1 Main at 24.3% is very low**, roughly half what Claude Sonnet 5 and Claude Opus 4.8 achieve on the same Cognition benchmark, indicating poor merge-acceptance quality; **Terminal-Bench 2.0 at 59.1% is far below the 85%+ coding reference**; **Vibe Code Bench (51.48%) and CursorBench 3.1 (48.8%) are around half**; and **DeepSWE, SciCode, SWE-bench Pro and the AA Coding Index are all absent**, so most of the band's references cannot be checked.
- **Cost efficiency: 38/100.** At the **$3 / $15 Anthropic Sonnet-tier rate** it sits exactly on the anchor that maps to ~60, with standard mitigations (10% cache reads, Batch at 50%, 300K Batch-output eligibility). What drives it far below the anchor is that it is **dominated by cheaper models from its own vendor and its own tier**: **Claude Sonnet 5 lists at $2/$10 — a third cheaper — and scores 65.96 against this model's 55.25**; **Claude Sonnet 5.5 also lists at $2/$10 and scores 83.87**, a 28.6-point composite gap at two-thirds the price, with a 1M context window, a 512-token caching floor and the best Agentic Index in the Claude line. There is no workload for which paying more for the older, weaker, smaller-context model is defensible.
- **Overall Score: 73.2/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (76 + 72 + 70 + 68 + 80) / 5 = 73.2 — a deep-legacy Sonnet with two real credentials and an evidence base too disputed to trust. What holds up: **ARC Prize-verified ARC-AGI-2 at 58.3%**, **Claw-Eval at 67.8%** from the benchmark's own leaderboard, **SWE-bench Verified at 79.6% independently confirmed at 77.4%**, **LiveCodeBench 82.1%**, **React Native Evals 80.6%** and **CharXiv 77.4%**. If your acceptance suite is already pinned to this model and your work is repository patching or mobile front-end generation within 200K tokens, it is competent. For anything else, three findings should stop adoption. **The reasoning evidence is unusable**: HLE spans 49% to 13.3% — a 35.7-point gap — GPQA spans 10 points across three sources with none clearing 90%, CritPt is 0.9%, and the AA-Omniscience Index is negative on a 68.5% hallucination rate. **The agentic ceiling is low**: ApprenticeBench at 2% is the worst in this dataset, Terminal-Bench is mid-band, and four tool-use references are unreported. And **the commercial case is indefensible**: Claude Sonnet 5.5 costs a third less, offers five times the context and scores 28.6 composite points higher.
