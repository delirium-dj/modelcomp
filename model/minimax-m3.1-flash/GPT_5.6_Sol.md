# Space Bunny Alpha — Independent Research Report

## Summary and evidence

Space Bunny Alpha is an anonymous preview model for long-context reasoning, multimodal understanding, structured output, and agent workflows. Its official project describes these capabilities but does not identify the model developer, architecture, weights, benchmark suite, or stable commercial terms. Community attribution to MiniMax M3.1 remains unconfirmed.

Source: [Space Bunny official repository](https://github.com/spacebunny-app/opencode-space-bunny)

## Cost and limitations

Preview access is free, but anonymity and unstable availability prevent dependable privacy, safety, and production judgments. Exact context and modality claims vary by route.

## Scores

- **Tool use: 68/100.** Agent workflows are supported without reproducible evaluation.
- **Reasoning: 63/100.** No verified reasoning benchmark exists.
- **Context window: 85/100.** It is advertised for long context, though exact official limits are unclear.
- **Multimodal: 72/100.** Multimodal understanding is claimed but not independently documented.
- **Coding: 68/100.** Developer-agent positioning is positive, with only anecdotal quality evidence.
- **Cost efficiency: 91/100.** Free preview use is valuable but temporary.
- **Overall Score: 71/100.** Half-up rounded mean: (68 + 63 + 85 + 72 + 68) / 5 = 71.2.

## Bottom line

Space Bunny is an interesting free experiment whose anonymous identity makes it unsuitable for sensitive or durable deployments.
