## Model card

- **Name:** GPT-6.1 Sol. No free API tier. Variant/alias flag: part of the **GPT-6 family** alongside **GPT-6 Astra** (flagship), **GPT-6 Sol** (its direct predecessor) and **GPT-6 Luna** (cheapest); a benchmark tracker records its variant identifier as `sol-6-1`. Do not confuse with **GPT-5.6 Sol**, a different generation.
- **Short description:** **OpenAI replaced GPT-6 Sol with GPT-6.1 Sol on 2026-09-29**, one day before Google announced Gemini 4 Argon and one day after Anthropic shipped Claude Sonnet 5.5 — all three at or near the same $2/$10 price point. A composite tracker places it **#9 of 783 at 77.58/100**, and its defining characteristic is **cost per result rather than peak capability**: independent measurement puts it at **$0.72 per Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index task against Claude Sonnet 5.5's $7.60** — more than ten times cheaper per completed task for a four-point index deficit. It also records **the highest CritPt score in this entire research pass (31.7%)** and the highest AA-LCR (83.0%) and AA-MMMU-Pro (86.0%) figures recorded here.
- **Provider / access:** OpenAI API, with an official model documentation page at `developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6.1-sol`. OpenAI published both an introduction post and a **system card addendum** for it. Reasoning: explicit reasoning mode with effort levels (the OpenAI family standard).
- **Release / knowledge:** **Released 2026-09-29.** **Knowledge cutoff: no verified public figure found.**
- **IDs:** `gpt-6.1-sol`. **No free-tier ID.**
- **Context window:** **1,050,000 tokens with a 128,000-token normal output limit** — confirmed by cross-vendor comparison against OpenAI's own documentation. The OpenAI family's **272K full-request repricing threshold** should be assumed to apply but was not separately verified for this model. **AA-LCR at 83.0% is the highest long-context reasoning figure recorded in this entire research pass.**
- **Modalities:** **Text and image input → text output** (the GPT-6 family standard; no audio or video in either direction). A strong AA-MMMU-Pro figure confirms the vision path. **No primary specification sheet was read directly for this model**, so the modality list follows the documented family pattern.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **$2.00 / 1M input, $0.10 / 1M cached input, $10.00 / 1M output** — confirmed by cross-vendor comparison citing OpenAI's current model documentation, and identical to Gemini 4 Argon's introductory rate and Claude Sonnet 5.5's list rate. The decisive figure is not the rate card but the measured throughput: **$0.72 per Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index task**.
- **Architecture:** Proprietary, closed weights. Parameter count and topology: not disclosed.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 86/100.** A broad and largely independent agentic record. **GDPval-AA at 1,575 Elo** is among the better figures in this dataset and **AA Briefcase at 1,564** corroborates on a private harness; **AA AutomationBench at 64.9%, measured independently, is strong** — higher than Gemini 4 Argon's 51.3% leading figure in Google's own comparison; Terminal-Bench-Science (57.0%) beats Claude Fable 5.1 and AA Terminal-Bench 4.0 (56.1%) is mid-field against the frontier. Four deductions hold it to 86: **GDPval remains ~175 Elo short of the ~1750+ reference**; **OpenAI's own AutomationBench figure (36.1%) is 28.8 points below the independent one**, so the harness sensitivity is large; **Tau2-bench, Tau3-Banking, Claw-Eval, MCP-Atlas, Toolathlon and OSWorld are every one of them unreported** — six methodology references with no value, triggering the explicit penalty; and GDP.pdf at 31.0% is weak on document-grounded agency.
- **Reasoning: 91/100.** The strongest independently measured reasoning profile in this part of the dataset, and three figures are category-leading. **CritPt at 31.7% is the highest in this entire research pass** on research-level physics, a benchmark where most models score in single digits — that is a genuine and rare capability signal. **AA-LCR at 83.0% is also the highest recorded here**, and **AA-Omniscience accuracy at 62.1% is likewise the best**, with a composite Omniscience Index of 41.5% that no other model in this dataset approaches. **AA-HLE at 52.9% clears the 40%+ frontier reference comfortably** and is independently measured. Three deductions: the **Intelligence Index of 51.8% remains below the 60+ frontier marker** and four points behind Claude Sonnet 5.5; **the 54.3% hallucination rate is high** despite the strong accuracy; and **no GPQA Diamond figure exists at all**, which leaves the methodology's primary knowledge reference unchecked.
- **Context window: 95/100.** A **1,050,000-token context with 128,000-token output** places it in the ≥1M tier (95–100), and it earns the floor comfortably on the back of **AA-LCR at 83.0% — the best long-context reasoning measurement in this research pass**. It does not go higher because the tier's 100 condition requires **≥98% retrieval at 512K+** and **no MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks figure exists**; because **MLCR-AA at 33.9%** suggests multi-document long-context reasoning is considerably harder for it than single-document; and because the OpenAI family's **272K full-request repricing threshold** should be assumed to apply, making the economically sane window roughly a quarter of the nominal one.
- **Multimodal: 70/100.** Input is **text and image** with text-only output and no audio or video path, placing it in the methodology's "+image in = 60–70" band, and it earns the **top of that band** on the strength of a single outstanding result: **AA-MMMU-Pro at 86.0% is the highest MMMU-Pro figure recorded in this entire dataset**, and it is independently measured rather than vendor-claimed. It cannot exceed the band because the input surface excludes video, audio and PDF regardless of quality, **GDP.pdf at 31.0% shows document handling is weak**, and **no CharXiv, OCR or video benchmark exists** — so a single excellent datapoint carries the whole dimension.
- **Coding: 82/100.** **DeepSWE at 71.9% comes within 2.1 points of the methodology's 74%+ frontier reference**, which is respectable, and **AA-SciCode at 54.2%** is just short of the 55%+ reference. The problem is coverage rather than quality: **only two coding benchmarks exist for this model** — **SWE-bench Verified, SWE-bench Pro, LiveCodeBench, Terminal-Bench 2.1 and Vibe Code Bench are all absent** — so three of the band's four references cannot be checked and there is no repository-scale, algorithmic or real-editor evidence at all. For a model ranked #9 of 783 overall, that is a conspicuous gap, and the score reflects two decent datapoints rather than a demonstrated coding capability.
- **Cost efficiency: 88/100.** The best value proposition among proprietary frontier models in this dataset, and the evidence is measured rather than asserted. **$2.00 / $10.00 with $0.10 cached input beats the $3/$15 anchor that maps to ~60**, and cached input at a true 5% multiplier is unusually aggressive. But the decisive figure is per-task: **independent measurement puts it at $0.72 per Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index task against Claude Sonnet 5.5's $7.60** — **more than ten times cheaper per completed task** — for a four-point index deficit (51.8 against 56) at an identical list price. That is the clearest demonstration in this dataset that per-token rates mislead and per-task cost is what matters. Three deductions: **no free tier**; the **272K repricing threshold** should be assumed to apply and punishes the long-context work the window advertises; and the price is **confirmed by cross-vendor comparison rather than read directly from OpenAI's pricing page**, so it warrants re-verification.
- **Overall Score: 84.8/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (86 + 91 + 95 + 70 + 82) / 5 = 84.8 — the strongest cost-per-result story in this dataset, wrapped around a genuinely excellent reasoning core and a thin coding record. Choose it for **research-level scientific reasoning**, where **CritPt at 31.7% is the best figure in this entire research pass**; for **long-context reasoning** (AA-LCR 83.0%, also the best here); for **factual reliability relative to peers** (AA-Omniscience accuracy 62.1% and index 41.5%, both the best recorded); for **visual understanding** (AA-MMMU-Pro 86.0%, also the best); for **business-process automation** (AA AutomationBench 64.9% independently measured); for **medical knowledge work** (HealthBench Professional 64.2%, near Claude Opus 5.5); and above all for **any high-volume workload where cost per completed task governs** — $0.72 against a direct competitor's $7.60 at the same list price is a decisive margin. Do not choose it for **coding without testing first**: only DeepSWE (71.9%) and SciCode (54.2%) exist, with no SWE-bench, LiveCodeBench or real-editor evidence at all. Do not choose it for structured tool-calling pipelines where τ²/τ³ or Claw-Eval behaviour matters — none of those are measured. Do not choose it for document pipelines (GDP.pdf 31.0%) or for audio and video work. And note two open questions that a buyer should close before committing: **no GPQA Diamond figure exists**, and **OpenAI's own AutomationBench number is 28.8 points below the independent one**, so harness choice matters more here than for most models.
