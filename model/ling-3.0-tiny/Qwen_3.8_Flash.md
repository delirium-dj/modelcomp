## Model card

- **Name:** Ling-3.0-tiny (Hugging Face `inclusionAI/Ling-3.0-tiny`, ModelScope `inclusionAI/Ling-3.0-tiny`, AA id `ling-3-0-tiny`, OpenRouter `inclusionai/ling-3.0-tiny` with a **`:free`** variant; curated id `opencode/ling-3.0-tiny`)
- **Short description:** "a **lightweight hybrid reasoning MoE model with 7.9B total parameters and only 1.3B activated parameters per token**", whose stated brief is accessibility rather than leaderboard position: "**deliver strong reasoning and agentic capabilities at low inference cost, making advanced model capabilities more accessible for local and resource-constrained deployment**", with **BF16, FP8 and INT4** weights shipped "for a wide range of hardware and deployment settings". Third of the Ling-3.0 line after the 124 B/5.1 B `Ling-3.0-flash` and its VL variant — same hybrid-linear recipe, ~1/16 the total parameters.
- **Provider / access:** **Open weights under the MIT License** (card `license: mit`; AA confirms "MIT", weights on Hugging Face). Deployment is the headline: "**validated on NVIDIA DGX Spark, Apple Silicon MacBook, and Mac mini**, enabling capable reasoning and agentic workloads **without datacenter-class GPUs**" — FP8 reaches **~100–105 tokens/s on DGX Spark** and **86–90 tokens/s on an M4 Pro MacBook** at **≈8.34 GiB peak memory at 8 K context**. Serving recipes: **SGLang** (`lmsysorg/sglang:dev-Ling-3.0-tiny`, **`--tp 1`** on a single 141 GB-class GPU or a 1-GPU Blackwell node, `--context-length 262144`, NEXTN/MTP speculative decoding, YaRN override) with an official cookbook (`docs.sglang.io/cookbook/autoregressive/InclusionAI/Ling-3.0-tiny`), plus a **vLLM recipe page** (`recipes.vllm.ai/inclusionAI/Ling-3.0-tiny`). Hosted: OpenRouter paid **and `:free`**. **Native hybrid reasoning:** "supports both fast responses and multi-step reasoning, with thinking mode configurable per request through `enable_thinking`" — thinking **on by default**, recommended `temperature=1.0, top_p=0.95, top_k=20`.
- **Release / knowledge:** **Released August 6, 2026** (OpenRouter: "Ling 3.0 Tiny was released on August 6, 2026"; AA lists **Released August 2026**). **No knowledge cutoff published** for this ID; disclosure is the family-level `inclusionAI/AI-Transparency` training-content summary. Not deprecated — AA still measures all four units (intelligence, speed, cost, verbosity) for it, unlike its 2.6-era siblings.
- **IDs:** `inclusionAI/Ling-3.0-tiny` (HF/ModelScope), `inclusionai/ling-3.0-tiny` / `…:free` (OpenRouter), `ling-3-0-tiny` (AA), `opencode/ling-3.0-tiny` (curated). **Aggregator coverage:** **BenchLM has a page** — **10 source-displayable rows but "no public overall score … remains Unranked"** (Overall "Not computed", 10 of 625 tracks), classified **Open Weight / Reasoning / 262K context**, model card linked; **every row is Artificial Analysis-sourced**. Siblings on that page: Ling 3.0 Flash (45.18), Flash VL (47.31), Flash FP8 (not computed), Flash Fin (not computed), Ling 3.1 Flash (61.94), Ling 2.6 Flash (32.65) — i.e. **Tiny is the only 3.0-line member with no computed composite**, so no cross-family number can be borrowed. AA has a live page with a full metric set; Vals AI does **not** list this ID (it does list the Flash).
- **Context window:** **262,144 tokens served (131,072 native × YaRN factor 2.0, `rope_theta 6,000,000`, `partial_rotary_factor 0.5`)** — AA reports **262k**, BenchLM **262K**, the card's launch command pins `--context-length 262144` and describes the recipe as "**256K YaRN context**". Architecture supports it: **3:1 KDA-to-MLA stacking** means three of every four layers hold a fixed-size recurrent state instead of a growing KV cache. **Practical caveat:** the vendor's own Terminal-Bench protocol runs "**max_new_tokens=32K**" (sub-64 K output ceiling for the methodology), and the advertised **8.34 GiB peak memory figure is measured at 8 K context** — filling 256 K on a laptop-class device is not the deployment this model was validated for. Curated `meta.json` says "128K total": **wrong (native-only, and even that is 131,072)**.
- **Modalities:** **text in / text out** (AA modality block; `pipeline_tag: text-generation`; no encoder, no vision/audio benchmark). Vision in this family is a separate model (`model/ling-3.0-flash-vl/`). Curated "Text in/out" is **correct**.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-08):** **$0.00 per 1M input and $0.00 per 1M output, with $0.00 average cost per Intelligence-Index task — ranked #1 of 142 models in its class on cost** (AA: "well priced … competitively priced", class medians $0.03 in / $0.15 out). That is the free OpenRouter route being measured, and it is corroborated on the vendor side by **MIT weights that fit one consumer-class accelerator**. The non-monetary price is real, though: AA measures **56.9 output tokens/s (#45/142), "slower than average"** against a class median of 84 — while the vendor reports **">160 tokens/s"** in AA testing and **100–105 tok/s FP8 on DGX Spark** / **86–90 on M4 Pro** locally, so the aggregator number most likely reflects the hosted free-tier route rather than the model's best achievable speed. Verbosity **230 M tokens for the Index run (#24/142, class median 82 M) — "very verbose"**, which costs wall-clock time even when tokens are free.
- **Architecture:** **7.9 B total / 1.3 B active per token**; hybrid-linear attention with **3 KDA (Kimi Delta Attention) + 1 MLA per 4-layer block**, sparse MoE FFN of **128 routed experts, activating 8 routed + 1 shared per token**; trained with **MTP** (NEXTN speculative decoding supported out of the box). Weight variants **BF16 / FP8 / INT4**. Design intent as stated: "more efficient long-context processing … broad model capabilities with only 1.3B activated parameters … **native hybrid reasoning enables fast responses for routine tasks and multi-step reasoning for complex tasks within a single model**".

The 25 is a *different index version*, not a contradiction — **this report scores the live 11** and notes that no v4.3.2-comparable figure existed at launch. The **Agentic Index 16** claim cannot be re-verified (BenchLM shows no Agentic-Index row for this ID). (3) **Do not inherit** from `model/ling-3.0-flash/` (124 B/5.1 B, measured Index 20 and Vals-verified) or `model/ling-2.6-flash/` (non-reasoning, Index 10, 96.7 % hallucination) — Tiny is its own checkpoint with its own measurements. AA flags "A non-reasoning variant may also exist." (4) **No composite of its own:** BenchLM "Not computed", so any site-side ranking for this folder rests on the five dimensions below rather than on an aggregator score. ### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 48/100.** It is built to be an agent model at the smallest possible footprint — native hybrid reasoning with a per-request `enable_thinking` switch, "real-world agentic workflows" as the design target, NEXTN/MTP for the low per-step latency agent loops need, and a 1-GPU/laptop deployment that makes it plausible as the *executor* inside a tool harness. But the measured agentic picture for this ID is weak and thin: **GDPval-AA normalized 3.2 %**, a **vendor-cited AA Agentic Index of 16** that no aggregator reproduces, **Terminal-Bench 2.1 run under AA protocol but its score never printed**, and **no τ²/τ³, BFCL, MCP-Atlas, Toolathlon, Claw-Eval, AutomationBench or OSWorld row exists for this checkpoint**. Scored in the low-mid band with the missing-harness penalty explicit, and no credit taken for the 124 B sibling's agentic results.
- **Reasoning: 50/100.** For **1.3 B active parameters** some of this is genuinely good — **GPQA Diamond 73.4 %** (independently measured) and **AA-LCR 60.3 %** are respectable long-horizon reasoning signals, and AA's verdict is "**above average**" for the class (**Index 11 vs median 8**, #36/142). Against the methodology's bands, though, 11 sits **below the 20–35 mid tier** and the supporting rows are correspondingly soft: **HLE 9.3 %**, **CritPt 0.0 %**, **SciCode 24.2 %**. Faithfulness is the tiebreaker downwards: **AA-Omniscience −19.3 with only 8.5 % accuracy** — but note the useful nuance for the site: its **30.5 % hallucination rate is the *lowest* measured in this whole Ling batch** (vs 44.1 % for 3.0 Flash and 96.7 % for 2.6 Flash), i.e. it refuses/answers blandly rather than inventing, which is a mild positive rather than a disaster.
- **Context window: 68/100.** **262,144** (131,072 native × YaRN 2.0) confirmed by AA, BenchLM and the vendor's own `--context-length 262144`, on a **3:1 KDA/MLA hybrid-linear stack** that keeps most layers' state fixed-size — the same architectural family that gives the bigger Ling models their long-context economics, and the only small model in this batch with a **real independent long-context measurement: AA-LCR 60.3 %**, which earns it placement above the 200 K ≈ 70 floor rather than at it. Held there and not higher by the **sub-64 K output reality** (`max_new_tokens=32K` in the vendor's own harness protocol, no published max-output figure), **no MRCR/AI-Needle retrieval run**, and the honesty check that the card's own memory figure (**8.34 GiB**) is quoted *at 8 K context*, not at 256 K.
- **Multimodal: 15/100.** **Text-only** — AA's modality block ("supports text input, outputs text") and a `text-generation` card with no vision/audio encoder and no multimodal benchmark. For an edge/local model this is a genuine market gap (on-device document and camera understanding is exactly what this deployment class is used for) and Tiny does not serve it: vision lives in `model/ling-3.0-flash-vl/`. 10–20 band, no lift.
- **Coding: 40/100.** Coding is named as a target capability ("balanced performance across general agent tasks, **coding** …") and the Terminal-Bench 2.1 protocol disclosure proves a terminal-agent run was actually performed — but **no coding result for this ID is readable anywhere**: BenchLM's entire coding section for Tiny is the single **AA-SciCode 24.2 %** row, there is **no SWE-bench, LiveCodeBench, DeepSWE, SWE-bench Multilingual, Coding Index or Terminal-Bench value**, and the vendor's benchmark table is an image. Against the band anchors (Coding Index 70+, SciCode 55+, TB 85+ for the frontier; mid 50–70) a 24.2 % SciCode with no other measurement is a low-band result; 40 reflects the 1.3 B-active context in which it is at least competitive-shaped, not measured competence.
- **Cost efficiency: 97/100.** The cleanest cost story in the registry and the reason this folder exists: **$0.00 per 1M input, $0.00 per 1M output and $0.00 per Intelligence-Index task — #1 of 142 in its class**, on **MIT weights**, with **BF16/FP8/INT4** provided, **`--tp 1`** single-GPU serving, and demonstrated **laptop/desktop deployment (DGX Spark, M4 Pro MacBook, Mac mini) at 86–105 tok/s with ≈8.34 GiB peak memory**, plus a free OpenRouter route (`:free`) and NEXTN/MTP speculative decoding to cut latency further. Deductions are small but real: the **free hosted tier is what AA priced**, so a paid/self-hosted price point is not actually documented; AA measures the served route at **56.9 tok/s, "slower than average"**, so zero monetary cost is paid in wall-clock; and **"very verbose" (230 M vs 82 M median)** burns time and KV-cache on long agent runs even when tokens cost nothing.
- **Overall Score: 44/100.** Mean of the five quality dimensions (48 + 50 + 68 + 15 + 40) / 5 = 221 / 5 = 44.2 → 44; Cost excluded per `RULES.md`. Best fit: **on-device and privacy-sensitive agent work** — laptops, Mac minis, DGX Spark, single-GPU edge servers — where the model must be **free to run, MIT-licensed, downloadable in INT4 and capable of switching thinking on and off per request**, and where 262 K context plus GPQA 73 % at 1.3 B active is "good enough" for routing, drafting, extraction and simple tool loops. It is **not** the pick for capability-first work: the 124 B **`ling-3.0-flash`** measures higher on almost every AA axis (Index 20 vs 11, GPQA 85.5 vs 73.4, Coding Index 50.6, Agentic 21.0) at $0.075/$0.22, and **Ling 3.1 Flash** is the family's scored leader (61.94). Also not for factual Q&A (Omniscience **−19.3, 8.5 % accuracy**), and not for anything needing a published coding or agent number — there is none for this checkpoint. **Curation fixes before sync:** `contextWindow` → **262,144 (131,072 native + YaRN ×2)**; `pricingNote` → "**$0.00/$0.00 per 1M on the measured free route (AA cost rank #1/142); MIT open weights, BF16/FP8/INT4**"; `short` → mention **7.9 B/1.3 B hybrid-reasoning MoE for local and edge deployment**.
