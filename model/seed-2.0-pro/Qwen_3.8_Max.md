## Model card

- **Name:** ByteDance Seed 2.0 Pro (**Seed2.0 Pro**; API id `doubao-seed-2-0-pro` / `seed-2-0-pro-260215`)
- **Short description:** **ByteDance Seed team's flagship closed multimodal agent model**, the top of the four-variant **Seed 2.0 family (Pro / Lite / Mini / Code)** released **2026-02-14** as the intelligence backbone of the **Doubao app (200M+ users)**, **Volcano Engine / BytePlus ModelArk** API, and **TRAE** coding IDE. Officially it "**focuses on long-chain reasoning and robustness in complex workflows**," and on **ByteDance's own numbers** it is frontier-class: **AIME 2025 98.3** (reported above GPT-5.2's 93 and Gemini 3 Pro High's 87), **HMMT Feb 97.3**, **GPQA Diamond 88.9**, **Codeforces 3020** (near-grandmaster), **LiveCodeBench v6 87.8**, **SWE-bench Verified 76.5**, gold medals at ICPC/IMO/CMO level, **SOTA video understanding (VideoMME 89.5, hour-long; TempCompass 89.6; VideoReasonBench 77.8)**, and strong tool use (**τ²-Bench 90.4 retail / 94.2 telecom**, BrowseComp 77.3, WideSearch 74.7). ⚠ **Three counterweights: (1) essentially every number is vendor-measured** from ByteDance's Seed 2.0 Model Card and merely *reproduced* — not independently re-run — by third parties (even the digitalapplied guide says "as independent verification of ByteDance's claims continues"); **(2) its only genuine independent anchor is the crowdsourced LMSYS Chatbot Arena (#6 Text, #3–4 Vision, Feb 2026)** — there is **no Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, no Vals SWE-bench row, no Epoch ECI, and no BenchLM composite** for Seed 2.0 Pro; **(3) it is already superseded by Seed 2.1 (2026-06-23)**, and its **production/repo-scale coding is weak** (SWE-bench Pro 46.9, NL2Repo-Bench 27.9, Terminal-Bench 2.0 55.8) despite the elite competitive-programming marks. Its enduring draw is **price**: ~**$0.50/$3.00 per 1M** ("~10× cheaper than Western frontier"), roughly a third of GPT-5.2's sticker for claimed-comparable reasoning.
- **Provider / access:** **ByteDance (Seed Team).** Served via **Volcano Engine** (`doubao-seed-2-0-pro`, `ark.cn-beijing.volces.com`, OpenAI-SDK-compatible) and **BytePlus ModelArk** (`bytedance/doubao-seed-2.0-pro-260215`) internationally; also routed by **DeepInfra** (this dataset's id `deepinfra/ByteDance/Seed-2.0-pro`), **APIYI** and other BytePlus partners. Consumer access through the **Doubao app** (free, 200M+ users) and coding via **TRAE IDE**. **Closed / proprietary** — parameter count and internals not disclosed. **No free-id tier** (`noFreeId: true`).
- **Release / knowledge:** **2026-02-14** (Seed 2.0 series launch; the Pro evaluation column is dated **0215**). Knowledge cutoff not stated in the sources reviewed. **Superseded by Seed 2.1 (released 2026-06-23)**, which adds 1M-context and MMLongBench-128K long-document work.
- **IDs:** `seed-2-0-pro` / `doubao-seed-2-0-pro` / `seed-2-0-pro-260215` / `bytedance/doubao-seed-2.0-pro-260215` (Volcano/BytePlus) / `deepinfra/ByteDance/Seed-2.0-pro` (this dataset).
- **Context window:** **256K (262,144) total / ~65K output** — 256K confirmed (BytePlus ModelArk lists 262K; the Mini sibling lists 256K in / 128K out / 128K thinking). Solid mid-tier: **below the 500K (Grok 4.5) and 1M (Qwen-Plus, Gemini, and Seed 2.0 Pro's own successor Seed 2.1) tiers**, level with the 256K class (Kimi K2.6).
- **Modalities:** **Text, image, video in → text out.** Confirmed a **native multimodal** model (the whole Seed 2.0 family "supports text, image, and video input"). ⚠ **No audio on Pro** — omni-modal audio-visual understanding is a **Seed 2.0 Lite (0428) upgrade only**, not the Pro; the meta correctly lists Pro as text+image+video. Video is a genuine strength (hour-long processing, temporal reasoning, streaming/multi-video QA).
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-24):** **~$0.50 input / $3.00 output per 1M; cached input ~$0.10** (BytePlus ModelArk / Volcano Engine; TrustedRouter lists $0.5275/$3.165/$0.1055). Positioned as **~10× cheaper than Western frontier** — vs GPT-5.2 ($1.75/$14) and Claude Opus 4.5 ($5/$25); apiyi puts Pro's input at "~1/3.7 of GPT-5.2's." Outstanding value for a claimed-frontier multimodal agent, though it is a **closed hosted tier** (no free self-host, unlike an Apache/MIT open-weight model). Meta's vague "Paid-tier pricing" is **enriched** here.
- **Architecture:** **Closed / proprietary — parameter count and internals not disclosed.** A **reasoning model** with **adjustable thinking effort** (the family exposes `reasoning_effort` levels — minimal/low/medium/hi on Mini; the Pro is tuned for deep "long-chain reasoning"), with **native multimodal fusion** (text+image+video) and heavy **agentic / long-horizon task** optimization (multi-step tool orchestration, GUI, deep research). Powers Doubao's deep-research and VideoCut features and TRAE's coding agent.

### Raw benchmarks found

Every numeric benchmark below is **ByteDance-self-reported** in the Seed 2.0 Model Card (2026-02-14) and reproduced by third-party guides (apiyi, digitalapplied) — **none independently re-run**. The one genuinely **independent** signal is the **crowdsourced LMSYS Chatbot Arena** rank.

**Independent — LMSYS Chatbot Arena (crowdsourced human-preference blind test):**

- **Seed 2.0 Pro Preview: #6 in the Text Arena overall, #3–4 in the Vision Arena (as of Feb 2026).** This is the only non-vendor anchor found — a strong human-preference result that **independently corroborates the vision/multimodal claim** (Vision Arena top-4) and places the model in the frontier conversation for text. **No Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index** exists for Seed 2.0 Pro (the AA "**Doubao Seed Code = 17**" entry is a *different, earlier* ByteDance model, Nov 2025). **No Vals SWE-bench Verified row, no Epoch ECI, no BenchLM composite.**

**Vendor — ByteDance Seed 2.0 Model Card (Pro / 0215 column; self-reported):**

- **Reasoning / math:** **AIME 2025 98.3** (reported > GPT-5.2's 93, Gemini 3 Pro High's 87), **AIME 2026 94.2**, **HMMT Feb 97.3**, **GPQA Diamond 88.9**, **MMLU-Pro 87.0**, BeyondAIME 86.5, FrontierSci-olympiad 74.0, SuperGPQA 68.7; **HLE (no tool, text-only) 32.4** (modest). ### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 81/100.** A genuinely broad agentic surface with strong vendor marks — **τ²-Bench 90.4 retail / 94.2 telecom** (API/tool interaction), **BrowseComp 77.3**, **WideSearch 74.7**, **GDPval 54.4**, deep-research and GUI workflows shipped in Doubao/TRAE — and it is explicitly built for "long-chain reasoning and robustness in complex workflows." Held **below Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (83)** and the independently-verified agentic leaders because **every tool number is vendor-measured** (no AA Index, no Vals), and the harder coding-agent tasks are middling (**Terminal-Bench 2.0 55.8**, **SWE-bench Pro 46.9**). Scored **above Qwen3.5-Plus (79)**, which had a demonstrated Vals long-horizon collapse — Seed 2.0 Pro shows no such independent evidence either way.
- **Reasoning: 82/100.** The vendor math is **elite** — **AIME 2025 98.3** (reported above GPT-5.2 and Gemini 3 Pro High), **HMMT Feb 97.3**, **GPQA Diamond 88.9**, BeyondAIME 86.5, ICPC/IMO/CMO golds — placing it in the frontier-reasoning conversation. But it is **entirely self-reported with no independent AA Index or replication**, the model is **superseded**, and **HLE (no-tool, text-only) 32.4%** is modest. Scored **level with the strong-vendor tier (Qwen3.8-Flash-Next 81, GPQA 91.7) and just above it on the strength of AIME 98.3**, but **below Kimi K2.6 (85)** which carries an independent AA Index 54.
- **Context window: 78/100.** **256K total / ~65K output** — confirmed mid-tier, **below the 500K (Grok 4.5, 83) and 1M (Qwen-Plus, Gemini, and its own successor Seed 2.1, 85) tiers**, and level with the 256K class (**Kimi K2.6 78**). Adequate for most agent/document work but not a long-context leader.
- **Multimodal: 84/100.** Its **strongest dimension** and the best-corroborated: **native text+image+video** with **SOTA-class video** (**VideoMME 89.5** hour-long, **TempCompass 89.6**, **VideoReasonBench 77.8**, streaming + multi-video QA, motion perception "surpassing human baseline") plus strong vision (**MathVision 88.8**, **MMMU 85.4**, CharXiv-DQ 93.5). Crucially, the **independent LMSYS Vision Arena #3–4** corroborates the vision strength beyond the vendor numbers. Scored **above the Qwen/Gemini video models (82)** on the strength and breadth of its video benchmarks, **below the broadest 5-modality models** because Pro has **no audio** (a Lite-only upgrade) and the numeric benchmarks remain vendor-run.
- **Coding: 80/100.** A **split profile**: **elite competitive/algorithmic coding** (**Codeforces 3020** near-grandmaster, **LiveCodeBench v6 87.8**, **SWE-bench Verified 76.5** — respectable, though below Opus 4.5's 80.9 and GPT-5.3-Codex's 83) but **weak production / repo-scale / agentic coding** (**SWE-bench Pro 46.9**, **NL2Repo-Bench 27.9**, **Terminal-Bench 2.0 55.8**, Vibe-Coding human-eval 48.4). With **no independent Vals row** to verify any of it, scored **level with Qwen3.5-Plus (80)** and **below Grok 4.5 / GPT-5.3-Codex (85)** — strong on puzzles, unproven on sustained real-world engineering.
- **Cost efficiency: 89/100.** Excellent value: **~$0.50/$3.00 per 1M (~$0.10 cached)**, positioned as **~10× cheaper than Western frontier** (vs GPT-5.2 $1.75/$14, Claude Opus 4.5 $5/$25) for a claimed-frontier multimodal agent with SOTA video and elite vendor math. Scored **level with Qwen3.5-Plus (89)** and **just below the MIT/Apache open-weight leaders (92)** because Seed 2.0 Pro is a **closed hosted tier with no free self-host** and its $3.00 output is a touch above the cheapest. Scored but **not** counted in the Overall per methodology.
- **Overall Score: 81/100.** Mean of Tool 81 + Reasoning 82 + Context 78 + Multimodal 84 + Coding 80 = 405/5 = 81.0 → 81. Best fit *at launch*: **cost-sensitive frontier-adjacent multimodal and reasoning workloads** — teams wanting **SOTA-class video understanding, elite competition math, and broad agentic tool use at ~1/3 of GPT-5.2's price** inside the ByteDance/Doubao/Volcano/TRAE ecosystem. **Not** the pick for **production/repo-scale coding** (SWE-bench Pro 46.9, NL2Repo 27.9), for anyone needing **independent (non-vendor) benchmark assurance** (only LMSYS Arena corroborates; no AA Index/Vals/Epoch), for **audio/omni-modal** work (that is Seed 2.0 Lite), for **1M-context** work (256K here; move to Seed 2.1), or where a **long-supported, non-superseded** model is required. **Divergence note:** my 81 is **3.9 below the queue's curated 84.9**, driven by the **vendor-only benchmark provenance** (no independent AA Index/Vals/Epoch/BenchLM), the **weak production-coding tail** (SWE-bench Pro 46.9, NL2Repo 27.9, Terminal-Bench 55.8), the **256K mid-tier context**, and its **already-superseded** status — against genuinely strong vendor results (AIME 98.3, Codeforces 3020, SOTA VideoMME 89.5, τ²-Bench 90/94), a corroborating **independent LMSYS Vision Arena top-4**, and a best-in-class low price.
