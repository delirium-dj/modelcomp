# ByteDance Seed 2.0 Pro — Independent Research Report

## Summary and evidence

Seed 2.0 Pro is ByteDance's February 2026 flagship general-purpose agent model. It targets complex instructions, scientific reasoning, documents, charts, images, and long video. ByteDance reported sixth place in LMSYS Text Arena and third in Vision Arena at launch, plus leading results across MathVision, OmniDocBench 1.5, DUDE, MMLongBench, and several video-understanding suites.

Sources: [Seed 2.0 launch and model evidence](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog/seed-2-0-official-launch), [Seed 2.0 model page](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/seed2)

## Cost and limitations

ByteDance claims roughly an order-of-magnitude lower token pricing than comparable frontier models, but its public English launch page does not state a stable dollar price or exact context limit. Access is centered on Doubao, TRAE, and Volcano Engine, and the company acknowledges room for improvement on difficult coding and contextual-learning evaluations.

## Scores

- **Tool use: 84/100.** Multi-step execution is a focus, but public tool-use measurements are limited.
- **Reasoning: 88/100.** Strong math, science, and visual reasoning evidence supports a high score.
- **Context window: 84/100.** Long-context results are strong, though the exact production limit is not clearly disclosed.
- **Multimodal: 95/100.** Documents, charts, images, streaming video, and hour-long video are standout strengths.
- **Coding: 82/100.** Capable, but ByteDance explicitly notes remaining gaps on difficult coding tasks.
- **Cost efficiency: 91/100.** Claimed order-of-magnitude pricing gains are promising, though exact global pricing is opaque.
- **Overall Score: 87/100.** Half-up rounded mean: (84 + 88 + 84 + 95 + 82) / 5 = 86.6.

## Bottom line

Seed 2.0 Pro is especially compelling for document and video-heavy enterprise work, with availability and pricing transparency its main drawbacks.
