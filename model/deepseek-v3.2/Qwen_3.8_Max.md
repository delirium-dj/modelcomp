> **Verdict:** DeepSeek V3.2 is the open-weight MIT-licensed MoE that sat behind DeepSeek's API from 1 December 2025 until the V4 preview on 24 April 2026 — the "official successor to V3.2-Exp" and DeepSeek's **first model to integrate thinking directly into tool-use** (tool calls work in both thinking and non-thinking modes). Its headline architectural change is **DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA)** — a lightning-indexer + top-k (k=2,048) token selector that drops core attention from O(L²) to O(Lk) with, per DeepSeek, virtually identical output quality — which is what funded a >50% API price cut. On vendor numbers (thinking mode) it is genuinely frontier-competitive for open weights: **SWE-bench Verified 73.1%** (vs GPT-5 High 74.9%), **LiveCodeBench 83.3%** (vs 84.5%), **AIME 2025 93.1%**, **HMMT Feb 2025 92.5%** (beats GPT-5 High's 88.3%), Codeforces 2386 — DeepSeek's own report says V3.2 "performs comparably to GPT-5." But the drags are real and independent: a **93.3% AA-Omniscience hallucination rate** (bottom-tier factual faithfulness, ~rank 151), a **128K context ceiling** the report admits blocked ~20%+ of its own search-agent cases, text-only modality, deprecated status (no live AA Intelligence/Speed measurement), and vendor-only agentic numbers. **Reattribution guard:** the IMO/CMO/ICPC/IOI 2025 gold medals and AIME 96.0% cited in meta belong to the separate **V3.2-Speciale** high-compute variant (API-only, no tool-use, expired 15 Dec 2025) — NOT the base `deepseek-v3.2` scored here. Scored Overall 67 (div +0.9 vs curated 66.1).

## Model card

- **Name:** DeepSeek V3.2
- **Short description:** DeepSeek's December-2025 open-weight MIT MoE flagship of the V3 line — a sparse-attention (DSA) efficiency upgrade over V3.1-Terminus that added scaled-RL post-training and, for the first time in a DeepSeek model, thinking integrated directly into tool-use. Positioned as a highly cost-efficient open agentic/reasoning alternative "comparable to GPT-5," served in paired thinking (`deepseek-reasoner`) and non-thinking (`deepseek-chat`) modes.
- **Provider / access:** Open weights on **Hugging Face** (`deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V3.2`, ~689.5 GB of files) under **MIT**; self-host via vLLM / SGLang (day-0 DSA support; official SGLang launch uses `--tp 8 --dp 8 --enable-dp-attention`). Historically served on DeepSeek's own API (`deepseek-chat` = non-thinking, `deepseek-reasoner` = thinking) and **10 API providers** (per AA), including OpenRouter. **Now deprecated on DeepSeek's first-party API** — the legacy `deepseek-chat`/`deepseek-reasoner` IDs were repointed to `deepseek-v4-flash` on 24 Apr 2026 with retirement scheduled 2026-07-24; AA suggests DeepSeek V4 Pro 0424 instead. Weights remain downloadable/self-hostable.
- **Release / knowledge:** **V3.2-Exp** (experimental DSA precursor) 29 September 2025; **V3.2 official** 1 December 2025. Built by continued training from a **DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus** checkpoint whose context had been extended to 128K. Post-training compute was **>10% of pre-training cost** (scaled RL + specialist distillation). Knowledge cutoff not separately published for V3.2 (inherits the V3.1-Terminus lineage).
- **IDs:** id `deepseek/deepseek-v3.2`; HF `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V3.2`; AA `deepseek-v3-2` (the AA page profiles the **non-reasoning** variant). Distinct siblings: **V3.2-Exp** (Sep-2025 precursor) and **V3.2-Speciale** (high-compute reasoning variant, `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale`).
- **Context window:** **128K (128,000 first-party)**; **163,840 on OpenRouter**; **65,536 max output**. DSA makes long-context efficient (O(Lk) core attention), and AA-LCR showed V3.2-Exp scoring ~4 points above V3.1-Terminus in reasoning mode with no long-context regression — but the ceiling is genuinely binding: DeepSeek's report notes **~20%+ of its search-agent test cases exceeded 128K**, and the V4 line moved to 1M.
- **Modalities:** **Text in / text out only** (no image; vision required the separate DeepSeek VL2). **Reasoning: HYBRID** — ships a thinking mode (`deepseek-reasoner`) and a non-thinking mode (`deepseek-chat`); the headline benchmarks below are thinking-mode. NOTE: AA labels its profiled page "non-reasoning" because it measures the `deepseek-chat` non-thinking variant, but the model itself IS a reasoner (thinking-in-tool-use is its signature feature) — corrected here.
- **Pricing:** **$0.28 / 1M cache-miss input, $0.028 / 1M cache-hit input, $0.42 / 1M output** — a single all-hours rate (no off-peak tier in that period), effective from the V3.2-Exp release (29 Sep 2025, down from $0.07/$0.56/$1.68) and held through the V3.2 period. AA blended rate **$0.29** (7:2:1 cache-hit/input/output). AA rates it "very competitive" (median $0.50 in / $1.80 out). **MIT self-host = $0.** These are now historical — V3.2 is off DeepSeek's current pricing page.
- **Architecture:** Open-weight **MoE, MLA (Multi-Head Latent Attention) + DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA)**. **671B total / 37B active** per the V3.1-Terminus backbone and the official inference config (`config_671B_v3.2.json`) — though **AA and meta list 685B total** (likely counting the added DSA lightning-indexer parameters); active is 37B in every source. DSA = a **lightning indexer** (few heads, FP8) that scores each query against preceding tokens + a **fine-grained top-k selector** (k=2,048) feeding only the selected KV entries to the main attention, cutting core complexity from O(L²) to O(Lk). Requires custom kernels (TileLang reference, DeepGEMM indexer, FlashMLA sparse attention); DeepSeek flagged a RoPE layout discrepancy in the earlier demo indexer on 17 Nov 2025. Agent training synthesized data across **1,800+ environments and 85k+ complex instructions**.

### Raw benchmarks found

> **Provenance:** Base-model numbers are **DeepSeek-vendor-reported** (thinking mode) from the V3.2 technical report (arXiv 2512.02556) and the 1 Dec 2025 release note — the report's benchmark tables are figure images, reproduced here via the well-sourced deepseekai.guide model card (primary sources: DeepSeek API docs, HF model cards, arXiv report, Change Log). The **one independent measurement** found is **AA-Omniscience Hallucination Rate 93.3%** (via the benchlm.ai mirror of the AA leaderboard; historical — AA now shows Intelligence Index / Speed / Cost-per-task all **N/A** and "No publicly available API" because the model is deprecated). **Reattribution guards applied throughout:** V3.2-Speciale's contest golds and AIME 96.0% are NOT credited to base V3.2; V3.2-Exp's ablation table is NOT the GA finals.

- **Agent / tool use:** DeepSeek's **first model to integrate thinking directly into tool-use**, supporting tool calls in both thinking and non-thinking modes — trained with a new massive agent-data synthesis method spanning **1,800+ environments and 85k+ complex instructions**. Vendor agent benchmark **τ²-bench (tau2) 80.3%** (from the release-note agent table; the exact value could not be independently re-verified — image source). DeepSeek frames V3.2 as "a highly cost-efficient alternative in agent scenarios, significantly narrowing the performance gap between open and frontier proprietary models." Constraint: the 128K ceiling truncated ~20%+ of search-agent runs. No independent AA agent measurement (deprecated). Comparators named: Kimi-K2-Thinking, MiniMax M2 (open, thinking-in-tool-use).
- **Reasoning / knowledge:** **AIME 2025 (Pass@1) 93.1%**, **HMMT Feb 2025 (Pass@1) 92.5%** (beats GPT-5 High's 88.3%), Codeforces rating **2386** — the report states V3.2 "performs comparably to GPT-5" and reaches "similar performance with Kimi-K2-Thinking and GPT-5 across multiple reasoning benchmarks." **BUT the independent AA-Omniscience Hallucination Rate is 93.3%** (~rank 151 — only ~6.7% non-hallucination), a bottom-tier factual-faithfulness result; DeepSeek's own report concedes V3.2 "trails frontier closed models such as Gemini-3.0-Pro on breadth of world knowledge and token efficiency." No GPQA / HLE value published for base V3.2 (Figure 1 references an HLE text-only subset but the base number is not extractable). **Guard:** AIME 96.0%, HMMT 99.2%, and the IMO/CMO/ICPC-World-Finals/IOI 2025 **gold medals belong to V3.2-Speciale** ("surpasses GPT-5," "rivals Gemini-3.0-Pro"), NOT base V3.2.
- **Coding:** **SWE-bench Verified (Resolved) 73.1%** (vs GPT-5 High 74.9% — near-parity), **LiveCodeBench (Pass@1-COT) 83.3%** (vs GPT-5 High 84.5%), **Codeforces rating 2386** (vs GPT-5 High 2537). A strong, well-rounded open-weight coding profile approaching GPT-5. Vendor-reported (thinking mode). **Guard:** V3.2-Exp's earlier ablation numbers (SWE-bench Verified 67.8%, Codeforces 2121, LiveCodeBench n/a, BrowseComp 40.1) are the September experimental precursor's, NOT the December GA finals; LiveCodeBench 88.7 / Codeforces 2701 are Speciale's.
- **Long context:** 128K native (163,840 on OpenRouter), 65,536 max output. DSA is the efficiency story — O(Lk) core attention, per-token cost rising far more slowly with position than V3.1-Terminus on DeepSeek's H800 deployment. AA-LCR (long-context reasoning): V3.2-Exp scored ~4 points above V3.1-Terminus in reasoning mode; Fiction.liveBench showed no regression. **But 128K is a fifth of the V4 line's 1M and demonstrably binding** (~20%+ of search-agent cases exceeded it). No RULER/MRCR retrieval number published.
- **Speed / cost:** AA **Speed N/A** ("No publicly available API" — deprecated; only the default 10k-token workload is retained and even that is unmeasured here). No independent tok/s. Cost: $0.28 in / $0.42 out ($0.028 cache-hit), **blended $0.29** (AA), a >50% cut from the prior $0.07/$0.56/$1.68; MIT self-host free. AA rates both input and output "very competitive" vs median. The DSA efficiency gain is the architectural basis for the price cut.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 75/100.** A genuine pioneer — DeepSeek's first model to integrate thinking directly into tool-use, with tool calls in both thinking and non-thinking modes, trained on synthesized agent data across 1,800+ environments / 85k+ instructions, and a strong vendor τ²-bench 80.3%. Tempered because the agentic evidence is vendor-only (the τ² value sits in a release-note image, not independently re-run), there is no independent AA agent measurement (deprecated), and the 128K context ceiling truncated ~20%+ of its own search-agent runs.
- **Reasoning: 71/100.** Elite-tier verifiable competition reasoning for an open model — AIME 2025 93.1% and HMMT Feb 2025 92.5% (the latter beating GPT-5 High's 88.3%), Codeforces 2386, "comparable to GPT-5." Heavily tempered by the independently-measured **93.3% AA-Omniscience hallucination rate** (bottom-tier factual faithfulness, ~rank 151), DeepSeek's own concession that it trails Gemini-3.0-Pro on world-knowledge breadth, and the absence of any GPQA/HLE/AA-Intelligence-Index value for the base model. The Speciale's 96.0% AIME and contest golds are NOT credited.
- **Context window: 65/100.** A 128K native window (163,840 on OpenRouter, 65,536 max output) made efficient by DSA's O(Lk) sparse attention (AA-LCR showed no long-context regression vs V3.1-Terminus) — but 128K is a fifth of the V4 line's 1M, is demonstrably binding (~20%+ of the vendor's own search-agent cases exceeded it), and no RULER/MRCR retrieval benchmark is published.
- **Multimodal: 50/100.** Text in / text out only; no image or audio (vision required the separate DeepSeek VL2).
- **Coding: 76/100.** A strong, well-rounded open-weight coding profile approaching GPT-5 High — SWE-bench Verified 73.1% (vs 74.9%), LiveCodeBench 83.3% (vs 84.5%), Codeforces 2386 (vs 2537). Tempered because all figures are vendor-reported in thinking mode, SWE-bench Verified 73.1% sits mid-pack against the 78%+ coding leaders, and the numbers are the December GA finals (the Exp ablation's 67.8% and Speciale's 88.7% LiveCodeBench are excluded).
- **Cost efficiency: 90/100.** Excellent value — $0.28/$0.42 per 1M ($0.028 cache-hit), blended $0.29, a >50% cut from the prior rates, rated "very competitive" by AA against a $0.50/$1.80 median, plus free MIT self-hosting of the exact API model. DSA's efficiency gain is the architectural basis for the low price. (Scored but NOT counted toward Overall.)
- **Overall Score: 67/100.** Half-up mean of the five quality dims (75+71+65+50+76)/5 = 67.4 → 67. Divergence **+0.9** vs the curated queue value 66.1 — a small positive gap: the frontier-competitive vendor reasoning/coding (AIME 93.1, HMMT 92.5, SWE-Verified 73.1, LiveCodeBench 83.3), pioneering thinking-in-tool-use, efficient DSA, and MIT open weights slightly outweigh the curated view, but are held close to it by the very real independent drags — the 93.3% AA-Omniscience hallucination rate, the binding 128K ceiling, text-only modality, deprecated status with no live AA Intelligence/Speed measurement, and vendor-only agentic numbers. Sits just below the mid-tier open agentic models scored earlier this run (longcat-2.0 / glm-5.1 / hy3 at Overall 72), whose larger contexts and broader independent benchmark coverage offset V3.2's cost/efficiency edge. Cost efficiency excluded from the mean per methodology.
