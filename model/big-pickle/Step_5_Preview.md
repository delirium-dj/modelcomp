## Model card

- **Name:** Big Pickle — **OpenCode's long-running stealth model**, a free Zen tier whose backend has been swapped repeatedly and is not disclosed. **A negative finding governs this report: BenchLM carries no `big-pickle` page in any spelling** (`big-pickle`, `big-pickle-1` and `Big-Pickle` all 404), so the 2026-11-06 re-research had no catalog to check against.
- **Short description:** **A free stealth experiment with intermittent history as GLM-4.6, DeepSeek-V4-Flash and possibly Ox Alpha, and a measured record that describes backends that have since been swapped.** Its entire public evidence base is two runs: **ORPT-Bench composite 0.615, 67% task success, 15.39 requests per solved task, $0** (below gpt-5.4-nano 0.789 and kimi-k2.5 0.785, above gpt-5.4 0.609, claude-sonnet-4-6 0.593 and glm-5.1 0.547) and **SWE-Atlas Codebase QnA 50.8%** (49.2% strict).
- **Provider / access:** **OpenCode Zen only** (`https://opencode.ai/zen/v1/chat/completions`); **no weights**; community notes it supports chat/completions but not `/v1/responses`. **Re-verified 2026-10-19: still listed live on the Zen models endpoint.**
- **Release / listing:** **2025-10-17** (models.dev catalog; `last_updated` still 2025-10-17 — **the entry has never been revised**).
- **IDs:** `opencode/big-pickle` (repo id); the Zen endpoint id.
- **Context window:** **200,000 tokens** (160,000 input limit); **max output 32,000** — **re-verified 2026-10-19 against the models.dev catalog JSON** (unchanged; the entry said 128,000 from launch until mid-2026 — **a shrink, not an expansion**).
- **Modalities:** **text in → text out**; interleaved reasoning (`reasoning_content`), tool calling, structured outputs.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-11-06):** **$0/$0 free, rate-limited, with the data-collection caveat** — the data may be used to improve the model.
- **Architecture:** **undisclosed, and by design.** The backend is whatever OpenCode currently serves; the intermittent history is GLM-4.6-era at launch, DeepSeek-V4-Flash-era and possibly Ox Alpha now. **This is the central evidential problem of this report and is stated rather than worked around.**
