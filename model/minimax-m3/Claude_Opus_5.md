## Model card

- **Name:** MiniMax M3. **Open weight** — the official model card is published on Hugging Face as **`MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-M3`**, alongside a separate launch blog. Variant/alias flag: distinct from **MiniMax M2.7** (its predecessor), **MiniMax M2.5**, **MiniMax M1 80k** and **MiniMax M3.1 Flash Preview** (a later sibling, not yet scored by trackers).
- **Short description:** MiniMax's open-weight flagship, and **classified by a benchmark tracker as "Non-Reasoning"** — which makes several of its results notable. It records **the lowest hallucination rate in this entire research pass (18.4%)**, **the highest Claw-Eval score here (74.5%)**, and **the joint-highest AA-IFBench (82.9%)**, with GPQA Diamond corroborated at 92.9%/92.7% across two independent labs. The mechanism behind the hallucination figure is explicit and must be read alongside it: **AA-Omniscience accuracy is only 16.7%, the lowest recorded here** — this model is reliable because it abstains heavily, not because it knows more.
- **Provider / access:** MiniMax's own API, Hugging Face weights (`MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-M3`), OpenRouter (`minimax/minimax-m3`) and third-party hosts. **Open weights mean the rate card is escapable** for organisations able to self-host.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released in the M3 cycle, succeeding MiniMax M2.7. **No verified public release date or knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-M3` (Hugging Face), `minimax/minimax-m3` (OpenRouter).
- **Context window:** **1,000,000 tokens.** Max output: no verified figure found. Long-context evidence is strongly split — **AA-LCR 83.0%** against **MLCR-AA 17.2%** (see benchmarks).
- **Modalities:** **Text and image input, with video evidenced by two published video benchmarks** (VideoMMMU 84.6%, Video-MME with subtitle 85.4%); output text only. No audio input and no non-text output documented or benchmarked. **No vendor specification sheet was retrievable**, so the modality list is inferred from the benchmark set.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **No rate card was verified for this model.** The decisive cost fact is structural: **the weights are public**, so the per-token rate is escapable for self-hosting organisations. MiniMax has historically priced aggressively — a sibling in this dataset, MiniMax M2.7, is tracked as a low-cost option. **Re-verify the live hosted rate before budgeting.**
- **Architecture:** **Parameter count, active parameters and topology are not disclosed** in the sources reached. A benchmark tracker classifies it as **Non-Reasoning**, i.e. it does not emit an explicit reasoning trace by default — which makes its GPQA, USAMO and IFBench results more striking, since they are achieved without the test-time compute most peers here rely on.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 82/100.** Three results are category-leading: **Claw-Eval at 74.5% is the best in this entire research pass** — and the methodology explicitly penalises its absence, so having the best one matters — **AA Harvey LAB at 88.4% is the strongest legal-agent figure here**, and **τ²-bench at 88.9% clears the Tau-family reference**. MCP Atlas (74.2%), BankerToolBench (76.1%), GDPval rubrics (74.7%) and BrowseComp (83.5%) corroborate a genuinely capable tool-calling model. The deductions are numerous and consistent, and they all concern autonomy rather than tool-calling: **AA Tau3 Banking at 15.3% sits in the methodology's mid band**, roughly 35 points below the frontier reference; **AA Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 2.0% is a near-total floor**; **AutomationBench (21.3%), AA-AnalystAgent (10.0%), GDP.pdf (9.8%) and OSWorld 2.0 (4.6%) are all floor-level**; **GDPval-AA at 1,246 Elo is ~500 below reference** and AA Briefcase at 1,090 is low; and Terminal-Bench 2.1 ranges from 53.6% to 66.0% depending on harness.
- **Reasoning: 78/100.** **GPQA Diamond clears the 90%+ frontier reference and is corroborated within 0.2 points by two independent labs (92.9% AA, 92.7% Vals)** — genuinely impressive for a model classified as non-reasoning, i.e. achieving this without the test-time compute most peers here spend. **AA-IFBench at 82.9% is the joint-highest in this dataset**, USAMO 2026 reaches 85.7%, and AA-LCR is 83.0%. The reliability figure requires careful reading rather than celebration: **an 18.4% hallucination rate is the lowest recorded in this entire research pass**, but **16.7% accuracy is also the lowest**, giving an AA-Omniscience Index of just 1.4% — the model is safe because it declines to answer, which is the right failure mode for retrieval-backed systems and the wrong one for unaided knowledge work. Four further deductions: **AA-HLE at 39.0% misses the 40%+ reference by 1 point**; **the Intelligence Index of 29.2% is mid-band**; **CritPt at 3.7% is near-zero**; and **MLCR-AA at 17.2% against AA-LCR's 83.0% is a 65.8-point collapse** on multi-document reasoning.
- **Context window: 95/100.** A **1,000,000-token window** places it in the ≥1M tier (95–100), and **AA-LCR at 83.0%** is among the strongest single-document long-context reasoning measurements in this research pass. It sits at the tier floor because the 100 condition requires **≥98% retrieval at 512K+** and **no MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks figure exists**; because **MLCR-AA at 17.2% shows multi-document reasoning across long inputs essentially fails**, which is the harder and more realistic case for a million-token window; and because **max output tokens are undocumented**.
- **Multimodal: 86/100.** **Two video benchmarks (Video-MME with subtitle 85.4%, VideoMMMU 84.6%) evidence video input**, placing it in the methodology's "+video/PDF in = 75–90" band, and the document stack is genuinely strong: **OmniDocBench 1.5 at 91.6% is essentially level with the best in this dataset**, and **MMMU-Pro at 78.1% is independently corroborated at 78.6%**. Three things keep it below the band ceiling: **OfficeQA Pro at 45.1% is weak** on structured office-document comprehension; **GDP.pdf at 9.8% is floor-level**, which sits oddly against the strong OmniDocBench result and suggests document *agency* is far weaker than document *recognition*; and **there is no CharXiv, OCR or audio benchmark**, with no audio input and no non-text output.
- **Coding: 78/100.** **SWE-bench Verified at 80.5%** is respectable and **LiveCodeBench at 82.2% is independently measured**, with SVG-Bench (63.7%) adding a useful specialist datapoint. Five deductions: **the AA Coding Index at 58.6% is 11.4 points below the methodology's 70%+ frontier reference**; **AA-SciCode at 47.1% is 7.9 points below the 55%+ reference**; **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 66.0% is far below the 85%+ coding reference**; **SWE-bench Verified falls to 75.0% under Vals AI's harness** and SWE-bench Pro is only 59%; and **NL2Repo (42.1%), VIBE V2 (50.1%) and KernelBench Hard (28.8%) are all weak**, with **DeepSWE and Vibe Code Bench absent** so the primary agentic-coding reference cannot be checked.
- **Cost efficiency: 85/100.** **This score carries a caveat: no rate card was verified.** What is verified and what drives it is structural — **the weights are public on Hugging Face**, so the per-token rate is escapable entirely for organisations able to self-host, and MiniMax has a track record of aggressive pricing in this dataset. For a model with the best Claw-Eval, best Harvey LAB, joint-best IFBench and lowest hallucination rate recorded here, open weights at an unknown but historically low hosted rate is a strong value position. Three deductions: the **price is unconfirmed** and must be re-verified; **parameter count is undisclosed**, so self-hosting feasibility cannot be assessed at all — this could be a 100B model or a 1T one; and **no free tier** is documented.
- **Overall Score: 83.8/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (82 + 78 + 95 + 86 + 78) / 5 = 83.8 — an open-weight tool-caller with an unusual and genuinely useful reliability profile. Choose it for **tool-calling and structured agent work**, where **Claw-Eval at 74.5% is the best in this entire research pass**, Harvey LAB reaches 88.4% and τ²-bench 88.9%; for **document ingestion** (OmniDocBench 1.5 91.6%, MMMU-Pro corroborated at 78.1–78.6%, two strong video benchmarks); for **instruction-following pipelines** (AA-IFBench 82.9%, joint-highest here); and above all for **retrieval-backed systems where confabulation is the primary risk** — an **18.4% hallucination rate is the lowest recorded in this dataset**, and in a RAG architecture where the model should defer to retrieved context, heavy abstention is exactly the right behaviour. Do not use it for **unaided factual work**: 16.7% accuracy is the lowest recorded here, and the same abstention that makes it safe makes it unhelpful without grounding. Do not use it for **autonomous long-horizon agency** — Tau3 Banking 15.3%, Terminal-Bench 4.0 2.0%, AutomationBench 21.3%, AnalystAgent 10.0% and GDPval 1,246 Elo all point the same way. Do not use it for **multi-document long-context reasoning** (MLCR-AA 17.2% against AA-LCR 83.0%) or for **repository-scale coding** (AA Coding Index 58.6%, SciCode 47.1%). And note that **parameter count is undisclosed**, so the open-weight advantage cannot actually be costed until MiniMax publishes it.
