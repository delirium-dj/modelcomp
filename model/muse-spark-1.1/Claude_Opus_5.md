## Model card

- **Name:** Muse Spark 1.1. Proprietary, **Reasoning**. Variant/alias flag: the `1.1` release in Meta's Muse Spark line, with **Muse Spark 1.2** and **Muse Spark 1.3** as successors and the original **Muse Spark** as predecessor.
- **Short description:** Meta Superintelligence Labs' **July 2026** multimodal reasoning model, built for agentic tasks and multi-agent orchestration. A composite tracker places it **#31 of 784 at 65.93/100** across 40 benchmarks. Two findings make it genuinely distinctive in this research pass. **Its AA-Omniscience Index of +28.1% is the highest found anywhere in this pass — and uniquely, it is built on real knowledge rather than abstention: raw accuracy of 52.1% is also the highest recorded here.** Every other model with a positive reliability index in this pass (GLM-5.2 at 24.3% accuracy, GLM-5.3 at 33.9%, Grok 4.3 at 34.6%) achieves calibration largely by knowing little and saying so; this one knows more than its peers *and* is better calibrated than almost all of them. Second, **MCP Atlas at 88.1% is the highest Model-Context-Protocol figure found anywhere in this pass**, with **Toolathlon at 75.6%**, **DeepSearchQA at 84.9%** and **OSWorld-Verified at 80.8%** also pass-leading or tied. Add **the highest tool-free HLE figure in this pass (52.2%)** and **the highest MMLU-Pro (88.7%)**. The counterweights are specific: **an AA Agentic Index of 27.5%**, **GDPval-AA at 1,375 Elo**, **ExploitGym at 0.8%** and **MRCR 1M at only 54.1%**.
- **Provider / access:** Meta AI, OpenRouter. Meta published a **dedicated "Muse Spark 1.1 evaluation report"**, which is the source of 20 of the 40 rows below — an unusually thorough vendor disclosure, and the reason this report has good coverage of agentic and multimodal benchmarks that most vendors omit.
- **Release / knowledge:** **July 2026** (repository metadata). **No verified knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `opencode/muse-spark-1.1` (OpenRouter `meta/muse-spark-1.1`). **No free-tier ID.**
- **Context window:** **1,048,576 tokens with 131K maximum output** (repository metadata). Unusually, retrieval is measured *at* the claimed length — and the result is a caveat rather than a credential: **MRCR 1M at 54.1%**, against DeepSeek V4 Pro's 83.5% on the same benchmark. **AA-LCR at 77.7%** is better.
- **Modalities:** **Text, image and PDF/file input → text output** (repository metadata). **PDF input places it in the methodology's "+video/PDF in" band.** No video or audio path; no non-text output. Measured breadth is good: **CharXiv at 88.4%** and **BabyVision at 76.3%**.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **$1.25 / 1M input, $4.25 / 1M output, with cached input at $0.15** (repository metadata). That is roughly **2.4× cheaper on input and 3.5× cheaper on output than the methodology's $3/$15 anchor**, with the cached rate about **8.3× below uncached** — an unusually steep caching discount. **No primary Meta rate card was reached; re-verify before budgeting.**
- **Architecture:** Proprietary, closed weights. Parameter count and topology: not disclosed.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 87/100.** Four pass-leading or tied-leading results, all vendor-reported but from an unusually detailed published evaluation report. **MCP Atlas at 88.1% is the highest Model-Context-Protocol figure found anywhere in this research pass** and directly substantiates the multi-agent orchestration positioning; **DeepSearchQA at 84.9% is also the highest here**; **Toolathlon at 75.6% is the highest raw Toolathlon figure in this pass**; and **OSWorld-Verified at 80.8% ties MiMo-V2.6-Flash for the lead**. Add **Cybench at 92.9%**, **WebArena-Verified at 69%** and a broad spread of domain agents (Finance Agent v2 57.2%, JobBench 54.7%, CyberGym 59.0%). Five deductions. **The AA Agentic Index of 27.5% is low and directly contradicts the individual results** — roughly half GLM-5.3's 53.4% — which suggests the composite harness penalises something the individual benchmarks do not capture, and that tension is a genuine warning rather than a measurement artefact to dismiss. **GDPval-AA at 1,375 Elo is 375 below the ~1750+ reference.** **Terminal-Bench 2.1 carries a 10.7-point vendor-to-independent gap** (80.0% against 69.3%) and neither figure reaches the anchor. **OSWorld 2.0 at 14.2% and ExploitGym at 0.8% both collapse.** And **Claw-Eval, τ²-bench, Terminal-Bench 3.0/4.0 and BrowseComp are unreported**, with the methodology explicitly penalising the Claw-Eval absence.
- **Reasoning: 88/100.** The strongest knowledge-and-reliability combination in this research pass. **HLE without tools reaches 52.2% — the highest tool-free figure found anywhere in this pass**, 12.2 points clear of the 40%+ reference, corroborated by 62.1% with tools and 46.2% independently at Artificial Analysis, so all three routes agree this is genuine frontier-exam capability. **MMLU-Pro at 88.7% is the highest in this pass.** **GPQA Diamond reaches 91.2% at Vals**, clearing the 90%+ reference. Above all, **the AA-Omniscience Index of +28.1% is the highest found anywhere in this pass, and — uniquely — it rests on the highest raw accuracy recorded here (52.1%)**: every other positively-indexed model in this pass is well-calibrated largely because it knows little, whereas this one knows the most and is still among the best calibrated. That is a materially more valuable property than either alone. Five deductions: **AA-GPQA Diamond at 89.8% misses the 90% reference by 0.2 points**, so only the Vals figure clears it; **the Intelligence Index of 33.7% is 26.3 points below the 60+ marker** and sits oddly against the HLE and MMLU-Pro results; **MRCR 1M at 54.1% is weak** for a model sold on a million-token window; **CritPt at 15.1% is low**; **the 50.0% hallucination rate remains substantial in absolute terms**; and **neither ARC-AGI nor FrontierMath has any figure**.
- **Context window: 92/100. Set below the ≥1M tier floor of 95, and the justification is a direct measurement rather than an inference.** The nominal window qualifies — **1,048,576 tokens with 131K maximum output** — and **AA-LCR at 77.7%** shows competent long-context reasoning. But **Meta published MRCR at the full 1M length and the result is 54.1%**, which is **29.4 points below DeepSeek V4 Pro's 83.5%** on the identical benchmark. That is the rare case where a vendor has measured retrieval at its own claimed ceiling and the number is mediocre, and it would be wrong to credit the tier floor against the vendor's own evidence. The score therefore sits in the upper 500K–1M territory: the window is real and usable, but depth performance at 1M is demonstrably weaker than peers carrying the same headline. **No RULER, CorpusQA or GraphWalks figure exists** to adjudicate further.
- **Multimodal: 84/100.** **PDF and file input places it in the methodology's "+video/PDF in = 75–90" band**, and the measured breadth inside that band is strong. **CharXiv at 88.4% is excellent chart reasoning** — among the highest in this research pass, within a point of GLM-5.3-Flash's 89.4% and comfortably ahead of GPT-5.4's 82.8% — and **BabyVision at 76.3% is the highest BabyVision figure in this pass**, roughly 23 points above GLM-5.3-Flash's 53.4%, which indicates real robustness on harder visual reasoning rather than only on charts. Three reasons it does not reach the band ceiling: **there is no video or audio input path**, so the top band is unavailable regardless of quality; **no MMMU or MMMU-Pro figure exists**, which is the benchmark most peers in this pass are measured on and its absence prevents direct comparison; and **no OCR benchmark exists**, so document *extraction* as distinct from document *reasoning* is unmeasured despite PDF input being the headline modality claim.
- **Coding: 82/100.** Solid and better-corroborated than most, with two references cleared. **The AA Coding Index at 71.3% clears the methodology's 70%+ reference** and **AA-SciCode at 58.8% clears the 55%+ reference** — both independently measured by Artificial Analysis. **LiveCodeBench at 85.9% and SWE-bench at 82.0%, both from Vals AI**, are genuinely strong independent results, and **SWE-bench Pro at 61.5%** is respectable. Three deductions: **deepSwe at 53.3% is 20.7 points below the 74%+ reference**, the largest single shortfall in this dimension; **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 80.0% misses the 85%+ coding reference by 5 points** and drops to 69.3% independently; and **SWE-bench Verified, Codeforces, CursorBench and Vibe Code Bench are all unreported**.
- **Cost efficiency: 72/100.** The rate card is good and the caching structure is unusually favourable. **$1.25 / $4.25 is roughly 2.4× cheaper on input and 3.5× cheaper on output than the methodology's $3/$15 anchor**, and **cached input at $0.15 is about 8.3× below the uncached rate** — one of the steeper caching discounts found in this pass, which matters a great deal for multi-agent orchestration where a shared system prompt and tool catalogue are re-sent on every turn. That is well matched to the model's actual strengths (MCP Atlas 88.1%, Toolathlon 75.6%). Three deductions. **It is superseded within its own family**: Muse Spark 1.2 scores 66.42 against this model's 65.93, and Muse Spark 1.3 is newer still, so the pricing should be checked against both before committing. **The agentic composite does not match the agentic components** — an AA Agentic Index of 27.5% alongside GDPval at 1,375 Elo means the real-world economic value per dollar is weaker than the individual benchmarks suggest. And **no primary Meta rate card was reached**, so all pricing rests on curated metadata.
- **Overall Score: 86.6/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (87 + 88 + 92 + 84 + 82) / 5 = 86.6 — a strong, broadly capable model whose single best property is one that most frontier models in this pass lack entirely. **Its AA-Omniscience Index of +28.1% is the highest found anywhere in this research pass, and it is the only model here that achieves a strong reliability index while also posting the highest raw knowledge accuracy (52.1%)** — the others are well-calibrated mainly because they know little. Combined with **the highest tool-free HLE in this pass (52.2%)** and **the highest MMLU-Pro (88.7%)**, that makes it the best choice here for work where both breadth of knowledge and trustworthiness matter together. On tools it is equally distinctive: **MCP Atlas at 88.1%, DeepSearchQA at 84.9% and Toolathlon at 75.6% all lead this pass**, with **OSWorld-Verified at 80.8% tied for the lead** and **Cybench at 92.9%**, which substantiates the multi-agent orchestration positioning. Add **CharXiv 88.4% and BabyVision 76.3%** on PDF-and-image input, **an AA Coding Index of 71.3% and AA-SciCode of 58.8% both clearing reference**, and **LiveCodeBench 85.9% / SWE-bench 82.0%** independently measured. Choose it for **multi-agent orchestration, MCP tool ecosystems, deep research, computer use, document and chart analysis, and any knowledge-intensive task where confabulation is costly.** Four cautions. **The AA Agentic Index of 27.5% contradicts the strong individual agentic results** and should be treated as a genuine warning about composite long-horizon performance, alongside **GDPval at 1,375 Elo**, **OSWorld 2.0 at 14.2%** and **ExploitGym at 0.8%**. **The million-token window underperforms at depth** — Meta's own MRCR 1M measurement is 54.1%, nearly 30 points below DeepSeek V4 Pro. **The Intelligence Index of 33.7% is low** and sits oddly against the HLE and MMLU-Pro figures. And **it is superseded by Muse Spark 1.2 and 1.3**, so verify pricing and capability against both.
