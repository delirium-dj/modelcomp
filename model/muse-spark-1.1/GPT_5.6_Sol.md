# Muse Spark 1.1 — Independent Research Report

## Summary and evidence

Muse Spark 1.1 is Meta's July 2026 multimodal reasoning model for agents. Meta highlights major gains in tool and computer use, coding, video understanding, and long-running workflows, and launched it through the Meta Model API preview and Meta AI Thinking mode. Early partner evidence emphasizes long-context agent workloads, but Meta's public announcement provides less reproducible numerical detail than leading model cards.

Source: [Meta Muse Spark 1.1 launch](https://ai.meta.com/blog/introducing-muse-spark-meta-model-api/)

## Cost and limitations

The public API began as a preview, with availability and commercial details less mature than established platforms. Strengths are native multimodality and agent orientation; limitations are sparse public benchmark methodology, unclear stable pricing, and rapid supersession by later Spark releases.

## Scores

- **Tool use: 90/100.** Tool and computer use are central improvements and product features.
- **Reasoning: 86/100.** Thinking mode is capable, though independently reproducible evidence is limited.
- **Context window: 89/100.** It targets long workflows, but precise limits are not clearly published.
- **Multimodal: 92/100.** Image and video understanding are explicit strengths.
- **Coding: 88/100.** Coding was a primary training and launch focus.
- **Cost efficiency: 88/100.** Meta positions Spark on the efficiency frontier, but stable public pricing is opaque.
- **Overall Score: 89/100.** Half-up rounded mean: (90 + 86 + 89 + 92 + 88) / 5 = 89.0.

## Bottom line

Muse Spark 1.1 is a capable multimodal agent model, best treated as a preview-era option rather than a transparently benchmarked production standard.
