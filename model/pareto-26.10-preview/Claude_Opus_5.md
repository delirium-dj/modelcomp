- **Provider / access:** Unbiased. Official model card at `unbiased.ai/model-card/`; preliminary results published at `unbiased.ai/`. **No independent harness has measured it**: no Artificial Analysis, Vals AI, ARC Prize, Epoch AI, Cursor or Gert Labs entry exists.
