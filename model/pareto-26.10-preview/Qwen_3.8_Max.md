## Model card

- **Name:** Pareto 26.10 Preview (API id `unbiased/pareto-26.10-preview`; vendor says call it `pareto`)
- **Short description:** **A composite ("blended") model, not a single set of weights.** From **Unbiased AI** (built by **Circuit & Chisel**, a remote-first US/Canada team), it "**runs a mix of frontier and open-source models against the same request and returns the best result**," choosing the model **per request without switching mid-conversation** (so prompt caching still works). Because the underlying model mix can change between releases, **its scores cannot be attributed to any single model.** The 26.10 preview's headline changes vs the September Pareto 26.9 are a **4× context jump (262,144 → 1,048,576 tokens)** and a **price cut to roughly a third**. Its real value proposition is **cost-efficiency**, and the name is apt: on the vendor's own charts it sits **on the Pareto (best-score-at-or-below-each-cost) frontier for all three benchmarks it reports** — **DeepSWE v1.1 69.9% at $0.24/task** (level with Fable 5's 70.0% but at ~1/56th the $13.50/task), **Terminal-Bench 4.0 50.8% at $0.48/task**, and **HLE text-only 49.9% at $0.008/task** (the *first* model on the HLE cost frontier), plus **GPQA-Diamond 92.4%**. ⚠ **Every one of those numbers is a vendor-run preliminary self-report** ("results are from October 1, 2026 runs and **may change before final publication**"; "**have not been independently reproduced**"), and on **raw score it trails the frontier on every benchmark** (Terminal-Bench 50.8 vs Claude Sonnet 5.5's 70.6; HLE 49.9 vs Fable 5.1's 59.1). **Independent verification is essentially absent** — benchlm.ai lists its public-lane score as **null** with only **1 shared sourced row** ("**evidence is too thin for a benchmark verdict**"), and the single third-party anchor (**Parallel's Search Intelligence Score 68.6 / 34.8-without-search at $116 per 1,000 tasks**) is for the **GA Pareto 26.10**, not this preview.
- **Provider / access:** **Unbiased AI** (platform of Circuit & Chisel). Served via the **Unbiased API** (`platform.unbiased.ai`, model id `pareto`) and resold by third-party gateways — **OpenRouter** (`unbiased/pareto-26.10-preview`), **Puter.js AI API**, and **AI/ML API** (`api.aimlapi.com/v1`, OpenAI-compatible `POST /v1/chat/completions`). **Proprietary** (a routing/ensemble layer over other models — there are no "Pareto weights" to download). **No free-id tier** (`noFreeId: true`), though Puter offers a free browser playground allowance. Listed here as `opencode/pareto-26.10-preview`.
- **Release / knowledge:** **2026-10-01** (preview). Promoted to the stable **GA "Pareto 26.10"** on **2026-10-15**. Unbiased states the **preview "may change without notice"** and points to the stable Pareto release for behavior you can pin — so production users are advised to stay on the GA/stable card. Knowledge cutoff not stated in the sources reviewed. **Not deprecated** — it is the current Pareto line, ahead of Pareto 26.9 (Sept) and the base Pareto.
- **IDs:** `unbiased/pareto-26.10-preview` (OpenRouter / Puter / AI-ML API) / `pareto` (Unbiased API) / `opencode/pareto-26.10-preview` (this dataset).
- **Context window:** **1,048,576 tokens in (1M) / 131,072 max output (131K)** — confirmed identically by Puter, AI/ML API and OpenRouter. This is a **4× increase** over Pareto 26.9's 262,144 and is one of the preview's two headline changes (enough for "a whole repository, a large document set or a long agent transcript in one prompt"). *Verified against the meta's rounded "1,000,000 in; 131,000 max output" — the exact figures are 1,048,576 / 131,072.* ⚠ No independent long-context benchmark (RULER/LongBench/etc.) validates *effective* use of the 1M window, and it is a preview build.
- **Modalities:** **Text + image in → text out** (capabilities: streaming, tool calling, vision). Screenshots, diagrams and scanned pages go into the prompt alongside surrounding text. **Image-only — no video, no audio.** *Matches the meta ("Text + image in; text out").* No vision-specific benchmark is reported for the 26.10 preview (the earlier 26.9 published MMMU-Pro 78 / ArXivMath 88, vendor-run).
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-25):** **$0.80 input / $0.03 cached / $3.20 output per 1M tokens** on the vendor card, OpenRouter and Puter — *verified against the meta's "$0.80/$3.20 per 1M (cached $0.03)," which is accurate.* **AI/ML API marks this up slightly** to $0.8464 / $0.03174 / $3.3856 (its prose also cites a $1.10032/$4.40128 tier — host-specific and inconsistent, so treat per-host pricing as provisional). The more meaningful figures for a composite are the vendor's **cost-per-task**: **$0.24 DeepSWE, $0.48 Terminal-Bench, $0.008 HLE, $0.004 GPQA-Diamond** — all on the cost-efficiency frontier, and the price fell to ~1/3 of the September Pareto release. ⚠ All cost/task numbers are **vendor-run preliminary**; being an ensemble, real spend depends on how many underlying models it invokes per request and on cache-hit rate.
- **Architecture:** **Composite / blended ensemble-routing layer** — "instead of one set of weights, it runs a mix of frontier and open-source models against the same request and returns the best result." The model is **chosen per request and does not switch mid-conversation** (preserving prompt caching). Sources describe the mechanism slightly differently — Puter says it "runs several models … and returns one answer," AI/ML API says it "runs a mix … and returns the best result" — but both agree it is **not a single checkpoint**. This is why **Unbiased publishes its losses alongside its wins** but also why **no score can be attributed to a single underlying model**, and why the mix (and therefore behavior) can change between releases.

### Raw benchmarks found

⚠ **Provenance warning:** every Pareto 26.10 Preview score below is **vendor-run by Unbiased** from a **preliminary October 1, 2026 run** that "**may change before final publication**" and has "**not been independently reproduced**." The comparison models' scores come from Artificial Analysis / Anthropic / OpenAI / the DeepSWE leaderboard, each on **their own task sets and harnesses**, so Unbiased itself notes they are "**not directly comparable with our runs**." There is **no independent AA Intelligence Index, no Vals SWE-bench-Verified row, no Epoch ECI, and no benchlm BenchAlign composite** for this preview.

**Vendor — Unbiased preliminary Oct-1-2026 run (cost-efficiency framing; "up-and-left is better"):**

- **DeepSWE v1.1 (agentic coding): 69.9% at $0.24/task.** vs GPT-6.1 Sol 75.2% ($0.65), GPT 6 Astra 74.0% ($4.43), **Fable 5 70.0% ($13.50/task — Pareto is level on score at ~1/56th the cost)**, GPT-6 Luna 66.6% ($0.22). On the frontier (best at-or-below its cost): GPT-6 Luna → Pareto 26.10 → GPT-6.1 Sol.
- **Terminal-Bench 4.0 (agentic terminal use): 50.8% at $0.48/task.** vs Claude Sonnet 5.5 70.6% ($12.54), GPT 6 Astra 59.6% ($5.86), GPT-6.1 Sol 56.1% ($1.82), Fable 5.1 55.1% ($15.78), DeepSeek 4.1 Flash 26.8% ($1.16), GPT-6 Luna 12.6% ($0.24). Frontier: GPT-6 Luna → Pareto 26.10 → GPT-6.1 Sol → GPT 6 Astra → Claude Sonnet 5.5.
- **Humanity's Last Exam, text-only (expert reasoning): 49.9% at $0.008/task.** vs Fable 5.1 59.1% ($1.59), Claude Sonnet 5.5 55.0% ($0.54), GPT 6 Astra 54.7% ($0.38), GPT-6.1 Sol 52.9% ($0.078), DeepSeek 4.1 Flash 39.2% ($0.042), GPT-6 Luna 38.5% ($0.012). **Pareto is the *first* model on the HLE cost frontier** (best score at-or-below $0.008/task).

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 78/100.** Supports **tool calling and streaming**, and its agentic-terminal number — **Terminal-Bench 4.0 50.8% at $0.48/task** — is **solid-mid**: it beats DeepSeek 4.1 Flash (26.8%) and GPT-6 Luna (12.6%) but trails **four** frontier models (Claude Sonnet 5.5 70.6, GPT 6 Astra 59.6, GPT-6.1 Sol 56.1, Fable 5.1 55.1). **DeepSWE v1.1 69.9%** is a respectable agentic-coding result. Held to **78** because there is **no AA Coding Agent Index, AA-Briefcase, AutomationBench or τ²-bench** row, both figures are **vendor-run preliminary and unreproduced**, and a composite's tool behavior depends on whichever underlying model is routed. Above the image-only floor but below the verified agentic leaders (GPT-5.6 Luna 84, Grok 4.7).
- **Reasoning: 80/100.** **GPQA-Diamond 92.4%** is genuinely **frontier-adjacent** (within 0.4 of GPT-5.4's 92.8% on benchlm's one shared row), and **HLE text-only 49.9% at $0.008/task** is **solid-mid** — ahead of DeepSeek 4.1 Flash (39.2%) and GPT-6 Luna (38.5%), behind Fable 5.1 (59.1), Sonnet 5.5 (55.0), Astra (54.7) and Sol (52.9%). The lone independent reasoning-adjacent anchor is **Parallel's Search Intelligence 68.6 / 34.8-without-search** (GA build). Scored **80** — crediting the strong GPQA and the independent search signal, but **discounted** because there is **no AA Intelligence Index and no BenchAlign composite** (benchlm public-lane = null), and both headline numbers are **vendor-run, preliminary, and unreproduced**.
- **Context window: 84/100.** **1,048,576 in / 131,072 out**, confirmed identically by **three independent hosts** (Puter, AI/ML API, OpenRouter) — a **4× jump** over Pareto 26.9's 262,144 and a headline feature. This is the **proven-1M tier** on paper (calibration 1M ≈ 85), nudged to **84** because it is a **preview build that "may change without notice"** and **no independent long-context benchmark** (RULER/LongBench/needle-in-haystack) validates *effective* use of the full 1M — and because, for a composite, the real window depends on the routed model. The **131K output** is generous. Level with the confirmed-500K+ tier and just below proven-1M flagships.
- **Multimodal: 77/100.** **Text + image in → text out** with streaming/tool-calling/vision — **image-only** (no video, no audio). Scored at the **image-only floor (77)** because **no vision-specific benchmark is reported for the 26.10 preview** (the 26.9's MMMU-Pro 78 / ArXivMath 88 are a *different, earlier* build and vendor-run), and the composite's vision capability inherits from whichever underlying model handles the request. Below the video-input models (Seed 2.0 Pro 84) and level with the image-only tier (GPT-5.6 Luna, Grok 4.5/4.7, Mistral Large 4 at 77–78).
- **Coding: 79/100.** **DeepSWE v1.1 69.9% at $0.24/task** is the strongest signal — **level with Fable 5's 70.0%** and ahead of GPT-6 Luna (66.6%), though behind GPT-6.1 Sol (75.2%) and GPT 6 Astra (74.0%). But **Terminal-Bench 4.0 is only 50.8%** (mid), and there is **no SWE-bench Verified, LiveCodeBench, SWE-bench Pro or Vals row** at all. Held to **79** because both figures are **vendor-run preliminary and unreproduced**, the DeepSWE leaderboard's own harness is "not directly comparable," and a composite's coding result is not attributable to a single model. Mid-tier — below the elite-Vals coding models (86) and the strong open-weight coders (85).
- **Cost efficiency: 90/100.** **The standout, and the entire product thesis.** Pareto sits **on the cost-efficiency (Pareto) frontier for all three reported benchmarks** — **$0.24/task on DeepSWE** (matching Fable 5's score at ~1/56th of its $13.50/task), **$0.48/task on Terminal-Bench**, and **$0.008/task on HLE (first on that frontier)** — with per-token pricing of **$0.80/$3.20 (cached $0.03)**, cut to **~⅓ of the September release**. Independently, **Parallel measured $116 per 1,000 search tasks** on the GA. Scored **90** — a genuinely frontier cost-efficiency position, held just below the 93 ceiling because it is a **preview with volatile, host-dependent pricing** (AI/ML API marks it up), the cost/task figures are **vendor-run**, and an ensemble's real spend scales with how many underlying models it invokes plus cache-hit rate.
- **Overall Score: 80/100.** Mean of the five quality dimensions = (78 Tool + 80 Reasoning + 84 Context + 77 Multimodal + 79 Coding) / 5 = **398/5 = 79.6 → 80** (Cost efficiency 90 is scored but **not** counted, per the v4 formula). **Divergence note:** my independent read is **80 vs the queue's curated 84.3 → −4.3.** The gap is justified by provenance, not capability dispute: the curated figure appears to credit the frontier-adjacent **GPQA 92.4**, **DeepSWE 69.9** and **1M context** largely at face value, whereas **all of Pareto's headline numbers are vendor-run preliminary self-reports explicitly "not independently reproduced" and "may change before final publication"**; the model **trails the raw-score frontier on every benchmark**; **independent verification is essentially absent** (benchlm public-lane = null, "too thin for a verdict"; the sole Parallel anchor is for the *GA*, not this preview); and it is a **composite whose scores are not attributable to a single model** and whose mix can change between releases. Its real strength — **best-in-class cost-efficiency** — is captured in the (uncounted) Cost dimension. This −4.3 mirrors mistral-large-4 (also a preview with mid independent standing), and the meta's own tradeoff already warns to "treat both scores and pricing as provisional."
