## Model card

- **Name:** Grok 4.5. Proprietary. Variant/alias flag: predecessor to **Grok 4.6** and **Grok 4.7**; the Grok 4.x line also contains 4.3, 4.20 (with a Multi-agent beta) and several Fast/Mini variants. **Superseded twice**, but a composite tracker still places it **#33 of 783 at 65.65/100**.
- **Short description:** xAI's Grok 4.5, and the model with **the best independently measured cost-per-task figure in this entire research pass**: Artificial Analysis recorded it at **$0.37 per Intelligence Index task — cheaper than every model in its intelligence cluster**, including GPT-5.6 Sol at medium ($0.39) and Muse Spark 1.2 ($0.40), and roughly a third of GPT-5.5's $1.18. Capability-wise it is strong on coding and reasoning: **SWE-bench 86.6% and LiveCodeBench 87.4% (both Vals AI), VulcanBench v3 89.9%, an AA Coding Index of 72.5% that clears the frontier reference**, and **GPQA Diamond corroborated at 93.1%/92.9% by two independent labs**.
- **Provider / access:** xAI's own API, OpenRouter (`x-ai/grok-4.5`), **Cursor** (which published its own Grok 4.5 launch post) and third-party hosts. Reasoning: explicit reasoning mode, benchmarked by Artificial Analysis at **"high"** effort.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released in the Grok 4.5 cycle, succeeding Grok 4.3. **No verified public release date or knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `grok-4-5`; `x-ai/grok-4.5` (OpenRouter).
- **Context window:** **500,000 tokens** — the top of the methodology's 200K–500K tier, and the same window its two successors carry. Max output: no verified figure found. **AA-LCR at 79.3%**, essentially level with Grok 4.6 (80.3%) and above Grok 4.7 (76.7%).
- **Modalities:** **No modality specification was retrievable.** Text in and text out are certain; **image input is confirmed in practice by a published AA-MMMU-Pro figure of 80.4%** — stronger evidence than exists for Grok 4.6 or 4.7, neither of which has any multimodal benchmark. No video or audio evidence.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **No rate card was verified**, but the measured figure is better than a rate card: **Artificial Analysis recorded $0.37 per Intelligence Index task, the cheapest in its intelligence cluster**, published in its Muse Spark 1.2 analysis of 2026-08-05. Third-party coverage separately characterises the successor Grok 4.6 as matching GPT-5.6 Sol at half the price (roughly $2/$10), and Grok 4.7's launch as "Same Price" again — so the line has held its pricing across three generations. **Re-verify before budgeting.**
- **Architecture:** Proprietary, closed weights. Parameter count not disclosed for 4.5; third-party coverage cites **1.5 trillion parameters for Grok 4.6**.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 78/100.** The evidence base is thin and the measured results are mid-field. **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 83.3% (vendor) falls to 67.8% under Vals AI's independent harness — a 15.5-point gap** — and **Terminal-Bench 3.0 at 15.7% is a steep fall** on the harder version. **GDPval-AA at 1,430 Elo is roughly 320 below the ~1750+ reference** and 213 Elo below its own successor, so this is the generation before xAI's long-horizon work became competitive. The **AA Agentic Index of 42.1%** is respectable but 11.3 points below Grok 4.6. And **Tau2-bench, Tau3-Banking, Claw-Eval, MCP-Atlas, Toolathlon, OSWorld and AutomationBench are every one of them unreported** — seven methodology-relevant references with no value, which triggers the explicit missing-evidence penalty.
- **Reasoning: 87/100.** Strong and unusually well corroborated. **AA-GPQA Diamond at 93.1% with Vals AI at 92.9% clears the 90%+ frontier reference and agrees within 0.2 points across two independent labs** — no vendor figure needed. **AA-HLE at 42.7% clears the 40%+ reference**, **ARC Prize's official leaderboard records ARC-AGI-2 at 52.6%** on a benchmark built to resist memorisation, **AA-LCR reaches 79.3%** and MMLU-Pro is independently confirmed at 89.2%. Four deductions: the **Intelligence Index of 38.8% is 21 points below the 60+ marker** (and the two published index values — 38.8% rescaled, 54 on the earlier version — cannot be reconciled); **ARC-AGI-3 at 0.3% is a floor result**; **CritPt at 15.4%** is mid-range; and the **54.1% hallucination rate** is high, with both successors improving on it materially.
- **Context window: 83/100.** **500,000 tokens sits near the top of the methodology's 200K–500K tier (65–84)**, and **AA-LCR at 79.3%** demonstrates the window is usable — essentially level with Grok 4.6's 80.3% and above Grok 4.7's 76.7%, so this generation's long-context reasoning has held up. Two things prevent a higher placement: the tier is size-defined and 500K is half the 1M that Google, Alibaba, Moonshot, Z.AI, MiniMax and DeepSeek now offer at comparable standing; and **no MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks retrieval measurement exists**, with max output tokens undocumented.
- **Multimodal: 66/100.** Input is **text and image** on the evidence of a published AA-MMMU-Pro figure, with text-only output; **no modality specification was retrievable**. **AA-MMMU-Pro at 80.4% is respectable and independently measured** — ahead of Claude Sonnet 5 and GPT-5.3 Codex — and it is worth noting that **this is the only multimodal benchmark published for any model in the Grok 4.5–4.7 range**, so this generation is the only one in the line with any measured vision capability at all. It sits mid-band because a single datapoint cannot demonstrate breadth: **no CharXiv, document, OCR, video or audio benchmark exists**, and Design Arena Website at 1,289 Elo is mid-field.
- **Coding: 88/100.** The strongest dimension, and almost all of it independent. **SWE-bench at 86.6% and LiveCodeBench at 87.4%, both from Vals AI**, are excellent and beat several nominally stronger models; **VulcanBench v3 at 89.9% is the highest figure recorded in this entire research pass** on that independent suite; **the AA Coding Index at 72.5% clears the methodology's 70%+ frontier reference**; and **AA-SciCode at 55.0% sits exactly at the 55%+ reference**. CursorBench 3.2 (66.7%), SWE-bench Pro (64.7%) and SWE Multilingual (78%) fill out a broad record. Three deductions: **DeepSWE at 53% is roughly 21 points below the 74%+ frontier reference** and far below both successors, so long-horizon agentic coding is this generation's clear weakness; **Terminal-Bench 2.1 falls 15.5 points under independent measurement**; and **PostTrainBench v1.1 at 23.4%** is low.
- **Cost efficiency: 88/100.** **This is the best-evidenced cost position in the entire research pass, because it is measured rather than quoted.** Artificial Analysis recorded **$0.37 per Intelligence Index task — explicitly the cheapest in its intelligence cluster**, against GPT-5.6 Sol at medium ($0.39), Muse Spark 1.2 ($0.40), GPT-5.6 Terra at max ($0.51), Kimi K3 at max ($0.86) and GPT-5.5 at xhigh ($1.18). A model delivering SWE-bench 86.6%, LiveCodeBench 87.4%, VulcanBench v3 89.9% and GPQA Diamond 93.1% at the lowest cost-per-task in its band is outstanding value, and the pricing has demonstrably held across the line (Grok 4.6 "half of GPT-5.6 Sol", Grok 4.7 "Same Price"). Three deductions: **no rate card was verified directly**; **no free tier** is documented; and the value case is partly historical — its own successors cost the same while scoring higher on GDPval (+213 Elo), DeepSWE (+12.9 to +18 points) and hallucination rate (−19.8 points).
- **Overall Score: 80.4/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (78 + 87 + 83 + 66 + 88) / 5 = 80.4 — a strong, exceptionally cheap coder and reasoner from the generation before xAI fixed its agentic weaknesses. Choose it for **repository-scale and algorithmic coding at minimum cost**: SWE-bench 86.6%, LiveCodeBench 87.4%, an AA Coding Index of 72.5% that clears the frontier reference, **VulcanBench v3 89.9% — the best in this dataset** — and an independently measured **$0.37 per task, the cheapest in its intelligence cluster**. Choose it for **graduate-level science** (GPQA Diamond corroborated at 93.1%/92.9% by two labs) and **novel abstract reasoning** (ARC Prize-verified ARC-AGI-2 52.6%). Do not choose it for **long-horizon agentic work** — DeepSWE at 53% is ~21 points off the frontier reference, GDPval-AA at 1,430 Elo is ~320 below it, Terminal-Bench 3.0 is 15.7%, and seven tool-use references are unreported. Do not rely on it for **multimodal breadth** (one benchmark) or **unaided facts** (54.1% hallucination rate). And weigh the succession carefully: **Grok 4.6 holds the same price and adds +213 GDPval Elo, +12.9 DeepSWE points, +11.3 AA Agentic Index points, better hallucination behaviour and SWE-bench at 95.6%** — for most workloads it strictly dominates, with this model's remaining edges being VulcanBench v3, AA-SciCode and the documented $0.37 per-task measurement.
