## Model card

- **Name:** Grok 4.5
- **Short description:** **xAI's mid-2026 closed-weights flagship, co-developed with Cursor and built around one idea: token-efficient agentic coding.** Launched **2026-07-08** at **$2/$6 per 1M** — deliberately priced "like a volume product, not a trophy tier" — it is the model that made **closed-model price compression** the story rather than a benchmark sweep. Its signature is **efficiency**: it resolves an average **SWE-bench Pro task in 15,954 output tokens vs Claude Opus 4.8-max's 67,020 (~4.2× fewer)**, at **80 tokens/sec**, so real-world cost-per-task is far below what the sticker price implies. On the neutral **Vals AI SWE-bench Verified** ruler it is genuinely elite on long-horizon work — **94 / 84 / 74 / 67** by difficulty band (67% on >4-hour tasks, among the best anywhere) — and **BenchLM's independent composite ranks it #6 overall (76.97), #6 in coding (71.25), #12 in agentic (62.09), behind Claude Opus 4.8 and ahead of GPT-5.5.** The honest read is **"competitive, not dominant"**: **Terminal-Bench 2.1 83.3** beats Opus 4.8-max (78.9) and sits beside GPT-5.5 (83.4), but it **trails Fable 5 on DeepSWE 1.0/1.1, Terminal-Bench and SWE-bench Pro**, and trails Opus 4.8 on SWE-bench Multilingual. ⚠ **Three counterweights: (1) it is image-only** (text + image/file in → text out; no video, no audio); **(2) product-training contamination risk** — Cursor "accidentally included an earlier Cursor codebase snapshot in training" and excludes CursorBench; BenchLM's rule is "product evidence is evidence of product fit, not general ranking evidence"; **(3) access** — heavily distributed through **Cursor and Grok Build**, and **not available in the EU at launch** (mid-July expected).
