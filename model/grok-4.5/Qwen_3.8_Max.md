## Model card

- **Name:** Grok 4.5
- **Short description:** **xAI's mid-2026 closed-weights flagship, co-developed with Cursor and built around one idea: token-efficient agentic coding.** Launched **2026-07-08** at **$2/$6 per 1M** — deliberately priced "like a volume product, not a trophy tier" — it is the model that made **closed-model price compression** the story rather than a benchmark sweep. Its signature is **efficiency**: it resolves an average **SWE-bench Pro task in 15,954 output tokens vs Claude Opus 4.8-max's 67,020 (~4.2× fewer)**, at **80 tokens/sec**, so real-world cost-per-task is far below what the sticker price implies. On the neutral **Vals AI SWE-bench Verified** ruler it is genuinely elite on long-horizon work — **94 / 84 / 74 / 67** by difficulty band (67% on >4-hour tasks, among the best anywhere) — and **BenchLM's independent composite ranks it #6 overall (76.97), #6 in coding (71.25), #12 in agentic (62.09), behind Claude Opus 4.8 and ahead of GPT-5.5.** The honest read is **"competitive, not dominant"**: **Terminal-Bench 2.1 83.3** beats Opus 4.8-max (78.9) and sits beside GPT-5.5 (83.4), but it **trails Fable 5 on DeepSWE 1.0/1.1, Terminal-Bench and SWE-bench Pro**, and trails Opus 4.8 on SWE-bench Multilingual. ⚠ **Three counterweights: (1) it is image-only** (text + image/file in → text out; no video, no audio); **(2) product-training contamination risk** — Cursor "accidentally included an earlier Cursor codebase snapshot in training" and excludes CursorBench; BenchLM's rule is "product evidence is evidence of product fit, not general ranking evidence"; **(3) access** — heavily distributed through **Cursor and Grok Build**, and **not available in the EU at launch** (mid-July expected). It is now the earlier of xAI's 4.x line, superseded by **Grok 4.6 and 4.7**.
- **Provider / access:** **xAI (SpaceXAI).** First-party API via the **SpaceXAI console** (`grok-4.5`; aliases `grok-4.5-latest`, `grok-build-latest`; regions `us-east-1`/`us-west-2`), **OpenRouter** (`x-ai/grok-4.5`), and **Vercel AI Gateway**; plus deep product integration in **Cursor** (desktop/web/iOS/CLI/SDK, all plans, trained jointly on trillions of tokens of Cursor data) and as the **default model in Grok Build**. A **fast variant** is priced at $4/$18. **Closed / proprietary** — no open weights. **No free-id tier** (`noFreeId: true`); limited-time free usage was offered in Grok Build/Cursor at launch. Listed here as `opencode/grok-4.5`.
- **Release / knowledge:** **2026-07-08** (BenchLM + Musk's same-day X post; datanorth reported it July 13, EU availability expected mid-July). Knowledge cutoff not stated in the sources reviewed. Superseded within the 4.x line by **Grok 4.6** and **Grok 4.7**.
- **IDs:** `grok-4.5` (xAI console) / `grok-4.5-latest` / `grok-build-latest` / `x-ai/grok-4.5` (OpenRouter) / `opencode/grok-4.5` (this dataset).
- **Context window:** **500,000 (500K) tokens, with an unusually deep output window (meta: up to ~450K out)** — confirmed 500K by xAI docs/BenchLM/datanorth. **Higher-context pricing applies above 200K tokens** (relevant for long agent loops). The deep output window is best-in-class for generating large artifacts (multi-file code, decks, spreadsheets) in one pass.
- **Modalities:** **Text, image, and file input → text output** (**image-only** — no video, no audio). Reasoning model with **function calling, structured outputs, web search, X (Twitter) search, code execution, and configurable reasoning**. Grok Build adds native artifact generation (multi-sheet Excel, PowerPoint with native shapes, Word docs).
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-24):** **$2.00 input / $6.00 output per 1M**; **cached input** reported at **$0.50 at launch (benchlm + datanorth, citing xAI docs)** while the **scaffolded meta and llm-stats list $0.30** — a likely **post-launch price cut**, so treat $0.30 as current and $0.50 as the July launch figure (flagged, not silently overwritten). Higher-context pricing above 200K tokens; Cursor fast-variant at $4/$18. The defining economic fact is **token efficiency (~4.2× fewer output tokens per SWE-bench Pro task than Opus 4.8-max)** — effective cost-per-completed-task is far below the per-token sticker.
- **Architecture:** **Closed / proprietary — parameter count and internals not disclosed.** Trained across **tens of thousands of NVIDIA GB300 GPUs**, with **reinforcement learning spanning hundreds of thousands of multi-step software-engineering tasks**; trained jointly with Cursor on trillions of tokens of Cursor codebase/tool-interaction data, with a broader mix than Composer 2.5 (STEM, research papers, knowledge work). Cursor notes Grok 4.5 and Composer are **different model weight classes**.

### Raw benchmarks found

Two independent anchors exist — the neutral **Vals AI** coding ruler and **BenchLM's** mapped composite — alongside a **mixed-source vendor/Cursor table** (xAI's own harness for most rows). Meta's headline vendor claims (SWE-bench 86.6%, GPQA 93.1%) are **not** corroborated in the independent sources reviewed.

**Independent — Vals AI SWE-bench Verified (bash-tool-only neutral harness):**

- **Grok 4.5: 94 / 84 / 74 / 67** by difficulty band (<15 min / 15 m–1 h / 1–4 hr / >4 hr) — an **elite long-horizon profile** (67% on >4-hour tasks); on the same ruler Grok 4.6 = 96/96/93/100 and Claude Opus 5 = 98/97/90/100, so 4.5 is just below its successor but ahead of most of the field at the top bands.

**Independent — BenchLM composite (mapped + normalized, "Supported" evidence):**

- **#6 overall at 76.97**, **#6 in coding at 71.25**, **#12 in agentic work at 62.09** — behind Claude Opus 4.8, ahead of GPT-5.5. Note the **agentic rank (#12) is well below the coding rank (#6)** — broader office/knowledge-agent work is its relative weak spot.

**Mixed-source vendor/Cursor table (xAI official chart + Cursor launch post; xAI harness for DeepSWE 1.0, DataCurve mini-swe-agent for DeepSWE 1.1):**

- **Terminal-Bench 2.1: 83.3%** — beats Opus 4.8-max (78.9%), beside GPT-5.5-xhigh (83.4%), just behind Fable 5 (84.3%)
- **DeepSWE 1.0 (provider harness): 62.0%** — beats Opus 4.8-max (55.75%), behind Fable 5-max (66.1%) and GPT-5.5-xhigh (64.31%)
- **DeepSWE 1.1 (DataCurve mini-swe-agent): 53.0%** — below Opus 4.8-max (59%), well below Fable 5-max (70%) and GPT-5.5-xhigh (67%)
- **SWE-bench Pro resolve: 64.7%** — behind Fable 5-max (80.4%) and Opus 4.8-max (69.2%), beside GLM 5.2 (62.1%)
- **SWE-bench Multilingual (Cursor): 78.0%** — strong; behind Opus 4.8 (84.4%), beside GPT-5.5 (77.8%)
- **Token efficiency: 15,954 output tokens per SWE-bench Pro task vs 67,020 for Opus 4.8-max (~4.2× fewer); ~2× the token efficiency of comparable leaders; 80 tokens/sec**
- **Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark: #1** (vendor launch claim; no visible score published — treated as a claim, not a ranked row)

> Provenance: Grok 4.5 is **better-evidenced than the recent vendor-only previews** (deepseek-v4-vision-exp, qwen-3.8-flash-next): it has a **strong independent Vals row (94/84/74/67)** and an **independent BenchLM composite (#6/#6/#12, Supported)**, so its coding and overall placement are verified, not merely attested. The remaining table is **mixed-source and vendor-harness** — BenchLM is explicit that DeepSWE 1.0 is "within each model provider's harness," DeepSWE 1.1 is a DataCurve run, and Cursor's rows are self-reported — and it carries a real **contamination caveat**: Cursor "accidentally included an earlier Cursor codebase snapshot in training" and therefore **excludes CursorBench**; BenchLM's governing rule is "**product evidence is evidence of product fit, not general ranking evidence.**" The scaffolded meta.json was **verified rather than inherited**: 500K context ✓, text+image/file image-only modalities ✓, $2/$6 pricing ✓, Cursor co-development + token-efficiency standout ✓, and the "adoption flows through Cursor/Build" tradeoff ✓ (though it *is* also on the first-party SpaceXAI console + OpenRouter, so that framing is slightly overstated). **Two flags, not silent edits:** (a) **cached price** — meta/llm-stats say **$0.30**, but benchlm + datanorth (July launch, citing xAI docs) say **$0.50**; I treat $0.50 as the launch figure and $0.30 as a likely later cut. (b) meta's **SWE-bench 86.6% / GPQA 93.1%** are **vendor claims not corroborated** in the independent sources (which report SWE-bench *Pro* 64.7% and no independent GPQA). **No benchmark was invented.**

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 82/100.** A full tool suite (**function calling, structured outputs, web search, X search, code execution, configurable reasoning**) plus **elite long-horizon coding-agent endurance** on the neutral Vals ruler (**74% at 1–4 hr, 67% at >4 hr**) and **#1 on Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark** (vendor). Held at 82 — **below Grok 4.7 (88)** and the independently-verified agentic leaders — because **BenchLM ranks its broader agentic work only #12 (62.09)**, well under its #6 coding rank, meaning office/knowledge-agent tasks (not bash-tool SWE) are the weak spot; image-only input also limits GUI-agent scope versus Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (AndroidWorld/OSWorld).
- **Reasoning: 82/100.** **BenchLM #6 overall (76.97)** is a solid independent composite, and the meta's vendor **GPQA 93.1%** would be elite *if* independently confirmed (it is not, in the sources reviewed). Trained on coding/science/engineering/math with configurable reasoning. Scored **below Grok 4.6/4.7 and Kimi K2.6 (85, AA Index 54)** — there is **no Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index** for 4.5 and the strongest reasoning figure (GPQA) is vendor-only — but the independent BenchLM overall placement keeps it firmly in the strong tier.
- **Context window: 83/100.** **500K native** (below the 1M club's 85) but with an **exceptionally deep output window (~450K out per meta)** — best-in-class for generating large artifacts and long agent loops in a single pass. **Higher-context pricing above 200K** is a real cost caveat for long loops. Scored above a plain 500K model on the strength of the deep output window; below the confirmed-1M-native models (85).
- **Multimodal: 77/100.** **Image-only** (text + image/file in → text out; **no video, no audio**), with no standout multimodal benchmark published in the sources reviewed. Scored level with **Grok 4.7 (77, image-only)** and below the video/audio models — Qwen3.8-Flash-Next and Qwen3.8-27B (text+image+video, 82), Inkling (text+image+audio, 82), Kimi K2.6 (text+image+video, 79). Breadth is the limiting factor, not quality.
- **Coding: 85/100.** The standout dimension, and independently anchored: **Vals SWE-bench Verified 94/84/74/67** (elite long-horizon — 67% at >4 hr beats Kimi K2.6's 0% and DeepSeek-V4-Flash's 67% ties), **Terminal-Bench 2.1 83.3** (beats Opus 4.8-max 78.9), **SWE-bench Multilingual 78.0**, **SWE-bench Pro 64.7**, and the **~4.2× token-efficiency lead**. Scored level with **Kimi K2.6 / GPT-5.2 (85)** — the independent Vals long-horizon is the anchor — but held **below Grok 4.6 (Vals 96/96/93/100)** and the Fable 5 tier because the mixed table is **"competitive, not dominant"** (trails Fable 5 on DeepSWE 1.0/1.1, Terminal-Bench and SWE-bench Pro) and BenchLM ranks coding #6, not top-3.
- **Cost efficiency: 86/100.** The whole thesis of the release: **$2/$6 with ~4.2× fewer output tokens per SWE-bench Pro task than Opus 4.8-max, at 80 tok/sec** — BenchLM's "closed-model price compression," where effective cost-per-completed-task is far below the sticker. Cached input ($0.30 current / $0.50 launch) helps repeated repo/policy context; Cursor bundles usage in plans. Held **below the open-weight cost leaders (DeepSeek-V4-Vision-Exp / Inkling 92, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next 88)** because it is **proprietary with no self-host path** and has an **above-200K context premium** — but it is the strongest *closed-model* value story in this batch. Scored but **not** counted in the Overall per methodology.
- **Overall Score: 82/100.** Mean of Tool 82 + Reasoning 82 + Context 83 + Multimodal 77 + Coding 85 = 409/5 = 81.8 → 82. Best fit: **high-volume, long-running agentic coding inside Cursor/Grok Build or via a $2/$6 API**, where token efficiency, an elite long-horizon Vals profile, a 500K/deep-output context, and image+file input deliver frontier-adjacent coding at a volume-tier price. **Not** the pick for video/audio multimodal work (image-only), for the absolute strongest coding (Fable 5 / Opus 4.8 / Grok 4.6 lead the mixed table), for broad knowledge-agent work (BenchLM #12 agentic), for EU users at launch (unavailable), or for anyone needing open weights / self-hosting / weight-level audit. **Divergence note:** my 82 is **3.1 below the queue's curated 85.1**, driven by the **image-only modality floor (Multimodal 77)**, the **"competitive-not-dominant" mixed coding table** (it trails Fable 5 and Opus 4.8 on several rows despite the strong Vals long-horizon), the **CursorBench contamination / product-fit caveat**, and its status as the **earlier 4.x gen now superseded by Grok 4.6/4.7** — against genuinely elite independent long-horizon coding (Vals 94/84/74/67), a #6 independent composite, and a category-defining token-efficiency/price story.
