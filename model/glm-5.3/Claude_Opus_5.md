## Model card

- **Name:** GLM-5.3. **Open weights.** Variant/alias flag: flagship of the GLM-5 family, above GLM-5.2, GLM-5.3-Flash, GLM-5.1, GLM-5-Turbo, GLM-5 and the vision-specialised GLM-5V-Turbo. Exposes a `reasoning_effort` parameter with three levels — `low`, `high`, `max` — **defaulting to `max`**, and Z.AI instructs that leaderboard reproduction must use the default. A `clear_thinking` chat-template flag defaults to `false`.
- **Short description:** Z.AI's flagship, and **the strongest open-weight model in this research pass.** A composite tracker places it **#20 of 784 at 68.61/100**, far above GLM-5.2 (61.47) and GLM-5.3-Flash (57.37). Z.AI states plainly that **"GLM-5.3 uses the same base model as GLM-5.2 — every gain comes from post-training"**, and the gains are large: a claimed 50% improvement on an in-house code benchmark, open-source SOTA on Terminal-Bench 3.0 and Agents' Last Exam, and state of the art on CyberGym. Three findings make this model exceptional. **GDPval-AA v2 at 1,769 Elo clears the methodology's ~1750+ frontier reference and is the highest GDPval figure recorded anywhere in this research pass** — above Claude Fable 5 (1,743), Qwen3.8-Max (1,739) and GPT-5.6 Sol (1,730). **SWE-bench at 95.4% from Vals AI is the second-highest independently measured coding result in this pass.** And **the AA-Omniscience Index is +14.3% on a 29.6% hallucination rate — the only positive reliability index, and by a wide margin the lowest confabulation rate, found anywhere in this research pass.** The one hard limitation is categorical: **it is text-only.**
- **Provider / access:** Weights public on Hugging Face under the `glm-5.3` licence — **1,489,168 downloads in the last month**, 8 finetunes and 72 community quantizations. First-party deployment support for **SGLang, vLLM, TokenSpeed, Transformers, KTransformers and Unsloth**, plus **Ascend NPU** via vLLM-Ascend, xLLM and SGLang. Also hosted (Novita and 4+ other inference providers). Technical report: arXiv **2602.15763**, "GLM-5: from Vibe Coding to Agentic Engineering", published **2026-02-17**.
- **Release / knowledge:** Paper published **2026-02-17**; HF collection updated Aug 27. **No verified knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `zai-org/GLM-5.3`. Self-hostable, so effectively free-tier if you own the hardware.
- **Context window:** **1,000,000 tokens.** Z.AI's own evaluation footnotes confirm the window is real and used: NL2Repo, FrontierSWE, PostTrainBench and SWE-Marathon were all run **"under 1M context"**, Agents' Last Exam at **1M context with 64K max output**, DeepSWE at **400K context**, Terminal-Bench 3.0 at **400K context with 128K maximum output**, and HLE-with-tools at **300K context with a context-management strategy and a 163,840-token generation cap**. **AA-LCR at 79.7%.** Being open weights, there is no vendor repricing threshold — you set the window.
- **Modalities:** **Text in → text out. Text-only.** The Hugging Face card carries `pipeline_tag: text-generation` with languages English and Chinese, the architecture tag is `glm_moe_dsa` with no vision tower, and Z.AI ships a **separate** vision model (GLM-5V-Turbo) in the same family. **Note the family inversion: GLM-5.3-Flash has verified video input (MMVU 80.5%) and strong chart reasoning (CharXiv 89.4%), while this flagship has neither.** If your workload touches images, video or documents, the Flash tier is the more capable model despite being the weaker one overall.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **Weights are free under the `glm-5.3` licence.** **No verified Z.AI API rate card was found** for the hosted endpoint — treat the hosted option as unpriced here. Self-hosting cost is driven by size: **753B parameters**, served as BF16 / F8_E4M3 / F32. Throughput reference: Z.AI's own ExploitGym footnote cites Artificial Analysis TPS figures and rescales **GLM-5.3 at 115 TPS**, against **Kimi K3 at 40 TPS** and **Qwen3.8-Max at 47 TPS** — roughly 2.9× and 2.4× faster respectively.
- **Architecture:** **Open weights, 753B parameters**, MoE with DSA (`glm_moe_dsa`). Same base model as GLM-5.2; all improvement is post-training. Tensor types BF16, F8_E4M3, F32.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 90/100.** The strongest agentic evidence base in this research pass. **GDPval-AA v2 at 1,769 Elo clears the ~1750+ frontier reference — the only model in this pass to do so on the raw Elo scale — and beats all seven comparators in Z.AI's own table including Claude Fable 5, Qwen3.8-Max and GPT-5.6 Sol.** The **AA Agentic Index of 53.4% is the highest recorded here**, **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 88.2% sits on the ~88% anchor**, **Terminal-Bench 3.0 at 28.3% is the highest TB3 figure in this pass**, **AA Tau3 Banking at 50.3% clears the Tau-family reference**, and **CyberGym at 84.5% beats every comparator**. Deductions prevent a higher score: **the Terminal-Bench vendor-to-Vals spread is 16.7 points (88.2% against 71.5%)**, the widest in this pass; **exploitation collapses — ExploitGym solves 130 of 869 tasks (15.0%) and ExploitBench reaches 54.4% against Claude Fable 5's 78.0%**, so the "emergent cyber capability" claim holds for discovery but not exploitation; **GDP.pdf at 11.2% is near-floor**; **Claw-Eval, OSWorld, MCP-Atlas, BrowseComp and τ²-bench are all unreported**; and **every vendor figure was produced in Claude Code at max reasoning effort with 6–10 hour per-task budgets**.
- **Reasoning: 86/100.** Two frontier references are cleared and one result is unique in this dataset. **AA-GPQA Diamond at 91.7% clears the 90%+ reference** (independently 88.1% at Vals), **AA-HLE at 42.3% clears the 40%+ reference**, **AA-LCR reaches 79.7%**, and **HLE-with-tools reaches 62.5%**. The standout is reliability: **the AA-Omniscience Index of +14.3% on a 29.6% hallucination rate is the only positive index and by far the lowest confabulation rate found anywhere in this research pass**, where comparators run from 68.5% to 96.5% with negative indices — this model abstains instead of inventing. Four deductions: the **Intelligence Index of 44.8% is 15.2 points below the 60+ marker**; **raw Omniscience accuracy is only 33.9%**, so the excellent calibration comes with narrow knowledge breadth; **CritPt at 19.1% and MLCR-AA at 48.3% are mid-band**; and **neither ARC-AGI nor FrontierMath has any published figure**, leaving abstract reasoning and research mathematics wholly unmeasured. A competitor-reported Gray Swan IPI of 31.5% also suggests weak prompt-injection resistance.
- **Context window: 95/100.** A **1,000,000-token window** places it in the ≥1M tier (95–100), and this is better-evidenced than most: **Z.AI's own footnotes confirm NL2Repo, FrontierSWE, PostTrainBench, SWE-Marathon and Agents' Last Exam were all evaluated at 1M context**, with DeepSWE and Terminal-Bench 3.0 at 400K and HLE-with-tools at 300K under explicit context management, and **AA-LCR at 79.7%** corroborates usable long-context reasoning. Being open weights, **there is no vendor repricing threshold** — the full window is yours to configure, with 128K–163,840-token generation caps demonstrated. It sits at the tier floor rather than above it because the 100 condition requires **≥98% retrieval at 512K+** and **no MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks figure exists**, and because **MLCR-AA at 48.3% is materially weaker than AA-LCR**, indicating the long-context strength is uneven across harnesses.
- **Multimodal: 18/100.** **This model is text-only**, which caps it in the methodology's "text-only = 10–20" band regardless of any other merit: the Hugging Face card is tagged `pipeline_tag: text-generation`, the `glm_moe_dsa` architecture has no vision tower, Z.AI ships vision capability as a **separate** model (GLM-5V-Turbo), and **no MMMU, CharXiv, document, OCR, video or audio benchmark exists for it**. The corroborating negative is **GDP.pdf at 11.2%** — document-grounded work is near-floor. It is placed near the top of the band because **Design Arena Website at 1,308 Elo** shows it generates high-quality visual artefacts *through code*, which is the only visual capability it has. **The family inversion deserves emphasis: GLM-5.3-Flash has verified video input (MMVU 80.5%) and strong chart reasoning (CharXiv 89.4%), so for any image, video or document workload the weaker Flash model is the correct choice over this flagship.**
- **Coding: 90/100.** The best open-weight coding evidence in this research pass, and the strongest figures are independent rather than vendor-supplied. **SWE-bench at 95.4% from Vals AI is the second-highest result recorded anywhere in this pass**, 0.2 points behind Grok 4.6; **the AA Coding Index at 74.8% clears the 70%+ frontier reference and is the highest recorded here**; **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 88.2% clears the 85%+ coding reference**; and three further independent measurements cluster at 78–80% (**LiveCodeBench 80.5%, VulcanBench v3 78.3%, Proximal's FrontierSWE 78.1%**), with **AA-SciCode at 59.0% clearing its reference** too. Four deductions: **DeepSWE at 66.9% is 7.1 points below the 74%+ reference** and behind GPT-5.6 Sol, Claude Fable 5 and Kimi K3; **FrontierSWE falls 47.9 points from v1 (78.1%) to v2 (30.2%)**; **ProgramBench reaches only 19.0% against Claude Fable 5's 33.0%**, so genuinely novel program synthesis remains weak; and **SWE-bench Verified, SWE-bench Pro and CursorBench are unreported**. Z.AI's "most capable open-weights model for coding" claim and its 50% in-house gain are plausible on this evidence but not independently verifiable.
- **Cost efficiency: 88/100.** **The weights are public under the `glm-5.3` licence**, so the per-token rate is escapable entirely, and the ecosystem is genuinely production-ready rather than nominal: **1,489,168 downloads last month**, 72 community quantizations, first-party support for SGLang, vLLM, TokenSpeed, Transformers, KTransformers and Unsloth, plus **Ascend NPU** deployment. Throughput is competitive, not just the licence — **Artificial Analysis measures it at 115 TPS against Kimi K3's 40 and Qwen3.8-Max's 47**. For zero per-token cost you get **the highest GDPval Elo in this pass (1,769, above the frontier reference)**, **SWE-bench 95.4%**, **the highest AA Coding Index (74.8%)**, **GPQA 91.7%**, **HLE 42.3%** and **the only positive reliability index found here** — frontier-proprietary results on owned hardware. Three deductions: **753B parameters is real capital expenditure** (FP8 weights alone are roughly 750 GB, so multi-node or 8×H200-class hardware before the first token); **no Z.AI API rate card was verified**, so the hosted path is unpriced in this assessment; and **the headline figures assume max reasoning effort with 6–10 hour per-task budgets**, which is a substantial hidden compute bill the licence does not cover.
- **Overall Score: 75.8/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (90 + 86 + 95 + 18 + 90) / 5 = 75.8 — and **the mean badly understates this model for text work, because a single categorical limitation costs it roughly ten points of Overall.** On its own terrain it is the best open-weight model in this research pass and competitive with any proprietary flagship: **GDPval-AA 1,769 Elo clears the frontier reference and beats Claude Fable 5, Qwen3.8-Max and GPT-5.6 Sol**; **SWE-bench 95.4% (Vals) is second-highest in this pass**; the **AA Coding Index (74.8%) and AA Agentic Index (53.4%) are both the highest recorded here**; **GPQA 91.7% and HLE 42.3% clear both reasoning references**; **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 88.2% and 3.0 at 28.3% are at or above anchor**; **CyberGym 84.5% beats all seven of Z.AI's comparators**; and **the AA-Omniscience Index of +14.3% on a 29.6% hallucination rate is the single most trustworthy reliability profile in this entire research pass** — it abstains where other models confabulate. Choose it for **agentic engineering, repository-scale coding, vulnerability discovery, long-context text work and any task where being wrong is worse than being unhelpful**, and run it on your own hardware. Four cautions govern adoption. **It is text-only** — no image, video, document or audio input, GDP.pdf at 11.2%, and the weaker GLM-5.3-Flash is strictly better for multimodal work. **Exploitation lags discovery badly** — 130 of 869 ExploitGym tasks, ExploitBench 54.4% against Claude Fable 5's 78.0%. **The headline Terminal-Bench figure carries a 16.7-point independent discount** (88.2% vendor against 71.5% at Vals) and every vendor number assumes max effort with 6–10 hour task budgets. And **knowledge breadth is narrow** — 33.9% raw Omniscience accuracy, a 44.8% Intelligence Index, and no ARC-AGI or FrontierMath measurement at all.
