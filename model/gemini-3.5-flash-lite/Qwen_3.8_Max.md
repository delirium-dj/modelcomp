## Model card

- **Name:** Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite (Artificial Analysis styles it **"Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite"**; the reasoning variant is the one benchmarked — a non-reasoning variant may also exist).
- **Short description:** **Google's July-2026 budget / latency-floor tier — "our fastest, most cost-effective 3.5-class model," released 2026-07-21 alongside Gemini 3.6 Flash and 3.5 Flash Cyber and "designed for both low-latency tasks and tasks where high throughput is critical."** It is **current, not deprecated**. Its genuine strengths are **speed (413.8 tok/s, #5/151 — the fastest class), a full 1M-token context, broad text/image/audio/video input, conciseness (59M Index tokens vs a 100M median) and a free lane on AI Studio / OpenCode Zen**. But it is a **LITE** model: its **AA Intelligence Index v4.3.2 = 22 (#44/173)** is "well above average" *for its price tier* (median 13) yet **low in absolute terms** — roughly half of GPT-6 Luna (38) or Claude Haiku 5.5 (43) — and its own scaffolded tradeoff is that "**the lite tuning gives up reasoning depth... it lives on volume, not difficulty.**" The independent **Vals SWE-bench Verified row is 86 / 73 / 40 / 33** (notably **no >4h long-horizon collapse**) and **GPQA Diamond 83.8%** is a real bright spot. ⚠ Two meta issues: the short's "**SWE-bench 75%**" is **unconfirmed** — Google's model card reports **SWE-bench *Pro* 54.2%** (a harder benchmark) and the only independent SWE-bench-Verified evidence is the banded Vals row; and the meta's modality list **omits VIDEO** (AA confirms text, image, speech **and video** input).
- **Provider / access:** **Google** (Google DeepMind) — first-party **Gemini API**, plus **Google AI Studio** and **OpenCode Zen** (both with a **free tier**), and **4 API providers** total per AA. **Proprietary**; parameter count undisclosed by Google. Listed here as `google/gemini-3.5-flash-lite`.
- **Release / knowledge:** **2026-07-21** (current; ~3 months old at time of writing). Announced on the Google blog the same day as Gemini 3.6 Flash and 3.5 Flash Cyber. Knowledge cutoff not surfaced in this research.
- **IDs:** `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` / `gemini-3-5-flash-lite` (AA slug) / `google/gemini-3.5-flash-lite` (this dataset).
- **Context window:** **1,048,576 (1M) tokens** — AA confirms "**1M, ~1500 A4 pages**," matching the meta exactly. ⚠ Long-context *retrieval* degrades at the extreme: Google's own **GDM-MRCR** 8-needle accuracy is **72.2% at 128k but only 21.3% at the full 1M pointwise** — the window is genuinely 1M, but multi-needle recall near the ceiling is weak. Max output tokens not surfaced.
- **Modalities:** **Text + image + speech (audio) + video in → text out.** ⚠ **Corrected/enriched from the meta**, which lists "Text, image, audio, PDF in" and **omits video** — AA explicitly confirms "**text, image, speech, and video input, outputs text**" ("Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite is multimodal"). PDF is a document format handled through the text/image path. **Reasoning: yes** (extended thinking / chain-of-thought; TTFT 7.85s reflects thinking time).
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-25):** **$0.30 input / $2.50 output per 1M** (cache discount 90%; blended 7:2:1 = **$0.33/1M**), confirmed by AA and a third-party pricing analysis. **Free tier** on Google AI Studio and OpenCode Zen. AA rates input "**somewhat expensive**" (class median $0.24) and output "**expensive**" (median $0.80) — i.e. it is *not* the cheapest in its band — but the **absolute cost is low** and it costs just **$0.19 per Intelligence Index task** (#45/66).
- **Architecture:** **Proprietary; parameter count undisclosed.** A **lite / distilled** member of the Gemini 3.5 family tuned for **throughput and low latency** rather than peak difficulty — the lightest 1M-context tier in Google's lineup. Signature trait: **speed** (413.8 tok/s, #5/151) paired with **conciseness** (59M Index output tokens vs a 100M median), making it a volume/high-throughput workhorse.

### Raw benchmarks found

Flash-Lite is a **current budget tier**, so there is no launch-vs-rebased gap. The story is a **low absolute Intelligence Index (22) offset by best-in-class speed, a full 1M window, broad multimodality, a free lane, and a genuinely solid independent Vals SWE-bench row for a lite model.** I separate independent from vendor evidence.

**Independent — Artificial Analysis (Intelligence Index v4.3.2, current):**

- **AA Intelligence Index = 22 (#44/173)** — "above average among comparable models" and "**well above average among other models in a similar price tier** (median: 13)"; 3 of 4 Intelligence units. In absolute terms this is **low** — about half of GPT-6 Luna (38) and Claude Haiku 5.5 (43), and far below the frontier.
- **Speed 413.8 tok/s (#5/151)** — "notably fast," 4 of 4 Speed units, vs a class median of 116.2. **TTFT 7.85s** ("at the higher end," median 1.70s) because of reasoning thinking time.
- **Cost** $0.30 in / $2.50 out per 1M (in "somewhat expensive" vs $0.24 median; out "expensive" vs $0.80 median), cache discount 90%, **$0.19 per Index task (#45/66)**, 3 of 4 Cost units. **Verbosity 59M output tokens (#25/69)** — "fairly concise" vs a 100M median.
- **AA-measured evaluations: GPQA Diamond 83.8%** (graduate-level science — a genuine bright spot) · **Humanity's Last Exam 17.5%** · **SciCode 40.9%**.
- **Context 1M (~1500 A4 pages); modalities text/image/speech/video in → text out; reasoning yes; 4 API providers; released July 21, 2026; proprietary, params undisclosed.**

**Independent — Vals AI SWE-bench Verified (bash-tool-only, mini-swe-agent, banded):**

- **86 / 73 / 40 / 33** across **<15min / 15m–1h / 1–4h / >4h** — strong short/mid-horizon resolve rates and, notably, **no long-horizon collapse** (33% on >4h tasks, where most Flash/lite-tier models fall to 0%). For reference the **full Gemini 3.5 Flash scores 87 / 78 / 52 / 33** — Flash-Lite trails its bigger sibling by only a few points on the short bands, which is a **strong result for a lite model** and the single best independent coding anchor here.

**Vendor — Google model card (deepmind.google, 2026-07-21):**

- **SWE-bench Pro 54.2%** resolve · **Terminal-Bench 54%** (Terminus 2, v2.1) · **MLE-Bench 39.2** avg position · **GDPval-AA 1140 Elo** (v2) · **OSWorld-Verified 74%** success · **CharXiv Reasoning 76.5** (with tools) / **74.5** (no tools) · **GDM-MRCR 72.2** (128k avg, 8-needle) / **21.3** (1M pointwise, 8-needle).

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 74/100.** Agentic evidence is **vendor-only** and mid-tier for the class: **Terminal-Bench 54%** (Terminus 2, v2.1), **OSWorld-Verified 74%**, **GDPval-AA 1140 Elo**, **MLE-Bench 39.2** — respectable numbers, but all from Google's own model card, and the **AA Intelligence Index (22)** reflects a weak agentic/reasoning basket overall. The independent **Vals >4h band (33%)** shows it does *not* collapse on long-horizon work, which is a plus for a lite model. Held to **74** — below the mid-80 band (Tool 80) given the lite tuning, vendor-only sourcing, and low absolute Index; above a text-only or no-agentic-evidence model.
- **Reasoning: 75/100.** **AA Intelligence Index v4.3.2 = 22 (#44/173)** is "well above average" *for its price tier* (median 13) but **low in absolute terms** — about half of GPT-6 Luna (38) / Claude Haiku 5.5 (43). **GPQA Diamond 83.8%** is a genuine bright spot (graduate-level science near some frontier models), but **HLE 17.5%** and **SciCode 40.9%** are weak, and it *is* a reasoning model (extended thinking). Scored **75** — the meta's own tradeoff ("lite tuning gives up reasoning depth") is borne out; GPQA keeps it from falling further.
- **Context window: 84/100.** A **true 1M-token window** (AA: "~1500 A4 pages"), matching the meta's 1,048,576 exactly — the top tier alongside Claude Haiku 5.5 (1M) and GPT-6 Luna (1.05M). Held to **84** (just under the 85–86 1M-proven band) because Google's own **GDM-MRCR** shows long-context *retrieval* degrades sharply at the ceiling (**72.2% @128k → 21.3% @1M pointwise, 8-needle**) and max-output tokens were not surfaced — so the window is real but multi-needle recall near 1M is weak.
- **Multimodal: 83/100.** **Text + image + speech (audio) + video in → text out** — four input modalities (video **corrected into** the meta, which omitted it), among the broadest in the dataset, with a **verified visual-reasoning score (CharXiv 76.5 with tools / 74.5 without)**. Scored **83** — above the image-only band (77–78) and the native-video band (82) for the audio+video+chart-reasoning breadth, tempered because it is a lite model whose visual *depth* likely trails the full Flash/Pro line.
- **Coding: 77/100.** The **independent Vals SWE-bench Verified row (86 / 73 / 40 / 33)** is the key anchor — **strong short/mid-horizon and no >4h collapse**, only a few points behind the full Gemini 3.5 Flash (87/78/52/33), which is impressive for a lite tier. But harder coding is weaker: **SWE-bench Pro 54.2%** and **SciCode 40.9%** (vendor/AA). Scored **77** — the mid band: the Vals row lifts it, while the Pro/SciCode depth and the meta's unconfirmed "SWE-bench 75%" keep it below the strong-published 79 tier.
- **Cost efficiency: 88/100.** **$0.30/$2.50 per 1M**, blended **$0.33**, just **$0.19 per Index task (#45/66)**, a **free tier** on AI Studio/Zen, **413.8 tok/s (#5/151)** and **concise output (59M tokens)** — Google's "most cost-effective 3.5-class model." AA does flag input "somewhat expensive" ($0.24 median) and output "expensive" ($0.80 median) *within its class*, so it is not the absolute cheapest, but the free lane + speed + conciseness + low absolute per-token cost make it **strong value**. Scored **88** — below the Pareto-frontier/open-weight 90–95 band, above plain cheap-hosted (84). (Not counted in Overall.)
- **Overall Score: 79/100.** Mean of the five quality dimensions = (74 Tool + 75 Reasoning + 84 Context + 83 Multimodal + 77 Coding) / 5 = **393/5 = 78.6 → 79** (Cost efficiency 88 is scored but **not** counted, per the v4 formula). **Divergence note:** my independent read is **79 vs the queue's curated 82.7 → −3.7**, in line with the other vendor-heavy / lower-absolute-intelligence models this pass (qwen-3.5-397b −4, claude-opus-4.5 −3.8, gpt-6-luna −3.8). The curated **82.7** rightly rewards Flash-Lite's **excellent speed, full 1M context, broad multimodality, free tier and solid Vals SWE-bench row**; my **79** additionally weights its **low absolute AA Intelligence Index (22)** and the **lite reasoning/coding depth** that its own tradeoff concedes ("lives on volume, not difficulty"). Unlike the deprecated flagships, it is **current** — so there is no deprecation penalty, and the gap is purely "great budget workhorse, modest absolute intelligence." Best fit today: **high-throughput, low-latency volume work** — agentic search, document processing, classification/extraction, and large-fan-out pipelines where a **free/cheap 1M-context multimodal model at 414 tok/s** matters more than frontier reasoning.
