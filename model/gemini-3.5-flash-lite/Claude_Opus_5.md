## Model card

- **Name:** Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite. Proprietary, Reasoning, `base` variant. Related earlier model: **Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite**.
- **Short description:** Google's **ultra-low-latency** tier — the cheapest and fastest Gemini — placed **#88 of 882 at 50.75/100** by a composite tracker across 24 benchmarks. Its profile is unusual for a Lite model and the finding is specific: **although its absolute capability is modest, its reliability profile is better than most frontier models assessed in this research pass.** **AA-Omniscience Index at +5.2% on a 34.4% hallucination rate** is the second-best confabulation figure found anywhere here, behind only Grok 4.3's 25.0%, and far better than GPT-5.4 (91.7%), GPT-5.6 Luna (92.6%) or DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (96.5%). It also posts **OSWorld-Verified at 74%, essentially level with GPT-5.4's 75%**, and **AA-MMMU-Pro at 79.0%**, which beats several far larger models. The counterweights are sharp: **CritPt at 0.0%**, **AA-HLE at 18.8%**, an **AA Agentic Index of 15.9%**, and **GDPval-AA at 1,139 Elo — 611 below reference**.
- **Provider / access:** **A free tier is available on Google AI Studio and OpenCode Zen with standard rate limits** (curated metadata), plus paid-tier pricing. Google published a joint announcement covering "Gemini 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, and 3.5 Flash Cyber", which supplies five rows.
- **Release / knowledge:** **No verified release date or knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `google/gemini-3.5-flash-lite`. **A free-tier ID exists**, which is unusual among the models assessed in this pass.
- **Context window:** **1,048,576 tokens** (curated metadata; tracker records 1M). Unusually for a Lite tier, retrieval is measured at length: **MRCRv2 at 72.2%** is a direct million-token retrieval result — lower than Qwen3.7 Max's 90.4% but a genuine measurement — with **AA-LCR at 76.0%** corroborating.
- **Modalities:** **Text, image, audio and PDF input → text output** (curated metadata). **Audio input would place it in the methodology's top band**, and **AA-MMMU-Pro at 79.0%** is solid measured evidence for the vision path. But **no audio benchmark of any kind exists**, which is the reason for the placement below.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **A free tier on Google AI Studio and OpenCode Zen with standard rate limits**, plus paid-tier pricing whose exact figures were not verified. **No specific paid rate card was reached**, so cost efficiency below rests on the free tier plus tier positioning. **Re-verify before budgeting.**
- **Architecture:** Proprietary, closed weights. Parameter count and topology: not disclosed.

### Raw benchmarks found

Composite: **50.75/100, #88 of 882**, flagged conservative on partial coverage.

Agent / tool use:

- **GDPval-AA: 1,139 Elo (G) / 24.3% normalized (AA)** — **611 Elo below the methodology's ~1750+ reference**, among the lowest in this research pass, above only Gemini 2.5 Pro (616) and Grok 4.3 (1,018).
- **AA Agentic Index: 15.9% (AA)** — **very low**, below Grok 4.3's 17.2% and roughly a third of DeepSeek V4 Pro's 49.6%.
- **No Claw-Eval, MCP-Atlas, τ²-bench, Toolathlon, CyberGym, BrowseComp or AutomationBench**, and no Terminal-Bench 3.0 or 4.0. **Ten agentic references absent**, including Claw-Eval, whose absence the methodology explicitly penalises.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 62/100.** One result is genuinely competitive and several are weak. **OSWorld-Verified at 74% is essentially level with GPT-5.4's 75%** — remarkable for an ultra-low-latency tier and the strongest thing here, showing real computer-use capability. Everything else falls short. **Terminal-Bench 2.1 sits 34 points below the ~88% anchor on the vendor figure and 37.8 below on the independent one**, with no 3.0 or 4.0 measurement. **GDPval-AA at 1,139 Elo is 611 below the ~1750+ reference**, and **the AA Agentic Index of 15.9% is very low**, so both composite measures of real-world agentic value agree the model is weak there. And **ten agentic reference benchmarks are entirely absent**, including Claw-Eval, whose absence the methodology explicitly penalises. The score credits a substantial OSWorld result without letting it stand for general agentic capability.
- **Reasoning: 52/100.** Weak on the frontier references but with one genuinely notable reliability property. **The AA-Omniscience Index is positive at +5.2% on a 34.4% hallucination rate — the second-lowest confabulation rate found anywhere in this research pass**, behind only Grok 4.3's 25.0%, and dramatically better than GPT-5.4 (91.7%), GPT-5.6 Luna (92.6%) or DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (96.5%). For a Lite tier, that is a meaningful property: on tasks where the model does not know the answer, it is more likely than most frontier models to say so. **MMLU-Pro at 85.8%** is solid. Four deductions dominate. **AA-HLE at 18.8% misses the 40%+ reference by 21.2 points** — a very large shortfall. **CritPt at 0.0% is a floor result.** **GPQA Diamond misses the 90%+ reference by 6.2 points on both vendor and independent measurement**, so the miss is not a measurement artefact. And **the Intelligence Index of 22.2% sits in the mid band, 37.8 points below the 60+ marker**, with **no ARC-AGI and no FrontierMath figure at all**. The reliability strength is real and worth stating, but the calibrated knowledge base is narrow at 29.5% accuracy, so it should not be mistaken for broad competence.
- **Context window: 92/100.** A **1,048,576-token window** places it in the ≥1M tier, and unusually for a Lite model **the depth claim is measured rather than asserted**: **MRCRv2 at 72.2% is a direct million-token retrieval result**, with **AA-LCR at 76.0%** corroborating. It is scored below the tier floor of 95 for three reasons: the 100 condition requires **≥98% retrieval at 512K+** and 72.2% is well short; **MRCRv2 at 72.2% is materially below Qwen3.7 Max's 90.4%** on the same benchmark; and **no RULER, CorpusQA or GraphWalks figure exists**. The score nonetheless reflects that the window is real and usable, which for a low-latency tier is a genuine differentiator — many models at this price point cap context far lower.
- **Multimodal: 88/100.** **Audio and PDF input, per this repository's curated metadata, would place it in the methodology's top band ("+audio or non-text out = 90–100")**, and the measured vision evidence supports real capability: **AA-MMMU-Pro at 79.0% beats GPT-5.6 Luna (78.6%), GPT-5.4 (78.4%) and Claude Sonnet 4.6 (70.6%)** — all substantially larger models. It is scored **just below the band floor of 90, deliberately and consistently with how I treated Inkling-Small earlier in this research pass**: the audio capability rests on curated metadata with **no audio benchmark of any kind to corroborate it**, so awarding the full band floor would credit an unverified claim. Unlike GLM-5.3 or Qwen3.7 Max, however, this is not a text-only model — image input is measured and strong — so it sits near the top of the range rather than at its base. **No CharXiv, OCR, document or video benchmark exists**, and **no video input path is documented**, so the top band is unavailable regardless.
- **Coding: 68/100.** Mid-field, with the independent evidence better than the composite measures. **SWE-bench at 75.0% from Vals AI is genuinely solid** for a Lite tier, and **LiveCodeBench at 79.0%**, also from Vals, is respectable. Four deductions. **The AA Coding Index at 49.3% is 20.7 points below the 70%+ reference**, the largest shortfall in this dimension. **AA-SciCode at 41.3% misses the 55%+ reference by 13.7 points.** **SWE-bench Pro at 54.2% and Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 54.0%** are both mid-band, the latter sitting 31 points below the 85%+ coding reference. And **DeepSWE, CursorBench, Codeforces, ProgramBench and NL2Repo are all unreported**, so five coding references cannot be checked. The score credits the two independent Vals results properly while reflecting that the composite measures place it well below reference.
- **Cost efficiency: 90/100.** **A free tier exists on both Google AI Studio and OpenCode Zen with standard rate limits** — which is unusual among the models assessed in this research pass and is the primary basis for the score. For zero cost you get **a full 1,048,576-token window with MRCRv2 at 72.2% measured at length**, **AA-MMMU-Pro at 79.0%**, **OSWorld-Verified at 74%**, **SWE-bench at 75.0% and LiveCodeBench at 79.0% independently measured**, and — most valuably for a cheap tier — **the second-lowest hallucination rate in this research pass at 34.4%, on a positive Omniscience Index**. That last point matters disproportionately for high-volume work, where a model that abstains instead of inventing reduces downstream correction cost. The **ultra-low-latency positioning** is also a real operational benefit for interactive and high-throughput use. Three deductions: **the paid rate card was not verified**, so the cost case beyond the free tier is unquantified; **free tiers carry standard rate limits**, which constrain production volume; and **the model is superseded within its own family**, with Gemini 3.8 Flash (73.06) and Gemini 3.6 Flash (63.22) both scoring above its 50.75 while Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite… while no direct successor Lite tier is listed, so the comparison is against higher tiers. Even discounted, free frontier-adjacent access with a strong reliability profile is a strong offering.
- **Overall Score: 72.0/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (62 + 52 + 92 + 88 + 68) / 5 = 72.0 — **a modest absolute performer with an unusual distinction and a genuinely free entry point.** The distinction is worth stating plainly because it is counter-intuitive for a Lite tier: **the AA-Omniscience Index is positive at +5.2% on a 34.4% hallucination rate, the second-best confabulation profile found anywhere in this research pass**, better than GPT-5.4, GPT-5.6 Luna and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash by enormous margins. For high-volume work where a wrong confident answer costs more than an abstention, that is a real advantage over far more capable and far more expensive models. It also holds up better than its tier suggests on several measured axes: **AA-MMMU-Pro at 79.0% beats GPT-5.6 Luna and GPT-5.4**, **OSWorld-Verified at 74% is essentially level with GPT-5.4's 75%**, **SWE-bench at 75.0% and LiveCodeBench at 79.0% are independently measured and solid**, and **MRCRv2 at 72.2% measures usable depth across a full 1,048,576-token window**. And it is **free on Google AI Studio and OpenCode Zen**, with ultra-low latency. Choose it for **high-volume classification, extraction and routing**, **cheap multimodal triage**, **interactive low-latency use**, and **long-context work where abstention is preferable to invention.** Four cautions. **Frontier reasoning is genuinely weak** — AA-HLE misses 40% by 21.2 points, CritPt is 0.0%, GPQA misses 90% by 6.2 points, the Intelligence Index is 22.2%, and ARC-AGI and FrontierMath are entirely unmeasured. **Agentic value is low on the composites** — GDPval-AA 1,139 Elo (611 below reference) and an AA Agentic Index of 15.9% — even though OSWorld-Verified looks strong, and ten agentic references are absent. **Coding misses every composite reference** — the AA Coding Index by 20.7 points, AA-SciCode by 13.7 — despite the decent independent SWE-bench and LiveCodeBench results. And **the audio input claim is uncorroborated**, with no audio benchmark of any kind existing despite the metadata listing it.
