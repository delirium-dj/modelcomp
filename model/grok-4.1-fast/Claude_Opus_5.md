## Model card

- **Name:** Grok 4.1 Fast. Proprietary. **Variant/alias flag — this matters more than usual:** this is the **base, "Non-Reasoning"** configuration. A **separate `grok-4.1-fast-reasoning` sibling exists on the same rate card** and scores materially higher on the same composite tracker (**46.25 against this model's 36.4**). Several pricing aggregators conflate the two under one page. Every figure below belongs to the Non-Reasoning base unless stated.
- **Short description:** xAI's cheap high-throughput tier, released **2025-11-19**. OpenRouter markets it as **"xAI's best agentic tool calling model… customer support and deep research."** The measured evidence does not support that positioning: a composite tracker places it **#142 of 784 at 36.4/100** on only **12 of 649 benchmarks**, and the figures that do exist are weak in a consistent pattern — **an Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index of 11.3%**, **GPQA Diamond at 63.7%**, **HLE at 5.1%**, **CritPt at 0.0%**, **AA-LCR at 31.3% despite a multi-million-token window**, and **an AA-Omniscience Index of −50.9% built on 17.2% accuracy against an 82.3% hallucination rate**. **There is not a single coding benchmark published for it.** Note for reconciliation: this repository's `average.md` records an Overall of 87.1 for this model; the independent evidence gathered here supports nothing close to that, and the tracker's own 36.4/100 at #142 corroborates the lower assessment.
- **Provider / access:** xAI API, OpenRouter, and resellers. Independent benchmarks routed through Artificial Analysis.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released **2025-11-19** (pricepertoken aggregator). **No verified knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `grok-4.1-fast`; sibling `grok-4.1-fast-reasoning`. **No free-tier ID verified**, though one aggregator lists $0.0000/$0.0000, which almost certainly reflects a promotional free window rather than the standing rate.
- **Context window:** **Disputed. Four independent aggregators (OpenRouter, openmark.ai, aicomp.prygn.com, llmcloudhub, cloudprice.net, pricepertoken.com) all state 2,000,000 tokens; the benchmark tracker states 1M.** I could not reach xAI's own model documentation — `https://docs.x.ai/docs/models/grok-4-1-fast` returns 404 — so the figure is recorded as **2M per aggregator consensus, with the tracker's 1M noted as a conflict**. Either way the window is large and, critically, **demonstrably not usable at depth: AA-LCR is 31.3%**. Max output: no verified figure found.
- **Modalities:** **Text and image input → text output.** Image input is confirmed by a measured **AA-MMMU-Pro of 48.4%**. No audio, video or PDF path found; no non-text output.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **$0.20 / 1M input, $0.50 / 1M output** — agreed by three aggregators (openmark.ai, aicomp.prygn.com, gptproto.com). **Two conflicting figures were also found and are recorded rather than discarded: cloudprice.net states "from $5.00 per 1M input" and pricepertoken.com states $0.0000/$0.0000.** The $0.20/$0.50 consensus is the most likely standing rate; **no primary xAI pricing page was reached, so re-verify before budgeting.** The same rate card covers the stronger Reasoning sibling.
- **Architecture:** Proprietary, closed weights. Parameter count and topology: not disclosed.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 52/100.** One result deserves credit: **τ²-bench at 63.7% clears the methodology's Tau-family ~50%+ reference**, which is real evidence of structured tool-calling competence and is consistent with xAI positioning this model for customer support. **Gert Labs at 47.32%** adds a second independent datapoint. But the score cannot go higher because **ten of the methodology's agentic reference benchmarks are simply unreported** — Terminal-Bench at every version, GDPval-AA, OSWorld, Claw-Eval, BrowseComp, Toolathlon, MCP-Atlas, CyberGym, ApprenticeBench and the AA Agentic Index. The methodology explicitly penalises a missing Claw-Eval; here almost the entire agentic battery is missing. A vendor claim of **"best agentic tool calling model"** resting on two measurements is marketing, not evidence, and is scored as such.
- **Reasoning: 26/100.** Weak on every axis that was measured, with no measurement at all on the axes that might have rescued it. **The Intelligence Index of 11.3% falls below the methodology's mid band entirely**; **GPQA Diamond at 63.7% misses the 90%+ reference by 26.3 points**; **AA-HLE at 5.1% misses the 40%+ reference by 34.9 points**; **CritPt is 0.0%**; and **AA-IFBench at 36.5%** indicates it also struggles to follow complex instructions. The reliability profile is the worst found in this research pass: **an AA-Omniscience Index of −50.9% on 17.2% accuracy and an 82.3% hallucination rate** — the low accuracy is what distinguishes this from other over-confident models, because it is confabulating from a genuinely thin knowledge base rather than over-reaching from a deep one. **No ARC-AGI, FrontierMath, MMLU-Pro or SimpleQA figure exists**, so there is no abstract-reasoning, mathematics or breadth measurement to weigh against the above.
- **Context window: 72/100. This score is deliberately set far below the methodology's ≥1M tier floor of 95, and the justification is explicit.** The nominal window qualifies — four aggregators state **2,000,000 tokens** — but three findings make scoring it at the tier floor indefensible. First, **the window size is itself disputed**: the benchmark tracker records 1M against the aggregators' 2M, and **xAI's own model documentation was unreachable (404)**, so no primary source confirms either figure. Second, and decisively, **AA-LCR at 31.3% is the weakest long-context reasoning measurement in this entire research pass** — less than half of GLM-5.3's 79.7% and DeepSeek V4 Pro's 80.3%, both of which carry the same nominal 1M. A window the model cannot reason across is a specification, not a capability. Third, **there is no MRCR, RULER, CorpusQA or GraphWalks evidence**, so not even raw retrieval at depth is demonstrated — by contrast DeepSeek V4 Pro publishes MRCR 1M at 83.5%. The score therefore reflects *measured* usable depth, roughly equivalent to a well-behaved 200K–500K model, rather than the advertised headline.
- **Multimodal: 62/100.** Input is **text and image** with text-only output and no audio, video or PDF path, which fixes it in the methodology's "+image in = 60–70" band. It sits in the **lower part** of that band on measured breadth: **AA-MMMU-Pro at 48.4%** is a genuine confirmation that the vision path works, but it is mid-low — roughly 30 points behind GPT-5.6 Luna's 78.4% and Claude Sonnet 4.6's AA-MMMU-Pro of 70.6% — and **no CharXiv, document, OCR, video or audio benchmark exists**, so chart reasoning and document extraction are entirely unmeasured.
- **Coding: 25/100. This is an evidence-absence score and is flagged as such rather than presented as a measurement.** **Not one coding benchmark was found for this model** — no SWE-bench in any variant, no LiveCodeBench, no Terminal-Bench, no DeepSWE, no Codeforces, no AA Coding Index, no CursorBench. Twelve benchmark slots are populated and none of them is coding. Per the methodology, missing data is penalised rather than estimated, and the surrounding evidence gives no reason to assume competence: **an Intelligence Index of 11.3%**, a **Non-Reasoning** configuration, and **CritPt at 0.0%** are not the profile of a capable coding model. **If xAI or an independent harness publishes coding results for this ID, this dimension should be rescored first** — it is the largest single source of uncertainty in this report.
- **Cost efficiency: 55/100.** The rate card is excellent in isolation — **$0.20 / $0.50 is roughly a fifteenth of the methodology's $3/$15 anchor**, paired with a multi-million-token nominal window, and that combination is well suited to cheap high-volume classification, routing and extraction. Three findings pull the score down to the middle. **It is strictly dominated by its own sibling at the same price**: `grok-4.1-fast-reasoning` shares this rate card and scores **46.25 against 36.4** on the same composite — a 27% capability gain for zero additional per-token cost, which makes choosing the base variant hard to justify on any workload. **The reliability profile imposes a hidden verification cost** the rate card does not show: at **17.2% accuracy with an 82.3% hallucination rate and a −50.9% reliability index**, nothing it outputs can be trusted unsupervised, so human or model-based checking must be budgeted on top. And **the pricing itself is unverified and internally contradictory across sources** — $0.20/$0.50 from three aggregators, "from $5.00" from a fourth, $0.0000 from a fifth, with no primary xAI page reachable.
- **Overall Score: 47.4/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (52 + 26 + 72 + 62 + 25) / 5 = 47.4 — a cheap, fast, **thinly evidenced and demonstrably unreliable** model whose marketing substantially overstates it. **Reconciliation note: this repository's `average.md` records 87.1 for this model. Nothing in the independent evidence supports that, and the tracker's own composite of 36.4/100 at #142 of 784 corroborates the far lower assessment reached here.** What is genuinely creditable: **τ²-bench at 63.7% clears the Tau-family reference**, **AA-MMMU-Pro at 48.4% confirms a working vision path**, the **$0.20/$0.50 rate card is among the cheapest found anywhere in this pass**, and the nominal window is very large. Use it for **high-volume, low-stakes, verifiable text and image work** — classification, routing, extraction, first-pass triage — where output is checked downstream and being wrong is cheap. Do not use it for anything else, for four reasons. **It is unreliable in a specific and disqualifying way**: 17.2% accuracy, an 82.3% hallucination rate and a −50.9% reliability index, the worst found in this research pass. **Its reasoning is weak on every measured axis** — Intelligence Index 11.3%, GPQA 63.7%, HLE 5.1%, CritPt 0.0% — and unmeasured on every axis that might have redeemed it. **The large window is not usable at depth**: AA-LCR 31.3%, the weakest here, with no retrieval evidence at all. And **there is no coding evidence whatsoever**. Finally, **if you are paying this rate card, buy the Reasoning sibling instead** — same price, 27% higher composite.
