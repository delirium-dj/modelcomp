## Model card

- **Name:** MiMo-V2.6-Distill-Qwen-9B
- **Short description:** Xiaomi MiMo's 9B agentic SFT checkpoint, released alongside the MiMo-V2.6 Pro/Flash open-weight drops as a starting point for agentic reinforcement-learning research. It is a distillation of Qwen3.5-9B onto MiMo-generated data — not a MiMo MoE model, and not the same thing as MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL or MiMo-V2.6-Pro-RL.
- **Provider / access:** Open weights only — `XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Distill-Qwen-9B` on Hugging Face (mirrored on ModelScope). **No hosted inference provider serves it** (HF: "This model isn't deployed by any Inference Provider"), so it is not reachable through any API ID. Serve locally with SGLang (`--reasoning-parser mimo`), vLLM or Docker Model Runner; the checkpoint ships its own tokenizer and MiMo v2.6 chat template.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released with the MiMo-V2.6 series on 2026-09-22 UTC (HF repositories created 2026-09-21 UTC; Xiaomi's announcement dated 2026-09-22; MiMo-V2.6 technical report 2026-09-21). No knowledge cutoff published by Xiaomi.
- **Context window:** **262,144** — re-verified from the checkpoint's own `config.json` (`max_position_embeddings` / `context_length`), independently confirmed 2026-10-13 by llms.ninja (citing `config.json` directly), modelfit.io (config **and** the GGUF metadata) and aquanode.io (which derives its KV-cache table from the same config). **Do not confuse it with the 1,048,576 figure on the Pro/Flash cards** — that belongs to the MoE siblings, not this one. Qwen documents 262,144 as native for Qwen3.5-9B and extensible to ~1,010,000 with RoPE scaling; no RoPE-scaled run is documented here.
- **Modalities:** Image-Text-to-Text on the HF pipeline tag, **but the config also carries video token ids alongside a 27-layer vision tower** (modelfit.io, 2026-10) — so image input is verified and video input is *structurally present but unverified*; one GGUF converter explicitly declines to claim video behaviour. **No audio encoder** in this 9B distill, unlike the Pro/Flash siblings which keep a 308M audio tokenizer. Reasoning: yes (thinking enabled via `chat_template_kwargs: {"enable_thinking": True}`, surfaced with `--reasoning-parser mimo`). Tool use: supported (`--tool-call-parser`). Text out only.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-13):** **No API price — there is no hosted route.** Weights are MIT-licensed, so the only cost is self-hosting. Quantized footprints: BF16 ~18.8 GB weights / **21.0 GB VRAM total**; Q8_0 9.55 GB; Q6_K 7.36–7.79 GB; Q5_K_M 6.47–6.88 GB; Q4_K_M 5.63–5.84 GB; INT4 4.4 GB weights, 5.3 GB total (aquanode, llms.ninja, bartowski build tables). Measured local generation: **6.0–6.3 tok/s at Q8_0 / 4K context** on a 16 GB M-series Mac (modelfit, 2026-10); 23.1–23.9 tok/s at 4-bit `oQ4e` / 16K on M5 (10-core, 32 GB) and M4 Max (40-core, 128 GB) (OMLX community benchmark, 2026-09-23).
- **Architecture:** Dense 9B, inherited from Qwen3.5-9B — **32 blocks, 8 full-attention (every 4th) + 24 linear/Gated-DeltaNet**, 16 query heads against 4 KV heads at head dim 256, hidden 4,096, vocab 248,320, 27-layer vision tower, 1 MTP speculative layer declared in config (absent from the released safetensors — GGUFs were converted `--no-mtp`). No audio encoder. BF16, ~55 community quantizations and 11 merges exist.
- **Training data:** Weighted SFT mixture of **77.4B total tokens, 27.2B loss-bearing** — Code 23.2B (29.9%), General 22.0B (28.5%), Visual 21.2B (27.4%), Cyber 11.0B (14.2%).

### Raw benchmarks found

> Every number below is Xiaomi's own evaluation of the released **SFT checkpoint**, as published on the Hugging Face model card (re-verified verbatim 2026-10-13 against the README, its `raw` copy and the ModelScope mirror) and reproduced from the MiMo-V2.6 technical report. Baseline is Qwen3.5-9B, the fine-tune's parent. Rows marked † are Xiaomi-internal evaluation sets and are not independently reproducible. `avg@3` / `avg@1` are the vendor's aggregation over multiple attempts, not the `pass@1` convention used elsewhere in this dataset. **As of 2026-10-13 no independent lab has reproduced any row of this table** (modelfit.io: "we found no third-party reproduction of those numbers"), so the figures below are the primary evidence and the field context that follows is the only independent signal that exists.

Coding:

- SWE-bench Verified: **61.1** (avg@3) vs 60.0 base — published to the `SWE-bench/SWE-bench_Verified` leaderboard. **Independent field context (new): ranks 46/50, 8th percentile, against a field leader of 96** (BenchmarkList, 2026-10-13) — i.e. near the top of its field despite being a 9B; whatmodel.sh scores the run "beats 58/131", 8th pct, difficulty "hard", field average 23%.
- SWE-bench Pro: **44.6** (avg@3) vs 32.0 base — published to the `ScaleAI/SWE-bench_Pro` leaderboard. **Independent field context (new): ranks 55/58, 5th percentile, only 12.7 pp behind the field leader Claude Opus 4.6 Max at 57.3** (BenchmarkList). whatmodel.sh: "beats 23/55", difficulty "very hard", field average 9.6%.
  > **Conflict worth recording:** BenchmarkList's "5th pct" and whatmodel.sh's "beats 23/55" look contradictory but are not — BenchmarkList ranks from the top of a 58-model SWE-bench-Pro field, whatmodel from the bottom of a differently-composed 55-model run. Both agree the model is far above the SWE-bench-Pro average; only the *sense* of the percentile differs. Read the absolute gap to the leader, not the percentile, as the real signal.
- Terminal-Bench 2.1: **37.1** (avg@1) vs 27.0 base — published to `harborframework/terminal-bench-2.1`. **Independent field context (new): 102/194, 48th percentile — dead mid-field — 54.3 pp behind the field leader Fable 5.1 at 91.4** (BenchmarkList). whatmodel.sh: "beats 13/56", difficulty "very hard".
- MiMo Code (mini)†: **51.6** (avg@3) vs 19.5 base.
- LiveCodeBench, SciCode, OJBench, Vibe Code Bench, DeepSWE: **no verified public score found** for this checkpoint.

Tool use / agentic:

- AutomationBench v1.0.6: **30.3** (avg@1) vs 5.0 base — a 6× improvement over its parent. whatmodel.sh ranks it "beats 3/6", difficulty "very hard", field average 5.5%, and flags the run **"noisy · small run"**.
- Toolathlon-Verified: **35.2** (avg@1) vs 25.9 base. **This is the most consequential new finding and it is negative.** whatmodel.sh (2026-10-13) places 35.2 as **"beats 1/18"** — the model beats exactly one of eighteen peers on this benchmark, and is scored "blunt · at its level". The llm-stats Toolathlon leaderboard (42 models, updated 2026-10-12) puts the field average at **0.526** and the leader (GLM-5.3-Flash) at **0.784**; LLMBoard's Toolathlon-Verified board (updated 2026-10-12) lists the live leaders as Claude Opus 5.5 77.80%, Claude Sonnet 5.5 77.80%, MiMo-V2.6-Pro 76.90%, MiMo-V2.6-Flash 73.60% — and this 9B distill is **not listed at all**. Sharpest framing: **Xiaomi's own RL-trained siblings score 73.6–76.9 on the very same benchmark, more than double the SFT distill's 35.2** (MiMo-V2.6 technical report comparison table). The 9.3-point gain over its parent is real; the absolute placement is near the floor.
- JobBench: **18.3** (avg@1) vs 2.6 base. **Independent field context (new): 41/48, 15th percentile, 49.5 pp behind the field leader O-5 at 67.8** (BenchmarkList). The JobBench paper itself reports that the strongest model it evaluated, Claude Opus ~4.7 under Claude Code, reaches only 45.9% — so this benchmark's whole field is weak, which is why the absolute figure understates the model's relative competence here.
- OfficeQA: **19.5** (avg@1) vs 9.0 base.
- MiMo General (mini)†: **62.2** (avg@1) vs 28.5 base.
- τ³-Banking / τ²-bench, GDPval-AA, Claw-Eval, OSWorld: **no verified public score found**.

Reasoning / knowledge:

- MiMo Cyber (mini)†: **31.3** (avg@3) vs 5.7 base — the largest relative gain on the card (5.5×), and one of the four rows carrying a dagger, i.e. not reproducible.
- GPQA Diamond, HLE, CritPt, AA-LCR, Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, LiveBench: **no verified public score found** for this checkpoint. The parent's own figures (Qwen3.5-9B: GPQA Diamond 81.7, MMLU-Pro 82.5, LiveCodeBench v6 65.6) are **Qwen's numbers for Qwen3.5-9B**, not measurements of this distillation, and are deliberately not carried over. whatmodel.sh's composite likewise contains **no reasoning-benchmark row at all**.

Long context:

- 262,144 tokens, re-verified 2026-10-13 from the checkpoint's own `config.json` by three independent readers (llms.ninja, modelfit.io, aquanode.io). **No MRCR / RULER / GraphWalks / long-context retrieval measurement exists for this checkpoint**, and every published benchmark above is short-context.
- **New practical finding:** because only the 8 full-attention layers keep a growing KV cache (32,768 bytes/token at 16-bit; the 24 linear layers hold a fixed ~50 MB float32 state), the cache costs **0.13 GB at 4K, 1.0 GB at 32K, 4.0 GB at 128K and 8.0 GB at the 262K maximum**, per aquanode.io's table derived from the model config. On a 16 GB card the KV cache, not the weights, is what decides your usable context — a materially different picture from the 262,144 headline.

Multimodal:

- MiMo Visual Coding (mini)†: **64.0** (avg@1) vs 61.7 base — the only visual number, and it is an internal set.
- **New structural finding:** the config carries **video token ids** as well as image, alongside a 27-layer vision tower (modelfit.io); the base MiMo-V2.6 report describes Pro/Flash as text+audio+video+image, and this 9B keeps vision but drops audio. One GGUF conversion states plainly that "video behavior is not claimed by this release" (ngquocvinh GGUF card). Native image input remains verified by the `image-text-to-text` pipeline tag and the documented `AutoProcessor` image path.
- MMMU / MMMU-Pro / CharXiv: **no verified public score found**.
- **New practical finding:** attaching the vision projector on a 16 GB Mac collapsed generation from 6.0–6.3 tok/s to **0.1 tok/s** in two runs — a memory-ceiling symptom (12.7 GB recommended working set, 8.87 GB weights + 0.58 GB projector) rather than a defect in the vision path, but it means the multimodal capability is effectively unreachable on that class of machine.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 46/100.** *Revised down from 52.* The SFT produces a real step-change over its parent — AutomationBench 30.3 vs 5.0, JobBench 18.3 vs 2.6, MiMo General 62.2 vs 28.5 — and the independent leaderboards now place it in absolute context that the 2026-09-30 draft did not have. **Toolathlon-Verified 35.2 beats only 1 of 18 peers** (whatmodel) against a field average of 0.526 and a leader at 0.784 (llm-stats, 2026-10-12), and **Xiaomi's own RL-trained MiMo-V2.6-Flash and Pro score 73.6 and 76.9 on the identical benchmark** — the vendor's own family comparison puts this SFT checkpoint at less than half its siblings. AutomationBench 30.3 beats only 3 of 6 and is flagged "noisy · small run". Terminal-Bench 2.1 37.1 sits mid-field at the 48th percentile. What keeps it out of the floor band (35–45) is genuine: the 6× AutomationBench jump, the 5.5× cyber jump, and a JobBench 15th-percentile placement on a benchmark whose *paper* says its best model reaches only 45.9%.
- **Reasoning: 52/100.** Unchanged, because nothing has changed: there is still **no GPQA Diamond, HLE, CritPt, AA-LCR or Intelligence Index measurement** for this checkpoint, and whatmodel.sh's independent composite likewise carries no reasoning row. It cannot be placed above the methodology's mid band of 55–65 by evidence. The Cybersecurity result (MiMo Cyber 31.3, up from 5.7) is the only capability signal and it is an internal set; the parent's 81.7 GPQA belongs to Qwen3.5-9B, not here.
- **Context window: 70/100.** *Revised down from 72.* The 262,144 ceiling itself is now triple-verified from the checkpoint's own `config.json` and correctly distinguished from the siblings' 1M, so it stays in the 200K–500K band (65–84). What pulls it down is new: no retrieval test exists at *any* depth, the distill card still publishes no context figure of its own, and the KV-cache arithmetic shows the cache alone costs 8.0 GB at the maximum — so on the hardware this class of model actually runs on, the practically usable window is far below the headline, and the only real local runs reported sit at 4K–16K.
- **Multimodal: 66/100.** *Revised up from 64.* Native image input is verified structurally (`image-text-to-text`, `AutoModelForMultimodalLM`, documented image payloads), and the 2026-10 pass added the **video token ids and 27-layer vision tower in the config** — a third input modality beyond what the first pass recorded. It stays well below 75 because there is still **no audio encoder** in this checkpoint (the Pro/Flash siblings keep one), no non-text output, and not one image or video benchmark outside the single internal MiMo Visual Coding row. The video capability is flagged as *structurally present but unverified* — one GGUF conversion explicitly declines to claim it — and it is effectively unusable on a 16 GB machine.
- **Coding: 67/100.** *Revised up from 65.* SWE-bench Verified 61.1 and SWE-bench Pro 44.6 are respectable for a 9B dense model, and the independent percentile data now shows how respectable: **SWE-bench Pro 5th-from-top of 58, only 12.7 pp behind Claude Opus 4.6 Max**, and **SWE-bench Verified 8th-from-top of 50** against a leader at 96. Pro is up 12.6 points over its parent and both are on public leaderboards. It is capped below the frontier band by Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 37.1 (48th percentile, 54.3 pp behind Fable 5.1), by the vendor's `avg@3` aggregation rather than `pass@1`, and by the fact that this is a pre-RL SFT checkpoint — Xiaomi's own RL-trained Flash reaches 87.6 on Terminal Bench 2.1 and 67.9 on DeepSWE v1.1.
- **Cost efficiency: 92/100.** *Revised down from 97.* Still no per-token price of any kind — MIT weights, self-hosted, $0 in tokens — and the quantized options are genuinely cheap: INT4 at 4.4 GB weights and Q4_K_M at 5.63 GB fit an 8 GB card. But the 2026-10 pass quantified the real cost more honestly than "$0" did: BF16 needs 21.0 GB of VRAM, generation is **6.0–6.3 tok/s** on consumer Apple silicon (against 23.1–23.9 on an M4 Max at 4-bit), the KV cache adds up to 8.0 GB at full context, and enabling the vision projector **collapsed generation to 0.1 tok/s** on a 16 GB machine. "Free" here means *your own hardware and your own ops*, and the throughput is roughly an order of magnitude below any frontier API.
- **Overall Score: 60/100.** (46 + 52 + 70 + 66 + 67) / 5 = 60.2 → **60**. *Revised down from 61, but the shape changed more than the total: tool use −6 and cost −5 against coding +2 and multimodal +2.* Best fit unchanged in substance — a self-hosted 9B agentic/RL research starting point, or a private local coding-and-vision executor where data must not leave the machine — and the 2026-10 pass makes the case sharper in both directions. **For coding it is unusually strong for its size** (top-decile on both SWE-bench suites). **For tool use it is not** (bottom-1-of-18 on Toolathlon-Verified, half of Xiaomi's own RL siblings). It is still not a frontier choice, since every quality row rests on vendor-run evaluations, no independent lab has reproduced a single one, and half of the most flattering rows are internal sets.
