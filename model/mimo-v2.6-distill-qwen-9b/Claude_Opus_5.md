## Model card

- **Name:** MiMo-V2.6-Distill-Qwen-9B
- **Short description:** A **9B agentic research checkpoint**, not a product. Xiaomi's own framing is unusually literal: it is "a 9B agentic model developed by Xiaomi MiMo through **supervised fine-tuning of Qwen3.5-9B on MiMo-generated data**… covering **coding, general-purpose agent tasks, visual coding, and cybersecurity**. We release **this SFT checkpoint as a starting point for open research in agentic reinforcement learning**." Crucially, Xiaomi shipped it **alongside its RL environments and training code** — this is the stage *before* RL, deliberately handed over so others can do the RL. [BenchmarkList](https://benchmarklist.com/models/xiaomi-mimo-v2.6-distill-qwen-9b/) puts the correct reading plainly: "**Treat it as a baseline for RL experiments, not as a competitor to the MiMo-V2.6 Pro or Flash models.**" Distinct model; `mimo-v2.5-pro`, `mimo-v2.5-free` and other MiMo entries have their own folders.
- **Provider / access:** **Open weights only, MIT-licensed** (`XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Distill-Qwen-9B`). ⚠️ **There is no hosted route anywhere** — not on OpenCode Zen, OpenRouter or Hugging Face inference; the `opencode/mimo-v2.6-distill-qwen-9b` ID was verified absent from `zen/v1/models` on 2026-09-30. **Official GGUF conversions were published by ggml-org on 2026-09-21**, so llama.cpp, Ollama and LM Studio are the practical paths.
- **Release / knowledge:** **2026-09-21/22** (BenchmarkList dates the release Sep 22; the GGUF conversion landed Sep 21). Knowledge cutoff: inherited from Qwen3.5-9B; not separately published.
- **IDs:** `XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Distill-Qwen-9B` (Hugging Face). Free in the only sense available — **MIT weights, self-host or nothing**.
- **Context window:** **262,144 tokens (256K)** per `config.json`, using a **hybrid linear/full attention** stack. Max output: not disclosed.
- **Modalities:** **Text + image + video in → text out** per `config.json`, and "**visual coding**" is one of the four stated training domains. Audio tokens exist in the vocabulary but **there is no audio tower in `config.json`**, so audio is not usable. Reasoning: yes. Tool calls: yes.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-08):** **$0 — MIT open weights, self-hosted.** **18.8 GB at BF16**, or **5.841 GB at Q4_K_M** (Local AI Stats), which puts it inside a 12 GB consumer GPU.
- **Architecture:** **~9.4B dense** (Local AI Stats measures 8.954B total parameters — the gap is presumably embedding accounting), a **dense SFT over Qwen3.5-9B** rather than a new architecture. No MoE, no new attention design beyond the inherited hybrid linear/full stack.

### Raw benchmarks found

> **All four figures are Xiaomi's own first-party runs.** BenchLM has no page for this model (404); BenchmarkList carries **4 score rows, "4 first-party"**, and no independent evaluator has measured it. BenchmarkList's caveat is worth adopting verbatim: "the numbers come from Xiaomi's own runs, **several of its evaluations are internal**, and the model is not offered through an API."

Coding — 3 evaluations, median 8th percentile:

- **SWE-bench Verified: 61.1%** — rank 46 of 50, 8th percentile (field leader O-5 at 96)
- **SWE-bench Pro: 44.6%** — rank 55 of 58, 5th percentile (field leader O-5.5 at 89.9). **The key comparison Xiaomi draws is against its own base: 44.6% versus Qwen3.5-9B's 32.0%, a +12.6-point gain** — this is the clearest evidence the distillation did real work.
- **Terminal-Bench 2.1: 37.1%** — rank 102 of 194, **48th percentile.** This is the most impressive number in the report: a **9B model at the median of 194 models** on a hard terminal-agent harness.

Agentic — 1 evaluation, median 15th percentile:

- **JobBench: 18.3%** — rank 41 of 48, 15th percentile. The benchmark's own description matters for calibration: JobBench "covers **130 agentic tasks across 35 occupations**", each "packaged as a workspace of heterogeneous reference files, requiring the agent to reason through the cluttered information streams of real professional work", graded "by a fact-anchored chain of rubrics, **averaging 35.6 binary criteria per task**". And decisively: "We evaluate 36 models; **the strongest, Claude Opus 4.7 under Claude Code, reaches only 45.9%.**" So 18.3% is roughly **40% of the current frontier**, not a floor result.

Head-to-head, as BenchmarkList computes it:

- **0 wins, 15 losses, rank #8 of 8** against GLM 5.3, Kimi K3, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, Fable 5.1, MiMo-V2.6-Pro, Opus 5.5 and Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B. Largest deficits: **SWE-bench Pro −94.7 points versus O-5.5** and **−91.2 versus Fable 5.1**.

**Absent entirely:** no GPQA Diamond, MMLU-Pro, AIME, HLE, ARC-AGI, CritPt, Omniscience or hallucination figure; **no multimodal benchmark of any kind** despite image and video input; **no cybersecurity benchmark** despite cybersecurity being a stated training domain; and no MRCR, RULER or AA-LCR measurement despite a 256K window.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 58/100.** Better than the percentile ranks suggest, once the field is understood. **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 37.1% places a 9B model at the 48th percentile of 194** — that is a genuinely strong agentic result for the parameter count, and it is the single best indicator that MiMo's agent-data distillation transferred. **JobBench 18.3% against a field whose best model reaches only 45.9%** is roughly 40% of frontier performance on a deliberately brutal occupational-workflow benchmark. Capped at the midpoint because the evidence is thin (two agentic-adjacent numbers, both first-party), there is **no BFCL, τ²-bench, GDPval, Toolathlon or MCP figure**, and the model is explicitly the **pre-RL checkpoint** — Xiaomi is telling you the agentic capability is unfinished.
- **Reasoning: 38/100.** Scored low on complete absence of evidence. **There is no GPQA, MMLU-Pro, AIME, HLE, ARC-AGI or CritPt figure at all**, and I will not import Qwen3.5-9B's base figures, which belong to a different checkpoint. Reasoning is supported architecturally, and 61.1% SWE-bench Verified is not reachable without real multi-step reasoning — that inference is what keeps this above the floor. But a 9B dense SFT checkpoint that has not yet had RL applied is not a reasoning model, and Xiaomi does not claim it is.
- **Context window: 58/100.** **262,144 tokens from a 9B dense model** is a generous specification, inherited from Qwen3.5-9B's hybrid linear/full attention stack, and it is architecturally plausible rather than aspirational. But **no retrieval measurement exists at any depth** — no MRCR, RULER, needle test or AA-LCR — and **no max-output figure is published**. Scored on a credible spec with zero verified recall.
- **Multimodal: 40/100.** An unusual case: **real capability, zero measurement.** `config.json` supports **image and video input**, and "**visual coding**" is one of four explicit training domains, so this is not a nominal claim. But **not one multimodal benchmark has been published** — no MMMU, MMMU-Pro, OCR, chart, document or video evaluation — so there is no way to know whether the vision path is competent or vestigial. The audio situation is instructive about care being warranted: **audio tokens exist in the vocabulary but there is no audio tower**, i.e. the artefact contains traces of a capability it does not have. Scored above the text-only floor for genuine multimodal input, well below mid-band for having nothing to verify it.
- **Coding: 62/100.** The strongest-evidenced dimension and the model's purpose. **SWE-bench Verified 61.1% and SWE-bench Pro 44.6% from a 9B dense model are strong absolute figures**, and the **+12.6-point SWE-bench Pro gain over the Qwen3.5-9B base (44.6 vs 32.0)** is a clean, like-for-like demonstration that distilling MiMo-V2.6's agent traces into a small model works. Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 37.1% corroborates. The counterweight is scale honesty: **8th and 5th percentile ranks**, and **−94.7 points behind the SWE-bench Pro leader**. This is a competent small coding model, not a competitive one, and Xiaomi says so.
- **Cost efficiency: 90/100.** Excellent, and the generosity goes beyond the licence. **MIT weights at $0**, **5.841 GB at Q4_K_M** so it runs on a 12 GB consumer GPU, **official ggml-org GGUF conversions on day one**, and — unusually — **Xiaomi released its RL environments and training code alongside the checkpoint**. That makes this a genuine research contribution rather than a marketing drop: the point is that someone else can take the SFT stage and run the RL. Getting 61.1% SWE-bench Verified and median-of-194 Terminal-Bench performance on consumer hardware for nothing is strong value. The one real deduction: **no hosted route exists anywhere** — verified absent from Zen, OpenRouter and HF inference — so there is no zero-setup path and no managed option at all.
- **Overall Score: 51.2/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dims (58 + 38 + 58 + 40 + 62) / 5 = 51.2. Read this as a **research baseline score, which is exactly what the artefact is**. Best fit: **agentic RL research** — take the SFT checkpoint and Xiaomi's released environments and training code, and run the RL stage yourself — plus **local small-model coding work** on consumer hardware where 61.1% SWE-bench Verified at 5.8 GB is a good trade. Do not deploy it as a production agent (pre-RL, JobBench 18.3%, 0 wins in head-to-head), do not rely on its reasoning (zero benchmarks), and do not assume the vision path works (zero benchmarks, and the stray audio tokens are a caution). The honest praise here is for Xiaomi's release practice: publishing the intermediate checkpoint, the RL environments and the training code, while labelling it plainly as a starting point rather than a product.
