## Model card

- **Name:** GPT-6 Sol. Proprietary; official model documentation at `developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-sol`. Variant/alias flag: part of the **GPT-6 family** with **GPT-6 Astra** (flagship), **GPT-6 Luna** (cheapest) and **GPT-6.1 Sol** — the last of which **replaced this model on 2026-09-29**. Distinct from GPT-5.6 Sol, its direct predecessor.
- **Short description:** **This model had one of the shortest production lives in this dataset.** OpenAI shipped it on **2026-09-22 — ninety minutes after Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5.5, at half the token price** — and replaced it with GPT-6.1 Sol **seven days later**. A composite tracker nonetheless places it **#7 of 783 at 79.2/100**, ahead of GPT-6.1 Sol (77.58) and GPT-5.6 Sol (78.95). Its standout results are agentic: **Agents' Last Exam at 56.4% and OSWorld 2.0 at 60.5% are both the highest figures recorded in this entire research pass**, the latter by a very large margin.
- **Provider / access:** OpenAI API and ChatGPT. Reasoning: explicit reasoning mode with effort levels (OpenAI family standard).
- **Release / knowledge:** **Released 2026-09-22; superseded by GPT-6.1 Sol on 2026-09-29.** **Knowledge cutoff: no verified public figure found.**
- **IDs:** `gpt-6-sol`. **No free-tier ID.**
- **Context window:** **1,050,000 tokens** — the GPT-6 family standard, which also implies a **128,000-token normal output limit** and the **272K full-request repricing threshold**, though neither was separately verified for this model. **AA-LCR at 83.7% is among the highest long-context reasoning figures in this research pass.**
- **Modalities:** **Text and image input → text output** (GPT-6 family standard; no audio or video). A strong AA-MMMU-Pro figure confirms the vision path. **No primary specification sheet was read directly**, so modalities follow the documented family pattern.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **Approximately $2 / 1M input and $10 / 1M output.** Independent comparison coverage states OpenAI shipped it **"at half the token price"** of Claude Opus 5.5 ($4/$20), and its direct replacement GPT-6.1 Sol is documented at exactly $2.00 / $0.10 cached / $10.00. **Re-verify before budgeting** — no primary OpenAI pricing page for this model was retrieved. The same coverage found that **Sol is cheaper than Claude Opus 5.5 to any Intelligence Index score up to about 44, then runs out of headroom.**
- **Architecture:** Proprietary, closed weights. Parameter count and topology: not disclosed.

### Raw benchmarks found

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 87/100.** Two results are the best in this entire research pass and both matter. **Agents' Last Exam at 56.4%** leads every model here on open-ended agentic difficulty, and **OSWorld 2.0 at 60.5% leads by an enormous margin** — the next best is Gemini 3.6 Flash at 33.8%, and most models in this dataset score in single digits on that benchmark version. That is a genuinely differentiated computer-use capability rather than a marginal lead. **AA Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 43.9%** is also among the best on the hardest terminal harness, with **AA AutomationBench at 61.6%**, GDPval-AA at 1,505 Elo and AA Briefcase at 1,479 filling out a strong profile. Four deductions: **GDPval remains 245 Elo short of the ~1750+ reference**; **OpenAI's own AutomationBench figure (33.2%) is 28.4 points below the independent one**, so harness choice matters a great deal; **Terminal-Bench 2.1, Tau2-bench, Tau3-Banking, Claw-Eval, MCP-Atlas and Toolathlon are every one of them unreported**; and a **Gray Swan prompt-injection success rate of 10.1%** means roughly one injection attempt in ten succeeds — acceptable with sandboxing, poor against the Claude line's 1.0% or Gemini 4 Argon's 0.7%.
- **Reasoning: 88/100.** A strong and almost entirely independent profile. **CritPt at 30.9% is the second-highest in this research pass** on research-level physics — a benchmark where most models score in single digits or low teens — **AA-HLE at 47.9% comfortably clears the 40%+ frontier reference** and is among the highest here, **AA-LCR at 83.7%** is near the top of this dataset, and **the AA-Omniscience composite index of 27.1% on 54.5% accuracy** is the fourth-best reliability profile recorded. Four deductions: the **Intelligence Index of 47.6% is 12 points below the 60+ frontier marker**; **no GPQA Diamond figure exists at all**, which leaves the methodology's primary knowledge reference unchecked for a model ranked #7 overall; **MLCR-AA at 16.1% against AA-LCR's 83.7% is a 67.6-point collapse** on multi-document reasoning; and **the 60.1% hallucination rate is high** despite the good composite.
- **Context window: 95/100.** A **1,050,000-token context** places it in the ≥1M tier (95–100), and **AA-LCR at 83.7%** is among the strongest long-context reasoning measurements in this research pass. It sits at the tier floor because the 100 condition requires **≥98% retrieval at 512K+** and **no MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks figure exists**; because **MLCR-AA at 16.1%** shows multi-document reasoning across that window largely fails; and because the GPT-6 family's **272K full-request repricing threshold** should be assumed to apply, making the economically sane window roughly a quarter of the nominal one.
- **Multimodal: 69/100.** Input is **text and image** with text-only output and no audio or video path, placing it in the methodology's "+image in = 60–70" band. **AA-MMMU-Pro at 82.9% is strong and independently measured** — third-best in this dataset — and it is the only multimodal evidence that exists. It sits just below the band ceiling because a single datapoint, however good, cannot demonstrate breadth: **there is no CharXiv, document-understanding, OCR, video or audio benchmark at all**, so chart reading and document extraction — both standard at this tier — are entirely unmeasured.
- **Coding: 80/100.** **Only two coding benchmarks exist for this model**, which is a conspicuous gap for something ranked #7 of 783. Of the two, one is genuinely excellent: **AA-SciCode at 57.6% clears the methodology's 55%+ frontier reference and is the highest such figure in this entire research pass**. The other falls short: **DeepSWE at 68.8% is 5.2 points below the 74%+ frontier reference**, behind GPT-6 Astra (74.1%), DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (74.2%) and Claude Opus 5.5 (74.2%). **SWE-bench Verified, SWE-bench Pro, LiveCodeBench, Terminal-Bench at any version and Vibe Code Bench are all absent**, so three of the band's four references cannot be checked and there is no repository-scale, algorithmic or real-editor evidence whatsoever. 80 reflects one excellent and one sub-reference datapoint with no corroboration.
- **Cost efficiency: 82/100.** At approximately **$2 / $10 it beats the $3/$15 anchor that maps to ~60**, and the comparative evidence is specific and useful: independent analysis found that **GPT-6 Sol is cheaper than Claude Opus 5.5 to any Intelligence Index score up to about 44, then runs out of headroom** — a precise statement of where the value proposition holds and where it stops. Four deductions: the **price is unconfirmed** by any primary source for this model; **no free tier**; the **272K repricing threshold** should be assumed to apply and punishes long-context work; and most decisively, **it was replaced seven days after launch by GPT-6.1 Sol at the documented same $2/$10**, which scores higher on CritPt (31.7% vs 30.9%), AA-MMMU-Pro (86.0% vs 82.9%), AA-HLE (52.9% vs 47.9%), the Intelligence Index (51.8% vs 47.6%) and the whole HealthBench suite. There is no price argument for the older model.
- **Overall Score: 83.8/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (87 + 88 + 95 + 69 + 80) / 5 = 83.8 — a strong agentic and scientific reasoner with a seven-day shelf life. Its genuinely differentiated credential is computer use: **OSWorld 2.0 at 60.5% is nearly double the next-best figure in this entire dataset**, and **Agents' Last Exam at 56.4% leads outright** — if your workload is desktop or browser automation on the current benchmark generation, nothing else here measures close. Add **CritPt 30.9%** (second-best research-physics reasoning), **AA-SciCode 57.6%** (best in this dataset), **AA-HLE 47.9%**, **AA-LCR 83.7%** and **AA Terminal-Bench 4.0 43.9%**, and the capability case is real. Three things should govern any decision. **It is superseded**: GPT-6.1 Sol arrived seven days later at the same documented $2/$10 and scores higher on every overlapping reasoning and multimodal benchmark — the only rows where this model might still lead are OSWorld 2.0 and Agents' Last Exam, which GPT-6.1 Sol has not published. **The coding evidence is two benchmarks deep** and one of them misses its reference. And **no GPQA Diamond figure exists**, so the single most-cited knowledge benchmark is unmeasured for a model that a composite tracker ranks #7 of 783.
