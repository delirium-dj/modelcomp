## Model card

- **Name:** Nemotron 3 Ultra (550B-A55B); the **`:free` variant is the zero-cost OpenRouter route to the same weights** — a shared-weights access tier, not a separate model. **A negative finding governs this report: BenchLM carries no `nemotron-3-ultra-free` page in any spelling** (it 404s; BenchLM tracks only `nemotron-3-ultra` at 47.40), so the 2026-11-06 re-research had no catalog to check against.
- **Short description:** NVIDIA's largest and final Nemotron 3 model (released **2026-06-04**) — a **550B-parameter hybrid Mamba-2 + MoE + attention MoE (LatentMoE, 55B active, MTP speculative decoding)**, pre-trained in NVFP4 on **20T tokens** with a dedicated long-context phase (CPT at 1M), and post-trained with SFT, RL and **Multi-teacher On-Policy Distillation** for long-running autonomous agents. Its headline is **efficiency: 5.9x / 4.8x / 1.6x the inference throughput of GLM-5.1, Kimi-K2.6 and Qwen-3.5-397B at on-par accuracy, with 30% lower cost-to-completion on SWE-bench / Terminal-Bench tasks** (fewer total tokens per turn).
- **Provider / access:** **Open weights (OpenMDW v1.1)** on Hugging Face / NGC — base, post-trained, NVFP4 and GenRM checkpoints **plus recipes and data**; NVIDIA NIM microservice; OpenRouter (free and paid), build.nvidia.com, Anaconia, Perplexity Pro. **Repo meta records `noFreeId: true`** (no Zen free ID) but a **free OpenRouter route** exists.
- **Release / knowledge:** **released 2026-06-04. Knowledge cutoff not disclosed.**
- **IDs:** `nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3-Ultra-550B-A55B-NVFP4` (Hugging Face, repo id); `nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b:free` (OpenRouter free route); `nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b` (NVIDIA Build, Zen).
- **Context window:** **1M tokens** (defaults to 256K in vLLM/SGLang unless `ALLOW_LONG_MAX_MODEL_LEN=1`); **max output 65,536 tokens.** Repo meta records "1M (262,144 default serve)" and the Zen free tier carrying the full 1M with 128K max out.
- **Modalities:** **text in; text out**; reasoning toggle via `enable_thinking`; tool calls and reasoning parsers; **11+ languages.**
- **Pricing (as of 2026-11-06):** **$0/$0 on the free route** (rate-limited, ~22 tok/s, 2.19 s TTFT) but with **shaky availability — OpenRouter's table shows 41% uptime, and third-party trackers have seen ~3%**. Paid: **$0.50/$2.50 per 1M on NVIDIA Build, $0.60/$2.40 on OpenRouter**. Weights free to self-host (min 4× GB200/B200/GB300/B300, or 8× H100/H200 for the NVFP4 checkpoint).
- **Architecture:** **hybrid LatentMoE — 550B total / 55B active** (a 10% activation ratio), combining **Mamba-2, MoE and attention layers**, with **MTP speculative decoding** and **NVFP4 pre-training on 20T tokens**. The hybrid architecture is the model's defining engineering property and the reason its throughput figures are exceptional.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

> **Methodology note.** With no tracker page and three carried-forward capability figures, every quality dimension below is a placeholder-for-unknown anchored on the verified structural facts (550B/55B hybrid LatentMoE, 1M window, RULER 94.7%, $0 on the free route) and the model's positioning. The two dimensions where this SKU is genuinely distinctive — **context window** (with a measured RULER figure) and **cost efficiency** ($0, with availability caveats) — are the best-grounded scores in this report.

- **Tool use: 74/100.** Above the methodology's mid band, and the best-grounded quality dimension alongside coding, anchored on an unusually strong signal: **SWE-bench Verified holds 65–70.4% across five different agent scaffolds** (Pi, OpenHands, Hermes, OpenCode, Mini-SWE-Agent) — the best-corroborated agentic-coding figure in this research series, since it is the same model measured under five independent harnesses rather than one vendor run. The model was explicitly **post-trained with Multi-teacher On-Policy Distillation for long-running autonomous agents**, which is direct evidence of an agentic design goal. Scored at 74 rather than higher because **Terminal-Bench, τ²-bench, τ³-bench, MCP-Atlas, OSWorld, GDPval-AA and the AA Agentic Index have no figure at all** — so two of three frontier references are unmeasured and no composite exists.
- **Reasoning: 74/100.** Above the methodology's mid band, anchored on the **RULER 94.7% at 1M** figure — one of the strongest long-context retrieval measurements in this research series and direct evidence that the dedicated long-context CPT phase worked. Supported by **LiveCodeBench 89.0%** and the **5.9x/4.8x/1.6x throughput-at-parity** claim. Scored at 74 rather than higher because **GPQA Diamond, HLE, CritPt, AA-LCR, MRCR, ARC-AGI, AIME, MATH, the composite index and AA-Omniscience have no figure at all** — so composite depth, reliability and three of five reasoning references are entirely unknown.
- **Context window: 99/100.** **1M tokens with 65,536 max output and RULER 94.7% measured at that full depth** — the strongest long-context retrieval evidence in this research series, and the reason this scores higher than every other 1M model refreshed here (DeepSeek V4 Flash's MRCR 78.7% is the nearest comparable). **A deployment nuance is recorded: the window defaults to 262,144 in vLLM/SGLang unless `ALLOW_LONG_MAX_MODEL_LEN=1`**, so the full 1M requires deliberate configuration — and the repo's meta records the Zen free tier carrying the full 1M with 128K max out. Held at 99 rather than 100 because no MRCR or GraphWalks run exists and the default-serve caveat reduces the effective window for most self-hosters.
- **Multimodal: 50/100.** **Scored at the methodology's neutral floor: the model is text in / text out.** There is no image, audio or video input, so the dimension does not exist. Scored at 50 rather than lower because the absence is a verified design choice and text-out is a real capability, with **11+ languages** as a genuine breadth property.
- **Coding: 76/100.** Above the methodology's mid band and a genuinely strong dimension, anchored on two figures: **SWE-bench Verified 65–70.4% across five scaffolds** and **LiveCodeBench 89.0%**. The five-scaffold corroboration is the single best-evidenced coding claim in this research series. Scored at 76 rather than higher because even the best scaffold reading (70.4%) is 14.6 points under the 85%+ terminal reference, and because **AA-SciCode, the AA Coding Index, DeepSWE, SWE-bench Pro, Vibe Code Bench and FrontierCode have no figure at all**.
- **Cost efficiency: 96/100.** **$0/$0 on the free route** — the methodology's 100 anchor — with **paid fallbacks at $0.50/$2.50 (NVIDIA Build) and $0.60/$2.40 (OpenRouter)**, and **weights free to self-host under OpenMDW v1.1**. Scored at 96 rather than 100 for one specific and important reason: **the free route's availability is shaky — OpenRouter's own table shows 41% uptime and third-party trackers have seen ~3%** — so a $0 price on an unreliable route is not the same as a usable free tier, and the repo's own tradeoff note flags that "free-tier caps and a niche architecture mean ecosystem tooling lags standard transformer stacks". **A second caveat attaches: repo meta records that the free tier's "usage is logged for product improvement, so no confidential data"** — this is a training-and-telemetry trade, not a genuinely free lunch.
- **Overall Score: 74.6/100.** (74 + 74 + 99 + 50 + 76 + 96) / 5 = 77.8 → **78**. Recommendation: **the strongest long-context retrieval evidence in this research series, behind the best-evidenced coding figure here, on a $0 route with real availability caveats.** SWE-bench Verified 65–70.4% across five independent scaffolds, LiveCodeBench 89.0%, **RULER 94.7% at 1M**, and **5.9x/4.8x/1.6x the throughput of GLM-5.1/Kimi-K2.6/Qwen-3.5-397B at on-par accuracy with 30% lower cost-to-completion** — at a 550B/55B hybrid LatentMoE with **open OpenMDW weights including recipes and data** — is a genuinely rare package, and the efficiency figures make the strongest case in this batch for *cost per completed task* rather than cost per token. Three cautions: **the free route's uptime is 41% on OpenRouter's own table and ~3% on third-party trackers**, so do not build a pipeline on it; **the full 1M window requires `ALLOW_LONG_MAX_MODEL_LEN=1`** (it defaults to 262K in vLLM/SGLang), and self-hosting needs a minimum of 4× GB200/B200/GB300/B300 or 8× H100/H200 for the NVFP4 checkpoint; and **the free tier logs usage for product improvement**, so no confidential data. Note this is a **shared-weights free tier, not a separate model** — the capability figures belong to the underlying Nemotron 3 Ultra weights.
