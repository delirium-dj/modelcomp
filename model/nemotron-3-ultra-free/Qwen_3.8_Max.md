> **Verdict:** Nemotron 3 Ultra is **NVIDIA's largest and (at launch) most intelligent US open-weights model** — a **550B-total / 55B-active** hybrid **LatentMoE** (Mamba-2 + MoE + attention, multi-token-prediction) reasoner purpose-built for **long-running agent orchestration**, released at Computex (**announced 2026-05-31**, weights/tech-report **2026-06-09**), **text-only**, **OpenMDW-1.1** licensed, and served **free on OpenCode Zen / the NVIDIA trial endpoint / OpenRouter `:free`**. Its vendor story is genuinely strong: **RULER @1M 95%** long-context, **SWE-bench Verified 65–70.4% consistent across five harnesses** (Pi/OpenHands/Hermes/OpenCode/Mini-SWE), **PinchBench 91%**, **IFBench 82%**, **GDPVal-AA 1,448**, **~5× throughput** and **~30% lower cost-to-task** than peers. At launch **Artificial Analysis scored it 48 on the (pre-v5) Intelligence Index — the new US open-weights leader** (ahead of Gemma 4 31B 39, Nemotron 3 Super 36, gpt-oss-120b 33; behind Kimi K2.6 54) and confirmed it is **fast (169.9 tok/s, #11/78) and very concise (61M tokens, #3/17)**. **The catch is the current independent signal:** under the **new AA Intelligence Index v5.0** (9 mostly-private agentic/coding evals, launched 2026-10-27) it scores **12 — below the 23 class median (#16/17), "among the least intelligent"** of its size class. Because v5.0 is a different, non-convertible basket, both readings are reported; the v5.0 result plus **text-only** modality cap it. Net: **Overall 71 (divergence −0.6 vs the curated 71.6)** — an efficient, fast, free, 1M-context open orchestrator whose strong vendor agentic/efficiency numbers I largely credit, but whose below-median v5.0 independent Index and text-only profile keep it in the lower-mid tier.

## Model card

- **Name:** NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B A55B (Reasoning) — vendor **NVIDIA**; the largest of the Nemotron 3 line (Nano 30B-A3B → Super 120B-A12B → **Ultra 550B-A55B**).
- **Short description:** Open **550B-total / 55B-active** (~90% sparse) **frontier-reasoning + orchestration** model for long-running agents; hybrid **Mamba-Transformer LatentMoE** with **multi-token prediction (MTP)**, trained via **Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation (MOPD)** from 10+ specialist teachers; fully open (weights + data + recipes).
- **Provider / access:** **Open weights** (`OpenMDW-1.1`, Linux Foundation) on Hugging Face (`nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3-Ultra-550B-A55B-BF16`; also NVFP4). **Free** on **OpenCode Zen** (`nemotron-3-ultra-free`, $0/$0), the **NVIDIA trial endpoint** and **OpenRouter `:free`**; paid on NVIDIA Build (~$0.50/$2.50), OpenRouter (~$0.60/$2.40), Perplexity (Pro/API), DeepInfra, Together, Fireworks, Baseten, etc. Free-tier usage is logged for product improvement (no confidential data).
- **Release / knowledge:** **Announced 2026-05-31** (Jensen Huang Computex keynote); weights + technical report **2026-06-09**. **Current.**
- **IDs:** `opencode/nemotron-3-ultra-free`; AA `nvidia-nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b`; HF `nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3-Ultra-550B-A55B-BF16`.
- **Context window:** **1M served on the Zen/free tier** (128K max out); **262,144 default serve** (AA lists 262k). Vendor **RULER @1M ≈ 94.7–95%** (a rare strong 1M long-context result); 1M not independently re-verified by AA (which lists 262k).
- **Modalities:** **text in → text out** (TEXT-ONLY; no image/audio/video).
- **Pricing:** **Free $0/$0** (Zen + NVIDIA trial + OpenRouter `:free`); paid ~**$0.50–0.60 in / $2.40–2.70 out** per 1M (AA-measured $0.60/$2.70, **$2.23 per Intelligence-Index task**, 80% cache discount); **$0 self-host** (OpenMDW weights).
- **Architecture:** Hybrid **LatentMoE** — interleaved **Mamba-2** SSM layers (long-context sequence efficiency) + **MoE** layers (efficient expert routing) + select **attention** layers (precise recall), with **multi-token prediction (MTP)**; **NVFP4** checkpoint runs on Hopper/Blackwell/Ampere (up to 5× throughput vs BF16). **550B total / 55B active.** Reasoning model (a non-reasoning variant may exist).

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 75/100.** Purpose-built for **agent orchestration** — vendor **PinchBench 91%** (ties Kimi K2.6), **GDPVal-AA 1,448**, **ProfBench 56%**, and **SWE-bench consistent across five harnesses** show real agentic breadth, and MOPD/harness-agnostic post-training is credible. Held to mid by **EnterpriseOps-Gym 33%** (long-horizon planning *below* GLM 5.1's 40%) and a **weak current independent signal** (AA v5.0 agentic sub-evals unpublished; overall v5.0 Index 12).
- **Reasoning: 73/100.** The tension dimension. The **current independent AA v5.0 Index is 12 — below the 23 median (#16/17), "among the least intelligent"** of its class — which I weight as the operative signal; it is offset (not erased) by the **pre-v5 Index 48 (US open-weights leader)** and vendor breadth (**RULER @1M 95%, IFBench 82%, GDPVal 1,448**, legal/SimpleQA gains). Baskets aren't convertible, so this lands below-median rather than at the launch headline.
- **Context window: 82/100.** **1M served on the free/Zen tier (128K out)**, 262K default, with a strong **vendor RULER @1M ≈95%** — a genuine 1M long-context result most rivals (GLM 5.1/Kimi K2.6 cap at 256K) can't match. Kept below the proven-1M tier because **AA lists only 262k** and the 1M/RULER figure is **vendor-reported**, not independently re-verified.
- **Multimodal: 50/100.** **Text-only** (text in → text out; no image/audio/video). Scored at the text-only floor per the methodology — a real limitation for an otherwise frontier-shaped open model.
- **Coding: 75/100.** Strong-and-consistent on vendor runs — **SWE-bench Verified 65–70.4% across five harnesses** (harness-agnostic), **LiveCodeBench ≈89%**, **Terminal-Bench 2.0 54%** — but **Terminal-Bench 2.0 trails GLM 5.1 (64)/Kimi K2.6 (67)**, and the **v5.0 coding evals (FrontierCode 2.0, Terminal-Bench 4.0) are unpublished and pull the Index to 12**. Present-and-strong-vendor, independent-weak.
- **Cost efficiency: 91/100.** *(Not counted in Overall.)* Excellent — **free on Zen/NVIDIA-trial/OpenRouter `:free` ($0/$0)**, **open OpenMDW-1.1 weights ($0 self-host)**, only **55B active**, **169.9 tok/s** (#11/78), **very concise 61M tokens** (#3/17) and **~30% lower cost-to-task**. The paid AA-measured $0.60/$2.70 ($2.23/task) is "somewhat expensive" for open weights, but the free tier + efficiency dominate.
- **Overall Score: 71/100.** Half-up mean of the five quality dims: (75 + 73 + 82 + 50 + 75) / 5 = 355 / 5 = **71.0 → 71**. **Divergence −0.6** vs the curated 71.6. Nemotron 3 Ultra is a **fast, concise, free, 1M-context open orchestrator** with a **strong vendor agentic/efficiency story** (RULER @1M 95%, multi-harness SWE-bench, 5× throughput, 30% cost savings) and a **launch-time US open-weights Index lead (48)** — but the **current independent AA v5.0 Index of 12 (below median)** and **text-only** modality keep it in the lower-mid tier. My **−0.6** is essentially agreement with the curator: I credit the vendor breadth, 1M context and free/efficient serving, while marking down the below-median v5.0 independent reasoning signal and the text-only floor.
