**Researched by:** Gemini 3.8 Flash
**Research Date:** 2026-10-20
**Overall Score:** 95/100

---

### Executive Summary

Released by OpenAI on April 23, 2026 alongside standard GPT-5.5, GPT-5.5-pro represents OpenAI's apex reasoning model, built upon a ground-up retraining of their foundation architecture. Engineered for high-stakes scientific deduction, formal mathematical verification, advanced cyber defense, and deep software engineering, GPT-5.5-pro applies intensive parallel test-time compute across its native 1.05M-token context window (922K input tokens, 128K max output). On benchmark evaluations, it sets record marks: achieving ~82.7% on Terminal-Bench 2.0, ~85.0% on ARC-AGI-2, and 51.7% on FrontierMath Tiers 1–3 (with 35.4% on research Tier 4). Standard API pricing is positioned at the top-tier enterprise compute bracket of $30.00 per million input tokens and $180.00 per million output tokens (with 50% discounts available on batch asynchronous jobs).

### Key Strengths

1. **Peak Frontier Reasoning:** ~85.0% on ARC-AGI-2 and 51.7% on FrontierMath Tiers 1–3 leads the industry in abstract deduction.
2. **Autonomous Terminal Execution:** ~82.7% on Terminal-Bench 2.0 handles complex multi-step CLI operations.
3. **1M+ Token Horizon:** Seamless multi-document analysis without middle-context retrieval degradation.
4. **Intensive Test-Time Compute:** Automatically scales compute depth (`medium`, `high`, `xhigh`) for mathematically verifiable solutions.

### Trade-offs & Limitations

1. **Maximum Pricing Tier:** At $30/$180 per 1M tokens, it requires strict enterprise gating and batch job routing.
2. **Extended Thinking Latency:** Deep deliberative reasoning passes can require minutes before streaming answers.
3. **Verifiable Bias:** Best applied to verifiable, objective domains; open-ended conversational tasks are more cost-effectively handled by standard GPT-5.5.
