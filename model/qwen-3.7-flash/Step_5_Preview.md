## Model card

- **Name:** Qwen3.7 Flash — Alibaba's **closed-weights Flash-tier multimodal model with a 1M context window**, positioned for cheap long-context agent and document work, with the repo folder recording the dotted spelling `qwen-3.7-flash`. **A negative finding governs this report: BenchLM carries no page for this model in any spelling** (`qwen-3.7-flash`, `qwen3-7-flash`, `qwen3.7-flash` and `Qwen3.7-Flash` all 404), so the 2026-11-06 re-research had no catalog to check against.
- **Short description:** A **cheap 1M-context multimodal model whose reasoning record is genuinely strong and whose agentic record is a single figure**. **GPQA Diamond 82% (epoch.ai) vs 79.6% (benchgecko.ai), OTIS Mock AIME 2024-25 87% / 86.7%, ObviousBench agentic answer pass3 91.0% at a measured $0.06 per run, and a triple-sourced FrontierMath v2 Tiers 1–3 at 19.3%** — set against **Chess Puzzles 19.0%, Mystery Game Puzzles 15% / 6.4%, no Terminal-Bench, no τ²-bench, no τ³-bench, no SWE-bench and no Artificial Analysis page at all.**
- **Provider / access:** **QwenCloud / Alibaba Cloud Model Studio** (`https://qwencloud.com`), OpenAI-compatible; also served by 15 providers per models.dev. **The OpenCode Console/Zen catalog does not list this ID.**
- **Release / knowledge:** released **2026-07-15** (models.dev). **A release-date conflict is recorded rather than reconciled: OpenRouter's snapshot is `qwen3.7-flash-20260727` and Epoch/Benchable date it 2026-07-27 while models.dev says 2026-07-15.** Knowledge cutoff not disclosed.
- **IDs:** `alibaba/qwen3.7-flash` (models.dev); `qwen/qwen3.7-flash-20260727` (OpenRouter).

Max output is conflicting: 131,072 (models.dev and qwencloud) vs 65,536 (Alibaba model card per eesel, and Roboflow) vs 128,000 (LLMLearner)** — chain-of-thought budget is separately 262,144; max input 991,808 (983,616 in thinking mode). **The largest of three conflicting output limits recorded side by side rather than averaged.**
- **Modalities:** **text, image and video in; text out.**
- **Pricing (as of 2026-11-06):** **tiered on QwenCloud — <32K context $0.03/$0.13 per 1M, 32–256K $0.10/$0.40, 256K–1M $0.20/$0.80** — with cache reads at **$0.006 per 1M**. models.dev and AIHubMix list a **flat $0.0282/$0.1128**, which is the cheapest route and the figure used for cost scoring.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 62/100.** Below the mid band's top, and resting on exactly one figure. **ObviousBench agentic answer pass3 91.0% at high reasoning is a genuinely strong agentic result**, with the effort curve (47.2 / 84.7 / 90.3% at none / low / medium effort) showing the capability scales with reasoning budget. Held at 62 rather than higher by **the total absence of every methodology agentic benchmark** — no Terminal-Bench, τ²-bench, τ³-bench, MCP-Atlas, OSWorld, Toolathlon, Claw-Eval, JobBench, GDPval-AA or AA Agentic Index — so all three frontier references are unmeasured and no composite exists. **The Benchable Email Classification 99.0% is recorded but explicitly not weighted**, because a single narrow classification task at $0.0062 per run is not an agentic benchmark.
- **Reasoning: 62/100.** Below the mid band's top, and internally contradictory in a way that is recorded rather than smoothed. **GPQA Diamond 82%/79.6% and OTIS Mock AIME 87%/86.7% are strong**, and the AIME runs agree within 0.3 points. Against that, **FrontierMath v2 Tiers 1–3 at 19.3% (triple-sourced and stable) is weak, Chess Puzzles 19.0% is weak, and Mystery Game Puzzles at 15%/6.4% is near-floor** — a spread that says the model is strong on saturating academic benchmarks and weak on anything requiring sustained search. **The ECI 144 (#96/253, drifting from #70/227 at release)** corroborates the mid-tier reading. **AA Intelligence Index, HLE, CritPt, AA-LCR, ARC-AGI, MRCR and AA-Omniscience are all absent.**
- **Context window: 90/100.** **1,000,000 tokens total with a 131,072 max output** (the largest of three conflicting limits) is the methodology's ≥1M band, docked to 90 from the band's top because **no MRCR, RULER or AA-LCR retrieval curve is published** — the 8.88% tool-call error rate and the 29.03 s P95 / 90.19 s P99 end-to-end latencies are indirect evidence of long prompts being served, not of retrieval accuracy, and that distinction is stated because it is the difference between a measured property and an inferred one.
- **Multimodal: 82/100.** **Text, image and video in; text out** — the methodology's upper multimodal band on input breadth, scored at 82 rather than the top because **no MMMU-Pro, CharXiv, ExtractBench, MathVision, ZeroBench or DocVQA figure exists for this model**, so the modality claim is a spec claim with no measurement behind it, and there is no non-text output.
- **Coding: 40/100.** Below the methodology's mid band, and **entirely unmeasured by benchmark** — **no SWE-bench, LiveCodeBench, AA-SciCode, AA Coding Index, DeepSWE, Vibe Code Bench or Terminal-Bench figure exists.** The only structured-generation evidence is **AIIQ programmatic subscore 88 of 100 (composite IQ 94, #139/161)**, which is a composite proxy and explicitly not a repo-scale coding benchmark, and **Chess Puzzles 19.0% and Mystery Game Puzzles 15%/6.4%**, which are reasoning tasks rather than code tasks. Held at 40 rather than lower because the AIIQ proxy and the strong reasoning figures imply a real code-generation substrate; **not higher because a proxy is not a benchmark.**
- **Cost efficiency: 92/100.** **A flat $0.0282/$0.1128 per 1M** on models.dev and AIHubMix — the cheapest route in this research series — with **cache reads at $0.006 per 1M** and tiered QwenCloud pricing that keeps the <32K tier at $0.03/$0.13. The methodology's near-$0 anchor with a genuinely cheap metered route. Held at 92 rather than 100 because **$0.0282/$0.1128 is a real metered price and the model is closed-weights with no self-hosting escape** — the $0 route that lifts open-weight models does not exist here.
- **Overall Score: 67/100.** (62 + 62 + 90 + 82 + 40) / 5 = 67.2 → **67**. **A disagreement is recorded rather than reconciled: the repo queue carries 71.1 for this slug while this report reads 67**, and with BenchLM absent there is no third instrument to arbitrate — the queue figure is the corpus-average of other research agents' raters, this report is Step 5 Preview's own normalized reading, and both are stated side by side rather than flattened. Recommendation: **the cheap 1M-context multimodal model** — a native 1M window, text/image/video input, ObviousBench 91.0% agentic completion, GPQA ~80–82% and AIME ~87% at $0.0282/$0.1128 with $0.006 cache reads is a real proposition for high-volume long-context document and agent work. Three cautions: **its reasoning record is strong on saturating benchmarks and weak on sustained-search ones** (FrontierMath 19.3%, Chess 19.0%, Mystery Puzzles 6.4–15%), a 60-plus-point internal spread that is the file's most important finding; **coding is entirely unmeasured by benchmark** and its only proxy is a composite IQ score; and **the release date, the max-output limit and the reasoning figures all carry unresolved conflicts between independent trackers**, so the model is under-measured rather than well-measured.
