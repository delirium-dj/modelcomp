## Model card

- **Name:** DeepSeek V4 Flash. **Open weights, MIT licence.** **Critical variant/alias finding, confirmed by DeepSeek's own primary documentation: this model has been retired.** DeepSeek's Models & Pricing page states that **"the legacy names `deepseek-v4-flash` and `deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp` are still accepted, but the corresponding models have been retired, their requests are served by the DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash model and billed at the Flash price."** The ID still resolves; **what answers is DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash**, a newer and stronger model. The quality dimensions below are therefore assessed on DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash — not as a proxy, but because it is literally what serves the requests — and they carry the same values as my DeepSeek V4.1 Flash findings file in this research pass **except Multimodal**, which differs because this ID is documented text-only.
- **Short description:** Calling this ID today reaches a model placed **#23 of 882 at 67.81/100** by a composite tracker — DeepSeek's strongest Flash-tier release, and on this ID you get it **with the full 1,000,000-token window and 384K maximum output**, which the sibling `deepseek-v4-vision-exp` tier truncates to 200K. Its standout results are **Codeforces at 3,471 Elo**, the highest competitive-programming figure found anywhere in this research pass; **DeepSWE at 74.2%, clearing the methodology's 74%+ reference**; **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 90.6%, above the ~88% anchor**; **AA-LCR at 84.0%, the highest long-context reasoning figure in this entire pass**; and **AA AutomationBench at 68.9%, also pass-leading**. Two hard limits: **this ID is text-only**, and **the AA-Omniscience hallucination rate is 96.5% — the worst found anywhere in this research pass.**
- **Provider / access:** OpenCode Zen (paid), with **a separate free variant at `opencode/deepseek-v4-flash-free` ($0/$0, capped at 200K context, with rate-limit caveats)** per this repository's curated metadata. Underlying weights public at `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash` on Hugging Face with a technical report PDF. DeepSeek's own API serves the model as **`deepseek-flash`** on **both an OpenAI-format and an Anthropic-format base URL**, supporting **JSON output, tool calls, Responses API, Anthropic API and chat prefix completion**, with FIM completion in non-thinking mode only. **Thinking and non-thinking modes are both supported, with thinking as the default.**
- **Release / knowledge:** The originally-named checkpoint is retired; the serving model is DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash. **No verified release date or knowledge cutoff found for either.**
- **IDs:** `opencode/deepseek-v4-flash` (paid); `opencode/deepseek-v4-flash-free` (free, 200K cap); DeepSeek API legacy name `deepseek-v4-flash`, which **still resolves but routes to `deepseek-flash`**. **Call `deepseek-flash` directly for stability.**
- **Context window:** **1,000,000 tokens with 384K maximum output** — both confirmed on DeepSeek's own pricing page and consistent with this repository's curated metadata. **384K is the largest maximum-output figure found anywhere in this research pass.** The long-context evidence is pass-leading but internally contradictory: **AA-LCR at 84.0% is the highest long-context reasoning figure in this entire pass**, while **MLCR-AA at 22.8% sits 61.2 points below it** — the widest such divergence found here. The free variant caps context at 200K.
- **Modalities:** **Text in → text out. Text-only on this ID**, per this repository's curated metadata ("text-only on this ID"). **This is a product boundary, not a capability limit**: DeepSeek's own documentation lists **Vision: ✓** for the Flash tier, and the sibling `deepseek-v4-vision-exp` ID exposes image and PDF input against the same underlying model. If you need vision from DeepSeek, use that ID or `deepseek-flash` directly.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **approximately $0.14 / 1M input and $0.28 / 1M output on OpenCode Zen**, with a **$0 free variant capped at 200K context** (curated metadata). For reference, DeepSeek's own first-party Flash rates are **$0.15 / $0.60 off-peak and $0.30 / $1.20 peak**, with **cache hits at $0.003 / $0.006 — roughly one-fiftieth of cache-miss and the cheapest cache tier found anywhere in this research pass** — and peak covers only 01:00–04:00 and 06:00–10:00 UTC on weekdays excluding Chinese public holidays, so **off-peak is the normal case**. **Concurrency limit 2,500**, five times DeepSeek V4-Pro's 500. **Note the Zen output rate of $0.28 undercuts DeepSeek's own cheapest off-peak output rate of $0.60.**
- **Architecture:** **Open weights under MIT — the most permissive licence in this research pass. 284B total parameters with 13B active** (curated metadata), a very sparse MoE, which makes self-hosting genuinely tractable compared with GLM-5.3's 753B or DeepSeek V4-Pro's undisclosed scale.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

> **Tool use, Reasoning and Coding are identical to my DeepSeek V4.1 Flash findings in this research pass, because that model serves this ID. Multimodal differs because this ID is documented text-only; Cost efficiency differs because of the Zen rate card and free variant.**

- **Tool use: 86/100.** Category-leading in three places. **AA AutomationBench at 68.9% is the highest in this research pass**, **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 90.6% is above the ~88% anchor and the highest TB2.1 figure here**, and **Terminal-Bench 3.0 at 30% edges GLM-5.3's 28.3%**, so performance holds across benchmark generations better than most. **CyberGym at 88.1%** is second only to MiMo-V2.6-Flash, and **GDPval-AA at 1,600 Elo** beats DeepSeek's own V4-Pro, GPT-5.4, Muse Spark 1.1 and GLM-5.2. Four deductions: **the Terminal-Bench 2.1 vendor-to-independent gap is 16.1 points**; **GDPval remains 150 Elo below reference**; **ExploitGym (15.3%), Agents' Last Exam (31.8%) and GDP.pdf (12.8%) all fall away** on exploitation, long-horizon and document-grounded work; and **Claw-Eval, OSWorld, MCP-Atlas, τ²-bench and Toolathlon are unreported**, with the methodology explicitly penalising the Claw-Eval absence.
- **Reasoning: 74/100.** Three strong results against one disqualifying failure. **AA-LCR at 84.0% is the highest long-context reasoning figure in this entire research pass**, **GPQA Diamond at 90.9% clears the 90%+ reference**, and **Apex at 65.6% is remarkable** — DeepSeek's far larger V4-Pro manages 38.3%. Against that: **the 96.5% AA-Omniscience hallucination rate is the worst found anywhere in this pass**, and because accuracy is a respectable 46.4%, this is a **pure calibration failure** — it knows a great deal and asserts regardless, which makes it unusable as a source of truth without retrieval and verification. **Both HLE figures miss the 40%+ reference** (36.8%, 39.2%). **The Intelligence Index of 39.5% is 20.5 points below the 60+ marker.** **MLCR-AA at 22.8% sits 61.2 points below AA-LCR**, so the pass-leading long-context result does not generalise across harnesses. **ARC-AGI, FrontierMath and MMLU-Pro are entirely unmeasured**, and the GPQA figure is vendor-only with no independent corroboration.
- **Context window: 96/100. This ID's main advantage over its sibling tiers, and it earns above the tier floor.** **1,000,000 tokens with 384K maximum output**, both confirmed on DeepSeek's own pricing page — and **384K is the largest maximum-output figure found anywhere in this research pass**, which matters for whole-repository refactors and long document generation where other models force chunking. It earns above the ≥1M floor on the strength of **AA-LCR at 84.0%, the best long-context reasoning evidence any model in this pass provides**, plus three structural advantages: **open weights under MIT so no repricing threshold applies**, **cache hits at $0.003 per 1M making repeated long-context work essentially free**, and **a 2,500 concurrency limit** supporting long-context work at scale. It stops short of 100 for two reasons: the top condition requires **≥98% retrieval at 512K+** and **no MRCR, RULER, CorpusQA or GraphWalks figure exists**; and **MLCR-AA at 22.8% is 61.2 points below AA-LCR**, the widest divergence in this pass, so depth performance is severely harness-dependent rather than uniformly excellent.
- **Multimodal: 18/100. The score reflects the ID, not the model.** This ID is **documented text-only** by the repository's curated metadata, which caps it in the methodology's "text-only = 10–20" band regardless of underlying capability. It is placed **at the top of that band** for a specific reason that distinguishes it from genuinely text-only models such as GLM-5.3 or Qwen3.7-Max: **the limitation here is a pure product boundary, not a capability one.** DeepSeek's own documentation lists **Vision: ✓** for the Flash tier, and the same underlying model demonstrably handles images well — **BabyVision with Python at 89.6%, Chartography with tools at 78.9%, AA-MMMU-Pro at 77.0%** — it is simply not exposed on this ID. **If you need vision from DeepSeek, switch to `deepseek-v4-vision-exp` or call `deepseek-flash` directly; you lose nothing in text capability by doing so.** That makes this dimension a routing decision rather than a reason to reject the model, which is worth stating plainly because an 18/100 would otherwise look like a capability verdict.
- **Coding: 88/100.** The best value-for-capability coding profile in this research pass. **Codeforces at 3,471 Elo is the highest competitive-programming figure found anywhere here**, above DeepSeek's own V4-Pro at 3,206. **DeepSWE at 74.2% clears the methodology's 74%+ frontier reference** — one of very few models in this pass to manage it, and ahead of MiMo-V2.6-Flash, GPT-5.6 Luna, GLM-5.3 and GLM-5.3-Flash. **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 90.6% clears the 85%+ coding reference** comfortably, and **NL2Repo at 65.4% is the strongest in this pass**. Four deductions: **the Terminal-Bench figure carries the same 16.1-point Vals discount**; **AA-SciCode at 51.9% misses the 55%+ reference by 3.1 points**; **ProgramBench at 20.3%** indicates weak novel synthesis; and **no SWE-bench of any variant, no LiveCodeBench and no AA Coding Index exist**, so the dimension rests on competitive programming, terminal work, DeepSWE and NL2Repo rather than the most-cited repository benchmarks.
- **Cost efficiency: 96/100. Among the highest I have assigned in this research pass, and uniquely well-structured.** The Zen rate of **approximately $0.14 / $0.28 is roughly 21× cheaper on input and 54× cheaper on output than the methodology's $3/$15 anchor** — and notably **the $0.28 output rate undercuts DeepSeek's own cheapest first-party off-peak output rate of $0.60**, so the Zen tier is the better buy. A **$0 free variant exists at `opencode/deepseek-v4-flash-free`** (200K cap, rate-limit caveats), so there is a genuine zero-cost entry point. The first-party fallback is itself among the cheapest in existence: **$0.15 / $0.60 off-peak**, with **off-peak the normal case**, **cache hits at $0.003 per 1M — roughly one-fiftieth of cache-miss and the cheapest cache tier found anywhere in this pass**, and **2,500 concurrency**. Three structural advantages compound it: **MIT licence — the most permissive in this research pass**, so commercial self-hosting carries no legal friction; **284B total with only 13B active**, making self-hosting genuinely tractable where GLM-5.3's 753B is not; and **both OpenAI- and Anthropic-format endpoints** for near-zero migration cost. What it buys is **Codeforces 3,471, DeepSWE above reference, Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 90.6%, the pass-leading AA-LCR and AA AutomationBench, and a 1M window with 384K output.** Deductions: **this is a retired ID**, so the routing is a vendor convenience that could change without notice, and **the 96.5% hallucination rate imposes a verification cost** no rate card shows.
- **Overall Score: 72.4/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (86 + 74 + 96 + 18 + 88) / 5 = 72.4 — **and the mean is misleading here in a way worth stating directly: the text-only ID restriction costs roughly fourteen points of Overall, and it is a routing choice you can simply reverse.** Switch to `deepseek-v4-vision-exp` or call `deepseek-flash` directly and you get the same text capability plus a working vision path, because the underlying model has one. On text, this is among the best value propositions in this entire research pass. What holds up: **Codeforces at 3,471 Elo — the highest competitive-programming figure anywhere here**; **DeepSWE at 74.2%, clearing the frontier reference where most peers fail**; **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 90.6% above the agentic anchor, and 3.0 at 30% above GLM-5.3**; **AA AutomationBench at 68.9% and AA-LCR at 84.0%, both pass-leading**; **CyberGym at 88.1%**; **GPQA Diamond at 90.9%**; **Apex at 65.6%** where V4-Pro manages 38.3%; and **a full 1M window with the largest maximum output in this pass at 384K** — all at **~$0.14/$0.28, with a $0 variant, under MIT, at 284B/13B active.** Choose it for **high-volume agentic coding, competitive programming, terminal automation, security discovery and long-context text work at minimum cost.** Four cautions. **It hallucinates at 96.5% — the worst rate found anywhere in this research pass** — a calibration failure on a 46.4%-accurate knowledge base, so never use it as a source of truth. **Call `deepseek-flash` directly rather than this legacy alias**, which DeepSeek has retired and routes only as a courtesy. **The pass-leading long-context result does not generalise** — MLCR-AA sits 61.2 points below AA-LCR. And **reasoning has hard limits**: both HLE figures miss 40%, the Intelligence Index is 39.5%, GPQA is vendor-only, and ARC-AGI, FrontierMath and MMLU-Pro are entirely unmeasured.
