# GLM-5.3 Free — Independent Research Report

## Summary and evidence

GLM-5.3 Free is the free-access service tier of Z.ai's GLM-5.3 rather than different model weights. The underlying model is reported at **88.2 Terminal-Bench 2**, **66.9 DeepSWE**, **84.5 CyberGym**, **73.0 Toolathlon**, and **62.5 HLE with tools**.

Sources: [Z.ai GLM-5.3 announcement and benchmarks](https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.3)

## Cost and limitations

The free tier earns maximum direct-price efficiency, but quotas, availability, latency, and data handling can differ from paid service. Capability scores therefore track the base GLM-5.3 model while the cost score reflects this access tier.

## Scores

- **Tool use: 94/100.** Published agent and terminal evaluations show excellent tool execution.
- **Reasoning: 91/100.** Hard reasoning results remain frontier-class for the underlying weights.
- **Context window: 96/100.** The large supported window suits repository-scale work, subject to free-tier limits.
- **Multimodal: 73/100.** Multimodality is less central and less thoroughly evidenced than coding.
- **Coding: 95/100.** DeepSWE, Terminal-Bench, and CyberGym provide strong coding-agent evidence.
- **Cost efficiency: 100/100.** The service is free at the point of access, with quota caveats.
- **Overall Score: 90/100.** Half-up rounded mean: (94 + 91 + 96 + 73 + 95) / 5 = 89.8.

## Bottom line

GLM-5.3 Free offers the base model's strong coding ability at zero direct price when its service limits are acceptable.
