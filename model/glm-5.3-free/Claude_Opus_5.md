## Model card

- **Name:** GLM 5.3 Free. **Open weights.** **Critical variant/alias finding, which governs this entire report: this is not a distinct model — it is Z.AI's flagship GLM-5.3 served on OpenCode Zen's free promotional tier with a reduced context window.** The repository's own curated metadata identifies it as "Z.AI's flagship open-weights GLM-5.3 MoE model optimized for agentic software development, complex reasoning, and multi-step tool execution", with a **204K context window** (against GLM-5.3's native 1M) and a **"Free OpenCode Zen promotional tier… for fast agentic coding and tool calls"**. The weights, and therefore the capability, are GLM-5.3's. **Accordingly, four of the five quality dimensions below carry the same values as my GLM-5.3 findings file in this research pass; Context window and Cost efficiency differ, and those two differences are the entire point of this report.**
- **Short description:** The best free-tier model in this research pass, and by a wide margin. The underlying GLM-5.3 is placed **#20 of 784 at 68.61/100** by a composite tracker across 49 benchmarks, and Z.AI states plainly that **"GLM-5.3 uses the same base model as GLM-5.2 — every gain comes from post-training."** Three of its results are pass-leading: **GDPval-AA at 1,769 Elo clears the methodology's ~1750+ frontier reference**, **the AA Coding Index at 74.8% is the highest found in this research pass**, and **the AA-Omniscience Index of +14.3% on a 29.6% hallucination rate is the single most trustworthy reliability profile found anywhere here.** Getting that at **$0** is the finding. Two hard limits apply: **the model is text-only**, and **this tier exposes 204K of a 1M window — roughly a fifth of the model's actual capability.**
- **Provider / access:** **OpenCode Zen free promotional tier.** Underlying weights public at `zai-org/GLM-5.3` on Hugging Face under the `glm-5.3` licence — **1,489,168 downloads in the last month**, 8 finetunes, 72 community quantizations, with first-party deployment support for SGLang, vLLM, TokenSpeed, Transformers, KTransformers, Unsloth and **Ascend NPU**. Technical report: arXiv **2602.15763**, "GLM-5: from Vibe Coding to Agentic Engineering", published **2026-02-17**.
- **Release / knowledge:** Underlying paper published **2026-02-17**. **No verified knowledge cutoff found.** **No verified start or end date for the free promotional tier** — treat its availability as impermanent.
- **IDs:** `opencode/glm-5.3-free`; underlying weights `zai-org/GLM-5.3`. **This is itself the free-tier ID.**
- **Context window:** **204,000 tokens on this tier** — the single most important difference from the base model, and a **~5× reduction from GLM-5.3's native 1,000,000**. For reference, Z.AI's own evaluation footnotes ran NL2Repo, FrontierSWE, PostTrainBench, SWE-Marathon and Agents' Last Exam **at 1M context**, Terminal-Bench 3.0 and DeepSWE at 400K, and HLE-with-tools at 300K — **so every one of the headline agentic results below was produced at a context length this tier cannot reach.** That caveat is carried into the scores. **AA-LCR at 80.0%**… more precisely **79.7%** for GLM-5.3, against **MLCR-AA at 48.3%**.
- **Modalities:** **Text in → text out. Text-only**, confirmed three ways: the repository's curated metadata states "Text in/out"; the Hugging Face card carries `pipeline_tag: text-generation` with no vision tower in the `glm_moe_dsa` architecture; and Z.AI ships vision as a **separate** model (GLM-5V-Turbo). The corroborating negative is **GDP.pdf at 11.2%**. **Within-family note: GLM-5.3-Flash has verified video input (MMVU 80.5%) and excellent chart reasoning (CharXiv 89.4%), so for any image, video or document workload the weaker Flash model is the correct choice over this one.**
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **$0 on the OpenCode Zen free tier.** The weights are additionally free to self-host under the `glm-5.3` licence. Self-hosting cost is driven by size — **753B parameters** served as BF16 / F8_E4M3 / F32 — so "free" means free *as served here*, not cheap to replicate. Throughput reference: Z.AI's own ExploitGym footnote cites Artificial Analysis and rescales **GLM-5.3 at 115 tok/s**, against Kimi K3 at 40 and Qwen3.8-Max at 47. **No verified Z.AI paid API rate card was found**, so there is no priced fallback to quote if the promotional tier ends.
- **Architecture:** **Open weights, 753B parameters**, sparse MoE with DSA (`glm_moe_dsa`). Same base model as GLM-5.2; all improvement is post-training. Exposes `reasoning_effort` with `low` / `high` / `max`, **defaulting to `max`**, and a `clear_thinking` chat-template flag defaulting to `false`.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

> **Tool use, Reasoning, Multimodal and Coding are identical to my GLM-5.3 findings in this research pass, because the weights are identical. Context window and Cost efficiency differ, and those two differences define this tier.**

- **Tool use: 90/100.** The strongest agentic evidence base in this research pass. **GDPval-AA v2 at 1,769 Elo clears the ~1750+ frontier reference and beats all seven comparators in Z.AI's own table** including Claude Fable 5, Qwen3.8-Max and GPT-5.6 Sol. The **AA Agentic Index of 53.4% is the highest recorded here**, **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 88.2% sits on the ~88% anchor**, **Terminal-Bench 3.0 at 28.3% is the highest TB3 figure in this pass**, **AA Tau3 Banking at 50.3% clears its reference**, and **CyberGym at 84.5% beats every comparator**. Deductions: **the Terminal-Bench vendor-to-Vals spread is 16.7 points**; **exploitation collapses** — ExploitGym solves 130 of 869 tasks and ExploitBench reaches 54.4% against Claude Fable 5's 78.0%; **GDP.pdf at 11.2% is near-floor**; **Claw-Eval, OSWorld, MCP-Atlas, BrowseComp and τ²-bench are unreported**; and **every vendor figure assumes Claude Code at max reasoning effort with 6–10 hour task budgets at 300K–1M context — which this 204K tier cannot provide.**
- **Reasoning: 86/100.** Two frontier references cleared and one result unique in this dataset. **AA-GPQA Diamond at 91.7% clears the 90%+ reference**, **AA-HLE at 42.3% clears the 40%+ reference**, **AA-LCR reaches 79.7%**, **HLE-with-tools reaches 62.5%**, and **MMLU-Pro is 86.8%**. The standout is reliability: **the AA-Omniscience Index of +14.3% on a 29.6% hallucination rate is the only positive index and by far the lowest confabulation rate found anywhere in this research pass** — for a free tier, that is the most valuable property on this page, because it is the one thing you cannot compensate for with downstream tooling. Four deductions: the **Intelligence Index of 44.8% is 15.2 points below the 60+ marker**; **raw Omniscience accuracy is only 33.9%**, so excellent calibration comes with narrow breadth; **CritPt at 19.1% and MLCR-AA at 48.3% are mid-band**; and **neither ARC-AGI nor FrontierMath has any published figure**, leaving abstract reasoning and research mathematics wholly unmeasured. Competitor-reported Gray Swan IPI of 31.5% also suggests weak prompt-injection resistance.
- **Context window: 74/100. This is a full tier below the base model's 95, and the drop is the defining limitation of this tier.** **204,000 tokens** places it in the methodology's 200K–500K band (65–84) at essentially the 200K anchor of 70, and it is **a ~5× reduction from GLM-5.3's native 1,000,000**. It is set modestly above the anchor for one reason: **AA-LCR at 79.7% is strong**, so the window it does expose is demonstrably usable at depth, and being open weights there is no repricing threshold. Three reasons it is not higher. **MLCR-AA at 48.3% is 31.4 points below AA-LCR**, so depth performance is harness-dependent. **No MRCR, RULER, CorpusQA or GraphWalks figure exists.** And most importantly, **the reduction invalidates the provenance of the headline results**: Z.AI's own footnotes record NL2Repo, FrontierSWE, PostTrainBench, SWE-Marathon and Agents' Last Exam as run at 1M context, Terminal-Bench 3.0 and DeepSWE at 400K, and HLE-with-tools at 300K under explicit context management — **every one of those exceeds 204K**, so the agentic and coding figures quoted above were obtained under conditions this tier cannot reproduce. Expect real degradation on long-horizon repository work specifically.
- **Multimodal: 18/100.** **Text-only**, capped in the methodology's "text-only = 10–20" band regardless of other merit, and confirmed three independent ways: the repository's curated metadata ("Text in/out"), the Hugging Face `pipeline_tag: text-generation` with no vision tower in `glm_moe_dsa`, and Z.AI shipping vision as a **separate** GLM-5V-Turbo model. **No MMMU, CharXiv, document, OCR, video or audio benchmark exists**, and the corroborating negative is **GDP.pdf at 11.2%** — document-grounded work is near-floor. Placed near the top of the band because **Design Arena Website at 1,308 Elo** shows strong visual-artefact generation *through code*, the only visual capability it has; that figure is **explicitly excluded** from the input-modality assessment. **For image, video or document work, GLM-5.3-Flash is the correct choice despite being the weaker model overall.**
- **Coding: 90/100.** The best open-weight coding evidence in this research pass, and the strongest figures are independent. **SWE-bench at 95.4% from Vals AI** is second-highest recorded anywhere here; **the AA Coding Index at 74.8% clears the 70%+ reference and is the highest in this pass**; **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 88.2% clears the 85%+ coding reference**; and three further independent measurements cluster at 78–80% (**LiveCodeBench 80.5%, VulcanBench v3 78.3%, Proximal's FrontierSWE 78.1%**), with **AA-SciCode at 59.0% clearing its reference**. Four deductions: **DeepSWE at 66.9% is 7.1 points below the 74%+ reference**; **FrontierSWE falls 47.9 points from v1 to v2 (30.2%)**; **ProgramBench reaches only 19.0%** against Claude Fable 5's 33.0%, so genuinely novel synthesis is weak; and **SWE-bench Verified, SWE-bench Pro and CursorBench are unreported**. Note additionally that **Proximal ran FrontierSWE at 1M context** — above this tier's ceiling.
- **Cost efficiency: 95/100. The highest Cost efficiency score I have assigned in this research pass, and it is straightforward to justify: the tier costs $0 and the model behind it is frontier-grade.** For nothing you get **GDPval-AA at 1,769 Elo above the frontier reference**, **the highest AA Coding Index in this pass (74.8%)**, **the highest AA Agentic Index (53.4%)**, **SWE-bench at 95.4%**, **AA-GPQA Diamond at 91.7%**, **AA-HLE at 42.3%**, and **the only positive reliability index found anywhere here**. The underlying weights are additionally public, so there is a migration path that does not depend on the promotion continuing — with a genuinely production-ready ecosystem behind it (1.49M monthly downloads, 72 quantizations, SGLang/vLLM/Transformers/KTransformers/Unsloth plus Ascend NPU) and competitive throughput at **115 tok/s**, roughly 2.9× Kimi K3's 40. Three deductions keep it off 100. **The tier is explicitly promotional** with no verified start or end date, and **no Z.AI paid rate card was found**, so there is no priced fallback to plan against — a real continuity risk for anything production-bound. **The 204K window is a fifth of the model's capability**, so the free tier does not deliver the full asset. And **self-hosting as the escape route is expensive**: 753B parameters means roughly 750 GB even at FP8, i.e. multi-node or 8×H200-class hardware before the first token.
- **Overall Score: 71.6/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (90 + 86 + 74 + 18 + 90) / 5 = 71.6 — and the mean understates this tier badly for its intended use, because **two structural factors, not capability, account for roughly nineteen points**: text-only modality costs about fourteen points of Overall against a multimodal peer, and the 204K context ceiling costs about four more against the base model's 1M. **On text, at $0, nothing else in this research pass is close.** What holds up: **GDPval-AA 1,769 Elo clears the frontier reference and beats Claude Fable 5, Qwen3.8-Max and GPT-5.6 Sol**; **the AA Coding Index (74.8%) and AA Agentic Index (53.4%) are both the highest recorded here**; **SWE-bench 95.4% (Vals)**; **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 88.2% and 3.0 at 28.3%**; **CyberGym 84.5%**; **GPQA 91.7% and HLE 42.3%** both clearing reference; and **an AA-Omniscience Index of +14.3% on a 29.6% hallucination rate — the only positive reliability index in this entire pass**, which for a free tier is the single most valuable property it has. Choose it for **free agentic software development, repository coding, tool execution and text analysis where being wrong is worse than being unhelpful.** Four cautions. **It is text-only** — no image, video, document or audio input, GDP.pdf 11.2%, with GLM-5.3-Flash the correct pick for multimodal work. **The 204K ceiling invalidates the headline provenance** — Z.AI measured the agentic results at 300K–1M context inside Claude Code with 6–10 hour task budgets, so expect degradation on long-horizon work specifically. **The tier is promotional with no documented end date and no priced fallback**, so do not build production dependencies on it without a self-hosting plan. And **knowledge breadth is narrow** — 33.9% raw accuracy, a 44.8% Intelligence Index, and no ARC-AGI or FrontierMath measurement at all.
