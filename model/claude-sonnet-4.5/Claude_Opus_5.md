## Model card

- **Name:** Claude Sonnet 4.5. Proprietary, **Non-Reasoning**, `base` variant, with a **Claude Sonnet 4.5 Thinking** sibling tracked separately (composite not computed).
- **Short description:** Anthropic's **September 2025** Sonnet flagship — **the deepest legacy model assessed in this research pass.** A composite tracker places it **#99 of 882 at 48.57/100** on just **12 of 613 benchmarks**, and **twenty-one newer Anthropic models score above it**, including four later Sonnet generations: Sonnet 4.6 (55.29), Sonnet 5 (65.95) and Sonnet 5.5 (83.9). Its curated description — "best-in-class long-horizon coding agent and computer-use model of its generation; superseded by Sonnet 4.6" — is accurate in both halves, and the evidence supports the specific claim: **SWE-bench Verified at 77.2%** and **OSWorld-Verified at 61.4%** were genuinely strong for late 2025. But the generation has moved: **Terminal-Bench 2.0 at 50% is 38 points below the methodology's anchor**, **ARC-AGI-2 at 13.6% is among the lowest in this pass**, **FrontierMath v2 Tier 4 at 4.167%** is near-floor, and **GPQA at 83.4% misses the 90% reference by 6.6 points.** It still carries the **$3.00/$15.00** rate card that newer, better Anthropic models have since undercut.
- **Provider / access:** Claude API, and the usual Anthropic cloud surfaces. **Note a provenance quirk that shapes this whole report: seven of the twelve populated benchmark rows come from Anthropic's *Claude Opus 4.5 system card*** — that is, a later model's card reporting Sonnet 4.5 as a comparison baseline — rather than from Sonnet 4.5's own launch materials.
- **Release / knowledge:** **September 2025** (repository metadata). **No verified knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `claude-sonnet-4-5`; sibling `claude-sonnet-4-5-thinking`. **No free-tier ID.**
- **Context window:** **200,000 tokens standard with 64K maximum output, plus a 1,000,000-token beta gated to Tier 4+** (repository metadata). The 1M beta is real but **gated behind a spend tier**, so it is not generally available. **There is no long-context measurement of any kind** — no AA-LCR, MRCR, RULER, CorpusQA or GraphWalks figure exists.
- **Modalities:** **Text, image and file input → text output** (repository metadata). **The "file" element is asserted but unverified** — across all twelve populated benchmark slots there is **no document, OCR, chart or any other multimodal measurement**, so document handling cannot be confirmed and is not credited below.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **$3.00 / 1M input, $15.00 / 1M output, with cached input at $0.30** (repository metadata). This is **exactly the methodology's reference anchor**, with a 10× caching discount. Independently corroborated earlier in this research session by The AI Rankings' Gemini 3 Pro guide, which lists Claude Sonnet 4.5 at $3.00/$15.00.
- **Architecture:** Proprietary, closed weights. Parameter count and topology: not disclosed.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 58/100.** One result is genuinely creditable and was category-leading in its day: **OSWorld-Verified at 61.4%** substantiates the "best-in-class computer-use model of its generation" claim, and in September 2025 it was. The generation has since moved decisively — MiMo-V2.6-Flash and Muse Spark 1.1 both reach 80.8% on the same benchmark. Everything else is weak or absent. **Terminal-Bench 2.0 at 50% is 38 points below the ~88% anchor**, with **no 2.1, 3.0 or 4.0 figure** to update it. **VITA-Bench at 17.0% and JobBench at 27.7%** both collapse on long-horizon work. And **eight of the methodology's agentic reference benchmarks are entirely unreported** — Claw-Eval, GDPval-AA, MCP-Atlas, τ²-bench, BrowseComp, Toolathlon, CyberGym and the AA Agentic Index — with the methodology explicitly penalising the Claw-Eval absence. For a model sold on agentic capability, two usable datapoints is not an evidence base.
- **Reasoning: 48/100. The weakest reasoning assessment in this research pass, and both the measured and unmeasured evidence point the same way.** What is measured is poor: **GPQA at 83.4% misses the 90%+ reference by 6.6 points**; **ARC-AGI-2 at 13.6% is among the lowest in this pass**, and the next Sonnet generation improved more than fourfold on it; and **FrontierMath v2 at 13.495% / 4.167% is near-floor**, against GPT-5.6 Luna's 78.6% / 58.5%. Only **AIME 2025 at 87%** is respectable, and AIME is the most saturated of the competition-maths benchmarks. What is *unmeasured* compounds it: **no HLE, no MMLU-Pro, no AA-LCR, no CritPt, no Intelligence Index and no AA-IFBench**, so five of the dimension's principal references cannot be checked. Most consequentially, **there is no AA-Omniscience measurement at all**, which means **the hallucination rate and calibration of this model are completely unknown** — a figure that has ranged from 25.0% to 96.5% across the models assessed in this pass, and therefore the single largest unquantified deployment risk here.
- **Context window: 72/100.** **200,000 tokens standard with 64K maximum output** places it essentially at the methodology's 200K anchor of 70. It is set marginally above that for one reason: **a 1,000,000-token beta exists**, which is a genuine capability. Three reasons it goes no higher. **The 1M beta is gated to Tier 4+**, a spend threshold, so it is not generally available and cannot be assumed for most deployments. **There is no long-context measurement of any kind** — no AA-LCR, MRCR, RULER, CorpusQA or GraphWalks — so neither the 200K standard window nor the 1M beta has any demonstrated usable depth; by contrast Qwen3.7 Max publishes MRCRv2 at 90.4% and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash AA-LCR at 84.0%. And **the 64K output ceiling is small** against DeepSeek's 384K or the 128–131K common across this pass. For family context, **Claude Sonnet 5.5 offers 1M as standard at a lower price.**
- **Multimodal: 64/100.** The curated metadata records **text, image and file input**, and **file input would ordinarily justify the methodology's "+video/PDF in = 75–90" band — but I decline to award it here, and the reason is specific.** Across **all twelve populated benchmark slots there is not one multimodal measurement of any kind**: no MMMU, MMMU-Pro, CharXiv, document, OCR, video or audio figure. The "file" capability is therefore asserted by metadata and corroborated by nothing, so the model is placed in the **"+image in = 60–70" band** instead — image input being the only non-text modality I can treat as certain for this generation. Within that band it sits **below Claude Sonnet 4.6's 68**, deliberately: the successor has actual measurements (CharXiv 77.4%, AA-MMMU-Pro 70.6%) where this model has none, and a model with zero measured visual breadth should not score level with one that has demonstrated it. **Design Arena Website at 1,196 Elo is excluded** as an output-quality measure — and is in any case the lowest such figure in this research pass.
- **Coding: 68/100.** One strong result carries the entire dimension, and it carries it further than expected. **SWE-bench Verified at 77.2%** is genuinely good and substantiates the "best-in-class long-horizon coding agent of its generation" claim — and notably, **it has aged well in relative terms: Claude Sonnet 4.6 reaches only 79.6% vendor / 77.4% independent on the same benchmark**, so a full Sonnet generation added roughly two points. On repository patching specifically, this model remains competitive with its direct successor. Everything else is missing or poor. **Terminal-Bench 2.0 at 50% is 35 points below the 85%+ coding reference**, ruling out terminal-driven agentic coding. And **nine of the dimension's reference benchmarks are unreported** — LiveCodeBench, DeepSWE, SWE-bench Pro, SWE Multilingual, the AA Coding Index, SciCode, CursorBench, Codeforces and Vibe Code Bench — so the dimension rests on a single datapoint plus one bad one. The score credits the SWE-bench result properly while reflecting that nothing else supports it.
- **Cost efficiency: 30/100.** **The rate card sits exactly on the methodology's $3.00/$15.00 reference anchor**, which maps to roughly 60, with a useful 10× caching discount at $0.30 and standard Anthropic mitigations. The score is halved from there because **it is comprehensively dominated inside Anthropic's own lineup, on both price and capability simultaneously.** **Claude Sonnet 5.5 lists at $2/$10 — a third cheaper — and scores 83.9 against this model's 48.57**, a 35.3-point composite gap, while also offering **1M context as standard** rather than a Tier-4-gated beta. **Claude Sonnet 5 also lists at $2/$10 and scores 65.95.** Even **Claude Sonnet 4.6, at this same $3/$15, scores 55.29**. In total **twenty-one newer Anthropic models rank above it**. There is no workload and no budget for which paying the anchor rate for a September 2025 Non-Reasoning model is defensible when the same vendor sells a cheaper model that scores 35 points higher with five times the standard context. The score is not lower only because the rate card is not itself unreasonable — the problem is entirely relative.
- **Overall Score: 62.0/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (58 + 48 + 72 + 64 + 68) / 5 = 62.0 — a historically important model with one durable strength and no remaining commercial case. The curated description is accurate on both counts, and the evidence confirms the specific claim: **SWE-bench Verified at 77.2% was best-in-class for September 2025, and it has aged remarkably well — Claude Sonnet 4.6 gained only about two points on it across a full generation.** **OSWorld-Verified at 61.4%** likewise justified the computer-use positioning at the time. **AIME 2025 at 87%** is respectable. If your acceptance suite is pinned to this exact model for repository patching inside 200K tokens, it remains competent at that one thing. Four findings rule it out otherwise. **The evidence base has collapsed to 12 of 613 benchmark slots** — eight agentic references, nine coding references and five reasoning references are simply unreported, and **with no AA-Omniscience measurement the hallucination rate is completely unknown**, which is the largest unquantified risk in this report. **Reasoning is weak where measured**: GPQA misses 90% by 6.6 points, ARC-AGI-2 is 13.6%, and FrontierMath v2 Tier 4 is 4.167%. **Agentic capability does not reach current anchors**: Terminal-Bench 2.0 at 50% is 38 points short, with no newer version measured. And **it is comprehensively superseded**: twenty-one newer Anthropic models score higher, and **Claude Sonnet 5.5 costs a third less, scores 35.3 composite points higher, and gives you 1M context as standard.**
