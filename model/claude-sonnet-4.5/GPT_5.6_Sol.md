# Claude Sonnet 4.5 — Independent Research Report

## Summary and evidence

Claude Sonnet 4.5 is Anthropic's September 2025 coding, agent, and computer-use model. It accepts text and images, supports extended thinking and agent memory/context editing, and offers 200K standard context with a one-million-token configuration. Anthropic reports **77.2% SWE-bench Verified** (82.0% with high compute) and **61.4% OSWorld-Verified**, plus sustained focus beyond 30 hours.

Source: [Anthropic Sonnet 4.5 launch](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-4-5)

## Cost and limitations

Standard pricing is **$3 input and $15 output per million tokens**. Long-context operation is more expensive, and the model is now legacy behind Sonnet 4.6 and 5.x. Audio and video are unsupported.

## Scores

- **Tool use: 91/100.** Computer use, memory, context editing, and Agent SDK support are excellent.
- **Reasoning: 87/100.** Strong professional reasoning, now behind newer Claude generations.
- **Context window: 91/100.** 200K standard and optional 1M provide flexibility, with cost tradeoffs.
- **Multimodal: 87/100.** Vision and computer use are strong; audio/video are absent.
- **Coding: 91/100.** SWE-bench and long-running coding were standout strengths.
- **Cost efficiency: 81/100.** Capability is good, but $15 output is less competitive now.
- **Overall Score: 89/100.** Half-up rounded mean: (91 + 87 + 91 + 87 + 91) / 5 = 89.4.

## Bottom line

Sonnet 4.5 remains a strong established coding agent, though current Claude releases improve context, performance, and value.
