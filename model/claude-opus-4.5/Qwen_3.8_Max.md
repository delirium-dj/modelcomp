## Model card

- **Name:** Claude Opus 4.5 (this dataset maps to Artificial Analysis's **"Claude Opus 4.5 (Reasoning/Thinking)"**; a **non-reasoning variant** also exists and scored separately at launch).
- **Short description:** **Anthropic's November-2025 flagship that "carried its agentic-coding reputation into the mainstream" — at launch the #2 most intelligent model in the AA Intelligence Index (70, Thinking), narrowly behind Gemini 3 Pro (73) and tying GPT-5.1 (high), with state-of-the-art coding.** Its launch credentials were **genuinely elite and independently verified**: **official SWE-bench Verified 80.9% (max effort)**, **Vals AI ranked it #1 on SWE-bench Verified** (ahead of Gemini 3 Pro and GPT-5.1-Codex), **highest Terminal-Bench Hard of any model (44%)**, **MMLU-Pro 90%** (ties Gemini 3 Pro), **AA-Omniscience #2**, and best-in-class **token efficiency** (48M Index tokens vs Gemini 3 Pro's 92M). ⚠ **Now deprecated and heavily superseded** — AA flags "**Anthropic has launched a newer release, Claude Opus 4.6**," and it has since been overtaken by **five successors (Opus 4.6 → 4.7 → 4.8 → 5 → 5.5)**. Its **current AA Intelligence Index v4.3.2 = 29 (an *estimate*, #98/215, barely above the median of 27)** — a steep rebasing from the launch-era 70. The meta's "three successors" is understated (it is five), and its "SWE-bench Verified 76.8%" is the **medium-effort** figure (the headline max-effort score is **80.9%**).
- **Provider / access:** **Anthropic** — first-party API, plus **Google Vertex, Amazon Bedrock and Microsoft Azure**, and via the **Claude app and Claude Code**. **Proprietary** (parameters undisclosed). `noFreeId: true`. Listed here as `anthropic/claude-opus-4.5`.
- **Release / knowledge:** **2025-11-24.** ⚠ **Deprecated** — AA only continues benchmarking the **default 10k-input workload**; all other results are historical. **Knowledge cutoff: Aug 1, 2025.**
- **IDs:** `claude-opus-4-5` / `claude-opus-4-5-thinking` (Anthropic API + AA slug) / `anthropic/claude-opus-4.5` (this dataset).
- **Context window:** **200,000 total (64,000 max output).** *Verified against the meta's "200K," AA ("200k, ~300 A4 pages") and the launch article ("200K tokens / 64K max output").* This was "the then-standard window" at launch but is now a **dated, sub-256K limit** — the meta's own primary tradeoff — against a frontier that has moved to 1M.
- **Modalities:** **Text + image in → text out.** *Verified against the meta and AA ("supports text and image input, outputs text").* Reasoning: **yes** (extended thinking; a separate non-reasoning variant scored AA Index 60 at launch, then the **#1 non-reasoning model**). **No native video or audio.**
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-25):** **$5.00 input / $25.00 output per 1M** (cache discount 90%). *Verified against the meta and AA.* A **dramatic cut from Opus 4.1's $15/$75**, moving it closer to Sonnet 4.5 ($3/$15) — but AA still rates both input (median $2) and output (median $10) as "**expensive**" and the model "**particularly expensive when comparing to other models of similar price**." Cost per Index task is now **N/A** (deprecated); at launch it cost ~**$1.5k** to run the Index (down from Opus 4.1's $3.1k).
- **Architecture:** **Proprietary; parameter count undisclosed.** A **thinking/reasoning** model with an **adjustable effort parameter** (medium matches Sonnet 4.5's best SWE-bench while using **76% fewer output tokens**; max reaches 80.9%). Signature trait: **token efficiency** — it "significantly increased intelligence without a large increase in output tokens," sitting on the **Pareto frontier** of the Output-Tokens-vs-Intelligence-Index chart, unlike peers that lean on heavier inference-time reasoning.

### Raw benchmarks found

Opus 4.5 is a **launch-peak-vs-current-rebased** case: an independently-verified #2 flagship in Nov 2025 that five successors and an Index rebasing (v4.0-era → v4.3.2) have since overtaken. I separate the two eras explicitly.

**Launch-era independent — Artificial Analysis (Nov 25, 2025):**

- **AA Intelligence Index = 70 (Thinking), #2 most intelligent** — behind Gemini 3 Pro (73), tying GPT-5.1 high (70), ahead of Kimi K2 Thinking (67) and Grok 4 (65); **+7 over Sonnet 4.5 (Thinking), +11 over Opus 4.1**. **Non-reasoning mode = 60, the #1 non-reasoning model** (ahead of Qwen3 Max 55).
- **Terminal-Bench Hard 44% — the highest of ANY model** at launch · **MMLU-Pro 90% (ties Gemini 3 Pro)** · **CritPt 5%** (frontier physics; behind Gemini 3 Pro's 9%).
- **AA-Omniscience Index #2 (score 10)** — behind Gemini 3 Pro Preview (13); **accuracy 43% (2nd-highest), hallucination rate 58% (4th-lowest)**.
- Largest uplifts over Sonnet 4.5 (Thinking): **LiveCodeBench +16pp, Terminal-Bench Hard +11pp, τ²-Bench Telecom +12pp, AA-LCR +8pp, HLE +11pp** — "**Anthropic's best scores yet across all 10 benchmarks**."
- **Token efficiency: 48M output tokens** to run the Index (vs Gemini 3 Pro high 92M, GPT-5.1 high 81M, Grok 4 Reasoning 120M), though ~50% more than Opus 4.1's 30M.

**Launch-era independent — Vals AI + swebench.com + METR:**

- **Vals AI SWE-bench Verified: #1 at launch** (ahead of Gemini 3 Pro and GPT-5.1-Codex). Banded row (Thinking): **88 / 75 / 40 / 0** across <15min / 15m–1h / 1–4h / **>4h** — strong short/mid, but a **long-horizon collapse to 0% on >4h tasks**.
- **swebench.com official (mini-SWE-agent): 74.4%** resolved — the neutral-scaffold number (vs Anthropic's own 80.9%).
- **METR long-horizon autonomy: ~320 minutes** (≈5.3h task horizon; vs GPT-5's 214) — a strong long-horizon result *for late 2025* (measured differently from the Vals >4h SWE band).

**Current — Artificial Analysis (v4.3.2, deprecated):**

- **AA Intelligence Index v4.3.2 = 29 (Estimated), #98/215** — "above average among comparable models (median: 27)"; only the 10k workload is still benchmarked, the rest estimated. A steep drop from the launch 70 under the harder 10-eval basket.
- **Speed 53.7 tok/s (#150/186) — "notably slow"** (avg 82). **Cost per Index task N/A; Verbosity N/A** (deprecated). Input/output both flagged "expensive."

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 81/100.** Launch agentic was **verified-elite**: **Terminal-Bench Hard 44% (highest of any model)**, **τ²-Bench Telecom +12pp** over Sonnet 4.5, **METR long-horizon ~320 min**, and the #2 AA Index (agentic-heavy basket). Held to **81** (not the 84+ current-agentic-leader band) because it is **deprecated with five successors** that far exceed it, AA now benchmarks only the 10k workload, and the **Vals >4h SWE band is 0%** — so sustained long-horizon agentic work is its weak edge. Above gpt-5.1 (80) for the stronger verified launch agentic.
- **Reasoning: 79/100.** Launch reasoning was **independently #2** (AA Index 70; **MMLU-Pro 90%** tying Gemini 3 Pro, **AA-Omniscience #2** with 43% accuracy / 58% hallucination, **CritPt 5%**, HLE +11pp). But the **current v4.3.2 Index estimate is 29 (#98/215, barely above the median 27)** and it is deprecated. Scored **79** — a notch above gpt-5/5.1 (78) because its current Index (29) is *above* median (theirs were below) and its launch reasoning was verified-#2, but capped by the rebasing, deprecation, and five successors.
- **Context window: 76/100.** **200,000 total (64K max output)** — verified, and "the then-standard window" at launch, but now a **dated sub-256K limit** that the meta itself flags as the primary tradeoff. Scored **76**, below the 256K band (78) and well below the 400K (80) and 1M (85) tiers that now define agentic work — a real constraint for the "dependable multi-step work" it is touted for.
- **Multimodal: 77/100.** **Text + image in → text out** (image-only), with capable but **unscored-here** vision (no MMMU/multimodal benchmark surfaced in this research). **No native video or audio.** Scored **77** — the standard image-only tier, a touch below the 78 given to image+file models with a verified visual-reasoning score (GPT-5 MMMU 81.5%, Claude Haiku 5.5 Chartography 46.4%).
- **Coding: 81/100.** The **defining strength, independently verified at launch**: **Vals SWE-bench #1** (ahead of Gemini 3 Pro and GPT-5.1-Codex), **official SWE-bench Verified 80.9% (max)**, **Terminal-Bench Hard 44% (#1)**, **LiveCodeBench +16pp**, with the neutral mini-SWE-agent at 74.4%. Held to **81** (below the current elite-Vals 86–88 band) because it is **deprecated**, **five successors** now reach far higher (Opus 5 ~97% Vals SWE-bench), and the **Vals >4h band is 0%**. Above gpt-5.1 (79) and gpt-5 (77) for the genuinely Vals-verified #1 launch coding.
- **Cost efficiency: 76/100.** **$5/$25 per 1M** was a **3× cut from Opus 4.1** ($15/$75) and it is **token-efficient** (48M Index tokens, Pareto frontier; Index run cost fell $3.1k→$1.5k). But AA rates both input and output "**expensive**" (medians $2/$10) and the model "**particularly expensive**" for its price class, and cost-per-task is now **N/A** (deprecated). Scored **76** — premium per-token pricing only partly redeemed by real token efficiency. (Not counted in Overall.)
- **Overall Score: 79/100.** Mean of the five quality dimensions = (81 Tool + 79 Reasoning + 76 Context + 77 Multimodal + 81 Coding) / 5 = **394/5 = 78.8 → 79** (Cost efficiency 76 is scored but **not** counted, per the v4 formula). **Divergence note:** my independent read is **79 vs the queue's curated 82.8 → −3.8**, consistent with the other **launch-peak-vs-current-rebased** deprecated flagships (gpt-5.2 −2.8, grok-4.3 −4.2, gpt-5.1 −4.9, gpt-5 −5.8). The curated **82.8** captures Opus 4.5's **Nov-2025 standing as the #2 model and a Vals-#1 coding champion**; my **79** reflects the **current reality** — **deprecated, five successors deep, a rebased AA Index estimate of 29 (#98/215, barely above median), a dated 200K window, slow (53.7 tok/s) and expensive ($5/$25)**. Its genuinely elite, *independently verified* launch agentic/coding (Terminal-Bench Hard #1, Vals SWE-bench #1, 80.9% official) keep it at a solid **79** — level with gpt-5.1, whose weaker verified coding is offset by a larger 400K window. Best fit today: **a legacy reproducibility pick** (the meta's own words) for teams needing deterministic parity with late-2025 Anthropic agentic-coding behavior — not new deployments, which should use Opus 5/5.5 or a current budget model.
