# Claude Opus 4.5 — Independent Research Report

## Summary and evidence

Claude Opus 4.5 is Anthropic's November 2025 high-end reasoning, coding, and computer-use model. It accepts text and images, offers extended thinking and effort control, and has **200K context** with **64K output**. Anthropic reports **80.6% SWE-bench Verified**, **51.6% SWE-bench Pro**, **76.2% SWE-bench Multilingual**, and **66.26% OSWorld**.

Sources: [Anthropic launch](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-5), [Opus 4.5 system card](https://assets.anthropic.com/m/64823ba7485345a7/Claude-Opus-4-5-System-Card.pdf), [model documentation](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/models/overview)

## Cost and limitations

Pricing is **$5 input and $25 output per million tokens**, with $0.50 cache reads and a 50% Batch discount. The model is now legacy; its 200K window, cost, and benchmark results trail newer Opus generations.

## Scores

- **Tool use: 91/100.** Computer use, advanced tools, memory, and subagent coordination remain strong.
- **Reasoning: 90/100.** Extended thinking is capable but several generations behind today's frontier.
- **Context window: 84/100.** 200K is practical, though far below current million-token models.
- **Multimodal: 88/100.** Vision and computer use are strong; audio and video are absent.
- **Coding: 91/100.** Its SWE-bench results remain excellent despite its age.
- **Cost efficiency: 72/100.** $25 output is costly for a legacy model.
- **Overall Score: 89/100.** Half-up rounded mean: (91 + 90 + 84 + 88 + 91) / 5 = 88.8.

## Bottom line

Opus 4.5 remains capable for established integrations, but newer Claude models offer better context, performance, and value.
