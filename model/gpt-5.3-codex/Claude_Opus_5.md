## Model card

- **Name:** GPT-5.3 Codex. Proprietary. Variant/alias flag: **a coding-specialist line, not a general-purpose flagship** — siblings in the Codex family include **GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark** (a separate entry in this dataset), **GPT-5.2-Codex**, **GPT-5.1-Codex** and **GPT-5.1-Codex-Max**. Do not read Codex figures as GPT-5.3 general-model figures; OpenAI published a dedicated **GPT-5.3-Codex System Card** for this model.
- **Short description:** OpenAI's Codex-line coding model. It does what a coding specialist should: **Vibe Code Bench at 61.77% is the highest figure recorded anywhere in this research pass** on Vals AI's real-world build benchmark, with SWE-bench Verified at 85% and LiveCodeBench at 87.3%. The trade-off is severe and quantified: **an AA-Omniscience hallucination rate of 89.2% — the second-worst in this entire dataset**, behind only Gemini 3 Pro's 88%… in fact exceeding it, making this the **highest confabulation rate recorded here**. It is a build tool, not a source of truth.
- **Provider / access:** OpenAI API and the Codex product surface; OpenRouter (`openai/gpt-5.3-codex`). Reasoning: explicit reasoning mode.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released in the GPT-5.3 cycle. **No verified public release date or knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `gpt-5.3-codex`; `openai/gpt-5.3-codex` (OpenRouter). Sibling: `gpt-5.3-codex-spark`. **No free-tier ID.**
- **Context window:** **400,000 tokens** — the same class as GPT-5.2, and notably **larger than the 128K–200K that GPT-5 and GPT-5.1 offered**, though well short of the 1.05M of the GPT-5.6 and GPT-6 flagships. Max output: no verified figure found. **AA-LCR at 83.3% is among the highest long-context reasoning figures in this research pass**, which matters for a model meant to hold whole repositories.
- **Modalities:** **Text and image input → text output.** A published AA-MMMU-Pro figure confirms the vision path. No audio or video.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **No rate card was verified for this model.** For family context, OpenAI's documented Codex-adjacent rates in this generation ran from GPT-5.4 at $2.50/$15 to GPT-5.5 at $5/$30; the Codex line is typically priced near the base model of its generation. **Re-verify against OpenAI's live pricing page before budgeting.**
- **Architecture:** Proprietary, closed weights. Parameter count and topology: not disclosed.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 76/100.** **τ²-bench at 86% clears the methodology's Tau-family ~50%+ frontier reference** and **Terminal-Bench 2.0 at 77.3%** sits in the upper mid band, with OSWorld-Verified at 64.7% and Gert Labs at 57.47% adding mid-field corroboration. Four deductions: **no Terminal-Bench 2.1 or later figure exists**, so the ~88% anchor cannot be tested and the 2.0 result cannot be compared with the current generation; **GDPval-AA, Tau3-Banking, Claw-Eval, MCP-Atlas and Toolathlon are all unreported** — five of the methodology's six references with no value, triggering the explicit penalty; **JobBench at 33.7%** is weak on real occupational tasks; and most practically, the **89.2% hallucination rate** makes unsupervised agentic operation hazardous, because a model that confabulates nine times in ten when wrong will confidently invent library names, API signatures and file paths inside a tool loop.
- **Reasoning: 76/100.** Three references are met and one is notably strong for a coding specialist: **AA-GPQA Diamond at 91.5% clears the 90%+ frontier reference**, **AA-HLE at 42.5% clears the 40%+ reference**, **AA-LCR at 83.3% is among the highest long-context reasoning figures in this entire research pass**, and AA-IFBench at 75.4% indicates reliable instruction following. Against that: **the Intelligence Index of 32.5% is mid-band**, 27 points below the frontier marker; **CritPt at 16.9%** is mid-range; **MRCR, RULER and GraphWalks are all unreported**; and the decisive deduction is **an 89.2% AA-Omniscience hallucination rate — the highest recorded in this dataset.** Accuracy of 52.9% is respectable, so the problem is not ignorance but calibration: it answers confidently regardless of whether it knows. For a model whose output is executed rather than read, that is a serious property.
- **Context window: 82/100.** **400,000 tokens** places it in the methodology's 200K–500K tier (65–84), and it is scored near the band ceiling because **AA-LCR at 83.3% is among the strongest long-context reasoning measurements in this research pass** — genuinely good evidence that the window is usable, which matters disproportionately for a model expected to hold entire repositories in context. Two things prevent a higher placement: the tier is size-defined and 400K is well short of the 1.05M that OpenAI's own GPT-5.6 and GPT-6 flagships offer; and **no MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks retrieval figure exists**, with max output tokens also undocumented.
- **Multimodal: 67/100.** Input is **text and image** with text-only output and no audio or video, placing it in the methodology's "+image in = 60–70" band. **AA-MMMU-Pro at 78.5% is respectable** and ahead of several general-purpose peers here. The deduction that keeps it mid-band rather than at the ceiling is specific and relevant to its purpose: **Design Arena Website at 1,169 Elo is the lowest figure recorded in this research pass** — for a coding model whose output is frequently user interfaces, a last-place generated-design score is a material weakness, and it echoes the "card problem" documented for GPT-5.5. **No CharXiv, document, OCR, video or audio benchmark exists**, so chart and document comprehension — directly useful when reading specifications and design files — is unmeasured.
- **Coding: 88/100.** The dimension this model exists for, and the headline result is the right one: **Vibe Code Bench at 61.77% from Vals AI is the highest figure recorded anywhere in this research pass** on a benchmark that measures building real applications rather than patching isolated issues — which is precisely what a Codex-line model is for. **SWE-bench Verified at 85%** is strong, **LiveCodeBench at 87.3% is independently measured**, and SWE-Rebench (58.2%) adds a third independent datapoint. Four deductions: **SWE-bench Verified falls to 78.0% under Vals AI's harness**, a 7-point independent trim; **SWE-bench Pro at 56.8% is mid-field** and 12.4 points behind Claude Opus 4.8; **Terminal-Bench 2.0 at 77.3% is below the methodology's 85%+ coding reference** with no newer version measured; and **DeepSWE, SciCode and the AA Coding Index are all absent** — which for a dedicated coding model is a conspicuous gap, leaving the methodology's primary agentic-coding reference unchecked.
- **Cost efficiency: 62/100.** **This score carries an explicit caveat: no rate card was verified for this model.** It rests on family positioning — OpenAI's documented rates in this generation ran from GPT-5.4 at $2.50/$15 to GPT-5.5 at $5/$30, and Codex-line models are typically priced near the base model of their generation, implying something in the $2.50–$5 input range. On that assumption it sits around or slightly worse than the $3/$15 anchor. Three deductions: the **price is unconfirmed** and must be re-verified; **no free tier** exists; and the capability-per-dollar case is weak against its own family — a composite tracker places it **#46 of 783**, behind eleven later OpenAI models, and **GPT-5.6 Terra at a documented $2/$12 offers a 1.05M context window, six reasoning-effort levels including `none`, the full agentic tool surface and a higher composite score (73.21 against 62.49)**. The one genuine counterargument is specialisation: Terra has no Vibe Code Bench figure, and 61.77% on real application builds is not something a general model has demonstrated.
- **Overall Score: 77.8/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (76 + 76 + 82 + 67 + 88) / 5 = 77.8 — a genuine coding specialist with one property that must shape how it is deployed. Choose it for **building real applications**, where **Vibe Code Bench at 61.77% is the best figure in this entire research pass** and no general-purpose model has matched it; for **algorithmic coding** (LiveCodeBench 87.3%, independently measured); for **repository-scale work within 400K tokens**, where AA-LCR at 83.3% is among the best long-context reasoning results here; and where **GPQA Diamond at 91.5% and HLE at 42.5%** show it has not traded away general reasoning to specialise. Do not treat its output as trustworthy without verification: **an 89.2% AA-Omniscience hallucination rate is the highest recorded in this dataset**, and at 52.9% accuracy that means it answers confidently whether or not it knows — hallucinated library names, API signatures and file paths are the predictable failure mode, and they are exactly the failures that pass review and break in production. Do not choose it for UI-first work (**Design Arena Website 1,169 Elo, the lowest recorded here**), for unsupervised agentic operation (five of six tool-use references unreported, JobBench 33.7%), or for contexts beyond 400K. And confirm the price, because the whole value comparison against GPT-5.6 Terra at a documented $2/$12 turns on it.
