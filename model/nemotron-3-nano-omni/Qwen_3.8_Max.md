# Nemotron 3 Nano Omni 30B-A3B — findings by Qwen 3.8 Max

- Source: NVIDIA (`nemotron-3-nano-omni`; id `nvidia/nemotron-3-nano-omni-30b-a3b-reasoning`)
- Date: 2026-10-28 (UTC)
- Overview and scoring methodology: `../../model-comparison.md`
- Cross-model signed log: `../../model-findings.md`

> **Verdict:** Nemotron 3 Nano Omni is **NVIDIA's current (April 2026) open-weight omni-modal reasoning model** — a genuine **text + image + video + audio → text** system built as the "perception and context sub-agent" for enterprise agent stacks. It pairs the **Nemotron 3 Nano 30B-A3B** hybrid **Mamba-Transformer-MoE** backbone (23 Mamba SSM layers + 23 MoE layers of 128 experts/top-6 + 6 grouped-query-attention layers; **~30B total / 3B active**) with a **C-RADIOv4-H** dynamic-resolution vision encoder and a **Parakeet-TDT-0.6B-v2** native-audio encoder, extending context from its predecessor's 128K to **256K/262,144** (65,536 out; **100+ page documents**, **5+ hours of audio**). Its standout is **breadth + efficiency**: on NVIDIA's own tables it **leads open omni peer Qwen3-Omni** on documents (MMLongBench-Doc 57.5 vs 49.5), GUI/agentic computer use (**OSWorld 47.4 vs 29.0**, ScreenSpot-Pro 57.8), video (Video-MME 72.2), audio (VoiceBench 89.4, OpenASR WER 5.95) and is the **most cost-efficient open video model on MediaPerf** (9× throughput/GPU). But the evidence is **entirely vendor-run** — **Artificial Analysis has no page for the *Omni* (404)** and there is **no independent harness re-run**; the widely-quoted **AA Intelligence Index 9 is the *separate text-only base* Nemotron 3 Nano 30B-A3B, NOT this Omni** (reattribution guard), and the Omni's own text scores actually trail that base (AIME25 82.1 vs 89.1; LiveCodeBench 63.2 vs 68.3). Coding is thin (no SWE-bench/Terminal-Bench). It is **free** on NVIDIA Build + OpenRouter and open-weight. Net: **Overall 78 (divergence +5.6 vs the curated 72.4)** — a best-in-class *open* omni perception reasoner whose nano-class text/coding ceiling and vendor-only evidence keep it out of the frontier tier; my +5.6 credits its four-modality breadth and verified multimodal leadership above the curated mean.

## Model card

- **Name:** Nemotron 3 Nano Omni 30B-A3B (Reasoning) — vendor **NVIDIA**; successor to **Nemotron Nano V2 VL** (12B), built on the **Nemotron 3 Nano 30B-A3B** text backbone.
- **Short description:** Open **omni-modal understanding** model unifying **video, audio, image and text** for enterprise document analysis, long audio-video reasoning, ASR, **agentic computer use (GUI)** and general multimodal reasoning. Ships **BF16 / FP8 / NVFP4** checkpoints.
- **Provider / access:** **Open-weight** (downloadable checkpoints on Hugging Face; NVIDIA open-model licensing). Hosted **free** on **NVIDIA Build** and the **OpenRouter `:free`** route; paid ~**$0.20/$0.80** per 1M (DeepInfra). Supports `tools`/`tool_choice`/`reasoning` toggles. **Not on OpenCode Zen** (`noFreeId: true` refers to the free *Zen* slot, not the free Build/OpenRouter routes). No EU in-region routing.
- **Release / knowledge:** **April 2026** (arXiv 2604.24954 dated 2026-04-27; NVIDIA HF blog 2026-04-28). **Current.**
- **IDs:** `nvidia/nemotron-3-nano-omni-30b-a3b-reasoning`; HF `nvidia/Nemotron-3-Nano-Omni-30B-A3B-Reasoning-{BF16,FP8,NVFP4}`.
- **Context window:** **256,000–262,144 total / 65,536 max output** (NVIDIA states both; extended from the predecessor's 128K). Handles **100+ page documents** and **5+ hours of audio**; audio trained 0.5 s–20 min per clip.
- **Modalities:** **text, image, video, audio in → text out.** True omni **input** (native audio via Parakeet, native video via Conv3D tubelets + Efficient Video Sampling, dynamic-resolution vision) but **text-only output** (no native speech/image generation).
- **Pricing:** **$0** (NVIDIA Build + OpenRouter free) / ~**$0.20 in / $0.80 out** per 1M (DeepInfra); self-host **$0** (open weights).
- **Architecture:** Hybrid **Mamba-Transformer MoE** — **23 Mamba** selective-SSM layers + **23 MoE** layers (**128 experts, top-6 routing, shared expert**) + **6 grouped-query-attention** layers; **~30B total / 3B active**. Encoder-projector-decoder: **C-RADIOv4-H** vision (dynamic resolution, 1,024–13,312 patches/image) + **Parakeet-TDT-0.6B-v2** audio (16 kHz, ~12.5 tok/s), each via a 2-layer MLP projector; tokens interleaved for joint cross-modal reasoning. **Toggleable reasoning** (reasoning-on/off modes).

### Raw benchmarks found

> **Provenance:** every capability figure below is **NVIDIA's own** (arXiv 2604.24954 + HF blog), i.e. **vendor-self-reported**, run against Qwen3-Omni / Qwen3.5-Omni / Nemotron Nano V2 VL. **Artificial Analysis has no page for the Omni (404)** and **Vals AI none**; **benchlm.ai** explicitly states there is "**no benchmark result shared by both models**" so it "does not support a quality verdict." There is therefore **no independent harness re-run** — the numbers are credible (VLMEvalKit + NeMo-Skills, standard frameworks) but unverified by a neutral third party.

- **Vendor — document / OCR (reasoning on):** **MMLongBench-Doc 57.5** (V2 VL 38.0, Qwen3-Omni 49.5, Qwen3.5-Omni 53.6 — **leads open peers**); **OCRBenchV2 EN/ZH 67.0/52.7**; **CharXiv RQ/DQ 63.6/88.9**; DocVQA 95.6; ChartQA 90.3; OCRBench 86.6; InfoVQA 86.8.
- **Vendor — GUI / agentic computer use:** **OSWorld 47.4** (Qwen3-Omni 11.1→29.0 — a large lead for a 3B-active model); **ScreenSpot-Pro 57.8–59.3**; ScreenSpot 90.3; ScreenSpot-v2 93.4. **TauBench V2 (Telecom) 42.7** (tool/agentic; ≈ base 42.2).
- **Vendor — STEM / visual reasoning:** MMMU (val) 70.8 (reasoning on; Qwen3-Omni 67.8, Qwen3.5-Omni 76.9); **MathVista-Mini 82.8**; RefCOCO 90.5; TreeBench 51.6; CV-Bench 84.2.
- **Vendor — video / audio-visual:** **Video-MME 72.2** (w/o sub; Qwen3-Omni 70.5, Qwen3.5-Omni 77.0); **WorldSense 55.4**; **DailyOmni 74.1–74.5**. Ranks **most cost-efficient open video model on MediaPerf**.
- **Vendor — audio / speech:** **VoiceBench 89.4** (Qwen3-Omni 88.8, Qwen3.5-Omni 87.8); **OpenASR avg WER 5.95** (lower-better; Qwen3-Omni 6.55); MMAU 74.6 (trails Qwen3-Omni 77.5, Qwen3.5-Omni 80.4); TED-LIUM long-form WER 3.11.
- **Vendor — text-only (Table 10; Omni vs its own base vs Qwen3-Omni):** **MMLU-Pro 77.3** (base 78.3, Qwen3-Omni 61.6); **GPQA-Diamond 72.2 no-tools** (base 73.0, Qwen3-Omni 73.1); **AIME25 82.1 no-tools** (base **89.1**, Qwen3-Omni 73.7); **LiveCodeBench v5 63.2** (base **68.3**); IFBench 74.2; **AA-LCR 41.0** (long-context reasoning; base 35.9); SciCode 32.0 (base 33.3). The Omni's stated goal is to *retain* the base LLM's text ability while adding vision/audio — it mostly does, but slips on AIME/LiveCodeBench.
- **Vendor — efficiency:** **9× higher output throughput/GPU** at a fixed interactivity target and **3× single-stream** vs Qwen3-Omni on B200; 7.4× system efficiency for multi-document, 9.2× for video use cases.
- **Artificial Analysis / Vals AI:** **no entry for the Omni** (`nvidia-nemotron-3-nano-omni-30b-a3b-reasoning` 404s) — **no independent Intelligence Index.** ⚠ AA's **Index 9** belongs to the **separate text-only base** `nemotron-3-nano-30b-a3b-reasoning` and is **NOT** this model's.

> **Meta-verification:** the curated meta is **accurate** on the headline specs (id, "audio+vision+video+text in," 256–262K/65,536-out, April 2026, free Build + OpenRouter route, "not on Zen," ~30B-A3B open, "senses and responds, frontier models conclude"). **Six clarifications:** (1) **~30B-A3B is canonical** (meta's short says "31B/3B-active"; NVIDIA names it 30B-A3B — the exact total is ~30-31B, active 3B). (2) It is a **toggleable reasoning model** (reasoning on/off) — the meta's name says "reasoning" but the two-mode behavior is material to the scores. (3) **Hybrid Mamba-Transformer-MoE** (23 Mamba + 23 MoE/128-expert/top-6 + 6 GQA) with **C-RADIOv4-H + Parakeet** encoders — the architecture that enables the long-multimodal-context and 9× efficiency, unstated in the meta. (4) **No independent verification** — **AA has no Omni page (404)**; the **Index 9** in circulation is the **base text model's**, and the Omni's **own text scores trail that base** (AIME 82.1 vs 89.1, LCB 63.2 vs 68.3). (5) **All benchmarks are vendor-run** (no neutral harness). (6) **License** is NVIDIA open-model (open-weight, downloadable) — not confirmed verbatim this session. **Reattribution guards:** the **base Nemotron 3 Nano 30B-A3B** (text-only, AA Index 9), the **predecessor Nemotron Nano V2 VL** (12B), **nemotron-3-ultra-free** (queue L141) and **Nemotron 3.5 Super VL / Lightning** are all **separate models** — none of their numbers are used here.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 78/100.** Purpose-built as an enterprise **perception/GUI sub-agent** with strong vendor grounding — **OSWorld 47.4** (vs Qwen3-Omni 29.0), ScreenSpot-Pro 57.8, ScreenSpot-v2 93.4 — plus `tools`/`tool_choice` support and a toggleable reasoning mode. Held below 80 by a **mid TauBench V2 (Telecom) 42.7**, the explicit *sub-agent* (not autonomous-agent) positioning, and **vendor-only** evidence.
- **Reasoning: 78/100.** A **reasoning model** (on/off) with solid nano-class results — **AIME25 82.1**, GPQA-Diamond 72.2, MMLU-Pro 77.3, and strong *multimodal* reasoning (MathVista-Mini 82.8, MMMU 70.8, CharXiv 63.6/88.9) — but it **trails its own text-only base** (AIME 89.1, GPQA 73.0) and has **no independent AA Intelligence Index** (404); the 3B-active nano ceiling caps it.
- **Context window: 80/100.** **256K/262,144 total, 65,536 out**, with genuinely *proven* long-**multimodal**-context handling — **100+ page documents**, **5+ hours of audio**, a Mamba-Transformer long-context design, and a vendor **AA-LCR 41.0** (up from the base's 35.9). Two points above the plain-256K floor for that demonstrated retrieval; still well below the 1M-context cohort.
- **Multimodal: 84/100.** Its raison d'être and the strongest dimension — a **true four-modality-input omni** (text + image + **native video** + **native audio**) with **best-in-class *open*** results across documents (MMLongBench-Doc, OCRBenchV2), video (Video-MME 72.2, most cost-efficient open video model on MediaPerf) and audio (VoiceBench 89.4, OpenASR WER 5.95), beating Qwen3-Omni on most axes. Capped below 85 because output is **text-only** (no native speech/image generation, unlike a Gemini-Live-class model) and all figures are vendor-run.
- **Coding: 70/100.** The weakest dimension: **LiveCodeBench v5 63.2** and **SciCode 32.0** (both *below* its own text base's 68.3/33.3), with **no SWE-bench, Terminal-Bench or repo-scale agentic** result. It is a perception/omni model, not a coder — present-but-modest, vendor-only.
- **Cost efficiency: 94/100.** *(Not counted in Overall.)* Exceptional — **$0 on NVIDIA Build + OpenRouter free**, **open-weight** self-host, ~**$0.20/$0.80** paid, a tiny **3B-active** MoE, and **9× throughput/GPU** (most cost-efficient open video model on MediaPerf). Among the best value in the whole dataset.
- **Overall Score: 78/100.** Half-up mean of the five quality dims: (78 + 78 + 80 + 84 + 70) / 5 = 390 / 5 = **78.0 → 78**. **Divergence +5.6** vs the curated 72.4. Nemotron 3 Nano Omni is the **best-in-class *open* omni perception-reasoner** at its size — four-modality input, long multimodal context, strong GUI/document/video/audio results and near-unbeatable cost — but a **nano-class text/coding ceiling, text-only output, and an entirely vendor-reported evidence base (no AA/Vals)** hold it to the high-70s. My +5.6 over the curated 72.4 reflects crediting its verified four-modality **Multimodal 84** and free/open **efficiency** more than the curated mean appears to, while still penalizing the below-base text/coding and absence of independent verification.
