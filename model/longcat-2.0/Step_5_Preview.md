## Model card

- **Name:** LongCat-2.0 — Meituan's LongCat team flagship, and **the revealed identity behind the `owl-alpha` stealth listing** (refreshed in this same batch). **A negative finding governs this report: BenchLM carries no `longcat-2.0` page in any spelling** (`longcat-2.0`, `longcat-2-0` and `LongCat-2.0` all 404), so the 2026-11-06 re-research had no catalog to check against.
- **Short description:** **The open-weights agentic-coding value pick of mid-2026**: GPT-5.5-class SWE-bench Pro and a native 1M context at roughly a sixth of the price, MIT-licensed and self-hostable — **SWE-bench Pro 59.5 (edging GPT-5.5's 58.6), Terminal-Bench 2.1 70.8 (within 0.1 of Gemini 3.1 Pro), SWE-bench Multilingual 77.3, BrowseComp 79.9, RWSearch 78.8, FORTE 73.2 (ties Opus 4.6), IFEval 90.0, GPQA-Diamond 88.9 and IMO-AnswerBench 81.8.**
- **Provider / access:** LongCat platform (`longcat.ai` / `api.longcat.chat`, OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible), OpenRouter; **weights on Hugging Face / ModelScope (BF16, INT8, FP8 variants) under MIT.**
- **Release / knowledge:** **2026-06-30** (weights followed the announcement). **Knowledge cutoff not disclosed.**
- **IDs:** `meituan/longcat-2.0` (repo id); the LongCat platform model id.
- **Context window:** **1M tokens native** (hundreds of billions of 1M-context training tokens); **max output 128K.**
- **Modalities:** **text in → text out** — **vision arrives only in the separate 2.5 preview generation**, which is recorded as a sibling non-import point below.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-11-06):** **list $0.75/M input, $2.95/M output with free cached context reads; promotional $0.30/$1.20; MIT weights free to self-host** (16x H20 recommended).
- **Architecture:** **MoE with LongCat Sparse Attention (SI/CLI/HI) + 3-step MTP + N-gram embeddings.**

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 70/100.** Mid-band. **FORTE 73.2 (ties Opus 4.6), BrowseComp 79.9 and RWSearch 78.8** are genuinely upper-mid agentic results, competitive with Gemini 3.1 Pro on FORTE/RWSearch. Held at 70 by **the absence of any MCP-Atlas, Toolathlon, OSWorld, GDPval-AA or AA Agentic Index figure** — so two of three frontier references are unmeasured and no composite exists — and by **BrowseComp trailing the best frontier models by ~5 points**. **The production-traffic evidence is attributed as the reason this score is credible despite the unmeasured references**: ~10.1T monthly tokens, 559B/day, +242% MoM and #1 on Hermes Agent by monthly volume is real-world corroboration no benchmark provides.
- **Reasoning: 76/100.** Mid-band. **GPQA-Diamond 88.9%, IMO-AnswerBench 81.8%, IFEval 90.0% and Writing Bench 83.8%** are upper-mid across four different axes, unusual corroboration for an agentic model. Held below the frontier tier by **the total absence of HLE, CritPt, AA-LCR, ARC-AGI, AIME, MATH, MMLU-Pro, the AA Intelligence Index and AA-Omniscience** — so composite depth and reliability are entirely unknown — and by **IFEval trailing Gemini 3.1 Pro and GPT-5.5 by 5–6 points** despite the newest Opus checkpoints regressing below it.
- **Context window: 90/100.** **A native 1M window under LongCat Sparse Attention with hundreds of billions of 1M-context training tokens and linear-complexity serving** — the methodology's ≥1M band, docked to 90 because **no MRCR, RULER or needle-retrieval curve is published and independent long-context verification is absent**, so the window's usable depth is a spec claim rather than a measurement.
- **Multimodal: 12/100.** **Text-only (text in → text out)** — the methodology's text-only band (10–20). **A sibling non-import is recorded: image input arrives only in the separate LongCat 2.5 preview generation, and that sibling's capability is named as the comparison point and explicitly not merged into this SKU's multimodal score.**
- **Coding: 74/100.** Mid-band, and the strongest dimension — **the strongest open-weights coding profile in its comparison set**: **SWE-bench Pro 59.5 (edging GPT-5.5's 58.6), Terminal-Bench 2.1 70.8 (near Gemini 3.1 Pro), SWE-Multilingual 77.3.** Held below the frontier band by **Terminal-Bench 2.1 vs Opus 4.8 (78.9, an 8.1-point gap)** and **SWE-Pro vs Opus 4.7/4.8 (64.3/69.2, 4.8–9.7 points)**, and by **the absence of SWE-bench Verified, LiveCodeBench, AA-SciCode, the AA Coding Index and DeepSWE**, so two of three coding references are unmeasured.
- **Cost efficiency: 90/100.** **$0.30–0.75/M input and $1.20–2.95/M output with free cached context reads, plus MIT weights for self-hosting** — roughly **6–10x cheaper than GPT-5.5 for the same SWE-Pro class of work**, the methodology's ~$0.6/$2.2 → 92 range adjusted for **the promotional pricing volatility** ($0.30/$1.20 promo against $0.75/$2.95 list).
- **Overall Score: 64/100.** (70 + 76 + 90 + 12 + 74) / 5 = 64.4 → **64**. **A disagreement is recorded rather than reconciled: the repo queue carries 71.0 for this slug while this report reads 64**, and with BenchLM absent there is no third instrument to arbitrate — the queue figure is the corpus-average of other research agents' raters, this report is Step 5 Preview's own normalized reading, and both are stated side by side rather than flattened. Recommendation: **the open-weights agentic-coding value pick** — GPT-5.5-class SWE-bench Pro and a native 1M context at roughly a sixth of the price, MIT-licensed and self-hostable, with the strongest production-traffic evidence in this research series from its stealth period. Three cautions: **text-only**, with vision arriving only in the separate 2.5 preview generation and explicitly not imported here; **every number is vendor-reported** with no independent run; and **it is now superseded within its own family and by the frontier tier on Terminal-Bench 2.1** (Opus 4.8 78.9 against this model's 70.8), so it is a value pick rather than a capability pick.
