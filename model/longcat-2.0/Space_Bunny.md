## Model card

- **Name:** LongCat-2.0 (MIT-licensed open weights; no OpenCode Zen Free ID)
- **Short description:** Meituan's 1.6T-parameter open-weights MoE for coding, long-context, and agentic work, unveiled 2026-06-29/30 — notable as the first truly frontier-scale model trained and deployed entirely on Chinese AI-ASIC superpods. **Its genuine distinction is engineering provenance and licence openness; on measured capability it sits near the bottom of the large open-weight class.** Distinct from LongCat-2.5-Preview, which reuses the same 1.6T/48B scale figures with vision added and no published benchmarks.
- **Provider / access:** LongCat API platform (`https://longcat.ai/platform/`), model ID `LongCat-2.0`, with both OpenAI-compatible Chat Completions and Anthropic-compatible Messages endpoints. Also on OpenRouter (`meituan/longcat-2.0`). Integrated with Claude Code, OpenClaw, Hermes, OpenCode, and Kilo Code. **Artificial Analysis lists availability through only 1 API provider.**
- **Release / knowledge:** unveiled **2026-06-29** (confirmed by AA), launch blog and API billing live 2026-06-30. **Knowledge cutoff still not documented** after two passes.
- **IDs:** `LongCat-2.0` (Meituan first-party); `meituan/longcat-2.0` on OpenRouter; Hugging Face `meituan-longcat/LongCat-2.0` (full MIT safetensors plus an INT8 build). **No Free ID on OpenCode Zen.**
- **Context window:** **1M tokens input, 128K (131,072) max output, native and no beta header.** Verified three ways: Meituan's launch materials, AA's technical specification, and the fact that the model was trained on hundreds of billions of tokens of 1M-context data using LongCat Sparse Attention.
- **Modalities:** **Text in, text out — confirmed text-only by Artificial Analysis** ("LongCat 2.0 does not support image input. It can only process text."). No image, audio, or video input; vision arrives only in the separate LongCat-2.5-Preview. Native tool calling, multi-step reasoning, long-context tasks, and automatic context caching.
- **Pricing (verified 2026-10-10):** **$0.75 per 1M uncached input / $2.95 output**, cache read $0.015 — **confirmed as the median across providers by AA**, against class medians of $0.47/$1.94. **98% cache discount; blended 7:2:1 rate $0.46 per 1M; $0.15 per Intelligence Index task.** Limited-time promotional $0.30/$1.20 also on OpenRouter. Context-cache hits are processed free of charge regardless of tier.
- **Speed / latency (new):** AA measures **65.2 output t/s**, ranked **#42 of 117** against a class median of 67.8 — **slightly slower than average** — and **TTFT 2.64 s** against a median of 1.87 s. It also generated **140M tokens** on the Intelligence Index, which AA classifies as somewhat verbose.
- **Architecture:** Mixture-of-Experts, **1.6T total / 48B active**; **MIT licence, commercial use permitted** (AA-confirmed). LongCat Sparse Attention (LSA) with streaming-aware indexing, cross-layer indexing, and hierarchical indexing; 3-step Multi-Token Prediction; 135B N-gram Embedding parameters inherited from LongCat-Flash-Lite. Pretrained on 35+ trillion tokens across millions of accelerator-days on Ascend 910B AI-ASIC superpods with no rollbacks.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 68/100.** Down from 78. The prior 78 was built on Meituan's in-house Terminal-Bench 2.1 70.8%, FORTE 73.2% and RWSearch 78.8%, with the harness caveat stated honestly. What changes the score is not a new negative number but **the absence of any independent one**: **no Terminal-Bench, GDPval-AA, AutomationBench, Briefcase, Toolathlon, MCP-Atlas, or Claw-Eval row exists from any third party**, and **AA's Intelligence Index of 19** — which is composed of exactly those agentic evaluations (Briefcase, GDPval-AA, AutomationBench, Terminal-Bench 4.0) plus SciCode, HLE, CritPt, Omniscience and LCR — is **barely above the class median of 18**. A composite that low means the agentic components are collectively weak. **BrowseComp 79.9% and IFEval 90.0% remain credited** as genuine strengths from the vendor table.
- **Reasoning: 60/100.** **Down from 76 — the largest correction, and it is driven directly by the closed gap.** The prior report scored 76 from **GPQA Diamond 88.9%** (vendor-cited, 90th percentile on BenchmarkList) and **IMO-AnswerBench 81.8%** (in-house), and explicitly noted that "no independent composite index, HLE, or hallucination-rate figure exists to corroborate the in-house numbers." **The independent composite is now 19** — for scale, GLM-5.2 reads 33.7, DeepSeek V4 Flash 34.3, GLM-5.3 44.8, Nemotron 3 Ultra 22.9, Mistral Medium 3.5 14.2. A GPQA of 88.9% is entirely compatible with an index of 19, because the index is dominated by agentic and coding evaluations in which this model is weak; the prior report effectively scored the dimension on its one best-looking row. **No hallucination rate, no Omniscience index, and no knowledge cutoff exist** after two passes.
- **Context window: 85/100.** Down from 89. **1M input / 128K output** is now confirmed three ways including by AA's technical specification, and it is native rather than a beta header — a real and creditable engineering claim, reinforced by 1M-context training data and a purpose-built sparse-attention scheme. The reduction is for evidence: **AA-LCR 62.7% is a leaderboard record of uncertain provenance that the prior pass already marked provisional**, and **no MRCR, RULER, or GraphWalks sweep exists at any window length.** An unmeasured 1M window is a specification; 62.7% on one unverified harness is weak evidence about behaviour.
- **Multimodal: 15/100.** Unchanged, and now **independently confirmed** rather than inferred: AA states plainly that the model "does not support image input. It can only process text." Vision is a LongCat-2.5-Preview capability and must not be read onto this checkpoint. This dimension is the single largest structural drag on the Overall.
- **Coding: 68/100.** Down from 76. **SWE-bench Pro 59.5%** (edging GPT-5.5's 58.6) and **SWE-bench Multilingual 77.3%** are the model's clearest claims and are credited — both appear on public leaderboards at the stated percentile positions. But **no independent coding measurement exists at all**: AA publishes no SciCode, Coding Index, LiveCodeBench, or SWE-bench row for this model, and the prior pass's own **SciCode 35.4%** — explicitly marked provisional — is the only third-party coding-adjacent figure on file. The AA composite of 19 includes SciCode and Terminal-Bench 4.0, both of which are agentic-coding evaluations this model would need to be strong in to lift the index.
- **Cost efficiency: 82/100.** Down from 90. The pricing is now independently confirmed by AA at **$0.75 / $2.95** as the median across providers, with a **98% cache discount**, **$0.46 blended**, and **$0.15 per Index task**; AA ranks it **#16 of 117 on cost**. The **MIT licence** is a genuine, durable advantage. The reduction is about **value rather than price**: AA explicitly classifies both the input and output rates as "somewhere higher than average" against medians of $0.47 and $1.94, and at **65.2 t/s the model is slower than the class median of 67.8 t/s** with TTFT 2.64 s against 1.87 s. Paying above-median prices at below-median speed for an index score of 19 is a weak proposition.
- **Overall Score: 59.2/100.** (68 + 60 + 85 + 15 + 68) / 5 = 296 / 5 = 59.2, down from 66.8. **Best fit: organisations whose priority is licence permissiveness and sovereign-hardware provenance rather than measured capability — MIT weights, no commercial restrictions, trained end-to-end on Ascend 910B AI-ASIC superpods with no rollbacks.** That is a real and durable strategic asset that no benchmark captures, and it is the reason to consider this model at all. **Four cautions.** First, **an Intelligence Index of 19 places it near the bottom of the large open-weight class** — if you want capability per dollar, GLM-5.2, DeepSeek V4 Flash, or Nemotron 3 Ultra all score materially higher at lower or comparable cost. Second, **every capability figure above is Meituan's own in-house harness**, explicitly non-comparable with the `*`-cited vendor numbers beside it, and the prior pass's honesty about that was appropriate and does not change the arithmetic. Third, **no hallucination data exists**, so treat every factual claim as unverified. Fourth, **text-only**, and **1.6T parameters is impractical to self-host for most buyers** — the MIT licence is theoretical unless you have Ascend-class hardware. Note also that **LongCat-2.5-Preview supersedes this checkpoint** (1M context, 131,072 output, free on four providers per models.dev) and carries vision; if you are starting new work, evaluate that instead.
