## Model card

- **Name:** Step 5 Preview
- **Short description:** **StepFun's (阶跃星辰) flagship open-weight model for agentic software engineering and professional knowledge work** (released ~2026-09-20; open weights 2026-10-15). It is a **600B-total / 27B-active sparse MoE** built on a deliberately **"narrow-but-deep" 92-transformer-block** design — *as deep as Qwen3.8-Max and Kimi K3 but with <¼ the total and <⅓ the active parameters* — which StepFun says delivers "long-horizon agentic capabilities comparable to trillion-scale counterparts at much lower cost." Its real strength is **domain knowledge work**: on Artificial Analysis' capability indexes (checked 2026-10-22) it is **#3 of 98 open-weight models on Legal (46.2), #3 of 102 on Economics (52.9), #3 of 49 on Healthcare & Medical (41.1)**, and #4 on Engineering (45.0) and Finance (45.4). ⚠ But it is **not a frontier lead** — the **AA Intelligence Index is 44**, the same AA tables rank it **#42-59 across *all* models** (e.g. #57/225 Legal, #42/232 Economics), and its coding/terminal numbers are **mid-tier and "uneven"** (SWE-bench Verified ~44.6%, Terminal-Bench 4.0 33.3%). It is a **preview** — best for experiments, not production guarantees.
- **Provider / access:** **StepFun** (Shanghai). API on the StepFun Open Platform; **open weights released 2026-10-15** (`stepfun-ai/Step-5-Preview` on Hugging Face / ModelScope; vLLM recipe published). **No free tier**; rate-limit tier V0 (under $15 cumulative top-up): 5 concurrency / 100 RPM / 500K TPM. Listed here as `opencode/step-5-preview`.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released **~2026-09-20** (AA lists "September 2026"); open weights **2026-10-15**. Knowledge cutoff not stated in the sources reviewed.
- **IDs:** `step-5-preview` / `opencode/step-5-preview` (StepFun line: Step 3.5 Flash → **Step 5 Preview**).
- **Context window:** **1,000,000 (1M) tokens total / 64,000 (64K) max output.** Long context is made efficient by **Sparse GQA** (sparse indexing inspired by DeepSeek DSA + block-wise token merging, block size 8, k=4096 → 512 selected blocks), supporting training/inference up to 1M. The **64K output cap is modest** (below the 128-131K leaders and far below MiniMax M3's 512K).
- **Modalities:** **Text, image, video in → text out.** Combines research, multimodal understanding and tool use to analyze **long documents, charts, screenshots, images and video**. No audio; text-only output; no dedicated multimodal benchmark was published in the sources reviewed.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-23):** **$1.00 / $2.70 per 1M in/out; $0.10 cache-hit input; no free tier.** A **mid-budget rate** — cheaper than ember-1 ($3/$15) and claude-sonnet-5.4 ($2.50/$12.50), pricier than qwen-3.8-flash ($0.16/$0.47) and mimo-v2.6-free ($0). The narrow-but-deep design is explicitly aimed at lowering cost per unit of agentic capability.
- **Architecture:** **600B sparse MoE / 27B active**, **"narrow but deep" (92 blocks)**, **3:1 interleaved Gated Sliding-Window Attention (efficient) + Gated/Sparse GQA (full attention)**. Trained in **mixed-precision FP8** with stability innovations for deep nets — **FP32 hidden states, FP32 RMSNorm scale (zero-centered RMSNorm), and fine-grained gradient clamp** (clipping the top ~0.00004% of gradient values/layer to suppress spikes). **Agent-native data synthesis** (million-level verifiable environments across scientific reasoning, SWE, ML R&D and professional work) + **agentic RL** targeting training-inference mismatch, rollout efficiency, context management and credit assignment.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 82/100.** Designed agent-native — 92-block long-horizon architecture, million-level verifiable agentic environments, tool use across long documents/charts/screenshots/video, and agentic RL for long rollouts. But the measured agentic result is **mid-tier and uneven**: **Terminal-Bench 4.0 33.3%** (above Gemini 3.8 Flash's 19.1%, below GPT-5.6 Sol's 37.3% and far below Opus 5's 51.8%), and meta.json's own tradeoff names "uneven terminal performance." Preview status and vendor-reported tool numbers hold it in the low-80s.
- **Reasoning: 83/100.** The genuine strength is **domain-knowledge reasoning** — top-3-4 among ~98-102 open-weight models on Legal/Economics/Healthcare/Engineering/Finance AA indexes, plus **HLE 46.5%** (above qwen-3.8-flash). Capped by the **AA Intelligence Index 44** (mid-tier general composite, below the closed frontier and MiMo-V2.6-Pro's 46.32) and the all-model domain ranks of **#42-59** — a strong open-weight peer showing, not a frontier lead.
- **Context window: 86/100.** **1M-token context** made economically usable by **Sparse GQA** (DSA-inspired sparse indexing + block-wise merging) — a real long-context engineering contribution. Held below the 88-89 leaders because the **64K output cap is modest** (vs 128-131K leaders, 512K for MiniMax M3) and no independent long-context-recall figure was published.
- **Multimodal: 79/100.** Native **text + image + video input** with documented use on charts, screenshots, documents and video → **text-only output**. Scored around the other text/image/video models (MiniMax M3 80) but below the omnimodal leaders (muse-wonder/mimo at 82, which add audio) because there is **no audio, no generative output, and no published multimodal benchmark** to verify quality.
- **Coding: 78/100.** The relative weakness — **SWE-bench Verified ~44.6%** is far below the frontier (80-97%) and the coding story rests on a vendor **avg@4 49.0%** of ambiguous harness plus a **#4/98 open-weight Engineering** index (#42/224 across all models). StepFun *claims* "frontier-level software engineering," but the transcribed numbers are mid-tier; credited above pure-knowledge models for real agentic-coding capability and open-weight self-hostability, held well below ember-1 (89) / mimo-v2.6-free (88) / minimax-m3 (86) whose SWE figures are far higher.
- **Cost efficiency: 89/100.** Good value — **$1.00/$2.70** with **$0.10 cache hits**, and the narrow-but-deep design explicitly targets "trillion-scale-comparable agentic capability at much lower cost"; open weights allow self-hosting to remove per-token cost. Tempered by **no free tier**, the low V0 rate limits, and preview-status pricing/support uncertainty. Scored but **not** counted in the Overall per methodology.
- **Overall Score: 82/100.** Mean of Tool 82 + Reasoning 83 + Context 86 + Multimodal 79 + Coding 78 = 408/5 = 81.6 → 82. Best fit: **long-context, multimodal professional knowledge work** — legal/economics/healthcare/finance research and document/chart/video analysis where its top-3-4 open-weight domain indexes, 1M Sparse-GQA context and mid-budget rate shine; it is **not** the pick for frontier SWE-bench coding or hard terminal agency, and preview status means experiments over production guarantees. Independent read: my **82 sits meaningfully below the queue's curated 86.5** — the gap is driven by the **independent AA evidence itself** (Intelligence Index 44; all-model domain ranks #42-59, explicitly "not a top-10 finish"), the **mid-tier SWE-bench Verified ~44.6% and Terminal-Bench 4.0 33.3%**, and **preview** status, weighed against genuinely strong open-weight-relative domain knowledge, a sophisticated efficient architecture and good cost. Internally consistent: Step 5 Preview (82) lands above the gated code-only gemini-3.8-flash-cyber (80) but below the stronger-coding open-weight peers qwen-3.8-flash / ember-1 / mimo-v2.6-free (84) and minimax-m3 (84) — an accurate portrait of a knowledge-work specialist whose general-composite mean is capped by mid-tier coding/terminal results and an honest, independent AA showing.
