## Model card

- **Name:** Step 5 Preview
- **Short description:** **StepFun's (阶跃星辰) flagship open-weight model for agentic software engineering and professional knowledge work** (released ~2026-09-20; open weights 2026-10-15). It is a **600B-total / 27B-active sparse MoE** built on a deliberately **"narrow-but-deep" 92-transformer-block** design — *as deep as Qwen3.8-Max and Kimi K3 but with <¼ the total and <⅓ the active parameters* — which StepFun says delivers "long-horizon agentic capabilities comparable to trillion-scale counterparts at much lower cost." Its real strength is **domain knowledge work**: on Artificial Analysis' capability indexes (checked 2026-10-22) it is **#3 of 98 open-weight models on Legal (46.2), #3 of 102 on Economics (52.9), #3 of 49 on Healthcare & Medical (41.1)**, and #4 on Engineering (45.0) and Finance (45.4). ⚠ But it is **not a frontier lead** — the **AA Intelligence Index is 44**, the same AA tables rank it **#42-59 across *all* models** (e.g. #57/225 Legal, #42/232 Economics), and its coding/terminal numbers are **mid-tier and "uneven"** (SWE-bench Verified ~44.6%, Terminal-Bench 4.0 33.3%). It is a **preview** — best for experiments, not production guarantees. **[2026-11-02: canonical AA slug is `step-5` (active, not deprecated); v5.0 composite reads 29 (#7/23 open-weight) vs the draft's v4-era "44" — basket rebase, non-convertible, same "above-class, not-frontier" conclusion; AA spec lists text+image only, and the license is "StepFun Model License 1.0."]**
- **Provider / access:** **StepFun** (Shanghai). API on the StepFun Open Platform; **open weights released 2026-10-15** (`stepfun-ai/Step-5-Preview` on Hugging Face / ModelScope; vLLM recipe published). **No free tier**; rate-limit tier V0 (under $15 cumulative top-up): 5 concurrency / 100 RPM / 500K TPM. Listed here as `opencode/step-5-preview`.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released **~2026-09-20** (AA lists "September 2026"); open weights **2026-10-15**. Knowledge cutoff not stated in the sources reviewed.
- **IDs:** `step-5-preview` / `opencode/step-5-preview` (StepFun line: Step 3.5 Flash → **Step 5 Preview**).
- **Context window:** **1,000,000 (1M) tokens total / 64,000 (64K) max output.** Long context is made efficient by **Sparse GQA** (sparse indexing inspired by DeepSeek DSA + block-wise token merging, block size 8, k=4096 → 512 selected blocks), supporting training/inference up to 1M. The **64K output cap is modest** (below the 128-131K leaders and far below MiniMax M3's 512K).
- **Modalities:** **Text, image, video in → text out.** Combines research, multimodal understanding and tool use to analyze **long documents, charts, screenshots, images and video**. No audio; text-only output; no dedicated multimodal benchmark was published in the sources reviewed. **[2026-11-02: modality-overstatement correction — AA's authoritative spec lists input modality as TEXT AND IMAGE ONLY; the video capability is vendor-claimed and unconfirmed by the neutral harness. A measured vision cell now exists (AA-MMMU-Pro 76.4, mid). Multimodal 79 → 75.]**
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-23):** **$1.00 / $2.70 per 1M in/out; $0.10 cache-hit input; no free tier.** A **mid-budget rate** — cheaper than ember-1 ($3/$15) and claude-sonnet-5.4 ($2.50/$12.50), pricier than qwen-3.8-flash ($0.16/$0.47) and mimo-v2.6-free ($0). The narrow-but-deep design is explicitly aimed at lowering cost per unit of agentic capability.
- **Architecture:** **600B sparse MoE / 27B active**, **"narrow but deep" (92 blocks)**, **3:1 interleaved Gated Sliding-Window Attention (efficient) + Gated/Sparse GQA (full attention)**. Trained in **mixed-precision FP8** with stability innovations for deep nets — **FP32 hidden states, FP32 RMSNorm scale (zero-centered RMSNorm), and fine-grained gradient clamp** (clipping the top ~0.00004% of gradient values/layer to suppress spikes). **Agent-native data synthesis** (million-level verifiable environments across scientific reasoning, SWE, ML R&D and professional work) + **agentic RL** targeting training-inference mismatch, rollout efficiency, context management and credit assignment.
