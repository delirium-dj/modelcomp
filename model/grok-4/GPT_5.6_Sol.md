# Grok 4 — Independent Research Report

## Summary and evidence

Grok 4 is SpaceXAI's July 2025 reasoning model with native tool use, real-time web and X search, text-and-image input, and **256,000-token context**. SpaceXAI reported **15.9% ARC-AGI-2**, while Grok 4 Heavy reached **50.7% Humanity's Last Exam** on the text-only subset and 61.9% USAMO 2025 through parallel test-time compute.

Source: [SpaceXAI Grok 4 launch](https://x.ai/news/grok-4)

## Cost and limitations

The original API launched at **$3 input and $15 output per million tokens**. It has been superseded by several 4.x generations with larger context, lower prices, and better coding. Heavy's parallel inference also increases latency and task cost.

## Scores

- **Tool use: 88/100.** Native search and code tools were leading at launch.
- **Reasoning: 89/100.** Heavy achieved excellent academic results through scaled test-time compute.
- **Context window: 86/100.** 256K is useful but modest by current standards.
- **Multimodal: 86/100.** Text and vision are supported without broad media inputs.
- **Coding: 87/100.** Strong reasoning aids coding, though later Grok models specialize further.
- **Cost efficiency: 76/100.** Original prices and Heavy compute are expensive versus current options.
- **Overall Score: 87/100.** Half-up rounded mean: (88 + 89 + 86 + 86 + 87) / 5 = 87.2.

## Bottom line

Grok 4 was a major reasoning release, but newer Grok models are cheaper, longer-context, and stronger for production agents.
