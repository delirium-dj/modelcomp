> **Verdict:** GLM-5 is **Zhipu AI (Z.ai) & Tsinghua's** February-2026 open-weight flagship — the model that **started the GLM-5.x line** — and a genuine landmark for open weights: a **744B-total / 40B-active MIT MoE** (256 experts, 80 layers, doubling GLM-4.5's 355B/32B) trained on **28.5T tokens**, using **DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA) + Multi-Latent Attention (MLA)** and a sequential Reasoning→Agentic→General RL pipeline with on-policy cross-stage distillation. Its headline is the shift "from vibe coding to agentic engineering": **SWE-bench Verified 77.8%** and **Terminal-Bench 2.0 56.2%** (both SOTA open-source at release), **τ²-Bench 89.7%**, **HLE 50.4**, **#1 open model on both LMArena Text Arena and Code Arena**, and — per its own paper — the **first open-weights model to reach 50 on the (old) AA Intelligence Index v4.0** (up from GLM-4.7's 42), claimed "comparable to Claude Opus 4.5 and GPT-5.2 (xhigh), better than Gemini 3 Pro" (~20% avg gain over GLM-4.7). **But the caveats are real:** it is **deprecated** (GLM-5.1/5.2/5.3 supersede it — SWE-Pro 58.4→62.1, Terminal-Bench 81.0, 1M context), **text-only**, **every headline number is vendor-published** (the arxiv paper is Zhipu's own), the current **AA Intelligence Index v5.0 is N/A** (the "50" is the non-convertible v4.0 basket), AA measures **speed below average (67.5 tok/s, #44/79, 2/4)** and **hosted price above median ($1.00/$3.20, blended $0.66)** across **5 providers**, and an independent pre-release review (Verdent) of the GLM *series* flags weak Terminal-Bench/surgical-edit behavior, multi-file-reasoning gaps, framework-specific weakness, and no contamination disclosure. Scored Overall **70** (div **+4.3** vs curated 65.7) — a strong, cheap-to-self-host (MIT) agentic-coding release whose vendor-heavy evidence and superseded status temper the dimension mean.

## Model card

- **Name:** GLM 5 (GLM-5)
- **Short description:** Zhipu AI (Z.ai) & Tsinghua's open-weight **744B/40B-active MIT MoE** flagship (Feb 2026) that opened the GLM-5.x line and pushed the "vibe coding → agentic engineering" paradigm — SOTA open-source SWE-bench Verified (77.8%) and Terminal-Bench 2.0 (56.2%), #1 open model on both LMArena arenas, and the first open model to hit 50 on AA's (old) Intelligence Index v4.0. Text-only, 200K context, reasoning, now deprecated in favour of GLM-5.1+.
- **Provider / access:** Open weights on **Hugging Face** (`zai-org/GLM-5`) and ModelScope (`ZhipuAI/glm-5`) under **MIT** (free commercial self-host, $0). Hosted via **5 API providers** per AA (median $1.00/$3.20); Z.ai list price = OpenCode-Zen price (`$0.20/M` cached read). Part of the OpenCode catalog as `opencode/glm-5`. Full-stack adapted to **7 Chinese GPU platforms** (Huawei Ascend, Moore Threads, Hygon, Cambricon, Kunlunxin, MetaX, Enflame).
- **Release / knowledge:** Released **February 2026** (Z.ai blog 12 Feb 2026; arxiv 2602.15763v1 posted 17 Feb 2026; AA "February 2026"). **Deprecated** — AA banner: "Z AI has launched a newer release, GLM-5.1. We suggest considering it instead." Pre-training began from a **27T-token corpus** (code/reasoning-weighted), totalling **28.5T tokens** for the base model; knowledge cutoff not separately published.
- **IDs:** id `opencode/glm-5`; HF `zai-org/GLM-5`; ModelScope `ZhipuAI/glm-5`; AA `glm-5` (profiles the **reasoning** variant; "a non-reasoning variant may also exist"); arxiv `2602.15763`.
- **Context window:** **200,000 (200K) total** (meta adds **128,000 max output** — AA lists 200k but does not separately confirm the output cap). Mid-training progressively extended context **4K → 200K** on long-context agentic data; DSA sparse attention keeps long-context cost down. Superseded by GLM-5.2's **1M** window.
- **Modalities:** **Text input → text output only** (AA FAQ: "does not support image input… not multimodal"). **Reasoning: Yes** — extended thinking / chain-of-thought (AA profiles the reasoning variant). Verdent notes a thinking-mode latency trade-off (40+ s deep reasoning vs <10 s with thinking disabled).
- **Pricing:** **$1.00 / 1M input, $3.20 / 1M output** (AA median across 5 providers; input "somewhat expensive" vs $0.47 median, output "expensive" vs $1.95 median), **blended $0.66** (7:2:1), **80% cache discount** (≈ $0.20/M cached read). **Free to self-host** (MIT weights). TTFT 1.47s (better than the 1.60s size-class median).
- **Architecture:** **744B-total / 40B-active Mixture-of-Experts** — **256 experts, 80 layers** (layer count reduced to cut expert-parallelism communication overhead), doubling GLM-4.5 (355B/32B). Unifies **Agentic, Reasoning, Coding (ARC)** in one "Model-of-Experts." Adopts **DSA (DeepSeek Sparse Attention)** to dynamically allocate attention by token importance (lower train/inference cost), **MLA (Multi-Latent Attention)** with reduced KV vectors for long-context memory savings, and **Muon Split** (per-head matrix orthogonalization of the up-projections) so attention logits stay stable without clipping. Post-training: sequential **Reasoning RL → Agentic RL → General RL** with **On-Policy Cross-Stage Distillation** (anti-forgetting) on a new async RL infra built on the "slime" framework.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 75/100.** A strong, purpose-built agentic model — **τ²-Bench 89.7% (elite)**, **Terminal-Bench 2.0 56.2% (SOTA open at release)**, plus MCP-Atlas / BrowseComp / Vending-Bench 2 / CC-Bench-V2 long-horizon results and an async Agent-RL pipeline for planning/self-correction. Held below the top agentic tier because Terminal-Bench 2.0 56.2% is only mid in absolute terms, the long-horizon figure values are image-only (not machine-verified here), the independent Verdent review of the GLM series flags "complete file replacement" over surgical edits, and it is superseded by GLM-5.1's longer-horizon results (8 h autonomous, TB2.1 56.9).
- **Reasoning: 73/100.** Landmark for open weights — the **first open model to reach 50 on AA Intelligence Index v4.0** (+8 over GLM-4.7), **HLE 50.4**, and vendor-claimed parity with Claude Opus 4.5 / GPT-5.2 (xhigh). Tempered because that "50" is the **old, non-convertible v4.0 basket** (current **v5.0 is N/A**), per-eval GPQA/CritPt/Omniscience scores are figure-only, all of it is vendor-published, and Zhipu publishes no contamination report.
- **Context window: 76/100.** A solid **200K** window (mid-trained 4K→200K on long-context agentic data) with **DSA + MLA** for efficient, low-memory long-context handling. Capped below the higher tiers because 200K is now table-stakes for a flagship, the 128K output cap is meta-only (unconfirmed by AA), no RULER/MRCR retrieval number was readable, and the GLM-5.2 successor jumps to **1M**.
- **Multimodal: 50/100.** **Text-only** — AA FAQ explicitly confirms "does not support image input… not multimodal." Scored at the corpus convention for a text-only model (no vision/audio/video in or out).
- **Coding: 76/100.** The strongest, best-corroborated dimension — **SWE-bench Verified 77.8% (SOTA open-source at release**, just under GPT-5.1 Codex ~77.9%, below Opus 4.5 ~80.9%) and **#1 open model on LMArena Code Arena** (independent human judgment). Held below the very top because it is the *predecessor* (GLM-5.1 SWE-Pro 58.4 / GLM-5.2 improve further), the numbers are vendor-published, and the independent series-level review flags framework-specific and multi-file-reasoning gaps.
- **Cost efficiency: 83/100.** **Free MIT self-host** ($0, "cheapest 744B-class price") is excellent value, but the **hosted price is above median ($1.00/$3.20, blended $0.66**, input somewhat-expensive / output expensive vs $0.47/$1.95), speed is below average (67.5 tok/s), and it is deprecated. The 80% cache discount and 5-provider availability help. (Scored but NOT counted toward Overall.)
- **Overall Score: 70/100.** Half-up mean of the five quality dims (75+73+76+50+76)/5 = 350/5 = 70.0 → 70. Divergence **+4.3** vs the curated queue value 65.7 — a sizable positive gap: the curated view appears to weight the **deprecated/superseded status**, the **vendor-only headline numbers**, the **text-only** modality, and the below-average speed/hosted price more heavily, whereas the dimension-mean credits the **genuinely strong, partly-independent agentic-coding profile** (SWE-bench Verified 77.8% SOTA-open, #1 open on both LMArena arenas, τ²-Bench 89.7%, first open model to 50 on AA v4.0) and the **free MIT self-hosting** of a 744B-class MoE. Sits just below the GLM-5.1 report (b4, Overall 72) — consistent with GLM-5 being the strong *base* of the line that 5.1/5.2/5.3 then extended (SWE-Pro, longer horizon, 1M context). Cost efficiency excluded from the mean per methodology.
