## Model card

- **Name:** GLM-5.3-Flash. **Open weight** — the official model card is published on Hugging Face as **`zai-org/GLM-5.3-Flash`**. Variant/alias flag: the GLM-5 family is large — siblings include **GLM-5.3** (the full-size version, scoring 65.7 on a composite tracker against Flash's 58.56), **GLM-5.2**, **GLM-5.1**, **GLM-5**, **GLM-5-Turbo**, **GLM-5V-Turbo** and a separate **GLM-5 (Reasoning)** variant.
- **Short description:** Z.AI's Flash-tier open-weight model, documented in its own launch post. **It records the only GDPval-AA Elo in this entire research pass that clears the methodology's ~1750+ frontier reference — 1,773** — and **the highest SWE-bench figure recorded here at 92.0%, independently measured by Vals AI**. It is also one of only two models in this dataset with a published **Tau3-Banking** score. Against that, the vendor-versus-independent spread on Terminal-Bench is 21.4 points, which is the main reason to treat its launch figures with care.
- **Provider / access:** Z.AI's own API, Hugging Face weights (`zai-org/GLM-5.3-Flash`), OpenRouter (`z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`) and third-party hosts. **Open weights mean the rate card is escapable** for organisations able to self-host. Reasoning: explicit reasoning mode.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released in the GLM-5.3 cycle. **No verified public release date or knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `zai-org/GLM-5.3-Flash` (Hugging Face), `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` (OpenRouter). Related earlier model: GLM-4.7-Flash.
- **Context window:** **1,000,000 tokens.** Max output: no verified public figure found. Long-context evidence exists and is mixed — **AA-LCR 80.0%** against **MLCR-AA 51.1%** (see benchmarks).
- **Modalities:** **Text and image input, with video evidenced by a published MMVU score of 80.5%**; output text only. No audio input and no non-text output documented or benchmarked. **No vendor specification sheet was retrievable**, so the modality list is inferred from the benchmark set.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **No rate card was verified for this model specifically.** For family context, the full-size **GLM-5.3 is $1.40 / 1M input and $4.40 / 1M output** per an independent open-weight comparison verified earlier in this research session; the Flash tier is positioned below that. **Open weights make the per-token rate escapable.** Note also a licence caveat recorded for the GLM line in that same comparison: GLM-5.3 carries a **custom licence requiring security review for large Model-as-a-Service operators**, which should be checked before commercial redistribution. **Re-verify both the live rate and the licence before deploying.**
- **Architecture:** **Parameter count not disclosed in the sources reached.** For family scale, the independent comparison records full-size GLM-5.3 at **753B total parameters with active parameters unpublished**; the Flash tier is smaller but its figures were not found.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 87/100.** The headline is genuine and unique in this dataset: **GDPval-AA at 1,773 Elo is the only figure in this entire research pass that clears the methodology's ~1750+ frontier reference**, placing it nominally above Claude Fable 5.1 (1,735) and GPT-6.1 Sol (1,575) on professional knowledge work. **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 84.3% is reproduced exactly by Artificial Analysis's own leaderboard**, **Toolathlon-Verified reaches 78.4%**, **AA AutomationBench at 60.4% is measured higher than the vendor's own 48.8%**, and a **published Tau3-Banking figure of 47.2%** satisfies a reference that almost no model in this dataset can offer. Four deductions hold it to 87: the GDPval figure is **vendor-reported and unreplicated**, so the single most important number here is unverified; **Vals AI measures Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 62.9% against the 84.3% that two other sources agree on — a 21.4-point harness spread**; **AA Terminal-Bench 4.0 collapses to 32.8%**; and **Claw-Eval, MCP-Atlas and OSWorld are unreported**, with Agents' Last Exam (26.3%), EnterpriseOps-Gym (33.2%) and GDP.pdf (15.4%) all weak.
- **Reasoning: 82/100.** **GPQA Diamond clears the 90%+ frontier reference under Artificial Analysis (91.2%)** though not under Vals AI (86.4%), **AA-HLE at 39.9% lands 0.1 points short of the 40%+ reference**, **AA-LCR reaches 80.0%**, MMLU-Pro is independently confirmed at 86.1%, and **the Intelligence Index of 41.8% is level with Claude Opus 4.8** and well above every Flash-tier peer here. Four deductions: the Index remains **18 points below the 60+ frontier marker**; **MLCR-AA at 51.1% is 29 points below AA-LCR**, showing multi-document reasoning is much weaker than single-document; **CritPt at 15.4%** is mid-range; and the **AA-Omniscience Index of 7.5%** is low, with accuracy and hallucination rate not separately published so factual reliability cannot be properly assessed.
- **Context window: 95/100.** A **1,000,000-token window** places it in the ≥1M tier (95–100), supported by **AA-LCR at 80.0%** — a respectable long-context reasoning measurement. It sits at the tier floor rather than above it for three reasons: the 100 condition requires **≥98% retrieval at 512K+** and **no MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks figure exists**; **MLCR-AA at 51.1%** indicates that reasoning across multiple long documents degrades substantially; and **max output tokens are undocumented**.
- **Multimodal: 86/100.** **MMVU at 80.5% evidences video input**, which places it in the methodology's "+video/PDF in = 75–90" band, and the measured breadth within that band is good: **CharXiv at 89.4% is strong chart reasoning** — within half a point of Claude Opus 4.8's with-tools figure — with Chartography at 78.0% and OfficeQA Pro at 62.4% corroborating on document-adjacent work. Three things keep it below the band ceiling: **BabyVision at 53.4%** shows basic perceptual reasoning is weak; **there is no MMMU-Pro, OmniDocBench or OCR benchmark**, so general visual understanding and document extraction are unmeasured; and **all five multimodal figures are vendor-reported** with no independent replication, on a model where the vendor-independent spread elsewhere reaches 21 points.
- **Coding: 88/100.** **SWE-bench at 92.0%, independently measured by Vals AI, is the highest SWE-bench figure recorded anywhere in this research pass** — and being independent rather than vendor-reported makes it the strongest single coding datapoint here. **LiveCodeBench at 80.5% is also Vals-measured**, and **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 84.3% is doubly sourced** and essentially at the methodology's 85%+ coding reference. Four deductions: **DeepSWE at 63.4% is roughly 11 points below the 74%+ frontier reference**; **AA-SciCode at 51.6% is below the 55%+ reference**; **FrontierSWE v2 at 18.1% on Proximal's independent leaderboard is very low**, matching the pattern seen for other models there and warning that merge-acceptance quality may be far weaker than SWE-bench suggests; and **SWE-bench Pro and Vibe Code Bench are absent**. Note also that the 92.0% is on Vals' SWE-bench harness, not SWE-bench Verified, so it is not directly comparable to Verified figures elsewhere in this dataset.
- **Cost efficiency: 88/100.** **This score carries a caveat: no rate card was verified for the Flash tier specifically.** What is verified and what drives the score is structural. **The weights are public on Hugging Face**, so the per-token rate is escapable entirely for organisations able to self-host — and unlike the 2.4T-parameter Qwen3.8-Max or the 2.8T Kimi K3, a Flash-tier model is a realistic single-node deployment. For hosted context, the **full-size GLM-5.3 lists at $1.40 / $4.40**, well below the $3/$15 anchor, and the Flash tier sits below that. Three deductions: the **Flash rate is unconfirmed** and must be re-verified; the GLM line carries a **custom licence requiring security review for large Model-as-a-Service operators**, which is a real constraint on commercial redistribution and must be checked; and **no free tier** is documented.
- **Overall Score: 87.6/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (87 + 82 + 95 + 86 + 88) / 5 = 87.6 — the strongest open-weight Flash-tier model in this research pass, and the only model in it that clears the GDPval frontier reference. Choose it for **professional knowledge work** (GDPval-AA 1,773 Elo, uniquely above the ~1750+ reference, though vendor-reported); for **repository-scale coding**, where **Vals AI's independently measured SWE-bench 92.0% is the highest figure in this dataset** and LiveCodeBench reaches 80.5%; for **terminal agency** (Terminal-Bench 2.1 84.3%, reproduced exactly by Artificial Analysis); for **chart and video analysis** (CharXiv 89.4%, MMVU 80.5%); and for **any deployment where owning the weights matters** at a tier small enough to self-host realistically. Three cautions should govern adoption. **The harness spread is large**: Terminal-Bench 2.1 ranges from 62.9% to 84.3% depending on who measured, so run your own evaluation before trusting the vendor's launch table. **The single most important number — GDPval-AA 1,773 — is vendor-reported and unreplicated**, and it is the one figure that would justify choosing this over much more expensive peers. And **check the licence**: the GLM line's custom terms require security review for large Model-as-a-Service operators, which may block redistribution business models.
