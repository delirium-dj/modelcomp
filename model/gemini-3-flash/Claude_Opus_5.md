## Model card

- **Name:** Gemini 3 Flash. Variant/alias flag: independent leaderboards index it as **`gemini-3-flash-preview`**, so the publicly benchmarked artefact appears to be a **preview build** rather than a GA release. A benchmark tracker classifies it as **"Non-Reasoning"** — it is the Flash tier before Google added the `thinking_level` control that 3.5 Flash onward carry.
- **Short description:** The original Gemini 3 Flash, and **the deepest-legacy Flash entry in this queue.** A composite tracker places it **#60 of 783 at 56.3/100**, against Gemini 3.5 Flash's 63.95, 3.6 Flash's 65.16, 3.7 Flash's 67.98 and 3.8 Flash's 73.95 — **four Flash generations have shipped since.** Its evidence base is thin (24 benchmarks) and the picture it paints is sharply bimodal: **LiveCodeBench at 85.6% and MMLU-Pro at 88.6%, both independently measured by Vals AI**, sit alongside **an Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index of 17.9% — the lowest recorded in this entire research pass** — and **a 92.4% hallucination rate** producing a negative reliability index.
- **Provider / access:** Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Vertex AI and OpenRouter (`google/gemini-3-flash-preview`).
- **Release / knowledge:** Released in the Gemini 3 cycle, before Gemini 3.5 Flash (2026-05-19). **No verified public release date or knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `gemini-3-flash-preview` (the ID used by independent leaderboards and OpenRouter).
- **Context window:** **1,000,000 tokens.** Max output: no verified figure found. **AA-LCR at 55.3% is the lowest long-context reasoning figure recorded in this entire research pass** — 28.7 points below DeepSeek V4.1 Flash's 84.0% and 24.7 below Gemini 3.5 Flash's 69.3%.
- **Modalities:** **Text and image input → text output** on Gemini Flash-line precedent, evidenced by a published AA-MMMU-Pro figure. **No Google specification sheet for this model was retrievable**, so the modality list is inferred; later Flash generations document text, image, video, audio and PDF input, but **that breadth cannot be assumed for this one**.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **No rate card was verified for this model.** For family context, an independent pricing guide verified earlier in this research session positioned **"Gemini 3 Flash Preview" as a budget option below Gemini 3.5 Flash's $1.50/$9.00**, and the Flash-tier list has since settled at $1.50/$7.50 with introductory rates of $0.75/$3.75. **Re-verify before budgeting.**
- **Architecture:** Proprietary, closed weights. **Parameter count, architecture, training corpus and compute are all undisclosed** — Google discloses none of these for the Flash line.

### Raw benchmarks found

- **Gert Labs: 56.63%** (independent rankings)
- **JobBench: 11.4%** (independent paper) — **the lowest JobBench figure recorded here**, against Qwen3.8-Max's 53.4% and Claude Opus 4.6's 36.7%.
- GDPval-AA / Tau3-Banking / MCP-Atlas / Toolathlon / OSWorld / AutomationBench: **no verified public score found.**

- **AA-HLE: 15.0% (AA)** — **25 points below the 40%+ frontier reference**, and among the lowest HLE figures in this dataset.
- **AA-LCR: 55.3% (AA)** — **the lowest long-context reasoning figure recorded in this entire research pass.** No MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks figure.
- **CritPt: 1.4% (AA)** — effectively zero.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 64/100.** The one creditable element is that a **Claw-Eval figure exists at all (49.2%)** — the methodology explicitly penalises its absence, and only four models in this dataset have one. Everything else is weak or missing. **τ²-bench at 43.3% falls below the methodology's ~50%+ Tau-family frontier reference** and is by a wide margin the lowest τ² result in this research pass. **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 53.9% sits in the mid band**, far from the ~88% anchor. **JobBench at 11.4% is the lowest recorded here.** And **GDPval-AA, Tau3-Banking, MCP-Atlas, Toolathlon, OSWorld and AutomationBench are every one of them unreported** — six methodology references with no value. 64 reflects a model that can call tools but has no demonstrated agentic capability.
- **Reasoning: 60/100.** Two results are genuinely good and both are independent: **MMLU-Pro at 88.6% from Vals AI** and **AA Global-MMLU-Lite at 92.7%** show real breadth of knowledge, with **Epoch AI's FrontierMath v2 Tiers 1–3 at 35.6%** ahead of several nominally stronger models. Everything else is poor, and the pattern is consistent. **The Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index of 17.9% is the lowest recorded in this entire research pass** — below even the methodology's mid-band floor of 20. **AA-HLE at 15.0% is 25 points below the 40%+ reference.** **GPQA Diamond misses the 90%+ reference under both independent labs (87.9% Vals, 81.2% AA), which disagree by 6.7 points.** **CritPt is 1.4%**, **AA-IFBench is 55.1%**, and most seriously the **AA-Omniscience Index is negative at −4.3% on a 92.4% hallucination rate** — the second-worst confabulation rate in this dataset. At 45.8% accuracy it knows a fair amount and asserts almost all of it regardless of correctness.
- **Context window: 88/100.** This score deliberately sits **well below the ≥1M tier floor of 95**, and the justification is a measurement rather than an absence. The nominal window is **1,000,000 tokens**, but **AA-LCR at 55.3% is the lowest long-context reasoning figure recorded in this entire research pass** — 28.7 points below DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, 24.7 below its own successor Gemini 3.5 Flash, and 14.7 below Claude Opus 4.5's 200K-window result. A million-token window that reasons worse over long inputs than a 200K model is not a 1M capability in any operational sense. **No MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks figure exists** to contradict that reading, and max output tokens are undocumented. 88 credits the nominal capacity while recording that the one direct measurement of its usefulness is the worst available.
- **Multimodal: 66/100.** Input is **text and image** with text-only output, placing it in the methodology's "+image in = 60–70" band. **AA-MMMU-Pro at 78.6% is respectable and independently measured** — level with MiniMax M3 and ahead of Claude Opus 4.5 — and it is the only multimodal evidence that exists. Three things hold it mid-band: **no Google specification sheet was retrievable**, so even the image-only modality list is inferred rather than documented, and the richer text/image/video/audio/PDF surface that later Flash generations carry **cannot be assumed here**; **Design Arena Website at 1,203 Elo is low**; and **no CharXiv, document, OCR, video or audio benchmark exists**, so chart and document capability — the standard Flash-tier use case — is entirely unmeasured.
- **Coding: 72/100.** **LiveCodeBench at 85.6% from Vals AI is genuinely strong** — higher than Claude Sonnet 5 or MiniMax M3 on the same independent leaderboard — and **SWE-bench at 75.0%** is credible mid-field. That is the case for the score. Against it: **Vibe Code Bench at 20.20% is very low**, roughly a third of GPT-5.3 Codex's 61.77%, which says the model writes competent isolated code but cannot build working applications; **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 53.9% is far below the 85%+ coding reference**; and **SWE-bench Verified, SWE-bench Pro, DeepSWE, SciCode and the AA Coding Index are all absent**, so four of the band's references cannot be checked.
- **Cost efficiency: 68/100.** **This score carries an explicit caveat: no rate card was verified.** It rests on family positioning — an independent pricing guide placed **"Gemini 3 Flash Preview" as a budget option below Gemini 3.5 Flash's $1.50/$9.00**, and the Flash tier has since settled at $1.50/$7.50 list with $0.75/$3.75 introductory rates. A genuinely cheap Flash-tier model with LiveCodeBench at 85.6% and MMLU-Pro at 88.6% would be reasonable value. Four deductions hold it under 70: the **price is unconfirmed**; the publicly benchmarked artefact appears to be a **preview build**, with no GA guarantee; **no free tier** is documented; and the generational economics are decisive — **four Flash releases have followed, with 3.7 and 3.8 Flash both launching at $0.75/$3.75 introductory while scoring 11.7 and 17.7 composite points higher**. Paying anything for this model's capability when its successors cost the same or less is not defensible.
- **Overall Score: 70/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (64 + 60 + 88 + 66 + 72) / 5 = 70 — a deep-legacy preview model with two good independent results and a great deal of missing or poor evidence. What genuinely holds up: **LiveCodeBench at 85.6%**, **MMLU-Pro at 88.6%** and **AA Global-MMLU-Lite at 92.7%**, all independently measured — this model writes competent code and has broad multilingual knowledge. What does not: **the lowest Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index in this entire research pass (17.9%)**, **the lowest AA-LCR (55.3%)** despite a 1M window, **the lowest τ²-bench (43.3%)** and **the lowest JobBench (11.4%)**, with **a 92.4% hallucination rate producing a negative reliability index** and **Vibe Code Bench at 20.20%**. **Do not select this model for any new workload.** Its own successors are cheaper or the same price and better on every axis: **Gemini 3.8 Flash scores 73.95 against this model's 56.3** and launched at $0.75/$3.75 introductory, with documented text/image/video/audio/PDF input, three thinking levels, a 97.0% GDM-MRCR v2 128K average and DeepSWE at 73.7%. If this model is already in production the migration path is obvious; if it is under evaluation, note that **not one Google-published benchmark figure exists for it** — everything known comes from third parties, and most of it is unflattering.
