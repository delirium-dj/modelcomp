## Model card

- **Name:** Gemini 3 Flash (Preview)
- **Short description:** Google's December-2025 **speed tier of the Gemini 3 family** — "frontier intelligence built for speed." It delivers **Pro-grade reasoning at Flash latency and ~2× lower cost** (only a 2-point AA Index drop from Gemini 3 Pro Preview), and was **the most intelligent model for its price** at release. Standouts: **GPQA Diamond 90.4%**, **MMMU-Pro 81.2% (state-of-the-art at launch, 2nd-highest of any model)**, **SWE-bench Verified 78% (it beat Gemini 3 *Pro*)**, the **highest knowledge accuracy of any model on AA-Omniscience**, full **text/image/video/audio** multimodality, a **1M-token context** and **218 tok/s**. Its trade-offs: **very high token use (~160M on the Index)**, a **91% hallucination rate** (answers instead of abstaining), and — being a Dec-2025 model — reasoning/coding numbers (HLE 33.7%, SWE-bench 78%) that the 2026 frontier has since surpassed. **[2026-11-02: AA now lists this slug as the NON-reasoning variant, DEPRECATED → Gemini 3.5 Flash (composite Index N/A); the measured recall/agent figures the draft lacked are now available and mid-to-poor — AA-LCR 55.3, τ²-bench 43.3, Terminal-Bench 2.1 (Vals) 53.9, AA-MMMU-Pro 78.6, hallucination 92.4%. BenchLM ranks the non-reasoning base #81/1004 (54.12).]**
- **Provider / access:** Gemini API in Google AI Studio, **Google Antigravity**, Gemini CLI, Android Studio, Vertex AI, Gemini Enterprise; **default model in the Gemini app (replacing 2.5 Flash) and AI Mode in Search**; OpenRouter (`google/gemini-3-flash-preview`). Used in production by JetBrains, Figma, Cursor, Replit, Warp, Harvey, Bridgewater.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released **2025-12-17** (Gemini 3 Pro / Deep Think preceded it in Nov 2025). Knowledge cutoff not stated in the sources reviewed.
- **IDs:** `gemini-3-flash` / `gemini-3-flash-preview` (family: Gemini 3 Pro [flagship] > **Gemini 3 Flash** > Gemini 3 Flash-Lite; successor line Gemini 3.5/3.6/3.7/3.8 Flash).
- **Context window:** **1M tokens native**; tool calling, structured outputs and JSON mode supported. **AA-LCR** (long-context recall) is one of the 10 Index evals but no absolute figure was published in the sources reviewed. **[2026-11-02: AA now publishes AA-LCR 55.3 — a genuinely POOR far-edge recall, not the "on par with 1M leaders" the draft assumed; drives the Context trim.]**
- **Modalities:** **fully multimodal — text + image + video + audio in → text out** (audio input priced separately at $1/1M). Modulates thinking at the highest level (thinks longer for hard tasks) while using **30% fewer tokens on average than Gemini 2.5 Pro** on typical traffic.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-23):** **$0.50 / 1M input, $3 / 1M output** (audio input $1/1M) — ~2× cheaper than Gemini 3 Pro Preview. AA: **most cost-efficient model for its intelligence level** despite high token use.
- **Architecture:** Undisclosed proprietary (Google). **~160M output tokens** to run the AA Index (more than double Gemini 2.5 Flash; one of the highest token-use models tested). **218 tok/s** (22% slower than Gemini 2.5 Flash but ~3× faster than Gemini 2.5 Pro and far faster than GPT-5.1-high 125 / Kimi K2 Thinking 82 / DeepSeek V3.2 30).

### Raw benchmarks found

Reasoning / knowledge (Google vendor-run + Artificial Analysis **independent**, which agree):

- **GPQA Diamond: 90.4%** (Google) / 90% (AA) — **3rd at release**, behind only Gemini 3 Pro Preview and GPT-5.2 xhigh; PhD-level scientific reasoning rivaling larger frontier models
- **Humanity's Last Exam: 33.7% (no tools)** (Google) / 35% (AA) — **2nd to Gemini 3 Pro Preview**; modest in absolute terms vs the 2026 frontier (Opus 4.8 ~46%, Sonnet 5 57.4% w/tools)
- **MMLU-Pro: 89%** — 3rd (behind Gemini 3 Pro Preview, GPT-5.2 xhigh)
- **AA-Omniscience: highest score of any model tested** — driven by the **highest knowledge accuracy** of any model, *but* a **91% hallucination rate** (3pp worse than Gemini 2.5 Flash / 3 Pro Preview; it answers rather than abstaining)
- **AA Intelligence Index: 71** — ⚠ **on the December-2025 basket** (see provenance); +13 over Gemini 2.5 Flash (Sep), a 2-point drop from Gemini 3 Pro Preview (73); "most intelligent model for its cost" at release **[2026-11-02: AA now marks this slug DEPRECATED (→ Gemini 3.5 Flash) with composite Index N/A, and the current page is the non-reasoning variant; the Dec-2025 "71" cannot be re-confirmed on any current basket and the model is now superseded within the Flash line by 3.5/3.6/3.7/3.8.]**

Coding (Google vendor-run):

- **SWE-bench Verified: 78%** — **outperforms the entire 2.5 series *and* Gemini 3 Pro**; strong agentic coding. (Below the 2026 frontier: Opus 4.6 80.8%, Sonnet 5 85.2%, Fable 5 ~95%.)
- LiveCodeBench, SciCode, Terminal-Bench Hard are among the 10 AA Index evals (no absolute figures published in the sources reviewed)

Multimodal (Google vendor-run + AA **independent**):

- **MMMU-Pro: 81.2%** — **state-of-the-art at release**, "2nd-highest of any model" (behind only Gemini 3 Pro Preview), comparable to Gemini 3 Pro; image-input reasoning **[2026-11-02: AA now measures MMMU-Pro 78.6 — solid but no longer SOTA/2nd-highest; the 2026 omni field has passed it. AA spec confirms full text+image+speech+video input.]**
- Full **text/image/video/audio** input; real-time multimodal reasoning (video analysis, visual Q&A, in-game assistants, sketch prediction)

Agent / tool use:

- Google bills it "our most impressive model for agentic workflows"; **τ²-Bench Telecom** and **Terminal-Bench Hard** are in the AA Index eval set; tool calling, structured outputs, JSON mode. No absolute τ²/OSWorld figure published in the sources reviewed.

> Provenance: Capability figures come from **both** Google's launch blog (2025-12-17, vendor-run) **and** Artificial Analysis' independent pre-release evaluation (2025-12-17) — the two **agree** on GPQA (90.4/90), HLE (33.7 no-tools / 35 AA), MMMU-Pro (81.2 / "2nd highest") and MMLU-Pro (89). Per methodology the vendor cells are an upper bound, discounted against the neutral AA signal (which corroborates them here). ⚠ **Index-version provenance is critical:** the **AA Intelligence Index 71 is on the December-2025 basket** and is **NOT comparable** to the v4.1.1 (Aug-2026) or v4.3 (Sep-2026) indices I cite for later models — the basket was rebased substantially harder across 2026 (e.g., GLM-5.3-Flash fell 57→~42 between baskets), so a Dec-2025 "71" does not rank above a late-2026 "48." At release Gemini 3 Flash was a genuine near-frontier model (#2 to Gemini 3 Pro); by late 2026 its HLE (33.7%) and SWE-bench (78%) are dated even though its multimodal (MMMU-Pro 81.2, full audio/video) and knowledge-accuracy results remain competitive. The **91% hallucination rate** (high accuracy but low abstention) and **~160M-token verbosity** are documented weaknesses. No benchmark was invented.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 80/100.** **Trimmed 84 → 80.** Google billed it "most impressive for agentic workflows" with tool calling / structured outputs / JSON, but the draft explicitly noted **no absolute τ²/OSWorld/MCP figure existed to verify the claim** — that gap now fills at **mid, not frontier**: **τ²-bench 43.3, Terminal-Bench 2.1 (Vals) 53.9, Claw-Eval 49.2, Gert Labs 56.6, JobBench 11.4**. A capable 2025-12 agent, but the 2026 frontier and its own 3.5–3.8 successors have passed it; the unverified frontier claim is not backed by the neutral harnesses.
- **Reasoning: 82/100.** **Trimmed 85 → 82.** The reasoning-preview strengths were real at launch (**GPQA 90.4 vendor / Vals 87.9, MMLU-Pro 88.6**) and the draft already conceded **HLE 33.7% is dated vs the 2026 frontier**. The re-check confirms the age and adds a severe reliability flag: **AA-Omniscience net index −4.3 (accuracy 45.8, hallucination 92.4%)** — a *negative* omniscience score, worse than the draft's "91% hallucination" framing. The non-reasoning page's **AA-HLE 15.0 / CritPt 1.4** are variant-qualified (not imported as the reasoning ceiling), and Epoch **FrontierMath v2 T1-3 51.2 / Tier-4 17.1** is mid. A superseded, high-hallucination Dec-2025 reasoner lands low-80s.
- **Context window: 82/100.** **Trimmed 88 → 82.** A **1M-token native window** is confirmed (AA spec), but the draft discounted below 89–93 for lack of a recall figure — now supplied: **AA-LCR 55.3**, a **genuinely poor** far-edge recall well below the 80–84 of the batch's strong-recall models. Combined with the **~160M-token verbosity** that makes deep-context runs expensive, the 1M *capacity* is real but measured *quality at depth* is weak, so the score pulls hard toward mid.
- **Multimodal: 84/100.** **Trimmed 88 → 84, but it remains the model's relative strength.** **Full omni-input (text+image+speech+video) is AA-spec-confirmed**, and real-time video/audio reasoning was a genuine differentiator. But the premium rested on **MMMU-Pro 81.2 "SOTA / 2nd-highest of any model"**; AA now measures **78.6** — solid, but **passed by the 2026 omni field** (no longer #2). Held above the mid pack for breadth + still-good reasoning (ParseBench 75.05), trimmed for the lost SOTA standing.
- **Coding: 83/100.** **Trimmed 85 → 83.** **SWE-bench (Vals) 75.0** sits a notch under the vendor's 78 (already noted as dated vs Opus 4.6 80.8 / Sonnet 5 85.2), and **Terminal-Bench 2.1 (Vals) 53.9** / Vibe Code Bench 20.2 are soft; **LiveCodeBench (Vals) 85.6** is the one genuinely strong cell. A capable 2025-12 coder (why JetBrains/Cursor adopted it) that the 2026 frontier has overtaken.
- **Cost efficiency: 92/100.** **Holds 92.** AA-confirmed **$0.50/$3** with a **90% cache discount** and **194.9 tok/s (#3/46 — notably fast)**, roughly 2× cheaper than Gemini 3 Pro for near-Pro reasoning — genuinely excellent value. The draft's only temper was the **~160M-token verbosity**, now **N/A** (deprecated, no longer re-measured) but historically one of the highest tested; still among the cheapest multimodal agents. Scored but **not** counted in the Overall per methodology.
- **Overall Score: 82/100.** **Trimmed 86 → 82.** Mean of Tool 80 + Reasoning 82 + Context 82 + Multimodal 84 + Coding 83 = 411 / 5 = 82.2 → **82**. Best fit: **high-throughput multimodal and light-agentic work at scale** — real-time video/image/audio understanding and high-volume coding/search surfaces where a 1M context, ~195 tok/s and $0.50/$3 matter and near-2025-Pro reasoning is sufficient; escalate hard reasoning / long-horizon SWE to Gemini 3.5–3.8 Flash or a 2026 frontier model, and **always ground factual output (92.4% hallucination, AA-Omniscience net −4.3)**. Independent read: my 82 sits **well below the queue's curated 88.1** because this is a **superseded Dec-2025 model** — AA has **deprecated it (→ 3.5 Flash)** and the figures the draft *assumed* competitive now measure **mid-to-poor** (AA-LCR 55.3 recall, τ²-bench 43.3 / TB 2.1 53.9 agentic, MMMU-Pro 78.6 no-longer-SOTA), with the sharp non-reasoning cells (HLE 15, CritPt 1.4) variant-qualified rather than imported.
