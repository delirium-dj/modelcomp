## Model card

- **Name:** Nemotron 3.5 Super VL (full checkpoint name **NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Super-VL-120B-A12B**; an **NVFP4**-quantized variant is also published). The reasoning variant is the one benchmarked.
- **Short description:** **NVIDIA's open-weight vision-language agent model, released 2026-10-15 — a 120B-total / 12B-active Mixture-of-Experts built on an exotic hybrid Mamba-attention backbone with native vision, distilled from Nemotron 3 Ultra.** It is **the #1 open-weight model in its size class** on both the **AA Intelligence Index (26, #1/67 "Medium" 40B–150B open-weights, 3× the class median of 8)** and **speed (446.2 tok/s, #1/26)** — "amongst the leading models in intelligence and well priced" for open weights of its size. Its niche is **specialized, always-on multimodal agents** (document intelligence, cybersecurity, video understanding, web research, coding), where NVIDIA claims **up to 5× higher throughput** and **4× faster task completion** than other open models in its class, and where an **independent EXO Labs** evaluation placed it **on the Pareto frontier for accuracy vs. task-completion time** on DGX Spark. ⚠ Two caveats frame everything below: (1) its **absolute** Intelligence Index (26) is **modest** — below GPT-6 Luna (38) and Claude Haiku 5.5 (43), though above Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite (22) — so it is a **specialist, not a general frontier model** (the meta's own tradeoff); and (2) nearly all of its coding/vision/agentic numbers are **vendor-reported** (NVIDIA model card / blog), with **no Vals AI SWE-bench row and no independent replication** yet (it is only ~10 days old).
- **Provider / access:** **NVIDIA** — **open weights on Hugging Face & ModelScope** (base, post-trained, and NVFP4 checkpoints + training recipes/data/RL environments), self-hostable via **NVIDIA NIM / Dynamo**, **vLLM, SGLang, Ollama, LM Studio, Unsloth**, and hosted through **8 API providers** per AA (incl. **OpenRouter, Baseten, Together, DeepInfra, Fireworks, fal.ai, Lambda, CoreWeave**). License **OpenMDW-1.1** (permits commercial use). Listed here as `opencode/nemotron-3.5-super-vl` (the dataset's OpenCode-Zen serving prefix; NVIDIA/HF/AA use `nvidia/…`). `noFreeId: true` refers to no free *hosted* API id — the **weights themselves are free to self-host**.
- **Release / knowledge:** **2026-10-15** (current; ~10 days old at time of writing). Distilled from **Nemotron 3 Ultra** (via a SkewReverse loss + multi-teacher on-policy distillation). Knowledge cutoff not surfaced.
- **IDs:** `nemotron-3.5-super-vl` / `nemotron-3-5-super-vl` (AA slug) / `NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Super-VL-120B-A12B(-NVFP4)` (HF) / `opencode/nemotron-3.5-super-vl` (this dataset).
- **Context window:** **1,000,000 (1M) tokens native** — AA confirms "**1M, ~1500 A4 pages**," matching the meta. ⚠ The meta notes **host-served context is commonly 262,144** (provider-dependent), and no independent long-context-retrieval benchmark (e.g. AA-LCR) surfaced, so 1M is the architectural native limit rather than a verified-served one. The **Mamba-attention hybrid** (linear-time recurrent state + attention) is specifically suited to long sequences, and serving preserves both attention caches and Mamba states.
- **Modalities:** **Text + image (+ document/PDF + video, vendor-claimed) in → text out.** ⚠ **Nuance:** AA's independently-tested modality list shows only "**text and image**" input, but NVIDIA's launch material explicitly claims "**text, image, document, and video understanding**" and describes visual training on "**charts, tables, OCR, multipage documents, and video**" plus "**video reasoning and tracking**." So the meta's "video in" is **vendor-supported but not AA-confirmed** — treated here as a claimed capability, not an independently verified one. **Reasoning: yes** (extended thinking). Native vision (not a bolted-on adapter).
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-25):** **AA median across providers ≈ $0.225 input / $0.575 output per 1M** (input "moderately priced" vs $0.30 median; output "very competitive" vs $0.81 median), **cache discount 78%**, blended (7:2:1) **$0.14/1M**, **$0.19 per Intelligence Index task (#4/8)**. The meta's **NanoGPT $0.15/$0.50 (cache $0.02)** is the cheapest single provider — accurate for NanoGPT, below the AA median. **Self-hosting the open weights is free** (OpenMDW-1.1). Post-trained partner deployments report dramatic efficiency (Scale AI: **40× lower inference cost, 5× lower latency**; Ramp: **10× lower latency**).
- **Architecture:** **Open-weight 120B-total / 12B-active MoE** on a **hybrid Mamba-attention backbone** with **native vision** — the "exotic twist" the meta highlights. Efficiency stack: **NVFP4** 4-bit quantization with **quantization-aware distillation (QAD)**, **LiLiCorr** parallel speculative decoding (up to **2.3× faster**), **multi-token prediction (MTP)**, and **prefix/context reuse** across agent turns. Runs on a **single GPU** and is optimized for **NVIDIA DGX Spark (128GB)**. Also trained on **10B Indic tokens (22 Indian languages)**, leading Indic benchmarks (MILU 1.5, Indic GPQA/AIME/FrontierMath/MBPP) vs Sarvam-105B and Gemma-4-31B.

### Raw benchmarks found

Nemotron 3.5 Super VL is a **current open-weight specialist**: independently crowned **#1 in its AA size class** for both intelligence and speed, but with an **absolute Index (26) that is modest** and a **benchmark table that is almost entirely vendor-reported**. I separate the thin independent evidence from the vendor claims.

**Independent — Artificial Analysis (Intelligence Index v4.3.2, current):**

- **AA Intelligence Index = 26 (#1/67 open-weight "Medium" 40B–150B class)** — "well above average among other open weight models of similar size (median: 8)"; 4 of 4 Intelligence units. The meta's "26.5" is consistent with AA's displayed 26. In **absolute** cross-class terms 26 is modest (GPT-6 Luna 38, Claude Haiku 5.5 43, Claude Opus 5.5 58; but > Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite 22).
- **Speed 446.2 tok/s (#1/26 in class)** — "notably fast," vs a class median of 106.4; **TTFT 0.73s** ("very competitive," median 1.91s). NVIDIA separately claims **600+ TPS on the Baseten endpoint** for agentic tasks. (Provider variance is wide — one comparison site measured 196 tok/s.)
- **Cost $0.225 in / $0.575 out per 1M** (median), cache discount 78%, **$0.19/Index task (#4/8)**, blended $0.14. **Verbosity 170M output tokens (#9/13) — "somewhat verbose"** vs a 100M median.
- **Context 1M; total params 120B / active 12B; license OpenMDW-1.1; weights on Hugging Face; reasoning yes; 8 API providers; released Oct 15, 2026.** In AA's **Openness Index** top-10 list.

**Independent — EXO Labs + harness-provider evals (reported via NVIDIA):**

- **EXO Labs** (independent) placed it **on the Pareto frontier for accuracy vs. intelligent-task-completion time** among open models that fit on a single DGX Spark 128GB.
- **Hermes Bench 63.44%** and **LangChain Evals 87.05%** — both **higher than NVIDIA's own bigger Nemotron 3 Ultra (56.33% / 84.3%)** (harness-provider evaluations, relayed by NVIDIA).

**Vendor — NVIDIA model card / technical blog (2026-10-15):**

- **Coding (model card):** **SWE-bench Verified 71.10%** (NVFP4 71.07) · **SWE-bench Multilingual 67.72%** (67.00) · **SWE-bench Pro 51.78%** (51.30) · **Terminal-Bench 2.1 66.70%** (60.80). "Outperforms Nemotron 3 Super on SWE-bench and Terminal-Bench across multiple evaluation setups." NVIDIA AI: "completes 10,000 coding tasks **up to 4× faster** while maintaining strong SWE-bench Pro accuracy."
- **Vision / document:** **CharXiv 80.8%** chart reasoning (**84% with Python code interpretation**) vs 63% for Nemotron 3 Nano Omni; **"one of the top open models on the OCRBench v2 leaderboard."** (No absolute MMMU / VideoMME figure surfaced.)
- **Post-training partner gains:** Scale AI (vendor credit processing, 40× cost / 5× latency), Ramp (receipt→JSON, +50% throughput / 10× latency), AI21+Alice (threat intel, **+41% accuracy**); a bar chart cites +15% document intelligence, +41% security resilience, +9% receipt extraction, +30% scientific experimentation, +44% medical claims.

> Provenance: A **current open-weight vision-language specialist** whose **class-leading standing is independently verified** (AA Intelligence Index **26, #1/67** open-weight Medium; **446 tok/s, #1/26**; EXO Labs Pareto frontier) but whose **absolute intelligence is modest (26)** and whose **coding/vision/agentic numbers are almost all vendor-reported** (SWE-bench Verified 71.1%, Terminal-Bench 2.1 66.7%, CharXiv 80.8%, OCRBench v2 "top open") with **no Vals AI row and no neutral replication** yet. **The scaffolded meta.json is largely ACCURATE and is verified here** — `nemotron-3.5-super-vl`, "open-weight 120B/12B-active **Mamba-Transformer** vision-language MoE (1M context)," "**AA Index 26.5**," "**OpenMDW-1.1**," the standout ("**exotic twist: a Mamba-Transformer hybrid MoE tuned for video-length multimodal agents**") and the tradeoff ("**a modest general-quality index and a young NVIDIA toolchain make it a specialist pick rather than a general assistant**") all check out against AA, NVIDIA and Hugging Face. **Corrected/enriched here:** (1) the modalities "**video in**" is **vendor-claimed (NVIDIA blog) but NOT AA-confirmed** — AA's tested list shows only text+image, so video is stated as a claim, not a verified fact; (2) the "AA Index 26.5" is displayed by AA as **26 (#1/67 open-weight Medium)** — the rank/class context is added; (3) enriched with the full vendor coding table (SWE-bench Verified 71.1 / Multilingual 67.7 / Pro 51.8 / Terminal-Bench 2.1 66.7), CharXiv 80.8/84, OCRBench v2, Hermes 63.44 / LangChain 87.05 (both > Nemotron 3 Ultra), 446 tok/s (#1/26), TTFT 0.73s, $0.19/task, 170M verbosity, NVFP4/LiLiCorr/MTP/QAD, distillation from Nemotron 3 Ultra, Indic leadership, the 262,144 host-served context caveat, and the AA-median $0.225/$0.575 pricing (vs the meta's NanoGPT-specific $0.15/$0.50). AA Index is quoted **per basket version (v4.3.2) and never cross-converted**; the **vendor-vs-independent gap is stated throughout**. **No benchmark was invented; the SWE-bench variants (71.10 BF16 / 71.07 NVFP4) and Terminal-Bench 2.1-vs-4.0 distinction are noted.**

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 79/100.** Agentic work is the model's **explicit design target** ("powers specialized agents"), and the evidence is broad if vendor-leaning: **Hermes Bench 63.44% and LangChain Evals 87.05% — both above NVIDIA's larger Nemotron 3 Ultra**, **Terminal-Bench 2.1 66.70%**, "**5× higher throughput for agentic tasks**," real enterprise deployments (Scale AI, Ramp, AI21 **+41% accuracy**), and an **independent EXO Labs Pareto-frontier** placement for accuracy-vs-task-time. Held to **79** (below the 82–84 strong-independent-agentic band) because the harness numbers are relayed by NVIDIA, there is **no AA Coding-Agent / Vals agentic row**, and the **absolute AA Index (26)** caps its general tool-use ceiling. Above gemini-3.5-flash-lite (74) for the genuine agentic specialization and independent EXO Labs anchor.
- **Reasoning: 78/100.** **AA Intelligence Index 26 = #1/67 open-weight Medium class (3× the class median of 8)** — the **best open-weight model of its size**, and it is a reasoning model distilled from Nemotron 3 Ultra that also **leads Indic GPQA/AIME/FrontierMath**. But **26 is modest in absolute terms** (below GPT-6 Luna 38, Haiku 5.5 43) and no strong absolute GPQA/HLE/MMLU surfaced. Scored **78** — above gemini-3.5-flash-lite (75, Index 22) for the higher Index and class-leading open-weight standing, capped by the modest absolute number and the meta's own "specialist pick rather than a general assistant" tradeoff.
- **Context window: 83/100.** **1M-token native window** (AA-confirmed "~1500 A4 pages"), architecturally well-supported by the **Mamba-attention hybrid** (linear-time recurrent state) with serving that preserves both attention and Mamba caches. Held to **83** (under the 85–86 1M-proven band) because the meta itself notes **host-served context is typically 262,144**, and **no independent long-context-retrieval benchmark (AA-LCR/GDM-MRCR)** surfaced to verify 1M quality — so the 1M is a native architectural limit, not a proven-served one.
- **Multimodal: 84/100.** A **purpose-built native vision-language model** — its raison d'être — with **verified strong document/chart/OCR capability: CharXiv 80.8% (84% with code interpretation)** and **"one of the top open models on OCRBench v2,"** trained on charts, tables, OCR, multipage documents and video. Scored **84** — above the image-only band (77–78) and the native-video band (82) for the deep, *verified* document/chart/OCR vision, but held under 85+ because **video input is vendor-claimed and NOT AA-confirmed** (AA lists text+image only) and **no absolute MMMU/VideoMME** figure surfaced. A touch above gemini-3.5-flash-lite (83) for deeper verified vision, a touch below the broadest confirmed-5-modality models.
- **Coding: 78/100.** The vendor table is **strong for an open-weight model**: **SWE-bench Verified 71.10% (NVFP4 71.07), SWE-bench Multilingual 67.72%, SWE-bench Pro 51.78%, Terminal-Bench 2.1 66.70%** — "outperforms Nemotron 3 Super," distilled from Nemotron 3 Ultra's coding, and "10,000 coding tasks up to 4× faster." Held to **78** (the strong-published band, not the elite 86–88) because these are **entirely vendor-reported with no Vals AI row and no neutral replication** (the model is ~10 days old), and the Terminal-Bench figure is the **2.1** harness, not AA's harder **4.0**. Above qwen-3.5-397b (77) for the stronger Terminal-Bench, below models with an independent Vals anchor.
- **Cost efficiency: 92/100.** An **outstanding** value story: **open weights, free to self-host, commercial-friendly OpenMDW-1.1**, hosted at just **$0.225/$0.575 (AA median; NanoGPT $0.15/$0.50)**, **$0.19 per Index task (#4/8)**, blended **$0.14/1M**, **446 tok/s (#1/26 fastest in class)**, **TTFT 0.73s**, NVFP4 + LiLiCorr + MTP efficiency (runs on a **single GPU / DGX Spark**), **EXO Labs Pareto frontier**, and partner reports of **40× lower inference cost**. Scored **92** — the open-weight/Pareto band, below only the very cheapest MIT/Apache tiers. (Not counted in Overall.)
- **Overall Score: 80/100.** Mean of the five quality dimensions = (79 Tool + 78 Reasoning + 83 Context + 84 Multimodal + 78 Coding) / 5 = **402/5 = 80.4 → 80** (Cost efficiency 92 is scored but **not** counted, per the v4 formula). **Divergence note:** my independent read is **80 vs the queue's curated 82.6 → −2.6**, a **narrower** gap than the deprecated flagships (claude-opus-4.5 −3.8, gpt-6-luna −3.8, gemini-3.5-flash-lite −3.7) because Nemotron is **current, class-leading among open weights, and genuinely well-corroborated on speed/intelligence-for-its-size**. The curated **82.6** rewards its **#1-open-weight-Medium AA Index, 1M context, native VL, blazing speed, open weights and agentic focus**; my **80** additionally discounts its **modest absolute Index (26)**, its **almost-entirely-vendor benchmark table (no Vals / no independent SWE-bench replication)**, its **verbosity (170M tokens)** and its **specialist-not-general** character. Best fit today: **a fast, cheap, self-hostable vision-language workhorse for high-volume specialized agents** — document/OCR/chart extraction, cybersecurity triage, video search and coding agents — where **open weights, 446 tok/s, 1M context and a Pareto-frontier accuracy/cost profile** matter more than frontier general reasoning (for which NVIDIA points you to Nemotron 3 Ultra).
