## Model card

- **Name:** Mistral Large 4 — "**ML4**", nicknamed "**Le Chonk**" in Mistral's own launch post; API surface is the **public preview** (Artificial Analysis titles its page "Mistral Large 4 Preview", BenchLM's row is "Mistral Large 4 / variant: base")
- **Short description:** Mistral's flagship: a **1 trillion-parameter, 52 B-active sparse MoE**, "**natively multimodal**" model trained from scratch for coding, agentic workflows, cybersecurity, finance/law knowledge work and visual grounding. Mistral positions it as "the frontier of **open-weight** performance" and explicitly as the strongest open model built **outside China** in cyber — i.e. a sovereignty play as much as a capability play ("Forged in Europe", trained on **3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs** in Mistral's own European datacentres, served on the same infrastructure).
- **Provider / access:** **Proprietary at the moment, open weights promised.** The launch post states "**Weights drop end of this month**" (October 2026) and that "until then, we are red-teaming the model … with cybersecurity leaders, vetted partners, and state authorities, who will access the same model with **reduced moderation and expanded cyber capabilities**". So the registry entry documents a **preview checkpoint**, not the eventual open-weight artefact, and **no licence has been announced yet** — do not assume Apache 2.0. Access today: preview API on Mistral Studio + Mistral's own serving; BenchLM's "Source Type: **Pending**" reflects exactly this unfinished state.
- **Release / knowledge:** launched **2026-10-06** (vendor post dated October 6, 2026; AA: "Released October 2026") — i.e. **two days old at the time of writing**. No knowledge-cutoff date has been published; Mistral only says training is "from scratch" with "a significant share … multilingual, spanning **more than 160 languages**, including every official EU language", and that architecture details, further benchmarks and post-training methodology will be shared "as we work toward releasing the weights". Treat every current number as provisional — the vendor itself says the model "continues to improve rapidly".
- **IDs:** `mistral-large-4` (Mistral Studio/API), curated `opencode/mistral-large-4`, BenchLM `benchlm.ai/models/mistral-large-4`, Artificial Analysis `artificialanalysis.ai/models/mistral-large-4` (page labelled **Preview**); family predecessor on BenchLM is `mistral-large-3` (33.27).
- **Context window:** **524k tokens** per Artificial Analysis' technical-spec block ("~786 A4 pages"). **Three-way conflict flag:** curated `meta.json` says **131,072 total (32,768 out)**, AA says **524k**, BenchLM says **1M**. The independent aggregator (AA) is the only source that states a measured window, so 524 K is what this report scores; the curated figure is ~4× too small and the BenchLM figure is ~2× too large, and neither is corroborated. Max output is **not published by the vendor** — the curated "32,768 out" is the only claim and it is unverified (if true it triggers the methodology's sub-64 K output caveat).
- **Modalities:** **text + image in, text out.** AA: "supports text and image input, outputs text"; the vendor post calls it "natively multimodal" with demos in dense-scene grounding, engineering-drawing inspection, PDF evidence retrieval and gigapixel satellite imagery. **No audio in, no video in, no image/audio output.** **Curated conflict:** `meta.json` says "Text in/out", which under-states the model — the vision capability is one of its headline features.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-08):** Artificial Analysis reports **$1.36 / $4.18 per 1M input/output** with a **90 % cache discount** and **$1.13 per Intelligence-Index task** (#56 of 226) — "moderately priced" input (median $2.00) and "competitively priced" output (median $10.00). **Curated conflict:** `meta.json` lists **$2.00 / $6.00**, which matches neither AA's first-party/blended figure nor any vendor price sheet quoted in the launch post (Mistral has not published preview pricing in the announcement). Expect both numbers to change at general availability. `noFreeId: true` in the curated file, though the vendor invites free preview traffic on Mistral Studio.
- **Architecture:** **1,000 B total / 52 B active** parameters (vendor), sparse MoE, multimodal from pretraining, trained from scratch on 3,800 Grace Blackwell GPUs. AA classifies the measured configuration as the **reasoning** variant ("This page shows the reasoning version of this model. A non-reasoning variant may also exist"); BenchLM types it "Hybrid". Serving: **116.1 output tok/s** (#37 of 226 — "notably fast" vs median 75), but **"very verbose"**: 200 M tokens generated across the full Index run against a median of 81 M (#105 of 226).
- **Identity flags:** (1) The folder documents a **preview** of an unreleased-final model — re-verification is mandatory once weights land. (2) It is *not* the same object as `model/mistral-medium-3.5/` or the `Ministral`/`Small` lines. (3) "Open-weight" is a **promise**, not a fact, at this date — no HF repo, no licence text, no parameter-level technical report exists yet. (4) Vendor's own safety framing (reduced-moderation cyber tier, refusal-rate results) is a deployment consideration worth recording.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 72/100.** A genuinely strong agentic profile that stops short of the frontier gate: **AutomationBench 59.9 %** across 657 real business workflows, **AA-Briefcase 1,393 Elo**, **GDPval-AA 1,424** (46.2 % normalized) — above the methodology's ~900–1,200 mid band but below the ~1,750 frontier anchor — **Finance Agent v2 54.7 %**, **GDP.pdf 18.6 %**, plus cyber-agentic depth (**Cybench 93 %**, AA Cyber Index top-five, 82 % on reproduce-and-patch). **Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 26.8 % AA / 28.3 % vendor** is the weak leg. No **τ²/τ³/Toolathlon/Claw-Eval/OSWorld** row exists for this ID at all, so the contract's missing-harness penalty applies, and the whole picture comes from a two-day-old preview whose serving configuration AA labels "reasoning variant".
- **Reasoning: 68/100.** **AA Intelligence Index 38** (#64/226, median 26) is above the mid band's 20–35 reference but well under the 60 frontier anchor, and it is a *composite* rather than a frontier signal. **AA-HLE 35.0 %** sits just below the 40 % frontier gate; **AA-LCR 81.3 %** is a strong long-context reasoning result; **AA-SciCode 54.2 %** and the vendor's SciCode-Verified SOTA claim (unquantified) point at real scientific reasoning, backed by an independent-ish **CritPt 10.6 %** that is respectable on a physics-reasoning harness. The deduction is faithfulness: **AA-Omniscience index −5.3 with only 25.8 % accuracy** (hallucination 41.9 %, refusal-heavy behaviour inflating the gap) means knowledge reliability is a clear weakness, and no GPQA/MMLU-Pro/AIME row exists to test the harder end.
- **Context window: 86/100.** Scored on **AA's measured 524k window** — the 500 K–1 M tier (85–94) — with the tier's midpoint rather than its top because **no MRCR/AI-Needle retrieval measurement exists** for the ID and because the three sources disagree badly (curated 131,072 / BenchLM 1M / AA 524k). **Unresolved caveat:** the only max-output claim on record is the curated **32,768**, which is unverified; if it is accurate the sub-64 K output ceiling drags this dimension down materially (long-window retrieval is worthless if you cannot emit a long answer), and if the vendor's real limit is higher the dimension should rise toward 92. This is the single most important number to re-check after general availability.
- **Multimodal: 70/100.** **Text + image in, text out** — the methodology's +image band (60–70), placed at its ceiling because the vision claim is substantiated rather than nominal: **AA-MMMU-Pro 76.4 %** (independent), vendor **Dense 200 grounding 42 % vs GPT-6-Astra 41 %**, chart work (ChartQA Pro, GDP.pdf) and gigapixel satellite/engineering-drawing demos that combine grounding with tool use. It cannot enter the 75–90 video/PDF tier: **no video input, no audio input, no image/audio output**, and only a portion of the vision evidence is third-party measured. The curated "Text in/out" would have scored this 15 — corrected against two independent-of-each-other sources (AA spec block + vendor capability demos).
- **Coding: 80/100.** The best-evidenced dimension after agentic work, and the clearest "frontier-adjacent, not frontier" case. Against the contract's coding gates ML4 clears **SciCode 54.2 → 55-ish** (54.2 AA, vendor claims open-weight SOTA on SciCode-Verified), gets **DeepSWE v1.1 61.7 %** (gate is 74+) and **Terminal-Bench 4.0 28.3 %** (a much harder harness than 2.1, so not comparable to the 85 % anchor), while **Vibe Code Bench 78.40 % (Vals)** is outstanding — the highest value recorded on that harness anywhere in this registry so far, and independently run. Add **SWE-Atlas-QnA 59.4 %**, a vendor **Coding Agent Index of 49.8 %** (claimed ahead of DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 and Qwen3.8 Max), and a **blind Surge AI human ranking of 3.74, second only to Claude Opus 5** and above Kimi K3/GLM-5.3. No SWE-bench Verified or LiveCodeBench row exists for the ID, so the top of the frontier band is not reachable — and preview-status numbers may move.
- **Cost efficiency: 85/100.** **$1.36 / $4.18** per 1M tokens sits essentially on the methodology's $1.25/$4.25 ≈ 88 anchor, with a **90 % cache discount** and a measured **$1.13 per Intelligence-Index task** (#56/226), and AA calls it "well priced … above average in intelligence"; **116 tok/s** is notably fast, which keeps wall-clock cost down. Withheld: it is **"very verbose"** — 200 M tokens per Index run against a median of 81 M, so per-request token burn is roughly 2.5× a typical peer and the $1.13/task figure already reflects that; pricing is an unannounced **preview** rate that will change at GA; and the curated sheet's $2.00/$6.00 disagrees with the measured figure, so the true contract price is not yet established.
- **Overall Score: 75/100.** Mean of the five quality dimensions (72 + 68 + 86 + 70 + 80) / 5 = 376 / 5 = 75.2 → 75; Cost excluded per `RULES.md`. Best fit **today**: **European-sovereignty, self-host-on-prem roadmaps and cyber/security work** — vulnerability reproduction and patching (82 % on the AA Cyber sub-test, Cybench 93 %, top-five globally), agentic business workflows (AutomationBench 59.9 %, Briefcase 1,393), finance/legal document deliverables (Finance Agent v2 54.7 %, Harvey LAB claim over all OSS), scientific/STEM coding, and image-grounded engineering/satellite/document work at fast throughput and low price. Explicitly **not** the pick when you need a settled, fully-measured production model: it is a **two-day-old preview** with **no published licence, cutoff, max-output or final price**, only **18 of 625** BenchLM tracks, no τ-bench or SWE-bench Verified row, mediocre factual reliability (**25.8 %** accuracy on AA-Omniscience) and very high verbosity. Re-score this folder when the weights ship.
