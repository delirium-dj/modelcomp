## Model card

- **Name:** Mistral Large 4 — "**ML4**", nicknamed "**Le Chonk**" in Mistral's own launch post; API surface is the **public preview** (Artificial Analysis titles its page "Mistral Large 4 Preview", BenchLM's row is "Mistral Large 4 / variant: base")
- **Short description:** Mistral's flagship: a **1 trillion-parameter, 52 B-active sparse MoE**, "**natively multimodal**" model trained from scratch for coding, agentic workflows, cybersecurity, finance/law knowledge work and visual grounding. Mistral positions it as "the frontier of **open-weight** performance" and explicitly as the strongest open model built **outside China** in cyber — i.e. a sovereignty play as much as a capability play ("Forged in Europe", trained on **3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs** in Mistral's own European datacentres, served on the same infrastructure).
- **Provider / access:** **Proprietary at the moment, open weights promised.** The launch post states "**Weights drop end of this month**" (October 2026) and that "until then, we are red-teaming the model … with cybersecurity leaders, vetted partners, and state authorities, who will access the same model with **reduced moderation and expanded cyber capabilities**". So the registry entry documents a **preview checkpoint**, not the eventual open-weight artefact, and **no licence has been announced yet** — do not assume Apache 2.0. Access today: preview API on Mistral Studio + Mistral's own serving; BenchLM's "Source Type: **Pending**" reflects exactly this unfinished state.
- **Release / knowledge:** launched **2026-10-06** (vendor post dated October 6, 2026; AA: "Released October 2026") — i.e. **two days old at the time of writing**. No knowledge-cutoff date has been published; Mistral only says training is "from scratch" with "a significant share … multilingual, spanning **more than 160 languages**, including every official EU language", and that architecture details, further benchmarks and post-training methodology will be shared "as we work toward releasing the weights". Treat every current number as provisional — the vendor itself says the model "continues to improve rapidly".
- **IDs:** `mistral-large-4` (Mistral Studio/API), curated `opencode/mistral-large-4`, BenchLM `benchlm.ai/models/mistral-large-4`, Artificial Analysis `artificialanalysis.ai/models/mistral-large-4` (page labelled **Preview**); family predecessor on BenchLM is `mistral-large-3` (33.27).
- **Context window:** **524k tokens** per Artificial Analysis' technical-spec block ("~786 A4 pages"). **Three-way conflict flag:** curated `meta.json` says **131,072 total (32,768 out)**, AA says **524k**, BenchLM says **1M**. The independent aggregator (AA) is the only source that states a measured window, so 524 K is what this report scores; the curated figure is ~4× too small and the BenchLM figure is ~2× too large, and neither is corroborated. Max output is **not published by the vendor** — the curated "32,768 out" is the only claim and it is unverified (if true it triggers the methodology's sub-64 K output caveat).
- **Modalities:** **text + image in, text out.** AA: "supports text and image input, outputs text"; the vendor post calls it "natively multimodal" with demos in dense-scene grounding, engineering-drawing inspection, PDF evidence retrieval and gigapixel satellite imagery. **No audio in, no video in, no image/audio output.** **Curated conflict:** `meta.json` says "Text in/out", which under-states the model — the vision capability is one of its headline features.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-08):** Artificial Analysis reports **$1.36 / $4.18 per 1M input/output** with a **90 % cache discount** and **$1.13 per Intelligence-Index task** (#56 of 226) — "moderately priced" input (median $2.00) and "competitively priced" output (median $10.00). **Curated conflict:** `meta.json` lists **$2.00 / $6.00**, which matches neither AA's first-party/blended figure nor any vendor price sheet quoted in the launch post (Mistral has not published preview pricing in the announcement). Expect both numbers to change at general availability. `noFreeId: true` in the curated file, though the vendor invites free preview traffic on Mistral Studio.
- **Architecture:** **1,000 B total / 52 B active** parameters (vendor), sparse MoE, multimodal from pretraining, trained from scratch on 3,800 Grace Blackwell GPUs. AA classifies the measured configuration as the **reasoning** variant ("This page shows the reasoning version of this model. A non-reasoning variant may also exist"); BenchLM types it "Hybrid". Serving: **116.1 output tok/s** (#37 of 226 — "notably fast" vs median 75), but **"very verbose"**: 200 M tokens generated across the full Index run against a median of 81 M (#105 of 226).
- **Identity flags:** (1) The folder documents a **preview** of an unreleased-final model — re-verification is mandatory once weights land. (2) It is *not* the same object as `model/mistral-medium-3.5/` or the `Ministral`/`Small` lines. (3) "Open-weight" is a **promise**, not a fact, at this date — no HF repo, no licence text, no parameter-level technical report exists yet. (4) Vendor's own safety framing (reduced-moderation cyber tier, refusal-rate results) is a deployment consideration worth recording.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 74/100.** (Revised by the 2026-11-03 second pass from 72/100: the previously weak Terminal-Bench 4.0 leg firmed from 26.8 % AA / 28.3 % vendor to **41 %** on post-preview RL, lifting the agentic floor; the missing τ²/τ³/Toolathlon/OSWorld harness penalty still caps the band.) No **τ²/τ³/Toolathlon/Claw-Eval/OSWorld** row exists for this ID at all, so the contract's missing-harness penalty applies, and the whole picture comes from a two-day-old preview whose serving configuration AA labels "reasoning variant".
- **Reasoning: 68/100 (held).** (2026-11-03 second pass: the AA Intelligence Index "drop" from **38 to 26** is the v5.0 leaderboard rebuild — on a consistent v5 scale ML4 went 25 (preview) → 26, above the open-model median 23 — scale drift, **not** a capability downgrade; the 25.8 % Omniscience faithfulness weakness is unchanged, so the band stays 68.) **AA Intelligence Index 38** (#64/226, median 26) is above the mid band's 20–35 reference but well under the 60 frontier anchor, and it is a *composite* rather than a frontier signal. **AA-HLE 35.0 %** sits just below the 40 % frontier gate; **AA-LCR 81.3 %** is a strong long-context reasoning result; **AA-SciCode 54.2 %** and the vendor's SciCode-Verified SOTA claim (unquantified) point at real scientific reasoning, backed by an independent-ish **CritPt 10.6 %** that is respectable on a physics-reasoning harness.
