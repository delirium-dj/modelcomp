## Model card

- **Name:** Mistral Large 4 (**ML4**; nickname "**le Chonk**"; API id `mistral-large-4` / `mistral/mistral-large-4` on Vercel AI Gateway)
- **Short description:** **Mistral AI's largest model and its European-sovereignty flagship** — a **1.05T-total-parameter, 52B-active granular mixture-of-experts**, **natively multimodal (text + image in → text out)** with a dedicated **1.6B vision encoder**, trained **from scratch on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in Mistral's own European datacenters** across **160+ languages (every official EU language)**. Announced **2026-10-06** as a **public preview** (the RL run is still in progress) with **open weights promised "by the end of October 2026"** plus a final build. Its pitch is **not** raw frontier capability — on the independent **Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3.2 it scores 38** (above the 26 median for its price class, and a huge jump from **Mistral Large 3's 9**), which Simon Willison summarizes as Mistral being "**back to being maybe about 6 months behind the frontier**" (just behind DeepSeek 4.1 Flash). Where ML4 genuinely leads: **cyber-defense** (**AA Cyber Index vulnerability reproduce-and-patch 82% — highest of any model**, because Mistral says closed models like Opus 5.5 / GPT-6 Astra *refuse* the task rather than fail it; **Cybench 93%**), **visual grounding** (**Dense 200 42%**, edging GPT-6 Astra's 41% — charts, documents, engineering drawings), **EU data residency/sovereignty**, and **safety** (Lakera B3 93.3%, KORA 1.691/2). ⚠ **Coding is mid, not frontier** — **Surge AI blind human eval 3.74/5** (beats GLM-5.3 3.60, Kimi K3 3.59, GLM-5.2 3.40, but trails **Claude Opus 5's 4.22**), **DeepSWE v1.1 61.7%**, a weak **Terminal-Bench 4 28.3%**, and the community **Vals Index 48.05% (behind GLM-5.3's 53.51%, at $13.78 vs $7.25 per test)**; the developer verdict is "**not a reason to move a coding agent off Claude or GPT-6-class models**." It is also **very verbose** (AA measured ~2.5× the median output tokens — 200M to run its index vs an 81M median), so real cost-per-task exceeds the sticker.
- **Provider / access:** **Mistral AI.** Live on **Mistral's own API** (`mistral-large-4`, `api.mistral.ai`, with a `reasoning_effort` parameter) and **Vercel AI Gateway** (`mistral/mistral-large-4`, OpenAI-/Anthropic-compatible) since Oct 6. **European-trained and -hosted** — Mistral operates the EU deployment "independently of other digital service providers" (a procurement/sovereignty differentiator). **Currently proprietary** (AA labels it "Proprietary" — no public weights yet); **open weights promised end of October 2026** after red-teaming with security partners and state authorities. **No free-id tier** (`noFreeId: true`). Listed here as `opencode/mistral-large-4`.
- **Release / knowledge:** **2026-10-06** (public preview; weights + a "final version" due end of Oct 2026 — co-founder Guillaume Lample says the downloadable checkpoint **may differ from today's preview**). Knowledge cutoff not stated in the sources reviewed. **Not deprecated** — it is the current Mistral flagship, ahead of Large 3 / Medium 3.5.
- **IDs:** `mistral-large-4` (Mistral API) / `mistral/mistral-large-4` (Vercel AI Gateway) / `opencode/mistral-large-4` (this dataset).
- **Context window:** ⚠ **Meta correction — the scaffolded meta's "131,072 total (32,768 out)" is wrong (it matches the 2024-era Mistral Large 2, not Large 4).** Actual: **1M listed on Mistral's model card, but 524K listed by both Vercel AI Gateway and Artificial Analysis for the *served preview*, with 262K max output**. developersdigest's guidance is to "**plan around 524K until Mistral's own endpoint proves otherwise**." So the honest served figure is **524K / 262K-out** (1M claimed but unproven on the preview) — comfortably above the meta's 131K and into the 500K+ tier.
- **Modalities:** ⚠ **Meta correction — the scaffolded meta's "Text in/out" is wrong.** ML4 is **natively multimodal: text + image in → text out**, with a dedicated **1.6B vision encoder** and visual grounding called out as one of its **strongest relative claims** (**Dense 200 42% ≈ GPT-6 Astra 41%**; charts, documents, engineering drawings). **No video, no audio** (image-only beyond text).
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-25):** ⚠ **Meta correction — the scaffolded meta's "$2.00/$6.00" matches neither the list nor the sale price.** Actual (Mistral API standard tier): **list $1.36 input / $0.14 cached / $4.18 output per 1M**, currently on a **50% launch sale at $0.68 / $0.07 / $2.09** (no published end date — "budget at list price"). Vercel AI Gateway passes through the sale price. Independently, **AA's cost-per-index-task is $1.13**, and the **Vals Index runs $13.78/test** (vs GLM-5.3's $7.25) — so the **verbosity (2.5× median tokens)** makes effective cost higher than the sticker. Expect the price to fall further once weights ship and third-party hosts compete (the Kimi K3 / GLM-5.3 precedent).
- **Architecture:** **Granular mixture-of-experts, hybrid instruct + reasoning: 1.05T total parameters, 52B active, plus a 1.6B vision encoder** (Mistral's model card; launch-day coverage said 49B active — **use 52B**, Mistral's current figure). **Adjustable reasoning via `reasoning_effort`** (docs list none/low/high/max across models, but Simon Willison reports ML4 accepts only **`none` and `high`**, and found the setting made little visible difference: 2,717 output tokens at `high` vs 3,275 at `none`). Trained from scratch on 3,800 Grace Blackwell GPUs in EU datacenters. At 1.05T, self-hosting the eventual weights needs ~1 TB at 8-bit / ~525 GB at 4-bit — **multi-GPU server hardware, not a workstation**.

### Raw benchmarks found

Every headline number below is **vendor-selected by Mistral** in its Oct-6 announcement (several are third-party-*run* — Artificial Analysis, vals.ai, Surge AI — but chosen and published by Mistral). The genuinely **independent** anchors are the **AA preview measurements** and **community/Vals/Plotly** reports.

**Independent — Artificial Analysis (measured on Mistral's API, preview build) + community:**

- **AA Intelligence Index v4.3.2: 38** — vs a **median of 26 for its price class**; a **big jump from Mistral Large 3's 9**. Simon Willison: sits "**just behind DeepSeek 4.1 Flash** (a 552B model)"; Mistral is "**~6 months behind the frontier**." *(Basket caveat: v4.3.2 — not directly comparable to a v4.1 score such as GPT-5.6 Luna's 51.2.)*
- **AA speed/cost:** output **116.1 tokens/s** (median 74.6), **time-to-first-token 1.46 s** (median 3.84 s) — fast; but **~200M output tokens to run the index (median 81M — very verbose, ~2.5×)** and **cost-per-index-task $1.13**.
- **Vals Index (community-cited): 48.05%** vs **GLM-5.3 53.51%**, at **$13.78 vs $7.25 per test** — behind GLM-5.3 and pricier per test.
- **Plotly private 43-question data-analytics exam: 32/43 passed (74%) for $1.94/run** (up from 25/43 = 58% for $20.51 on Mistral Medium 3.5); misses were judgment calls; "**not yet on the cost-quality frontier**."
- A custom-language hands-on benchmark found ML4 **~level with Kimi K3 and Claude Sonnet 5.5**, but **slower than Sonnet 5.5 and more expensive than both**.

**Vendor — Mistral announcement (vendor-selected; third-party-run where noted):**

- **Coding:** **DeepSWE v1.1 61.7%**, **SWE-Atlas-QnA 59.4%**, **Terminal-Bench 4 28.3%** (weak), **Coding Agent Index 49.8%** (ahead of DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 and Qwen3.8 Max); **Surge AI blind human rating 3.74/5** (Claude Opus 5 4.22, GLM-5.3 3.60, Kimi K3 3.59, GLM-5.2 3.40) — *the most useful line, since it includes a closed model*.
- **Agents / knowledge work:** **AutomationBench (657 business workflows) 59.9%** (ahead of Kimi K3, MiMo-V2.6-Pro, DeepSeek V4 Pro); **AA-Briefcase 1,393 Elo** (ahead of DeepSeek V4 Pro).
- **Cyber (its headline):** **AA Cyber Index vulnerability reproduce-and-patch 82% — highest of any model** (Mistral says Opus 5.5 / GPT-6 Astra score near zero because they **refuse**); **Cybench (40 CTF challenges) 93%**.
- **Vision:** **Dense 200 (visual grounding) 42%** (GPT-6 Astra 41%). **Safety:** Lakera B3 attack resistance 93.3%; KORA 1.691/2.

> Provenance: This is a **preview-stage, independently-measured-but-mid** profile with a **strong niche** (EU sovereignty + cyber-defense + visual grounding). The one clean independent anchor — **AA Intelligence Index v4.3.2 = 38** — puts it **above its price-class median (26)** and a **large jump over Large 3 (9)**, but **~6 months behind the closed frontier** (Willison); the vendor coding picture (**Surge 3.74 < Opus 5 4.22**, **Terminal-Bench 4 28.3%**, **Vals Index 48.05% < GLM-5.3 53.51%**) confirms **mid-tier coding**, and the **cyber "highest of any model" claim is refusal-inflated** (closed models decline the task). Note the vendor numbers are **Mistral-selected** even where third-party-run, and **HN flagged benchmark-selection concerns** (e.g. Mistral's chart showing GLM 5.3 at 61 on DeepSWE 1.1 where other listings show 69). **The scaffolded meta.json was materially wrong and is corrected here on three fields:** (1) **context** — meta "131,072 (32,768 out)" → actual **524K served / 1M on card, 262K out** (the meta matches 2024's Mistral Large 2); (2) **modalities** — meta "Text in/out" → actual **text + image in → text out** (1.6B vision encoder, Dense 200 visual grounding); (3) **pricing** — meta "$2.00/$6.00" → actual **list $1.36/$4.18, 50%-sale $0.68/$2.09**. The meta's *tradeoff* ("131K window and paid-only access look dated") is therefore **half-wrong** (the window is 524K–1M, not 131K; "paid-only" is true *for now* — open weights are promised end of Oct). Its *standout* ("the multilingual European option … EU data posture") is **accurate and is the real differentiator**. Parameter count (1.05T/52B), the vision encoder, EU Grace-Blackwell training, and the weights-date were **enriched** from the model card / announcement. **No benchmark was invented; vendor-vs-independent and the meta corrections are stated throughout.**

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 80/100.** A genuinely broad agentic surface that **leads several strong models on vendor-selected tests** — **AutomationBench 59.9%** (ahead of Kimi K3, MiMo-V2.6-Pro, DeepSeek V4 Pro), **AA-Briefcase 1,393 Elo** (ahead of DeepSeek V4 Pro), **Coding Agent Index 49.8%** (ahead of DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 and Qwen3.8 Max), and a **cyber-defense edge (AA Cyber Index reproduce-and-patch 82%, "highest of any model"; Cybench 93%)**. Held to **80** because the numbers are **Mistral-selected on a preview build**, the **cyber lead is refusal-inflated** (closed models decline, not fail), and **Terminal-Bench 4 is weak (28.3%)**. Its **AA-Briefcase 1,393** sits **below Grok 4.7's 1,657**, so it is competitive but not the agentic ceiling.
- **Reasoning: 80/100.** The independent **AA Intelligence Index v4.3.2 = 38** is **above its price-class median (26)** and a **major jump from Large 3 (9)** — real progress — but Willison places it "**just behind DeepSeek 4.1 Flash**" and "**~6 months behind the frontier**," and the **`reasoning_effort` dial made little visible difference** in his test. No GPQA/math figure was published in the sources reviewed. Scored **level with its solid-mid independent standing** — above the deprecated/superseded gemini-2.5-pro (79) but **below the independently-verified frontier-reasoning tier (Kimi K2.6 85, GPT-5.6 Luna 84)**.
- **Context window: 83/100.** **524K served / 1M on the model card, 262K max output** (corrected from the meta's 131K). The served preview is **524K** (AI Gateway + AA agree; "plan around 524K"), which places it **level with the confirmed-500K tier (Grok 4.5, 83)** and **below the proven-1M models (85)** — the 1M is claimed on the card but **not yet proven on the served preview**, and the open checkpoint's real context is an open question for weight-release day. The **262K output window is generous**.
- **Multimodal: 78/100.** **Native text + image in → text out** (corrected from the meta's "text-only") with a **dedicated 1.6B vision encoder** and **visual grounding highlighted as a strength** (**Dense 200 42%**, edging **GPT-6 Astra's 41%**; charts, documents, engineering drawings). But it is **image-only — no video, no audio** — and the absolute vision number (42%) is modest. Scored **level with GPT-5.3-Codex (78)** and **just above the image-only floor (Grok 4.5/4.7, GPT-5.6 Luna, 77)** on its native multimodality + grounding, **well below the video models (Seed 2.0 Pro 84, Qwen video 82)**.
- **Coding: 78/100.** **Mid-tier, not frontier** — the honest read across vendor *and* independent sources. **Surge AI blind human eval 3.74/5** beats the Chinese open models (GLM-5.3 3.60, Kimi K3 3.59, GLM-5.2 3.40) but **trails Claude Opus 5 (4.22)**; **DeepSWE v1.1 61.7%** is respectable; but **Terminal-Bench 4 is weak (28.3%)**, the **community Vals Index 48.05% sits behind GLM-5.3's 53.51%**, a custom-language bench found it **~level with Kimi K3 / Sonnet 5.5 but slower and pricier**, and the developer consensus is "**not a reason to move a coding agent off Claude or GPT-6-class models**." Scored **below Qwen3.5-Plus (80)** and **well below GPT-5.6 Luna (86, elite Vals 96/92/86/67)**.
- **Cost efficiency: 80/100.** A **low headline sale price ($0.68/$2.09; list $1.36/$4.18)** and **imminent open weights** (self-host + third-party-host price competition, per the Kimi K3/GLM-5.3 precedent) are attractive — **but** ML4 is **very verbose (~2.5× median output tokens; 200M to run the AA index)**, so **AA cost-per-task is $1.13** and the **Vals Index runs $13.78/test vs GLM-5.3's $7.25** for **mid capability**. Plotly: "**not yet on the cost-quality frontier**"; HN: "**pricier than GPT-6 Luna and DeepSeek 4.1 Flash**." Scored **well below GPT-5.6 Luna (93, Pareto-frontier $0.21/task)** and the cheap open-weight leaders; self-hosting the 1.05T weights also needs ~525 GB–1 TB (multi-GPU). Scored but **not** counted in the Overall per methodology.
- **Overall Score: 80/100.** Mean of Tool 80 + Reasoning 80 + Context 83 + Multimodal 78 + Coding 78 = 399/5 = 79.8 → 80. Best fit: **EU-sovereignty and regulated-work deployments** (EU-trained, EU-hosted, end-to-end Mistral-operated), **defensive cyber/security research that closed models refuse** (AA Cyber Index 82%, Cybench 93%), and **visual-grounding / document- and chart-heavy work** (Dense 200 42%) — plus teams that want a **large non-Chinese open-weight model** once the weights land end of October. **Not** the pick for **best-in-class coding agents** (Surge 3.74 ≪ Opus 5 4.22; Terminal-Bench 4 28.3% — stay on Claude/GPT-6-class), for **lowest cost-per-token** (verbosity + $13.78/test; DeepSeek 4.1 Flash / GLM-5.3-Flash are cheaper), for **video/audio multimodal** (image-only), or for **self-hosting today** (no weights/license yet — wait for month-end). **Divergence note:** my 80 is **4.3 below the queue's curated 84.3** — a wide gap driven by (a) the **scaffolded meta being materially wrong** (131K context, text-only, $2/$6 — all corrected), which likely inflated the curated score, and (b) the **independent evidence placing a preview build ~6 months behind the frontier** (AA Index 38, Vals Index 48.05% < GLM-5.3, Surge 3.74 < Opus 5, Terminal-Bench 4 28.3%, verbose at 2.5× tokens) — against genuine strengths: the **highest AA Cyber Index reproduce-and-patch (82%)**, competitive **visual grounding**, **524K–1M context**, **1.05T/52B scale**, a **unique EU-sovereignty posture**, and **open weights imminent**.
