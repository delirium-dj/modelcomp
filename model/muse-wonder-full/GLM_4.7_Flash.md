# GLM 4.7 Flash vs Muse Wonder Full

## Model Overview

**Muse Wonder Full** is Meta's most capable frontier model, part of the Muse Wonder series that delivers state-of-the-art performance on personal and professional agentic tasks. Released October 12, 2026, it represents Meta's next step in personal superintelligence, combining **Active Memory** and **Active Focus** capabilities.

- **Provider**: Meta (Meta AI)
- **Release Date**: October 12, 2026
- **Access**: Meta Model API, Muse Code subscribers
- **Pricing**: Not published (proprietary)
- **Status**: Closed, API-only

## Key Capabilities

### Active Memory & Active Focus

Muse Wonder introduces two critical innovations for personal agents:

- **Active Memory**: Carries important context about users across conversations and actively retrieves relevant details
- **Active Focus**: Sustained effort toward challenging goals, trying alternative approaches and adapting to new information

These capabilities are evaluated through FocusBench and SWE-Marathon, testing work on long-running software projects.

### Domain Expertise

Muse Wonder applies specialized knowledge across:
- Law (Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark)
- Finance (financial analysis, chart/table interpretation)
- Health (health information processing)

### Frontier Coding

Muse Wonder delivers frontier coding performance supporting projects from small applications to complex software. It was trained on diverse, long-running tasks to sustain progress across the development cycle.

### Visual & Creative Capabilities

- Builds playable 3D kitchen games from image inputs
- Generates interactive landing pages
- Creates museum websites

## Comparative Analysis

### vs. Claude Fable 5

Both models represent frontier capabilities with strong agentic and coding performance. Muse Wonder Full emphasizes personal superintelligence with strong memory and focus capabilities, while Claude Fable 5 has stronger cybersecurity and biology capabilities (with safeguards).

### vs. GPT-5.6 Sol

GPT-5.6 Sol shows stronger performance on specific benchmarks like Agents' Last Exam (53.6 vs. Fable 5's 40.5) and Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (58.9 vs. Fable 5's 59.9). However, both models deliver frontier performance with different specializations.

### vs. Gemini 3.7 Flash

Gemini 3.7 Flash is more focused on coding and enterprise workflows. Muse Wonder Full provides stronger personal agent capabilities with Active Memory and Active Focus innovations.
