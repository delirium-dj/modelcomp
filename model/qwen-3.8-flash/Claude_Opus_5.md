## Model card

- **Name:** Qwen 3.8 Flash. **Variant/alias finding that governs this report, and it is an unusual one: this is the *managed* Qwen3.8-Flash API tier, which this repository's curated metadata explicitly distinguishes from the open-weight `Qwen3.8-Flash-Next` checkpoint.** The benchmark tracker that covers 784 models **carries no entry for the managed tier at all** — only for Flash-Next. The two share the defining architectural facts (**~6B active parameters**, **262K native context**, **text/image/video input**), so Flash-Next is the only sound evidential basis available, and **the quality dimensions below are assessed on its measured results with that inference stated openly.** The one dimension where the managed tier genuinely differs is **Context window: the API serves 1M against Flash-Next's 262K native.**
- **Short description:** Alibaba's **multimodal MoE technical preview** served as a managed API — cheap, fast, and far more capable than ~6B active parameters would suggest. On the Flash-Next evidence, a composite tracker places the checkpoint **#35 of 784 at 64.15/100**. Three results stand out for the size class: **GDPval-AA at 1,648 Elo**, the highest in this research pass outside the GLM-5.3 family and ahead of DeepSeek V4 Pro (1,590), Claude Opus 4.8 (1,588) and GPT-5.6 Luna (1,582); **an AA Coding Index of 73.0%, clearing the methodology's 70%+ reference**; and **CharXiv at 90.6%, the strongest chart reasoning found anywhere in this pass.** Add **GPQA Diamond clearing 90% twice over** and **IFBench at 81.3%, tied for the pass lead.** The consistent weaknesses: **HLE misses the 40%+ reference on all three measurements**, and **no Terminal-Bench or SWE-bench figure exists at any version.**
- **Provider / access:** Alibaba managed API. The underlying weights are additionally public — the curated metadata records **"$0 self-host"** — so this is a managed convenience tier over an open-weight model rather than a closed product.
- **Release / knowledge:** The related Flash-Next checkpoint is reported as a **125B-A6B MoE released circa 2026-08-26**. **No verified release date or knowledge cutoff found for the managed tier specifically.**
- **IDs:** `opencode/qwen-3.8-flash`; distinct sibling `opencode/qwen-3.8-flash-next`. **No free-tier ID**, though self-hosting is free per the curated metadata.
- **Context window:** **1,000,000 tokens via the API, over a 262,144-token native window, with 131,072 maximum output** (repository metadata). **This is the one dimension where the managed tier beats Flash-Next**, and it is also where the most care is needed: **the 1M figure is API-level, achieved over a 262K native window** — the sibling's metadata names YaRN as the extension mechanism — so it is an extrapolation rather than a trained window. **AA-LCR at 79.7%** is strong but was measured at native length.
- **Modalities:** **Text, image and video input → text output.** **Video input is confirmed by LVBench at 76.6%** on the shared checkpoint. No audio path; no non-text output.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **approximately $0.15 / 1M input, $0.47 / 1M output**, with **$0 self-hosting** (repository metadata). That is roughly **20× cheaper on input and 32× cheaper on output than the methodology's $3/$15 anchor**. For comparison, the Flash-Next checkpoint is listed at **$0.12 / $0.40 on the Vercel AI Gateway** — so **the managed tier costs about 25% and 18% more respectively, and what that premium buys is the 1M API window and Alibaba-side operation.** **No primary Alibaba rate card was reached; re-verify before budgeting.**
- **Architecture:** **Sparse MoE, ~6B active parameters** (repository metadata). The related Flash-Next checkpoint is reported at **125B total / 6B active**.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

> **Tool use, Reasoning, Multimodal and Coding are assessed on Qwen3.8-Flash-Next's measured results and carry the same values as my Qwen 3.8 Flash Next findings file in this research pass, because the two share active-parameter count, native context and modalities. Context window and Cost efficiency differ, and those differences define the managed tier.**

- **Tool use: 78/100.** Strong relative to size. **GDPval-AA at 1,648 Elo is the highest in this research pass outside the GLM-5.3 family**, ahead of DeepSeek V4 Pro, Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.6 Luna — all vastly larger — which is genuine evidence that a ~6B-active MoE can do economically valuable work. **Agents' Last Exam at 51.2% is roughly double GLM-5.3's 28.5%**, and **AndroidWorld 84.5%, CoWorkBench 73.9% and Toolathlon-Verified 73.5%** form a coherent multi-step agency picture. Four deductions: **GDPval remains 102 Elo below the ~1750+ reference**; **OSWorld 2.0 at 19.4%** shows weak desktop computer-use; **no Terminal-Bench exists at any version**, a documented vendor omission rather than an oversight; and **Claw-Eval, MCP-Atlas, τ²-bench, CyberGym, BrowseComp, OSWorld-Verified and the AA Agentic Index are all unreported**, with the methodology explicitly penalising the Claw-Eval absence.
- **Reasoning: 78/100.** Two credentials against three shortfalls. **GPQA Diamond clears the 90%+ reference on both measurements (91.7% vendor, 92.3% independent), with the independent figure higher than the vendor's** — a credibility signal for the whole table. **IFBench at 81.3% ties Grok 4.3 for the pass lead**, and **AA-LCR at 79.7%** matches models with four times the nominal window. Against that: **HLE misses the 40%+ reference on all three measurements (35.9%, 35.9%, 38.0%)**, and the vendor reporting the identical figure with and without tools implies **tools added nothing** — informative about reasoning depth. **The Intelligence Index of 39.8% is 20.2 points below the 60+ marker**, **CritPt is 11.1%**, and **the AA-Omniscience Index is negative at −9.7% on just 24.5% accuracy**. **ARC-AGI, FrontierMath and MMLU-Pro are entirely unmeasured.**
- **Context window: 86/100. This is the dimension where the managed tier genuinely beats its open-weight sibling, and the score sits between the two tiers deliberately.** The API serves **1,000,000 tokens with 131,072 maximum output**, against Flash-Next's 262,144 native — so the managed tier delivers roughly four times the usable window, which is a real product advantage and the main thing the price premium buys. It is scored in the 500K–1M band rather than the ≥1M tier (95–100) for three reasons stated plainly: **the 1M is an API-level figure over a 262K native window**, with the sibling's metadata naming YaRN as the extension mechanism, and YaRN extrapolation typically degrades quality at depth; **the supporting AA-LCR figure of 79.7% was measured at native length, not extended length**, so nothing demonstrates the extension preserves quality; and **no MRCR, RULER, CorpusQA or GraphWalks measurement exists at any length**. The score credits a genuinely larger served window without crediting a trained one.
- **Multimodal: 86/100.** **Video input is confirmed by LVBench at 76.6%**, placing it in the methodology's "+video/PDF in = 75–90" band, and the measured breadth is excellent for the size class. **CharXiv at 90.6% is the strongest chart reasoning found anywhere in this research pass** — above GLM-5.3-Flash, Muse Spark 1.1 and GPT-5.4 — and holds 84.6% even without tools, so it is not a tool-use artefact. **MathVision at 90.6% (95.7% with Python) is exceptional visual mathematics**, **RealWorldQA at 88.5%** is strong, and **AA-MMMU-Pro at 79.8% leads GPT-5.6 Luna (78.6%) and Grok 4.3 (78.1%)**. Three reasons it does not reach the band ceiling: **no audio input path**, so the top band is unavailable; **Vision2Web at 64.0% and ERQA at 72.3%** mark real limits on grounded web and embodied reasoning; and **no OCR benchmark exists**, so document extraction is unmeasured.
- **Coding: 80/100.** Good, with one reference cleared and one conspicuous hole. **The AA Coding Index at 73.0% clears the methodology's 70%+ reference** — the highest in this research pass after GLM-5.3's 74.8%, and ahead of GPT-5.6 Luna, Muse Spark 1.1 and GPT-5.4. **LiveCodeBench v6 at 91.9%** is very high and **SWE Multilingual at 81%** shows the capability is not English-only. Four deductions: **DeepSWE at 58.7% is 15.3 points below the 74%+ reference**, below even GLM-5.3-Flash's 63.4%; **AA-SciCode at 50.6% misses its reference by 4.4 points**; **NL2Repo (48.1%) and SWE-bench Pro (62.5%)** are mid-field; and **neither SWE-bench nor SWE-bench Verified nor Terminal-Bench has any figure**, so the dimension rests on self-contained problems rather than real codebases — a serious gap for a model positioned for "cheap agentic coding".
- **Cost efficiency: 92/100.** Excellent, and structurally unusual in a good way: **this is a managed convenience tier over an open-weight model, so you get operational simplicity without lock-in.** **Approximately $0.15 / $0.47 is roughly 20× cheaper on input and 32× cheaper on output than the methodology's $3/$15 anchor**, and **self-hosting is free** per the curated metadata — so the rate card is fully escapable. What it buys is not a toy: **GDPval-AA at 1,648 Elo beating DeepSeek V4 Pro and Claude Opus 4.8**, **an AA Coding Index above the frontier reference**, **GPQA above 90% twice**, **IFBench tied for the pass lead**, **the strongest CharXiv in this pass**, **video input**, and **a 1M API window**. At ~6B active, inference is cheap whether Alibaba runs it or you do. Three deductions: **it costs about 25% more on input and 18% more on output than the Flash-Next checkpoint on Vercel ($0.12/$0.40)**, so if you do not need the 1M API window the sibling is cheaper for the same weights; **this is a technical preview**, so stability and continued availability are not assured; and **no primary Alibaba rate card was reached**, so all pricing rests on curated metadata.
- **Overall Score: 81.6/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (78 + 78 + 86 + 86 + 80) / 5 = 81.6 — **and together with its Flash-Next sibling this is the best size-to-capability ratio found in this research pass.** A **~6B-active** model that posts **GDPval-AA at 1,648 Elo (ahead of DeepSeek V4 Pro, Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.6 Luna)**, **an AA Coding Index of 73.0% above the frontier reference**, **GPQA Diamond clearing 90% on both measurements with the independent figure higher than the vendor's**, **IFBench at 81.3% tied for the pass lead**, **CharXiv at 90.6% — the strongest chart reasoning found anywhere here**, **MathVision at 90.6%**, **RealWorldQA at 88.5%**, **video input**, and **a 1M API window** — at roughly **$0.15 / $0.47 with free self-hosting** — is a genuinely strong package. Choose it over the Flash-Next sibling **specifically when you need the 1M API window or Alibaba-side operation**; choose Flash-Next when you want the same weights ~20% cheaper. Four cautions. **It is a technical preview**, so do not build production dependencies without a fallback. **Repository-scale coding is unproven** — no SWE-bench of any variant, no Terminal-Bench at any version, DeepSWE at 58.7% — which is the weakest point in the "cheap agentic coding" positioning. **Depth of reasoning is limited**: HLE misses 40% on all three measurements with tools adding nothing, the Intelligence Index is 39.8%, CritPt is 11.1%, the Omniscience Index is −9.7% on 24.5% accuracy, and ARC-AGI, FrontierMath and MMLU-Pro are entirely unmeasured. And **no benchmark has ever been run against this managed tier** — every figure here belongs to the Flash-Next checkpoint, used as the closest tracked relative.
