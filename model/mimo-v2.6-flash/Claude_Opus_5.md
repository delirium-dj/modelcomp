## Model card

- **Name:** MiMo-V2.6-Flash. **Open weight under the MIT licence** — commercial use, modification and redistribution with no revenue thresholds or Model-as-a-Service carve-outs. Xiaomi's own catalogue describes it as a "full-modality, high-intelligence, low-cost reasoning model — the best balance for high-frequency calls and large-scale tasks in professional workflows". No `$0` API tier. Variant/alias flag: siblings in the same release are **MiMo-V2.6-Pro** (1.02T/42B, $0.435/$0.87), **MiMo-V2.6-Pro-UltraSpeed** (API-only, 10x price) and **MiMo-V2.6-Distill-Qwen-9B** (a research SFT checkpoint of Qwen3.5-9B, explicitly not a small Flash).
- **Short description:** The cheaper sibling of Xiaomi's omnimodal flagship, released **2026-09-22** (Hugging Face repositories went live late 2026-09-21 UTC) as part of the MiMo-V2.6 series, which Xiaomi framed around "scaling reinforcement learning toward self-improvement" using **one mixed RL run across coding, general agents, visual tasks and cybersecurity** rather than separate per-domain runs. The practically important finding is how little it gives up: on Xiaomi's own benchmark table Flash is **within a few points of Pro on most agentic and coding rows at roughly a third of the price**, and the gap only opens materially on offensive-security work. It also **beats Pro outright on CyberGym**.
- **Provider / access:** Xiaomi's MiMo API platform (`mimo-v2.6-flash`), **OpenRouter** (`xiaomi/mimo-v2.6-flash`, 7 providers — more than Pro's 4), plus Xiaomi MiMo Studio, MiMo Code and the MiMo Desktop app. Weights on Hugging Face as `XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL`. Xiaomi open-sourced the surrounding tooling with the release: a **verl RL-framework fork**, the **Uni-Agent** long-horizon agent framework, and **mimoagent**, a minimal coding agent. Xiaomi recommends `temperature=1.0` and `top_p=0.95`, and self-hosting requires the `mimo` reasoning and tool-call parsers or tool calling will not parse.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released 2026-09-22. **Knowledge cutoff: no verified public figure found.**
- **IDs:** `mimo-v2.6-flash` (Xiaomi platform), `xiaomi/mimo-v2.6-flash` (OpenRouter), `XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL` (Hugging Face).
- **Context window:** **1M tokens** per Xiaomi's model card (1,048,576); **OpenRouter lists 1,050,000 with a maximum output of 131,072 tokens** — both values are recorded, and unlike Pro this model has a **documented output ceiling**. The window is made servable by a specific architectural choice rather than brute force (see Architecture).
- **Modalities:** **Omnimodal input — text, image, video and audio**; output text only. Critically, the multimodal stack is **not downscaled from Pro**: Flash carries the **identical 681M-parameter MiMo ViT vision encoder and the identical 308M AudioTokenizer plus 127M patch encoder**, so its perceptual front end is the flagship's. Reasoning: yes (a `mimo` reasoning parser is required when self-hosting). Tool calls: yes. JSON mode: not separately documented in the sources checked.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **$0.14 / 1M input, $0.0028 / 1M cached input, $0.28 / 1M output** on Xiaomi's first-party API — roughly **a third of Pro's $0.435/$0.87** — and Xiaomi states pricing is unchanged from the V2.5 series. **OpenRouter lists input at $0.07 / 1M**, cheaper than first-party. Artificial Analysis measured the practical figure at **$0.06 per Intelligence Index task**, against Pro's $0.13. For external context, AA characterises both meters as "moderately priced" against medians of $0.30 input and $1.15 output — i.e. roughly half the median input price and a quarter of the median output price.
- **Architecture:** **Sparse MoE with no shared experts — 309B total parameters, 15B active per token, routing each token through 8 of 256 routed experts.** 48 layers, of which **39 use a 128-token sliding window and 9 attend globally**; hidden size 4096. Hybrid attention is what keeps the KV cache small enough for a 1M context to be servable, and Flash's global-attention budget is proportionally *larger* than Pro's (9 of 48 layers versus 10 of 70). **Speculative decoding via the same 5-layer multi-token-prediction drafter predicting 7 tokens per forward pass.** Checkpoint is **~178 GB on disk** (most tensors in a packed 8-bit container with FP8 and BF16 for the rest; Xiaomi does not name the exact quantisation scheme). **This is the practical self-hosting pick of the family**: Xiaomi's tuned SGLang recipe runs **TP 8 on a single node**, and community quantisations appeared within hours of release — **NVFP4 builds for Blackwell GPUs, a W4A8 build targeting two RTX PRO 6000 cards, MLX 4-bit and mxfp4 builds for Apple Silicon, and early GGUFs** — all unofficial and unbenchmarked. **Pre-training data is not public**, so "open weight" here is not "open source".

### Raw benchmarks found

> **Vendor-reported** figures come from Xiaomi's MiMo-V2.6-Pro model-card evaluation table, which includes a Flash column alongside Pro, V2.5-Pro, Claude Opus 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol; rows labelled "MiMo" are internal benchmarks nobody outside Xiaomi can reproduce. Xiaomi's release-notes page gives a different DeepSWE figure for Flash than its model card (65.7 vs 67.9), implying different run settings — both are recorded. **Independent** figures are Artificial Analysis measurements and third-party composites.

Agent / tool use:

- **Terminal Bench 2.1: 87.6** (vendor) — within 2.3 points of Pro's 89.9 and just under the methodology's ~88% frontier anchor, but marginally behind **Claude Opus 5 (89.1)** and **GPT-5.6 Sol (88.8)**. V2.5-Pro scored 65.2.
- **Terminal Bench 4.0: 28.8** (vendor; Pro 34.9, **Claude Opus 5 49.0 and GPT-5.6 Sol 39.9 both far ahead**) — a 20-point deficit to Opus 5 on the harder version.
- Tau3-Banking / Tau2-Bench: no verified public score found
- **GDPval-AA 2.1: not reported for Flash at all** — Xiaomi's table shows a dash in this column, so the methodology's primary agentic Elo reference cannot be checked.
- Claw-Eval / ClawProBench: no verified public score found
- **Toolathlon-Verified: 73.6** (vendor; Pro 76.9, GPT-5.6 Sol 74.9, **Claude Opus 5 80.6 ahead**)

A 15B-active-parameter model leading two frontier models on end-to-end business execution is the standout result here. V2.5-Pro scored 16.0.

- **CyberGym: 95.1** (vendor) — **higher than Pro's 94.0**, though no competitor figures were published so the absolute level is uncontextualised.

This is the single most important operational caveat: the model is cheap per token but slow per token, which partially cancels the economics on latency-sensitive high-volume work — precisely the use case Xiaomi positions it for.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 85/100.** The headline is genuinely impressive for a 15B-active model: **AutomationBench v1.0.6 at 52.3 beats both Claude Opus 5 (50.3) and GPT-5.6 Sol (45.8)** on end-to-end business execution while landing within 0.8 points of its own flagship, and **Terminal Bench 2.1 at 87.6 sits just under the ~88% frontier anchor** and within 1.5 points of two closed flagships. Toolathlon-Verified 73.6 and OSWorld-Verified 80.8 are both within ~2 points of Pro. Four deductions hold it to 85: **GDPval-AA 2.1 is not reported for this model at all**, so the methodology's primary agentic Elo reference is unmeasurable; **Terminal Bench 4.0 at 28.8 trails Claude Opus 5 by 20 points**, so the 2.1 result does not survive the harder version; **ExploitBench at 25.3 is 45–53 points behind the closed flagships**, the widest gap in the table; and **Tau3-Banking and Claw-Eval are unreported** (explicit methodology penalty), with every agentic figure coming from Xiaomi's own harnesses.
- **Reasoning: 74/100.** This is the weakest-evidenced dimension of any model in this research pass. **Not one of the methodology's five named reasoning references has a published value** — no GPQA Diamond, no HLE, no MRCR/LCR, no CritPt — and unlike Pro, **no Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index score is retrievable**, nor does Flash appear in AA's published open-weight top five. The only reasoning-adjacent signals are a **ProgramBench score of 26.0 that trails Claude Opus 5 by 11 points**, an **uncontextualised CyberGym 95.1** with no competitor data, and a third-party composite placing it **#28 of 212 at 66.4/100**. The architecture is consistent with a genuine capability gap rather than merely missing data: at **15B active parameters against Pro's 42B**, and with Xiaomi itself positioning Flash as the lower tier whose agentic parity "only opens on offensive-security work", the honest read is a strong agentic model with unverified and probably mid-tier general reasoning depth.
- **Context window: 95/100.** A **1M-token window** (1,048,576 per Xiaomi; 1,050,000 with a documented **131,072 max output** per OpenRouter — an output figure Pro notably lacks) places it in the ≥1M tier (95–100), and the engineering behind it is disclosed: **39 of 48 layers on a 128-token sliding window with 9 attending globally**, which keeps the KV cache servable, plus the same 5-layer MTP drafter as the flagship. Its global-attention budget is proportionally *larger* than Pro's (18.8% of layers versus 14.3%), so the usual sliding-window retrieval concern is weaker here. It stays at the tier floor because the 100 condition (≥98% retrieval at 512K+) has **no supporting evidence whatsoever** — no MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks figure exists for this model or its sibling.
- **Multimodal: 90/100.** Genuinely **omnimodal input — text, image, video and audio** — and the decisive point is that the perceptual stack is **not cut down from the flagship**: Flash ships the **identical 681M MiMo ViT and the identical 308M AudioTokenizer plus 127M patch encoder** as Pro, so a model a third of Pro's size and a third of its price has the same purpose-built front end. That places it at the floor of the methodology's top band (90–100) on audio ingestion. It earns the floor and no more because the **only published visual result is MiMo VisualCoding at 71.5 — an internal benchmark nobody outside Xiaomi can reproduce** (albeit one where it beats Claude Opus 5) — with **no MMMU, CharXiv, LVBench, OmniDocBench or ASR figure at all**, and output is text-only.
- **Coding: 84/100.** **Terminal Bench 2.1 at 87.6 clears the methodology's 85%+ coding reference**, the V2.5 → V2.6 DeepSWE jump from 19.0 to 67.9 is enormous, and **MiMo VisualCoding at 71.5 beats Claude Opus 5's 70.0**. Three deductions: **DeepSWE v1.1 at 67.9 (or 65.7 in Xiaomi's release notes) is 6–8 points below the 74%+ frontier reference** and loses to both Claude Opus 5 (74.0) and GPT-5.6 Sol (73.0); **ProgramBench at 26.0 is 11 points behind Opus 5**; and **SWE-bench Verified, SWE-bench Pro, LiveCodeBench, SciCode and Vibe Code Bench are all absent**, so three of the band's four references cannot be checked and the only visual-coding evidence is unreproducible.
- **Cost efficiency: 95/100.** The best cost profile in this dataset. **$0.14 input / $0.28 output on first-party and $0.07 input on OpenRouter** is roughly **two orders of magnitude below the $3/$15 anchor**, **cached input at $0.0028 is effectively free**, and the independent verdict confirms it: **Artificial Analysis measured $0.06 per Intelligence Index task — less than half Pro's $0.13** — against market medians of $0.30 input and $1.15 output. Two structural advantages compound it: the capability arrived at **unchanged V2.5-series pricing**, and the **MIT licence plus a ~178 GB checkpoint makes this the one frontier-adjacent omnimodal model in this dataset that is realistically self-hostable on a single 8-GPU node**, with community NVFP4, W4A8 (two RTX PRO 6000), MLX and GGUF builds appearing within hours — including Apple Silicon targets. The deductions are real but narrow: **AA measures only 51 tokens per second and explicitly calls it "notably slow"**, which directly undercuts the high-frequency, large-scale positioning Xiaomi markets; the community quantisations are unofficial and unbenchmarked; and there is no $0 API tier.
- **Overall Score: 85.6/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (85 + 74 + 95 + 90 + 84) / 5 = 85.6 — the correct default for **high-volume agentic and omnimodal work at the lowest credible cost**: it beats Claude Opus 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol on AutomationBench, lands within ~2 points of its own flagship on Terminal Bench 2.1, Toolathlon and OSWorld, carries the flagship's full vision and audio encoders, and costs $0.06 per task with near-free cached input. It is also **the self-hosting pick of its family** — MIT weights, ~178 GB, one node, with same-week community quants down to two workstation GPUs or Apple Silicon. Three things to weigh against it. **Throughput: 51 tokens/second is slow**, which partly cancels the per-token economics on exactly the latency-sensitive pipelines it targets — measure wall-clock cost per completed task, not price per million tokens. **Reasoning is unverified**: not one standard reasoning benchmark has been published and no Intelligence Index value is retrievable, so do not deploy it where general reasoning depth is the product. And **do not use it for security work** — ExploitBench 25.3 against 70–78.5 for the closed flagships, and 47.9 for Pro, is the one place the Flash/Pro gap is decisive. For the hardest terminal agency (Terminal Bench 4.0 28.8) step up to Pro or a closed flagship.
