## Model card

- **Name:** MiMo V2.6 Free. **Open weights, MIT licence.** **Variant/alias finding that governs this report: this is not a distinct model — it is Xiaomi's omnimodal MiMo-V2.6-Flash served as OpenCode Zen's $0 tier.** The repository's curated metadata identifies it as "Xiaomi's open-weights omnimodal Flash MoE (309B/15B, MIT) served as the $0 Zen free tier; top pick for high-volume agentic coding at near-zero cost", with the **same 1,048,576-token window and 128K output as the paid Flash tier** and the **same omnimodal input surface**. **Because the weights, the window and the modalities are all identical to MiMo-V2.6-Flash, all five quality dimensions below carry the same values as my MiMo-V2.6-Flash findings file in this research pass. Cost efficiency is the only difference, and it is the entire point of this report.**
- **Short description:** Alongside GLM 5.3 Free, this is one of the two best free-tier models in this research pass — and it is the better of the two for anything that is not pure text, because **it is genuinely omnimodal: text, image, video and audio in.** A composite tracker places the underlying MiMo-V2.6-Flash at **#33 of 784 with 65.33/100**, which understates it, because three of its results lead this entire research pass: **CyberGym at 95.1%**, **OSWorld-Verified at 80.8%** and **AutomationBench at 52.3%**. **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 87.6% sits essentially on the methodology's ~88% anchor.** All of that from a **15B-active** model, free. The counterweight is specific and serious: **reasoning is the weakest-evidenced dimension of any model in this research pass** — there is **no GPQA, no MMLU-Pro, no ARC-AGI and no FrontierMath figure at all** — and **the AA-Omniscience Index is −12.7% on a 54.4% hallucination rate.**
- **Provider / access:** **OpenCode Zen free tier ($0 in/out)**, with a **paid fallback at $0.14 / $0.28 per 1M**. Underlying weights public at `XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL` on Hugging Face under the **MIT licence**; technical report published at `XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Pro-RL/blob/main/MiMo_V2_6_technical_report.pdf`. Sibling: **MiMo-V2.6-Pro** (74.13 composite).
- **Release / knowledge:** **No verified release date or knowledge cutoff found.** **No verified start or end date for the free tier** — treat its availability as impermanent, though unlike GLM 5.3 Free it has a **documented, cheap paid fallback**.
- **IDs:** `opencode/mimo-v2-6-free`; underlying weights `XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL`. **This is itself the free-tier ID.**
- **Context window:** **1,048,576 tokens per Xiaomi's model card; OpenRouter lists 1,050,000 with a maximum output of 131,072 tokens** — both figures are recorded, and the discrepancy is trivial. **Crucially, the free tier carries the same window as the paid Flash tier** (the repository metadata states 1,048,576 with 128K out), which is **not** the case for GLM 5.3 Free, where the free tier is truncated to 204K. **AA-LCR at 74.3%.** Being MIT-licensed open weights, there is no vendor repricing threshold.
- **Modalities:** **Omnimodal input — text, image, video and audio in → text out.** This is the decisive advantage over every text-only model in this research pass and over GLM 5.3 Free specifically. Audio input places it in the methodology's top multimodal band. Measured breadth is thin, however: **AA-MMMU-Pro at 73.1% is the only multimodal benchmark published for it.**
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **$0 input / $0 output on the OpenCode Zen free tier.** **Paid fallback: $0.14 / 1M input, $0.28 / 1M output** — and OpenRouter has listed input as low as **$0.07**. The weights are additionally free to self-host under MIT. **This is the cheapest capability-per-dollar profile found anywhere in this research pass**, and unlike GLM 5.3 Free the fallback is both documented and genuinely cheap, so the continuity risk is far lower.
- **Architecture:** **Open weights, MIT licence. 309B total parameters with 15B active** — a very sparse MoE (repository metadata), which is what makes both the throughput and the near-zero pricing feasible. The published checkpoint is an RL-tuned variant (`-RL`).

### Raw benchmarks found

> **24 of 649 tracked benchmark slots are populated** for the underlying MiMo-V2.6-Flash (tracker snapshot 2026-10-05) — a thin evidence base, and the thinness is concentrated in reasoning. **No benchmark figures exist for the `mimo-v2-6-free` tier specifically**; every row below is MiMo-V2.6-Flash, which is the correct basis given identical weights, window and modalities, but it is a stated inference. **(X)** = Xiaomi's own MiMo-V2.6 technical report PDF; **(AA)** = Artificial Analysis. Composite: **65.33/100, #33 of 784**, flagged conservative on partial coverage.

Agent / tool use:

- **CyberGym: 95.1% (X)** — **the highest CyberGym figure found anywhere in this research pass**, ahead of GLM-5.3 (84.5%), DeepSeek V4 Pro (83.3%), GPT-5.4 (79.0%) and GPT-5.6 Luna (77.9%). Vulnerability discovery is this model's single strongest measured capability.
- **OSWorld-Verified: 80.8% (X)** — **also the highest in this research pass**, ahead of GPT-5.4 (75%) and Claude Sonnet 4.6 (72.1%). Genuine computer-use competence.
- **Terminal-Bench 2.1: 87.6% (X)** — **essentially on the methodology's ~88% anchor**, from a 15B-active model.
- **AutomationBench: 52.3% (X)** — **the highest AutomationBench figure in this research pass**, ahead of GLM-5.3-Flash (48.8%) and GLM-5.3 (48.2%).
- **JobBench: 61.2% (X)** — far ahead of GPT-5.4 (38.9%) and Claude Sonnet 4.6 (36.9%).
- **Toolathlon-Verified: 73.6% (X)**; **GDPval-AA: 55.0% normalized (AA)** — **no raw Elo figure was published**, so this cannot be checked against the ~1750+ reference directly; the normalized value is comparable to GLM-5.3's 57.8%.
- **Terminal-Bench 4.0: 28.80% (X)**; **Agents' Last Exam: 27.6% (X)**; **ExploitGym: 6.0% (X)** — as with GLM-5.3, discovery vastly outperforms exploitation.
- Claw-Eval / MCP-Atlas / τ²-bench / BrowseComp / AA Agentic Index: **no verified public score found.**

Reasoning / knowledge — **this is the weakest-evidenced dimension of any model in this research pass**:

- **Not one of GPQA, GPQA-Diamond, MMLU-Pro, ARC-AGI (any version) or FrontierMath has any published figure.** Five of the methodology's principal reasoning and knowledge references cannot be checked at all.
- **AA-LCR: 74.3% (AA)** — respectable long-context reasoning.
- **AA-HLE: 35.1% (AA)** — **misses the 40%+ frontier reference by 4.9 points.**
- **Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index: 37.9% (AA)** — 22.1 points below the 60+ frontier marker.
- **CritPt: 12.0% (AA)** — weak.
- **AA-Omniscience: Index −12.7%, accuracy 27.0%, hallucination rate 54.4% (AA)** — **a negative reliability index.** The 54.4% hallucination rate is better than most frontier models assessed here (91.7% for GPT-5.4, 94.1% for DeepSeek V4 Pro) but far worse than Grok 4.3 (25.0%), GLM-5.2 (26.3%) or GLM-5.3 (29.6%), and **raw accuracy of 27.0% is very narrow.**

Coding:

- **Terminal-Bench 2.1: 87.6% (X)** — **clears the methodology's 85%+ coding reference.**
- **DeepSWE: 67.9% (X)** — **6.1 points below the 74%+ reference**, but note the generational jump: **MiMo-V2.5 scored 19.0% on the same benchmark**, so V2.6 improved by 48.9 points.
- **AA-SciCode: 51.3% (AA)** — misses the 55%+ reference by 3.7 points.
- **ProgramBench: 26.0% (X)** — weak on novel program synthesis.
- **No SWE-bench figure of any variant exists, and no AA Coding Index.** For a model whose curated positioning is "top pick for high-volume agentic coding", the absence of any SWE-bench measurement is the largest gap in this report.

Multimodal:

- **AA-MMMU-Pro: 73.1% (AA)** — the **only** multimodal benchmark published for this model, despite an omnimodal input surface. Comparable to Claude Sonnet 4.6's 70.6% and below GPT-5.6 Luna's 78.6%.
- **No CharXiv, document, OCR, video or audio benchmark exists** — so the video and audio input paths, which are this model's defining advantage, are **entirely unmeasured.**

### Normalized scores (1–100)

> **All five quality dimensions are identical to my MiMo-V2.6-Flash findings in this research pass, because the weights, the 1M window, the 128K output ceiling and the omnimodal input surface are all identical. Only Cost efficiency differs.**

- **Tool use: 85/100.** Genuinely impressive for a 15B-active model, with three pass-leading results. **CyberGym at 95.1% is the highest figure found anywhere in this research pass**, more than ten points above GLM-5.3, which Z.AI itself claims is state of the art for vulnerability discovery. **OSWorld-Verified at 80.8% is also the highest here**, ahead of GPT-5.4's 75%. **AutomationBench at 52.3% likewise leads this pass.** Add **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 87.6% sitting essentially on the ~88% anchor**, **JobBench at 61.2%** — roughly 22 points above GPT-5.4 — and **Toolathlon-Verified at 73.6%**. Four deductions. **ExploitGym at 6.0%** shows the same discovery-versus-exploitation gap seen in GLM-5.3, so the CyberGym strength does not extend up the attack chain. **Terminal-Bench 4.0 (28.80%) and Agents' Last Exam (27.6%) both fall away** on the newest and most open-ended harnesses. **GDPval is reported only as a normalized 55.0% with no raw Elo**, so it cannot be checked against the ~1750+ reference. And **Claw-Eval, MCP-Atlas, τ²-bench, BrowseComp and the AA Agentic Index are all unreported**, with the methodology explicitly penalising the Claw-Eval absence.
- **Reasoning: 74/100. This is the weakest-evidenced dimension of any model in this research pass, and the score reflects absence of evidence as much as poor evidence.** **Five of the methodology's principal reasoning references have no figure at all: GPQA, GPQA-Diamond, MMLU-Pro, ARC-AGI at any version, and FrontierMath.** Nothing can be said about graduate-level science, knowledge breadth, abstract pattern reasoning or research mathematics. What does exist is mixed-to-poor: **AA-LCR at 74.3% is respectable**, but **AA-HLE at 35.1% misses the 40%+ reference by 4.9 points**, **the Intelligence Index of 37.9% is 22.1 points below the 60+ marker**, **CritPt at 12.0% is weak**, and **the AA-Omniscience Index is negative at −12.7% on 27.0% accuracy and a 54.4% hallucination rate** — better than the 91–94% rates of several frontier models here, but far worse than the 25–30% achieved by Grok 4.3, GLM-5.2 and GLM-5.3. The score is set at 74 rather than lower because the agentic evidence is strong and agentic competence requires real reasoning; it is set no higher because, on the methodology's own terms, most of the dimension is simply unmeasured.
- **Context window: 95/100.** A **1,000,000-token window** — **1,048,576 per Xiaomi's model card, 1,050,000 with a documented 131,072 maximum output per OpenRouter** — places it in the ≥1M tier (95–100), and **AA-LCR at 74.3%** confirms it is usable at depth. Two structural advantages: being **MIT-licensed open weights there is no vendor repricing threshold**, and — importantly, in contrast to GLM 5.3 Free — **the free tier carries the full window rather than a truncated one**, so nothing is held back behind the paywall. It sits at the tier floor rather than above it because the 100 condition requires **≥98% retrieval at 512K+** and **no MRCR, RULER, CorpusQA or GraphWalks figure exists**, and because **AA-LCR at 74.3% is modest relative to 1M-class peers** such as GPT-5.4 (82.0%), DeepSeek V4 Pro (80.3%) and GLM-5.3 (79.7%).
- **Multimodal: 90/100.** **Genuinely omnimodal input — text, image, video and audio — which places it at the floor of the methodology's top band ("+audio or non-text out = 90–100")**, and makes it the only model among the free tiers in this research pass that can handle non-text input at all. That is a categorical advantage: GLM 5.3 Free scores 18 here because it is text-only, and the gap is not a matter of degree. It is held at the band floor rather than higher for one firm reason: **the capability is almost entirely unmeasured.** **AA-MMMU-Pro at 73.1% is the only multimodal benchmark published** — respectable, above Claude Sonnet 4.6's 70.6% but below GPT-5.6 Luna's 78.6% — and **there is no CharXiv, document, OCR, video or audio benchmark whatsoever**, so the video and audio paths that justify the band placement have no published evidence of quality behind them. The score credits a verified input surface, not demonstrated performance on it.
- **Coding: 84/100.** Strong where measured, with one conspicuous hole. **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 87.6% clears the methodology's 85%+ coding reference** — notable for a 15B-active model, and the strongest single coding datapoint here. **DeepSWE at 67.9% misses the 74%+ reference by 6.1 points**, but the generational trajectory is remarkable: **MiMo-V2.5 scored 19.0% on the same benchmark, so V2.6 gained 48.9 points**, which suggests the post-training pipeline is improving fast. Three deductions. **No SWE-bench figure of any variant exists, and no AA Coding Index** — for a model whose curated positioning is "top pick for high-volume agentic coding", the absence of the single most-cited repository-engineering benchmark is the largest evidential gap in this report. **AA-SciCode at 51.3% misses its reference by 3.7 points.** And **ProgramBench at 26.0%** indicates weak novel program synthesis.
- **Cost efficiency: 97/100. The highest Cost efficiency score I have assigned in this research pass, and it edges above GLM 5.3 Free for one specific reason: the fallback.** The tier itself is **$0 input and $0 output**. For that you get **three pass-leading agentic results (CyberGym 95.1%, OSWorld-Verified 80.8%, AutomationBench 52.3%)**, **Terminal-Bench 2.1 on the ~88% anchor**, **a full 1,048,576-token window with 131K output — not truncated, unlike GLM 5.3 Free's 204K** — and **omnimodal input including audio and video, which no other free tier here offers.** Three further strengths compound it. **The paid fallback is documented and genuinely cheap at $0.14 / $0.28, with OpenRouter input as low as $0.07** — roughly 21× to 43× below the methodology's $3/$15 anchor — so if the promotion ends the cliff is negligible, which is precisely the risk GLM 5.3 Free carries and this does not. **The weights are MIT-licensed**, the most permissive licence in this research pass, so there is no legal constraint on commercial self-hosting. And **309B total with only 15B active** makes self-hosting far more tractable than GLM-5.3's 753B — a meaningful practical difference in the escape path. Deductions are minor: **the free tier has no documented start or end date**, and **no primary Xiaomi rate card was reached** for the fallback figures.
- **Overall Score: 85.6/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (85 + 74 + 95 + 90 + 84) / 5 = 85.6 — **the highest Overall among free tiers in this research pass, and the correct default choice for high-volume agentic work at zero cost.** What holds up: **CyberGym at 95.1%, OSWorld-Verified at 80.8% and AutomationBench at 52.3% all lead this entire research pass**; **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 87.6% sits on the ~88% agentic anchor and clears the 85% coding reference**; **JobBench at 61.2% is roughly 22 points above GPT-5.4**; **the full 1M window with 131K output is available on the free tier, untruncated**; **input is genuinely omnimodal — text, image, video and audio**; and the whole thing is **MIT-licensed at 309B/15B-active**, so self-hosting is both legal and tractable. Against GLM 5.3 Free, the trade is clear and worth stating plainly: **GLM 5.3 Free is better at text reasoning and reliability** (positive +14.3% Omniscience Index against this model's −12.7%, GPQA 91.7% against no GPQA figure at all, and the highest AA Coding Index in this pass), **while MiMo V2.6 Free is better at perception, computer use, security discovery and context** (omnimodal against text-only, 1M untruncated against 204K, CyberGym 95.1% and OSWorld-Verified 80.8%). Three cautions govern adoption. **Reasoning is the least-evidenced dimension in this entire pass** — no GPQA, no MMLU-Pro, no ARC-AGI, no FrontierMath — so do not assume capability that has not been measured. **It hallucinates at 54.4% with a negative −12.7% reliability index on only 27.0% raw accuracy**, so it must be paired with retrieval and verification for anything factual. And **the omnimodal advantage is unproven**: AA-MMMU-Pro at 73.1% is the sole multimodal measurement, with the video and audio paths entirely untested.
