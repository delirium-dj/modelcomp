## Model card

- **Name:** Qwen 3.7 Max. **Proprietary** — note this, because most Qwen models assessed in this research pass are open-weight; the Max tier is not, so there is no self-hosting escape from its rate card. **Reasoning**, `base` variant.
- **Short description:** Alibaba's **May 2026** proprietary flagship agent model, positioned for long-horizon coding, office automation and multi-agent workflows. A composite tracker places it **#41 of 882 at 62.61/100** across an unusually deep **57 of 613 benchmarks**. Its profile is strongly weighted toward knowledge and reasoning rather than agency. **MMLU-Pro at 89.6% vendor / 89.3% independent is the highest MMLU-Pro figure found anywhere in this research pass**; **HMMT Feb 2026 at 97.1% is the highest competition-mathematics figure in this pass**; **GPQA Diamond clears 90% under three separate measurements agreeing within 2.2 points**; **both HLE figures clear the 40%+ reference**; **IFEval reaches 94.3%**; **MRCRv2 at 90.4% is a genuine million-token retrieval measurement**; and **the AA-Omniscience Index is positive at +13.5% on a 25.6% hallucination rate — the second-lowest confabulation rate found in this pass.** Against that, the agentic side is weak where it matters commercially: **GDPval-AA at 1,190 Elo is 560 below reference** and **the AA Agentic Index is 23.9%**. It is also **text-only** and **superseded by Qwen3.8 Max.**
- **Provider / access:** Alibaba API, OpenRouter (`qwen/qwen3.7-max`). Independent measurement via Artificial Analysis and Vals AI. Alibaba published a dedicated release page (`qwen.ai/blog?id=qwen3.7`) that supplies most rows below.
- **Release / knowledge:** **May 2026** (repository metadata). **No verified knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `opencode/qwen-3.7-max`; OpenRouter `qwen/qwen3.7-max`. **No free-tier ID, and no open weights** — unlike most of the Qwen line.
- **Context window:** **1,000,000 tokens with 131,072 maximum output** (repository metadata). Unusually well-evidenced at length: **MRCRv2 at 90.4%** is a direct million-token retrieval measurement — better than DeepSeek V4 Pro's MRCR 1M of 83.5% and far better than Muse Spark 1.1's 54.1% — with **AA-LCR at 79.0%** corroborating.
- **Modalities:** **Text in → text out. Text-only**, per this repository's curated metadata ("Text in/out; reasoning and tool calls"), and corroborated by the benchmark record: across 57 populated slots **the only multimodal entry is Design Arena Website**, which measures generated output rather than input. **Note the contrast within Alibaba's own line: Qwen3.8 Max and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next both accept image and video input and post CharXiv above 90%**, so text-only is a property of this specific model, not of the vendor.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **$1.48 / 1M input, $4.43 / 1M output on OpenRouter, with cached input at approximately $0.25** (repository metadata). That is roughly **2× cheaper on input and 3.4× cheaper on output than the methodology's $3/$15 anchor**, with the cached rate about **5.9× below uncached**. **No primary Alibaba rate card was reached; re-verify before budgeting.**
- **Architecture:** **Proprietary, closed weights.** Parameter count and topology: not disclosed.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 76/100.** A genuinely mixed picture that resolves as mid-field. On the credit side, four results are real: **Claw-Eval at 65.2% is present**, satisfying a benchmark the methodology explicitly penalises models for lacking; **τ²-bench at 94.7% is far above the Tau-family reference**; **MCP Atlas at 76.4% is third-highest in this research pass**, a meaningful credential for Model-Context-Protocol orchestration; and **BFCL v4 at 75.0%** is one of few function-calling measurements available in this pass. Five deductions weigh heavily against the "long-horizon… multi-agent workflows" positioning. **GDPval-AA at 1,190 Elo is 560 points below the ~1750+ reference and among the lowest in this pass** — so economically valuable real-world work is weak. **The AA Agentic Index of 23.9% is very low**, roughly half DeepSeek V4 Pro's, and corroborates that reading rather than contradicting it. **Terminal-Bench sits 18.3 to 27 points below the anchor** (69.7% vendor on 2.0, 61.0% independent on 2.1), with no 3.0 or 4.0 figure. **VITA-Bench (47.9%) and ResearchClawBench (18.7%) both fall away** on long-horizon work. And **OSWorld, Toolathlon, CyberGym and AutomationBench are all unreported.** Two of the better-looking rows — **QwenClawBench and QwenWebBench — are vendor-proprietary benchmarks with no external baseline**, so they are recorded but cannot be credited.
- **Reasoning: 86/100.** The strongest dimension by a clear margin, and unusually well-corroborated. **MMLU-Pro at 89.6% vendor / 89.3% independent is the highest MMLU-Pro figure found anywhere in this research pass**, agreeing to within 0.3 points. **HMMT Feb 2026 at 97.1% is the highest competition-mathematics figure in this pass**, with **IMOAnswerBench at 90.0%** alongside. **GPQA Diamond clears the 90%+ reference under three separate measurements within 2.2 points (92.4% / 92.3% / 90.2%)**. **Both HLE figures clear the 40%+ reference** (41.4% vendor, 40.5% independent, agreeing within 0.9). **IFEval at 94.3% and AA-IFBench at 80.5%** put instruction-following near the top of this pass. **MRCRv2 at 90.4%** demonstrates million-token retrieval. **The AA-Omniscience Index is positive at +13.5% on a 25.6% hallucination rate — the second-lowest in this pass.** And the multilingual battery is consistently strong (MAXIFE 89.2%, MMLU-ProX 87%, PolyMath 86.5%, INCLUDE 86.2%). Four deductions prevent a higher score: **the Intelligence Index of 29.5% is 30.5 points below the 60+ marker and flatly contradicts the knowledge results** — a contradiction reported rather than resolved, and a reason not to treat the MMLU-Pro and GPQA figures as the whole story; **raw Omniscience accuracy is only 31.1%**, so good calibration partly reflects a narrow knowledge base rather than judgement; **CritPt at 13.4% and Apex at 44.5%** show frontier physics and research mathematics remain weak despite the competition-maths strength; and **neither ARC-AGI nor FrontierMath has any figure**, leaving abstract reasoning unmeasured.
- **Context window: 96/100.** A **1,000,000-token window with 131,072 maximum output**, and it earns **above** the ≥1M tier floor because the depth claim is measured rather than asserted: **MRCRv2 at 90.4% is a direct million-token retrieval result**, and it is the best such figure found in this research pass — ahead of DeepSeek V4 Pro's MRCR 1M of 83.5% and far ahead of Muse Spark 1.1's 54.1%, both of which also publish at 1M. **AA-LCR at 79.0%** corroborates usable long-context reasoning. It stops short of 100 for three reasons: the top condition requires **≥98% retrieval at 512K+** and 90.4% does not meet it; **no RULER, CorpusQA or GraphWalks figure exists** to triangulate; and **the 131K output ceiling is modest** against DeepSeek's 384K, which matters for whole-repository generation.
- **Multimodal: 16/100.** **Text-only**, capped in the methodology's "text-only = 10–20" band, and the determination is well-supported: the repository's curated metadata states "Text in/out", and across **57 populated benchmark slots the only multimodal entry is Design Arena Website** — which is **explicitly excluded** as an output-quality rather than input-modality measure, and at 1,280 Elo sits below GLM-5.3 (1,308) and GLM-5.2 (1,294) anyway. It is placed mid-band rather than at the top because there is no visual capability of any kind to credit. **The within-vendor contrast is worth stating: Qwen3.8 Max and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next both accept image and video input and post CharXiv above 90%**, so if your workload touches images, Alibaba has strictly better options — including a cheaper, open-weight one.
- **Coding: 78/100.** Competent but short of the references, and the vendor figures need discounting. **LiveCodeBench at 91.6% vendor is independently confirmed at 87.1%** — a 4.5-point gap and the most trustworthy result here, showing genuine strength on self-contained competitive problems. **SWE Multilingual at 78.3%** is solid. But **SWE-bench Verified at 80.4% vendor sits against SWE-bench at 68.8% from Vals AI — an 11.6-point gap across related harnesses** — so repository-scale patching is mid-field rather than strong. Then the references are missed consistently: **the AA Coding Index at 66.0% misses 70%+ by 4 points**; **SciCode at 53.5% and AA-SciCode at 49.5% both miss 55%+**; **Terminal-Bench 2.0 at 69.7% misses the 85%+ coding reference by 15.3 points**; and **NL2Repo at 47.2% and OpenHarmony Bench at 53.4%** are weak. **DeepSWE, Codeforces, CursorBench and Vibe Code Bench are all unreported.** For a model positioned for "long-horizon coding", not clearing a single coding reference threshold is the central finding.
- **Cost efficiency: 54/100.** The rate card is reasonable in isolation — **$1.48 / $4.43 is roughly 2× cheaper on input and 3.4× cheaper on output than the $3/$15 anchor**, with **cached input at about $0.25, some 5.9× below uncached**, which suits multi-agent workflows that re-send a shared system prompt. What drives the score below the midpoint is **dominance inside Alibaba's own lineup, compounded by this being the one Qwen tier you cannot escape.** **Qwen3.8 Max scores 70.49 against this model's 62.61** and adds image and video input. More damaging still, **Qwen3.8-Flash-Next scores 64.11 — higher than this model — at $0.12 / $0.40 on Vercel**, which is roughly **12× cheaper on input and 11× cheaper on output**, while also being **open-weight** and **multimodal** with an **AA Coding Index of 73.0% against this model's 66.0%**. And because **this model is proprietary with no open weights**, there is no self-hosting fallback — unusual in the Qwen line and a real strategic cost. The score is not lower because the knowledge, mathematics, instruction-following and long-context results are genuinely top-tier and not fully replicated by the cheaper siblings.
- **Overall Score: 70.4/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (76 + 86 + 96 + 16 + 78) / 5 = 70.4 — **a knowledge-and-reasoning specialist mis-positioned as an agent model.** Its curated description emphasises "long-horizon coding, office automation and multi-agent workflows", and the evidence does not support that: **GDPval-AA at 1,190 Elo is 560 below reference**, **the AA Agentic Index is 23.9%**, **Terminal-Bench sits 18 to 27 points below anchor**, and **not one coding reference threshold is cleared**. What the evidence does support is excellent: **MMLU-Pro at 89.6%/89.3% is the highest in this research pass**; **HMMT Feb 2026 at 97.1% is the highest competition-mathematics figure here**, with IMOAnswerBench at 90.0%; **GPQA Diamond clears 90% three times within 2.2 points**; **both HLE figures clear 40%**; **IFEval reaches 94.3% and AA-IFBench 80.5%**; **MRCRv2 at 90.4% is the best million-token retrieval measurement in this pass**; **MMLU-Redux is 95% and the multilingual battery consistently above 86%**; and **the AA-Omniscience Index is positive at +13.5% on a 25.6% hallucination rate, second-lowest here.** Choose it for **knowledge-intensive question answering, competition mathematics, multilingual work, instruction-bound workflows and long-context document analysis** — tasks where breadth, calibration and a genuinely usable million-token window matter more than autonomous agency. Four cautions. **It is text-only**, while Alibaba's own Qwen3.8 Max and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next both handle image and video. **It is commercially dominated** — Qwen3.8-Flash-Next scores higher at roughly a twelfth of the price, open-weight and multimodal, with a better Coding Index. **It is proprietary**, so unlike most of the Qwen line there is no self-hosting escape. And **the Intelligence Index of 29.5% flatly contradicts the knowledge results**, which is a genuine warning that the strong MMLU-Pro and GPQA figures may not generalise to the composite tasks that index weights.
