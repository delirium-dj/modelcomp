# Grok 4 Fast — Independent Research Report

## Summary and evidence

Grok 4 Fast is xAI's efficiency-optimized Grok 4 variant. xAI documents a **2 million-token context window** and launch pricing of **$0.20 input / $0.50 output per million tokens** for shorter-context requests, alongside reasoning and non-reasoning modes.

Sources: [xAI Grok 4 Fast announcement](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-fast), [Grok 4 Fast model card](https://data.x.ai/2025-09-19-grok-4-fast-model-card.pdf)

## Cost and limitations

Long-context pricing can differ from the headline short-context rate. The efficiency tuning also gives up some peak reasoning and coding quality relative to xAI's largest full models.

## Scores

- **Tool use: 88/100.** Agent-oriented reasoning and API tool support are strong for a fast model.
- **Reasoning: 82/100.** Strong efficiency-adjusted reasoning is below the full frontier tier.
- **Context window: 99/100.** The documented 2M-token window is among the largest generally available.
- **Multimodal: 76/100.** Vision capability is useful, but broad native media coverage is not its focus.
- **Coding: 81/100.** Capable coding at high speed, without top-tier specialist benchmark evidence.
- **Cost efficiency: 99/100.** Headline token prices are exceptionally low for this context and capability level.
- **Overall Score: 85/100.** Half-up rounded mean: (88 + 82 + 99 + 76 + 81) / 5 = 85.2.

## Bottom line

Grok 4 Fast is a compelling economical choice for huge-context tool workflows that do not require maximum coding quality.
