## Model card

- **Name:** Ox Alpha. **Variant/alias status: anonymous stealth model.** It appeared on OpenRouter as **`stealth/ox-alpha` around 2026-08-20** with **no company announcement and no technical white paper**, and **no developer has claimed it.** It is described as **the first natively omnimodal model in OpenRouter's Stealth anonymous series.**
- **Short description:** **This report differs from every other in this research pass in one decisive respect: there is no verified benchmark data for this model whatsoever.** No benchmark tracker carries it (`benchlm.ai/models/ox-alpha` returns 404), no vendor has published results because no vendor has claimed it, and no independent harness — Artificial Analysis, Vals AI, ARC Prize, Epoch, Cursor — has measured it. Everything circulating is **unsigned community testing with opaque methodology**, which the most substantive source available explicitly says should be "treated as pending independent verification". What *is* independently confirmed is the specification: **a 1.05M-token context window, 130K maximum output, text/image/video input, a knowledge cutoff extending to November 2025, a zero-data-retention policy, and a cache hit rate of about 77.9%.** Three of the five quality dimensions below are therefore **explicit evidence-absence scores**, flagged as such rather than presented as measurements.
- **Attribution — reported, not confirmed:** The technical fingerprints point densely at **Zhipu's GLM family**, and the specific evidence is quantified rather than impressionistic: **the tokenizer matches GLM on all 11 measured indicators**; **a digit-probe test consumed 29 tokens — an exact GLM match, against DeepSeek's 98**; **the deployment configuration closely mirrors GLM-5.3**; and **Ox Alpha adopted GLM-5.3's new settings just two days after they shipped.** Zhipu founder Tang Jie had teased an upcoming multimodal mode three months earlier, and **previous Stealth anonymous models were eventually revealed as GLM-5 or MiMo releases**, so the "anonymous warm-up to collect high-concurrency data" pattern has precedent. The most likely reading is a **stealth test of a multimodal GLM** — which would be coherent, since GLM-5.3 is text-only while GLM-5.3-Flash is natively multimodal with video. **This attribution is explicitly NOT used as a scoring basis below**, because it is unconfirmed speculation; it is recorded because it is the best available guide to what the model probably is.
- **Provider / access:** OpenRouter (`stealth/ox-alpha`) and OpenCode Zen. **API is OpenAI-SDK compatible**, so it drops into Cursor, Aider and similar tools without adaptation.
- **Release / knowledge:** Listed **circa 2026-08-20**. **Knowledge cutoff extending to November 2025** — independently reported, and notably *older* than most frontier models assessed in this pass.
- **IDs:** `opencode/ox-alpha`; OpenRouter `stealth/ox-alpha`.
- **Context window:** **1,050,000 tokens with 130K maximum output** — independently confirmed (this repository's curated metadata records 1M / 131K out, consistent within rounding). **A cache hit rate of about 77.9%** is also reported, which is unusually specific for a stealth listing. **But there is no AA-LCR, MRCR, RULER, CorpusQA or GraphWalks measurement of any kind**, so nothing demonstrates the window is usable at depth.
- **Modalities:** **Text, image and video input → text output**, independently confirmed, with this repository's curated metadata additionally recording **PDF input**. Described as the first natively omnimodal model in the Stealth series. **No multimodal benchmark of any kind exists**, so the input surface is verified while its quality is entirely unmeasured.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **This needs flagging as a live discrepancy.** Ox Alpha launched with **a free week at $0 per million tokens** on OpenRouter and OpenCode Zen. It launched **circa 2026-08-20**; this report is dated **2026-10-03**, roughly six weeks later — **so the advertised free week has almost certainly expired**, yet this repository's curated metadata still records "Free Zen tier". **No post-preview rate card exists at all**, and the source consulted warns explicitly that "an anonymous test model can vanish or be repriced at any time". Treat the $0 as historical rather than current, and **verify the live OpenRouter listing before relying on it.**
- **Architecture:** **Undisclosed.** No parameter count, no topology, no licence. If the GLM attribution is correct it would be a sparse MoE, but that is inference, not fact.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

> **Three of the five dimensions below are explicit evidence-absence scores.** They are not measurements and are not estimates of likely capability — they reflect the methodology's rule that missing data is penalised rather than assumed favourable. **The GLM-5.3 attribution is deliberately not used as a scoring basis**, because borrowing a confirmed model's scores on the strength of tokenizer fingerprints would manufacture confidence that does not exist. **This report should be rescored from scratch the moment any signed benchmark lands.**

- **Tool use: 55/100. Evidence-absence score.** **Not one agentic benchmark exists** — no Terminal-Bench at any version, no GDPval-AA, no Claw-Eval, no OSWorld, no τ²-bench, no MCP-Atlas, no Toolathlon, no CyberGym, no AA Agentic Index. The methodology explicitly penalises a missing Claw-Eval; here the entire agentic battery is missing. The only signal is an unsigned community claim of beating GPT-5.6 Sol on "agentic tasks", which the reporting source itself discounts twice over for opaque methodology and free-period serving configuration. The score is set at the middle of the range rather than lower because two verified structural facts do support agentic use — **a 1.05M context with 130K output suits long-horizon loops**, and **OpenAI-SDK compatibility means it actually runs in Cursor and Aider** — and no higher because nothing whatsoever has been measured.
- **Reasoning: 55/100. Evidence-absence score, and this is the dimension where the absence matters most.** **No GPQA, no HLE, no ARC-AGI, no FrontierMath, no MMLU-Pro, no CritPt and no Intelligence Index.** Critically, **there is no AA-Omniscience measurement**, which means **the hallucination rate and calibration of this model are completely unknown** — and across this research pass that figure has ranged from 25.0% (Grok 4.3) to 96.5% (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash), a spread wide enough to make a model either unusually trustworthy or unusable. For an unattributed model with no accountable vendor, unknown reliability is a serious deployment risk rather than a neutral gap. The sole claim is olympiad-level mathematics in an **anonymous GitHub Gist** with no methodology or author. One further verified fact weighs slightly against: **the knowledge cutoff extends only to November 2025**, older than most frontier models assessed in this pass, so knowledge recency is a known limitation rather than an unknown.
- **Context window: 92/100.** The window itself is the best-evidenced thing about this model: **1,050,000 tokens with 130K maximum output, independently confirmed** and consistent with this repository's curated metadata, plus **a reported cache hit rate of about 77.9%** — unusually specific for a stealth listing, and directly useful for repeated long-context work. That places it squarely in the ≥1M tier. It is scored **just below the tier floor of 95** for one reason, stated plainly: **there is no long-context measurement of any kind — no AA-LCR, MRCR, RULER, CorpusQA or GraphWalks** — so while the window is confirmed to *exist*, nothing at all demonstrates it is usable at depth. Across this pass, measured depth at the same nominal size has ranged from 64.3% (Grok 4.3) to 83.7% (GPT-5.6 Luna) on AA-LCR, so the nominal figure is a weak predictor. The ≥1M tier's 100 condition (≥98% retrieval at 512K+) is unreachable on zero evidence.
- **Multimodal: 80/100.** **Video input is independently confirmed**, and this repository's curated metadata additionally records **PDF input**, which places it in the methodology's "+video/PDF in = 75–90" band — and the listing is specifically noted as **the first natively omnimodal model in OpenRouter's Stealth series**, so the capability is a deliberate design point rather than an afterthought. It is placed in the **middle** of that band rather than near the ceiling because **no multimodal benchmark of any kind exists** — no MMMU, MMMU-Pro, CharXiv, MMVU, LVBench, OCR or audio measurement. For comparison, the models I scored at 86 in this band earned it on measured breadth (GLM-5.3-Flash: CharXiv 89.4%, MMVU 80.5%; Qwen3.8-Flash-Next: CharXiv 90.6%, MathVision 90.6%, LVBench 76.6%). Here the input surface is verified and its quality is entirely unknown, so the score credits the former and withholds the latter. **There is also no audio path**, so the top band is unavailable regardless.
- **Coding: 60/100. Evidence-absence score, with one claim given minimal credit.** **No SWE-bench of any variant, no LiveCodeBench, no Terminal-Bench, no AA Coding Index, no SciCode, no CursorBench.** The single semi-specific claim is **"~80% DeepSWE"**, which if genuine would clear the methodology's 74%+ reference and rank at the very top of this research pass — above GLM-5.3 (66.9%), MiMo-V2.6-Flash (67.9%), GPT-5.6 Luna (67.2%) and DeepSeek V4 Pro (62.7%). **It is credited only marginally, for three reasons**: it comes from a single unsourced blog headline with no methodology; the authoritative source consulted warns that community tests on this model "vary wildly in method and sample size"; and **a figure that would be a double-digit outlier against every independently measured peer is weaker evidence than an in-range figure, not stronger.** The score is set above the other two evidence-absence dimensions because the coding claims are the most consistent and most specific of those circulating, and because the model is explicitly positioned for long-horizon coding agents. **If an 80% DeepSWE result is ever independently confirmed, this dimension should be rescored sharply upward — it is the largest single uncertainty in this report.**
- **Cost efficiency: 70/100. This score is dominated by unplannability, not by price.** On price alone it would be maximal: **$0 per million tokens** at launch on both OpenRouter and OpenCode Zen, for a 1.05M-token omnimodal model, with a **zero-data-retention policy**, a **~77.9% cache hit rate** and **OpenAI-SDK compatibility** that eliminates migration cost. Four deductions pull it down hard, and the first is the most important. **The free offer was a "free week" beginning circa 2026-08-20, so as of this report's date it has almost certainly expired** — while this repository's curated metadata still records a "Free Zen tier", a discrepancy flagged here as needing correction. **No post-preview rate card exists at all**, so there is nothing to plan against. **The model can vanish or be repriced at any time** — the explicit warning of the source consulted — and with **no accountable vendor there is no SLA, no deprecation notice and no support path.** And **"zero data retention" is a platform claim, not an audited guarantee**, which matters because an unattributed model gives you no idea whose infrastructure is processing your prompts. Free is only valuable if it is available; this is free-and-ephemeral.
- **Overall Score: 68.4/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (55 + 55 + 92 + 80 + 60) / 5 = 68.4 — **and this number should be read as a statement about evidence, not about capability.** Ox Alpha may well be considerably better than 68.4 suggests; community testing claims it beats GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Fable 5 on coding and agentic work, and the GLM-5.3 fingerprint evidence is dense enough that it is plausibly a strong multimodal frontier model. **But nothing has been measured.** No benchmark tracker lists it, no vendor has claimed it, and no independent harness — Artificial Analysis, Vals AI, ARC Prize, Epoch, Cursor — has produced a single figure. Under a methodology that penalises missing data rather than assuming the best, three of five dimensions can only be scored on absence. What is genuinely verified and genuinely useful: **a 1.05M-token window with 130K output and a ~77.9% cache hit rate**, **confirmed text, image, video and PDF input as the first natively omnimodal Stealth model**, **a zero-data-retention policy**, **OpenAI-SDK compatibility** that drops it straight into Cursor and Aider, and **a November 2025 knowledge cutoff.** Use it as the source consulted recommends: **try it on your own real tasks — whole-repository refactoring without RAG chunking, or diagnosing front-end interaction bugs from video — while keeping a known model such as GPT-5.6 Sol as a baseline.** Three cautions govern anything beyond experimentation. **Do not build a production pipeline on an anonymous model** that can disappear or be repriced without notice and carries no SLA. **The free window has almost certainly closed** and no replacement pricing exists, so the repository's "Free Zen tier" note needs verifying. And **reliability is completely unknown** — with no AA-Omniscience measurement, the hallucination rate could plausibly sit anywhere in the 25%-to-96% range observed across this research pass, which for an unattributed model with no accountable vendor is the single riskiest unknown here.
