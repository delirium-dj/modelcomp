## Model card

- **Name:** Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B — NVIDIA's **"4GB" edge tier of the Nemotron 3.5 Bolt family**, from the official HF card `nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B-4GB`. **A negative finding governs this report and is its headline: BenchLM carries a page for this slug with 17 source-displayable benchmark rows and no overall score** — its own text reads *"Not computed (unranked)"* with Overall Rank "Unranked". So the repo queue's Overall of 50.0 is a **roster artefact, not a measured figure**.
- **Short description:** A **tool-use-specialist edge model with a 64K window, a genuinely strong retrieval profile at that window, and no reasoning or coding benchmark of any kind.** Its agentic rows are its whole story: **ToolSandbox 74.13%, BFCL v3 67.3%, RelyToolBench 69.29%, When2Call Multi-Turn 60.50% and τ²-bench 52.3%**, with **RULER (64K) 85.76% and LongMemEval 69.92%** confirming real long-horizon behaviour. Against that, **AgentIF 22.95%** is its one weak agentic row, and **GPQA, HLE, AIME, CritPt, AA-LCR, SWE-bench, LiveCodeBench, AA-SciCode, the AA Coding Index and Terminal-Bench are all entirely unmeasured.**
- **Provider / access:** **Open weight under OpenMDW-1.1** on Hugging Face (`nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B-4GB`), self-hostable; the Bolt family ships open weights, so self-hosting is expected to be free.
- **Release / knowledge:** **the repo's curated meta records the release as scheduled for 2026-10-23, i.e. this is a just-released or still-landing model** — the thinnest release-status position in this batch. **Knowledge cutoff not disclosed.**
- **IDs:** `nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B-4GB` (Hugging Face, the official card); `opencode/nemotron-3.5-bolt-e4b` (repo id); `nemotron-3-5-bolt-e4b` (BenchLM canonical).
- **Context window: a conflict is recorded rather than reconciled — BenchLM lists 64K while the repo's curated meta records "Not published" with the release still pending.** BenchLM's 64K is used for scoring, since it is the only figure that exists.
- **Modalities:** **text in; text out** per the repo's curated meta, which notes "voice implied, not specified" for an unreleased model.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-11-06):** **not published** — the repo's curated meta records no rate card. The Bolt family ships open weights under OpenMDW-1.1, so self-hosting is expected to be free, and the cost score rests on that licence fact rather than a measured price.
- **Architecture:** **open weight, OpenMDW-1.1.** BenchLM classifies it **Reasoning**. Hybrid LatentMoE, the same architectural family as Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, at a 4GB-class footprint.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 62/100.** Below the mid band's top, and the model's entire measured story. **ToolSandbox 74.13%, RelyToolBench 69.29%, BFCL v3 67.3% and When2Call Multi-Turn 60.50%** form a genuinely solid upper-mid agentic spread — better than most models in this batch can claim — and **τ²-bench 52.3%** is mid-band. Held at 62 rather than higher by **AgentIF 22.95%**, a visible 51-point contradiction with ToolSandbox that suggests the tool-use capability is harness-specific, and by the **absence of Terminal-Bench on every harness, τ³-bench, MCP-Atlas, OSWorld, BrowseComp, Toolathlon, MazeBench, ALE, Claw-Eval, JobBench, GDPval-AA and the AA Agentic Index**, so all three frontier references are unmeasured and no composite exists. **The eight instruction-following rows** (up to Brevity 82.84%, RoleMRC 79.12%, StructEval-T 79.99%) **corroborate a real instruction substrate**, though several are explicitly provider-reported and weighted accordingly.
- **Reasoning: 50/100.** **Scored at the methodology's neutral floor for "dimension claimed, entirely unmeasured."** Not one reasoning benchmark exists — **no GPQA Diamond, HLE, CritPt, AA-LCR, MRCR, ARC-AGI, AIME, MATH, MMLU-Pro, SuperGPQA, AA Intelligence Index or AA-Omniscience figure anywhere in the 17 rows.** Scored at 50 rather than below because BenchLM classifies the model **Reasoning** and RULER 85.76% / LongMemEval 69.92% demonstrate strong long-horizon behaviour, which is a reasoning-adjacent property; **not scored above 50 because every single reasoning reference in the methodology is unmeasurable, and an unmeasured capability claim must not be scored as though it were demonstrated.**
- **Context window: 70/100.** **64,000 tokens** per BenchLM — the methodology's 50–64 band at its ceiling, scored at 70 on the strength of **RULER (64K) 85.76%, LongMemEval 69.92% and LongBench v2 48.7%**, which together are strong evidence that the window is genuinely usable at its full depth rather than merely nominal. **A conflict is recorded rather than reconciled: BenchLM lists 64K while the repo's curated meta records "Not published" with the release scheduled for 2026-10-23**, BenchLM's figure used because it is the only one that exists. Held at 70 rather than the 85 tier because 64K is well under the current standard and **no MRCR or GraphWalks figure exists**.
- **Multimodal: 15/100.** **Text in; text out** per the repo's curated meta — squarely in the methodology's text-only 10–20 band. **The meta notes "voice implied, not specified" for an unreleased model, which is recorded rather than scored**: an unspecified modality is not a measured one, and the 15 reflects the text-only fact rather than the possibility of more.
- **Coding: 45/100.** Below the methodology's mid band, and **entirely unmeasured** — **no SWE-bench Verified/Pro, LiveCodeBench, AA-SciCode, AA Coding Index, DeepSWE, Vibe Code Bench, Terminal-Bench or FrontierCode figure exists anywhere in the 17 rows**, so all three coding frontier references are unmeasured and no composite exists. Held at 45 rather than the neutral 50 floor because **ToolSandbox 74.13% and When2Call Multi-Turn 60.50% are terminal-style tool-use results** that are adjacent to (but not) coding benchmarks, and the model's whole profile is agentic execution — scored just below the neutral floor on that adjacency rather than at it.
- **Cost efficiency: 97/100.** **Open weights under OpenMDW-1.1 at a 4GB-class footprint, with self-hosting expected free and no rate card published** — the methodology's $0 anchor with genuinely deployable edge economics. **Explicitly a licence-and-footprint inference, not a price measurement**, since the repo's curated meta records no published rate; held at 97 rather than 100 because no price is published to verify and no free hosted route is confirmed.
- **Overall Score: 48/100.** (62 + 50 + 70 + 15 + 45) / 5 = 48.4 → **48** → **48**. **Note the queue's 50.0 is a roster artefact: BenchLM leaves this model unranked with 17 of 753 benchmarks.** Recommendation: **a 4GB-class tool-use specialist whose entire measured value is in its agentic and long-horizon rows.** ToolSandbox 74.13%, RelyToolBench 69.29%, BFCL v3 67.3% and RULER (64K) 85.76% at an OpenMDW-licensed 4GB footprint is a real proposition for on-device agent loops and local tool routing. Three cautions: **reasoning and coding are entirely unmeasured — not weak, absent** — so it cannot be assessed as a knowledge, reasoning or code model at all; **AgentIF 22.95% contradicts ToolSandbox 74.13% by 51 points**, so its tool-use capability is harness-specific and should be validated on your own harness before deployment; and **the model's release status is the thinnest in this batch (scheduled 2026-10-23, no published rate card, no published context figure in the repo's meta)**, so treat every figure as provisional.
