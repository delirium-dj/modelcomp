> **Verdict:** Claude 3.5 Sonnet is **Anthropic's era-defining 2024 workhorse** — the Sonnet generation that, in the meta's words, "*first made agentic coding (not just chat) a mass habit*." The line shipped in **two versions**: the **June 20, 2024** original (`claude-3-5-sonnet-20240620`; **MMLU 88.7%, HumanEval 92.0%, GPQA-Diamond 59.4%**, beating Claude 3 Opus at ~⅕ the cost, 64% on Anthropic's internal agentic-coding eval vs Opus's 38%) and the **October 22, 2024** upgrade (`claude-3-5-sonnet-20241022`, the folder's id — "**new** Claude 3.5 Sonnet" / "Sonnet 3.5 v2"), which added **computer use** (the first frontier model with a public GUI-control capability) and scored **SWE-bench Verified 49%** (then-SOTA, beating the prior 45%). Net: **Overall 75 (divergence +2.7 vs the curated 72.3)** — a historically pivotal, launch-SOTA coder whose 49% SWE-bench, ~59% GPQA and 200K context now sit far below today's floor; my +2.7 credits its genuine era-leading coding/vision slightly above the curated mean while fully accounting for retirement.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 72/100.** Historically pivotal — the October upgrade debuted **computer use** (first public frontier GUI-control) and posted **SWE-bench Verified 49%** (launch SOTA). Real agentic capability *for 2024*, but **retired**, non-reasoning, and 49%/early-computer-use is now far below the 2026 floor (frontier OSWorld 60–70%, SWE-bench 75%+).
- **Reasoning: 74/100.** Strong for its day — **MMLU 88.7%**, **GPQA-Diamond 59.4%** (both beat Claude 3 Opus in mid-2024) — but it is **non-reasoning** (no thinking mode; that arrived with Sonnet 3.7), has **no AA Index (delisted)**, and GPQA ~59% is now well below frontier (90%+). A capable pre-reasoning-generation model, dated.
- **Context window: 76/100.** **200K** tokens — the era-standard, ample for 2024 but a quarter of today's common 1M and below the 200K+ peers with proven long-context retrieval. Max output unpublished (~8K). Consistent with the other 200K Claude Sonnet generations.
- **Multimodal: 76/100.** **Text + image (vision) → text**, with genuinely good 2024-vintage document/chart/screenshot reading (the basis for computer use) — but **image-only** (no audio/video), **text-only output**, and its vision is dated versus current native-VL/omni models.
- **Coding: 76/100.** The raison d'être of its legend — **HumanEval 92.0%** (elite for 2024) and **SWE-bench Verified 49%** (launch SOTA), the model that mainstreamed agentic coding. But SWE-bench 49% is now far below floor (it trails Sonnet 4's ~61–75% and Sonnet 3.7's ~62%), and it is **retired** with no independent modern re-run.
- **Cost efficiency: 58/100.** *(Not counted in Overall.)* **Retired** (requests fail on Anthropic platforms), last published **$3/$15** — competitive in 2024 (⅕ of Opus) but mid-tier now, with **no free route** and no self-host option (proprietary).
- **Overall Score: 75/100.** Half-up mean of the five quality dims: (72 + 74 + 76 + 76 + 76) / 5 = 374 / 5 = **74.8 → 75**. **Divergence +2.7** vs the curated 72.3. Claude 3.5 Sonnet is a **historically transformative** model — the launch-SOTA agentic coder and first public computer-use frontier model — but as a **retired, non-reasoning, 200K, image-only** system with **49% SWE-bench / ~59% GPQA** and **no independent (AA) verification**, it lands in the mid-70s, correctly below the later Sonnet 3.7 (77) and Sonnet 4 (78) I scored. My +2.7 over the curated 72.3 credits its era-leading coding/vision and pioneering computer use slightly above the mean while fully weighing its retirement and sub-floor benchmarks.
