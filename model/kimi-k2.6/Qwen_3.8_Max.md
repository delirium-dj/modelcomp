## Model card

- **Name:** Kimi K2.6
- **Short description:** **Moonshot AI's open-weight 1T-parameter native-multimodal agentic MoE**, released **2026-04-20** (weights on Hugging Face under a **Modified MIT license**; subscribers got it 2026-04-13 after a ~1-week closed beta). It is the **highest-ranked open-weight model on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (54, #4 overall — behind only GPT-5.5's 60 and the Anthropic/Google/OpenAI tier at 57, of 234 open models among 367 evaluated)** and its headline differentiation is **first-party Agent Swarm orchestration at 300 sub-agents / 4,000 coordinated steps**, with vendor demos of **12+ hour autonomous coding runs** (the exchange-core rewrite: 13 hrs, 12 strategies, 1,000+ tool calls, 4,000+ lines, +185% throughput). Vendor benchmarks are strong — **SWE-Bench Pro 58.6% (ties GPT-5.5; ahead of GPT-5.4 57.7, Opus 4.6 53.4, Gemini 3.1 Pro 54.2)**, **HLE-with-tools 54.0% (leads the frontier)**, LiveCodeBench v6 89.6%, Terminal-Bench 2.0 66.7% — and pricing is disruptive (**$0.95/$4.00 per 1M, ~5× cheaper input / ~7× cheaper output than GPT-5.5; ~$0.30/run vs Opus 4.7's ~$1.10**). ⚠ Three honest counterweights: **(1) the defining long-horizon endurance claim is vendor-only and is contradicted by the one neutral harness** — on **Vals AI SWE-bench Verified (bash-tool-only) K2.6 scores 88/74/40/0**, collapsing to **40% on 1–4 hr tasks and 0% on >4 hr tasks**, and the exchange-core demo has "no complete public patch set, raw flame graphs, or full execution logs"; **(2) multimodal quality is weak despite broad coverage** — AA ranks it **#26 of 115 on Multimodal & Grounded (avg 68.1)**, its own "weakest category"; **(3) it is a specialist, not a universal frontier model** — pure single-turn reasoning trails the proprietary leaders (GPQA-Diamond 90.5 vs GPT-5.4's 92.8; AIME 2026 96.4 vs 99.2) and it is **token-hungry (~160M reasoning tokens to run the full AA Index)**. It now sits behind the newer **Kimi K3** generation for long-context work.
- **Provider / access:** **Moonshot AI (Beijing).** Free on **kimi.com** and the Kimi mobile app; API `kimi-k2.6` (**OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible**), the **Kimi Code CLI**, and open weights on **Hugging Face**. Day-0 serving on **vLLM, SGLang, KTransformers, OpenRouter, Cloudflare Workers AI, Baseten, MLX, Hermes Agent, OpenCode**; **9 paid API providers** (Fireworks, Parasail, Novita, Cloudflare, Together.ai FP4, DeepInfra FP4, SiliconFlow FP8, Clarifai, native Kimi). Subscription tiers $19/$39/$99/$199 unlock Agent Swarm + Kimi Claw cloud. Listed here as `opencode/kimi-k2.6`; **no free API id** (`noFreeId`).
- **Release / knowledge:** Released **2026-04-20** (open-weight HF drop; subscriber rollout 2026-04-13). K2 lineage: K2 (Jul 2025) → K2 Thinking (Nov 2025) → K2.5 (Jan 2026) → **K2.6 (Apr 2026)**; successor **Kimi K3** rumored at 3–4T parameters. Knowledge cutoff not published in the sources reviewed.
- **IDs:** `kimi-k2.6` (native/Kimi API) / `moonshotai/Kimi-K2.6` (Hugging Face) / `moonshotai/kimi-k2.6` (OpenRouter).
- **Context window:** **262,144 (256K) native; ~236K max output.** Mid-tier — **below the 400K–500K pack (GPT-5.2 400K, Grok 4.7 500K) and far below the 1M–2M frontier** (Gemini 3.1 Pro 2M); **no long-context extension path is published**, and the meta's own tradeoff note calls the window "comparatively short" and a reason K2.6 sits "behind the newer K3 generation for long-context work."
- **Modalities:** **Text + image + video in → text out.** ⚠ **Meta correction:** the scaffolded meta.json lists only "Text, image in," but both DeepInfra and the Moonshot HF card confirm K2.6 is **natively multimodal including video** — "a 400M-parameter **MoonViT** vision encoder enables native image **and video** input processing," and "K2.5 handled images; **K2.6 adds video** (mp4, mov, avi, webm, …, recommended up to 2K resolution)" in the same architecture with **no separate vision module**. Output is text only (no generative image/audio/video out).
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-24):** **$0.95 / $4.00 per 1M in/out** on the native Kimi API (**~5× cheaper input, ~7× cheaper output than GPT-5.5's $5/$30**); ⚠ **cached input $0.15/1M** (meta says $0.16 — corrected to $0.15 per DeepInfra and miraflow), a 75% automatic discount. **OpenRouter ~$0.80/$3.40.** Third-party blended (3:1): **Parasail $1.15 ($0.60/$2.80), DeepInfra FP4 $1.44 ($0.75/$3.50), Fireworks/Kimi/Cloudflare/Clarifai ~$1.71, SiliconFlow FP8 $2.15.** Independent per-run cost **~$0.30 (vs Opus 4.7 ~$1.10, 3.6× cheaper)**. Open weights = free self-host, but the footprint is heavy (4× H100 for INT4 at reduced context; GGUF needs 350GB+ unified memory).
- **Architecture:** **1T-total-parameter Mixture-of-Experts, 32B active per token** (inference compute ≈ a 32B model, capability ≈ 1T), with **384 experts (8 routed + 1 shared), 61 layers, Multi-head Latent Attention (MLA), a 160K-token vocabulary, native INT4 quantization**, and the **400M MoonViT** vision encoder. **Same architecture as K2.5** ("the deployment method can be directly reused") — the K2.6 delta is **post-training**: more compute on long-horizon stability, instruction following, and swarm coordination (undisclosed amount). Two inference modes — **Thinking** (temp 1.0, chain-of-thought) and **Instant** (temp 0.6, `{"thinking":{"type":"disabled"}}`); four product variants (Instant / Thinking / Agent / **Agent Swarm**). **Claw Groups** (research preview) opens the swarm to heterogeneous external agents/humans with K2.6 as coordinator.

### Raw benchmarks found

Independent (**Artificial Analysis**, **Vals AI**, third-party coding benchmark — weighted highest):

- **AA Intelligence Index: 54 — #4 overall, the highest-ranked open-weight model** (234 open of 367 evaluated); behind GPT-5.5 (60), Claude Opus 4.7 (57) and the Anthropic/Google/OpenAI tier (57). ⚠ **April-2026 basket — non-convertible** with the later re-based baskets used for other models in this log.
- **AA-Omniscience hallucination rate: 39%** (down sharply from K2.5's **65%**), placing it near Claude Opus 4.7 (36%) and MiniMax-M2.7 (34%) — a strong single-generation factuality gain driven by abstention.
- **AA Multimodal & Grounded: rank #26 of 115, avg 68.1** — its **weakest** category (vs Coding at #6). (Note: against GPT-5.5 *specifically* it averages 79.7 vs 70.4, so the weakness is relative to the whole field, not to every peer.)
- **AA token usage: ~160M reasoning tokens** to complete the full Intelligence Index — lower than Claude Sonnet 4.6 (~190M) but **much higher than GPT-5.4 (~110M)** and far above the open-weight median (~47M output tokens).
- **Vals AI SWE-bench Verified (bash-tool-only, mini-swe-agent, neutral harness): 88 / 74 / 40 / 0** by difficulty band (<15 min / 15 m–1 h / 1–4 hr / >4 hr) — strong on short tasks, **collapses on long-horizon work (40% then 0%)**, directly at odds with the vendor's 12-hour-autonomy story. (Kimi siblings on the same ruler: K3 92/95/93/67, K2.7-Code 89/75/50/33, K2.5 85/66/31/0.)
- **Independent coding benchmark (AkitaOnRails, Apr 2026): K2.6 = 87 vs Claude Opus 4.7 = 97 (Tier A vs Tier A, a 10-point gap)** at **$0.30/run vs $1.10/run (3.6× cheaper)**; "for ~80% of standard tasks K2.6 delivers 80–90% of Claude Code's quality at ~12% of the cost." **Vercel**: ">50% improvement on our Next.js benchmark" over K2.5.

Vendor-reported (**Moonshot AI** launch table — not independently reproduced; miraflow flags "no numbers … come from independent third-party replication at launch"):

- **SWE-Bench Pro: 58.6%** (ties GPT-5.5; ahead of GPT-5.4 xhigh 57.7, Opus 4.6 max 53.4, Gemini 3.1 Pro thinking-high 54.2, K2.5 50.7) — the headline claim
- **HLE-Full with tools: 54.0%** (leads GPT-5.4 52.1, Opus 4.6 53.0, Gemini 3.1 Pro 51.4) — strongest agentic-knowledge number
- **SWE-bench Verified: 80.2%**; **SWE-bench Multilingual: 76.7%**; **LiveCodeBench v6: 89.6%** (vs Opus 4.6 88.8); **Terminal-Bench 2.0: 66.7%** (Terminus-2 harness, up from K2.5's 50.8 — ⚠ harness-sensitive: other configs cite GPT-5.4 at 75.1)
- **Agentic search/browse: BrowseComp 83.2% (Agent Swarm mode 86.3%, up from K2.5 78.4); DeepSearchQA 92.5% F1 (vs GPT-5.4 78.6); Toolathlon 50.0% (up from 27.8)**
- **Pure reasoning: AIME 2026 96.4%; HMMT 2026 92.7%; GPQA-Diamond 90.5%** (all trail GPT-5.4's AIME 99.2 / GPQA 92.8)
- **Vision (with python): CharXiv 86.7; Math Vision 93.2**; frontend design **68.6% win+tie vs Gemini 3.1 Pro** (vendor claim)
- **Agent Swarm demos (vendor-run, unverified):** 300 sub-agents / 4,000 steps / 12+ hr; exchange-core 13-hr rewrite (+185% medium throughput, 0.43→1.24 MT/s); a 12-hr Zig inference-engine port (15→193 tok/s); a 5-day autonomous RL-infra ops agent. K2.5's ceiling was 100 sub-agents / 1,500 steps.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 83/100.** Genuinely differentiated **native swarm orchestration** (first-party 300-sub-agent / 4,000-step tooling no other open-weight model ships), **JSON mode + function calling on all 9 providers**, and leading vendor agentic-search numbers (**HLE-with-tools 54.0, BrowseComp 86.3, DeepSearchQA 92.5 F1, Toolathlon 50.0 doubling K2.5**). Held **below Grok 4.7 (88)** — whose agentic claims are *independently* verified (AA-Briefcase frontier, Coding Agent Index #4) — because K2.6's defining **12-hour/300-agent endurance is vendor-only** and the **one neutral harness (Vals) shows a long-horizon collapse to 40% then 0%**, which is precisely the capability being sold.
- **Reasoning: 85/100.** The strongest independent anchor: **AA Intelligence Index 54 — #4 overall and the highest-ranked open-weight model** (of 234 open / 367 total), just 3 points under the Anthropic/Google/OpenAI 57 tier, plus a **hallucination-rate drop to 39% from 65%** (near Opus 4.7's 36%). Tempered because it is explicitly **"a specialist, not a universal frontier model"** — pure single-turn reasoning **trails the proprietary leaders** (GPQA-Diamond 90.5 vs GPT-5.4 92.8; AIME 2026 96.4 vs 99.2), and GPT-5.5 leads the overall Index 60-to-54. Scored level with Grok 4.7 (85) — a different basket, but the same "elite-placement-yet-behind-the-pure-reasoning-frontier" profile.
- **Context window: 78/100.** **256K native (~236K out) with no published extension path** — respectable but **below the 400K–500K pack** (GPT-5.2 83 at 400K, Grok 4.7 83 at 500K) and far below the 1M–2M frontier; the meta's own tradeoff calls it "comparatively short" and a reason K2.6 sits behind K3 for long-context work. Scored **below Qwen3.8-27B (82)**, which pairs its 262K with a 1M YaRN extension K2.6 lacks.
- **Multimodal: 79/100.** **Broad coverage — text + image + video in via a native 400M MoonViT encoder (no bolt-on module)** — which earns it **above the image-only floor (Grok 4.7 at 77)**; vendor vision-with-python numbers are decent (CharXiv 86.7, Math Vision 93.2) and it beats GPT-5.5 head-to-head (79.7 vs 70.4). But the **independent AA rank is #26 of 115 (avg 68.1), its single weakest category**, so it sits **below the strong-vision Qwen video models** (Qwen3.8-27B 82, Qwen3.6-Plus 81). Coverage is wide; absolute quality is mid-tier.
- **Coding: 85/100.** The **leading open-weight coder**: vendor **SWE-Bench Pro 58.6 (ties GPT-5.5, ahead of GPT-5.4/Opus 4.6/Gemini 3.1 Pro)**, SWE-V 80.2, LiveCodeBench v6 89.6, Terminal-Bench 2.0 66.7; **independent AkitaOnRails Tier-A 87** (10 pts under Opus 4.7) and **Vercel's ">50% Next.js" gain**. Discounted a notch below Grok 4.7 (87) because the **neutral Vals harness shows the long-horizon band collapsing (40% at 1–4 hr, 0% at >4 hr)** and the flagship SWE-Pro tie is **vendor-only/unreproduced** — scored level with GPT-5.2 and Qwen3.8-27B (85).
- **Cost efficiency: 89/100.** Outstanding and partly independent: **$0.95/$4.00 (~5× cheaper input, ~7× cheaper output than GPT-5.5)**, third-party from **$0.60/$2.80 (Parasail)**, **$0.15 cached**, and a verified **~$0.30/run vs Opus 4.7's ~$1.10 (3.6× cheaper) at 80–90% of Claude Code quality for ~12% of the cost** — plus **open weights for data sovereignty**. Held below Qwen3.8-27B (93, genuinely single-GPU cheap) because K2.6's **1T footprint makes self-hosting expensive (4× H100 / 350GB+)** and it is **token-hungry (~160M reasoning tokens/Index run)**, so miraflow warns the "88% savings compresses to 60–70%" on reasoning-heavy work ("per-token cheap doesn't equal per-task cheap"). Scored but **not** counted in the Overall per methodology.
- **Overall Score: 82/100.** Mean of Tool 83 + Reasoning 85 + Context 78 + Multimodal 79 + Coding 85 = 410/5 = 82.0 → 82. Best fit: **cost-sensitive, long-horizon *agentic coding* and multi-agent orchestration where you need open weights, data sovereignty, or a ~3.6×-cheaper per-run bill** — autonomous web research (DeepSearchQA 92.5), swarm decomposition, frontend/coding-driven design, and short-to-mid SWE tasks (Vals 88/74). **Not** the pick for high-stakes single-turn reasoning (GPQA/AIME trail the proprietary frontier), strong multimodal quality (#26/115), >256K context, or *verified* multi-hour autonomy (Vals 0% at >4 hr; the 12-hr demos are vendor-only) — and it now sits behind Kimi K3. **Divergence note:** my 82 is **3.4 below the queue's curated 85.4**, driven by the fact that K2.6's headline differentiators (300-agent swarm, 12-hr runs, the GPT-5.5 SWE-Pro tie) are **vendor-reported and unreproduced**, while the **independent neutral evidence is mixed** — an elite AA Index #4 and superb cost-per-run, but a Vals long-horizon collapse to 0% and a weak #26/115 multimodal rank.
