## Model card

- **Name:** Kimi K2.6. **Open weight**, published on Hugging Face as **`moonshotai/Kimi-K2.6`** with its own model card and a separate technical blog. Variant/alias flag: distinct from **Kimi K2.7 Code** (a coding-tuned sibling), **Kimi K2.5** (its predecessor) and **Kimi K3** (the 2.8T flagship that succeeded it). Moonshot's own guidance positions K2.6 as the **production value default** beneath K3 — "K2.6 remains materially cheaper and already supports 262K context, vision, tools and optional non-thinking mode, so it can be the better production default for ordinary workloads."
- **Short description:** Moonshot AI's 1T-class open-weight workhorse, documented in both a Hugging Face model card and a technical blog. Its profile is sharply bimodal in the same way as several Chinese-lab models reviewed here: **τ²-bench at 95.9% and Claw-Eval at 62.3% are excellent structured tool-calling results**, while **GDPval-AA at 1,115 Elo and an AA Agentic Index of 22.1% place long-horizon autonomy near the bottom of this dataset**. The one hard structural limit that separates it from almost every peer reviewed here is its **256K context window** — a quarter of the 1M that is now standard at this tier.
- **Provider / access:** Moonshot's Kimi API (OpenAI-compatible Chat Completions at `https://api.moonshot.ai/v1`), Kimi.com and the Kimi apps, Kimi Code, **OpenRouter** (`moonshotai/kimi-k2.6`), and **downloadable weights on Hugging Face**. Reasoning: explicit reasoning mode with an **optional non-thinking mode** — a lever Kimi K3 removed (K3 runs max-effort reasoning by default), and a genuine cost advantage for routine traffic.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released ahead of Kimi K3 (2026-07-16). **No verified public release date or knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `moonshotai/Kimi-K2.6` (Hugging Face), `moonshotai/kimi-k2.6` (OpenRouter), `kimi-k2.6` on Moonshot's own API.
- **Context window:** **256K tokens (262,144)** — **this is the model's defining constraint.** Every other model in this tier of the dataset offers 1M. Max output: no verified public figure found. Long-context evidence is limited to **AA-LCR at 81.0%**; no MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks figure exists.
- **Modalities:** **Text and image input → text output.** Vision is confirmed both by Moonshot's positioning and by five published vision benchmarks. **No video, audio or PDF input** documented or benchmarked, and no non-text output.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **$0.95 / 1M cache-miss input, $0.16 / 1M cached input, $4.00 / 1M output** on Moonshot's API — roughly **a third of Kimi K3's $3 / $0.30 / $15 on input and under a third on output**, which is the whole basis of Moonshot's "better production default" recommendation. **Open weights** additionally make the rate card escapable for anyone able to self-host.
- **Architecture:** Sparse Mixture-of-Experts, **1T-class total parameters** (against Kimi K3's 2.8T). Exact total and active parameter counts, expert count and routing topology: **not disclosed in the sources reached.** Pre-training data is not public, so "open weight" is not "open source".

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 79/100.** The structured tool-calling evidence is genuinely strong and mostly independent: **τ²-bench at 95.9% from Artificial Analysis clears the Tau-family frontier reference by ~46 points**, **Claw-Eval at 62.3% from the benchmark's own leaderboard is the highest in this dataset** — and the methodology specifically penalises models that lack one — while **DeepSearchQA 92.5%, BrowseComp 83.2% and WideResearch 80.8%** show real autonomous-research capability. Everything involving long-horizon autonomy is weak: **GDPval-AA at 1,115 Elo is roughly 635 below the ~1750+ reference**, the **AA Agentic Index of 22.1% and APEX-Agents-AA of 28.5% are both near the bottom of this dataset**, **Terminal-Bench sits mid-band at 66.7% vendor / 53.6% independent**, MCP Atlas (55.9%) and Toolathlon (50%) are mid-field, ResearchClawBench reaches only 18.0%, and the vendor's OSWorld-Verified 73.1% sits against an independent OSWorld 2.0 result of 4.6%. **Tau3-Banking is unreported.**
- **Reasoning: 78/100.** **GPQA Diamond clears the 90%+ frontier reference on two of three measurements** — and notably **Artificial Analysis measured it higher (91.1%) than Moonshot claimed (90.5%)**, which strengthens rather than weakens confidence. Competition mathematics is excellent (AIME 2026 96.4%, HMMT Feb 2026 92.7%) and MMLU-Pro is independently confirmed at 87.6%. But four references are missed or weak: **HLE at 34.7% vendor / 37.5% independent is below the 40%+ reference on both**; **the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index of 27.0% is mid-band**, 33 points off the frontier marker; **CritPt at 8.0% is low** and **Epoch AI's independent FrontierMath v2 Tier 4 at 14.6%** confirms that exam strength does not reach research-level mathematics; and **AA-Omniscience accuracy of 32.6% with a 40.5% hallucination rate** indicates limited and only moderately reliable factual knowledge.
- **Context window: 76/100.** **256K tokens (262,144)** places it in the methodology's **200K–500K tier (65–84)**, and this is the single largest structural gap between K2.6 and essentially every peer in this part of the dataset — Claude Sonnet 5, Qwen3.8-Max, Gemini 3.5/3.6 Flash and Kimi K3 itself all offer 1M. It earns the upper part of that band on two grounds: **AA-LCR at 81.0%** is a respectable long-context reasoning figure, and 256K is genuinely sufficient for most single-repository and single-document workloads. It cannot go higher because **no MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks retrieval figure exists at all**, **max output tokens are undocumented**, and the ceiling is a hard architectural limit rather than a pricing tier — there is no configuration in which this model reads a million tokens.
- **Multimodal: 70/100.** Input is **text and images only** with text-only output, placing it in the methodology's "+image in = 60–70" band, and it earns the **top of that band** on unusually well-corroborated evidence: **MMMU-Pro at 79.4% from Moonshot is matched exactly at 79.4% by Artificial Analysis** — an exact independent reproduction, which is the strongest form of confirmation available — with **V\* at 96.9%** near-saturated on fine-grained visual search, MathVision at 87.4% and CharXiv at 80.4%. It cannot exceed the band because there is **no video, audio or PDF input path** and **no non-text output**, and because **no document-understanding or OCR benchmark exists** despite those being standard for vision-capable models at this tier.
- **Coding: 82/100.** Two results are strong and both are independently corroborated: **LiveCodeBench v6 at 89.6% vendor / 86.8% Vals** is excellent algorithmic coding, and **SWE-bench Verified at 80.2% vendor / 76.2% Vals** is solid repository work, with SWE Multilingual at 76.7% alongside. Four deductions: **the AA Coding Index at 61.8% is 8.2 points below the 70%+ frontier reference**; **SciCode at 52.2% vendor / 51.5% independent misses the 55%+ reference on both**; **Vibe Code Bench at 37.89% and CursorBench 3.1 at 47.6% are both below half** on real-editor and merge-acceptance work; and **DeepSWE is absent**, so the methodology's primary agentic-coding reference cannot be checked. Good at writing code, mid-tier at engineering it.
- **Cost efficiency: 88/100.** Among the best value in this dataset, and the case is structural as well as per-token. **$0.95 cache-miss input / $0.16 cached / $4.00 output is well below the $3/$15 anchor**, and critically it is **roughly a third of Kimi K3's $3/$0.30/$15** — Moonshot's own documentation recommends K2.6 as the production default precisely on this basis, reserving K3 for the hardest, longest and most visual work. Two further levers compound it: the **optional non-thinking mode** lets routine traffic skip reasoning tokens entirely — a control Kimi K3 removed by running max effort by default — and **the weights are public**, so the rate card is escapable for organisations able to self-host a 1T-class MoE. Deductions: self-hosting at that scale is still a serious infrastructure project, **no $0 API tier exists**, and the 256K ceiling means long-context work must be chunked, which adds orchestration cost that the headline rate does not capture.
- **Overall Score: 77/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (79 + 78 + 76 + 70 + 82) / 5 = 77 — a well-priced production workhorse with one hard structural limit and a clear specialisation. Choose it for **structured tool-calling and function-calling pipelines**, where **τ²-bench 95.9% and the best Claw-Eval figure in this dataset (62.3%)** are both independently sourced; for **autonomous research** (DeepSearchQA 92.5%, BrowseComp 83.2%, WideResearch 80.8%); for **algorithmic coding** (LiveCodeBench corroborated at 86.8–89.6%); for **competition mathematics** (AIME 2026 96.4%); and above all for **high-volume production traffic**, where $0.95/$4.00 with an optional non-thinking mode and public weights makes it roughly three times cheaper than its own flagship sibling for most ordinary work. Do not choose it for **anything beyond 256K tokens** — this is a hard architectural ceiling, not a pricing tier, and every peer at this standing offers 1M. Do not choose it for autonomous long-horizon work (**GDPval-AA 1,115 Elo, AA Agentic Index 22.1%**), for hard reasoning (Intelligence Index 27.0%, CritPt 8.0%, HLE below reference on both measurements), for repository-scale engineering (AA Coding Index 61.8%, Vibe Code Bench 37.89%), or for document, video or audio work, where no capability and no benchmark exists. Escalate to **Kimi K3** for the hardest, longest and most visual tasks — which is exactly what Moonshot itself advises.
