## Model card

- **Name:** Ling-3.0-flash (Hugging Face `inclusionAI/Ling-3.0-flash`, ModelScope mirror, AA id `ling-3-0-flash`, OpenRouter `inclusionai/ling-3.0-flash` with a **`:free`** variant; curated id `opencode/ling-3.0-flash`)
- **Short description:** InclusionAI's "**next-generation native hybrid reasoning model**" — the instruct/hybrid-reasoning line of the 3.0-flash pair (the thinking line is tracked as `Ling 3.0 Flash FP8`/quant variants and the reasoning-heavy successors are the **3.1** generation). Its whole argument is arithmetic: **124 B total / 5.1 B active** parameters, i.e. "≈12.4 % and ≈8.1 % of our previous 1T-class flagship Ring-2.6-1T", yet it "**matches or outperforms its predecessor across key benchmarks**" — a ~20× cut in active compute for flagship-adjacent agentic behaviour. Three headline features: a **native hybrid-linear architecture from the very start of pretraining** (5:1 Kimi Delta Attention : gated MLA, KDA fine-grained diagonal gating, **1/64 sparse MoE**), production-oriented efficiency, and "**comprehensive agentic evolution**" trained in **10,000+ interactive environments**.
- **Provider / access:** **Open weights under the MIT License** (`license: mit`, AA spec block confirms "MIT") — the most permissive class, no derivative branding terms (contrast the neighbouring `solar-open-2` Upstage Solar License). Serving stacks: **SGLang** (pre-built image `lmsysorg/sglang:dev-Ling-3.0-flash`, `--context-length 262144`, `--speculative-algorithm NEXTN`, `--reasoning-parser ling3` / `--tool-call-parser ling3`, official cookbook `docs.sglang.io/cookbook/autoregressive/InclusionAI/Ling-3.0-flash`) and **vLLM** from source (`--mamba-cache-mode align` — the linear-attention state —, `--enable-auto-tool-choice`, `--speculative-config '{"method":"mtp","num_speculative_tokens":3}'`). Hardware: **4× 141 GB-class GPUs (H20-3e / 4-GPU Blackwell)** for the low-latency recipe, **8× 80 GB (H100/H800)** otherwise — an order of magnitude cheaper to serve than the 1T siblings. Hosted: OpenRouter paid **and `:free`**. Agent frameworks named by the vendor as working well: **Claude Code, Kilo Code, Qwen Code, Hermes Agent, OpenClaw**.
- **Release / knowledge:** Artificial Analysis lists **Released August 2026**; the vendor's internal model id in Vals' leaderboard slug is `ant_ling-3.0-flash-2607` (**26-07** ⇒ July 2026 training/serving build). **No knowledge-cutoff date is published** (AA's spec table for this ID carries no cutoff row; disclosure exists only as an EU-AI-Act-style summary PDF, `inclusionAI/AI-Transparency/Ling-3.0-LLM_TDS-Summary.pdf`). **Status flag:** AA carries a **deprecation banner** — "This model is deprecated. We only continue performance benchmarking for the default 10 K input token workload. Results for other workloads are historical and no longer updated … InclusionAI has launched a newer release, **Ling 3.1 Flash**. We suggest considering it instead." The registry tracks 3.1 Flash separately at `model/ling-3.1-flash/`.
- **IDs:** `inclusionAI/Ling-3.0-flash` (HF/ModelScope), `inclusionai/ling-3.0-flash` / `…:free` (OpenRouter), `ling-3-0-flash` (AA), `ant_ling-3.0-flash-2607` (Vals), `opencode/ling-3.0-flash` (curated). **Aggregator coverage — unusually good for an open model:** BenchLM page exists (**Overall 45.18/100, #116 of 888**, 38 of 625 tracks, "partial coverage … so the overall score is conservative") with rows sourced **three ways**: vendor model card, **Artificial Analysis** and **Vals AI** (an independent third-party run). Vals AI tracks it directly. Family tree per BenchLM: siblings **Ling 3.0 Flash VL** (47.31, = `model/ling-3.0-flash-vl/`), **Ling 3.0 Flash FP8** (not computed), **Ling 3.0 Tiny** (not computed, = this queue's `ling-3.0-tiny`), **Ling 3.0 Flash Fin** (not computed; the finance-scope folder `models_finance/ling-3.0-flash-fin-free`), related earlier **Ling 2.6 Flash** (32.65), newer **Ling 3.1 Flash** (61.94).
- **Context window:** **262,144 tokens (256K)**. Triple-confirmed: AA spec block **262k**, BenchLM **262K**, and the vendor's own launch flags `--context-length 262144` with a documented **context training schedule 8 K → 32 K → 256 K** (256 K via YaRN extension in the SGLang recipe). Vendor evaluations were actually **run at the 256 K window** (SWE-bench series, Terminal-Bench 2.1 both specify "a 256K context window"), and AA's **AA-LCR 73.0 %** long-context-reasoning result is an independent measurement at length. **Output caveat applies:** the published decoding configs cap generation at **`max_new_tokens = 32K`** for SWE/Terminal-Bench work (128 K only in the in-house MiniAppBench), so per-turn output is materially smaller than the window; no formal max-output figure is published. The curated `meta.json` says "128K total" — **understated by half**.
- **Modalities:** **text in / text out.** AA: "supports text input, outputs text"; card `pipeline_tag: text-generation`; no vision/audio encoder and no MMMU-class row anywhere in the evaluation notes. Vision lives in a **different model**, `Ling-3.0-Flash-VL` (`model/ling-3.0-flash-vl/`) — do not inherit. Curated "Text in/out" is correct.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-08):** AA measures **$0.075 / $0.22 per 1M input/output** with an **80 % cache-read discount**, and calls both "**moderately priced**" because they sit *exactly at the class medians* ($0.07 / $0.22) — i.e. price-efficient for its size class but not an outlier. **No cost-per-task figure** is published (AA's Cost unit reads "**N/A … Unknown out of 4 units**", a direct consequence of deprecation freezing non-default workloads). The real economics story is elsewhere: **333.6 output tokens/s — #1 of 65 in its class, "notably fast"** vs median 130; MIT weights on a 4-GPU node; HiCache + Mooncake hierarchical caching "**eliminating redundant recomputation during long-horizon interactions and reducing TTFT by 60 % to over 80 % in long-input scenarios**" — the single most agent-relevant cost claim in the card. Offsetting all of it: **260 M output tokens for the Index run vs a 100 M class median — "very verbose"** (#10/65), so a chatty reasoning model that is cheap per token is not cheap per task.
- **Architecture:** **124 B total / 5.1 B active** (~4 % active ratio), **hybrid-linear MoE**: **35 KDA layers + 7 gated-MLA layers (5:1 alternating)**, 2 dense layers, **512 routed experts + 1 shared with 8 activated**, 32 attention heads, hidden 2560, expert intermediate 768, dense intermediate 6144, vocabulary **157,184**, trained with **MTP** (multi-token prediction) so NEXTN/MTP speculative decoding is a first-party recommendation rather than an afterthought. Thinking mode is **on by default** (chat template + `ling3` reasoning parser; disable via `enable_thinking: false`), recommended sampling `temperature=0.6, top_p=0.95, top_k=20`. Post-training built on **10,000+ interactive training environments** with end-to-end closed-loop execution across coding, general and deep-research agent tasks, consolidated as a **native hybrid reasoning** model.
- **Identity flags:** (1) **Scaffolded entry:** `meta.json` has `"scaffolded": true` with boilerplate `short` ("Ling 3.0 Flash model evaluation entry."), placeholder `"Standard pricing"` and a wrong context figure — three fields to fix before the next sync. (2) **Deprecated in favour of a tracked sibling:** AA points to **Ling 3.1 Flash** (`model/ling-3.1-flash/`, BenchLM 61.94 vs this one's 45.18 — a real generational gap); this folder should stay but be labelled as superseded. (3) **Do not confuse** with `model/ling-2.6.1t/` (1T instruct, AA Index 17 *estimated*), `model/ring-2.6.1t/` (1T reasoning), `model/ling-2.6-flash/`, `model/ling-3.0-tiny/`, `model/ling-3.0-flash-vl/` or the finance-scope `ling-3.0-flash-fin-free`. AA's page flags "A non-reasoning variant may also exist." (4) **Slug check:** `ling-3.0-flash` follows the registry convention and has no hyphen twin (rule-7 clean). (5) **Vendor-vs-AA metric hygiene:** the card's **τ³-banking 28.0 %** is explicitly "aligned with the AA leaderboard, utilizing GPT-5.4-mini (medium reasoning) for both user simulator and judge" — a vendor run *configured* to be comparable with AA, not an AA number; BenchLM lists them as separate sources and so does this report.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 70/100.** The strongest dimension and the model's stated reason for existing: **BFCL v4 73.0**, **MCP-Atlas 65.5**, **Terminal-Bench 2.1 57.0 (vendor) / 50.2 (independent Vals run)**, **τ³-banking 28.0**, **SkillsBench 44.8**, **GDPval-AA Elo 1107**, **BrowseComp 72.2 / WideResearch 73.6 / DRACO 70.4** for research agents, trained in **10,000+ interactive environments**, shipped with a dedicated `ling3` tool-call parser + `--enable-auto-tool-choice`, and named as working inside **Claude Code, Kilo Code, Qwen Code, Hermes Agent and OpenClaw**. What keeps it out of the frontier band (90+ needs TB-class 85 %+, τ³ ~50 %+ and GDPval ~1750): every agentic number is **mid-tier in absolute terms**, **AA's own Agentic Index is 21.0 %** and normalized GDPval **22.4 %**, and the vendor's 57.0 vs Vals' 50.2 gap on the same harness shows how much of the credit is protocol-dependent. Upper edge of the mid band, and the rare open model here whose agentic claims are at least *partially* independently reproduced.
- **Reasoning: 65/100.** **AA Intelligence Index 20 at #3 of 65** in its class (median 8) — "well above average" *within* the 40–150 B open-weight tier, and the top edge of the methodology's 20–35 mid band. Triple-confirmed **GPQA Diamond ~85.0 / 85.5 (AA) / 84.8 (Vals)** is a trustworthy number — the best-evidenced row in the folder — and math is genuinely strong (**AIME26 93.2, HMMT Feb 2026 87.0, IMOAnswerBench 83.7**). The deductions are equally well-evidenced: **HLE 22.7–23.7** (below the 40+ mid reference), **CritPt 1.7 %** (near-floor research physics), and **AA-Omniscience Index −17.9 with 44.1 % hallucination / 18.2 % accuracy** — more wrong answers than right ones on unseen factual questions, which caps how much a reasoning composite can be trusted off distribution.
- **Context window: 78/100.** **262,144 (256 K)** confirmed by AA, BenchLM and the vendor's own `--context-length 262144`, on an architecture *designed* for it — 5:1 KDA/MLA hybrid-linear stacking means 35 of 42 layers hold a fixed-size recurrent state, the context curriculum ran **8 K → 32 K → 256 K**, and long-context memory plus **60–80 % TTFT reduction** (HiCache + Mooncake L3) is exactly the bottleneck that agent loops hit at 200 K+. Placed above the tier's 200 K ≈ 70 anchor because retrieval **was measured independently** (**AA-LCR 73.0 %**) and vendor SWE/terminal harnesses ran at the full 256 K. Held below 85 by the **32 K `max_new_tokens`** in every published decoding recipe (an unresolved output-side ceiling the methodology penalises), no MRCR/AI-Needle row, and a curated sheet half the true value.
- **Multimodal: 15/100.** **Text-only**, twice-confirmed (AA modality block; `pipeline_tag: text-generation` with no encoder and no visual evaluation anywhere in the protocol notes). The **30–40 points of capability this family gains from vision sit in a different folder** — `model/ling-3.0-flash-vl/` (BenchLM 47.31) — so the 10–20 band here is a fact about the checkpoint, not about the family. No audio in/out, no image output, no document rendering.
- **Coding: 70/100.** Coding is the second pillar: **LiveCodeBench v5 82.8 %** with an independent **Vals 84.0 %** (a rare vendor/third-party agreement within 1.2 points), **SWE-bench Multilingual 72.4 %**, **SWE-bench Pro 56.6 %** and **SWE-bench 65.2 % (Vals)**, **Terminal-Bench 2.1 50–57 %**, plus **SciCode 41.2 / AA 42.0**. That is a competent agentic-coding profile at ~4 % active parameters — but against the band's frontier anchors (**DeepSWE 74+, TB 85+, SciCode 55+, AA Coding Index 70+**) the fit is clearly mid-tier: **AA Coding Index 50.6**, no DeepSWE/SWE-Pro-Vals/terminal-4.0 row, and 32 K output caps long patches.
- **Cost efficiency: 95/100.** The best price-performance story in this family: **$0.075 / $0.22 per 1M** sitting exactly on its class medians, an **80 % cache discount** (agents re-send context, so this is the discount that matters), **333.6 tok/s — fastest of 65 class peers**, MIT weights, a **4-GPU** deployment floor instead of a multi-node trillion-parameter job, **TTFT cut 60–80 %** by the built-in hierarchical cache, MTP/NEXTN speculative decoding supported out of the box, and a **`:free` OpenRouter endpoint** for evaluation. Two real deductions: **verbosity 260 M vs 100 M median ("very verbose")** — cheap tokens, expensive tasks, and AA publishes **no cost-per-task figure** because the ID is deprecated; and the deprecation itself means hosted pricing/routes may thin out as traffic moves to 3.1 Flash.
- **Overall Score: 60/100.** Mean of the five quality dimensions (70 + 65 + 78 + 15 + 70) / 5 = 298 / 5 = 59.6 → 60; Cost excluded per `RULES.md`. Best fit: **teams shipping agent loops that must be fast and cheap per call** — tool-heavy coding/research agents on SGLang or vLLM with HiCache, a Claude-Code-class harness, and long (≈256 K) trajectories, where **333 tok/s, $0.075/$0.22, 80 % cache pricing and 5.1 B active parameters** dominate the decision calculus. Unfit by construction for **multimodal** work (use `ling-3.0-flash-vl`), weak for **factual open-domain Q&A** (**AA-Omniscience −17.9, 44.1 % hallucination**), and a questionable choice for a *new* build given AA's explicit "**deprecated … consider Ling 3.1 Flash**" banner (BenchLM: 45.18 here vs 61.94 for 3.1 Flash). **Curation fixes before sync:** set `contextWindow` to **262,144 (256 K)**, replace "Standard pricing" with **$0.075 / $0.22 per 1M, 80 % cache read discount**, name the VL sibling in `short`, and add a "superseded by Ling 3.1 Flash" note.
