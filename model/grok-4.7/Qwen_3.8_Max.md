## Model card

- **Name:** Grok 4.7
- **Short description:** **xAI's larger-base coding-and-knowledge-work flagship**, released **2026-09-21** (40 days after Grok 4.6) at **Grok 4.6's unchanged price** ($2/$6). It is a **specialist leap in long-horizon agentic work and coding**, independently confirmed: **AA-Briefcase 1657 Elo (just behind Claude Opus 5 and Claude Fable 5.1 at the frontier)**, **GDPval-AA 1695 (+90 over 4.6)**, **AA Coding Agent Index 56 with Grok Build (#4 in native harnesses, behind only Fable 5.1, GPT-6 Astra and Opus 5)**, **AA Cyber Index v1 tied #1 (56%)**, **VulcanBench Frontier v4 #1 (93.15, ahead of Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5)** and a decisive **Harvey Legal Agent Benchmark win (19.6% vs Fable 6.7%, GPT-5.6 Sol 2.5%)**. ⚠ Three honest counterweights: **(1) it is extremely token-hungry — ~81k output tokens per task, ~2× Grok 4.6 — so at the same per-token rate a task now costs ~2 units**, eroding the value story; **(2) general reasoning is upper-mid, not frontier** — AA Intelligence Index **46 (rank 16 of 655)**, and AA says it "broadly matches Grok 4.6" outside agentic work (with an AA-LCR regression); **(3) it is image-only**, and Musk himself said "we need to fix multimodal performance" (no multimodal row in the launch table). It also **trails Fable 5.1 Max** on CursorBench (46.3 vs 51.8), Terminal-Bench 4.0 (38.0 vs 57.9), HealthBench and GDPval, so "most powerful" is not supported by xAI's own table — "highly competitive in its class" and "frontier in price-performance" are.
- **Provider / access:** **xAI.** API `grok-4.7` (xAI API, OpenRouter `x-ai/grok-4.7` at ~20% under direct), live in **Cursor, Grok Build, GitHub Copilot, Vercel AI Gateway/fx/eve**, third-party harnesses and cloud routers; trained to natively understand the **Grok Bot** harness. A **Fast variant** (Cursor/Grok Build only) runs 2× output speed at 2× price. **No free tier** (`noFreeId`). Listed here as `opencode/grok-4.7`.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released **2026-09-21** (16:17 UTC). **Knowledge cutoff: May 2026.** Grok 4.x cadence: 4.5 (Jul 16) → 4.6 (Aug 12) → **4.7 (Sep 21)**; successor **Grok 4.8** named Sep 13.
- **IDs:** `grok-4.7` (xAI API) / `x-ai/grok-4.7` (OpenRouter) / `grok-4.7-fast` (Fast variant).
- **Context window:** **500,000 (500K), unchanged from Grok 4.6.** Solidly above the 200K–400K pack, below the 1M-native frontier (Qwen3.7-Plus/MiniMax M3 1M, GPT-5.4 1.05M). Pricing **doubles above 200K prompt tokens** ($4/$12).
- **Modalities:** **Text + image in → text out.** Image-only — **no video, no audio, no generative multimodal output**, and **no vision benchmark in the launch table**; the founder explicitly flagged multimodal as needing fixes. The weakest axis of the model.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-24):** **$2.00 / $6.00 per 1M in/out ($0.50 cached) under 200K prompt tokens; $4.00 / $12.00 ($1.00 cached) at/above 200K**; **OpenRouter ~$1.60/$4.80**; **Fast variant $4.00/$12.00** (Cursor/Grok Build only). "Half the price of comparable models" — Fable 5.1 lists $10/$50, GPT-5.6 Sol $4/$20. ⚠ **Effective cost is ~2× the sticker story** because AA measured ~81k output tokens/task (vs 36–38k for Grok 4.6), so "a task that used to cost one unit now costs about two."
- **Architecture:** **"A new, larger base model"** than Grok 4.6 (no parameter count published; Musk's 2.1T figure and the SpaceX-corpus claim are **unconfirmed/absent** from the launch post). Trained with **a longer reinforcement-learning run on a harder mix of tasks weighted toward many-hour problems**, with **better self-verification** ("works longer on difficult tasks, checks its own work more carefully") and native **Grok Bot** harness understanding. Configurable reasoning effort **low → xhigh** (AA evaluated at xhigh); encrypted reasoning content on the Responses API.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 88/100.** The independent standout — **AA-Briefcase 1657 Elo places it at the frontier of long-horizon agentic knowledge work (just behind Opus 5 and Fable 5.1)**, **GDPval-AA 1695 (+90)**, **AA Coding Agent Index #4** in native harnesses, **Cyber Index tied #1**, native **Grok Bot** harness understanding and RL explicitly weighted toward "many-hour tasks" with better self-verification. Scored at the 88 tool tier (with GPT-5.4 and Sonnet 4.6) — held just below a 89+ peak because the **Cyber Index lead partly reflects other frontier models refusing ≥98% of tasks**, and the ~81k-token/task hunger makes sustained tool loops expensive.
- **Reasoning: 85/100.** **AA Intelligence Index 46 (rank 16 of 655, +2 over 4.6)** is strong in absolute ranking (top ~2.5%) and **factuality improved (AA-Omniscience 30→32, hallucination 29% from 34%)** — but AA states it **"broadly matches Grok 4.6" on the non-agentic index tasks** (with an **AA-LCR −3.7pp regression**), and the absolute index (46) sits well below the current frontier leaders (Gemini 3.8 Flash 59, Opus 5.5 58). The model's leap is **agentic-concentrated, not a pure-reasoning jump**. ⚠ Reconciliation with my Grok 4.6 report (Reasoning 87): that score reflected the **Aug-2026 AA basket** where Grok 4.6 read ~60–61 (#3); on the **rebased Sept-2026 basket** Grok 4.6 reads 44 and 4.7 reads 46 — the baskets are non-convertible, so 4.7 at 85 is *on par with* 4.6 on the same ruler, not a regression.
- **Context window: 83/100.** **500K, unchanged from Grok 4.6** — comfortably above the 200K–400K pack and paired with RL for "managing longer context" on many-hour tasks, but below the 1M-native frontier and with pricing that **doubles above 200K**. Scored level with Grok 4.6 (83).
- **Multimodal: 77/100.** **Text + image in → text out**, image-only — **no video, no audio, no generative output, and no vision benchmark published**; the founder explicitly said **"we need to fix multimodal performance."** This is the model's weakest axis and is scored at the image-only floor (level with Grok 4.6 and the other image-only peers at 77), well below video-capable models (Qwen3.7-Plus/Qwen3.6-Plus 81–82).
- **Coding: 87/100.** A genuine, independently-corroborated improvement over Grok 4.6 — **AA Coding Agent Index #4 (56, +9)** in native harnesses, **VulcanBench Frontier v4 #1 (93.15, ahead of Fable 5.1 max and Opus 5.5 high)**, **DeepSWE v1.1 73%**, and a near-doubling of Terminal-Bench 4.0 (vendor 20.3→38.0). Discounted a notch below the very top because the **independent signals are mixed** — **Snorkel's Senior SWE-Bench shows only +1.1 (40.0%, 6th)** and VulcanBench carries a judge-swap caveat — and it **trails Fable 5.1 Max on CursorBench (46.3 vs 51.8) and Terminal-Bench (38.0 vs 57.9)** on xAI's own table.
- **Cost efficiency: 80/100.** Nominally excellent — **$2/$6, half of Fable 5.1's $10/$50 and below GPT-5.6 Sol's $4/$20**, ~$1.60/$4.80 on OpenRouter — but **badly undermined by token hunger**: AA measured **~81k output tokens/task (~2× Grok 4.6's 36–38k)**, so at the same rate "a task that used to cost one unit now costs about two," and the Fast variant doubles the rate again. Scored well below Grok 4.6's 90 (same sticker, ~2× the tokens) but above Sonnet 4.6's 74 (Grok's sticker is genuinely low and the blow-up is ~2×, not Sonnet's ~3×). Scored but **not** counted in the Overall per methodology.
- **Overall Score: 84/100.** Mean of Tool 88 + Reasoning 85 + Context 83 + Multimodal 77 + Coding 87 = 420/5 = 84.0 → 84. Best fit: **long-horizon agentic knowledge work and repo-scale/legal coding agents** — multi-hour office/analytical tasks (AA-Briefcase frontier), professional deliverables (GDPval-AA 1695), defensive cybersecurity (Cyber Index tied #1), legal-agent work (Harvey #1) and heavy codebase engineering (VulcanBench #1) — where frontier agentic capability at a below-frontier sticker wins and token cost is secondary. **Not** the pick for pure/general reasoning (index 46, upper-mid), multimodal/vision work (image-only, founder-acknowledged weakness), token-frugal budgets (~2× the tokens of 4.6), or the hardest coding rows where Fable 5.1 Max still leads. Independent read: my **84 sits ~1.6 below the queue's curated 85.6** — a *modest* gap because Grok 4.7's headline agentic/coding claims are **independently verified** (AA-Briefcase/GDPval/Coding-Agent/Cyber/VulcanBench), with the discount driven by **image-only multimodality (77)**, **upper-mid general reasoning (85)** and the **token-hunger that erodes its cost story**. Internally consistent: Grok 4.7 (84) lands **level with its predecessor Grok 4.6 (84)** — the agentic/coding gains (Tool 87→88, Coding 86→87) are offset on the 5-quality-dimension mean by a reasoning read that is upper-mid on the current rebased basket, while the large **cost regression (90→80) is scored but not counted**; it sits with the strong 84-tier generalists (inkling-small, grok-4.6, qwen-3.7-plus, ember-1, mimo-v2.6-free, minimax-m3) and just below the 85-tier (gpt-5.4, claude-sonnet-4.6) — an accurate portrait of a specialist agentic/coding flagship whose balanced mean is capped by image-only multimodality rather than by any weakness in its frontier-verified tool use.
