## Model card

- **Name:** Grok 4.7. Proprietary; official model documentation at `docs.x.ai/developers/grok-4-7`. Variant/alias flag: successor to **Grok 4.6**; the Grok 4.x line also contains 4.5, 4.3, 4.20 (with a separate Multi-agent beta) and several Fast/Mini variants.
- **Short description:** xAI's Grok 4.7, released in September 2026 under the framing **"Same Price, Longer Horizons"**. A benchmark tracker records 26 published benchmarks but **assigns no composite score — it remains unranked**, which is itself a finding: there is less evidence here than for its own predecessor. What exists is strong on long-horizon work: **GDPval-AA at 1,695 Elo is the highest non-Anthropic figure in this research pass** and within 55 Elo of the methodology's ~1750+ frontier reference, **CursorBench 4.0 at 46.3% and FrontierSWE v2 at 29.5% are both the best figures recorded here** on their respective harder-version leaderboards, and **CWE-bench v1 at 68.0% ties the dataset's best at $2.75 per rollout — the cheapest of the models achieving that score.**
- **Provider / access:** xAI's own API, OpenRouter (`x-ai/grok-4.7`) and third-party hosts. Reasoning: explicit reasoning mode.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released September 2026, succeeding Grok 4.6. **No verified exact release date or knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `grok-4-7`; `x-ai/grok-4.7` (OpenRouter).
- **Context window:** **500,000 tokens** — unchanged from Grok 4.6, and the top of the methodology's 200K–500K tier. Max output: no verified figure found. **AA-LCR at 76.7%** — notably **3.6 points below Grok 4.6's 80.3%**, so long-context reasoning did not improve generationally.
- **Modalities:** **No modality specification was retrievable and no multimodal benchmark exists** beyond a Design Arena website-generation Elo. Text in and text out are certain; image input is probable on Grok-line precedent but **unverified**.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **No rate card was verified**, but independent coverage of the launch is headlined **"Grok 4.7 Release: Same Price, Longer Horizons"**, which indicates parity with Grok 4.6 — itself characterised as matching GPT-5.6 Sol at half the price, implying roughly **$2/$10**. A harder datapoint exists: on CWE-bench v1, **Grok 4.7's average rollout cost was $2.75** against Gemini 4 Argon's $6.63 for the same 68% score (Claude Opus 5.5: $0.79 at 67%). **Re-verify before budgeting.**
- **Architecture:** Proprietary, closed weights. Parameter count and topology not disclosed for 4.7; third-party coverage cited **1.5 trillion parameters** for Grok 4.6.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 86/100.** Long-horizon knowledge work is where this model earns its "Longer Horizons" framing: **GDPval-AA at 1,695 Elo is the highest non-Anthropic figure in this research pass** and within 55 Elo of the frontier reference, **AA Briefcase at 1,657** corroborates on a private harness, **AA AutomationBench reaches 65.6%**, and **CWE-bench v1 at 68.0% ties the dataset's best while costing $2.75 per rollout against Gemini 4 Argon's $6.63** — the same score for 40% of the spend. Four deductions: **AA Harvey LAB at 19.6% is a startling outlier** that xAI published itself, against MiniMax M3's 88.4%, so legal-agent work is a real weakness; **Terminal-Bench 4.0 falls from the vendor's 38.0% to Artificial Analysis's 25.8%** and Terminal-Bench 2.1 (73.4%) is below both the ~88% anchor and Grok 4.6's own 78.3%; **GDP.pdf at 20.0%** is weak; and **Tau2-bench, Tau3-Banking, Claw-Eval, MCP-Atlas, Toolathlon and OSWorld are every one of them unreported**.
- **Reasoning: 84/100.** **AA-HLE at 43.1% clears the 40%+ frontier reference**, the **Intelligence Index of 46.5% is among the stronger figures here** and improves on Grok 4.6, and the reliability profile is genuinely good — **an AA-Omniscience composite index of 32.0% with a 29.3% hallucination rate** is third-best in this research pass and a real generational improvement. Four deductions, and the first is the most consequential: **no GPQA Diamond figure exists for this model at all**, which is a regression from Grok 4.6 where two independent labs measured 94.9% and 94.7% — the methodology's primary knowledge reference is simply unmeasured here. **The Intelligence Index remains 13.5 points below the 60+ marker**; **AA-LCR at 76.7% is 3.6 points below its own predecessor**; and **MLCR-AA at 15.0% is a 61.7-point collapse** on multi-document reasoning, among the worst in this dataset.
- **Context window: 82/100.** **500,000 tokens sits at the top of the methodology's 200K–500K tier (65–84)**, unchanged from Grok 4.6. It is scored just below the band ceiling rather than at it, and the reason is a measured regression: **AA-LCR fell from 80.3% on Grok 4.6 to 76.7% here**, so long-context reasoning got slightly worse while the window stayed the same. **MLCR-AA at 15.0%** confirms multi-document work across that window largely fails. **No MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks figure exists**, and max output tokens are undocumented.
- **Multimodal: 62/100.** **No multimodal benchmark of any kind exists for this model**, and **no modality specification was retrievable**. Text in and text out are certain; image input is probable on Grok-line precedent but entirely unverified. The only adjacent datapoint, **Design Arena Website at 1,239 Elo, is down from Grok 4.6's 1,298** — a measured regression on generated-design quality. 62 sits near the floor of the methodology's "+image in" band to reflect a capability that probably exists on family lineage and is completely undemonstrated. **Verify vision against the live API before relying on it.**
- **Coding: 84/100.** Three results are the best in this research pass on their respective benchmarks, and notably all three are the *harder* versions where most models collapse: **CursorBench 4.0 at 46.3%**, **FrontierSWE v2 at 29.5%** and — just behind GPT-6 Sol — **AA-SciCode at 57.4%, which clears the methodology's 55%+ frontier reference**. **DeepSWE at 71.0% improves 5.1 points on Grok 4.6** though it remains 3 points below the 74%+ reference, and EEBench reaches 64.0%. The deduction is about evidence rather than capability: **SWE-bench Verified, SWE-bench Pro, LiveCodeBench, Vibe Code Bench and the AA Coding Index are all absent**, which is a marked regression from Grok 4.6's published record (SWE-bench 95.6%, LiveCodeBench 88.2%, AA Coding Index 76.8%). On the harder benchmarks this model leads; on the standard ones nobody has measured it.
- **Cost efficiency: 85/100.** **This score carries a caveat: no rate card was verified.** The launch framing — **"Same Price, Longer Horizons"** — indicates parity with Grok 4.6, itself characterised as matching GPT-5.6 Sol at half the price (roughly $2/$10). The hard datapoint is better than the framing: on **CWE-bench v1 it achieves 68.0% at $2.75 average rollout cost, tying Gemini 4 Argon's score at 40% of Argon's $6.63** — a direct, independently measured cost-per-result comparison in its favour. Three deductions: the **price is unconfirmed**; **no free tier** is documented; and the generational value case is genuinely mixed — at the same price it improves DeepSWE (+5.1), the Intelligence Index (+2.2), hallucination rate (−5.0) and GDPval (+52 Elo), but **regresses on AA-LCR (−3.6), Terminal-Bench 2.1 (−4.9) and Design Arena (−59 Elo), and loses the GPQA, SWE-bench, LiveCodeBench and AA Coding Index evidence entirely**.
- **Overall Score: 79.6/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (86 + 84 + 82 + 62 + 84) / 5 = 79.6 — a long-horizon specialist with a thin and uneven evidence base. The "Longer Horizons" positioning is supported where it matters: **GDPval-AA at 1,695 Elo is the best non-Anthropic figure here**, AA Briefcase 1,657 corroborates, **CursorBench 4.0 and FrontierSWE v2 are both the best in this dataset** — and crucially those are the *harder* benchmark versions where nearly every model collapses, which is a more durable signal than a high score on a saturating harness. **CWE-bench v1 at 68.0% for $2.75 a rollout** is the clearest cost-per-result win recorded in this research pass. Three cautions. **The evidence regressed from Grok 4.6**: no GPQA Diamond, no SWE-bench, no LiveCodeBench, no AA Coding Index, and a tracker that declines to assign it a composite score at all — so you know less about this model than about its predecessor. **Several measured things got worse**: AA-LCR, Terminal-Bench 2.1 and Design Arena all fell generationally. And **AA Harvey LAB at 19.6%, published by xAI itself**, makes legal-agent work a clear no. If your workload is long-horizon professional knowledge work or real-editor coding on hard tasks, this is a strong and cheap choice; if you need breadth of assurance, Grok 4.6 is better documented.
