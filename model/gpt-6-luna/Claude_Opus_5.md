## Model card

- **Name:** GPT-6 Luna. Proprietary, Reasoning. **Variant/alias flag: this is the low-cost volume tier of the GPT-6 family**, below **GPT-6 Astra** (84.9), **GPT-6.1 Sol** (81.46) and **GPT-6 Sol** (75.19). Separate **GPT-6 Luna (low)** and **GPT-6 Luna (medium)** effort configurations are tracked but uncomputed. Related earlier model: **GPT-5.6 Luna**. ARC Prize labels its verified results for this ID plainly (`openai-gpt-6-luna`), so its abstract-reasoning figures are third-party verified.
- **Short description:** OpenAI's budget tier of its current flagship generation — and on the numbers, **a strong value proposition that is essentially a lateral move from GPT-5.6 Luna rather than an upgrade.** A composite tracker places it **#32 of 882 at 65.63/100**, against GPT-5.6 Luna's 66.01. Its genuine strengths are **ARC Prize-verified ARC-AGI-1 at 86.70% and ARC-AGI-2 at 59.3%**, **AA-LCR at 83.3%**, **AA-MMMU-Pro at 79.7%** and a **secondary-transfer cache hit of $0.125 per 1M input**. Its weaknesses are sharp and specific: **Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 12.6%**, **ExploitGym at 11.6%**, **DeepSWE at 66.6% missing the 74% reference**, **HLE at 38.5% missing 40%**, and **a 76.7% AA-Omniscience hallucination rate on a 0.7% index** — reliability is the weak axis.
- **Provider / access:** OpenAI API and ChatGPT. Official documentation at `developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-luna`. OpenAI published a joint announcement, "Introducing GPT-6 Sol and Luna", which supplies one row; **most other rows come from the GPT-6 Astra system card** reporting Luna as a comparison baseline, or from Artificial Analysis.
- **Release / knowledge:** **No verified release date or knowledge cutoff found** for this specific ID. On GPT-6 family precedent this would be a 2026 release.
- **IDs:** `openai/gpt-6-luna`; effort variants `gpt-6-luna-low` and `gpt-6-luna-medium` tracked but uncomputed. **No free-tier ID.**
- **Context window:** **1,050,000 tokens** (tracker), consistent with the GPT-5.x/6 family's documented 1.05M. **AA-LCR at 83.3% is strong** — among the higher long-context reasoning figures in this research pass. On family precedent this implies a 922,000-token input cap, a 128,000-token output limit and a **272K full-request repricing threshold**, though none was separately verified for Luna.
- **Modalities:** **Text and image input → text output**, confirmed by **AA-MMMU-Pro at 79.7%**. No audio, video or PDF path; no non-text output.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **No primary OpenAI rate card for `gpt-6-luna` was reached** — the curated metadata states only "Paid-tier pricing". **Two Artificial Analysis-derived secondary figures were found and are recorded rather than treated as a rate card:** an input rate of **$1.75 per 1M** and a **secondary-transfer cache hit of $0.125 per 1M input**. If the $1.75 input figure is right, that is roughly **1.7× cheaper on input than the methodology's $3/$15 anchor**, and the cache rate is 14× below the input rate. **The output rate is unverified, so cost efficiency below is assessed on partial information.** Re-verify before budgeting.
- **Architecture:** Proprietary, closed weights. Parameter count and topology: not disclosed.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 62/100.** Mixed, and the gaps outweigh what is measured. On the credit side, **AA AutomationBench at 53.2%** is respectable, **AA Briefcase at 1,336 Elo** is mid-field, and **GDPval-AA at 1,437 Elo** places it above GPT-5.4, Grok 4.3 and Gemini 2.5 Pro. Four deductions are substantial. **The AA Agentic Index has no figure at all**, so the composite measure of agentic capability cannot be checked. **Nine agentic reference benchmarks are entirely absent** — Terminal-Bench 2.1 and 3.0, Claw-Eval, OSWorld, MCP-Atlas, τ²-bench, Toolathlon, CyberGym, BrowseComp — with the methodology explicitly penalising the Claw-Eval absence; notably **CyberGym had a figure for GPT-5.6 Luna (77.9%) but none for this model.** **AA Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 12.6% and ExploitGym at 11.6% are both low**, and **GDP.pdf at 22.8%** shows weak document-grounded agency. And **GDPval remains 313 Elo below the ~1750+ reference.**
- **Reasoning: 78/100.** The ARC results are third-party verified, which makes the strong half of this dimension trustworthy rather than merely claimed. **ARC Prize verifies ARC-AGI-1 at 86.70% and ARC-AGI-2 at 59.3%**, and **AA-LCR at 83.3% is among the highest long-context figures in this research pass**. Health capability is well-evidenced across five rows with **HealthBench Professional at 60.8%**. Five deductions. **AA-HLE at 38.5% misses the 40%+ reference by 1.5 points.** **The Intelligence Index of 38.1% is 21.9 points below the 60+ marker.** **CritPt at 19.4% is mid-band and MLCR-AA at 16.1% sits 67.2 points below AA-LCR** — the widest harness divergence in this pass, so the long-context strength does not generalise. **The AA-Omniscience Index is barely positive at +0.7% on a 76.7% hallucination rate**, which is poor calibration on a decent 43.8% accuracy. And **no GPQA, MMLU-Pro or FrontierMath figure exists at all** — GPQA's absence is conspicuous for a frontier-tier family member. The ARC-AGI-2 result also trails Gemini 3.8 Flash's 89.2% by a wide margin, so abstract reasoning is no longer leading.
- **Context window: 95/100.** **1,050,000 tokens** places it in the ≥1M tier (95–100), and **AA-LCR at 83.3%** is among the strongest long-context reasoning figures in this research pass, so the window is demonstrably usable at depth. It sits at the tier floor rather than above it for four reasons: the 100 condition requires **≥98% retrieval at 512K+** and no MRCR, RULER, CorpusQA or GraphWalks figure exists for this ID; **MLCR-AA at 16.1% is 67.2 points below AA-LCR**, the widest such divergence here, so depth performance is severely harness-dependent; the family's **922,000-token input cap** means the headline million cannot be filled; and the **272K repricing threshold** should be assumed to apply, though none of those three family parameters was separately verified for Luna.
- **Multimodal: 70/100.** **Text and image input with text-only output**, fixing it at the top of the methodology's "+image in = 60–70" band, on the strength of **AA-MMMU-Pro at 79.7%** — above GPT-5.6 Luna (78.6%) and GPT-5.4 (78.4%), and second only to Gemini 3.8 Flash (85.6%) and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (79.8%) among models assessed here. It cannot exceed the band because the input surface excludes video, audio and PDF, and because **no CharXiv, document, OCR, video or audio benchmark exists**, so chart reading and document extraction are entirely unmeasured.
- **Coding: 66/100.** Short of the references on the measures that exist, with most references missing. **DeepSWE at 66.6% misses the 74%+ frontier reference by 7.4 points** and sits below GPT-5.6 Luna, MiMo-V2.6-Flash, GLM-5.3 and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash — so **the GPT-6 tier did not improve on coding over its GPT-5.6 predecessor.** **AA-SciCode at 54.6% misses its reference by 0.4 points**, which is as close as a miss gets. Seven further deductions: **no SWE-bench of any variant** (the most-cited repository-engineering measure), no LiveCodeBench, no AA Coding Index, no Terminal-Bench 2.1, no CursorBench, no ProgramBench, no NL2Repo and no Codeforces. The dimension rests on two figures.
- **Cost efficiency: 78/100. This score is explicitly provisional because no output rate was verified.** The available evidence is favourable on input: **$1.75 per 1M input is roughly 1.7× cheaper than the $3/$15 anchor**, and **a secondary-transfer cache hit of $0.125 per 1M input is 14× below the input rate** — a steep caching discount that matters for agentic loops re-sending shared context. Being OpenAI's volume tier, the whole point is cost, and the positioning is consistent with that. Four deductions. **The output rate is unknown**, and for a reasoning model emitting long chains the output leg usually dominates spend, so the cost case cannot be closed. **There are two rate figures and no primary OpenAI rate card**, so all pricing rests on secondary sources. **The 272K repricing threshold** should be assumed on family precedent. And **the value case is weak on its own terms**: **GPT-5.6 Luna scored 66.01 against this model's 65.63 — a 0.38-point difference, essentially a tie — while GPT-5.6 Luna has a *verified* $1/$6 rate card.** Unless GPT-6 Luna's output rate decisively undercuts $6, OpenAI's newer volume tier offers no measurable capability gain over the older one at an unverified price. The score is not lower because the input rate and cache rate are genuinely aggressive.
- **Overall Score: 74.2/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (62 + 78 + 95 + 70 + 66) / 5 = 74.2 — a competent budget tier whose headline finding is that **it is a lateral move from its predecessor rather than an upgrade**: **GPT-5.6 Luna scores 66.01 on the same composite against GPT-6 Luna's 65.63**, a 0.38-point difference, and on the one coding benchmark both publish, **GPT-5.6 Luna actually scores higher (67.2% against 66.6% on DeepSWE)**. What holds up: **ARC Prize-verified ARC-AGI-1 at 86.70% and ARC-AGI-2 at 59.3%**, so abstract reasoning is genuinely strong and third-party confirmed; **AA-LCR at 83.3%** is among the best long-context reasoning figures here; **AA-MMMU-Pro at 79.7%** is near the top of the image-input band; **HealthBench Professional at 60.8%** with a full five-row health battery; and an aggressive **$1.75 input rate with $0.125 cache hits**. Choose it for **long-context analysis, image plus text work, health and life-sciences tasks, and ARC-style abstract reasoning at volume-tier prices.** Four cautions. **It is not an upgrade over GPT-5.6 Luna on measured capability**, which has a verified $1/$6 rate card — so compare pricing carefully before switching. **Agentic capability is thinly evidenced and partly weak**: no AA Agentic Index at all, nine agentic references absent including CyberGym where GPT-5.6 Luna scored 77.9%, and Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 12.6% with ExploitGym at 11.6%. **Coding is below reference** with seven references absent, and it did not improve on its predecessor. And **reliability is the weak axis**: a 76.7% hallucination rate on a +0.7% Omniscience index, with MLCR-AA 67.2 points below AA-LCR and no GPQA, MMLU-Pro or FrontierMath figure anywhere.
