# GPT-6 Luna — Independent Research Report

## Summary and evidence

GPT-6 Luna is OpenAI's most efficient GPT-6 model for focused, high-volume tasks. It supports six reasoning levels, text and image input, function calling, structured outputs, and the complete Responses tool stack. Its limits are **1,050,000 context tokens** and **128,000 output tokens**, with a May 18, 2026 knowledge cutoff.

Source: [OpenAI GPT-6 Luna documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-luna)

## Cost and limitations

Standard pricing is **$0.10 input, $0.01 cached input, and $0.50 output per million tokens**; Batch and Flex halve it. Long-context requests above 272K carry higher rates. Audio/video are unsupported, and the model prioritizes efficiency over Sol or Astra capability.

## Scores

- **Tool use: 91/100.** Full hosted tools, MCP, skills, and computer use enable broad automation.
- **Reasoning: 82/100.** Six effort levels are valuable, though Luna is the efficiency tier.
- **Context window: 97/100.** The 1.05M/128K limits are frontier-scale.
- **Multimodal: 85/100.** Image understanding is supported, without audio or video.
- **Coding: 84/100.** Well equipped for high-volume coding and agents, below Sol on difficult work.
- **Cost efficiency: 100/100.** Its pricing is exceptionally low for this context and tool breadth.
- **Overall Score: 88/100.** Half-up rounded mean: (91 + 82 + 97 + 85 + 84) / 5 = 87.8.

## Bottom line

GPT-6 Luna is a standout economical automation model, ideal for routing routine tasks away from costlier frontier tiers.
