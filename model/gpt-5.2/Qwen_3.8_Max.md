## Model card

- **Name:** GPT-5.2
- **Short description:** **OpenAI's December-2025 science-and-knowledge flagship** (released **2025-12-11**, weeks after Sam Altman's Gemini-3 "code red"), shipped as a three-model family — **Instant / Thinking / Pro** — with **five adjustable reasoning levels** (none → low → medium → high → **x-high**). At launch it was **independently the #1 model in the world**: **AA Intelligence Index v4.0 = 73 (x-high), tied with Gemini 3 Pro Preview and ahead of Claude Opus 4.5 (70)**; the **first model ever above 90% on ARC-AGI-1 (90.5% pass@2, Pro/x-high)** and **SOTA on ARC-AGI-2 (54.2% pass@2)**; **AIME 2025 99% (led)**; **AA Coding Index 62** (tied Gemini 3 Pro, ahead of Opus 4.5); and **SWE-bench Pro 55.6%** (a then-SOTA). ⚠ Two big caveats define its standing today: **(1) it is now superseded** (GPT-5.4/5.5/5.6 all followed; on the *current* rebased AA basket it reads ~42, the meta's figure — **not comparable** to the launch-era 73), and **(2) computer use is a glaring weakness — OSWorld just 37.9%** (vs Sonnet 4.6's 72.5%), so it is an API/tool-and-knowledge-work agent, **not** a GUI/desktop agent. Image-only multimodality and a 400K window (below the 1M frontier) round out the discounts.
- **Provider / access:** **OpenAI.** API IDs **`gpt-5.2`** (Thinking), **`gpt-5.2-chat-latest`** (Instant), **`gpt-5.2-pro`** (Pro); via **ChatGPT** (Plus, Pro, Go, Business, Enterprise) and the API; tool use through the **Responses API**; distillation allowed. **No free tier** (`noFreeId`). Listed here as `opencode/gpt-5.2`.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released **2025-12-11**. **Knowledge cutoff: 2025-08-31.** Superseded within the 5.x line by GPT-5.4 / GPT-5.5 / GPT-5.6 (the meta names GPT-5.5 as successor).
- **IDs:** `gpt-5.2` (Thinking) / `gpt-5.2-chat-latest` (Instant) / `gpt-5.2-pro` (Pro); OpenAI line: GPT-5.1 → **GPT-5.2** → GPT-5.4 → GPT-5.5 → GPT-5.6.
- **Context window:** **400,000 (400K) in / 128,000 (128K) out.** Below the 1M-native frontier (GPT-5.4 1.05M, Qwen3.7-Plus / MiniMax M3 1M), but large; **Pro and Thinking expose a Responses/compact endpoint** that *compresses* (summarizes) lengthy conversations rather than truncating, extending effective context for over-limit tasks.
- **Modalities:** **Text + image in → text out.** Vision was upgraded for knowledge work (reading documents, charts, screenshots into spreadsheets/presentations/code), but there is **no video, no audio, and no generative multimodal output**, and no dedicated vision-benchmark headline (MMMU/CharXiv) in the sources reviewed.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-24):** **Thinking & Instant: $1.75 / $0.175 cached / $14.00 per 1M in/cached/out**; **Pro: $21.00 / $168.00 per 1M in/out** (~40% above GPT-5.1's sticker). Variable reasoning lets callers trade cost for depth — but **x-high is token-hungry** (running the AA Intelligence Index cost **$1,294**, though less than Opus 4.5's $1,498). The landmark efficiency story: **90.5% on ARC-AGI-1 at ~$12/task vs ~$4,500/task a year earlier (~390× cheaper)**.
- **Architecture:** Undisclosed parameter count / architecture / training data (OpenAI reveals only "improvements across the board, including in pretraining"). Hybrid-reasoning family with **five reasoning levels (none/low/medium/high/x-high)**, **reasoning summaries**, and **distillation** permitted.

### Raw benchmarks found

- **AA Intelligence Index (v4.0, weighted avg of 10 benchmarks): 73 at x-high — #1 at launch**, tying Gemini 3 Pro Preview (73), beating Claude Opus 4.5 (70) and GPT-5.1 high (70); cost-to-run $1,294 (< Opus 4.5 $1,498). ⚠ **Rebased on later baskets: 51 (Feb 2026) and ~42 (Oct 2026, the meta's figure) — these are NOT comparable across index versions.**
- **AA Coding Index (avg LiveCodeBench / SciCode / Terminal-Bench Hard): 62** — tied Gemini 3 Pro Preview high (62), ahead of Claude Opus 4.5 (60)
- **ARC-AGI-1: 90.5% pass@2 (Pro, x-high) — SOTA, first model ever above 90%** (ahead of Gemini 3 Deep Think Preview 87.5%, Claude Opus 4.5 80%)
- **ARC-AGI-2: 54.2% pass@2 (Pro, high) — SOTA**, > GPT-5.2 Thinking x-high (52.9%); ~3× the accuracy at lower cost than GPT-5.1 Thinking high (17.6%)
- **AIME 2025: 99% at x-high — led** (ahead of GPT-5.1 Codex and Gemini 3 Pro high, both 96%); some sources cite 100%
- **MCP Atlas (Scale AI, tool use): 67.6%** — mid-tier (cf. Inkling-Small 79.6)
- **GDPval-AA (agentic real-world work): 1462 Elo** (below Sonnet 4.6's 1633)

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 81/100.** A capable **API/tool-calling and knowledge-work agent** — tool use via the Responses API, **MCP Atlas 67.6%** (Scale AI), **GDPval-AA 1462 Elo** and **Agentic Financial Analysis 59.0%**, with the context-compaction endpoint for long agent sessions. But it is held well below the 87–88 computer-use leaders (GPT-5.4 88, Sonnet 4.6 88) by a **glaring OSWorld weakness (37.9%** — roughly a mid-2025 Sonnet-4 level, vs 72–75% for the frontier): GPT-5.2 automates APIs, spreadsheets and code, **not desktops/GUIs**. MCP Atlas (67.6) is also mid-tier vs Inkling-Small's 79.6.
- **Reasoning: 86/100.** Genuinely elite and **independently verified as #1 at launch** — **AA Intelligence Index v4.0 73 (top spot)**, **ARC-AGI-1 90.5% (first model ever >90%)**, **ARC-AGI-2 54.2% (SOTA)**, **AIME 2025 99% (led)** and **GPQA Diamond 93.2%**. Kept at 86 (not higher, and never above its successor GPT-5.4's 86) because it is now **two generations superseded** — on the current rebased basket it reads ~42, and GPT-5.4/5.5/5.6, Gemini 3.8 and Opus 5.5 have all since surpassed its launch peak.
- **Context window: 83/100.** **400K in / 128K out** with a **Responses/compact summarization endpoint** that compresses rather than truncates over-limit conversations. Solid and generous on output, but discounted below the 1M-native frontier (GPT-5.4 1.05M at 85, Qwen3.7-Plus / MiniMax M3 1M) — 400K is now mid-pack for a flagship.
- **Multimodal: 78/100.** **Text + image in → text out**, with vision upgraded for knowledge work (documents/charts/screenshots → spreadsheets, decks, code). Scored just above the image-only peers (grok-4.6, claude-sonnet-4.6 at 77) because visual grounding is integrated into its GDPval/office-work strength — but capped at 78 because there is **no video, no audio, no generative multimodal output, and no published dedicated vision benchmark** to verify general visual reasoning.
- **Coding: 85/100.** Elite for a **general** (non-Codex-specialist) flagship at launch — **SWE-bench Pro 55.6% (then-SOTA)**, **SWE-bench Verified 80.0%** (≈ Opus 4.6 80.8%, > Sonnet 4.6 79.6%), **AA Coding Index 62** (tied Gemini 3 Pro, ahead of Opus 4.5), **Terminal-Bench 2.0 62.2%**, **Cybersecurity CTF 67.7%**, **SWE-Lancer IC Diamond 74.6%**. Held one notch below GPT-5.4's 86 because GPT-5.4 is the successor that **absorbed the GPT-5.3-Codex line and added native computer use**, while GPT-5.2's **OSWorld 37.9%** and superseded status cap its agentic-coding ceiling.
- **Cost efficiency: 70/100.** Better sticker than its successor — **$1.75/$14 (Pro $21/$168), $0.175 cached**, ~40% above GPT-5.1 — with **five reasoning levels** to trade cost for depth (Instant is cheap for simple tasks) and a **landmark ~390× per-task efficiency gain** on ARC-AGI-1 (~$12/task vs ~$4,500 a year prior). Dragged down because **x-high is token-hungry** ($1,294 to run the AA Index), **Pro is very expensive** ($21/$168), and there is **no free tier**. Scored but **not** counted in the Overall per methodology.
- **Overall Score: 83/100.** Mean of Tool 81 + Reasoning 86 + Context 83 + Multimodal 78 + Coding 85 = 413/5 = 82.6 → 83. Best fit: **science, deep/novel reasoning, competitive math and knowledge work** (spreadsheets, documents, presentations, code) via API/ChatGPT, and **tool-calling agents that don't need a GUI** — where a former-#1 reasoner at a mid-premium sticker still delivers. **Not** the pick for computer-use/GUI automation (OSWorld 37.9%), maximum context (400K < 1M), audio/video, or anyone who needs the current frontier (it's two generations superseded — use GPT-5.4/5.5/5.6). Independent read: my **83 sits ~2.8 below the queue's curated 85.8** — a *moderately large* gap because the curated value reflects GPT-5.2's **launch-era #1 standing** (AA v4.0 73), whereas my normalization charges it for **(a) superseded status** (current rebased AA ~42), **(b) a genuine computer-use weakness** (OSWorld 37.9% → Tool 81), **(c) image-only multimodality** (78) and **(d) a 400K window below the 1M frontier** (83) — its elite reasoning/coding are fully credited, but the 5-dimension mean is pulled down by the non-reasoning axes. Internally consistent: GPT-5.2 (83) lands **just below its own successor GPT-5.4 (85)** and below claude-sonnet-4.6 (85) and the 84-tier generalists, **level with Qwen 3.8 27B (83)** and just above step-5-preview (82) — an accurate portrait of a landmark former-#1 flagship whose weaknesses (computer use, image-only, 400K, superseded) now outweigh its still-elite reasoning on a balanced 5-dimension mean.
