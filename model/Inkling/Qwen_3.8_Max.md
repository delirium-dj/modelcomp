## Model card

- **Name:** Inkling
- **Short description:** **Thinking Machines Lab's first production foundation model** (the lab's seventeenth-month debut), released **2026-07-15** — an **open-weights (Apache 2.0) 975B-total / 41B-active Mixture-of-Experts transformer**, pretrained on **45T tokens of text, images, audio and video**, with a **1M-token context on the weights** and native **text + image + audio** input. It is explicitly positioned not as a frontier model but as **the leading U.S. open-weights *fine-tuning base*** — the vendor states plainly, **"Inkling is not the strongest overall model available today, open or closed."** Its genuine, independently-confirmed distinctions are: **AA Intelligence Index 41 (the leading U.S. open-weights release, +3 over Nemotron 3 Ultra's 38)**; **best-in-class token efficiency (25K output tokens per Index task vs Kimi K2.6's 38K, DeepSeek V4 Pro's 37K, GLM-5.2's 43K)**; **native audio multimodality that no other U.S. open-weights model matches (VoiceBench 91.4%, Audio MC 56.6% — the strongest open-weights audio model)**; strong agentic-general marks (**GDPval-AA v2 1238 Elo and MCP Atlas 76.0%, both beating Kimi K2.6**) and **Design Arena Agentic Web Dev 1257 Elo (#7, ahead of Kimi K2.6 and Claude Sonnet 4.6)**; plus excellent calibration/forecasting. ⚠ Three honest counterweights: **(1) reasoning and factuality are weak for its score band** — **GPQA Diamond 87.2%, HLE-text 29.7%, SimpleQA Verified 43.9% and AA-Omniscience 2.1 with a 63% hallucination rate**, all below the Chinese open-weights leaders (Kimi K2.6, GLM 5.2) and far below the closed frontier; **(2) core coding trails** — SWE-bench Verified 77.6%, SWE-bench Pro 54.3% and Terminal-Bench 2.1 63.8% sit **behind Kimi K2.6 (80.2/58.6/71.3), GLM 5.2 and DeepSeek V4 Pro**; **(3) the served context trails the claimed window** (1M on weights, but **256K on the Tinker API**, 64K–1M across hosts) and hosted pricing (~$1.87/$4.68) partly blurs the openness advantage. A lighter sibling, **Inkling-Small (276B/12B)**, launched two weeks later and matches it on many benchmarks (see report 47).
- **Provider / access:** **Thinking Machines Lab.** Open weights on **Hugging Face** (`thinkingmachines/inkling`, original + an **NVFP4** checkpoint for NVIDIA Blackwell), fine-tunable and served via the **Tinker** platform API (64K/256K context) with an **Inkling Playground**. Hosted APIs on **Together AI, Fireworks, Modal, Databricks, Baseten**; inference support in **SGLang, Miles, vLLM, TokenSpeed, llama.cpp (Unsloth), HF transformers**. Runs inside the **OpenCode** harness. Meta also lists **$0 routes (OpenRouter `inkling:free`, NVIDIA build, Apache-2.0 self-host)** though `noFreeId` is set. Listed here as `opencode/Inkling`.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released **2026-07-15** (first production model; research previews preceded it). **Inkling-Small** previewed alongside, launched ~2 weeks later. Knowledge cutoff not published in the sources reviewed; epistemics/forecasting results were measured **June 30 – July 13, 2026** on a pre-release checkpoint.
- **IDs:** `Inkling` / `thinkingmachines/inkling` (Hugging Face) / `inkling` (Tinker) / `inkling:free` (OpenRouter, per meta).
- **Context window:** **1,000,000 (1M) tokens on the open weights; 256K on the Tinker API (64K and 256K options); hosts serve 64K–1M.** Frontier-class on the weights, but ⚠ **the served default (256K) trails the claimed 1M** — the meta's own tradeoff. The architecture is explicitly long-context-optimized (**relative positional embedding, which "extrapolates better to longer sequences than … RoPE," plus 5:1 sliding-window/global attention**).
- **Modalities:** **Text + image + audio in → text out** (confirmed by both Artificial Analysis and the vendor; meta correct). **Encoder-free** multimodality: audio as **dMel spectrograms**, images as **40×40-pixel patches via a four-layer hMLP**, both projected through a lightweight embedding into a **shared hidden space processed jointly with text** by the decoder. **No video input** (video was in the pretraining corpus but is not an accepted input modality) and **text-only output**. Audio is the standout: Inkling "transcribes speech, follows spoken instructions, answers questions about recordings, and reasons over longer-form audio," and can call a **Python tool** to zoom/crop images during visual reasoning.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-24):** ⚠ **Meta correction.** Artificial Analysis (the authoritative independent pricer, and the source linked in meta's own `pricingLinks`) reports **$1.87 input / $0.374 cached / $4.68 output per 1M at 64K context, and $3.74 / $0.748 / $9.36 at 256K** — the scaffolded meta's **"~$1.00/$4.05"** is **not corroborated** (its input figure is roughly half AA's list; the vendor was running a **50%-off limited-time Tinker discount** at launch, which may explain part of the gap). **Open weights are free to self-host (Apache 2.0)**, and meta lists **$0 routes** (OpenRouter `inkling:free`, NVIDIA build). Meta's "186 tok/s" throughput was not independently confirmed in the sources reviewed.
- **Architecture:** **Mixture-of-Experts transformer, 975B total / 41B active**, MoE design "largely follows DeepSeek-V3": **256 routed experts + 2 shared experts, 6 routed active per token**, a **sigmoid router with auxiliary-loss-free load-balancing bias** (routed + shared scores normalized jointly). Attention **interleaves sliding-window and global layers at 5:1 with 8 KV heads**, uses **relative positional embeddings (not RoPE)**, and applies **short convolutions** after the key/value projections and on the attention/MLP residual-branch outputs. Pretrained on **45T tokens** (text/image/audio/video) with a **hybrid Muon (large matrices) + Adam optimizer**, weight decay coupled to the square of the learning rate, on **NVIDIA GB300 NVL72** systems. Post-trained via a small SFT bootstrap on synthetic data (partly from **Kimi K2.5**) then **large-scale asynchronous RL scaled to 30M+ rollouts** (reasoning reward improved log-linearly; the chain-of-thought *emergently compressed* to a telegraphic style under efficiency pressure). **Controllable thinking effort (0.2 → 0.99)** settable from within the harness; tool set/schema randomized during training for harness-agnosticism.

### Raw benchmarks found

Independent (**Artificial Analysis**, **Vals AI**, **Design Arena** — weighted highest; AA Index on the **July-2026 basket**):

- **AA Intelligence Index: 41 — the leading U.S. open-weights release** (+3 over Nemotron 3 Ultra's 38; beats Gemma 4 31B's 29 and gpt-oss-120b's 24), but **below Kimi K2.6 (54)** and the closed frontier. ⚠ **Basket-specific and non-convertible** with the Sept/Oct-2026 indices used elsewhere in this log.
- **AA token efficiency: 25K output tokens per Intelligence Index task** — the **most efficient** of the open-weights leaders (vs GLM-5.2 max 43K, Kimi K2.6 38K, DeepSeek V4 Pro max 37K); the vendor adds it **matches Nemotron 3 Ultra on Terminal-Bench 2.1 at ~1/3 the tokens**.
- **AA GDPval-AA v2: 1238 Elo** — **beats Kimi K2.6 (1190) and DeepSeek v4 Flash max (1189)**, though well below GLM 5.2 (1514) and the closed frontier (Fable 5 1760, GPT-5.6 Sol 1748).
- **AA τ³-Banking: 24%** (vendor 23.7%) — above Kimi K2.6 (21%) and DeepSeek v4 Flash max (23%).
- **AA-Omniscience: +2.1** (Accuracy **40%**, **hallucination rate 63%**) — above other U.S. open-weights (Nemotron 3 Ultra −1) but **below the leading open-weights models** (Kimi K2.6 6.0, GLM 5.2 4.0) and far below the frontier (Gemini 3.1 Pro 33, Fable 5 40). The **63% hallucination rate is a real weakness**.
- **Vals AI SWE-bench Verified (bash-tool-only, neutral harness): 86 / 75 / 57 / 33** by difficulty band (<15 min / 15 m–1 h / 1–4 hr / >4 hr) — mid-pack on short tasks but **notably stronger long-horizon than Kimi K2.6 (57 vs 40 at 1–4 hr; 33 vs 0 at >4 hr)**. (Sibling Inkling-Small: 94/80/43/33 — better short, worse long.)
- **Design Arena Agentic Web Dev (blinded human eval): 1257 Elo — #7 overall**, ahead of Kimi K2.6 (1249), Claude Sonnet 4.6 (1237), Kimi K2.7 Code (1234) and Grok 4.3 (1185); behind Claude Sonnet 5 (1333), Fable 5 (1329), Opus 4.8 (1285), GLM 5.2 (1275), Grok 4.5 (1271), GPT-5.6 Sol (1260).

Vendor-reported (**Thinking Machines Lab**; the vendor deliberately uses **AA-reported numbers** for HLE, GPQA, GDPval, τ³-Banking, AA-Omniscience and MMMU Pro, and flags harness caveats — effort=0.99, temp 1.0, 256K trajectory limit):

- **Reasoning:** AIME 2026 **97.1%** (competitive with frontier 98–99.9); **GPQA Diamond 87.2%** (low — trails Kimi K2.6 91.1, Gemini 3.1 Pro / GPT-5.6 Sol 94.1); **HLE text-only 29.7%** (low), **HLE with tools 46.0%** (trails Kimi K2.6 54.0, GLM 5.2 54.7, Fable 5 64.5)
- **Agentic coding:** **SWE-bench Verified 77.6%** (bash-only harness; trails Kimi K2.6 80.2, DeepSeek V4 Pro 80.6, GPT-5.6 Sol 82.2, Fable 5 95.0); **SWE-bench Pro Public 54.3%** (trails Kimi K2.6 58.6, DeepSeek V4 Pro 55.4, GLM 5.2 62.1, GPT-5.6 Sol 64.6); **Terminal-Bench 2.1 (best harness) 63.8%** (internal harness, web-search-contaminated rollouts scored 0; trails Kimi K2.6 71.3, GLM 5.2 82.7, GPT-5.6 Sol 89.5)
- **Agentic general:** **MCP Atlas 76.0%** (strong — beats Kimi K2.6 68.1, Nemotron 44.7; just below GLM 5.2 77.8, Gemini 3.1 Pro 78.2); **Toolathlon Verified 45.5%** (low — trails Kimi K2.6 58.0, GLM 5.2 59.9); **BrowseComp w/ ctx mgmt 77.1%** (low — trails Kimi K2.6 83.2, frontier 88–91)
- **Factuality/chat:** **SimpleQA Verified 43.9%** (low — trails DeepSeek V4 Pro 57.0, frontier 68–77); **IFBench 79.8%** (strong — 2nd only to Nemotron 81.4, beats Kimi K2.6 76.0, Gemini 3.1 Pro 77.1); **Global-MMLU-Lite 88.7%** (solid)
- **Vision:** **MMMU Pro (Std 10) 73.5%** (mid — trails Kimi K2.6 79.0, Gemini 3.1 Pro 82.0, Fable 5 84.2); **CharXiv RQ 78.1%**, **CharXiv RQ w/ python 82.0%** (mid)
- **Audio (the differentiator):** **VoiceBench 91.4%** (best open-weights; near Gemini 3.1 Pro's 94.3), **MMAU 77.2%**, **Audio MC 56.6%** (dominant among open — next is Qwen3-Omni 24.3, Nemotron-3-Nano-Omni 23.2)
- **Epistemics/forecasting (June 30–July 13 checkpoint):** **ForecastBench Brier Index 61.1 no-search / 63.7 with-search** (tied-top with Gemini 3.1 Pro, ahead of GPT-5.5 and Opus 4.8); **Prophet Arena Brier 0.1617**; trained for calibration + abstention; **Cognition's Propaganda & Censorship Eval found "strong patterns of censorship non-compliance"**
- **Safety:** **FORTRESS Adversarial 78.0%** (the strongest open-weights model compared, above Nemotron 3 Ultra 77.6 and Kimi K2.6 65.6); **FORTRESS Benign 95.9%**; **StrongREJECT 98.6%**

The scaffolded meta.json was verified against AA + the vendor rather than inherited: its **975B/41B MoE, Apache 2.0, 1M weights (hosts 64K–1M), text+image+audio, and 2026-07-15 release** are all **confirmed**, and its architecture was **enriched** (256+2 experts / 6 active, DeepSeek-V3-style router, relative-PE-not-RoPE, 5:1 sliding/global attention, encoder-free dMel-audio + 40×40-patch vision, 45T tokens, Muon+Adam, GB300 NVL72, 30M+ RL rollouts, effort 0.2–0.99). ### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 84/100.** A strong, **harness-flexible, token-efficient agent** with several *independent* agentic anchors that **beat Kimi K2.6**: **GDPval-AA v2 1238 Elo, MCP Atlas 76.0% (near-frontier, just below GLM 5.2/Gemini 3.1 Pro), τ³-Banking 24%** and **Design Arena Agentic Web Dev 1257 (#7, ahead of Kimi K2.6 and Claude Sonnet 4.6)** — plus randomized tool-schema training, controllable effort settable from the harness, and native OpenCode/Tinker tool loops (it famously **fine-tuned itself** end-to-end). Held at 84 (level with Qwen3.8-27B, below Grok 4.7's independently-verified 88) because the **harder agentic benchmarks are weak — Toolathlon Verified 45.5% and BrowseComp 77.1% trail Kimi K2.6, GLM 5.2 and the frontier**, and GDPval sits far below GLM 5.2's 1514.
- **Reasoning: 79/100.** The **leading U.S. open-weights reasoner (AA Intelligence Index 41, +3 over Nemotron 3 Ultra)** with competitive **AIME 2026 97.1%**, strong **IFBench 79.8%**, solid **Global-MMLU-Lite 88.7%** and **tied-top forecasting/calibration (ForecastBench 61.1)**. But absolute reasoning and factuality are **genuinely weak for this score band** — **GPQA Diamond 87.2%, HLE-text 29.7%, HLE-tools 46.0%, SimpleQA Verified 43.9%, AA-Omniscience 2.1 and a 63% hallucination rate** all sit **below Kimi K2.6 (Index 54, GPQA 91.1, HLE-tools 54.0, Omniscience 6.0)** and far below the closed frontier. Scored well below Kimi K2.6 (85) and Grok 4.7 (85): it is a **broad, balanced generalist and fine-tuning base, not a frontier reasoner** — the vendor says so explicitly.
- **Context window: 85/100.** **1M tokens on the open weights** is frontier-class (a club of only a handful: MiniMax M3, Qwen3.6-Plus, GPT-5.4, Gemini 2M), and the architecture is **purpose-built for long-context extrapolation (relative positional embeddings that "extrapolate better … than RoPE," 5:1 sliding-window/global attention)**. Discounted a notch below the 1M-*served* models (Qwen3.6-Plus 86) because **the practical served default is 256K on Tinker (64K–1M across hosts)** — the meta's own tradeoff: "the served context trails the claimed window."
- **Multimodal: 82/100.** **Native text + image + audio via an encoder-free design** (dMel spectrograms + 40×40-patch hMLP, jointly processed with text) — and the **audio capability is the best of any open-weights model** (**VoiceBench 91.4%, MMAU 77.2%, Audio MC 56.6%** — Audio MC is more than double the next open model). This earns it to the Qwen3.8-27B tier (82) on modality breadth and audio strength, but **vision quality is mid (MMMU Pro 73.5%, CharXiv RQ 78.1%, both below Kimi K2.6 and the frontier)** and there is **no video input**, so it does not reach the strong-vision leaders. A real differentiator for **multimodal/audio agents**, not for vision-heavy work.
- **Coding: 82/100.** The **leading U.S. open-weights coder**, strong on **frontend/web-dev (Design Arena 1257, #7)** and **token-efficient** (matches Nemotron on Terminal-Bench at 1/3 the tokens), with a **better neutral long-horizon profile than Kimi K2.6 (Vals 57/33 at 1–4 hr/>4 hr vs Kimi's 40/0)**. But the **core SWE benchmarks trail the open-weights leaders** — **SWE-bench Verified 77.6%, SWE-bench Pro 54.3%, Terminal-Bench 2.1 63.8%, all behind Kimi K2.6 (80.2/58.6/71.3), GLM 5.2 and DeepSeek V4 Pro** — and far behind the closed frontier (Fable 5's SWE-V 95.0, SWE-Pro 80.0). Scored below Kimi K2.6/GPT-5.2/Qwen3.8-27B (85): a capable, efficient base for coding fine-tunes, not a top-tier coder out of the box.
- **Cost efficiency: 92/100.** Excellent and multi-layered: **Apache-2.0 open weights (free self-host, incl. an NVFP4 Blackwell checkpoint)**, **$0 routes (OpenRouter `inkling:free`, NVIDIA build)**, hosted **$1.87/$4.68 @64K** with a **50%-off launch discount**, **best-in-class token efficiency (25K output tokens/task — the lowest of the open-weights leaders)**, controllable thinking effort, and fast serving. Held just below Qwen3.8-27B (93, genuinely single-GPU cheap) because Inkling's **975B footprint is a heavy self-host** and hosted pricing "blurs the openness advantage" (meta); scored above Kimi K2.6 (89) because Inkling is **far more token-efficient (25K vs 38K/task)** and has true $0 routes. Scored but **not** counted in the Overall per methodology.
- **Overall Score: 82/100.** Mean of Tool 84 + Reasoning 79 + Context 85 + Multimodal 82 + Coding 82 = 412/5 = 82.4 → 82. Best fit: **an open-weights, Apache-2.0 *fine-tuning base* for multimodal (especially audio) and agentic/tool-using systems where token efficiency, calibration, self-hosting/data sovereignty, and customization on Tinker matter more than raw frontier capability** — voice/audio agents, document+audio analysis, web-app/frontend generation (Design Arena #7), forecasting, and privately-hosted specialized coding tools. **Not** the pick for frontier single-turn reasoning or factuality (63% hallucination, GPQA/HLE/SimpleQA all low), top-tier out-of-the-box SWE coding (trails Kimi K2.6/GLM 5.2), vision-heavy multimodal work, or >256K *served* context. **Divergence note:** my 82 is **3.3 below the queue's curated 85.3**, driven by the honest read that Inkling's **independent reasoning/factuality (AA Index 41, SimpleQA 43.9%, Omniscience 2.1, 63% hallucination) and core coding (SWE-V 77.6, SWE-Pro 54.3, Terminal-Bench 63.8) trail both the Chinese open-weights leaders and the closed frontier** — the vendor itself disclaims frontier status ("not the strongest overall model … open or closed"). Its real, independently-verified strengths (leading U.S. open-weights index, best token efficiency, best open audio, strong MCP/GDPval/Web-Dev, Apache-2.0 + $0 routes + 1M weights) are exactly what make it an excellent *base*, which is what it was built to be.
