## Model card

- **Name:** Inkling, by **Thinking Machines Lab**. **Open weight under Apache 2.0** — tied with DeepSeek V4.1 Flash's MIT as the most permissive licence in this research pass, with no revenue thresholds, no Model-as-a-Service carve-outs and no security-review clause. A benchmark tracker classifies its reasoning type as **"Hybrid"** — neither pure reasoning nor pure non-reasoning, which is unique in this dataset. Variant/alias flag: a sibling **Inkling-Small** exists and, notably, **scores marginally higher on the same composite tracker (55.12 against Inkling's 54.8)**.
- **Short description:** Released **July 2026**. A **975-billion-parameter / 41-billion-active Mixture-of-Experts transformer with native text, image and audio reasoning** — the audio path being rare in this dataset. Independent positioning describes it as "optimized for high-reasoning tasks, specifically strategic analysis, agentic planning, and structured output", with "perfect internal scores in faithfulness and tabular data processing". The published record is sharply split: **AIME 2026 at 97.1% is the highest competition-mathematics figure in this entire research pass**, and **SWE-bench Verified at 77.6% is reproduced to the decimal by Vals AI** — while **AA AutomationBench at 5.0%, AA Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 1.0%, FrontierSWE v2 at 4.1% and an AA Briefcase of 834 Elo are all among the lowest figures recorded here.**
- **Provider / access:** Thinking Machines Lab's own API, OpenRouter (`thinkingmachines/inkling`), and **7 tracked providers** per an independent pricing aggregator, plus downloadable Apache 2.0 weights.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released **July 2026**. **Knowledge cutoff: no verified public figure found.**
- **IDs:** `thinkingmachines/inkling` (OpenRouter and aggregators). Sibling: Inkling-Small.
- **Context window:** **Contested across sources, and the spread is large.** A benchmark tracker, a pricing aggregator and two model indexes all report **1M tokens** (one as 1,049K); two others report **524K**; one reports **256K**. **All are recorded.** Max output: no verified figure found. Long-context evidence is split: **AA-LCR 77.3%** against **MLCR-AA 12.2%**.
- **Modalities:** **Native text, image and audio input → text output**, stated by two independent sources. **Audio input is rare in this dataset** — only the Gemini Flash line, Muse Spark and the MiMo/Qwen omni models document it. No video input and no non-text output.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **$0.95 / 1M input, $0.16 / 1M cached input, $4.05 / 1M output** — corroborated across three independent trackers (two of which round input to ~$1.00), with **7 tracked providers**. Well below the $3/$15 anchor, and **Apache 2.0 weights make the rate escapable entirely**.
- **Architecture:** **Mixture-of-Experts transformer — 975 billion total parameters, 41 billion active**, per two independent sources. The 41B active count matters: it puts self-hosting within reach of a single well-equipped node, unlike the 95B–104B-active open-weight alternatives. Expert count, routing topology and training corpus: not disclosed. A separate model index records it at **#46 of 172 with an Index score of 63.1 (interval 56.5–69.7), evidence labelled "measured"**, as of 2026-09-10.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 68/100.** Two figures are creditable — **MCP Atlas at 74.1%** is a genuine tool-use benchmark at mid-to-upper field, and **BrowseComp at 77.1%** shows real autonomous-research capability, with a Design Arena Agentic Web Dev Elo of 1,257 alongside. Everything else in this dimension is at or near the floor, and the pattern is unambiguous: **AA AutomationBench at 5.0% and AA Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 1.0% are both the lowest figures recorded in this entire research pass**; **AA Briefcase at 834 Elo is also the lowest here**; **GDPval-AA at 1,064 Elo is ~690 below the frontier reference**; and the AA Agentic Index (24.3%), AA-AnalystAgent (23.8%), Tau3 Banking (29.1%), CWE-bench v1 (37.0%) and GDP.pdf (12.8%) all corroborate. **Terminal-Bench 2.1 spans 16.2 points across three harnesses** with the vendor highest, and **Claw-Eval, Tau2-bench, Toolathlon and OSWorld are unreported.** This model can call a tool; it cannot finish an autonomous job.
- **Reasoning: 72/100.** One result is the best in this dataset — **AIME 2026 at 97.1%** — and **IFBench at 79.8%** plus an independently confirmed **MMLU-Pro of 86.3%** are solid. But the harder references are missed with unusual consistency, which makes the shortfall credible rather than artefactual: **GPQA Diamond lands at 87.9% / 87.2% / 87.1% across vendor and two independent labs — agreeing within 0.8 points and none clearing the 90%+ reference**; **HLE without tools is 30% (vendor) and 31.9% (independent), both ~8–10 points below the 40%+ reference**, with only the tool-augmented 46% clearing it. Add **an Intelligence Index of 25.0% in the mid band**, **CritPt at 5.4%**, **MLCR-AA at 12.2% against AA-LCR's 77.3%** — a 65.1-point collapse — and a **67.7% hallucination rate** giving a near-zero Omniscience composite. Excellent at closed-form mathematics, mid-tier at open-ended reasoning.
- **Context window: 90/100.** This score sits **below the ≥1M tier floor of 95 for two reasons, both evidential.** First, **the specification itself is contested**: four sources report ~1M (one as 1,049K), two report 524K and one reports 256K — a four-fold spread that no primary source resolves, so the nominal capacity cannot be stated with confidence. Second, the usability evidence is split: **AA-LCR at 77.3%** is respectable, but **MLCR-AA at 12.2%** is among the worst multi-document long-context figures in this dataset, and **no MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks measurement exists** to settle it. 90 credits a window that is probably ~1M and demonstrably usable for single-document work, while recording that both the number and its multi-document usefulness are unresolved. **Verify the window against your provider before designing around it.**
- **Multimodal: 88/100.** **Native text, image and audio input** places it in the methodology's top band (90–100) on audio ingestion — a capability only a handful of models in this dataset document — and the measured visual evidence is genuinely good and unusually well corroborated: **MMMU-Pro at 73.5% is reproduced exactly by Artificial Analysis**, and **CharXiv at 78.1% without tools is strong**, ahead of Claude Sonnet 5 and close to Claude Opus 4.8. It is scored at 88 rather than inside the top band for one decisive reason: **no audio benchmark exists at all.** The capability is documented by two sources but entirely unmeasured, so crediting it at full band value would overstate what is known. There is also no video input, no document or OCR benchmark, and no non-text output.
- **Coding: 76/100.** The corroboration here is the strongest available anywhere: **SWE-bench Verified at 77.6% from Thinking Machines Lab is reproduced to the decimal by Vals AI's independent leaderboard**, which removes any harness doubt about that figure, and **LiveCodeBench at 85.5% is also Vals-measured**. Four deductions: **the AA Coding Index at 52.1% is 17.9 points below the methodology's 70%+ frontier reference**, which is the most informative composite available; **AA-SciCode at 47.0% is 8 points below the 55%+ reference**; **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 63.8% is far below the 85%+ coding reference** and falls to 47.6% independently; and **FrontierSWE v2 at 4.1% is the lowest figure any model in this dataset posts on that leaderboard**, with **DeepSWE and Vibe Code Bench absent** so the primary agentic-coding reference cannot be checked. Competent at isolated patches, poor at sustained engineering.
- **Cost efficiency: 90/100.** Among the best in this research pass, and the case is unusually well founded. **$0.95 input / $0.16 cached / $4.05 output is well below the $3/$15 anchor**, corroborated across three independent trackers, with **7 tracked providers** giving real routing competition. Three structural factors compound it: **Apache 2.0 weights — tied with DeepSeek's MIT as the most permissive licence in this dataset**, with no revenue thresholds, no MaaS carve-outs and no security-review clause; **41 billion active parameters**, which puts self-hosting within reach of a single well-equipped node rather than the datacentre deployments the 95B–104B-active alternatives require; and an independent model index rating its evidence quality as **"measured"** rather than estimated. Two deductions: **no free tier** is documented, and the **contested context window** means you may be paying for capacity you cannot rely on.
- **Overall Score: 78.8/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (68 + 72 + 90 + 88 + 76) / 5 = 78.8 — a cheap, permissively licensed, genuinely multimodal model that is excellent at closed-form problems and poor at autonomous work. Choose it for **competition and closed-form mathematics**, where **AIME 2026 at 97.1% is the best figure in this entire research pass**; for **chart and visual reasoning** (CharXiv 78.1% unaided, MMMU-Pro exactly corroborated at 73.5%); for **audio-input pipelines**, where native audio under Apache 2.0 at 41B active parameters is a combination nothing else in this dataset offers — though note the capability is documented and unmeasured; for **structured output and tabular work**, which independent positioning identifies as its strength; and for **any deployment where licence permissiveness and self-hostability govern**. Do not choose it for autonomous agentic work under any circumstances: **AA AutomationBench 5.0%, AA Terminal-Bench 4.0 1.0%, AA Briefcase 834 Elo and FrontierSWE v2 4.1% are each the lowest figure recorded in this research pass**, and GDPval-AA at 1,064 Elo, the Agentic Index at 24.3% and AnalystAgent at 23.8% all agree. Do not choose it for sustained coding (AA Coding Index 52.1%), for multi-document long-context work (MLCR-AA 12.2%), or for unaided facts (67.7% hallucination rate). Two practical cautions: **verify the context window with your provider** — published figures span 256K to 1,049K — and **check whether Inkling-Small is sufficient**, since it scores marginally higher on the same composite tracker.
