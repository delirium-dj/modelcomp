## Model card

- **Name:** Gemini 3.6 Flash. No `-free` API SKU verified. Variant/alias flag: announced **together with Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite and Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber** in a single Google blog post, so figures from that announcement must be attributed carefully between the three. **This is a doubly-superseded legacy entry**: Gemini 3.7 Flash (2026-08-13) and Gemini 3.8 Flash (2026-09-02) both followed within six weeks, and Google's own 3.7 Flash documentation states that 3.7 is **"based on Gemini 3.6 Flash with algorithmic improvements"** — i.e. the successors are behaviour deltas on this same foundation, not new architectures.
- **Short description:** Released **2026-07-21** as the Flash-tier workhorse that opened Google's three-release-in-six-weeks Flash sequence. Its historical significance is a single independently verified result: **ARC Prize measured it at 60.4% on ARC-AGI-2** — higher than any other model's verified figure found in this entire research pass, including Gemini 3 Pro's subscription-gated Deep Think mode (45.1%) and GPT-5.2 Pro (54.2%).
- **Provider / access:** Gemini API, Google AI Studio and Vertex AI, plus the Gemini app and OpenRouter (`google/gemini-3.6-flash`). The capability surface is documented through its successor: Google states 3.7 Flash keeps 3.6's envelope, and that envelope is **context caching, code execution, computer use (preview), file search, function calling, Google Maps and Search grounding, structured outputs, thinking and URL context**, with four consumption modes (Standard, Batch, Flex, Priority) and **no audio generation, image generation or Live API**.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released 2026-07-21. **Knowledge cutoff: no verified figure found for 3.6 specifically**; its direct successor documents March 2026 with some domains potentially limited to January 2025.
- **IDs:** `gemini-3.6-flash`; `google/gemini-3.6-flash` (OpenRouter). **No free-tier API ID verified.**
- **Context window:** **1,048,576 input tokens / 65,536 max output** — the "1M-input and 64K-output class" that Google's 3.7 Flash documentation explicitly states 3.7 inherited unchanged from 3.6. Unusually for this dataset, **real retrieval evidence exists**: GDM-MRCR v2 at a 91.8% 128K average, plus an independent AA-LCR of 80.0%.
- **Modalities:** Input: **text, image, video, audio and PDF**. Output: text only. This rests on Google's own statement that 3.7 Flash is based on 3.6 with algorithmic improvements and that the release was "primarily a reasoning and agent-behaviour delta, not a new context, modality or storage architecture" — combined with 3.7's documented five-modality input list. **No Google specification sheet for 3.6 Flash itself was retrievable**, so the modality list is inherited rather than directly documented. Reasoning: yes — three thinking levels (`low`, `medium` default, `high`), with thinking tokens billed as output. Tool calls: yes.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **$1.50 / 1M input, $0.15 / 1M cached input, $7.50 / 1M output** — this is Google's standard Flash-tier list rate, established by 3.6 Flash and documented as the rate that Gemini 3.7 Flash's introductory pricing was explicitly *half of* until 2027-01-01, when 3.7 reverts to "$1.50 input and $7.50 output per million tokens, the same as 3.6 Flash". Batch and Flex run at half, Priority at 1.8x, and cache storage at $0.50 per 1M tokens per hour on this tier. Google Search grounding: 5,000 free requests per month shared across Gemini 3.x usage, then $14 per 1,000.
- **Architecture:** Proprietary, closed weights. **Parameter count, active parameters, topology, training corpus and compute are all undisclosed** — Google discloses none of these for the Flash line.

### Raw benchmarks found

> **25 of 645 tracked benchmark slots are populated**, with per-row source attribution — and the mix of provenances here is unusually good. **(G)** = Google's own launch announcement for 3.6 Flash / 3.5 Flash-Lite / 3.5 Flash Cyber. **(G-3.7)** = Gemini 3.6 Flash's column in Google's **August 2026 Gemini 3.7 Flash model card**, verified directly in this research session. **(AA)** = Artificial Analysis independent measurements. **(Vals)** = Vals AI's independent leaderboards. **(ARC)** = ARC Prize verified results. **(Cursor)** = Cursor's own CursorBench evals. **(OR)** = OpenRouter's benchmark page. A composite tracker places it at **65.16/100, #34 of 783**, flagged as conservative due to partial coverage. **Where Google's two surfaces disagree on the same benchmark, both are recorded.**

Agent / tool use:

- **Terminal-bench 2.1: 73.8% (Vals, independent)** versus **78.0% (G-3.7)** — a 4.2-point vendor-versus-independent gap. Both sit in the upper mid band, below the methodology's ~88% frontier anchor.
- **Terminal-bench 3.0: 5.4% (G-3.7)** — a floor result, against Gemini 3.7 Flash's 14.9% and GPT-5.6 Terra's 20.8%.
- Tau3-Banking / Tau2-Bench: no verified public score found
- **GDPval-AA: 1,423 Elo (G)** / **1,422 Elo (G-3.7)** / **38.2% normalized (AA)** — the two Google surfaces agree within 1 Elo, and the figure is **roughly 330 Elo below the methodology's ~1750+ frontier reference**.
- Claw-Eval / ClawProBench: no verified public score found
- Toolathon / MCP-Atlas / SWE Atlas Codebase QnA: no verified public score found
- **OSWorld-Verified: 83% (G)** versus **OSWorld 2.0: 33.8% (G-3.7)** — a ~49-point gap between Google's own two reported computer-use figures. These are different benchmark versions, but the magnitude is a strong warning against reading the 83% as general computer-use capability.
- **AA Agentic Index: 30.1% (AA)** — low, and the clearest independent signal that autonomous agency is mid-tier.
- **AutomationBench: 17.0% (G-3.7)**; **Agent's Last Exam: 24.2% (G-3.7)**; **Harvey LAB-AA: 85.1% (G-3.7)**

Reasoning / knowledge:

- **ARC-AGI-2: 60.4% (ARC Prize, verified)** — **the highest verified ARC-AGI-2 figure found anywhere in this research pass.** For context: Gemini 3 Pro scored 31.1% base and 45.1% with Deep Think, GPT-5.2 Pro 54.2%, GPT-5.2 Thinking 52.9%, Claude Opus 4.5 37.6%, Gemini 3.1 Pro 77.1% (vendor-reported, not ARC-verified). **ARC-AGI-1: 91.20% (ARC Prize, verified).**
- **GPQA Diamond: 93.4% (Vals)** and **92.8% (AA)** — **clears the methodology's 90%+ frontier reference, independently corroborated by two separate labs within 0.6 points.** No vendor figure needed.
- **HLE: 51.2% HLE-Verified (G-3.7)** versus **40.8% AA-HLE (AA)** — a 10.4-point vendor-versus-independent gap; **both clear the 40%+ frontier reference**, the independent one only just.
- **MRCR: GDM-MRCR v2 91.8% at a 128K average (G-3.7)**; **AA-LCR: 80.0% (AA)**
- **CritPt: 10.6% (AA)** — weak (GPT-5.4 Pro scores 30 on the same benchmark).
- **Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index: 34.0% (AA)** on the current rescaled index — **this sits in the methodology's mid band (20–35 → 55–65)**. **Conflicting figure: Google's 3.7 Flash card lists 3.6 Flash at 52** on an earlier index version. The two are not interchangeable and neither is dropped.
- **AA-Omniscience: Index 22.1%, accuracy 50.0%, hallucination rate 55.6% (AA)** — accuracy is respectable but **the 55.6% hallucination rate is high**, worse than Claude Opus 5.5 (59% is higher, but Sonnet 5.5 is 47% and Muse Spark 1.2 is 28%).
- **MMLU-Pro: 89.3% (Vals)**; **LABBench2: 76.1% (G-3.7)**; **BioMysteryBench: 80.6% human-solvable / 41.2% human-difficult (G-3.7)**

Coding:

- **LiveCodeBench: 88.1% (Vals, independent)** — among the highest in this dataset and independently measured.
- **AA-SciCode: 53.4% (AA)** — **just below the methodology's 55%+ frontier reference.**
- Vibe Code Bench: no verified public score found
- **DeepSWE: 49.0% (G)** versus **48.6% (G-3.7)** — the two Google surfaces agree within 0.4 points, and both are **roughly 25 points below the methodology's 74%+ frontier reference.**
- **AA Coding Index: 69.2% (AA)** — **fractionally below the 70%+ frontier reference.**
- **CursorBench 3.2: 53.5% (Cursor)**; **FrontierCode 1.1 Main: 34.4% (G-3.7)**; **Code Arena WebDev Elo: 1,538 (G-3.7)**

Multimodal / documents:

- **CharXiv Reasoning: 85.2% without tools / 89.4% with tools (G-3.7)** — notable because **Gemini 3.7 Flash actually regressed on both** (84.5% / 88.7%), making 3.6 Flash the better chart reasoner of the two.
- **LVBench: 84.2% (G-3.7)** — long video.
- **GDP.pdf: 22.0% (G-3.7)** — weak document handling.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 76/100.** **OSWorld-Verified at 83% (Google)** and **Harvey LAB-AA at 85.1%** are genuinely strong, and **Terminal-bench 2.1 at 73.8% independently measured by Vals AI** sits in the upper mid band — respectable for a Flash-tier model. But four pieces of evidence cap it firmly below the frontier band. **GDPval-AA at ~1,423 Elo is roughly 330 below the ~1750+ reference**, and the **independent AA Agentic Index of 30.1%** says the same thing from a different direction. **Terminal-bench 3.0 at 5.4% is a floor result**, so the 2.1 score does not survive a harder harness. Google's **own two computer-use figures differ by ~49 points** (OSWorld-Verified 83% versus OSWorld 2.0 33.8%), which makes the headline unreliable as a general capability claim. And **Tau3-Banking, Claw-Eval, MCP-Atlas and Toolathon are all unreported**, triggering the methodology's explicit missing-evidence penalty, with AutomationBench at 17.0% and Agent's Last Exam at 24.2% both near the floor.
- **Reasoning: 88/100.** The strongest independently verified reasoning evidence of any Flash-tier model here, and arguably of any model in this pass on one axis. **ARC Prize verified 60.4% on ARC-AGI-2 — the highest verified figure I found anywhere**, well above Gemini 3 Pro's Deep Think mode (45.1%) and GPT-5.2 Pro (54.2%), on a benchmark specifically built to resist memorisation — with **ARC-AGI-1 at 91.20%** alongside it. **GPQA Diamond clears the 90%+ reference and is corroborated twice independently (93.4% Vals, 92.8% AA)** with no reliance on a vendor number. HLE clears the 40%+ reference on both measurements, MMLU-Pro reaches 89.3% and GDM-MRCR v2 91.8%. Four deductions keep it below the frontier band: the **current-version Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index of 34.0% is mid-band**, 26 points below the 60+ marker, and cannot be reconciled with Google's claimed 52 on an older version; **the 55.6% hallucination rate is high** at only 50.0% accuracy; **CritPt at 10.6%** is roughly a third of the best figure here; and the vendor-independent HLE gap is 10.4 points, so vendor figures on this model need discounting.
- **Context window: 96/100.** A **1,048,576-token window** places it in the ≥1M tier (95–100), and it earns above the floor because — unlike most 1M claims in this dataset — **retrieval is actually measured**: GDM-MRCR v2 at a 91.8% 128K average plus an independent AA-LCR of 80.0%. Pricing is flat across the window with no long-context cliff, and cached input at $0.15 keeps large stable prefixes cheap. It stops short of the ceiling because the tier's 100 condition needs **≥98% retrieval at 512K+** and the published figure is **91.8% at only 128K**, with nothing measured at half a million tokens or beyond; and the **65,536-token output ceiling is half the frontier norm**, barely clearing the methodology's <64K caveat threshold.
- **Multimodal: 91/100.** Input spans **text, image, video, audio and PDF** — audio placing it in the methodology's top band (90–100) — and the measured results back the breadth rather than merely asserting it: **AA-MMMU-Pro 83.2% independently measured**, **CharXiv Reasoning 85.2% without tools and 89.4% with**, and **LVBench 84.2%** on long video. A point worth stating plainly: **its own successor regressed on CharXiv in both conditions**, so 3.6 Flash remains the better chart reasoner of the pair. Three things hold it near the band floor: the modality list is **inherited from Google's statement that 3.7 Flash is based on 3.6 with an unchanged envelope**, not directly documented for 3.6 itself; output is **text only** with no audio or image generation and no Live API; and **GDP.pdf at 22.0% is weak** — roughly four of five document tasks fail.
- **Coding: 83/100.** The independent evidence is the best part: **LiveCodeBench at 88.1% and SWE-bench at 79.6%, both measured by Vals AI**, are strong figures that do not depend on Google's harness. Google's two surfaces also agree closely on DeepSWE (49.0% / 48.6%), which is a good sign for its reporting integrity even though the number itself is weak. Four deductions: **DeepSWE at ~49% is roughly 25 points below the methodology's 74%+ frontier reference**; **the AA Coding Index at 69.2% falls fractionally short of the 70%+ reference** and **AA-SciCode at 53.4% short of the 55%+ reference**; **Terminal-bench 2.1 at 73.8–78.0% is well below the 85%+ coding reference**; and FrontierCode 1.1 at 34.4% plus CursorBench 3.2 at 53.5% place it mid-field on real-editor and merge-acceptance work. **SWE-bench Pro and Vibe Code Bench are absent.**
- **Cost efficiency: 80/100.** **$1.50 / $7.50 is better than the $3/$15 anchor that maps to ~60**, with **cached input at $0.15** making repeated large prefixes nearly free, **Batch and Flex halving the rate to $0.75/$3.75**, and flat pricing across the full 1M window with no long-context cliff. Against that, four deductions. **Thinking tokens bill as output**, so reasoning-heavy work pays the $7.50 rate. **Cache storage costs $0.50 per 1M tokens per hour** on top of token charges, and Search grounding costs **$14 per 1,000 requests** past a 5,000-request allowance shared across all Gemini 3.x usage. There is **no free API tier verified**. And decisively, the value case has been obsoleted twice by its own vendor: **Gemini 3.7 Flash launched at $0.75/$3.75 — literally half this list price — and Gemini 3.8 Flash matched that rate while scoring higher on nearly every row**, so paying 3.6 Flash's list price for 3.6 Flash's capability is only rational where an acceptance suite is already validated against this exact model.
- **Overall Score: 86.8/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (76 + 88 + 96 + 91 + 83) / 5 = 86.8 — a legacy Flash model with one genuinely exceptional and still-unmatched credential. Choose it for **novel abstract reasoning**, where **ARC Prize's verified 60.4% on ARC-AGI-2 is the highest such figure in this dataset** and beats models costing six times as much; for **chart and figure reasoning**, where it outperforms its own successor (CharXiv 85.2% / 89.4%); for **independently verified coding** (LiveCodeBench 88.1%, SWE-bench 79.6%, both Vals-measured); and for **graduate-level science** (GPQA Diamond corroborated at 92.8–93.4% by two labs). Do not choose it for autonomous long-horizon agency — **GDPval-AA ~1,423 Elo, AA Agentic Index 30.1%, AutomationBench 17.0% and Terminal-bench 3.0 at 5.4%** all converge on the same verdict. Do not choose it for repository-scale software engineering (DeepSWE ~49%), for document or PDF pipelines (GDP.pdf 22.0%), or for factual work without grounding (55.6% hallucination rate at 50.0% accuracy). And be clear about the economics: **Gemini 3.7 Flash shipped at half this price and 3.8 Flash matched that while scoring higher**, so unless you specifically need the ARC-AGI-2 or CharXiv results, the successors dominate it on every commercial axis.
