### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 78/100.** One genuinely category-leading result anchors this: **Claw-Eval at 70.4% from the benchmark's own independent leaderboard is the best in this entire dataset**, which matters because the methodology specifically penalises models that lack a Claw-Eval figure. **τ²-bench at 84.8% clears the Tau-family reference**, and BrowseComp (83.7%), DeepSearchQA (73.7%) and OSWorld-Verified (72.7%) are respectable. Five deductions hold it to 78: **GDPval-AA, MCP-Atlas, Toolathlon and Tau3-Banking are all unreported** — four of six methodology references missing; **Terminal-Bench stops at 2.0 (65.4%)** with no 2.1+ figure, so there is nothing to test against the ~88% anchor; **ApprenticeBench at 5% is a floor result** and the worst agentic figure here; ResearchClawBench (19.9%) and JobBench (36.7%) corroborate weak long-horizon autonomy; and τ² at 84.8% is 10–13 points behind the models leading that benchmark.
- **Reasoning: 72/100.** This dimension is dominated by an evidence-integrity problem rather than by weak results. **HLE spans 53% (Anthropic), 40% (Meta's chart) and 19.1% (Artificial Analysis) — a 33.9-point range, the widest vendor-independent divergence in this research pass**, and **GPQA Diamond spans 91.3% / 89.2% / 84.0%**, with Anthropic's figure clearing the 90%+ reference and neither independent measurement doing so. The most credible explanation is the documented **non-reasoning versus adaptive variant split**, which means the base ID scored here is materially weaker than Anthropic's system-card numbers imply. On the independent evidence the picture is poor: **Intelligence Index 26.4% (mid band)**, **CritPt 2.8%**, **AA-IFBench 44.6% — the lowest in this dataset**, **AA-LCR 67.0% — also the lowest**, and an **80.1% hallucination rate**. Two genuine strengths pull it up: **AIME 2025 at 99.8%** and **Epoch AI's independently measured FrontierMath v2 Tier 4 at 22.9%**, which beats several nominally stronger models.
- **Context window: 92/100.** A **1M-token input window** places it in the ≥1M tier, and **Anthropic's current documentation confirms 300K Batch-output beta eligibility** — an eligibility that Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1, at twice the price, do not have. It sits below the tier floor because the long-context evidence is **actively weak rather than merely absent**: **AA-LCR at 67.0% is the lowest such figure in this entire dataset**, 15 points below Claude Sonnet 5 and 14 below Gemini 3.5 Flash, and there is **no MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks figure at all**. **Max synchronous output is also undocumented** for this specific model.
- **Multimodal: 68/100.** Input is **text and images only** with text-only output, placing it in the methodology's "+image in = 60–70" band, and it does not earn the top of that band. **ScreenSpot Pro at 83.1% is genuinely strong** screen understanding, and MMMU-Pro reaches 77.3% vendor / 72.5% independent — though that 4.8-point gap is itself a discount signal. Three things cap it: **ERQA at 51.6%** is weak embodied-visual reasoning; **there is no CharXiv, document-understanding, OCR or video benchmark at all**, so chart and document capability — standard coverage for this tier — is entirely unmeasured; and MedXpertQA (MM) at 64.8% is mid-field.
- **Coding: 82/100.** Two independently measured results stand out: **Vibe Code Bench at 57.57% is the highest figure recorded in this research pass** on Vals AI's real-world build benchmark, and **React Native Evals at 84.1%** shows strong mobile front-end generation. **SWE-bench Verified at 80.8%** is solid even after Arcee's comparison trims it to 75.6%, and SWE-Rebench (65.3%) and LiveCodeBench Pro (70.7%) corroborate. Three deductions: **FrontierCode 1.1 Main at 26.9% is very low** — roughly half what Claude Sonnet 5 and Opus 4.8 achieve on the same Cognition benchmark, indicating poor merge-acceptance quality; **SWE-bench Pro at 53.4%** is mid-field; and **DeepSWE, SciCode and the AA Coding Index are all absent**, so three of the band's four references cannot be checked.
- **Cost efficiency: 40/100.** **This score carries an explicit caveat: no rate card was verified for this model.** It rests on the Anthropic Opus-tier standard of **$5 / $25** confirmed for Claude Opus 4.8 and Claude Opus 5 in the same generation, which sits meaningfully worse than the $3/$15 anchor that maps to ~60. The mitigations are standard (10% cache reads, Batch at 50%, 300K Batch-output eligibility). What drives it below the anchor is succession: **four Opus generations have shipped since**, and **Claude Opus 5.5 at $4 / $20 — 20% cheaper on both meters — scores 87.78 against this model's 64.26**, a 23.5-point composite gap for less money. Claude Sonnet 5.5 at $2/$10 also outscores it by 19 points. There is no workload where paying Opus 4.6 prices for Opus 4.6 capability is the right answer.
- **Overall Score: 78.4/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (78 + 72 + 92 + 68 + 82) / 5 = 78.4 — a deep-legacy model with two genuinely best-in-dataset credentials and serious evidence-integrity problems. The real strengths, both independently sourced: **Claw-Eval at 70.4% is the best in this dataset** and **Vibe Code Bench at 57.57% is also the best**, with React Native Evals (84.1%), ScreenSpot Pro (83.1%), AIME 2025 (99.8%) and Epoch-measured FrontierMath v2 Tier 4 (22.9%) all respectable. If you have a workload that maps onto Claw-Eval-style tool use or Vibe-Code-style app generation and an acceptance suite already pinned to this model, it holds up. For anything else, three findings should stop adoption. **The variant split makes vendor numbers unreliable for this ID** — HLE spans 53% to 19.1% and GPQA 91.3% to 84.0% depending on who measured, and the base ID is classified non-reasoning. **The independent reasoning evidence is poor**: Intelligence Index 26.4%, CritPt 2.8%, AA-IFBench 44.6% and AA-LCR 67.0% are all the lowest or near-lowest in this dataset, with an **80.1% hallucination rate**. And **the commercial case is indefensible** — Claude Opus 5.5 costs 20% less and scores 23.5 composite points higher. Use it only where it is already validated; route everything new to Opus 5.5 or Sonnet 5.5.
