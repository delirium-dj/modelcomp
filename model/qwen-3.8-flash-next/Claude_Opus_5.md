## Model card

- **Name:** Qwen3.8-Flash-Next. **Open weights** under the **Qwen Community licence.** Variant: **`experimental-preview`** — the tracker's own classification, and it matters: this is a preview checkpoint, not a stable release. **Explicitly distinct from the managed Qwen3.8-Flash API tier** per this repository's curated metadata, so the two should not be conflated.
- **Short description:** A composite tracker places it **#35 of 784 at 64.15/100** across 37 benchmarks — a remarkable placement for a model with only **~6B active parameters**. Three results are disproportionate to its size. **GDPval-AA at 1,648 Elo** is the highest GDPval figure in this research pass outside the GLM-5.3 family, beating Claude Opus 4.8 (1,588), DeepSeek V4 Pro (1,590) and GPT-5.6 Luna (1,582) — from a 6B-active model. **Agents' Last Exam at 51.2% is roughly double GLM-5.3's 28.5%** and nearly matches Qwen3.8-Max's 52.4%. And **the AA Coding Index at 73.0% clears the methodology's 70%+ reference**, with **LiveCodeBench v6 at 91.9%**. Two consistent weaknesses: **HLE misses the 40%+ reference on both measurements (35.9% vendor, 38.0% independent)**, and **no Terminal-Bench figure exists at any version** — a gap independent commentary confirms is a deliberate Qwen omission.
- **Provider / access:** Weights at `Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next` on Hugging Face under the **Qwen Community licence**. Served on the **Vercel AI Gateway**. Reported as a **125B-A6B MoE released around 26 August 2026**.
- **Release / knowledge:** Reported **circa 2026-08-26**. **No verified knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `opencode/qwen-3.8-flash-next`; weights `Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next`. **No free-tier ID**, though self-hosting is permitted under the community licence.
- **Context window:** **262,144 tokens native, extensible to 1M with YaRN** (repository metadata; tracker records 262K). **The distinction matters: the 1M figure is an extrapolation technique, not a trained window**, and YaRN extension typically degrades quality at depth. **AA-LCR at 79.7%** is strong — but measured at the native length, not at the extended one. Max output: **131,072 tokens** on the sibling managed tier; not separately verified here.
- **Modalities:** **Text, image and video input → text output, with tool use** (repository metadata). **Video input is confirmed by LVBench at 76.6%**, a long-video benchmark. No audio path; no non-text output.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **$0.12 / 1M input, $0.40 / 1M output on the Vercel AI Gateway** (repository metadata), plus **self-hosting under the Qwen Community licence**. That is roughly **25× cheaper on input and 37.5× cheaper on output than the methodology's $3/$15 anchor** — among the cheapest rate cards found anywhere in this research pass. **No primary Alibaba rate card was reached; re-verify before budgeting.**
- **Architecture:** **Open weights, 125B total language parameters with 6B active** — a very sparse MoE (repository metadata), which is what makes both the pricing and the size-to-performance ratio notable.

### Raw benchmarks found

Composite: **64.15/100, #35 of 784**, flagged conservative on partial coverage. **Note that no third-party harness other than Artificial Analysis appears at all** — there is no Vals AI, ARC Prize, Epoch or Cursor measurement for this checkpoint, which is consistent with its experimental-preview status.

Agent / tool use:

- **GDPval-AA: 1,648 Elo / 55.6% normalized (AA)** — **the highest GDPval figure in this research pass outside the GLM-5.3 family**, ahead of DeepSeek V4 Pro (1,590), Claude Opus 4.8 (1,588), GPT-5.6 Luna (1,582), Muse Spark 1.1 (1,375) and GLM-5.2 (1,418). It remains **102 Elo below the ~1750+ reference**, but for a 6B-active model this is the standout result on the page.
- **AndroidWorld: 84.5% (Qwen)** — strong mobile GUI agency.
- **CoWorkBench: 73.9% (Qwen)**; **Toolathlon-Verified: 73.5% (Qwen)**
- **Agents' Last Exam: 51.2% (Qwen)** — **roughly double GLM-5.3's 28.5%** and near Qwen3.8-Max's 52.4%; one of the highest ALE figures in this research pass.
- **JobBench: 55.7% (Qwen)**; **OSWorld 2.0: 19.4% (Qwen)**

Reasoning / knowledge:

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 78/100.** Strong relative to size, with one genuinely notable result. **GDPval-AA at 1,648 Elo is the highest in this research pass outside the GLM-5.3 family** — ahead of DeepSeek V4 Pro, Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.6 Luna, all vastly larger models — which is real evidence that a 6B-active MoE can do economically valuable work. **Agents' Last Exam at 51.2% is roughly double GLM-5.3's 28.5%**, among the highest ALE figures here, and **AndroidWorld at 84.5%, CoWorkBench at 73.9% and Toolathlon-Verified at 73.5%** form a coherent picture of competent multi-step agency. Four deductions. **GDPval still sits 102 Elo below the ~1750+ reference.** **OSWorld 2.0 at 19.4%** shows desktop computer-use remains weak. **No Terminal-Bench figure exists at any version** — a deliberate vendor omission per independent commentary — so the single most-used agentic harness cannot be checked. And **Claw-Eval, MCP-Atlas, τ²-bench, CyberGym, BrowseComp and the AA Agentic Index are all unreported**, with the methodology explicitly penalising the Claw-Eval absence.
- **Reasoning: 78/100.** Two genuine credentials against three consistent shortfalls. **GPQA Diamond clears the 90%+ reference on both measurements (91.7% vendor, 92.3% independent), with the independent figure exceeding the vendor's own** — a pattern that increases confidence in the whole table. **IFBench at 81.3% ties Grok 4.3 for the highest instruction-following figure in this research pass**, and **AA-LCR at 79.7% is strong**. Against that: **HLE misses the 40%+ reference on all three measurements (35.9%, 35.9%, 38.0%)**, and the vendor reporting an identical figure with and without tools implies tool access provided no benefit — which is itself informative about depth of reasoning. **The Intelligence Index of 39.8% is 20.2 points below the 60+ marker** and **CritPt is 11.1%**. **The AA-Omniscience Index is negative at −9.7% on just 24.5% accuracy**, among the narrowest knowledge bases measured here. And **ARC-AGI, FrontierMath and MMLU-Pro all have no figure**, so abstract reasoning, research mathematics and knowledge breadth are entirely unmeasured.
- **Context window: 80/100.** **262,144 tokens native** places it in the methodology's 200K–500K band (65–84), and it sits near the top of that band because **AA-LCR at 79.7% is strong** — comparable to GLM-5.3's 79.7% and DeepSeek V4 Pro's 80.3%, both nominally 1M — so the window it genuinely has is well used. It is deliberately **not** scored in the ≥1M tier despite the "extensible to 1M with YaRN" claim, for three reasons: **YaRN is an extrapolation technique rather than a trained window**, and typically degrades quality at depth; **the 79.7% AA-LCR figure was measured at native length, not extended length**, so there is no evidence the extension preserves quality; and **no MRCR, RULER, CorpusQA or GraphWalks measurement exists at any length**. Crediting a YaRN-extended ceiling as if it were native would reward a configuration flag over measured behaviour.
- **Multimodal: 86/100.** **Video input is confirmed by LVBench at 76.6%**, placing it in the methodology's "+video/PDF in = 75–90" band, and the measured breadth within that band is excellent for the size class. **CharXiv at 90.6% is the strongest chart reasoning found in this research pass** — above GLM-5.3-Flash (89.4%) and Muse Spark 1.1 (88.4%) — and holds 84.6% even without tools. **MathVision at 90.6% (95.7% with Python) is exceptional visual mathematics**, **RealWorldQA at 88.5%** is strong, and **AA-MMMU-Pro at 79.8% leads GPT-5.6 Luna (78.6%) and Grok 4.3 (78.1%)**. Three reasons it does not reach the band ceiling: **there is no audio input path**, so the top band is unavailable; **Vision2Web at 64.0% and ERQA at 72.3%** mark real limits on grounded web and embodied reasoning; and **no OCR benchmark exists**, so document extraction is unmeasured.
- **Coding: 80/100.** Good, with one reference cleared and one conspicuous absence. **The AA Coding Index at 73.0% clears the methodology's 70%+ reference** and is in fact the highest Coding Index in this research pass after GLM-5.3's 74.8% — ahead of GPT-5.6 Luna, Muse Spark 1.1 and GPT-5.4. **LiveCodeBench v6 at 91.9%** is very high, and **SWE Multilingual at 81%** shows the capability is not English-only. Four deductions: **DeepSWE at 58.7% is 15.3 points below the 74%+ reference** and below even GLM-5.3-Flash's 63.4%; **AA-SciCode at 50.6% misses its reference by 4.4 points**; **NL2Repo at 48.1% and SWE-bench Pro at 62.5%** are mid-field; and **neither SWE-bench nor SWE-bench Verified nor Terminal-Bench has any figure**, so the two most-cited repository-engineering measures are both unavailable and the dimension rests on self-contained problems rather than real codebases.
- **Cost efficiency: 93/100.** Outstanding. **$0.12 / $0.40 on the Vercel AI Gateway is roughly 25× cheaper on input and 37.5× cheaper on output than the methodology's $3/$15 anchor** — among the cheapest rate cards found anywhere in this research pass — and what it buys is not a toy: **GDPval-AA at 1,648 Elo beating DeepSeek V4 Pro and Claude Opus 4.8**, **an AA Coding Index of 73.0% above the frontier reference**, **GPQA Diamond above 90% twice over**, **IFBench tied for the pass lead at 81.3%**, **CharXiv at 90.6%** and **video input**. Three structural advantages compound it: **the weights are open under the Qwen Community licence**, so the price is escapable; **125B total with only 6B active** makes self-hosting genuinely tractable — far more so than GLM-5.3's 753B or DeepSeek V4 Pro's undisclosed scale; and the sparsity means low inference cost even self-hosted. Three deductions: **the Qwen Community licence is more restrictive than MIT** (which MiMo V2.6 and GLM-5.3-Flash carry), so commercial terms need checking; **this is an `experimental-preview` checkpoint**, so stability and continued availability are not assured; and **no primary Alibaba rate card was reached**.
- **Overall Score: 80.4/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (78 + 78 + 80 + 86 + 80) / 5 = 80.4 — **the best size-to-capability ratio found in this research pass.** A **6B-active** model that posts **GDPval-AA at 1,648 Elo (beating DeepSeek V4 Pro, Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.6 Luna)**, **an AA Coding Index of 73.0% above the frontier reference**, **GPQA Diamond clearing 90% on both measurements with the independent figure higher than the vendor's**, **IFBench tied for the pass lead at 81.3%**, **CharXiv at 90.6% — the strongest chart reasoning here**, **MathVision at 90.6%**, **Agents' Last Exam at roughly double GLM-5.3's figure**, and **video input** — at **$0.12 / $0.40** with open weights — is a genuinely unusual package. Choose it for **cheap high-volume multimodal agentic work, chart and visual-mathematics analysis, mobile GUI automation, and coding on self-contained problems**, and self-host it if the licence terms suit. Four cautions. **It is an experimental preview**, so do not build production dependencies without a fallback. **Repository-scale coding is unproven** — no SWE-bench of any variant and no Terminal-Bench at any version, with DeepSWE at 58.7% well below reference. **Depth of reasoning is limited**: HLE misses 40% on all three measurements and tools did not help, the Intelligence Index is 39.8%, CritPt is 11.1%, and ARC-AGI, FrontierMath and MMLU-Pro are entirely unmeasured. And **the 1M context claim rests on YaRN extension rather than a trained window**, with no retrieval evidence at extended length.
