## Model card

- **Name:** Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite
- **Short description:** Google's **June-2025 cheapest, fastest 1M-context multimodal model**, "built for high-frequency, small-payload automation." AA-classified **non-reasoning** (with an optional dynamic-**thinking** mode), it trades accuracy for **~300.9 tok/s (#2/59 on AA)** and **$0.10/$0.40** (lowest tracked price) with a **free tier on Google AI Studio**. Input is broad (**text + image + audio + video + PDF**), output text-only. ⚠ **Deprecated** — **Google Cloud (Vertex) retired it 2026-10-20** (that date has now passed) while the **Gemini API keeps serving it**; migrate to **Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite**. Capability is budget-tier: vendor **GPQA Diamond 64.6/66.7%**, **SWE-bench Verified single-attempt 31.6/27.6%**, **LiveCodeBench 33.7/34.3%**, **HLE 5.1/6.9%**, and — despite the 1M window — **poor long-context effectiveness (MRCR v2 1M pointwise 4.1/5.4%)**. There is **no AA Intelligence Index** (deprecated, "No publicly available API").
- **Provider / access:** **Google Gemini API** (`gemini-2.5-flash-lite`) at **$0.10/$0.40** per 1M in/out (**90% cache discount**), **free tier on Google AI Studio**. **NOT on OpenCode Zen** (Zen's Gemini catalogue starts at Gemini 3 Flash — no 2.5 route). **Google Cloud / Vertex retired it 2026-10-20**; the Gemini API tier lingers.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released **June 17, 2025** (llm-stats; AA "June 2025"). Knowledge cutoff **January 1, 2025**. **Deprecated** → Gemini 3.x Flash-Lite lineage.
- **IDs:** `google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite`; AA `gemini-2-5-flash-lite` (**Intelligence Index N/A — "No publicly available API," 0/4 units**); Gemini 2.5 family technical report arXiv `2503.16534`.
- **Context window:** **1,048,576 (1M) total / 65,536 (64K) max out** — the "cheapest 1M-context ticket in Google's lineup." ⚠ But **long-range retrieval is weak**: **MRCR v2 (1M, pointwise) 4.1% (non-thinking) / 5.4% (thinking)** and **MRCR v2 (8-needle, 128k avg) 16.6% / 30.6%** — the window is available, not reliably usable at range.
- **Modalities:** **Text + image + audio(speech) + video + PDF in → text out.** AA confirms "text, image, speech, and video"; Google docs/meta add **PDF**. Broadest-input tier (matches the full Gemini 2.5 Flash). **Text-only output** — no native image/audio generation (unlike Gemini 2.0 Flash).
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-28):** **$0.10 / $0.40** per 1M in/out (**90% cache**) — **the lowest price among tracked providers** (llm-stats); **free tier** on Google AI Studio (standard rate limits).
- **Architecture:** **Proprietary non-reasoning** (AA class) multimodal model with an **optional dynamic-thinking mode**; parameters undisclosed; Gemini 2.5 family. Interesting quirk: **thinking helps math (AIME 49.8→63.1) and GPQA slightly, but *lowers* single-attempt SWE-bench Verified (31.6→27.6)**.

### Raw benchmarks found

> Independent anchor: **none from AA** — the AA page returns **N/A for Intelligence Index, Cost-per-task, and Verbosity ("No publicly available API," 0/4 units)** because the model is deprecated and AA no longer runs its intelligence battery; only **Speed persists as a historical 4/4-unit measurement (300.9 tok/s, #2/59)**. Every quality number below is therefore **vendor (Google)**, from the official Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite scorecard (langbase mirror), reported as **Non-Thinking / Thinking**. The only independent-ish composite signal is **llm-stats #290** (near the bottom of ~290+ tracked). AA Index baskets are version-specific (v5.0, 9 evals, 716 models) and this model has **no v5.0 measurement**.
>
> **Scoring stance:** distinct shipped model + a full vendor scorecard + a persisted AA Speed measurement → **scored, not excluded** — but with modest, evidence-graded normalization given the vendor-only provenance, deprecated status, and objectively weak reasoning/coding/long-context numbers.

Vendor (Google official scorecard — Non-Thinking / Thinking):

- **GPQA Diamond: 64.6% / 66.7%** — modest graduate-level science reasoning (frontier is 85–93%).
- **AIME 2025: 49.8% / 63.1%** — decent math for a budget lite; thinking helps substantially.
- **Humanity's Last Exam (no tools): 5.1% / 6.9%** — very low (frontier 20–40%).
- **LiveCodeBench: 33.7% / 34.3%** — weak competitive coding.
- **SWE-bench Verified (single attempt): 31.6% / 27.6%** — weak agentic coding; ⚠ thinking mode *lowers* single-attempt SWE-bench.
- **SWE-bench Verified (multiple attempts): 42.6% / 44.9%** — the coding ceiling.
- **Aider Polyglot: 26.7% / 27.1%** — weak multi-language code editing.
- **MMMU: 72.9% / 72.9%** — solid visual reasoning (beats Gemini 2.0 Flash's 69.3%); **Vibe-Eval (Reka): 51.3% / 57.5%**.
- **FACTS grounding: 84.1% / 86.8%**; ⚠ **SimpleQA: 10.7% / 13.0%** — a **factuality regression** vs Gemini 2.0 Flash's 29.9%.
- **Global MMLU (Lite): 81.1% / 84.5%** — decent multilingual general knowledge.
- ⚠ **MRCR v2 (8-needle, 128k avg): 16.6% / 30.6%; MRCR v2 (1M, pointwise): 4.1% / 5.4%** — long-context retrieval is poor despite the 1M window.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 73/100.** A budget high-frequency model, **not an agent**: **SWE-bench Verified single-attempt 31.6%/27.6%**, multiple 42.6%/44.9%, **Aider Polyglot 26.7%/27.1%**, and **no AA Index** (no AutomationBench/Briefcase anchor). Broad multimodal input + the 1M window give it some tool/GUI/document-ingestion utility, and thinking mode does **not** help agentic coding (SWE-bench single drops). Weak-agentic Lite band.
- **Reasoning: 70/100.** AA-classified **non-reasoning** (optional thinking mode). **GPQA Diamond 64.6%/66.7%** and **AIME 2025 49.8%/63.1%** (thinking helps math) are modest, but **HLE 5.1%/6.9%** is very low and **SimpleQA 10.7%/13.0%** is a **regression** vs 2.0 Flash (29.9%). **No AA Intelligence Index** to anchor it. Low end of the non-reasoning/Lite band, discounted for deprecation and weak factuality.
- **Context window: 80/100.** **1,048,576 (1M) nominal / 64K out** — the "cheapest 1M-context ticket in Google's lineup," a genuine differentiator for large-document ingestion. But **MRCR v2 long-context effectiveness is poor: 1M pointwise 4.1%/5.4%, 8-needle 128k 16.6%/30.6%** — the window is available but not reliably retrievable at range. Credited above the 256K band (78) for the large nominal window, well below the 1M-proven band (85–86) for the weak MRCR.
- **Multimodal: 82/100.** The strength: **text + image + audio(speech) + video + PDF in** (AA confirms text/image/speech/video; Google docs add PDF) — the broad-input tier, matching the full Gemini 2.5 Flash. **MMMU 72.9%** (beats 2.0 Flash's 69.3%) and **Vibe-Eval 51–57%** show real visual understanding. **Text-only output** (no native image/audio generation, unlike Gemini 2.0 Flash's 85). Broad-input native-video tier, slightly discounted for budget-model multimodal depth.
- **Coding: 70/100.** Weak: **LiveCodeBench 33.7%/34.3%**, **SWE-bench Verified single 31.6%/27.6%**, **Aider Polyglot 26.7%/27.1%** — all far below the frontier coders (70–85% SWE-bench). Multiple-attempt SWE-bench 42.6%/44.9% is the ceiling. Vendor-only, no independent coding re-verification, and thinking doesn't help single-attempt agentic coding. Weak-but-present Lite band.
- **Cost efficiency: 93/100.** The defining strength: **$0.10/$0.40 per 1M (90% cache) — the lowest tracked price (llm-stats)**, a **free tier on Google AI Studio**, and **300.9 tok/s (#2/59 on AA)** — "the cheapest 1M-context ticket in Google's lineup… built for high-frequency, small-payload automation." Rock-bottom price + free tier + near-fastest speed. Trimmed from 95 by the **deprecation / Google Cloud retirement (2026-10-20, passed)** sunset risk. Not counted toward Overall.
- **Overall Score: 75/100.** Mean of Tool 73 + Reasoning 70 + Context 80 + Multimodal 82 + Coding 70 = 375 / 5 = 75.0 → 75. Best fit: **the cheap, fast, broad-multimodal 1M-context budget workhorse** — Google's June-2025 lowest-price ($0.10/$0.40), near-fastest (300.9 tok/s, #2/59) Gemini, with **text+image+audio+video+PDF input**, a **free tier**, and a **1M window**, aimed at **high-frequency, small-payload automation** — but **deprecated (Google Cloud retired 2026-10-20; migrate to Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite)**, with **weak reasoning (GPQA ~65–67%, HLE ~5–7%), weak coding (SWE-bench single ~28–32%, LiveCodeBench ~34%), poor long-context effectiveness (MRCR 1M ~4–5%), a SimpleQA factuality regression, and no AA Intelligence Index**. Ideal for **cost- and latency-sensitive, high-volume multimodal classification/extraction/summarization where top-tier accuracy isn't required**; **use Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite (successor) or Gemini 2.5 / 3.x Flash when reasoning, coding, or long-context retrieval matters**. **Divergence note:** curated queue 73.1 vs my 75 → **+1.9**. I credit the **broad-input multimodal (82)** and the **1M nominal window (80)**, and treat the exceptional cost/speed as uncounted; the curated score leans harder on the **deprecated status, weak reasoning/coding, and poor long-context effectiveness**, which I reflect in Reasoning 70 / Coding 70 / Tool 73 but which don't fully offset its multimodal breadth and window size. Lands **one below the current Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite (76)** — the older, weaker, deprecated sibling — and **three below the fuller Gemini 2.5 Flash (78)**.
