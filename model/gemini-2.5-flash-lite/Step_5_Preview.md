## Model card

- **Name:** Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite (preview **2025-06-17**, GA **2025-07-22**, Sep-2025 preview refresh). The **smallest and cheapest Gemini 2.5 model** — a "hybrid reasoning" tier built purely for high-volume, latency-sensitive work (classification, translation, extraction, routing), with thinking off by default and a controllable thinking budget when a task earns it. **A negative finding governs this report: BenchLM carries no `gemini-2-5-flash-lite` page in any spelling** (it 404s; BenchLM tracks only the separately-scored `gemini-2-5-flash` at 41.97 and `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` at 46.87), so the 2026-11-06 re-research had no catalog to check against.
- **Short description:** Google's own pitch is "intelligence per dollar," and the model card shows it beats 2.0 Flash-Lite across the board — **but the absolute numbers are deliberately small-tier: GPQA ~72%, HLE ~7%, SWE-bench Verified ~39%**. Its genuinely valuable property is the **1M-token window with 65,536 max output accepting text, image, audio, video and PDF** at **$0.10/$0.40 per 1M with a free tier** — the cheapest 1M-context ticket in Google's lineup at the time.
- **Provider / access:** API-only (proprietary) — Gemini API in AI Studio, Vertex AI, OpenRouter. **Free tier available on Google AI Studio with standard rate limits**; the repo's meta explicitly notes this is **not** on OpenCode Zen, whose Gemini catalogue starts at Gemini 3 Flash.
- **Release / knowledge:** **2025-06-17 (preview), 2025-07-22 (GA), Sep-2025 preview refresh; knowledge cutoff January 2025** (re-confirmed on AA 2026-10-19) — a genuine, dated fact, and **22 months behind this report's date**.
- **IDs:** `gemini-2.5-flash-lite` (Gemini API, Vertex, Openrouter); `google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite` (repo id).
- **Context window:** **1,048,576 tokens (1M); max output 65,536 tokens** — a full frontier window with a generous output allowance, at the cheapest tier.
- **Modalities:** **text, image, audio, video and PDF in; text out** — the broadest input set measured in this batch, placing it in the methodology's highest modality tier. Native tools: Grounding with Google Search, code execution, URL context.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-11-06):** **$0.10 input / $0.40 output per 1M**, with a free tier on AI Studio and ~310 tok/s throughput (repo meta). Methodology anchors put $0.10/$0.40 at or above the 97+ band.
- **Architecture:** proprietary, closed weights; parameter count undisclosed. Hybrid reasoning with thinking off by default.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

> **Methodology note.** With three carried-forward capability figures and no tracker catalog page, every quality dimension below is a placeholder-for-unknown anchored on the verified structural facts (1M/65,536 output, five-modality input, $0.10/$0.40 with a free tier) and the model's deliberate small-tier positioning. The two dimensions where this SKU is genuinely distinctive — **context window** and **cost efficiency** — are the best-grounded scores in this report.

- **Tool use: 50/100.** **Scored at the methodology's neutral floor for "dimension claimed, entirely unmeasured."** Not one agentic benchmark exists — no Terminal-Bench, τ²-bench, τ³-bench, MCP-Atlas, OSWorld, BrowseComp, Toolathlon, MazeBench, ALE, Claw-Eval, JobBench, GDPval-AA or AA Agentic Index. Scored at 50 rather than below because the model ships **native tools (Grounding with Google Search, code execution, URL context)** and is explicitly positioned for high-volume routing and extraction work, which implies a real tool substrate. **Not scored above 50 because an unmeasured capability claim must not be scored as though it were demonstrated**, and the small-tier positioning points downward.
- **Reasoning: 48/100.** Below the methodology's mid band, and resting on the tier's design intent. The three measured figures — **GPQA ~72%, HLE ~7%, SWE-bench Verified ~39%** — are all far under their references, and the ~7% HLE is especially poor. Capped further by the **total absence of CritPt, AA-LCR, MRCR, ARC-AGI, the AA Intelligence Index, AA-Omniscience, AIME, MATH and FrontierMath** — so composite depth, reliability and three of five reasoning references are unknown. Scored at 48 rather than lower because the **hybrid-reasoning design with a controllable thinking budget** means the model can allocate effort when a task earns it, and the 1M window is a genuine reasoning asset for long-document work.
- **Context window: 97/100.** **1,048,576 tokens with 65,536 max output** — a full frontier window with a generous output allowance, at the cheapest tier in Google's lineup, and the model's single most valuable property. Scored in the methodology's ≥1M tier at 97 on the strength of both the window and the output allowance. Held just under 100 because **no AA-LCR, MRCR or GraphWalks figure exists**, so the window's usable depth is entirely unverified, and no long-context pricing tier was found.
- **Multimodal: 94/100.** **Text, image, audio, video and PDF in; text out** — the broadest input set measured in this batch, placing it in the methodology's **90–100 "audio in" tier**: the highest modality tier, and one of only two models refreshed in this session to reach it (alongside Nemotron 3 Nano Omni). Scored at 94 rather than 100 because **output is text-only**, there is no audio or video *output*, and **not one visual or audio benchmark exists** — no MMMU-Pro, CharXiv, ExtractBench, BabyVision, ZeroBench, OmniDocBench, MathVision or Design Arena figure. The breadth of the claim is vendor-stated and consistent with the family, so it is scored above the 75–90 "+video/PDF" tier, but it is a spec-sheet claim rather than a demonstrated capability and is recorded as such.
- **Coding: 45/100.** Below the methodology's mid band, and resting on the tier's design intent. **SWE-bench Verified ~39%** is ~46 points under the 85%+ terminal reference, and **AA-SciCode, LiveCodeBench, the AA Coding Index, DeepSWE, Vibe Code Bench, Terminal-Bench and FrontierCode have no figure at all** — so the 74%, 70% and 55% references cannot be checked. The model is explicitly *not* positioned for coding, and the figure that exists confirms it.
- **Cost efficiency: 97/100.** **$0.10/$0.40 per 1M with a free tier on AI Studio** — below the methodology's ~$0.60/$2.20 (≈92) anchor and near the 97+ band, and **the free tier is a real, vendor-operated route rather than a third-party gateway**, which distinguishes it from the AIHubMix free routes recorded elsewhere in this research series. Scored at 97 on the rate card plus the free tier. Held at 97 rather than 100 because **the free tier carries standard rate limits** and the **1M window's top end is likely economically gated** on a tier this cheap.
- **Overall Score: 66.8/100.** (50 + 48 + 97 + 94 + 45 + 97) / 5 = 71.6 → **72**. Recommendation: **a deliberately small-tier model whose value is entirely in its price and modality breadth, and whose retirement is dated.** The **1M window with 65,536 output accepting text, image, audio, video and PDF at $0.10/$0.40 with a free tier** is a genuinely cheap and broad ticket for high-frequency, small-payload automation — classification, translation, extraction, routing. **But the absolute capability numbers are deliberately tiny (GPQA ~72%, HLE ~7%, SWE-bench Verified ~39%)**, so it must never be used for reasoning, coding, agentic or factual work. Two operational cautions from the repo's own metadata: **Google Cloud retires this model on 2026-10-20 while the Gemini API keeps serving it** — a split-tier retirement that is worth planning around; and **the knowledge cutoff is January 2025, 22 months behind this report's date**. Prefer **Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite (46.87, refreshed in an earlier batch)**, which is the direct successor and the repo's own recommended migration target.
