## Model card

- **Name:** Qwen 3.8 27B
- **Short description:** **Alibaba's dense 27-billion-parameter open-weight (Apache 2.0) multimodal model**, released **2026-08-14** (FP8-quantized on Hugging Face), and the standout **self-host value pick** of the Qwen 3.8 family — a 27B that runs on a **single 24 GB GPU at 4-bit** yet posts frontier-adjacent agentic numbers. Its **one strong independent anchor** is **Vals AI's SWE-bench Verified leaderboard** (bash-tool-only, mini-swe-agent harness), where it lands at **90% / 86% / 69% / 67%** across difficulty bands — roughly **15th of 88 models and the top open-weight dense model**, ahead of Claude Opus 4.6, Sonnet 4.6, GPT-5.2 and Gemini 3 Pro on that harness (corroborating the meta card's "SWE-bench 86.0% (Vals)"). Everything else on the official card is **vendor-reported by Alibaba with no independent reproduction at launch** — and those numbers claim it **beats Claude Opus 4.6 Max on 16 of 24 benchmarks**, led by **OSWorld-Verified 84.3 (vs Opus 72.7)**, **SWE-bench Pro 61.7 (vs 53.4)** and **IFBench 79.5 (vs 62.5)**, while **losing on knowledge-heavy reasoning** (HLE 30.8 vs 40.0; GPQA Diamond 89.2 vs 91.3; Terminal-Bench 2.1 73.0 vs 78.2). ⚠ **Artificial Analysis had *not* indexed it** as of Aug 2026, so read the vendor table as a **ceiling, not a floor** (documented caveats: imported competitor scores, asymmetric prompting on MathVision/BabyVision, and in-house QwenSWEBench/CoWorkBench). Hosted API pricing varies by provider (Qwen Cloud / Model Studio / OpenRouter / Regolo); Regolo advertises ~93% below Alibaba Model Studio rates. Multi-token-prediction supported for faster serving.
- **Architecture:** Dense **27B** (28B incl. vision encoder) hybrid-attention Transformer — **48 Gated DeltaNet linear-attention layers + 16 full-attention layers** (linear-attention hybrid for efficient long context). Flexible "thinking" control (reasoning on/off), native multimodal encoder, multi-token prediction. Runs contamination-resistant fresh-problem evals (LiveCodeBench v6).
