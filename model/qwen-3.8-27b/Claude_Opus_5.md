## Model card

- **Name:** Qwen3.8-27B. **Open weight** — official model card on Hugging Face as `Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B`. Variant/alias flag: a **Qwen3.8-27B (Chutes TEE)** confidential-compute deployment is tracked separately, and the Qwen3.8 line also contains **Qwen3.8-Max** (2.4T/95B), **Qwen3.8-Flash-Next** and **Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash**.
- **Short description:** **The most significant fact about this model is its size.** At **27 billion parameters** it is by far the smallest model in this part of the queue — genuinely deployable on a single workstation GPU — yet it posts **LiveCodeBench v6 at 90.3%**, **OSWorld-Verified at 84.3%**, **CharXiv at 90.2%**, **OmniDocBench 1.5 at 91.1%** and **GPQA Diamond at 90.5% (independently measured by Artificial Analysis)**. It is also notable as a **foundation others build on**: Perplexity's `pplx-decider-v1-27b` is derived from it and publishes a 12-benchmark decision panel averaging 74.76%. The counterweight is reliability — **an AA-Omniscience Index of −10.0%** on just **15.6% accuracy**.
- **Provider / access:** Alibaba Cloud DashScope, Hugging Face weights, OpenRouter and third-party hosts including a **Chutes TEE confidential-compute** variant. **At 27B, open weights are a genuinely usable escape from any rate card** — unlike the 552B–2.8T open-weight models elsewhere in this dataset.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released in the Qwen3.8 cycle. **No verified public release date or knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B` (Hugging Face). Related earlier model: Qwen3.6-27B.
- **Context window:** **262,144 tokens (262K).** Max output: no verified figure found. **AA-LCR at 82.0%** is a strong long-context reasoning figure for a 27B model; **MLCR-AA at 21.7%** is far weaker.
- **Modalities:** **Text and image input → text output**, evidenced by eleven published visual benchmarks. **No video or audio benchmark found** and no non-text output.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **No rate card was verified.** The relevant cost fact is structural: at **27B parameters with open weights**, this is the one model in this part of the dataset that a single organisation can realistically run on commodity hardware. **Re-verify any hosted rate before budgeting.**
- **Architecture:** **27 billion parameters**, dense or MoE not stated; topology and active-parameter count not disclosed in the sources reached. Pre-training data is not public.

### Raw benchmarks found

> **67 of 645 tracked benchmark slots are populated** — among the broadest coverage in this research pass. **(Q)** = Qwen's own Hugging Face model card; **(AA)** = Artificial Analysis; **(Vals)** = Vals AI; plus the VulcanBench Eval Suite 3 leaderboard and Perplexity's own pinned model card. A composite tracker places it at **57.58/100, #56 of 783**, flagged conservative on partial coverage.

- **Terminal-Bench 2.1: three-way disagreement — 73.0% (Q), 79.8% (AA), 58.4% (Vals)** — a **21.4-point spread** across vendor and two independent harnesses, with Artificial Analysis measuring *above* the vendor's own claim. All three are recorded.
- **AA Tau3 Banking: 48.0% (AA)** — just under the methodology's ~50%+ Tau3 frontier reference, and one of only a handful of published Tau3 figures here.
- **AA Agentic Index: 46.5% (AA)** — among the higher figures in this dataset, behind only Grok 4.6 (53.4%) and Claude Sonnet 5 (44.3%)… in fact ahead of Sonnet 5.
- **GDPval-AA: 1,423 Elo / 45.5% normalized (AA)**; **AA Briefcase: 1,397 Elo (AA)**; **CoWorkBench: 70.7% (Q)**; **WebArena-Verified: 64.8% (Q)**; **AA AutomationBench: 48.2% (AA)**; **AA EnterpriseOps-Gym: 44.2% (AA)**

- **AA Terminal-Bench 4.0: 5.6% (AA)** — a near-total floor on the hardest terminal benchmark.

- **AA-LCR: 82.0% (AA)**; **MLCR-AA: 21.7% (AA)** — a 60.3-point gap on multi-document reasoning.
- **CritPt: 5.4% (AA)** — near-zero.

- **AA-Omniscience: Index −10.0%, accuracy 15.6%, hallucination rate 30.3% (AA)** — a 30.3% hallucination rate is respectable, but **accuracy of 15.6% is among the lowest recorded here** and produces a **negative composite index** — one of only three negative Omniscience scores in this dataset. The reading is straightforward: a 27B model simply does not hold much factual knowledge.
- **IFBench: 79.5% (Q)**; **MMLU-Pro: 84.3% (Vals)**

These are classification and judgement tasks rather than generative benchmarks and sit outside general rankings, but they are real evidence that the model is a competent discriminator.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 83/100.** Genuinely impressive for a 27B model: **OSWorld-Verified at 84.3% matches Claude Opus 4.8's 83.4%**, **AndroidWorld at 81.9%** is strong mobile agency, **the AA Agentic Index of 46.5% is above Claude Sonnet 5's 44.3%**, **AA Tau3 Banking at 48.0%** is near the frontier reference on a benchmark almost nobody publishes, and CoWorkBench (70.7%) plus Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 79.8% under Artificial Analysis's harness corroborate. Four deductions: **GDPval-AA at 1,423 Elo is ~330 below the ~1750+ reference**; **AA Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 5.6% is a near-total floor** and the Terminal-Bench 2.1 figure spans 21.4 points across three harnesses, so the headline is unreliable; **GDP.pdf (16.6%) and JobBench (33.4%) are weak**; and **Claw-Eval, MCP-Atlas and Toolathlon are unreported**.
- **Reasoning: 74/100.** **GPQA Diamond clears the 90%+ frontier reference under Artificial Analysis (90.5%)** — and notably the independent lab measured *above* Qwen's own 89.2% — with **AA-LCR at 82.0%**, **IFBench at 79.5%**, MMLU-Pro independently confirmed at 84.3%, and a **Perplexity decision panel averaging 74.76%** that shows real competence as a discriminator. Four deductions: **HLE at 30.8%/33.9% is well below the 40%+ reference on both measurements**; **the Intelligence Index of 33.7% is mid-band**; **CritPt at 5.4% is near-zero** and **MLCR-AA at 21.7% is a 60.3-point drop from AA-LCR**; and most consequentially, **an AA-Omniscience Index of −10.0% on 15.6% accuracy** — a 27B model does not hold much factual knowledge, and its 30.3% hallucination rate, while respectable, cannot compensate for having little to recall.
- **Context window: 78/100.** **262,144 tokens** places it in the methodology's 200K–500K tier (65–84), and it is scored in the upper half of that band because **AA-LCR at 82.0% is a strong long-context reasoning figure** — particularly for a 27B model, where KV-cache pressure is the usual limiting factor. Three things prevent a higher placement: the window is a quarter of the 1M that most peers at this standing offer; **MLCR-AA at 21.7%** shows multi-document reasoning across that window largely fails; and **no MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks retrieval figure exists**, with max output tokens undocumented.
- **Multimodal: 70/100.** Input is **text and image** with text-only output, placing it in the methodology's "+image in = 60–70" band — and it earns the **top of that band** on the strongest measured evidence any model in that band offers in this dataset. **OmniDocBench 1.5 at 91.1% is essentially level with the best document figures here**, **CharXiv at 83.7% without tools is better than Claude Opus 4.8's 80.5%**, MathVision reaches 90.0%, RealWorldQA 85.9% and AA-MMMU-Pro is independently measured at 76.3%. That is eleven published visual benchmarks from a 27B model, most of them strong. It cannot exceed the band because there is **no video, audio or PDF input path** and **no non-text output**, and two results temper it: **Vision2Web (62.9%) and ERQA (65.5%)** show web-grounded and embodied visual reasoning are mid-field.
- **Coding: 82/100.** **LiveCodeBench v6 at 90.3% is among the highest in this entire research pass** (independently trimmed to 84.0%), and **SWE-bench at 86.0% from Vals AI is a strong independent result** that beats Claude Sonnet 5 and matches Qwen3.8-Max. VulcanBench v3 at 82.6% adds a third independent datapoint, and SWE-bench Pro reaches 61.7%. Four deductions: **DeepSWE at 42.2% is roughly 32 points below the 74%+ frontier reference** — the largest such shortfall among the capable models here, and a clear signal that long-horizon agentic coding is where 27B parameters bite; **the AA Coding Index at 68.1% falls 1.9 points short of the 70%+ reference**; **AA-SciCode at 46.6% is 8.4 points below the 55%+ reference** and NL2Repo is 42.3%; and **Vibe Code Bench is absent**.
- **Cost efficiency: 92/100.** **The structural case here is the strongest in this dataset, and it is about size rather than price.** At **27 billion parameters with open weights**, this is the only model in this part of the queue that a single team can realistically self-host on commodity hardware — against 552B (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash), 753B (GLM-5.3), 1.02T (MiMo-V2.6-Pro), 2.4T (Qwen3.8-Max) and 2.8T (Kimi K3) for the other open-weight options. A model that delivers LiveCodeBench 90.3%, SWE-bench 86.0%, OSWorld-Verified 84.3%, CharXiv 83.7% unaided and GPQA Diamond 90.5% at 27B is a genuinely different cost proposition: no per-token rate, no provider dependency, and a **Chutes TEE confidential-compute** option for regulated workloads. That Perplexity chose it as the base for its own decider model is independent evidence of its efficiency. Three deductions: **no hosted rate card was verified**; **no free tier** is documented; and the active-parameter count and whether it is dense or MoE are undisclosed, so exact VRAM requirements cannot be stated.
- **Overall Score: 77.4/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (83 + 74 + 78 + 70 + 82) / 5 = 77.4 — **the best capability-per-parameter model in this research pass.** Choose it when **self-hosting matters more than peak capability**: at 27B with open weights it runs where nothing else in this dataset can, and it still delivers LiveCodeBench 90.3%, an independently measured SWE-bench of 86.0%, OSWorld-Verified 84.3%, AndroidWorld 81.9%, OmniDocBench 1.5 91.1%, CharXiv 83.7% unaided and a GPQA Diamond that Artificial Analysis measures above 90%. Specific good fits: **document and chart ingestion**, **mobile and desktop UI automation**, **algorithmic coding**, **classification and judgement work** (the Perplexity panel at 74.76% across twelve tasks), and **confidential-compute deployments** via the TEE variant. Three limits to respect. **It does not know much**: AA-Omniscience accuracy of 15.6% with a negative composite index means it must be paired with retrieval for any factual task. **It cannot sustain long-horizon agentic coding**: DeepSWE at 42.2% is ~32 points off the frontier reference and AA Terminal-Bench 4.0 is 5.6%. And **do not trust the Terminal-Bench headline** — three harnesses span 58.4% to 79.8%, so run your own.
