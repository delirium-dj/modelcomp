## Model card

- **Name:** Jev 1.13 — TypeSafe's flagship and **the first "System One" model**: a type-safe decision model that answers with typed decisions and calibrated probabilities rather than generated text. **A negative finding governs this report: BenchLM carries no `jev-1.13` page in any spelling** (`jev-1.13`, `JEV-1.13`, `jev-1-13` and `jev` all 404), so the 2026-11-06 re-research had no catalog to check against.
- **Short description:** **The routing-and-classification specialist of this research series, and the clearest case of a model that deliberately fails metrics it was never built to meet.** Its results are exceptional on fixed-option classification — **IMDB, SST-2, HellaSwag and ARC at 95–99% accuracy; Belebele 86.7% across 122 languages; 27 of 37 dataset wins against Qwen3.8-27B and all 37 against Gemma-4-E4B; 94% accuracy on calculation-heavy MMLU questions against 91% on the rest** — and it has **no SWE-bench, Terminal-Bench, LiveCodeBench or agentic figure at all**, because its documented limitations state it **"is not trained to generate text."**
- **Provider / access:** **TypeSafe hosted API**; OpenCode Zen (`jev-1.13`, `jev-1.13-free`), Vercel AI Gateway, Cloudflare AI Gateway, NanoGPT, Vivgrid, NEAR AI. **Closed weights, no fine-tuning.**
- **Release / knowledge:** **2026-09-15** (API opened 2026-09-21). **Knowledge cutoff not disclosed.**
- **IDs:** `jev-1.13.0` and alias `jev-latest` (TypeSafe API); `jev-1.13` (OpenCode Zen); repo id `jev-1.13`.
- **Context window:** **64K tokens per request (32K reserved for `state` plus the longest question; some resellers list 32K).**
- **Modalities:** **text-only input** (string, JSON object, or array of text values); **output is typed decisions/probabilities — no image, audio, or video, and no text generation.**
- **Pricing (as of 2026-11-06):** **$0.042/M input, $0/M output** (free because there is no autoregressive generation; **~950 tokens per decision**); **free tier $0/$0 on OpenCode Zen**; rate limits 100K tok/s, 80 req/s.
- **Architecture:** **undisclosed; new architecture + new sampler + RLCD (calibrated decisions).** **The output format is the defining architectural fact**: this is not a text generator that classifies, it is a decision layer.

### Raw benchmarks found

Vendor-reported (TypeSafe's own docs and eval harness) with one independent third-party evaluation, carried from the prior verified research passes (2026-10-09, 2026-10-19) with re-attribution; no tracker catalog page exists for cross-check.

- **IMDB, SST-2, HellaSwag, ARC: 95–99% accuracy** (TypeSafe eval harness)
- **Belebele: 86.7% across 122 languages** (TypeSafe eval harness)
- **vs Qwen3.8-27B: wins 27 of 37 datasets** (**none of Qwen's nine leads outside bootstrap intervals**); **vs Gemma-4-E4B: wins all 37** (TypeSafe eval harness)
- **MMLU calculation-heavy questions answered more accurately than other MMLU questions: 94% vs 91%** (TypeSafe eval harness)
- **Calibration: "choice probabilities are well calibrated and support selective prediction"; UNFAIR-ToS micro-F1 0.50 → 0.75 with tuned thresholds**
- **ayautomate.com (791 labeled decisions vs four LLMs): "Jev was faster and far cheaper, level with the small models on accuracy, and its confidence score made a cheap cascade work"** — the only independent evaluation in the file
- **Artificial Analysis: not tracked**
- **Vendor marketing's "193.6x faster / 444.6x cheaper" claims are criticized as benchmarking against large reasoning models** — recorded as a marketing-provenance caveat
- **Documented limitations (TypeSafe's own docs): literal-reading, arithmetic and indirection failure modes**
- **No verified public score found** for SWE-bench, Terminal-Bench, LiveCodeBench, GPQA, HLE, AIME, MATH, CritPt, AA-LCR, MRCR, ARC-AGI (beyond the ARC row above), the AA Intelligence Index, AA-Omniscience, MCP-Atlas, OSWorld, GDPval-AA, the AA Agentic Index or any vision benchmark.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 40/100.** Below the methodology's mid band, and structurally so. **This model is the decision layer rather than a tool caller — no MCP or tool-calling capability exists.** Scored at 40 rather than the floor because **its structured execution is genuinely functional**: typed answers, routed across 37 task families, calibrated cascades, and it **dominates both 27B open baselines on decision accuracy** (27 of 37 against Qwen3.8-27B with none of Qwen's nine leads outside bootstrap intervals, and all 37 against Gemma-4-E4B). **That is real agentic-adjacent capability, but it is routing capability rather than tool capability**, and the distinction is stated rather than blurred.
- **Reasoning: 50/100.** At the methodology's neutral floor, and this score requires the most careful reading in the file. **ARC and HellaSwag at 95–99% and Belebele 86.7% across 122 languages are strong absolute numbers** — but they come from **next-token probabilities over fixed option sets, there is no math, HLE or agentic depth**, and **the vendor's own docs list literal-reading, arithmetic and indirection failure modes.** **The 94%-vs-91% MMLU calculation-heavy result is the most interesting single figure in the file** and is recorded as evidence that the model's calibration is real rather than lucky, but it does not make it a reasoner. Scored at the neutral floor because the capability is genuine within its frame and absent outside it.
- **Context window: 40/100.** **64K nominal but only 32K usable for `state`** (plus the longest question) — the methodology's 50–64 band at its floor, and scored below it because the effective usable window is half the nominal one. **The 32K `state` reservation is a spec fact rather than a measurement**, and no MRCR, RULER or AA-LCR figure exists to test durability at that depth.
- **Multimodal: 12/100.** **Text-only input, typed decision output** — squarely in the methodology's text-only 10–20 band. No image, audio or video input, so the dimension does not exist beyond the floor.
- **Coding: 15/100.** **Not a coding model: no SWE-bench, Terminal-Bench, SWE, LiveCodeBench figure exists or is attempted**, and **documented limitations state it "is not trained to generate text."** Scored at 15 — the bottom of the text-only band — rather than lower, because the methodology's floor is 1 and a 15 reflects "capability absent by design and documented as such" rather than "capability absent and unknown."
- **Cost efficiency: 97/100.** **$0.042/M input with output tokens free (there is no autoregressive generation), 100K tok/s throughput, ~950 tokens per decision, and a $0 free tier** — among the cheapest per decision in the catalog, at the methodology's near-$0 anchor. **Closed weights prevent self-hosting**, which is the one dock from a full-marks score.
- **Overall Score: 31/100.** (40 + 50 + 40 + 12 + 15) / 5 = 31.4 → **31** → **31**. **A disagreement is recorded rather than reconciled: the repo queue carries 40.8 for this slug while this report reads 31**, and with BenchLM absent there is no third instrument to arbitrate — the queue figure is the corpus-average of other research agents' raters, this report is Step 5 Preview's own normalized reading, and both are stated side by side rather than flattened. **And a scoring-frame note is recorded: this score measures a capability the model was never built to have** — the five dimensions are general-assistant dimensions, and a decision layer that cannot generate text will score badly on four of them by construction, so **31/100 here means "not a general assistant", not "poor model."** Recommendation: **the routing-and-classification specialist** — 95–99% accuracy on fixed-option tasks, calibrated probabilities, 86.7% Belebele across 122 languages, all 37 dataset wins against Gemma-4-E4B and 27 of 37 against Qwen3.8-27B, at a fraction of a cent per decision with a $0 free tier. Its independent evaluation is the strongest third-party evidence in this batch: **791 labeled decisions across four LLMs, where Jev was "faster and far cheaper, level with the small models on accuracy, and its confidence score made a cheap cascade work."** The right deployment is behind an LLM cascade, where its calibrated confidence is the mechanism that makes the cascade cheap. Three cautions: **it cannot generate text**, so it is not a chat, coding or writing model and its documented limitations (literal-reading, arithmetic, indirection) are the vendor's own; **Artificial Analysis does not track it**, so its evidence base is the vendor's harness plus one independent evaluation; and **the vendor's "193.6x faster / 444.6x cheaper" marketing claims are criticized as benchmarking against large reasoning models** and are not used in any score here.
