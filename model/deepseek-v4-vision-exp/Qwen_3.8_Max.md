## Model card

- **Name:** DeepSeek V4 Vision Exp (full name **DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp**)
- **Short description:** **DeepSeek's first multimodal model in the V4 family** — an **experimental** vision variant that adds a **vision encoder + aligner** to the **DeepSeek-V4-Flash** MoE backbone (284B total / 13B active) and continues training for visual understanding. It went live on the **DeepSeek API 2026-08-21** and open-weighted on **Hugging Face 2026-08-31 under a clean MIT license** (no strings — the sharpest contrast in the current open-weight wave, vs Qwen3.8-Flash-Next's community license). DeepSeek's claim is precise and modest: it **matches the text-only V4-Flash on text capabilities (agents, reasoning, world knowledge)** while lifting **multimodal-agent performance "close to Opus-4.8."** On the vendor card it **tops Anthropic's Opus-4.8 on ZeroBench (Pass@5 35.0 vs 34.0)**, **Agents' Last Exam (27.3 vs 25.7)** and **DeepSWE (59.3 vs 58.0)**, while **trailing it on ApexBench (36.5 vs 39.4), NL2Repo (57.7 vs 69.7), Chartography (64.3 vs 65.0) and Terminal-Bench 2.1 (83.9 vs 85.0)** — a card that honestly shows its own losing rows. It is **the UI-vision / image-to-code specialist**: it reasons from screenshots, charts and documents into working code. ⚠ **Three honest counterweights: (1) every benchmark is vendor-run — CellCog confirms "no independent reproduction of the headline scores has been published" (as of Sept 7); (2) it is experimental by name and status** ("a preview lane for image-to-code work, not a daily driver" — unproven stability), and it is the *Flash* efficiency tier, since superseded for text by **DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (552B)**; **(3) it is image-only** (text + image/PDF in → text out; no audio, no video). Its real anchor is the elite **V4-Flash coding lineage** — the text-only V4-Flash-0731 scores a near-frontier **91/87/90/67 on the independent Vals AI SWE-bench Verified** leaderboard, which Vision-Exp matches-or-beats on DeepSeek's own text-agent rows.
- **Provider / access:** **DeepSeek.** API `deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp` (Chat Completions, Messages & Responses; **DeepSeek Harness 0.1.1** out-of-the-box support), open weights on **Hugging Face** (`deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp`, MIT), day-one **SGLang** support (with **DSpark** speculative decoding) and **vLLM** merged Sept 2 (PR 54566); also served by **SiliconFlow** and others. Images via base64, external URL, or the new **Files API** (free; upload once, reference by `file_id`). Meta lists a **free OpenCode Zen tier** for multimodal experimentation. HF downloads passed **209,000** within a week of the weight drop. Listed here as `opencode/deepseek-v4-vision-exp`.
- **Release / knowledge:** **API live 2026-08-21; open weights 2026-08-31 (MIT).** The hosted model had ten days of production use before the weights dropped. Knowledge cutoff not published in the sources reviewed.
- **IDs:** `deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp` (DeepSeek API) / `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp` (Hugging Face).
- **Context window:** ⚠ **Meta correction — 1,048,576 (1M) tokens native, NOT the scaffolded meta's "200K."** CellCog confirms 1M from the published config, and SiliconFlow and the vLLM recipe both state 1M; it "carries over from the V4 family's hybrid attention work, which means long **multimodal** agent sessions, not just single-image Q&A." (Scored with a small hedge for the experimental status and the meta's divergent 200K figure, which may reflect an API served cap rather than the native window.)
- **Modalities:** **Text + image in → text out** (images: charts, screenshots, documents, photos; **PDFs** are handled as document images). **Image-only** — no audio, no video, no generative multimodal output. The card deliberately leads with **multimodal *agent*** benchmarks (ApexBench, ZeroBench, Chartography), "not captioning, not VQA." Images are tokenized for billing at **up to 384 tokens each**.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-24):** **Images bill at up to 384 tokens each at standard DeepSeek-V4-Flash rates** — i.e. DeepSeek's rock-bottom efficiency-tier pricing (the V4-Flash/V4.1-Flash API runs roughly **$0.15 input / a few $/1M output**). **MIT open weights = free self-host with no strings** (~168GB checkpoint, multi-GPU server territory, but "meaningfully lighter than the 1.6T-parameter V4-Pro"); **free OpenCode Zen tier** (per meta); **Files API free**. The cost story is excellent — the MIT license is the most permissive in the current open-weight wave (no MAU/revenue naming clause, no separate "AI Work Assistant business" license).
- **Architecture:** **DeepSeek-V4-Flash MoE backbone: 284B total parameters, 13B active per token**, with **256 routed experts (6 active) + 1 shared expert, 43 layers**. Vision-Exp **adds a vision encoder + aligner**, then continues training for visual understanding — the vision capability is **additive** ("maintains comparable performance on text-only agent tasks," backed by side-by-side numbers). Checkpoint is **mixed precision (FP4 expert weights, FP8 elsewhere), ~168GB on disk**; inference via **SGLang with DSpark speculative decoding** (a fused draft module) plus a minimal PyTorch reference. **MIT license.**

### Raw benchmarks found

⚠ **All benchmarks below are vendor-run (DeepSeek model card). CellCog: "the caveat that applies to every launch table this year applies here: these are the vendor's runs, at the vendor's settings, and independent replication is pending" — and as of Sept 7 none had been published.** There is **no Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index** entry and **no Vals AI row for Vision-Exp specifically**; the only independent anchor is the **text-only V4-Flash-0731 base**.

Vendor multimodal + text-agent table (**Vision-Exp** vs text-only **V4-Flash-0731** vs **Opus-4.8**, per DeepSeek's card):

- **ZeroBench (Pass@5): 35.0** — **tops Opus-4.8 (34.0)**; the meta's standout claim, **confirmed** (a notoriously hard visual-reasoning benchmark where most models score near zero)
- **Agents' Last Exam: 27.3** — **beats Opus-4.8 (25.7)** and V4-Flash-0731 (25.2)
- **DeepSWE: 59.3** — **beats Opus-4.8 (58.0)**, up sharply from V4-Flash-0731 (54.4)
- **Terminal-Bench 2.1: 83.9** — near Opus-4.8 (85.0), above V4-Flash-0731 (82.7)
- **Toolathlon-Verified: 75.9** — near Opus-4.8 (76.2), up from V4-Flash-0731 (70.3)
- **Chartography: 64.3** — just below Opus-4.8 (65.0); V4-Flash not run
- **ApexBench (Pass@1): 36.5** — **trails Opus-4.8 (39.4)** but a **large multimodal jump** over text-only V4-Flash (26.2, which ignored the images entirely)
- **NL2Repo: 57.7** — **trails Opus-4.8 badly (69.7)**; above V4-Flash-0731 (54.2)

Independent anchor (**Vals AI SWE-bench Verified**, bash-tool-only neutral harness) — for the **text-only base**, not Vision-Exp itself:

- **DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731: 91 / 87 / 90 / 67** by difficulty band (<15 min / 15 m–1 h / 1–4 hr / >4 hr) — **near-frontier, with excellent long-horizon (90% at 1–4 hr, 67% at >4 hr)**. Since Vision-Exp "maintains comparable performance on text-only agent tasks" and beats V4-Flash-0731 on DeepSWE/Toolathlon/Terminal-Bench on DeepSeek's own card, its text-coding capability is very likely at or above this strong independent baseline. (DeepSeek family on the same Vals ruler: **V4 Pro 0813 = 97/95/100/100** (#2 overall), **V4 = 88/76/45/0**.)

> Provenance: This model has **no independent benchmark replication** — a fact I weight heavily. The **entire Vision-Exp table (ZeroBench 35.0, ApexBench 36.5, DeepSWE 59.3, Terminal-Bench 2.1 83.9, Toolathlon 75.9, Agents' Last Exam 27.3, Chartography 64.3, NL2Repo 57.7) is DeepSeek's own**, run at DeepSeek's settings; CellCog explicitly flags replication as pending and unpublished, and notes the model **beats Opus-4.8 on only two rows (ZeroBench, Agents' Last Exam) plus DeepSWE, and trails it on ApexBench, Chartography, NL2Repo and Terminal-Bench** — "a card that shows its model losing rows is more credible than one that does not." The **one genuinely independent signal is the Vals AI SWE-bench Verified row for the text-only V4-Flash-0731 base (91/87/90/67)** — near-frontier — which supports the coding dimension by lineage (Vision-Exp is built on V4-Flash and matches-or-beats it on DeepSeek's text-agent rows), but Vision-Exp itself was **not** separately Vals-tested. The scaffolded meta.json was verified against CellCog/SiliconFlow/vLLM rather than inherited, and two fields are **corrected**: (a) **context window** — meta's **"200K" is wrong; the config-confirmed native context is 1M (1,048,576)**, corroborated by three sources; (b) the **architecture** was enriched from vague to confirmed (**284B/13B, 256 routed experts/6 active + 1 shared, 43 layers, vision encoder + aligner, DSpark speculative decoding, ~168GB FP8/FP4, MIT**). The meta's **ZeroBench 35.0% "topping Opus 4.8"** is **confirmed** (35.0 vs 34.0), as is **image-to-code/UI-reasoning** positioning and the **free Zen tier / MIT openness**. **No benchmark was invented; the vendor-only status is stated throughout.**

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 82/100.** Strong *claimed* agentic capability — **Toolathlon-Verified 75.9 (essentially level with Opus-4.8's 76.2, up from V4-Flash's 70.3)**, **Agents' Last Exam 27.3 (beats Opus-4.8)**, **ApexBench 36.5 (a real +10.3 multimodal jump)**, **NL2Repo 57.7**, smooth operation "across agent frameworks" combining vision with tools, and native DeepSeek Harness support. Held **below Inkling (84) and Grok 4.7 (88)** — whose agentic marks are *independently* verified — because **every Vision-Exp tool number is vendor-run with zero replication**, NL2Repo trails Opus-4.8 by 12 points, and the model is experimental. Scored level with the strong-but-unverified vendor tier.
- **Reasoning: 80/100.** DeepSeek states it **matches V4-Flash on reasoning and world knowledge**, and two hard benchmarks are genuinely impressive for the tier — **ZeroBench Pass@5 35.0 (tops Opus-4.8)** and **Agents' Last Exam 27.3 (beats Opus-4.8)**. But it is the **Flash efficiency tier** (below the 1.6T V4-Pro), the card publishes **no pure-reasoning benchmarks (no GPQA/AIME/HLE)** for this checkpoint, ZeroBench is Pass@5 (best-of-five), and again **nothing is independently replicated**. Scored **below Kimi K2.6 (85, AA Index 54)** and Inkling's peer set: solid reasoning evidenced by top-tier ZeroBench/ALE, discounted for tier, vendor-only status, and absent text-reasoning data.
- **Context window: 85/100.** **1M native (1,048,576)** — frontier-class (the 1M club: MiniMax M3, Qwen3.6-Plus, Inkling weights, GPT-5.4), carried over from the V4 family's **hybrid attention** and explicitly enabling "long **multimodal** agent sessions." Hedged a notch below the confirmed-served 1M models (Qwen3.6-Plus 86) because the model is **experimental** and the scaffolded meta's divergent "200K" hints at a possible API served cap (a weights-vs-served gap like Inkling's) that the sources reviewed did not fully pin down.
- **Multimodal: 80/100.** The model's **raison d'être** — a dedicated **vision encoder + aligner** making it a genuine **image-to-code / UI-reasoning specialist** (screenshots → working code), with **near-Opus-4.8 multimodal-agent performance** and a **ZeroBench win over Opus-4.8**. But it is **image-only** (no audio, no video — narrower than Inkling's text+image+audio and Qwen3.8-27B's text+image+video, both 82), it **trails Opus-4.8 on ApexBench, Chartography and NL2Repo**, and the multimodal numbers are **vendor-only**. Scored **above the image-only-with-weak-vision floor (Grok 4.7 at 77)** because its image reasoning is genuinely strong (ZeroBench 35.0 beats a frontier model), but **below the 3-modality models (82)** on breadth and below strong-vision leaders on verified quality.
- **Coding: 84/100.** The strongest dimension, on lineage and on the card: built on the **DeepSeek V4-Flash coding family — whose text-only 0731 base scores a near-frontier independent Vals 91/87/90/67** (90% at 1–4 hr, 67% at >4 hr) — Vision-Exp **beats Opus-4.8 on DeepSWE (59.3 vs 58.0)**, sits **near it on Terminal-Bench 2.1 (83.9 vs 85.0)**, improves DeepSWE/Toolathlon/Terminal-Bench over its own text-only base, and adds **image-to-code**. Discounted below Kimi K2.6/GPT-5.2 (85) because the **Vision-Exp-specific numbers are vendor-only/unreplicated**, **NL2Repo (57.7) trails Opus-4.8 (69.7) badly**, and it is experimental — but the elite independent V4-Flash Vals baseline keeps it high.
- **Cost efficiency: 92/100.** Excellent and multi-layered: **MIT open weights with no strings** (the most permissive license in the current open-weight wave — no MAU/revenue naming clause, no separate "AI Work Assistant business" license, unlike Qwen's community license), a **free OpenCode Zen tier**, **free Files API**, images billed at only **≤384 tokens each at rock-bottom V4-Flash rates**, and an efficient **13B-active** inference profile. Held just below Qwen3.8-27B (93, genuinely single-24GB-GPU hostable) because the **~168GB FP8/FP4 checkpoint is multi-GPU server territory**; scored level with Inkling (92). Scored but **not** counted in the Overall per methodology.
- **Overall Score: 82/100.** Mean of Tool 82 + Reasoning 80 + Context 85 + Multimodal 80 + Coding 84 = 411/5 = 82.2 → 82. Best fit: **open-weight (MIT), cost-sensitive image-to-code and multimodal *agent* work** — turning screenshots/UI mockups/charts/documents into working code, long (1M-context) multimodal agent sessions, and self-hosted or free-tier vision experimentation where a permissive license and near-Opus-4.8 multimodal-agent performance at DeepSeek pricing win. **Not** the pick for production-critical work needing independent verification (none exists), non-image modalities (audio/video), a stable non-experimental daily driver, top-tier NL2Repo/repo-scale generation (trails Opus-4.8 by 12 pts), or the strongest text reasoning (Flash tier, superseded by V4.1-Flash). **Divergence note:** my 82 is **3.2 below the queue's curated 85.2**, driven almost entirely by the **total absence of independent replication** (every headline number is DeepSeek's own), the **experimental status**, and the **image-only** modality scope — against genuinely strong but vendor-attested multimodal-agent results (ZeroBench/Agents'-Last-Exam/DeepSWE wins over Opus-4.8), an elite independent V4-Flash coding lineage (Vals 91/87/90/67), 1M context, and an unmatched MIT + free-tier cost story.
