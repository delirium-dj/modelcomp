> **Verdict:** MAI-Code-1.1-Flash is **Microsoft AI's proprietary, low-cost agentic-coding workhorse** for GitHub Copilot — released **2026-08-11** (successor to the June MAI-Code-1-Flash, which retired 2026-09-10; a 53 GB quantized **on-device** build arrived 2026-10-07). It is a **sparse MoE** — the August model card says **138B-total / 5B-active**, though Microsoft's own Oct-7 local-build post says **137B / 6.8B** (a vendor self-discrepancy) — with a **256K context**, **native vision (new in 1.1** — the June release was text-only**)**, and **no extended-reasoning mode** (deliberate multi-step planning is routed to the sibling **MAI-Thinking-1**). It exists to be *cheap and fast inside Copilot*, not to lead benchmarks: the meta's own tradeoff — "*only two published benchmark rows and a Copilot-centric surface make it a convenience pick rather than a benchmark leader*" — is confirmed. Those two rows are **vendor-self-reported** (SWE-bench Verified **72.6%**, Terminal-Bench 2.1 **62.9%**); **AA has no page (404) and Vals none**, and HokAI's independent ranking places its SWE-bench Verified at **#23/37 — *below* the peer median of 76.5%** — while DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 beats its Terminal-Bench (82.7% vs 62.9%) at a lower price. Its genuine wins are **cost** ($0.20/$0.02 cached/$1.20, a **73.3% cut** vs the predecessor; blended #23/88, cheaper than 75% of GA models; **free on-device calls**) and **Copilot-native reach** (nine surfaces, all plans). Net: **Overall 75 (divergence +2.4 vs the curated 72.6)** — a competent, very cheap, image-aware Copilot coder whose below-median, vendor-only evidence keeps it out of the leader tier.

## Model card

- **Name:** MAI-Code-1.1-Flash (vendor: **Microsoft AI**; MAI family, alongside the reasoning-focused **MAI-Thinking-1**).
- **Short description:** Microsoft's **small-tier agentic-coding** model for **GitHub Copilot** — a sparse MoE tuned for single-pass code generation, repository Q&A, refactoring and terminal-agent loops, with **native image input** (screenshots, diagrams, UI mockups) and **native function calling**. Explicitly a **cost/throughput workhorse**, not a frontier reasoner.
- **Provider / access:** **Proprietary** — **no open weights / no Hugging Face repo** (even the local build). Served through **GitHub Copilot** (VS Code, Visual Studio, JetBrains, Eclipse, Xcode, GitHub Mobile, Copilot CLI, cloud agent, Copilot Chat; Free→Enterprise, with Free/Student via auto-selection only and Business/Enterprise needing an admin policy that ships **off by default**) and **Microsoft Foundry on Azure**. **On-device** quantized build (~3.3 bits/weight, 53 GB; peaks 75.5 GB at 256K on a Surface Laptop Ultra / NVIDIA RTX Spark, 120 GB+ RAM) via Copilot's **Windows ML** provider. **Not** on OpenRouter, Zen, Bedrock, or Vertex.
- **Release / knowledge:** **2026-08-11** (1.1 refresh; predecessor 2026-06-02, retired 2026-09-10); on-device build **2026-10-07**. **Knowledge cutoff December 2025.** Current.
- **IDs:** `microsoft/mai-code-1.1-flash` (Copilot/Foundry); no HF id (proprietary).
- **Context window:** **256,000 total / 128,000 max output** (unchanged from the predecessor) — "holds an entire mid-size repository or a long multi-file diff."
- **Modalities:** **text, image, tool-calls in → text, code, tool-calls out.** **Native vision is new in 1.1** (the June MAI-Code-1-Flash was text-only). No audio/video.
- **Pricing:** **$0.20 in / $0.02 cached / $1.20 out per 1M** (GitHub Copilot + Microsoft Foundry + Azure retail, as of 2026-10-14) — a **73.3% cut** across all tiers vs MAI-Code-1-Flash's $0.75/$0.075/$4.50. Blended (3:1) **$0.45/M = #23/88** (cheaper than 75% of priced GA models; peer median $1.70). **On-device calls: $0.** `noFreeId: true` (no free hosted id; the free path is local inference).
- **Architecture:** Sparse **Mixture-of-Experts** — **138B total / 5B active** (Aug model card) *or* **137B / 6.8B active** (Oct-7 local post); on-device build uses mixed-precision ~3.3-bit quantization + **DFlash2 sliding-window speculative decoding** on a Windows ARM64 llama.cpp CUDA runtime. **No toggleable extended-reasoning mode** (adaptive/single-pass).

### Raw benchmarks found

> **Provenance:** every capability figure below is **Microsoft's own** (August model card + Oct-7 commandline.microsoft.com post) — i.e. **vendor-self-reported**. **Artificial Analysis has no page (404)** and **Vals AI none**, so there is **no independent harness re-run**. **HokAI** independently *ranks* the vendor numbers (it does not re-measure them) and labels both "vendor-reported."

- **Vendor — agentic coding (Microsoft model card):** **SWE-bench Verified 72.6%** (500 tasks; avg **8.6K tokens/solved task**); **Terminal-Bench 2.1 62.9%** (89 tasks; avg 17.0K tokens; run inside GitHub Copilot CLI). Microsoft reports a **+22% Terminal-Bench gain** over the June release, **+15% on .NET tasks**, and **~25% fewer tokens/completed task**.
- **Vendor — on-device quantized build (Microsoft, tested 2026-10-05):** SWE-bench Verified **70.80%**, Terminal-Bench 2.1 **66.29%** — vs **Unsloth GPT-OSS-120B GGUF** at **32.0% / 23.6%** on the same harness (the quantized build nearly matches the BF16 cloud variant at 80% smaller size).
- **Vendor — image-to-code (internal Microsoft tests):** Text2WebApp **74.1%**, ScreenShot2WebApp **42.1%** (the weak screenshot→code result caps the multimodal score).
- **Vendor peer context (Microsoft card's own runs):** MAI-Code-1-Flash (June) 71.6% / 51.7%; **Claude Haiku 4.5** 69.8% / 49.4%; **GPT-5.4-mini** 69.2% / 60.7% — so 1.1's SWE-bench lead over these is "a point or three," and the Terminal-Bench jump is the real gain.
- **Independent ranking (HokAI, aggregating the vendor score):** SWE-bench Verified **72.6% = #23/37** GA models publishing it, **below the peer median 76.5%**; blended price **#23/88**. HokAI con: **DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 posts 82.7% Terminal-Bench at a lower blended price**, so MAI "is not the strongest option on raw benchmarks alone."
- **Artificial Analysis / Vals AI:** **no entry** (`mai-code-1-1-flash` 404s) — no independent Intelligence Index.

> **Meta-verification:** the curated meta is **accurate** on the headline specs (138B/5B MoE, 256K/128K, text+image in, SWE-bench Verified 72.6%, Terminal-Bench 2.1 62.9%, $0.20/$1.20 + $0.02 cached, Copilot-centric, "not on Zen," "convenience pick rather than a benchmark leader"). **Three clarifications:** (1) **parameter discrepancy** — Microsoft's Aug card (138B/5B) vs its Oct-7 local post (**137B/6.8B**) disagree; the meta inherits the card's figures. (2) **native vision is new in 1.1** (the meta lists "image in" but not that the June predecessor lacked it) — and the only image evidence (Text2WebApp 74.1%, ScreenShot2WebApp 42.1%) is **internal**, with screenshot→code notably weak. (3) the model is **proprietary with no open weights** and **non-reasoning** (deliberate reasoning is offloaded to MAI-Thinking-1) — neither stated in the meta but both material to scoring. **Reattribution guard:** the predecessor **MAI-Code-1-Flash** (71.6% SWE-bench / 51.2% SWE-Pro, "beats Claude Haiku 4.5") is a **separate model/folder** (`../mai-code-1-flash/`); its numbers are **not** used here.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 76/100.** Purpose-built for agentic Copilot loops with **native function calling** and a respectable **Terminal-Bench 2.1 62.9%** (beats Haiku 4.5's 49.4% and GPT-5.4-mini's 60.7% in Microsoft's runs) — but that is the *only* agentic benchmark, it trails DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731's 82.7%, and the **non-reasoning single-pass** design limits complex multi-step planning.
- **Reasoning: 72/100.** **No extended-reasoning mode** (deliberate reasoning explicitly routed to MAI-Thinking-1), **no AA Intelligence Index (404)**, and **zero published analytic benchmarks** (no GPQA/AIME/HLE). It is a fast coder, not a reasoner — the weakest dimension.
- **Context window: 78/100.** A solid **256K / 128K-out** window that holds a mid-size repo in one pass; standard for the 256K tier, with **no independent long-context (RULER/MRCR) result** to push it higher.
- **Multimodal: 77/100.** **Native image input** (new in 1.1) reads screenshots/diagrams/UI mockups, evidenced only by **internal** Text2WebApp 74.1% and a weak **ScreenShot2WebApp 42.1%**; image-capable but not a dedicated VLM and not independently verified — just under the clean image-only tier. No audio/video.
- **Coding: 74/100.** Its raison d'être, but **mid-pack and vendor-only**: SWE-bench Verified **72.6% (#23/37, below the 76.5% peer median)**, Terminal-Bench 2.1 62.9% (below DeepSeek-V4-Flash's 82.7%). It beats its predecessor, Haiku 4.5 and GPT-5.4-mini, and adds +15% .NET, but no independent harness confirms it and it is not a leader.
- **Cost efficiency: 88/100.** *(Not counted in Overall.)* Outstanding value — **$0.20/$0.02 cached/$1.20** (a **73.3% cut** vs the predecessor), **blended #23/88 (cheaper than 75% of GA models)**, and **free on-device inference**; capped below the low-90s only by being **proprietary and Copilot/Foundry-locked** (no open weights, no self-host outside Copilot's Windows ML path).
- **Overall Score: 75/100.** Half-up mean of the five quality dims: (76 + 72 + 78 + 77 + 74) / 5 = 377 / 5 = **75.4 → 75**. **Divergence +2.4** vs the curated 72.6. MAI-Code-1.1-Flash is a cheap, fast, image-aware, Copilot-native agentic coder that clears its predecessor and budget peers, but the **non-reasoning design, below-median vendor-only SWE-bench, and absence of any independent (AA/Vals) verification** hold it to a mid-70s ceiling; my +2.4 reflects crediting its native vision, 256K context and best-in-class price slightly above the curated mean.
