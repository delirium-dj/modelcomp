> **Verdict:** Qwen3.5-4B is the dense ~**4.7B** (4.66B) member of Alibaba's **Qwen3.5 "Native Multimodal Agents"** family — released **2 March 2026** in the small-model wave (0.8B/2B/4B/9B), Apache 2.0, and natively multimodal (text + image + **video** via early fusion — there is no separate "-VL" variant). Its headline is that a 4.7B model "shouldn't be this good" on vision: **MMMU 77.6, VideoMME 83.5 (96% of the 27B's score), MathVision 74.6, We-Math 75.4, MLVU 82.8** — beating the previous-generation **Qwen3-VL-30B** (7.5× its size) on MMMU/MathVision/VideoMME — and it even **inverts on agents (τ²-Bench 79.9, above the 9B's 79.1)**. The architecture is a **3:1 Gated DeltaNet (linear) : Gated Attention (full) hybrid** (~75% linear layers) giving near-linear scaling, a **262K native context (YaRN-extendable past 1M)**, and edge-friendly memory (~5.5 GB at Q4, runs on an iPhone 15 Pro). But it is small: **coding is weak (LiveCodeBench 55.8)**, real-world visual Q&A is a soft spot (**SimpleVQA 43.4, below GPT-5-Nano's 46.0**), **thinking mode can loop**, and AA measures it **dead-last on speed (13.4 tok/s, #62/62)** in reasoning mode with only **1 API provider** (though self-host is free and a SiliconFlow free lane exists). Cost is excellent ($0.03/$0.15, blended $0.04). Scored Overall 70 (div +4.2 vs curated 65.8) — the verified multimodal/video excellence and agent inversion outweigh the small-model coding/reasoning limits.

## Model card

- **Name:** Qwen 3.5 4B (Qwen3.5-4B)
- **Short description:** A dense ~4.7B **native multimodal** (text + image + video) model from Alibaba's Qwen team — the "multimodal base for lightweight agents" in the Qwen3.5 small-model series. Exceptional vision/video for its size (beats last-gen Qwen3-VL-30B), hybrid thinking/non-thinking reasoning, 262K context, Apache 2.0, and small enough to run on a phone — but with limited coding and a slow reasoning-mode decode.
- **Provider / access:** Open weights on **Hugging Face** (`Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B`) and ModelScope under **Apache 2.0** (free commercial use, self-host); GGUF quantizations via Unsloth (Q3 ~4.5 GB, Q4 ~5.5 GB, Q6 ~7 GB, Q8 ~10 GB) — runs on llama.cpp/Ollama, an iPhone 15 Pro (8 GB) at Q4, or ~8 GB hardware. A **verified free hosted lane on SiliconFlow**; **1 paid API provider** per AA (median $0.03/$0.15); also served on Overshoot. Part of the OpenCode catalog as `opencode/qwen-3.5-4b`.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released **2 March 2026** (AA: "March 2026"; HF card updated 9 Mar 2026) as part of the three-week Qwen3.5 rollout — flagship 397B-A17B (16 Feb), mid-range 122B-A10B/35B-A3B/27B (24 Feb), small series 9B/4B/2B/0.8B (2 Mar). Knowledge cutoff not separately published for the 4B.
- **IDs:** id `opencode/qwen-3.5-4b`; HF `Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B`; AA `qwen3-5-4b` (profiles the **reasoning** variant; "a non-reasoning variant may also exist").
- **Context window:** **262,144 (262K) native**, **extensible past 1M (1,010,000) via YaRN**; **81,920 max output** (meta). The Gated DeltaNet hybrid keeps long-context memory pressure low (no ever-growing KV cache), which is how a 4.7B model reaches 262K natively and >1M extended on consumer hardware. AA lists 260–262k.
- **Modalities:** **Text + image + video input → text output** (native early-fusion multimodal, not a bolted-on vision adapter). **Reasoning: HYBRID** — a thinking mode (extended CoT; AA profiles this variant) and a non-thinking mode; **thinking mode can produce loops**, and non-thinking "may be more reliable for production" (overshoot). 201 languages.
- **Pricing:** **$0.03 / 1M input, $0.15 / 1M output** (AA median across providers; "very competitive" vs median $0.12/$0.30), **blended $0.04** (7:2:1). **Free** to self-host (Apache 2.0) and via the SiliconFlow free lane. TTFT 0.87s (competitive), but decode is slow in reasoning mode.
- **Architecture:** **Dense ~4.7B (4.66B)** causal LM — every parameter active (no MoE routing, unlike the 35B-A3B/122B-A10B/397B-A17B siblings). **Hybrid attention at a 3:1 ratio: ~75% Gated DeltaNet (linear, O(n)) layers + ~25% Gated/full Attention (O(n²)) layers** — Gated DeltaNet uses a decay gate (selective forgetting, Mamba2-like) + a delta rule (surgical KV updates) to avoid the retrieval degradation of earlier linear attention. Trained via **early fusion on trillions of multimodal tokens** (text/image/video from the start).

### Raw benchmarks found

> **Provenance:** Vision/video and agent numbers are **Qwen-vendor-reported**, reproduced via two full-read independent analyses — **overshoot.ai** (deployed all five small/mid models; per-model benchmark breakdown) and **enclaveai.app** (family guide) — plus the Qwen3.5 blog and HF/ModelScope cards. **Artificial Analysis** independently measures **Speed (13.4 tok/s, #62/62), TTFT (0.87s), and Cost ($0.03/$0.15)** but shows **Intelligence Index N/A** (v5.0 unscored) and "No publicly available API" for its eval charts. Text-reasoning figures (MMLU-Pro/GPQA/HMMT) below are from **apxml (secondary aggregator; page 403-blocked, snippet-only)** and are flagged lower-confidence. **Reattribution guards:** the 9B/27B/35B/397B family numbers are NOT credited to the 4B; the previous-gen Qwen3-VL/Qwen3-4B numbers are comparators only.

- **Agent / tool use:** **τ²-Bench (TAU2) 79.9** — an unusual **inversion where the 4B beats the 9B (79.1)** (overshoot: "the smaller model makes more decisive decisions"). Marketed as the "multimodal base for lightweight agents." No BFCL/SWE-bench/Terminal-Bench published for the 4B specifically (the 2B got BFCL-V4 43.6; the 27B got SWE-bench 72.4). Thinking-mode loops are an agentic-reliability risk. AA has no independent agent measurement.
- **Reasoning / knowledge:** Hybrid thinking mode; **visual math We-Math 75.4 (ties the 9B's 75.2 — "saturates early in the family")**. Text reasoning is **apxml-reported only (lower confidence): MMLU-Pro ~79.1, GPQA Diamond ~76.2, HMMT ~74/77** — strong for a 4.7B if accurate, but not corroborated by the fully-read overshoot/enclaveai pages (which detail the 9B's GPQA 81.7 / MMLU-Pro 82.5, not the 4B's). **Caveat: thinking mode can produce loops**; AA Intelligence Index N/A. Small-size general-knowledge limits apply.
- **Coding:** **Weak — LiveCodeBench 55.8** (overshoot: "coding is limited at this size"); no SWE-bench for the 4B. This is the clearest capability gap (the 9B reaches LiveCodeBench 65.6; the 27B SWE-bench 72.4). Expected for a 4.7B dense model.
- **Long context:** 262K native, YaRN-extendable past 1M, 81,920 max output — enabled by the Gated DeltaNet hybrid's near-linear scaling and low memory pressure. **Long-form video holds up: MLVU 82.8 (96.4% of the 27B), VideoMME 83.5 (96% of the 27B)** — "the difference between a 4B and a 27B is smaller than you'd expect." No RULER/MRCR text-retrieval number published for the 4B.
- **Speed / cost:** AA-measured **13.4 output tok/s (#62/62, 1 of 4 units — dead-last, "notably slow")** in reasoning mode, vs an 88.2 tok/s size-class median (~6.6× slower — the long thinking CoT + the single measured provider drive this); **TTFT 0.87s** (very competitive vs 1.11s median). **Cost $0.03/$0.15, blended $0.04** (very competitive), free self-host (Apache 2.0) + free SiliconFlow lane. Non-thinking mode would decode faster.
- **Vision / multimodal (the standout):** **MMMU 77.6** (beats prev-gen Qwen3-VL-30B's 76.0), **VideoMME 83.5** (vs Qwen3-VL-30B 79.9; ~1 pt below the 9B's 84.5), **MathVision 74.6** (vs Qwen3-VL-30B 65.7), **We-Math 75.4** (ties the 9B), **MLVU 82.8** (long video). A 4.7B model out-seeing a 30B predecessor "on the tasks that matter for real-time vision." **Weakness: SimpleVQA 43.4** (real-world visual Q&A trails even GPT-5-Nano's 46.0).

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 67/100.** A genuinely strong single agent result — **τ²-Bench 79.9, inverting above the 9B (79.1)** — and it is positioned as the "multimodal base for lightweight agents." Tempered because that is the only published agent benchmark for the 4B (no BFCL/SWE/Terminal-Bench), the model is small (4.7B), **thinking mode can loop** (an agentic-reliability risk), and AA has no independent agent measurement.
- **Reasoning: 65/100.** Hybrid thinking with verified strong **visual math (We-Math 75.4, tying the 9B)** and apxml-reported text reasoning (MMLU-Pro ~79.1, GPQA Diamond ~76.2, HMMT ~74/77) that would be impressive for a 4.7B — but those text figures are lower-confidence (secondary, uncorroborated by the fully-read sources), the **AA Intelligence Index is N/A**, **thinking mode can produce loops**, and small-size general-knowledge limits apply.
- **Context window: 78/100.** A large **262K native window, YaRN-extendable past 1M, with 81,920 max output** — remarkable for a 4.7B model and made practical by the Gated DeltaNet hybrid's near-linear, low-memory long-context scaling (long-form video MLVU 82.8 = 96.4% of the 27B). Tempered because >1M is an extension (not native), no RULER/MRCR text-retrieval number is published, and the small model can't fully exploit huge contexts on complex reasoning.
- **Multimodal: 82/100.** The standout dimension — **native text + image + video** (early fusion, no adapter) with **verified vision scores that beat the previous-gen Qwen3-VL-30B (7.5× its size): MMMU 77.6, VideoMME 83.5 (96% of the 27B), MathVision 74.6, We-Math 75.4, MLVU 82.8.** Video understanding is "the family's superpower" and barely degrades at 4B. Held below the very top only by the **SimpleVQA 43.4 weakness** (real-world visual Q&A trails GPT-5-Nano) and the absence of a broader independent vision audit.
- **Coding: 58/100.** The clearest gap — **LiveCodeBench 55.8** ("coding is limited at this size"), no SWE-bench, against the 9B's 65.6 and the 27B's SWE-bench 72.4. Adequate for light code assist in agentic/vision workflows, but not a coding model.
- **Cost efficiency: 92/100.** Outstanding — **$0.03/$0.15 per 1M (blended $0.04)**, rated "very competitive" by AA vs a $0.12/$0.30 median, plus **free Apache 2.0 self-hosting** that runs on ~8 GB / an iPhone and a free SiliconFlow lane. Only the slow reasoning-mode decode (13.4 tok/s) slightly offsets the value. (Scored but NOT counted toward Overall.)
- **Overall Score: 70/100.** Half-up mean of the five quality dims (67+65+78+82+58)/5 = 70.0 → 70. Divergence **+4.2** vs the curated queue value 65.8 — a sizable positive gap: the curated view appears to weight the small 4.7B size, weak coding (LiveCodeBench 55.8), dead-last AA speed (13.4 tok/s), and single API provider more heavily, whereas the dimension-mean credits the **exceptional, verified native-multimodal/vision performance (MMMU 77.6, VideoMME 83.5 — beating a 30B predecessor), the τ²-Bench agent inversion above the 9B, the large 262K/>1M context, and Apache-2.0 free/edge-friendly deployment**. Sits just below the mid-tier open models scored this run (deepseek-v3.2 at 67 is lower; hy3/longcat-2.0/glm-5.1 at 72 are higher on reasoning/coding breadth) — the 4B's multimodal excellence is offset by its coding/reasoning size limits. Cost efficiency excluded from the mean per methodology.
