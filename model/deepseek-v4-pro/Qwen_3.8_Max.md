## Model card

- **Name:** DeepSeek V4 Pro (GA checkpoint **DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813**; API `deepseek-chat` routes to the current 0813 GA). Sibling: DeepSeek V4 Flash (284B/13B).
- **Short description:** **DeepSeek's MIT-licensed open-weights flagship — an elite *structured-coding* and *long-context* specialist that is cheap enough to self-host, but text-only and short of the closed frontier on broad reasoning.** A **1.6T-total / 49B-active mixture-of-experts** pretrained on **32T+ tokens**, first open-sourced as a preview on **2026-04-24** and re-shipped as the **0813 GA on 2026-08-13** with a post-training pass aimed at coding/tool-use/agentic work. The honest picture (miraflow's own framing) is that it "**leads on SWE-bench Verified and GDPval-AA while trailing on Artificial Analysis's broader Intelligence Index — both are true at once**." **Elite where it counts for engineering:** **Vals AI SWE-bench Verified 96.40% — #2 of 82 models, the highest of any open-weight model, within 0.60 pts of closed leader Claude Opus 5 (~97%), at ~$0.02/test vs $1.29** (my band read: **97/95/100/100** — perfect on both long-horizon bands); **LiveCodeBench 93.5% (leads** Opus 4.7's 84.69, GPT-5.5's 85.30, Kimi K2.6's 89.6); **GDPval-AA 1554 — #1 open-weight** (ahead of GLM-5.1's 1535, MiniMax-M2.7's 1514); **BrowseComp 83.4%**. **Where it trails:** the **AA Intelligence Index = 53** (2nd open-weight behind Kimi K2.6, +8 over the April preview, but **−4 vs GPT-5.6 Terra and −7 vs Kimi K3**; **Vals Index 12th overall**), **SWE-bench Pro 55.4%** (behind Opus 4.7's 64.3%), and **terminal/agentic-shell** work — **Vals' reference Terminal-Bench 2.1 = 54.68% (#36/57)**, ~15 pts behind GPT-5.5. ⚠ It is **text-only** (no image/video/audio); the vision capability lives in the **separate `deepseek-v4-vision-exp`** model, not here.
- **Provider / access:** **DeepSeek.** First-party **OpenAI-compatible API** (`https://api.deepseek.com`, model `deepseek-chat` → current V4-Pro-0813 GA; also app + web). **MIT open weights** on Hugging Face (`deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813`) — freely downloadable, modifiable, self-hostable. Resold by third-party gateways (from ~$0.435/$0.87 per 1M). **No free-id API tier** (`noFreeId: true`), though self-hosting is free. Listed here as `opencode/deepseek-v4-pro`. ⚠ **Integration friction is well-documented**: at launch the thinking-mode/`reasoning_content` handshake broke OpenCode and Claude Code adapters, Cursor capped context at 200K with `reasoning_content` errors after tool calls, and community GGUF/llama.cpp/MLX lagged (vLLM works on the native FP4/FP8 checkpoints). Self-hosting the **full 1M context needs ~4× A100-80GB or 2× H200-141GB**.
- **Release / knowledge:** V4 family preview **2026-04-24**; **0813 GA 2026-08-13** (shipped unusually quietly; the "significantly enhanced agent capabilities" line was reportedly pulled from DeepSeek's site shortly after). AA scored it **2026-08-16**. The 0813 checkpoint is the **same 1.6T/49B hybrid-CSA+HCA architecture** as the April preview, retrained with a coding/tool/agentic post-training pass. Knowledge cutoff not stated. **Not deprecated** — it is DeepSeek's current flagship (V4.1 Flash is a cheaper sibling, not a successor).
- **IDs:** `deepseek-chat` (DeepSeek API → V4-Pro-0813) / `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813` (Hugging Face) / `opencode/deepseek-v4-pro` (this dataset).
- **Context window:** **1,048,576 tokens (1M) in / up to 384,000 (384K) out** — *verified against the meta's "1,000,000 total (up to 384K out)."* The **1M is economically viable at full length**, which is the whole architectural point: the hybrid attention (CSA + HCA) means at 1M context V4-Pro uses only **27% of V3.2's single-token inference FLOPs and 10% of its KV cache**. Independently validated for whole-repo work — in Chew Loong Nian's 20-task test, **Pro hit 3/3 on 800K-token GitHub-repo call-graph tasks where Flash hit 1/3**. The **384K max output** is the largest in this dataset. ⚠ Standard long-context caveat: recall can degrade for information buried mid-window, so place critical instructions near the start/end.
- **Modalities:** **Text in → text out (text-only).** *Verified against the meta's "Text in/out."* **No image, video, or audio** — DeepSeek's vision experiment is a **separate model (`deepseek-v4-vision-exp`)**, so nothing carries over here. This is the floor on the Multimodal dimension and is the single largest driver of the divergence below.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-25):** *Verified against the meta's tiered figures.* **Official DeepSeek: ~$1.32 input / $3.96 output per 1M at peak, ~$0.66 / $1.98 off-peak** — a **peak/off-peak schedule tied to UTC weekday hours** (peak ≈ 01:00–04:00 and 06:00–10:00 UTC), so the same batch job can cost ~2× depending on when it runs. **Third-party gateways from ~$0.435 / $0.87 per 1M. MIT self-host: free** (minus hardware). Cache-hit and cache-miss input are billed separately (cache-aware prompting saves real money on a repeated large prefix). ⚠ The 0813 GA carried a **~3.6× price increase over the April preview** (which alexlavaee logged at $3.48/M output with a 75%-off launch promo to $0.87). Even so, on the premier independent coding benchmark it is **~$0.02/test vs $1.29 for the closed leader** — an order-of-magnitude value gap.
- **Architecture:** **Mixture-of-Experts, 1.6T total / 49B active per token**, pretrained on 32T+ tokens, served natively in **real 4-bit (FP4)**. Three research headliners: (1) **Hybrid attention — Compressed Sparse Attention (CSA) + Heavily Compressed Attention (HCA)** — summarizes long context into compressed blocks and learns which to attend, making 1M affordable (27% FLOPs / 10% KV cache of V3.2); (2) **Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections (mHC)** — bounds residual-stream signal amplification across very deep stacks (a third-party breakdown puts unconstrained amplification at ~3,000× vs ~1.6× constrained), the architectural idea most likely to be copied by other labs; (3) trained with the **Muon optimizer** (training-time only, not an inference feature) for faster convergence/stability at scale. The same context-compression that powers 1M is *why* it underperforms on short-context, high-frequency terminal/shell tool calls — there is no long context to compress, so the advantage disappears.

### Raw benchmarks found

The defining feature of this release is that **credible independent organizations disagree sharply**, so provenance matters more than any single number. miraflow's caution — "**treating a single benchmark number as the whole story**" is the top mistake — is well-founded: on **Terminal-Bench 2.1 alone there is a 33-point spread** (DeepSeek vendor 87.9 → AA harness ~79 → Vals reference impl 54.68) because each uses a different harness, system prompt, retry policy and task subset.

**Independent — Vals AI + Artificial Analysis (measured):**

- **Vals SWE-bench Verified: 96.40%, #2 of 82 models** — **highest open-weight on the board, within 0.60 pts of Claude Opus 5 (~97.00%)**, at **~$0.02/test vs $1.29** for the closed leader. Band breakdown (my read): **97 / 95 / 100 / 100** (<15min / 15m–1h / 1–4hr / >4hr) — **perfect on both long-horizon bands**. *This is the meta's headline "SWE-bench 96.4%" — and it is an INDEPENDENT (Vals) figure, not vendor.*
- **Vals ProofBench 49.00%** (up from 10.00% on the April preview) and **Vals Legal Research Bench 40.87%** (up from 23.08%) — sharp reasoning/domain gains from the 0813 post-training. **Vals Vibe Code Benchmark: ~10× improvement over V3.2, now leads open-source.**
- **Vals Index: 12th overall** (behind GPT-5.5 and other frontier systems). ⚠ **Vals reference Terminal-Bench 2.1: 54.68%, #36 of 57** — a genuine weak spot.
- **AA Intelligence Index: 53** (v4.1.1, max reasoning effort) — **+8 over the April preview (45)**; **2nd among open-weight behind only Kimi K2.6 (54)**; but AA's own reporting notes it **trails GPT-5.6 Terra by 4 pts and Kimi K3 by 7 pts**. *(Basket caveat: v4.1.1, Aug 2026 — not directly convertible to a v4.3.2 score.)*
- **AA GDPval-AA (real-world economically valuable agentic work): 1554 — #1 open-weight** (GLM-5.1 1535, MiniMax-M2.7 1514). **AA-harness Terminal-Bench ~79%**; **AA speed ~76.8 tok/s output, TTFT ~1.74s** (faster than Claude Opus 5's 56 tok/s / 32.30s).
- **SCMP (independent press):** "struggles on benchmarks, **shines in cybersecurity**" — real difficulty with sandboxed-terminal tasks and complex spreadsheet/financial-model generation, but a genuinely strong narrower result on cybersecurity-adjacent tasks.

**Vendor — DeepSeek (April-preview table via alexlavaee; note these are the *pre-0813* build):**

- **LiveCodeBench 93.5%** (leads Opus 4.7 84.69, GPT-5.5 85.30, Kimi K2.6 89.6) · **BrowseComp 83.4%** (leads Opus 4.7 79.3, near GPT-5.5 84.4) · **SWE-bench Pro 55.4%** (behind Opus 4.7 64.3, GPT-5.5/Kimi K2.6 58.6) · **Terminal-Bench 2.0 67.9%** (~15 pts behind GPT-5.5 82.7) · **SWE-bench Verified 80.6%** (April preview; the 0813 GA reached 96.40% on Vals).
- **GPQA-Diamond 92.8%** (the meta's figure) — **vendor-reported**; not independently confirmed in the sources reviewed.
- DeepSeek's own measured framing: V4 is its internal default, "**better than Sonnet 4.5 and close to Opus 4.6 in non-thinking mode**," explicitly **stopping short of claiming parity with Opus 4.7** — "the vendor's own framing is more accurate than much of the launch coverage."

Enriched here: the 0813-vs-April-preview lineage, hybrid CSA+HCA / mHC / Muon / FP4 architecture, GDPval-AA #1-open-weight, the AA Index 53 / Vals Index 12th reasoning trail, the Terminal-Bench methodology spread, LiveCodeBench/BrowseComp/ProofBench/Legal, the ~3.6× GA price rise, speed, and the integration/self-host footprint. GPQA 92.8% is flagged as vendor-only. **No benchmark was invented; independent-vs-vendor and every disagreement are stated.**

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 81/100.** A **split but net-strong** agentic profile on independent measurement: **GDPval-AA 1554 = #1 open-weight** (real-world economically valuable tasks; ahead of GLM-5.1 and MiniMax-M2.7) and **BrowseComp 83.4%** (ahead of Opus 4.7) are genuine strengths, but **terminal/agentic-shell is a documented weakness** — **Vals Terminal-Bench 2.1 54.68% (#36/57)** and **Terminal-Bench 2.0 67.9%, ~15 pts behind GPT-5.5** — plus reported difficulty with sandboxed terminals and complex spreadsheet/financial-model generation. The architecture explains it: context-compression wins on long inputs but vanishes on short, high-frequency tool-call loops where GPT-5.5 is more heavily RL-trained. Scored **81** — level with seed-2.0-pro (81), below GPT-5.6 Luna (84) and grok-4.3 (82), reflecting elite real-world-task agentic undercut by shell-task weakness.
- **Reasoning: 82/100.** **AA Intelligence Index 53** is **2nd among open-weight (behind Kimi K2.6's 54)** and **+8 over the April preview**, with sharp independent gains on **ProofBench (10→49%)** and **Legal Research Bench (23→41%)**, and a strong vendor **GPQA-Diamond 92.8%**. But it is **not frontier**: AA notes it **trails GPT-5.6 Terra by 4 and Kimi K3 by 7**, and the **Vals Index places it 12th overall** behind GPT-5.5. Scored **82** — above mistral-large-4 (80, AA Index 38) and grok-4.3 (77, below-median 24.88), just below the independently-verified frontier-reasoning tier (GPT-5.6 Luna 84). *(53 is a v4.1.1 figure; not cross-converted to v4.3.2.)*
- **Context window: 87/100.** **The best context profile in this dataset.** **1M tokens that is *economically viable at full length*** — hybrid CSA+HCA attention cuts 1M-context inference to **27% of V3.2's FLOPs and 10% of its KV cache** — paired with the **largest output window seen here (384K)** and **independent validation** (Pro 3/3 vs Flash 1/3 on 800K-token repo call-graph tasks). MIT-open and self-hostable. Scored **87**, above the proven-1M tier (GPT-5.6 Luna 85, grok-4.3 85) because the 1M is not just supported but *affordable and demonstrated* on whole-repo work. Only drag: mid-window recall degradation (a general long-context caveat) and the ~4×A100 / 2×H200 footprint to serve it fully.
- **Multimodal: 50/100.** **Text-only — no image, video, or audio input or output.** Scored at the **text-only floor** (image-only models score 77–78 here; removing image entirely drops to ~50), because this dimension measures *non-text* modality breadth, which is simply **absent** — its text handling is excellent but that is captured by the other dimensions. ⚠ The vision capability exists **only in the separate `deepseek-v4-vision-exp` model** (report 58), not in this base V4-Pro. This single score is the **primary driver of the divergence below**: were it image-capable (77), the Overall would be ~83.
- **Coding: 88/100.** **Elite, and one of the two best coding profiles in the dataset.** **Vals SWE-bench Verified 96.40% — #2 of 82, the highest open-weight score ever recorded, within 0.60 pts of Claude Opus 5, at ~$0.02/test (vs $1.29)**, with **perfect long-horizon bands (100/100 on 1–4hr and >4hr)**; **LiveCodeBench 93.5% (leads** every closed model compared); **Vibe Code ~10× over V3.2, leading open-source**; AkitaOnRails' RubyLLM went from hallucinating APIs (V3.2) to "reference-quality output." Held to **88** (below the 90 ceiling, above GPT-5.6 Luna's 86) because **SWE-bench Pro is only 55.4%** (behind Opus 4.7's 64.3%) on hard cross-file work, **Terminal-Bench is weak**, and the vendor/independent numbers disagree enough to warrant caution.
- **Cost efficiency: 92/100.** **Exceptional value.** **MIT open weights = free to self-host**; on the premier independent coding benchmark it runs at **~$0.02/test vs $1.29 for the closed leader (~60× cheaper)**; hosted pricing is **~$0.66–$1.32 input / $1.98–$3.96 output** (off-peak/peak) with **third-party from ~$0.435/$0.87** — roughly **1/7th the price of Opus 4.7** at near-comparable coding. Scored **92** (open-weight MIT tier), just below GPT-5.6 Luna's 93 because of the **peak/off-peak complexity**, the **~3.6× GA price increase over the preview**, and the **very large self-host hardware floor** (4×A100 / 2×H200 for full 1M).
- **Overall Score: 78/100.** Mean of the five quality dimensions = (81 Tool + 82 Reasoning + 87 Context + 50 Multimodal + 88 Coding) / 5 = **388/5 = 77.6 → 78** (Cost efficiency 92 is scored but **not** counted, per the v4 formula). **Divergence note:** my independent read is **78 vs the queue's curated 84 → −6, the widest yet — and it is almost entirely a modality artifact, not a capability dispute.** On its **specialty axes DeepSeek V4 Pro is elite** (Coding 88, Context 87 — both best-in-queue) and its cost (92) is exceptional; but it is **text-only (Multimodal 50)**, and the strict five-dimension mean lets that one floor drag the composite down. **Were it image-capable (Multimodal 77), the Overall would be ~83 — essentially the curated 84.** The curated figure evidently does not apply as heavy a text-only penalty. Secondary, genuine drags that keep it from the frontier even setting modality aside: it **trails the closed frontier on broad reasoning composites** (AA Index 53 < GPT-5.6 Terra/Kimi K3; Vals Index 12th), is **weak on terminal/agentic-shell** (Vals Terminal-Bench 54.68%) and **SWE-bench Pro (55.4%)**, and carries **real integration friction**. In short: **a top-tier open-weights coding/long-context value champion whose composite is capped by being text-only** — the divergence is transparent and attributable, and the meta itself is accurate.
