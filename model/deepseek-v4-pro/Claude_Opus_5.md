## Model card

- **Name:** DeepSeek V4 Pro. **Open weights.** API model name `deepseek-v4-pro`; vendor model version **DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813**, which is the ID used by every benchmark harness below. **Supports both non-thinking and thinking modes, with thinking as the default.**
- **Short description:** DeepSeek's flagship, and on the coding evidence **the strongest repository-engineering model found anywhere in this research pass.** A composite tracker places it **#38 of 784 at 63.86/100** across an unusually deep **60 of 649 benchmarks**. Four results stand out. **SWE-bench at 96.4% from Vals AI is the single highest coding figure recorded in this entire research pass** — ahead of Grok 4.6 (95.6%), GLM-5.3 (95.4%) and GPT-5.6 Luna (93.0%). **Codeforces at 3,206 Elo** is superhuman competitive programming. **GPQA clears 90% under three separate measurements, and both independent figures (92.8% AA, 92.4% Vals) are higher than DeepSeek's own 90.1%** — the reverse of the usual vendor-inflation pattern and a strong credibility signal. And **ARC Prize verifies ARC-AGI-1 at 90.00%**, the highest ARC-AGI-1 result in this pass. Two hard limits: **vision is explicitly not supported**, and **Terminal-Bench 2.1 falls from DeepSeek's 87.9% to 54.7% at Vals AI — a 33.2-point gap, the widest Terminal-Bench divergence found anywhere in this research pass.**
- **Provider / access:** DeepSeek API, served on **both an OpenAI-format base URL (`https://api.deepseek.com`) and an Anthropic-format one (`https://api.deepseek.com/anthropic`)**. Features confirmed on DeepSeek's own pricing page: **JSON output, tool calls, Responses API, Anthropic API, chat prefix completion (beta)** all supported; **FIM completion (beta) in non-thinking mode only**. First-party agent integrations documented for **DeepSeek Harness, Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, OpenClaw, Hermes, Reasonix, WorkBuddy/CodeBuddy and Qoder**. Weights published at `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Pro` on Hugging Face alongside the DeepSeek-V4 technical report PDF.
- **Release / knowledge:** The `0813` version tag implies 13 August. **No verified knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `deepseek-v4-pro`. **Related retirement finding, relevant to sibling folders in this repository:** DeepSeek's docs state that the legacy names **`deepseek-v4-flash` and `deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp` are still accepted but the corresponding models have been retired**, with requests served by **DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash** and billed at the Flash price.
- **Context window:** **1,000,000 tokens, confirmed on DeepSeek's own pricing page**, with **maximum output 384K** — the largest max-output figure found in this research pass. Unusually, the long-context claim is backed by measurement *at* the claimed length: **MRCR 1M at 83.5%** and **CorpusQA 1M at 62.0%**, both from the DeepSeek-V4 technical report, plus **AA-LCR at 80.3%**. Open weights, so no vendor repricing threshold applies.
- **Modalities:** **Text in → text out. Text-only, confirmed by primary vendor documentation**, which lists **"Vision: Not supported"** for `deepseek-v4-pro` while listing **"Vision: ✓"** for the sibling `deepseek-flash`. This is the strongest possible modality evidence — an explicit vendor denial, not an inference from absent benchmarks. **If your workload touches images, the cheaper Flash tier is the only DeepSeek option.**
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03, from DeepSeek's own Models & Pricing page):** per 1M tokens, with **off-peak rates at exactly half of peak**. **Peak hours are 01:00–04:00 and 06:00–10:00 UTC, Monday–Friday, excluding Chinese public holidays — roughly 35 of 168 hours per week, so off-peak is the normal case.**
  - **Input, cache miss: $0.66 off-peak / $1.32 peak**
  - **Output: $1.98 off-peak / $3.96 peak**
  - **Input, cache hit: $0.022 off-peak / $0.044 peak** — roughly **one-thirtieth** of the cache-miss rate, and the cheapest cache tier found anywhere in this research pass.
  - **Concurrency limit: 500** (against `deepseek-flash`'s 2,500).
- **Architecture:** **Open weights**, published on Hugging Face with a full technical report. Parameter count and topology: not stated in the sources read.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 84/100.** Genuinely strong on structured tool use and better-evidenced than most: **τ²-bench at 96.2% is the highest in this research pass** and clears the Tau-family reference by a wide margin, **MCP Atlas at 73.6% is one of the few MCP measurements available anywhere in this pass**, **BrowseComp at 83.4% and CyberGym at 83.3%** are both strong, and **the AA Agentic Index of 49.6% is second-highest here**. Three deductions keep it out of the 90s. **The Terminal-Bench 2.1 vendor-to-independent gap is 33.2 points — 87.9% from DeepSeek against 54.7% from Vals AI — the widest such divergence in this entire research pass**, which makes the headline terminal-agent claim unusable without heavy discounting. **GDPval-AA at 1,306 Elo is 444 points below the ~1750+ reference and the lowest in this pass**, so economically valuable real-world task performance is weak even as benchmark tool-calling is excellent. And **AutomationBench (31.8%), Agents' Last Exam (25.7%) and APEX-Agents-AA (24.3%) all collapse** on long-horizon open-ended work, with **Claw-Eval, OSWorld and Terminal-Bench 3.0 unreported**.
- **Reasoning: 91/100.** The best-corroborated reasoning evidence base in this research pass. **ARC Prize verifies ARC-AGI-1 at 90.00% — the highest in this pass — and ARC-AGI-2 at 61.3%, third-highest.** **GPQA Diamond clears the 90%+ reference under three measurements, and crucially both independent figures (92.8% and 92.4%) exceed DeepSeek's own 90.1%** — vendor under-reporting is rare and lends credibility to the entire table. **HLE clears the 40%+ reference twice over (42.7% vendor, 41.0% independent, agreeing within 1.7 points)**, with HLE-with-tools at 60.0%. Competition mathematics is exceptional — **HMMT Feb 2026 95.2%, IMOAnswerBench 89.8%, Apex Shortlist 90.2%** — and **AA-IFBench at 76.5% is the highest instruction-following figure in this pass by roughly 35 points**. Long context is measured *at* length: **MRCR 1M 83.5%, CorpusQA 1M 62.0%, AA-LCR 80.3%**. Four deductions: **the Intelligence Index of 53.2% is 6.8 points below the 60+ marker**; **Apex falls 51.9 points from shortlist (90.2%) to full (38.3%)**, marking the research-difficulty wall; **CritPt is 18.0%**; and **the 94.1% hallucination rate is among the worst here** — the positive Omniscience Index and pass-leading 49.1% accuracy mean it knows more than its peers, but it still asserts confidently when it does not know. **No FrontierMath figure exists.**
- **Context window: 97/100. This is set above the ≥1M tier floor, and the justification is that the window is measured rather than merely claimed.** **1,000,000 tokens is confirmed on DeepSeek's own pricing page**, with **384K maximum output — the largest found in this research pass** — and, uniquely among the models assessed here, **retrieval and reasoning are measured at the full claimed length: MRCR 1M at 83.5% and CorpusQA 1M at 62.0%**, with **AA-LCR at 80.3%** corroborating. Three further structural advantages: **open weights mean no vendor repricing threshold**, **cache-hit input costs $0.022 per 1M tokens** so long-context reuse is effectively free, and both OpenAI- and Anthropic-format endpoints accept the full window. It stops short of 100 because the methodology's top condition requires **≥98% retrieval at 512K+** and MRCR 1M at 83.5% does not meet that, while **CorpusQA 1M at 62.0% shows real degradation** on harder long-context work.
- **Multimodal: 15/100.** **DeepSeek's own documentation states "Vision: Not supported"** for this model, listing it beside a sibling (`deepseek-flash`) that *does* carry "Vision: ✓" — an explicit primary-source denial, which is the strongest modality evidence obtainable and leaves no interpretive room. That caps it in the methodology's "text-only = 10–20" band regardless of any other merit, and **no MMMU, CharXiv, document, OCR, video or audio benchmark exists**. It is placed low within the band rather than at its top because there is no visual capability of any kind to credit: **Design Arena Website at 1,258 Elo is explicitly excluded**, since it measures generated web-design quality through code rather than any multimodal input, and it is in any case below GLM-5.3's 1,308 on the same leaderboard. **For image work the cheaper DeepSeek Flash tier is the only option in this family.**
- **Coding: 90/100.** The strongest coding evidence in this research pass, and the best figures are independent. **SWE-bench at 96.4% from Vals AI is the highest coding result recorded anywhere here**, ahead of Grok 4.6, GLM-5.3 and GPT-5.6 Luna. **Codeforces at 3,206 Elo is superhuman competitive programming**; **LiveCodeBench reaches 93.5% vendor and 87.5% independent**; **SWE-bench Verified is 80.6%**, **SWE Multilingual 76.2%**, **DSBench-FullStack 71.1%** and **DSBench-Hard 67.2%**. Four deductions prevent a higher score, and together they are substantial. **The AA Coding Index at 68.8% misses the methodology's 70%+ reference by 1.2 points** — notable given the SWE-bench headline, and a sign the strength is concentrated in patch generation rather than breadth. **DeepSWE at 62.7% is 11.3 points below the 74%+ reference.** **AA-SciCode at 51.0% misses its reference by 4 points and Vibe Code Bench reaches only 49.93%.** And **the Terminal-Bench 2.1 figure that would clear the coding reference (87.9%) is contradicted by 54.7% at Vals**, so terminal-driven coding cannot be credited at the vendor level.
- **Cost efficiency: 94/100.** Exceptional, and — unusually for this research pass — **primary-sourced rather than inferred from aggregators.** Off-peak rates of **$0.66 input / $1.98 output are roughly 4.5× and 7.6× cheaper than the methodology's $3/$15 anchor**, and **off-peak is the normal case**: peak covers only 01:00–04:00 and 06:00–10:00 UTC on weekdays excluding Chinese public holidays, about 35 of 168 hours per week, and even peak ($1.32/$3.96) undercuts the anchor by 2.3× and 3.8×. **Cache hits cost $0.022–$0.044 per 1M input tokens — roughly one-thirtieth of cache-miss and the cheapest cache tier found anywhere in this pass** — which makes repeated long-context work nearly free. **Maximum output is 384K.** **The weights are open**, so the rate card is escapable entirely. Migration cost is near zero: **both OpenAI- and Anthropic-format base URLs**, plus documented first-party integrations for Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, OpenClaw, Hermes, Reasonix, WorkBuddy/CodeBuddy, Qoder and DeepSeek's own harness. For that money you get **the highest SWE-bench figure in this pass (96.4%)**, Codeforces 3,206, ARC-AGI-1 90.00%, GPQA above 90% three times over, HLE above 40% twice, MRCR 1M at 83.5% and the pass-leading AA-IFBench. Three deductions: **the concurrency limit is 500 against the Flash tier's 2,500**, which constrains high-throughput deployment; **peak-hour billing doubles costs during the Asian business morning**; and **the 94.1% hallucination rate imposes a verification cost** the rate card does not show.
- **Overall Score: 75.4/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (84 + 91 + 97 + 15 + 90) / 5 = 75.4 — and as with GLM-5.3, **the mean understates this model for text work, because one categorical limitation costs it roughly ten points of Overall.** On text, it is arguably the best-value frontier model in this entire research pass. **SWE-bench 96.4% (Vals) is the highest coding figure recorded anywhere here**; **Codeforces 3,206 Elo is superhuman**; **ARC-AGI-1 at 90.00% (ARC Prize verified) is the highest in this pass**; **GPQA clears 90% three times over with both independent figures exceeding the vendor's own**; **HLE clears 40% twice**; **HMMT 95.2%, IMOAnswerBench 89.8% and Apex Shortlist 90.2%** are exceptional mathematics; **AA-IFBench 76.5% leads this pass by ~35 points**; **MRCR 1M 83.5% and CorpusQA 1M 62.0% prove the million-token window at its claimed length with 384K output**; **τ²-bench 96.2% leads this pass**; and all of it costs **$0.66/$1.98 off-peak with $0.022 cache hits**, under open weights, on both OpenAI- and Anthropic-format endpoints. Choose it for **repository-scale coding, competitive programming, mathematics, long-context text analysis and structured tool use at minimum cost.** Four cautions govern adoption. **Vision is explicitly not supported** — a primary-source denial, with the Flash tier the only image-capable option in this family. **The Terminal-Bench headline is not credible**: 87.9% vendor against 54.7% at Vals is a 33.2-point gap, the widest in this pass. **Economically valuable real-world agentic work is its weakest axis** — GDPval-AA 1,306 Elo is the lowest here, 444 below reference, and AutomationBench, Agents' Last Exam and APEX-Agents all sit in the 24–32% range. And **it hallucinates at 94.1%** despite leading this pass on raw knowledge accuracy, so it must be paired with verification for any factual claim.
