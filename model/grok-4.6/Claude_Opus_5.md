## Model card

- **Name:** Grok 4.6. Proprietary. Official model documentation at `docs.x.ai/developers/grok-4-6`. Variant/alias flag: the Grok 4.x line is crowded — **Grok 4.7** (its successor), **Grok 4.5**, **Grok 4.3**, **Grok 4.20** and several Fast/Mini variants all exist as separate entries.
- **Short description:** xAI's Grok 4.6, described in third-party coverage as a **1.5-trillion-parameter model that matches GPT-5.6 Sol at half the price**. It is one of the strongest models in this research pass on measured capability, and three of its figures are the best recorded here: **SWE-bench at 95.6% (Vals AI, independent)**, **an Artificial Analysis Agentic Index of 53.4%**, and **AA-GPQA Diamond at 94.9%, corroborated by Vals AI at 94.7%**. It also records **ARC Prize-verified ARC-AGI-2 at 67.1%**, second only to Claude Opus 4.8's 72.1% among verified results. Against all that sits one genuinely alarming number: **a Gray Swan indirect prompt-injection attack-success rate of 51.8%**, by far the worst security figure in this dataset.
- **Provider / access:** xAI's own API, OpenRouter (`x-ai/grok-4.6`) and third-party hosts. Reasoning: explicit reasoning mode.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released in the Grok 4.6 cycle, succeeding Grok 4.5 and preceding Grok 4.7. **No verified public release date or knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `grok-4-6`; `x-ai/grok-4.6` (OpenRouter).
- **Context window:** **500,000 tokens** — the top of the methodology's 200K–500K tier, and larger than every Claude and most OpenAI models here, though half the 1M that Google, Alibaba and Moonshot now offer. Max output: no verified figure found. **AA-LCR at 80.3%** is the long-context measurement.
- **Modalities:** **No modality specification was retrievable and no multimodal benchmark exists for this model** beyond a Design Arena website-generation Elo. Text in and text out are certain. The Grok line is generally vision-capable, so image input is probable, but it is **unverified for this model** and must be checked against the live API.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **No rate card was verified.** Third-party coverage characterises Grok 4.6 as matching **GPT-5.6 Sol at half the price** — Sol's documented rate being $4/$20, implying roughly **$2/$10** — and Artificial Analysis separately measured the predecessor **Grok 4.5 at $0.37 per Intelligence Index task, the cheapest in its intelligence cluster**. **Re-verify against xAI's live pricing before budgeting.**
- **Architecture:** Proprietary, closed weights. Third-party coverage cites **1.5 trillion parameters**; active parameters, topology and whether it is dense or MoE are not disclosed in the sources reached.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 89/100.** The strongest independently measured agentic profile in this research pass. **The AA Agentic Index of 53.4% is the highest recorded here**, **AA AutomationBench at 66.7% is likewise the highest**, **AA Tau3 Banking at 50.7% clears the methodology's ~50%+ reference** — a benchmark almost no model in this dataset reports at all — and **GDPval-AA at 1,643 Elo** is among the better figures here. Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 78.3% (Vals) and CWE-bench v1 at 57.0% corroborate. Four deductions hold it to 89: **GDPval remains ~107 Elo short of the ~1750+ reference**; **Terminal-Bench 3.0 collapses to 26.5%** and **ApprenticeBench is 13%**, so the harder agentic harnesses expose real limits; **Claw-Eval, MCP-Atlas, Toolathlon and OSWorld are all unreported**; and the **51.8% Gray Swan prompt-injection success rate** is an operational disqualifier for any agent that ingests untrusted content, which is most of them.
- **Reasoning: 92/100.** Exceptional, and unusually well corroborated. **AA-GPQA Diamond at 94.9% with Vals AI at 94.7% is the highest GPQA pair in this entire research pass, agreeing within 0.2 points across two independent labs** — no vendor figure required. **ARC Prize's verified ARC-AGI-2 at 67.1% is the second-highest verified score here**, on a benchmark built to resist memorisation, with ARC-AGI-1 at 87.00% alongside. **AA-HLE at 42.9% clears the 40%+ reference**, AA-LCR reaches 80.3%, MMLU-Pro is independently confirmed at 89.4%, and **the AA-Omniscience profile is genuinely good — a 34.3% hallucination rate against 48.2% accuracy and a 30.5% composite index** is among the most reliable in this dataset. Three deductions: the **Intelligence Index of 44.3% is 16 points below the 60+ frontier marker**; **ARC-AGI-3 at 2.1% is a floor result**; and **CritPt at 17.1%** is only mid-range.
- **Context window: 84/100.** **500,000 tokens sits exactly at the top of the methodology's 200K–500K tier (65–84)**, and it is scored at the band ceiling because **AA-LCR at 80.3%** demonstrates the window is usable rather than merely advertised. Two things prevent any higher placement: the tier is defined by size and 500K is half the 1M that Google, Alibaba, Moonshot and Z.AI now offer at this standing; and **no MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks retrieval measurement exists**, with max output tokens also undocumented.
- **Multimodal: 62/100.** **No multimodal benchmark of any kind exists for this model** — no MMMU, MMMU-Pro, CharXiv, document, OCR, video or audio figure — and **no modality specification was retrievable**. Text in and text out are certain; image input is probable on Grok-line precedent but unverified. The only adjacent datapoint is **Design Arena Website at 1,298 Elo**, which is a respectable generated-website quality score but measures output design rather than visual understanding. 62 sits near the floor of the methodology's "+image in" band to reflect a capability that probably exists on family lineage and is entirely undemonstrated. **Verify vision against the live API before relying on it.**
- **Coding: 91/100.** The best-evidenced coding profile in this research pass, and almost all of it independent. **SWE-bench at 95.6% from Vals AI is the highest figure recorded anywhere here**, **the AA Coding Index at 76.8% is also the highest and clears the methodology's 70%+ reference by 6.8 points**, **AA-SciCode at 56.5% clears the 55%+ reference** — which few models manage — **LiveCodeBench reaches 88.2%**, **CursorBench 3.2 at 70.8% is the highest recorded**, and VulcanBench v3 adds 87.0% from a third independent leaderboard. Three deductions: **DeepSWE at 65.9% is roughly 8 points below the 74%+ frontier reference**; **CursorBench falls 29.4 points from v3.2 to v4.0 (41.4%)**; and **FrontierSWE v2 at 25.3%** is low in absolute terms, though it is the best figure any model in this dataset posts on that leaderboard. Note the SWE-bench figure is Vals' harness, not SWE-bench Verified.
- **Cost efficiency: 84/100.** **This score carries a caveat: no rate card was verified.** The case rests on two pieces of third-party evidence — coverage describing Grok 4.6 as **matching GPT-5.6 Sol at half the price**, which implies roughly **$2/$10** against Sol's documented $4/$20, and Artificial Analysis's measurement that the predecessor **Grok 4.5 cost $0.37 per Intelligence Index task, the cheapest in its intelligence cluster**. If the implied rate holds, a model with the best SWE-bench, best AA Coding Index, best AA Agentic Index and best GPQA pair in this dataset at ~$2/$10 is outstanding value. Three deductions: the **price is unconfirmed** and must be re-verified; **no free tier** is documented; and the **51.8% prompt-injection success rate imposes a real engineering cost** — sandboxing, scoped credentials and output review are mandatory rather than optional, and that is spend the rate card does not show.
- **Overall Score: 83.6/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (89 + 92 + 84 + 62 + 91) / 5 = 83.6 — a model with four best-in-dataset credentials and one disqualifying security weakness. On capability it is close to the top of this research pass: **SWE-bench 95.6%, AA Coding Index 76.8%, AA Agentic Index 53.4%, AA AutomationBench 66.7% and GPQA Diamond at 94.9%/94.7% are all the highest figures recorded here**, with ARC Prize-verified ARC-AGI-2 at 67.1% second only to Claude Opus 4.8 and a genuinely good 34.3% hallucination rate. Choose it for **repository-scale and algorithmic coding**, **autonomous business automation**, **graduate-level science**, and **novel abstract reasoning** — on published evidence it leads or near-leads all four. Three things must govern adoption. **Do not deploy it as an agent over untrusted content without hard isolation**: a 51.8% Gray Swan indirect-injection success rate is roughly 50x Claude Opus 5.5's and 74x Gemini 4 Argon's, and that is the single most consequential number on this page. **Do not assume vision works** — no modality specification and no multimodal benchmark exist. And **confirm the price**, because the entire value argument rests on third-party characterisation rather than a published rate card. Also note the harder-harness pattern: Terminal-Bench 3.0 (26.5%), CursorBench 4.0 (41.4%), FrontierSWE v2 (25.3%) and ARC-AGI-3 (2.1%) all fall sharply, so the headline results should be read as upper bounds on the current benchmark generation rather than as durable capability.
