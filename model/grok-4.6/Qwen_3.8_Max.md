## Model card

- **Name:** Grok 4.6
- **Short description:** **xAI's flagship and an incremental (supplemental-training) upgrade to Grok 4.5** — not a new base model — released **2026-08-12** with a particular focus on **long-running agents and more ambitious interactive/visual work**. It is the first Grok release to land **within striking distance of GPT-5.6 and Claude Opus 5 on independent boards**: on the **Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index it reaches ~60–61, tying GPT-5.6 Sol for #3** (just behind Opus 5 and GPT-5.6) — a ~4–5-point jump over Grok 4.5 — and it **tops GDPval** (professional/economic knowledge work) and the **Harvey legal bench (15.8%** vs GPT-5.6 Sol 2.5% / Opus 5 11.3%). Its other headline is **cost**: at **~$0.83/task** it delivers near-frontier intelligence for a fraction of what Opus 5 charges. ⚠ Results are **mixed by benchmark** — it trails GPT-5.6 Sol Max (73%) and Opus 5 (70%) on **DeepSWE (65.9%, 3rd)**, sits **6th on the private Vals AI Index**, is **verbose** (far more output tokens, aggressive self-checking), has only **500K context**, and **image-only** multimodality with a noted **visual-design-polish weakness**. Superseded within weeks by Grok 4.7. **[2026-11-02: AA confirms DEPRECATION ("SpaceXAI launched Grok 4.7"), composite Index N/A with a residual default-10k cell of 44.3, speed 69.6 tok/s #129/195 (slow), spec text+image→text / 500K re-confirmed verbatim.]**
- **Provider / access:** **xAI** (branded "SpaceXAI" in the Cursor co-release). Available in **Cursor** and **Grok Build** (2× included usage for launch week), plus the **xAI API** and partners **OpenRouter, Vercel, Cloudflare**. ⚠ meta.json's stated tradeoff: **access flows largely through partner surfaces** rather than a broad first-party API. **No free tier** (`noFreeId`). Listed here as `xai/grok-4.6`. Launched alongside **Grok Bot**, an always-on agent platform (cloud VMs, auto-spawned sub-agents, teach-by-walkthrough) aimed at non-developer knowledge workers.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released **2026-08-12**. Built on the **same ~1.5T-parameter foundation as Grok 4.5** via a **longer supplemental training run** — curated model-generated reasoning/technical data, high-quality engineering data (much sourced through **xAI's acquisition of Cursor**), an improved optimizer/recipe, **Grok-4.5-regenerated SFT trajectories** (filtered by model-based checks), then **agentic RL** across knowledge work, general coding, kernel optimization, web development and CAD. Knowledge cutoff not stated in the sources reviewed (Grok 4.7 docs list a May-2026 cutoff).
- **IDs:** `grok-4.6` / `xai/grok-4.6` (xAI line: Grok 4.5 → **Grok 4.6** → Grok 4.7 → [Grok 5 reportedly targeted before end-2026]). A **fast variant** ships at 2× price for latency-sensitive work.
- **Context window:** **500,000 (500K) tokens.** Solid but **below the 1M leaders** (GPT-5.4 1.05M, MiniMax M3 1M/512K, Inkling-Small 1M-claimed). Pricing **doubles above a 200K prompt** ($4/$12, $1 cached), so very-long-context use carries a steep surcharge.
- **Modalities:** **Text + image in → text out.** Tuned for "more ambitious interactive and visual work" — Cursor reports **stronger first passes on visual/interactive projects** than Grok 4.5 (establishing structure + visual language in one pass). ⚠ Independent hands-on testing found **visual-design polish a weakness** (layout/spacing issues, a cut-off button vs GPT-5.6 Sol's cleaner output); no audio, no video, text-only output, and no published vision benchmark (MMMU/CharXiv) in the sources reviewed.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-23):** **$2 / $6 per 1M in/out**, **$0.50 cached** for prompts <200K; **$4 / $12, $1 cached** for prompts >200K; **fast variant at 2×**. **Mid-frontier pricing** — roughly **half** the per-token cost of comparable OpenAI/Anthropic frontier models, and **~$0.83/task** on AA's cost chart (vs Grok 4.5 high ~$0.36 at a lower index; one 3D-scene task ~$0.38 vs ~$2 for Opus 5). The "cheap-and-smart" quadrant is its core selling point.
- **Architecture:** **~1.5T-parameter** foundation (shared with Grok 4.5; undisclosed active count), reasoning model with **variable effort** ("Grok 4.6 high" in evals). Recursive self-improvement (Grok 4.5 generated its SFT data). Trained on xAI's Colossus GPU capacity; the Cursor acquisition supplies the coding-data flywheel.
