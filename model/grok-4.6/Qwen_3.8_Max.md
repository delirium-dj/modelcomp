## Model card

- **Name:** Grok 4.6
- **Short description:** **xAI's flagship and an incremental (supplemental-training) upgrade to Grok 4.5** — not a new base model — released **2026-08-12** with a particular focus on **long-running agents and more ambitious interactive/visual work**. It is the first Grok release to land **within striking distance of GPT-5.6 and Claude Opus 5 on independent boards**: on the **Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index it reaches ~60–61, tying GPT-5.6 Sol for #3** (just behind Opus 5 and GPT-5.6) — a ~4–5-point jump over Grok 4.5 — and it **tops GDPval** (professional/economic knowledge work) and the **Harvey legal bench (15.8%** vs GPT-5.6 Sol 2.5% / Opus 5 11.3%). Its other headline is **cost**: at **~$0.83/task** it delivers near-frontier intelligence for a fraction of what Opus 5 charges. ⚠ Results are **mixed by benchmark** — it trails GPT-5.6 Sol Max (73%) and Opus 5 (70%) on **DeepSWE (65.9%, 3rd)**, sits **6th on the private Vals AI Index**, is **verbose** (far more output tokens, aggressive self-checking), has only **500K context**, and **image-only** multimodality with a noted **visual-design-polish weakness**. Superseded within weeks by Grok 4.7.
- **Provider / access:** **xAI** (branded "SpaceXAI" in the Cursor co-release). Available in **Cursor** and **Grok Build** (2× included usage for launch week), plus the **xAI API** and partners **OpenRouter, Vercel, Cloudflare**. ⚠ meta.json's stated tradeoff: **access flows largely through partner surfaces** rather than a broad first-party API. **No free tier** (`noFreeId`). Listed here as `xai/grok-4.6`. Launched alongside **Grok Bot**, an always-on agent platform (cloud VMs, auto-spawned sub-agents, teach-by-walkthrough) aimed at non-developer knowledge workers.
- **Release / knowledge:** Released **2026-08-12**. Built on the **same ~1.5T-parameter foundation as Grok 4.5** via a **longer supplemental training run** — curated model-generated reasoning/technical data, high-quality engineering data (much sourced through **xAI's acquisition of Cursor**), an improved optimizer/recipe, **Grok-4.5-regenerated SFT trajectories** (filtered by model-based checks), then **agentic RL** across knowledge work, general coding, kernel optimization, web development and CAD. Knowledge cutoff not stated in the sources reviewed (Grok 4.7 docs list a May-2026 cutoff).
- **IDs:** `grok-4.6` / `xai/grok-4.6` (xAI line: Grok 4.5 → **Grok 4.6** → Grok 4.7 → [Grok 5 reportedly targeted before end-2026]). A **fast variant** ships at 2× price for latency-sensitive work.
- **Context window:** **500,000 (500K) tokens.** Solid but **below the 1M leaders** (GPT-5.4 1.05M, MiniMax M3 1M/512K, Inkling-Small 1M-claimed). Pricing **doubles above a 200K prompt** ($4/$12, $1 cached), so very-long-context use carries a steep surcharge.
- **Modalities:** **Text + image in → text out.** Tuned for "more ambitious interactive and visual work" — Cursor reports **stronger first passes on visual/interactive projects** than Grok 4.5 (establishing structure + visual language in one pass). ⚠ Independent hands-on testing found **visual-design polish a weakness** (layout/spacing issues, a cut-off button vs GPT-5.6 Sol's cleaner output); no audio, no video, text-only output, and no published vision benchmark (MMMU/CharXiv) in the sources reviewed.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-23):** **$2 / $6 per 1M in/out**, **$0.50 cached** for prompts <200K; **$4 / $12, $1 cached** for prompts >200K; **fast variant at 2×**. **Mid-frontier pricing** — roughly **half** the per-token cost of comparable OpenAI/Anthropic frontier models, and **~$0.83/task** on AA's cost chart (vs Grok 4.5 high ~$0.36 at a lower index; one 3D-scene task ~$0.38 vs ~$2 for Opus 5). The "cheap-and-smart" quadrant is its core selling point.
- **Architecture:** **~1.5T-parameter** foundation (shared with Grok 4.5; undisclosed active count), reasoning model with **variable effort** ("Grok 4.6 high" in evals). Recursive self-improvement (Grok 4.5 generated its SFT data). Trained on xAI's Colossus GPU capacity; the Cursor acquisition supplies the coding-data flywheel.

### Raw benchmarks found

Intelligence / reasoning (**independent** Artificial Analysis + private Vals AI; else xAI-reported):

- **AA Intelligence Index: ~60–61** (independent 9-benchmark composite) — **ties GPT-5.6 Sol for #3**, just behind Claude Opus 5 (#1) and GPT-5.6 (#2); ~4–5 points above Grok 4.5. ⚠ AA Index is **basket-specific and non-convertible** across versions.
- **GPQA: 94.9%** (meta.json / xAI-reported) — frontier-level science reasoning
- **GDPval: top score** (xAI-reported; "Grok 4.6 high" ahead of GPT-5.6 Sol and Opus 5 Max) — real economic/professional knowledge work
- **Harvey legal bench: 15.8%** (vs GPT-5.6 Sol 2.5%, Opus 5 11.3%) — a standout legal result
- **Vals AI Index (private, independent): 6th** — behind Gemini 3, GPT-5.6 Sol and Meta's Muse Spark 1.2 (a tempering signal vs the AA tie)

Coding / agentic (mixed provenance):

- **Terminal-Bench 2.1: 88.4%** (meta.json / xAI-reported) — a **leading** terminal-agentic result (well above GPT-5.4's TB 2.0 75.1%); terminal-based agentic tasks reportedly jumped from ~15% (Grok 4.5) to ~26% on a harder subset
- **DeepSWE: 65.9% (3rd)** — behind GPT-5.6 Sol Max 73% and Opus 5 70%; reviewers treat DeepSWE as a closer proxy for day-to-day coding "feel"
- **Cursor Bench 3.2: just behind Opus 5 Max** (Cursor-internal coding benchmark)
- Strong real-world hands-on: built a working Portal-style physics puzzle clone and a Pokémon Red emulator + harness in single extended sessions; more **self-testing/verification** on long trajectories than Grok 4.5

> Provenance: The **AA Intelligence Index (~60–61, tying GPT-5.6 Sol), GDPval and cost-per-task (~$0.83) are independent Artificial Analysis** figures — corroborated across the **official Cursor/xAI co-release**, mindstudio.ai and LinkedIn — and are weighted highest (with the standard **basket-version non-convertibility** caveat). Release/pricing/context/modality/training from the official xAI/Cursor announcement, kingy.ai, datanorth.ai, eigent.ai and cellcog.ai. ### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 87/100.** A genuine strength — **Terminal-Bench 2.1 88.4%** (a leading terminal-agentic result), an explicit **long-running-agent** design focus (stays with complex multi-step tasks; more self-testing/verification on long trajectories), **agentic RL across kernel optimization, web dev and CAD**, and the **Grok Bot** always-on agent platform (cloud VMs + sub-agents). Held below GPT-5.4's 88 because GPT-5.4 owns the **#1 OSWorld-Verified computer-use** result and a broader measured agent stack, while Grok 4.6's **DeepSWE is only 3rd (65.9%)** and its GUI/computer-use grounding is not independently established here.
- **Reasoning: 87/100.** Frontier-tier and **independently corroborated** — **AA Intelligence Index ~60–61 tying GPT-5.6 Sol (#3 behind Opus 5 / GPT-5.6)**, **GPQA 94.9%**, a **top GDPval** score and a standout **Harvey legal 15.8%**. Capped at 87 (not higher) because it is **#3, not #1** on the AA composite, sits **6th on the private Vals AI Index**, and the AA figure is basket-specific/non-convertible — a strong frontier reasoner that trails Opus 5 / GPT-5.6 / Gemini 3 at the very top.
- **Context window: 83/100.** **500K tokens** — ample for most agentic/knowledge-work sessions but **below the 1M leaders** (GPT-5.4 1.05M, MiniMax M3 1M/512K-guaranteed, Inkling-Small 1M-claimed), and the **price doubles above a 200K prompt**, which discourages routinely filling the window. Scored below the 85–89 long-context leaders accordingly.
- **Multimodal: 77/100.** **Text + image in → text out**, tuned for "ambitious interactive and visual work" with **stronger visual/interactive first passes than Grok 4.5** (Cursor). Held to 77 (between claude-sonnet-5.4's 76 and GPT-5.4's 80) because there is **no audio or video, no published vision benchmark** (MMMU/CharXiv) to verify quality, and independent hands-on testing flagged a **visual-design-polish weakness** (layout/spacing issues, cut-off elements).
- **Coding: 86/100.** Strong agentic coding — **Terminal-Bench 2.1 88.4%** (leading), **Cursor Bench 3.2 just behind Opus 5 Max**, and convincing real-world builds (Portal-physics clone, Pokémon emulator) in single sessions. Discounted to 86 because **DeepSWE is 3rd (65.9%, behind GPT-5.6 Sol Max 73% / Opus 5 70%)**, the model is **verbose** (far more output tokens, aggressive double-checking — a token-efficiency drag even at a low per-token rate), and reviewers note "benchmark scores and lived coding experience don't always match."
- **Cost efficiency: 90/100.** The standout commercial argument — **$2/$6 ($0.50 cached)** at **~$0.83/task**, delivering **near-frontier intelligence (AA ~60–61) for roughly half** the cost of GPT-5.6/Opus 5 (one 3D-scene task ~$0.38 vs ~$2 for Opus 5), landing in AA's "cheap-and-smart" upper-left quadrant. Tempered by the **>200K price doubling**, the **fast-variant 2× premium**, **no free tier**, and verbosity that inflates token counts. Scored but **not** counted in the Overall per methodology.
- **Overall Score: 84/100.** Mean of Tool 87 + Reasoning 87 + Context 83 + Multimodal 77 + Coding 86 = 420/5 = 84.0 → 84. Best fit: **long-running, cost-sensitive agentic coding and professional knowledge work** — marathon research/analysis/codebase sessions, legal and economic knowledge work (GDPval/Harvey strengths) and terminal-heavy agents where near-frontier intelligence at ~$0.83/task and $2/$6 pricing is decisive. **Not** the pick for precise visual-design polish, audio/video, very-long (>500K) contexts, or absolute peak coding (DeepSWE trails GPT-5.6 Sol Max / Opus 5). Independent read: my **84 sits ~2.3 below the queue's curated 86.3** — the gap driven by the **500K context**, **image-only multimodality with a design-polish caveat**, **DeepSWE 3rd / Vals AI 6th**, verbosity and its **superseded** status, weighed against genuinely frontier **independent** reasoning (AA ~60–61 tie), a leading Terminal-Bench 2.1 and excellent cost. Internally consistent: Grok 4.6 (84) lands **just below GPT-5.4 (85)** — GPT-5.4's larger 1.05M context, stronger measured vision (MMMU Pro 82.1%) and #1 OSWorld computer use outweigh Grok 4.6's edge in raw reasoning/terminal scores — and alongside the strong open-weight generalists (inkling-small, ember-1, mimo-v2.6-free, minimax-m3 at 84), an accurate portrait of a frontier-intelligence value flagship whose 5-dimension mean is pulled down by context and multimodal limits rather than by reasoning or coding.
