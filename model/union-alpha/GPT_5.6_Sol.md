# Union Alpha — Independent Research Report

## Summary and evidence

Union Alpha is an anonymous preview model exposed through OpenCode-compatible routes. Public listings describe a **256K context window**, image input, tool calling, structured output, and OpenAI-compatible access, but no verified developer, architecture, weights, or reproducible benchmark card is available. Claims that it blends several models remain unverified.

Sources: [Union Alpha product listing](https://union-alpha.org/), [OpenCode setup listing](https://unionalpha.io/how-to-use-union-alpha.html)

## Cost and limitations

The OpenCode preview was free, while subsequent routes have shown changing commercial terms. Anonymous routing prevents reliable privacy, stability, safety, and capability assessment.

## Bottom line

Union Alpha is suitable for experimentation, not high-confidence production selection until its provider and evaluations are disclosed.
