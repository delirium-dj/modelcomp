## Model card

- **Name:** Inkling Small (Inkling-Small)
- **Short description:** **Thinking Machines Lab's (Mira Murati's startup) efficient open-weights sibling of Inkling** — a **276B-total / 12B-active Mixture-of-Experts** transformer (trained on NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 systems) released **2026-07-30** under **Apache 2.0**. Its thesis is *"comparable performance to Inkling at a quarter of its size"*: it **matches or beats the 975B/41B-active Inkling on reasoning and agentic coding** (HLE text-only 31.6% vs 29.7%; SWE-bench Verified 80.2% vs 77.6%) while trailing it on **knowledge coverage and factuality** (SimpleQA Verified 20.6% vs 43.9%). Like Inkling it is **natively multimodal (encoder-free text + image + audio)** with a **controllable "thinking effort" dial** (minimal → xhigh) and a **claimed 1M-token context** (commonly served at 64K/256K). A genuinely broad, balanced open-weight generalist — strong coding/reasoning efficiency, competitive multimodality, weak pure knowledge — and, importantly, **most of its headline reasoning/agentic numbers come from independent harnesses** (Artificial Analysis, Scale AI, ARC Prize, Forecasting Research Institute), not just vendor runs.
- **Provider / access:** **Thinking Machines Lab**. **Full open weights** on Hugging Face (`thinkingmachines/Inkling-Small`, plus NVFP4 variants for Blackwell-class hardware) under **Apache 2.0**; self-host via SGLang / vLLM / TokenSpeed / Unsloth / HF stacks. Hosted API + fine-tuning + text/image/audio chat on **Tinker** (Tinker Playground). Listed here as `opencode/inkling-small`.
- **Release / knowledge:** Full weights released **2026-07-30** (an earlier **Inkling-Small (preview)** checkpoint was shown alongside Inkling on **2026-07-15**). Began training *after* Inkling, letting the lab revise the pre-training data mix/ML recipe; post-trained partly via **on-policy distillation with Inkling as teacher**, then **~2 more weeks of agentic-coding RL**. Knowledge cutoff not stated in the sources reviewed.
- **IDs:** `inkling-small` / `opencode/inkling-small` / `thinkingmachines/Inkling-Small` (Thinking Machines line: **Inkling** 975B/41B → **Inkling-Small** 276B/12B).
- **Context window:** **Up to 1,000,000 (1M) tokens claimed**; in practice **Tinker commonly serves 64K / 256K**, and the lab's own coding evals use a **256K max-token trajectory limit**. ⚠ The **served context is a fraction of the claimed window** (meta.json's stated tradeoff) — plan deployments around ~256K, not 1M.
- **Modalities:** **Text + image + audio in → text out.** Uses the **same natively multimodal encoder-free architecture as Inkling**: audio as **dMel spectrograms**, images as **40×40-pixel patches** through a **four-layer hMLP**, both lightly embedded and processed jointly with text tokens. Improved **Python-assisted visual** work (crop/zoom/programmatic inspection of small chart text). No video input; text-only output (understanding, not generation).
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-23):** **Open weights — free to self-host (Apache 2.0)**; on **Tinker**, Inkling-Small output is **$1.20 / 1M tokens** (vs Inkling $4.05/1M), with a limited-time launch discount. Self-hosting still needs **server-class infrastructure** (276B total, even at 12B active — "not a 7B laptop default"). The **12B-active** design is the efficiency lever: cheap per-token compute for long reasoning traces.
- **Architecture:** **276B-total / 12B-active sparse MoE** transformer, trained on **NVIDIA GB300 NVL72**. **Encoder-free native multimodality** (dMel audio spectrograms; 40×40 image patches via 4-layer hMLP; joint text/image/audio token stream). **Variable "thinking effort"** (minimal → xhigh; benchmarks at effort 0.99, temp 1.0) traces a performance-compute curve so users can trade cost vs capability per task. Post-training combined **on-policy distillation from the Inkling teacher** with extended **agentic-coding RL**.

### Raw benchmarks found

Reasoning / knowledge (mostly **independent** — Artificial Analysis, ARC Prize, Forecasting Research Institute; effort 0.99):

- **AA Intelligence Index v4.1: 40.0%** — near Inkling's 41.0%, above MiMo V2.5 (37.0) / Minimax M2.7 (38.0) / Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite (36.0), below GPT-5.6 Luna (49.0). ⚠ This is the **v4.1 basket** — **not convertible** to the current v4.3 frontier scores (Gemini 3.8 Flash 59, Opus 5.5 58) cited elsewhere in this dataset.
- **GPQA Diamond: 89.5%** (AA) — top of the compared table (ties GPT-5.6 Luna; > Inkling 87.2%)
- **HLE (Humanity's Last Exam): 31.6% text-only** (AA; > Inkling 29.7%), **47.8% with tools** (> Inkling 46.0%)
- **ARC-AGI-1: 84.0% / ARC-AGI-2: 40.1%** (ARC Prize; > Inkling 79.5% / 36.5%) — strong abstract reasoning for open-weight
- **AIME 2026: 95.5%; HMMT Feb 2026: 90.2%** — strong competition math
- **IFBench: 82.2%** (instruction following — **best in the compared table**, > Inkling 79.8%, GPT-5.6 Luna 67.3%); **CritPt: 8.3%**
- **ForecastBench (no search) Brier Index: 61.3** (Forecasting Research Institute; > Inkling 60.1, GPT-5.5 59.3) — a calibration/epistemics strength
- ⚠ **Factuality gap:** **SimpleQA Verified 20.6%** (vs Inkling 43.9%) and **AA Omniscience index −9.0** (vs Inkling 2.1) — the honest weak spot; do **not** treat Small as a knowledge-base replacement for Inkling.

Coding / agentic (**vendor bash-only / internal harness** for the Inkling family unless noted; contamination zeros applied):

- **SWE-bench Verified: 80.2%** (> Inkling 77.6%, Qwen3.5-397B 76.4%, ≈ Minimax M2.7 79.9%; below GPT-5.6 Luna 93.0%). ⚠ **meta.json's "SWE-bench 82.2%" is a conflation — 82.2% is Inkling-Small's *IFBench* score; the official lab table reports SWE-bench Verified = 80.2%.**
- **SWE-bench Pro (public): 55.9%** (> Inkling 54.3%; ≈ MiMo V2.5 56.1% / Minimax M2.7 56.2%; below GPT-5.6 Luna 62.7%)
- **Terminal-Bench 2.1 (best harness): 64.7%** (internal harness; > Inkling 63.8%, MiMo V2.5 63.7%; below GPT-5.6 Luna 82.5%)
- **SciCode: 48.7%** (AA; > Inkling 46.1%)
- **MCP Atlas public/all: 79.6 / 79.2%** (Scale AI — **strong**, top-3 of the compared table ≈ Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite 79.8/76.8%)
- **GDPval-AA v2: 1269 Elo** (AA; > Inkling 1238; below MiniMax M3 1390.8, GLM 5.2 1514, GPT-5.6 Luna 1530)
- **Toolathlon Verified: 54.4%; AA-Briefcase: 917 Elo; BrowseComp (w/ context mgmt): 77.4%; τ³-Banking: 15.5%** (weak — below Inkling 23.7%, GLM 5.2 26.8%)

Multimodal (AA / Scale AI; effort 0.99):

- **Vision — MMMU Pro (Standard 10): 74.0%** (AA; below Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite 79.0%, Qwen3.5-397B 77.3%, ≈ Inkling 73.5%); **CharXiv RQ original / with Python: 77.4 / 81.3%** (Python-assisted visual lift)
- **Audio — Audio MC 54.9%** (Scale AI), **MMAU 77.0%**, **VoiceBench 90.1%** — **near specialist omni models** (cf. Qwen3.5-Omni-Plus MMAU 81.1 / VoiceBench 92.4), a real open-weight differentiator

Safety: **StrongREJECT 98.4%**; **FORTRESS adversarial 71.6% / benign 96.9%** (adversarial softer than Inkling's 78.0%; benign answering stays high).

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 85/100.** Genuinely strong, independently-measured agentic tool use: **MCP Atlas 79.6/79.2% (Scale AI — top-3 of the compared table)**, Terminal-Bench 2.1 64.7% (best open-weight harness result here, > Inkling), Toolathlon Verified 54.4%, BrowseComp 77.4%, and it "runs smoothly across a variety of coding and agent harnesses." Held below the closed frontier (GPT-5.6 Luna TB 2.1 82.5%) and capped by weak spots — **τ³-Banking 15.5%** and AA-Briefcase 917 (below MiniMax M3 1107.8, GLM 5.2 1266). The 12B-active efficiency makes it a cost-effective tool-use workhorse.
- **Reasoning: 84/100.** A real strength on **independent** boards — **GPQA Diamond 89.5%** (top of table), **HLE 31.6% text / 47.8% tools** (beats Inkling), **ARC-AGI-2 40.1%** (strong for open-weight), AIME 95.5%, **IFBench 82.2%** (best in class) and ForecastBench 61.3 (calibration). Capped by the **AA Index v4.1 40.0%** (mid-pack, well below the v4.3 frontier) and, above all, the **large factuality gap** — **SimpleQA Verified 20.6% and AA Omniscience −9.0** — so it reasons well but *knows* less than Inkling and the closed leaders.
- **Context window: 85/100.** **1M-token context claimed** with the encoder-free architecture, but the **practical served window is ~256K** (Tinker commonly 64K/256K; the lab's own coding evals cap trajectories at 256K) — meta.json's explicit tradeoff. Scored with the other 1M-claimed models but **discounted below MiniMax M3 (89**, which guarantees a 512K floor + 512K output) because the served ceiling is a quarter of the claim and no independent long-context-recall figure was published.
- **Multimodal: 82/100.** **Native text + image + audio** input (encoder-free) → text out. **Audio is a standout** for open-weight — MMAU 77.0%, VoiceBench 90.1%, Audio MC 54.9% (Scale AI), near specialist omni models — and Python-assisted vision (CharXiv +Python 81.3%) is a genuine agent-friendly lift. Vision proper is mid-pack (**MMMU Pro 74.0%**, below Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite 79.0%). Held at 82 (with the omnimodal muse-wonder/mimo) despite **no video and no generative output**, because the audio quality is strong and independently measured.
- **Coding: 85/100.** Strong open-weight coding — **SWE-bench Verified 80.2%** (> Inkling 77.6%), **SWE-bench Pro 55.9%**, **Terminal-Bench 2.1 64.7%**, SciCode 48.7% — landing in the same band as minimax-m3 (SWE-V 80.5% / SWE-Pro 59%) and below the closed frontier (GPT-5.6 Luna 93.0%). Discounted a notch below ember-1 (89) / mimo-v2.6-free (88) because those post higher SWE-V (92%+) and because Inkling-Small's coding numbers rest on the lab's **bash-only/internal harness** (contamination zeros applied), not a neutral board.
- **Cost efficiency: 90/100.** Excellent value for the capability band — **Apache 2.0 open weights** (more permissive than MiniMax/Xiaomi community licenses), free self-host, **12B-active** compute that makes long reasoning traces cheap, and **$1.20/1M output on Tinker** (vs Inkling $4.05/1M). explainx frames it as the pick for "max coding/agent TFLOP efficiency." Tempered by the **serious inference infrastructure** a 276B-total model still needs and the young ecosystem ("fewer deployment rails than the established open families"). Scored but **not** counted in the Overall per methodology.
- **Overall Score: 84/100.** Mean of Tool 85 + Reasoning 84 + Context 85 + Multimodal 82 + Coding 85 = 421/5 = 84.2 → 84. Best fit: **cost-efficient agentic coding and multimodal (esp. audio) tool-use** where a fine-tunable, Apache-2.0 open-weight model with a controllable effort dial and independent-harness-verified reasoning shines — and **not** knowledge-heavy/factuality-critical work (route that to full Inkling or a search-augmented path; the SimpleQA 20.6% / Omniscience −9.0 gap is the tell). Independent read: my **84 sits ~2.4 below the queue's curated 86.4**, consistent with how the other strong open-weight generalists scored here (ember-1 84 vs 86.7, mimo-v2.6-free 84 vs 86.7, minimax-m3 84 vs 86.6) — the gap reflecting the **large factuality deficit**, the **served-context discount (256K vs 1M claimed)**, the **bash-only coding harness**, and the **AA Index v4.1 40.0%** well below the v4.3 frontier, weighed against genuinely strong independent-harness reasoning, class-best instruction following, standout open-weight audio and Apache-2.0 efficiency. Internally consistent: Inkling-Small (84) clusters with the open-weight value plays (ember-1, mimo-v2.6-free, minimax-m3 at 84), above the preview/gated specialists step-5-preview (82) and gemini-3.8-flash-cyber (80) — an accurate portrait of a broad, efficient, independently-verified open-weight generalist whose mean is capped by a real knowledge gap and a served-window discount.
