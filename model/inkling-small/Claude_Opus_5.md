## Model card

- **Name:** Inkling-Small. **Open weights.** **Reasoning type: Hybrid** — one of very few hybrid-reasoning models in this research pass. Variant: `small`, sibling of **Inkling**.
- **Short description:** Thinking Machines Lab's smaller model, and **it beats its own larger sibling**: a composite tracker places **Inkling-Small at #62 of 882 with 54.5/100 against Inkling's 53.3**. Within the family, the small model is the better one on measured aggregate — an inversion worth knowing before paying for the larger. Its profile is sharply specialised, and this repository's curated description — "strong math and knowledge, weak agentics" — is **validated precisely by the evidence**. On the strong side: **IFBench at 82.2% is the highest instruction-following figure found anywhere in this research pass**, ahead of Grok 4.3 and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (both 81.3%); **AIME 2026 reaches 95.5% and HMMT Feb 2026 90.2%**; **ARC Prize independently verifies ARC-AGI-1 at 84.00%**; **MCP Atlas at 79.6% is second-highest in this pass**; and **Vals AI measures SWE-bench at 82.2% — higher than the vendor's own 80.2%**. On the weak side: **an AA Agentic Index of 24.9%**, **GDPval-AA at 1,191 Elo (559 below reference)**, and **an AA Coding Index of 53.0%, 17 points below reference.**
- **Provider / access:** **Open weights, free to self-host** (curated metadata). Thinking Machines Lab published a dedicated **Inkling-Small launch post**, which supplies 18 of the 39 rows below — a full vendor disclosure covering agentic, coding, multimodal, reasoning, knowledge, instruction-following and mathematics benchmarks. **No licence is stated in the sources read**, which is a gap worth closing before commercial use.
- **Release / knowledge:** **No verified release date or knowledge cutoff found.**
- **IDs:** `opencode/inkling-small`; sibling `inkling`. Self-hostable, so effectively free-tier if you own the hardware.
- **Context window:** **1,000,000 tokens** (tracker and curated metadata agree). **AA-LCR at 75.7%** confirms usable long-context reasoning. Max output: no verified figure found.
- **Modalities:** **Text and image input, plus audio input → text output** (curated metadata: "Text in/out; image and audio input"). **The audio path is asserted by curated metadata only — no audio benchmark of any kind exists**, and that limitation is reflected in the score below. Measured visual capability is good: **CharXiv at 81.3% (77.4% without tools)** and **MMMU-Pro at 74%, corroborated at 74.0% independently.** No video path.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **Open weights, free to self-host** (curated metadata). **No hosted rate card was found**, and **no parameter count is published**, so the practical cost of self-hosting cannot be estimated — an unusual gap for an open-weight model, and one that matters more than it would for a model with a documented size.
- **Architecture:** **Open weights, hybrid reasoning.** Parameter count, topology and licence: **not disclosed in the sources read.**

### Raw benchmarks found

> **39 of 613 tracked benchmark slots are populated** (tracker snapshot **2026-10-06**; the tracker has grown to 882 models, where earlier reports in this pass cited 784). **(TML)** = Thinking Machines Lab's own Inkling-Small launch post, which supplies 18 rows; **(AA)** = Artificial Analysis; **(Vals)** = Vals AI; **(ARC)** = ARC Prize's verified results page, labelled **"(XHigh)"**. Composite: **54.5/100, #62 of 882**, flagged conservative on partial coverage.

Agent / tool use:

- **MCP Atlas: 79.6% (TML)** — **the second-highest MCP-Atlas figure in this research pass**, behind only Muse Spark 1.1 (88.1%) and ahead of GLM-5.2 (76.8%), Qwen3.7 Max (76.4%), DeepSeek V4 Pro (73.6%) and GPT-5.4 (70.6%). A real credential for Model-Context-Protocol orchestration.
- **BrowseComp: 77.4% (TML)** — strong web research, comparable to GPT-5.4's 82.7%.
- **Toolathlon-Verified: 54.4% (TML)**
- **Terminal-Bench 2.1: 64.7% (TML)** against **55.1% (Vals)** — a 9.6-point gap, and **both figures sit far below the ~88% anchor**, by 23.3 and 32.9 points.
- **AA Agentic Index: 24.9% (AA)** — **very low**, half DeepSeek V4 Pro's 49.6% and less than half GLM-5.3's 53.4%.
- **GDPval-AA: 1,191 Elo (AA) / 31.2% normalized (AA)** — **559 Elo below the ~1750+ reference, among the lowest in this research pass**, and almost identical to Qwen3.7 Max's 1,190.
- Claw-Eval / OSWorld / τ²-bench / CyberGym / Terminal-Bench 3.0 or 4.0 / AutomationBench: **no verified public score found.**

Reasoning / knowledge:

- **IFBench: 82.2% (TML)** — **the highest instruction-following figure found anywhere in this research pass**, ahead of Grok 4.3 (81.3%), Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (81.3%), AA-IFBench for Qwen3.7 Max (80.5%) and DeepSeek V4 Pro (76.5%).
- **AIME 2026: 95.5% (TML)** and **HMMT Feb 2026: 90.2% (TML)** — strong competition mathematics, substantiating the "strong math" claim.
- **ARC-AGI-1: 84.00% (ARC Prize, verified, XHigh)** and **ARC-AGI-2: 40.1% (TML)** — the ARC-AGI-1 figure is **independently verified by ARC Prize**, which makes it among the more trustworthy rows here. Note the **"XHigh"** configuration label: these are maximum-effort results.
- **GPQA / GPQA-Diamond: 89.5% (TML)**, corroborated at **89.5% (AA)** — **but this misses the 90%+ frontier reference by 0.5 points**, and **Vals AI measures 83.6%**, a 5.9-point spread.
- **MMLU-Pro: 85.6% (Vals)**; **AA-LCR: 75.7% (AA)**
- **HLE: 47.8% (TML)** with tools, but **HLE w/o tools: 31.6% (TML)** and **AA-HLE: 33.3% (AA)** — **both tool-free figures miss the 40%+ reference**, by 8.4 and 6.7 points, so the 47.8% headline is substantially tool-assisted.
- **Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index: 25.7% (AA)** — **34.3 points below the 60+ frontier marker**, and in the methodology's mid band.
- **AA-Omniscience: Index −8.9%, accuracy 33.2%, hallucination rate 63.0% (AA)** — a negative reliability index on a narrow knowledge base.
- **CritPt: 8.3% (TML)** — near-floor on frontier physics. **FrontierMath: no verified public score found.**

Coding:

- **SWE-bench: 82.2% (Vals, independent)** against the vendor's own **SWE-bench Verified: 80.2% (TML)** — **the independent figure exceeds the vendor's**, which is a credibility signal and unusual.
- **LiveCodeBench: 85.9% (Vals, independent)** — strong on self-contained problems.
- **SWE-bench Pro: 55.9% (TML)**; **SciCode: 48.7% (TML) / AA-SciCode: 49.7% (AA)** — **both miss the 55%+ reference**, by 6.3 and 5.3 points.
- **Terminal-Bench 2.1: 64.7% (TML)** — **20.3 points below the 85%+ coding reference.**
- **AA Coding Index: 53.0% (AA)** — **17 points below the methodology's 70%+ reference**, and among the lower Coding Index figures in this research pass.
- DeepSWE / Codeforces / CursorBench / NL2Repo / Vibe Code Bench: **no verified public score found.**

Multimodal:

- **CharXiv: 81.3% (TML)**, **77.4% without tools (TML)** — solid chart reasoning, and the tool-free figure shows it is not a tool artefact.
- **MMMU-Pro: 74% (TML)**, corroborated at **74.0% (AA)** — exact agreement between vendor and independent.
- **No audio benchmark of any kind exists**, despite audio input being claimed; **no OCR, document or video benchmark either.**

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 66/100.** Two results are genuinely good and the rest confirms the "weak agentics" description. **MCP Atlas at 79.6% is the second-highest in this research pass** — a substantive credential for Model-Context-Protocol orchestration, and MCP measurements are scarce enough that it counts for a lot. **BrowseComp at 77.4%** shows real web-research capability, and **Toolathlon-Verified at 54.4%** is mid-field. Four deductions, and they are consistent rather than scattered. **The AA Agentic Index of 24.9% is very low** — half DeepSeek V4 Pro's and less than half GLM-5.3's — and **GDPval-AA at 1,191 Elo is 559 points below the ~1750+ reference**, so the two composite measures of real-world agentic value both say the same thing. **Terminal-Bench 2.1 sits 23.3 points below the anchor on the vendor figure and 32.9 below on the independent one**, with no 3.0 or 4.0 measurement. And **Claw-Eval, OSWorld, τ²-bench, CyberGym and AutomationBench are all unreported**, with the methodology explicitly penalising the Claw-Eval absence. The curated description's "weak agentics" is accurate and should be taken at face value.
- **Reasoning: 80/100.** The strongest dimension, and the "strong math and knowledge" half of the description holds up. **IFBench at 82.2% is the highest instruction-following figure found anywhere in this research pass** — a genuinely useful property, since a model that reliably does what it is told compounds well in pipelines. **AIME 2026 at 95.5% and HMMT Feb 2026 at 90.2%** are strong competition mathematics. **ARC Prize independently verifies ARC-AGI-1 at 84.00%**, with ARC-AGI-2 at 40.1% — third-party verification makes these among the most trustworthy rows on the page. **MMLU-Pro at 85.6%** and **AA-LCR at 75.7%** round it out. Five deductions prevent a higher score. **GPQA at 89.5% misses the 90%+ reference by 0.5 points on both the vendor and Artificial Analysis measurements, and Vals AI measures only 83.6%** — a 5.9-point spread, so the near-miss is the optimistic reading. **Both tool-free HLE figures miss the 40%+ reference** (31.6% vendor, 33.3% independent), so the 47.8% headline is substantially tool-assisted. **The Intelligence Index of 25.7% is 34.3 points below the 60+ marker.** **CritPt at 8.3% is near-floor.** And **the AA-Omniscience Index is negative at −8.9% on a 63.0% hallucination rate and only 33.2% accuracy** — so neither breadth nor calibration is a strength. The **"XHigh"** label on the ARC results is a further reminder that the headline figures are maximum-effort.
- **Context window: 95/100.** A **1,000,000-token window** — agreed by the tracker and this repository's curated metadata — places it in the ≥1M tier (95–100), and **AA-LCR at 75.7%** confirms it is usable at depth, comparable to MiMo-V2.6-Flash's 74.3%. Being open weights, **there is no vendor repricing threshold**, so the full window is yours to configure. It sits at the tier floor rather than above it for three reasons: the 100 condition requires **≥98% retrieval at 512K+** and **no MRCR, RULER, CorpusQA or GraphWalks figure exists**; **AA-LCR at 75.7% is modest** against 1M-class peers such as DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (84.0%), GPT-5.6 Luna (83.7%) and Qwen3.7 Max (79.0% with MRCRv2 at 90.4%); and **max output tokens are undocumented**.
- **Multimodal: 88/100.** **Audio input, per this repository's curated metadata, would place it in the methodology's top band ("+audio or non-text out = 90–100")**, and the measured visual evidence inside that range is genuinely good: **CharXiv at 81.3% is solid chart reasoning and holds 77.4% without tools**, so it is not a tool artefact, and **MMMU-Pro at 74% is corroborated to the decimal at 74.0% by Artificial Analysis** — exact vendor-independent agreement, which is rare and strengthens confidence. It is scored **just below the band floor of 90, deliberately**: the audio capability rests **solely on unverified curated metadata**, and **no audio benchmark of any kind exists** across 39 populated slots, so awarding the full band floor would credit a claim with zero corroboration. For comparison within this pass, MiMo V2.6 Free scores 90 at the floor on a *verified* omnimodal surface including video. Two further limits: **there is no video path**, and **no OCR or document benchmark exists**.
- **Coding: 76/100.** Good on self-contained and repository problems, weak on the composite measure. The best evidence is independent and unusually favourable: **Vals AI measures SWE-bench at 82.2%, which exceeds the vendor's own SWE-bench Verified figure of 80.2%** — vendor under-reporting is rare and lends credibility to the whole table — and **LiveCodeBench at 85.9% from Vals AI** is strong. Four deductions. **The AA Coding Index at 53.0% is 17 points below the methodology's 70%+ reference**, the largest single shortfall in this dimension and hard to reconcile with the SWE-bench result, which suggests the strength is narrow. **SciCode at 48.7% vendor and 49.7% independent both miss the 55%+ reference.** **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 64.7% is 20.3 points below the 85%+ coding reference**, ruling out terminal-driven agentic coding. And **DeepSWE, Codeforces, CursorBench, NL2Repo and Vibe Code Bench are all unreported**, so five of the dimension's references cannot be checked.
- **Cost efficiency: 92/100.** **The weights are open and free to self-host**, so the per-token rate is escapable entirely — and the decisive within-family finding is that **this smaller model beats its larger sibling on measured aggregate: 54.5 against Inkling's 53.3**, so there is no capability argument for paying more for the bigger model. What free self-hosting buys is substantial: **the highest IFBench figure in this research pass (82.2%)**, **AIME 2026 at 95.5%**, **ARC Prize-verified ARC-AGI-1 at 84.00%**, **the second-highest MCP Atlas (79.6%)**, **SWE-bench at 82.2% independently measured**, **LiveCodeBench at 85.9%**, **CharXiv at 81.3%** and **a full 1M window**. Three deductions keep it off the mid-90s, and two are documentation gaps rather than capability ones. **No licence is stated in any source read**, which is a genuine blocker for commercial deployment and unusual for an open-weight release. **No parameter count is published**, so the hardware cost of self-hosting cannot be estimated — a more serious gap than it sounds, since the difference between a 30B and a 700B model is the difference between one GPU and a cluster. And **no hosted rate card exists**, so there is no managed fallback to price against.
- **Overall Score: 81.0/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (66 + 80 + 95 + 88 + 76) / 5 = 81.0 — a **sharply specialised open-weight model whose curated description, "strong math and knowledge, weak agentics", is validated almost line by line** and should be read literally. What holds up, with much of it independently verified: **IFBench at 82.2% is the highest instruction-following figure found anywhere in this research pass**; **AIME 2026 at 95.5% and HMMT Feb 2026 at 90.2%**; **ARC Prize verifies ARC-AGI-1 at 84.00%**; **MCP Atlas at 79.6% is second-highest here**; **Vals AI measures SWE-bench at 82.2%, above the vendor's own figure**, with **LiveCodeBench at 85.9%**; **CharXiv at 81.3% and MMMU-Pro at 74% corroborated to the decimal**; a **1M window with AA-LCR at 75.7%**; and **free self-hosting on weights that outperform the larger sibling**. Choose it for **cheap open-weight mathematics, instruction-bound pipelines, MCP tool orchestration, web research and self-contained coding.** Four cautions. **Agentics are genuinely weak** — an AA Agentic Index of 24.9%, GDPval at 1,191 Elo (559 below reference), and Terminal-Bench more than 20 points below anchor on both measurements — so do not deploy it as an autonomous long-horizon agent. **The AA Coding Index at 53.0% sits 17 points below reference** despite the strong SWE-bench result, which means the coding strength is narrower than the headline suggests. **Reliability and breadth are limited**: GPQA misses 90% by 0.5 points on the best reading and 6.4 on the worst, both tool-free HLE figures miss 40%, the Intelligence Index is 25.7%, CritPt is 8.3%, and the Omniscience Index is −8.9% on a 63.0% hallucination rate. And **two documentation gaps need closing before commercial use** — **no licence and no parameter count are published anywhere in the sources read**, which respectively block legal clearance and hardware planning.
