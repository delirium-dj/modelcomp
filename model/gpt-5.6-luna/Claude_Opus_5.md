## Model card

- **Name:** GPT-5.6 Luna. Proprietary. Variant/alias flag: **Luna is the fastest and cheapest tier of the GPT-5.6 family**, below **Sol** (flagship, $4/$20) and **Terra** (balanced, $2/$12); a **GPT-5.6 Cyber** sibling and a separately tracked **GPT-5.6 Luna (low)** effort configuration also exist. Note that ARC Prize's ARC-AGI-1 result for it is labelled **"(Max)"**, so some figures reflect maximum effort.
- **Short description:** Released **2026-07-09** alongside Sol and Terra. **This is the most under-rated model in this research pass.** It is OpenAI's *cheapest* GPT-5.6 tier at **$1 / $6**, yet it carries the **full 1,050,000-token context**, posts **the highest FrontierMath figures recorded anywhere in this dataset by an enormous margin — Tiers 1–3 at 78.6% and Tier 4 at 58.5%** — records **SWE-bench at 93.0% (Vals AI, independent)**, **GPQA Diamond above 90% under three separate measurements**, and **ARC Prize-verified ARC-AGI-2 at 59.5%**. The counterweight is severe and specific: **a 92.6% AA-Omniscience hallucination rate producing a −10.3% composite index.**
- **Provider / access:** OpenAI API and ChatGPT. **Cognition published a dedicated "GPT-5.6 models in Devin" evaluation** and Cursor ran it through CursorBench, so third-party tooling adopted it. Reasoning: explicit reasoning mode with effort levels (a separate "low" configuration is tracked).
- **Release / knowledge:** Released **2026-07-09**. **Knowledge cutoff 2026-02-16** (the GPT-5.6 family standard, documented by OpenAI for Sol and Terra).
- **IDs:** `gpt-5.6-luna`; a `gpt-5.6-luna-low` configuration is tracked separately. **No free-tier ID.**
- **Context window:** **1,050,000 tokens** — **identical to Sol and Terra**, which is the single most remarkable thing about this tier: OpenAI did not cut the context to cut the price. On family precedent this implies a 128,000-token output limit, a 922,000-token input cap and the **272K full-request repricing threshold**, though none was separately verified for Luna. **AA-LCR at 83.7% is among the highest long-context reasoning figures in this research pass.**
- **Modalities:** **Text and image input → text output** (GPT-5.6 family standard; no audio or video). Confirmed in practice by three closely agreeing MMMU-Pro measurements.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-03):** **$1.00 / 1M input, $6.00 / 1M output** — documented in a sourced model page verified earlier in this research session, which states that within the family "Luna is the fastest and cheapest at $1/$6". That is **a quarter of Sol's $4/$20 and half Terra's $2/$12**. **Re-verify before budgeting**; no primary OpenAI page for Luna was read directly.
- **Architecture:** Proprietary, closed weights. Parameter count and topology: not disclosed.

### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 86/100.** Strong across the board for any tier, let alone the cheapest one. **OSWorld 2.0 at 45.6% is the second-highest figure in this entire research pass** on a benchmark where most models score under 10% — that is a genuinely differentiated computer-use capability. **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 84.7% vendor / 79.0% independent** is close to the ~88% anchor, **BrowseComp 83.3%** and **CyberGym 77.9%** are both strong, **GDPval-AA at 1,582 Elo exceeds GPT-6 Sol's 1,505**, and the **AA Agentic Index of 42.7%** matches Claude Opus 4.8. Four deductions: GDPval remains 168 Elo short of the ~1750+ reference; **Terminal-Bench 3.0 at 14.3%, ExploitGym at 12.4% and ApprenticeBench at 7% all collapse** on harder or more open-ended harnesses; **Tau2-bench, Tau3-Banking, Claw-Eval and MCP-Atlas are all unreported**; and the 5.7-point Terminal-Bench vendor-independent gap means the headline needs a discount.
- **Reasoning: 88/100.** The standout dimension, and one figure is in a class of its own. **FrontierMath v2 Tier 4 at 58.5% is roughly double the next-best figure in this entire dataset** (Claude Opus 4.8's 31.3%), with Tiers 1–3 at 78.6% corroborating — this is the strongest research-mathematics evidence recorded anywhere in this research pass, from OpenAI's cheapest GPT-5.6 tier. **GPQA Diamond clears the 90%+ frontier reference under three separate measurements agreeing within 1.2 points (92.3% / 91.7% / 91.1%)**, **ARC Prize verifies ARC-AGI-2 at 59.5%**, **AA-LCR reaches 83.7%**, **CritPt at 20.6% is mid-to-upper range**, and the **Intelligence Index of 51.2% exceeds GPT-6 Sol's 47.6%**. The deduction is large and specific: **a 92.6% AA-Omniscience hallucination rate gives a −10.3% composite index** — the third-worst confabulation rate in this dataset — and at 42.7% accuracy that is a calibration failure, not an ignorance one. **AA-HLE at 39.5% also misses the 40%+ reference by 0.5 points**, and ARC-AGI-3 is a floor result at 0.2%.
- **Context window: 95/100.** **1,050,000 tokens — identical to GPT-5.6 Sol and Terra.** That OpenAI shipped its cheapest tier with the flagship's full window is the single most commercially significant fact about this model, and **AA-LCR at 83.7% is among the highest long-context reasoning figures in this research pass**, so the window is demonstrably usable. It sits at the tier floor rather than above it because the ≥1M tier's 100 condition requires **≥98% retrieval at 512K+** and **no MRCR, RULER or GraphWalks figure exists**; because the family's **922,000-token input cap** means the headline million cannot be filled; and because the **272K full-request repricing threshold** should be assumed to apply, making the economically sane window roughly a quarter of nominal — though at $1/$6 even the repriced rate is cheap.
- **Multimodal: 70/100.** Input is **text and image** with text-only output and no audio or video, fixing it in the methodology's "+image in = 60–70" band. It earns the **top of that band** on the strength of unusually tight corroboration: **MMMU-Pro at 78.4% from OpenAI is independently measured at 78.6% by Artificial Analysis — a 0.2-point agreement** — with 79.5% when Python is available. That is credible mid-to-upper-field visual understanding. It cannot exceed the band because the input surface excludes video, audio and PDF regardless of quality, and because **no CharXiv, document, OCR or audio benchmark exists**, so chart reading and document extraction are entirely unmeasured.
- **Coding: 88/100.** Excellent, and the best evidence is independent. **SWE-bench at 93.0% from Vals AI is the second-highest figure recorded in this entire research pass**, behind only Grok 4.6; **the AA Coding Index at 71.5% clears the methodology's 70%+ frontier reference**; **Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 84.7% is essentially at the 85%+ coding reference**; and **VulcanBench v3 at 85.5%** adds a third independent datapoint. Third-party tooling adoption corroborates — Cognition evaluated it in Devin (FrontierCode 1.1 Extended 55.1%) and Cursor ran CursorBench 3.2 (61.1%). Four deductions: **DeepSWE at 67.2% is 6.8 points below the 74%+ frontier reference**; **AA-SciCode at 53.6% misses the 55%+ reference by 1.4 points**; **CursorBench falls 25.2 points from v3.2 to v4.0 (35.9%)**; and **SWE-bench Verified, LiveCodeBench and Vibe Code Bench are absent**.
- **Cost efficiency: 90/100.** **The best price-to-capability ratio among proprietary models in this entire research pass.** At **$1.00 / $6.00 it is roughly a third of the $3/$15 anchor**, and what that buys is extraordinary for a bottom-tier model: **the flagship's full 1,050,000-token context** (OpenAI cut the price without cutting the window), **SWE-bench 93.0%**, an **AA Coding Index above the frontier reference**, **GPQA Diamond above 90% under three measurements**, **ARC-AGI-2 verified at 59.5%**, **AA-LCR 83.7%**, and **FrontierMath Tier 4 at double the next-best model in this dataset**. Within its own family it costs **a quarter of Sol ($4/$20) and half of Terra ($2/$12)** while posting a *higher* Intelligence Index than GPT-6 Sol and a *higher* GDPval Elo. Three deductions: the **price is unconfirmed by any primary OpenAI page**; the **272K repricing threshold** should be assumed; and the **92.6% hallucination rate imposes a verification cost** the rate card does not show.
- **Overall Score: 85.4/100.** Mean of the five non-cost dimensions (86 + 88 + 95 + 70 + 88) / 5 = 85.4 — and the headline is that **this is the best value model in this research pass.** OpenAI's cheapest GPT-5.6 tier, at $1/$6, delivers the flagship's full 1M context, **FrontierMath v2 Tier 4 at 58.5% — roughly double the next-best figure in this entire dataset**, **SWE-bench at 93.0% (second-highest here)**, **GPQA Diamond above 90% under three agreeing measurements**, **ARC Prize-verified ARC-AGI-2 at 59.5%**, **OSWorld 2.0 at 45.6% (second-highest here)**, **AA-LCR 83.7%** and an **AA Coding Index that clears the frontier reference** — while scoring a *higher* Intelligence Index and GDPval Elo than GPT-6 Sol, which costs twice as much. Choose it for **research-level mathematics**, **repository-scale coding**, **computer use**, **long-context work** and **graduate-level science** at minimum cost. One finding must govern deployment: **a 92.6% hallucination rate with a −10.3% composite reliability index** means this model answers confidently whether or not it knows — never use it as a source of truth, and pair it with retrieval for any factual task. Secondary cautions: the harder agentic harnesses collapse (Terminal-Bench 3.0 14.3%, ApprenticeBench 7%), four tool-use references are unreported, there is no multimodal breadth beyond MMMU-Pro, and the $1/$6 rate — the whole basis of the value case — rests on a sourced third-party page rather than a primary OpenAI pricing document.
