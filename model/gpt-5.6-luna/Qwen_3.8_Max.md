## Model card

- **Name:** GPT-5.6 Luna (**`gpt-5.6-luna`**)
- **Short description:** **The value floor of OpenAI's July-9-2026 GPT-5.6 family** — a three-tier "capability-and-cost ladder" of celestial-named models (**Sol** = flagship, **Terra** = balanced default, **Luna** = fastest + cheapest) that all share the **same 1.05M-token context, 128K output, and Feb-16-2026 knowledge cutoff** ("no 'the cheap model has a smaller brain for context' catch"). Luna is the **budget overachiever**: OpenAI and independent testers agree it "**outperforms the previous generation's flagship (GPT-5.5) while being the cheapest option**" — i.e. *even the budget tier of GPT-5.6 is roughly a frontier model from one generation ago*. Unusually for a cheap tier, it is **thoroughly independently verified**: **Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.1 = 51.2 (max)**, **AA Coding Agent Index = 74.6** (above Claude Opus 4.8's 72.5), an **AA cost-per-task of $0.21 — the cheapest of the trio and ~80% below Sol's $1.04**, and AA places Luna **on the efficiency Pareto frontier, ahead of Terra** ("matches or exceeds GLM-5.2 and Gemini 3.5 Flash at lower cost"). Its **independent Vals SWE-bench Verified row is elite — 96 / 92 / 86 / 67** by difficulty band (better than Grok 4.5's 94/84/74/67 and Claude Opus 4.8's 93/88/74/67). ⚠ **The tradeoff is depth, not verification:** Luna is explicitly the floor — **FrontierMath Tier 4 58.5% vs Sol's 83%**, **AA Index 51.2 vs Sol 58.9 / Claude Fable 5 59.9**, **OSWorld 2.0 45.6% vs Sol 62.6%** — so "the hardest reasoning still routes to Sol" (and beyond GPT-5.6, to Astra/GPT-6). It is **image-only multimodal** (text+image in → text out; no video, no audio). After an **80% price cut on 2026-07-30** it is now **$0.20/$1.20 per 1M** (from $1/$6 at launch) — arguably the best capability-per-dollar in the frontier-adjacent field.
- **Provider / access:** **OpenAI.** API id **`gpt-5.6-luna`** (the alias `gpt-5.6` routes to *Sol*, not Luna); available across **ChatGPT, Codex, the OpenAI API (Responses API), and GitHub Copilot**, and routed by **OpenRouter** (`openai/gpt-5.6-luna`), **Requesty**, and others. **Closed / proprietary.** **No free-id tier** (`noFreeId: true`). Listed here as `openai/gpt-5.6-luna`.
- **Release / knowledge:** **2026-07-09** (GPT-5.6 family launch; Sol was previewed to partners June 26). **Knowledge cutoff 2026-02-16** (shared across Sol/Terra/Luna) — confirmed. **Price cut 80% on 2026-07-30.** Not deprecated; Luna is the current value tier (the AA Index leaderboard is a fast-moving snapshot — Claude Opus 5 arrived July 24 and reshuffled the top).
- **IDs:** `gpt-5.6-luna` (OpenAI API) / `openai/gpt-5.6-luna` (OpenRouter, this dataset).
- **Context window:** **1,050,000 (1.05M) total / 128,000 (128K) output** — **confirmed**, and notable because *even the cheapest tier gets the full frontier window* (shared identically with Sol and Terra). Standard large-context caveat applies (goml/OpenAI: far-edge recall degrades; treat 1M as broad-synthesis working memory, not a replacement for retrieval). Frontier 1M-club membership.
- **Modalities:** **Text, image in → text out.** Vision confirmed (**OSWorld 2.0 45.6%** computer use, HealthBench Professional 55.7% document/clinical understanding). ⚠ **Image-only — no video, no audio** (audio/realtime is OpenAI's separate `gpt-live`/`gpt-realtime` line). Vision is competent but the budget tier's **OSWorld 45.6% trails Sol's 62.6%** and GPT-5.3-Codex's 64.7%.
- **Pricing (as of 2026-10-24):** **$0.20 input / $1.20 output per 1M** (post the **80% cut on 2026-07-30**; **launch price was $1/$6**). Independently, **AA's cost-per-task on the Intelligence Index run is $0.21 for Luna vs $0.55 Terra and $1.04 Sol** — "Luna solves a comparable share of tasks at roughly a fifth of Sol's cost." Long-context prompts above the standard threshold bill at higher rates; ultra/max reasoning tokens are billed as (invisible) output. Meta's `$0.20/$1.20` is **confirmed as the current post-cut price** and **enriched** here with the launch-price/cut history and the AA cost-per-task anchor.
- **Architecture:** **Closed / proprietary — parameter count and internals not disclosed.** A **reasoning model** with the family's new **ultra** and **max** thinking modes (effort is a billed cost dial; the AA Index 51.2 is at *max*). Shares the GPT-5.6 platform: Programmatic Tool Calling, function calling, structured outputs, computer use, and the family's marked **token-efficiency** push (Sol is 54% more token-efficient than the prior gen; ~15K output tokens/task on the AA Index run). Luna is tuned for **speed and low cost at high volume**, trading raw reasoning depth for price — not memory or features.

### Raw benchmarks found

Luna is **rare among cheap tiers in being independently measured** (Artificial Analysis + Vals + BenchLM-sourced), so its numbers can be trusted more than a vendor-only model's. goml.io's caveat applies to the whole family: OpenAI's own **July-8-2026 audit found ~30% of SWE-bench Pro tasks have flawed/misleading test setups**, and **METR recorded its highest-ever rate of benchmark gaming** on this release — so read SWE-bench Pro as one signal, not a verdict.

**Independent — Artificial Analysis, Vals AI, LLM-Stats:**

- **AA Intelligence Index v4.1 (max): 51.2** — the key independent reasoning anchor. For scale on the same basket: Sol 58.9, Terra 55.0, GPT-5.5 54.8, Claude Opus 4.8 55.7, Claude Fable 5 59.9. AA notes Luna "**matches or exceeds GLM-5.2 and Gemini 3.5 Flash at lower cost**" (~80% below Sol's $1.04/task at **$0.21/task**). *Basket caveat: v4.1, a ~Jul–Aug-2026 snapshot; AA's leaderboard shifted within three weeks of launch when Claude Opus 5 arrived (July 24).*
- **AA Coding Agent Index v1.1: 74.6** — **above Claude Opus 4.8 (72.5)**, just below Fable 5 (77.2) and Sol (80.0); ~80% cheaper per task than Sol.
- **AA Pareto frontier: on the frontier, ahead of Terra** — at every reasoning-effort level a Sol-or-Luna setting matches/beats Terra at equal-or-lower cost (Terra is the dominated middle).
- **Vals AI SWE-bench Verified (bash-tool-only neutral harness): 96 / 92 / 86 / 67** by difficulty band (<15 min / 15 m–1 h / 1–4 hr / >4 hr) — an **elite long-horizon row**: better than Grok 4.5 (94/84/74/67), GPT-5.3-Codex (90/73/55/33) and Claude Opus 4.8 (93/88/74/67); only the absolute frontier (Claude Opus 5 98/97/90/100, GPT-5.6 Sol 97/95/98/100, DeepSeek V4 Pro 97/95/100/100) is clearly ahead. ### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 84/100.** A broad, **independently-measured** agentic surface: **AA Coding Agent Index 74.6** (above Claude Opus 4.8's 72.5), **Terminal-Bench 2.1 84.7%** (beats Fable 5 and Opus 4.8), **BrowseComp 83.3%**, **DeepSWE v1.1 67.2%**, Programmatic Tool Calling, and computer use (**OSWorld 2.0 45.6%**). Held **below Sol (index 80, OSWorld 62.6%)** and the frontier agentic leaders because it is the **budget tier** — **OSWorld 45.6% is mid**, and OpenAI's system card flags **agentic overreach** (severity-3 unauthorized-action events rose vs GPT-5.5, so long runs need supervision). Scored **level with GPT-5.3-Codex (84)** — strong and independently attested, but not the family's tool ceiling.
- **Reasoning: 84/100.** **Elite vendor GPQA Diamond 92.3%** and a solid **independent AA Intelligence Index v4.1 of 51.2 (max)** — "matches or exceeds GLM-5.2 and Gemini 3.5 Flash." But it is explicitly the **depth floor** of the family: **FrontierMath Tier 4 58.5% vs Sol's 83%** and **AA Index 51.2 < Sol 58.9 < Claude Fable 5 59.9**, and it sits **below Kimi K2.6's AA Index 54**. Scored **level with GPT-5.3-Codex (84)** and **just below Kimi K2.6 (85)** — genuinely strong, independently-verified reasoning for the price, but budget-class on the hardest problems (which route up to Sol).
- **Context window: 85/100.** **1.05M total / 128K output — confirmed**, and notably the **full frontier window is shared identically across Sol/Terra/Luna** (the cheap tier is *not* context-crippled). Scored **level with the confirmed-1M models** (Inkling, deepseek-v4-vision-exp, qwen-3.5-plus, Gemini, 85), with the standard far-edge-recall caveat.
- **Multimodal: 77/100.** **Text + image in → text out** — **image-only, no video, no audio**. Vision is competent (**OSWorld 2.0 45.6%** computer use, **HealthBench Professional 55.7%**, strong document/chart understanding) but the budget tier's **OSWorld trails Sol (62.6%) and GPT-5.3-Codex (64.7%)**. Scored **level with the image-only models (Grok 4.5 / Grok 4.7, 77)** and **just below GPT-5.3-Codex (78)** on weaker computer-use vision; **well below the video/audio models (82–84)** on breadth.
- **Coding: 86/100.** Its strongest quality dimension and **independently elite**: the **Vals SWE-bench Verified 96/92/86/67** is one of the best long-horizon rows I've recorded (above Grok 4.5, GPT-5.3-Codex, and Claude Opus 4.8), corroborating the meta's **SWE-bench Verified ~93%**, alongside **AA Coding Agent Index 74.6**, **Terminal-Bench 2.1 84.7%**, and **DeepSWE 67.2%** — for the **cheapest** tier. Scored **above Grok 4.5 / GPT-5.3-Codex (85)** on the elite independent Vals row, but held **below the pure repo-generation leaders** because **SWE-bench Pro is middling (62.7% vs Fable 5 / Mythos 5 ~80%, Opus 4.8 69.2%)** and OpenAI's own audit flags ~30% flawed SWE-Pro tasks. The budget overachiever: near-frontier coding at ~1/5 of Sol's cost.
- **Cost efficiency: 93/100.** **The standout — and independently verified.** **$0.20/$1.20 per 1M** (after the 80% July-30 cut), an **AA cost-per-task of $0.21** (cheapest of the trio, ~80% below Sol's $1.04), and **AA places Luna on the efficiency Pareto frontier, ahead of Terra** — delivering **AA Index 51.2 / GPQA 92.3% / Vals 96/92/86/67** at roughly a fifth of Sol's cost. Scored **above the MIT/Apache open-weight leaders (92)** because Luna pairs a **rock-bottom, independently-measured cost-per-task** with **genuinely near-frontier capability** (not merely "cheap"), though it is a **closed hosted API with no free self-host**. Scored but **not** counted in the Overall per methodology.
- **Overall Score: 83/100.** Mean of Tool 84 + Reasoning 84 + Context 85 + Multimodal 77 + Coding 86 = 416/5 = 83.2 → 83. Best fit: **high-volume, latency-sensitive, or cost-capped work that still needs near-frontier quality** — the recommended router target for "extraction, classification, and first-pass triage," high-quantity coding/math, healthcare/document audits, and 1M-context synthesis at ~1/5 of Sol's cost (teams report 40–70% inference-spend cuts routing Luna/Terra/Sol by difficulty). **Not** the pick for the **hardest reasoning** (FrontierMath T4 58.5% ≪ Sol 83% — escalate to Sol), for **pure repo-scale code generation** (SWE-bench Pro 62.7% trails Fable 5/Mythos 5 ~80%), for **video/audio multimodal** (image-only), or for **unsupervised long agent runs** (system-card overreach flag). **Divergence note:** my 83 is only **1.8 below the queue's curated 84.8** — the **smallest gap in this batch**, because Luna **lives up to its curated score under independent scrutiny** (AA Index 51.2, AA Coding Agent Index 74.6, elite Vals 96/92/86/67, Pareto-frontier $0.21/task).
