# GPT-5.6 Luna — Independent Research Report

## Summary and evidence

GPT-5.6 Luna is OpenAI's nano-tier model for cost-sensitive, high-volume work. It supports six reasoning levels, text and image input, function calling, structured output, and the full Responses tool suite, including web/file search, code interpreter, hosted shell, computer use, MCP, skills, and patch application. It provides **1,050,000 context tokens**, **128,000 maximum output**, and a February 16, 2026 knowledge cutoff.

Source: [OpenAI GPT-5.6 Luna documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-5.6-luna)

## Cost and limitations

Pricing is **$0.20 input, $0.02 cached input, and $1.20 output per million tokens**; inputs above 272K cost twice as much and output 1.5 times as much. Audio/video are unsupported, and Luna trades capability for throughput versus Terra and Sol.

## Scores

- **Tool use: 89/100.** The broad Responses tool suite is unusually complete for a budget model.
- **Reasoning: 78/100.** Adjustable reasoning helps, but this is explicitly the smallest GPT-5.6 tier.
- **Context window: 97/100.** 1.05M input and 128K output are exceptional.
- **Multimodal: 84/100.** Image input is useful; audio and video are absent.
- **Coding: 80/100.** Strong platform integration suits routine coding, not the hardest engineering work.
- **Cost efficiency: 98/100.** Very low prices provide excellent high-volume economics.
- **Overall Score: 86/100.** Half-up rounded mean: (89 + 78 + 97 + 84 + 80) / 5 = 85.6.

## Bottom line

GPT-5.6 Luna is an excellent million-context automation model when volume and cost matter more than frontier reasoning.
