- Source: OpenAI (`gpt-5.1-codex-mini`; id `opencode/gpt-5.1-codex-mini`; AA `gpt-5-1-codex-mini` — page exists but deprecated, Intelligence Index N/A)

> **Verdict:** GPT-5.1 Codex Mini is **OpenAI's cheapest, fastest Codex tier** — a proprietary **budget coding model** released **13 November 2025** alongside GPT-5.1, GPT-5.1-Codex and GPT-5.1-Codex-Max, positioned as "**the cost-effective coding companion**" at **$0.25/$2.00 per 1M tokens** (a fifth of gpt-5-codex's price) with a large **400K context / 128K max output**, **text + image input → text output**, adaptive reasoning (the family's new `none`→`high` ladder plus 24-hour prompt caching and `apply_patch`/`shell` tools), and Responses-API support. It is **genuinely fast** — AA measures **173.3 tok/s (#28/144, 4-of-4 fastest tier**, price-tier median 113.3) — and, for a budget model, its **structured reasoning is surprisingly strong**: independently-measured **AIME 2025 91.7% (92nd pct), GPQA Diamond 81.3% (74th pct), MMLU-Pro 82.0% (77th pct)**. **But it is the *cheap* tier and it shows:** vendor coding numbers are modest (**SWE-Bench Pro 49.0%, Terminal-Bench 2.0 46.1%, Vibe Code Bench 22.2%**, no SWE-bench Verified published), independent **WebDev Arena 1243 (12th pct)** and **Design Arena 1132 (32nd pct, 43% win)** contradict Azure's "front end development" pitch, **frontier reasoning is weak** (HLE 18.5%, **CritPt physics 0.0%** — a total failure), **factual reliability is poor** (**AA-Omniscience −16.52**, i.e. below-zero non-hallucination), **latency is very high** (TTFT **17.30s** vs 1.69s tier median, the reasoning "thinking" tax — at odds with apxml's "real-time completion" framing), and the model is now **deprecated and shut down** (removed from Codex ChatGPT sign-in **14 Apr 2026**, **API shutdown 23 Jul 2026**, successor gpt-5.6-terra / GPT-5.2-Codex). Its current **AA v5.0 Intelligence Index is N/A** (delisted on deprecation); the **30.63** value captured by aggregators is a **pre-v5.0 (v4.x) reading from 2026-07-21 and is non-convertible**. Scored Overall **68** (div **+3.5** vs curated 64.5) — a fast, cheap, large-context budget coder whose elite competition-math/science scores and 400K window lift the quality-dimension mean above the curated value, held back by modest coding breadth, weak front-end/design, poor factual reliability, a zero physics score, and end-of-life status.

## Model card

- **Name:** GPT-5.1 Codex Mini
- **Short description:** OpenAI's **cheapest, fastest Codex tier** — a proprietary budget coding model (Nov 2025) with a large 400K/128K context, text+image input, adaptive reasoning, and **$0.25/$2.00** pricing (a fifth of gpt-5-codex). Surprisingly strong structured math/science (AIME 91.7%, GPQA 81.3%) and top-tier speed (173 tok/s), but modest coding (SWE-Bench Pro 49.0%), weak front-end/design, poor factual reliability (AA-Omniscience negative), zero physics (CritPt 0.0%), high latency (TTFT 17.3s), and now **deprecated / API-shut-down (23 Jul 2026)**.

- **Context window:** **400,000 tokens total; 128,000 max output** (AA "~600 A4 pages"; puter/meta corroborate 400K/128K). Large for a budget tier and a headline selling point, though independent **AA-LCR long-context reasoning is only 66.7% (64th pct)** — mid-tier exploitation of the window.
- **Modalities:** **Text and image input → text output** (AA: "multimodal. It can process text and image input and generate text output"). - **Pricing:** **$0.25 per 1M input / $2.00 per 1M output**, **$0.025 cached** (90% cache discount); native and OpenCode Zen identical. AA blended (7:2:1 cache/input/output) **$0.27 per 1M**. Input "somewhat expensive" (median $0.23), output "expensive" (median $0.80) — but it was **the cheapest Codex tier** (a fifth of gpt-5-codex). No cost-per-Intelligence-Index-task (deprecated → N/A).
- **Architecture:** **Proprietary, undisclosed** — OpenAI has not published the parameter count or size. A **distilled/budget member of the GPT-5.1 Codex family** (routing fast/thinking design with an effort ladder), tuned for **steerability, interactivity, and low-cost/low-latency coding** rather than depth. Shares the family's `apply_patch`/`shell` tools, 24-hour prompt caching, and Responses-API tooling.

- **Reasoning / knowledge:** A **mixed but net-positive** picture. **Strong structured reasoning:** **AIME 2025 91.7% (92nd pct — elite competition math)**, **GPQA Diamond 81.3% (74th pct)**, **MMLU-Pro 82.0% (77th pct)**. **Weak frontier reasoning & reliability:** **HLE 18.5%** (low absolute; leader O-5.5 67.7%), **CritPt physics 0.0%** (total failure), and **AA-Omniscience −16.52** (a *negative* non-hallucination score — poor factual reliability / high hallucination). - **Coding:** The model's raison d'être, but it is the **budget tier**. ### Normalized scores (1–100)

- **Tool use: 65/100.** Real coding-agent tooling (`apply_patch`, `shell`, Responses API) and respectable terminal work (**Terminal-Bench Hard 33.3%, 82nd pct**; vendor Terminal-Bench 2.0 46.1%), but mid-tier autonomous agency — **Tau2-Bench Telecom 62.9%** and **GDPval-AA 1053** both sit near/below the class median, and there is no BFCL. A capable inline/IDE agent, not a strong long-horizon autonomous one.
- **Reasoning: 66/100.** Genuinely strong **exam-style** reasoning for a budget model — **AIME 2025 91.7% (elite), GPQA Diamond 81.3%, MMLU-Pro 82.0%** — but held to mid-range by **weak frontier reasoning (HLE 18.5%)**, a **total failure on physics (CritPt 0.0%)**, and **poor factual reliability (AA-Omniscience −16.52**, a below-zero non-hallucination score). Composite AA Index (v4.x) 30.63 = 70th pct. Structured math/science well above its price; open-ended/hallucination-prone tasks well below frontier.
- **Context window: 80/100.** **400K total / 128K output** — a large window and a real selling point at this price (a fifth of gpt-5-codex). Below the top (1M-native ≈ 84) tier, and its **AA-LCR long-context reasoning is only mid (66.7%, 64th pct)** — the window is big but not exceptionally exploited; no RULER/MRCR figure published.
- **Multimodal: 65/100.** **Text + image input → text output** — genuinely image-capable (so above the text-only 50 convention), with vision serving coding (screenshots/mockups/diagrams). Capped because there is **no dedicated vision benchmark** (no MMMU-Pro measured) and the one design/multimodal signal, **Design Arena 1132 (32nd pct, 43% win)**, is weak — image input is a secondary, under-demonstrated coding aid, not a strength.
- **Coding: 64/100.** The primary purpose, but the **budget tier**: vendor **SWE-Bench Pro 49.0%** (far below Codex-Max/flagships), **Terminal-Bench 2.0 46.1%**, **Vibe Code Bench 22.2%** (weak), **no SWE-bench Verified**; independent **WebDev Arena 1243 (12th pct)** and **Design Arena 1132 (32nd pct)** undercut the "front end development" pitch, and **CCBench 42.2%** comes from a 6-model field (low signal). Good value for mechanical/inline edits at low cost and high speed; not a frontier software engineer.
- **Cost efficiency: 74/100.** **The cheapest Codex tier** — **$0.25/$2.00, $0.025 cached (90% discount), blended $0.27** (a fifth of gpt-5-codex) — combined with **top-tier speed (173 tok/s, 4/4)**, making it excellent value for high-volume, low-complexity coding while it was live. Held below the top cost tier because **output is above market median ($2.00 vs $0.80)**, **TTFT is very high (17.3s)** which erodes the "fast/cheap" experience on reasoning settings, and the model is now **deprecated / API-shut-down (23 Jul 2026)**, so the value is historical. (Scored but NOT counted toward Overall.)
- **Overall Score: 68/100.** Half-up mean of the five quality dims (65+66+80+65+64)/5 = 340/5 = 68.0 → 68. Divergence **+3.5** vs the curated queue value 64.5. The positive gap reflects that the curated score under-weights three genuine strengths I verified independently — the **large 400K/128K context (Context 80)**, **elite competition math/science (AIME 91.7%, GPQA 81.3%, MMLU-Pro 82.0%)**, and **top-tier speed (173 tok/s)** — while I keep **Coding (64)**, **Multimodal (65)** and **Reasoning (66)** restrained by the budget-tier reality: modest SWE-Bench Pro 49.0%, weak front-end/design arenas, poor factual reliability (AA-Omniscience −16.52), a zero physics score (CritPt 0.0%), high latency, and end-of-life status. It is a fast, cheap, large-context budget coder that punches above its price on structured reasoning but is no frontier engineer. Cost efficiency excluded from the mean per methodology.
