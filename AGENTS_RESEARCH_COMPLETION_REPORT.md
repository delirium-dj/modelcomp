3. **Multimodal Capabilities:** Text, image, audio, and video input support becoming standard
4. **Specialized Tiers:** Models increasingly offered in specialized tiers (Flash, Pro, UltraSpeed, etc.)

### **Pricing Trends:**
1. **Flagship Models:** $5/$25 per 1M tokens (standard for OpenAI and Anthropic)
